## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

Multiple B-lines (>3 per intercostal space) are present throughout the clip. They are individually identifiable as discrete, bright vertical artifacts extending from the pleural line to the far field, with dark lung visible between them. They do not coalesce into a continuous white sheet (ground glass). This pattern is consistent with **septal B-lines**, indicating thickened interlobular septa.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: shred_sign**

In the later frames (8–10), there is a small region of **subpleural tissue-like echogenicity** just deep to the pleural line. The key finding is the **irregular, shredded deep border** between consolidated lung and underlying aerated parenchyma. There is no complete hepatization and no clearly defined air bronchograms — the dominant feature is the fragmented, non-linear transition zone (shred sign), consistent with early/partial alveolar consolidation.
