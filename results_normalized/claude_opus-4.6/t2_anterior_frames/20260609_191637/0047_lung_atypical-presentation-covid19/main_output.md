# Frame-by-Frame Analysis

## Frame 1
Bright, curved pleural line visible with distinct vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending from the pleural line toward the bottom of the image. These are discrete and well-spaced. Some posterior acoustic shadowing from ribs is noted.

## Frame 2
Pleural line visible as a smooth curving hyperechoic structure. Fewer vertical artifacts; the subpleural space appears relatively dark/anechoic.

## Frame 3
The pleural line appears slightly irregular. Below it, there is an area of increased echogenicity with some tissue-like texture beginning to appear. Horizontal A-lines are faintly visible.

## Frame 4
Bright pleural line with multiple vertical reverberation artifacts extending below. Some coalescence of artifacts is seen centrally. A subpleural hyperechoic area is beginning to form.

## Frame 5
A distinct subpleural area of tissue-like echogenicity is now visible. The deep border is irregular, with a fragmented interface between consolidated and aerated lung.

## Frame 6
Similar to Frame 5 — subpleural tissue-like area with an irregular, jagged deep margin. The shredded border between consolidated and aerated parenchyma is becoming more apparent.

## Frame 7
Broader subpleural consolidated area with bright vertical artifacts at its margins. The tissue texture resembles liver parenchyma (hepatization). Possible punctate hyperechoic foci within.

## Frame 8
Consolidation persists with an irregular deep border. Discrete B-lines visible at the lateral edges of the consolidated region.

## Frame 9
Large subpleural consolidated zone with shredded deep margins. Some bright punctate foci within the consolidated area suggesting possible air bronchograms.

## Frame 10
The consolidated region fills much of the field of view. Tissue-like echotexture is prominent with irregular borders persisting.

---

# B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**

**Subtype: septal**

Discrete, well-spaced vertical hyperechoic artifacts are visible in multiple frames (particularly Frames 1, 4, 7, and 8), arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. They are individually identifiable with dark lung between them. These are most prominent at the margins/borders of the consolidated area.

---

# Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: shred_sign**

**Key findings:**
- **Tissue-like hepatization** is visible in Frames 5–10 as a subpleural area of solid-appearing, echogenic lung parenchyma resembling liver texture.
- **Shred sign** is the predominant pattern: The deep border between the consolidated lung and the aerated lung beneath is irregular, jagged, and "shredded" — clearly visible in Frames 5, 6, 8, and 9.
- **Possible air bronchograms**: Punctate hyperechoic foci are suggested within the consolidated region in Frames 7 and 9, though the shred sign dominates.

---

# Summary

| Finding | Result | Subtype |
|---------|--------|---------|
| B-lines | **True** | **Septal** |
| Consolidation | **True** | **Shred sign** |

This pattern is consistent with early/peripheral alveolar consolidation (e.g., pneumonia, ARDS) with discrete interstitial B-lines at the margins of the consolidation zone, and a characteristic irregular ("shredded") deep border indicating partial aeration loss in the anterior lung zone.
