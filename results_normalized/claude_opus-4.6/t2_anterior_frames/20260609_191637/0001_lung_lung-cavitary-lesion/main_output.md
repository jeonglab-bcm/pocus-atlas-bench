## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1–5:** The pleural line is identifiable in the superficial field. Below it, there is a hypoechoic, tissue-like (hepatized) region with scattered punctate and linear hyperechoic foci within it, consistent with air bronchograms. The deep border of this consolidated region appears somewhat irregular.

**Frames 6–8:** The consolidated area remains visible, with prominent linear hyperechoic structures (branching air bronchograms) becoming more apparent within the hepatized parenchyma. The tissue-like echotexture is clearly distinct from normal aerated lung.

**Frames 9–10:** The consolidation continues to be evident. Adjacent to the consolidated region, some discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) can be seen extending from the pleural line into the deeper field, likely arising from partially aerated lung at the margins of the consolidation. A-line-like reverberation artifacts are also visible in the deeper, more aerated portions.

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** In several frames, discrete vertical hyperechoic lines are visible arising from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the image, particularly at the borders between consolidated and aerated lung. These are well-spaced with dark lung between them.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal** (discrete, well-spaced B-lines at the periphery of the consolidation, suggesting interstitial involvement adjacent to the consolidated region)

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** Across all frames, there is a clearly hepatized (tissue-like, liver-echotexture) subpleural region in the left anterior lung zone. Within this consolidated parenchyma, punctate and linear hyperechoic foci are visible, representing air-filled bronchi (air bronchograms). Some frames also show an irregular, shredded deep border (shred sign) between the consolidated and aerated lung.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "air_bronchogram"** (predominant finding: hyperechoic foci/linear structures within hepatized lung tissue)
