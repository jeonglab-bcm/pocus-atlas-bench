# 0133_lung_jr_subpleuralconsolidation

# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: RT Lung Upper (Anterior Zone)

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1–3:** The chest wall layers and pleural line are visible. Below the pleural line, there is heterogeneous, tissue-like echotexture with multiple punctate hyperechoic foci scattered within it. The deep border appears irregular in some regions. A few discrete vertical artifacts are noted at the margins.

**Frames 4–6:** A well-defined area of subpleural consolidation becomes more prominent. The lung parenchyma appears solid and liver-like (hepatized). Within this hepatized tissue, multiple bright punctate and linear hyperechoic structures are visible, consistent with air bronchograms. An anechoic region (possibly small effusion or aerated lung interface) is seen deep to the consolidation.

**Frames 7–10:** The consolidation remains the dominant finding. The shredded, irregular deep border (shred sign) is visible in some frames where consolidated tissue transitions to aerated lung. Air bronchograms remain prominent throughout. Some discrete vertical artifacts (B-lines) are seen at the periphery of the consolidated area where partially aerated lung exists.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

At the margins of the consolidation, discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend downward, consistent with septal B-lines. These are well-spaced and clearly separated, indicating interstitial involvement in the lung adjacent to the consolidated region. They are not confluent/coalescent.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: air_bronchogram**

### Key Findings:
1. **Hepatization:** The subpleural lung parenchyma has a solid, tissue-like appearance resembling liver echogenicity, indicating complete loss of aeration.
2. **Air bronchograms:** Multiple punctate and linear hyperechoic foci are clearly visible within the hepatized lung throughout the clip — the predominant feature.
3. **Shred sign:** An irregular, shredded deep margin is visible in several frames where consolidated lung transitions to aerated lung.

---

## Summary

| Finding | Result | Subtype |
|---------|--------|---------|
| B-lines | **True** | Septal |
| Consolidation | **True** | Air bronchogram |

This pattern is consistent with **pneumonia or ARDS** with established alveolar consolidation containing air bronchograms in the right upper anterior zone, with surrounding interstitial involvement (septal B-lines at margins).
