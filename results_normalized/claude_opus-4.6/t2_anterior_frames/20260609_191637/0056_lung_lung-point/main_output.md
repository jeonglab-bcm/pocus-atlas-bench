# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Description

**Frames 1-3:** The superficial soft tissue layers and a bright pleural line are visible at ~1 cm depth. On the left, a rib creates posterior acoustic shadowing. Below the pleural line, there is a sub-pleural area of tissue-like echogenicity (not normally aerated lung). Some hyperechoic punctate foci are noted within this area.

**Frames 4-6:** The pattern continues with a small sub-pleural consolidated region becoming more apparent centrally. The echogenicity resembles solid tissue (hepatized lung). Scattered bright foci within the consolidated zone become more conspicuous — consistent with trapped air in bronchi.

**Frames 7-8:** A prominent vertical hyperechoic artifact extends downward from the pleural line/edge of consolidation. This discrete vertical artifact is consistent with a B-line arising from the boundary of aerated and non-aerated lung. The consolidation with internal bright foci persists.

**Frames 9-10:** The sub-pleural consolidation remains visible with its irregular deep border and internal punctate hyperechoic foci. Some discrete vertical artifacts are still seen at the margin of consolidated tissue.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines = true**

**Subtype: septal**

Discrete, well-separated vertical hyperechoic artifacts are visible in several frames (particularly frames 7-8), arising from the pleural line and extending downward. They are few in number and clearly separated, consistent with a septal B-line pattern. These likely arise at the interface between consolidated and aerated lung parenchyma.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: air_bronchogram**

Key findings supporting consolidation:
- **Hepatization:** A sub-pleural region of tissue-like echogenicity resembling liver parenchyma is present below the pleural line
- **Air bronchograms:** Punctate and short linear hyperechoic foci are visible *within* the hepatized tissue, representing air-filled bronchi surrounded by non-aerated lung
- **Irregular border (shred sign):** The deep margin of the consolidation is somewhat irregular/shredded against the deeper aerated lung

The **predominant** finding is **air bronchograms** within hepatized lung tissue, making this the primary consolidation type classification.

---

## Summary

| Finding | Result | Subtype |
|---------|--------|---------|
| B-lines | **Present** | Septal |
| Consolidation | **Present** | Air bronchogram |

This pattern is consistent with a small anterior sub-pleural consolidation (e.g., pneumonia, atelectasis, or early ARDS) with air bronchograms and associated septal B-lines at the margins of the consolidated zone.
