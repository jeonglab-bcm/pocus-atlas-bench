# Summary of Findings

## B-lines Assessment

Across multiple frames (1, 4, 5, 10), discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts are visible arising from the pleural line. These are well-spaced with dark lung visible between them, not confluent. They appear predominantly at the margins of consolidated areas.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal** (discrete, well-spaced B-lines, ≤3 per intercostal space visible between zones of consolidation)

## Consolidation Assessment

Multiple frames (especially 6, 7, 8, 9) demonstrate:
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: Subpleural lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic
- **Shred sign**: An irregular, fragmented deep border between consolidated and aerated lung is consistently visible
- **Air bronchograms**: Scattered punctate hyperechoic foci within the hepatized region (frames 4, 7)

The predominant and most consistent feature is the **irregular shredded deep border**.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "shred_sign"** (predominantly irregular, fragmented deep border between consolidated subpleural lung and deeper aerated lung)
