# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Left Lung, Anterior Zone

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1–3:** A bright, well-defined pleural line is visible. Below it, I observe discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending toward the bottom of the screen. A-lines (horizontal reverberation artifacts) are partially visible but intermittently disrupted by these vertical artifacts. There is subtle sliding movement between frames.

**Frames 4–6:** The pleural line remains clearly delineated. A few discrete vertical artifacts (B-lines) are seen arising from the pleural line, extending downward. Dark lung parenchyma is visible between these lines. No tissue-like echotexture is observed below the pleural line.

**Frames 7–8:** Similar findings persist. The B-lines remain discrete and well-spaced. A-lines are still partially visible in the intercostal window. No sub-pleural consolidation or hepatization is identified.

**Frames 9–10:** The pattern is consistent with prior frames. Discrete vertical artifacts continue, with preserved dark spaces between them. No shred sign or air bronchograms are seen.

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Discrete, well-spaced hyperechoic vertical artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the far field without fading. Dark lung parenchyma is clearly visible between individual B-lines. A-lines are partially disrupted but not completely obscured. The B-lines move with respiration, consistent with true B-lines.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

These are discrete, well-separated B-lines (≤3 per intercostal space in most frames), consistent with thickened interlobular septa. They do not coalesce into a confluent "white lung" pattern.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** Below the pleural line, there is no tissue-like (hepatized) echotexture resembling liver parenchyma. No irregular, shredded deep borders (shred sign) are identified. No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within solid-appearing lung tissue (air bronchograms) are seen. The sub-pleural region maintains an aerated appearance throughout all frames.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
