# Frame-by-Frame Analysis

## Individual Frame Observations:

**Frames 1–3:** The superficial soft tissue layers (skin, subcutaneous fat, muscle) are visible at the top. The pleural line appears as a bright hyperechoic horizontal line. Just below/at the pleural line, a single well-circumscribed, oval, anechoic structure is visible. The deeper field shows horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines). No vertical artifacts extending to the bottom of the screen.

**Frames 4–6:** Similar appearance. The oval anechoic structure remains visible and well-defined near the pleural line. It has smooth borders, no internal echoes, and no irregular margins. A-lines persist in the far field.

**Frames 7–8:** As the probe sweeps, **two** distinct rounded anechoic structures become visible, strongly suggesting **vascular structures** (likely intercostal or internal mammary vessels seen in cross-section). The deeper lung field maintains an A-line pattern.

**Frames 9–10:** The two rounded structures persist. Surrounding tissue has normal echogenicity. No tissue-like (hepatized) lung parenchyma visible. A-lines remain in the far field.

---

## B-Lines Assessment

**Observations:**
- No vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line
- No artifacts extending to the bottom of the screen
- Horizontal A-lines (reverberation artifacts) dominate the deeper field across all frames
- The pleural line is well-visualized and smooth

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = false**

No B-lines are present. The A-line pattern indicates normal lung aeration at this zone.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observations:**
- The anechoic round structures are **completely echo-free** internally — not tissue-like/hepatized
- **No shred sign**: No irregular, shredded border between consolidated and aerated lung
- **No air bronchograms**: No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within the structures
- The structures are well-circumscribed with smooth walls, consistent with **blood vessels** (confirmed by the appearance of two adjacent round structures in later frames, typical of artery-vein pairs)
- No solid, liver-like parenchymal echotexture is seen

**Conclusion: consolidation = false**
**consolidation_type = null**
