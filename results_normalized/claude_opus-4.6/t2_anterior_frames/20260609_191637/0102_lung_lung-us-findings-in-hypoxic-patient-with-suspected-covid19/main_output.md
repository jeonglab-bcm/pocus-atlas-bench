## B-lines Assessment

**Observations:** Across all 10 sampled frames, the predominant pattern is the pleural line followed by horizontal A-line reverberation artifacts and a dark (anechoic) subpleural field. While isolated faint vertical streaks are noted in frames 5 and 14, they do not meet full B-line criteria (they fade before reaching the screen bottom and are not laser-like or clearly erasing A-lines).

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **subtype = null**

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observations:** No tissue-like hepatization is seen in any frame. The subpleural space remains air-filled (dark) throughout the clip. There is no shred sign (irregular deep border between consolidated and aerated lung). No air bronchograms (punctate hyperechoic foci within hepatized tissue) are identified. The lung parenchyma appears normally aerated.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
