# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-10:** Each frame demonstrates consistent findings:
- **Superficial layers:** Subcutaneous tissue and chest wall musculature are clearly delineated in the near field
- **Pleural line:** A bright, hyperechoic horizontal line is visible at approximately 1 cm depth
- **Rib shadows:** Dark, rounded acoustic shadows (ribs) are seen on the left side of the field, creating an intercostal window
- **Sub-pleural space:** Multiple parallel, evenly-spaced horizontal hyperechoic lines are present below the pleural line — these are classic **A-lines** (reverberation artifacts)
- **Lung sliding:** Subtle variations between frames at the pleural line suggest preserved lung sliding
- **No vertical artifacts** extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen are identified in any frame

## B-lines Assessment

**Observations:** All 10 frames demonstrate a dominant **A-line pattern**. The horizontal reverberation artifacts are clearly visible, evenly spaced, and progressively attenuating with depth. No hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen are present.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **Subtype = null**

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observations:** There is no tissue-like (hepatized) parenchyma visible below the pleural line. No irregular shredded borders between consolidated and aerated lung are identified. No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci suggesting air bronchograms are present. The lung surface maintains its normal highly reflective interface.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

## Summary

This is a **normal anterior lung ultrasound** demonstrating an A-line pattern with preserved lung sliding, consistent with normal lung aeration. No pathologic B-lines or consolidation are identified.
