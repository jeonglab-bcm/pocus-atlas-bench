# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is clearly identified as a bright hyperechoic horizontal line at the top of the image. Two to three discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen. Dark lung parenchyma is clearly visible between these artifacts. No tissue-like pattern is seen below the pleural line.

**Frames 4-6:** The same pattern persists. The B-lines remain discrete and well-separated. They move slightly with respiration, consistent with lung sliding. The spaces between the B-lines remain dark (anechoic), and A-lines are not clearly dominant but are partially obscured in the regions between B-lines.

**Frames 7-8:** Consistent pattern continues — approximately 2-3 well-defined vertical artifacts extending from the pleural line to the far field without fading. No coalescence or merging of the B-lines is observed.

**Frames 9-10:** Same findings. The B-lines remain discrete and individually countable. No consolidation pattern, no shred sign, and no air bronchograms are visualized in any frame.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

**Rationale:** Throughout all 10 frames, 2-3 discrete, well-spaced B-lines are consistently present, arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading. Dark lung parenchyma is clearly visible between each B-line. They do not coalesce or form a white-out pattern. This is consistent with the **septal** pattern, suggesting thickened interlobular septa (e.g., early interstitial edema or mild interstitial disease).

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = false**

**consolidation_type = null**

**Rationale:** No tissue-like hepatization, no irregular shredded deep border (shred sign), and no punctate/linear hyperechoic foci (air bronchograms) are identified in any frame. The subpleural lung does not demonstrate solid organ-like echotexture. The lung parenchyma, aside from the B-line artifacts, appears normally aerated.
