# Frame-by-Frame Analysis

## Frame 1-2:
A bright pleural line is visible at ~1.5 cm depth. Below it, a broad hyperechoic vertical structure extends downward to 5-6 cm. Multiple bright foci are scattered within. Dark (anechoic) areas flank the structure. This appears to represent consolidated lung tissue with embedded bright artifacts.

## Frame 3:
The pleural line is visible with tissue-like (hepatized) lung beneath it. An irregular border between consolidated and aerated lung is seen. Some bright linear echoes are present within the solid-appearing tissue.

## Frames 4-5:
Clear consolidation is evident — the lung parenchyma appears solid and gray/echogenic (liver-like texture) from the pleural line extending to ~4-5 cm. Punctate and linear hyperechoic foci are visible within this hepatized tissue, consistent with **air bronchograms**.

## Frames 6-7:
The most striking frames — a large area of hepatized lung tissue is visible with very prominent **bright punctate/rounded foci** at ~3-4 cm depth within the solidified lung. These represent air bronchograms within completely consolidated parenchyma.

## Frames 8-9:
Transitional pattern — irregular bright vertical artifacts extend from the pleural line downward, with shredded borders between consolidated and partially aerated lung. Some confluent vertical artifacts (B-lines) are visible at the consolidation margins.

---

# B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines = true**

In the transitional zones (frames 1-2, 8-9), confluent vertical hyperechoic artifacts extend from the pleural line to the screen bottom, arising at the margins of the consolidation. These coalesce into a diffuse bright pattern without clearly separated individual lines.

**Subtype: ground_glass** — The B-lines are confluent and coalescing rather than discrete and well-separated, creating a "white lung" appearance in the transition zones between consolidated and aerated lung.

---

# Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

Key findings:
- **Hepatization**: Frames 3-7 demonstrate solid, tissue-like lung parenchyma with echogenicity resembling liver
- **Air bronchograms**: Frames 4-7 show prominent punctate and linear hyperechoic foci within the hepatized tissue, representing air-filled bronchi surrounded by consolidated alveoli
- **Shred sign elements**: Frames 8-9 show irregular, shredded borders between consolidated and aerated regions

**Consolidation type: air_bronchogram** — The predominant and most striking feature across multiple frames is the presence of bright punctate/linear hyperechoic foci (air-filled bronchi) embedded within hepatized lung parenchyma, particularly prominent in frames 5-7.
