# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Anterior Zone

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1–4:** A bright, well-defined pleural line is visible at the top of the image. Below it, the lung field appears predominantly dark (anechoic) with clearly visible **horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines)** repeating at regular intervals equidistant from the pleural line. No vertical artifacts are identified extending toward the bottom of the screen.

**Frames 5–7:** The pleural line remains bright and smooth. There is subtle increased echogenicity immediately below the pleural line in some frames, but no discrete vertical artifacts extending to the far field. A-lines remain the dominant finding.

**Frames 8–10:** A-lines continue to dominate. The pleural line shows normal sliding (evidenced by slight changes in its appearance between frames). The lung field below remains dark with no B-lines or tissue-like patterns.

## B-lines Assessment

The A-line dominant pattern indicates normal lung aeration at this anterior zone.
