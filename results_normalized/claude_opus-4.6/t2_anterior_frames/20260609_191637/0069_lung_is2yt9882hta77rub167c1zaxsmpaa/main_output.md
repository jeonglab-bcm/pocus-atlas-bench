# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Description

**Frames 1–2:** Superficial soft tissue layers are visible with a clearly identified pleural line (hyperechoic horizontal line). Below the pleural line, relatively normal-appearing lung is seen with some horizontal reverberation (A-line) artifacts and minimal vertical artifacts.

**Frames 3–5:** The probe appears to cross over a rib. A bright hyperechoic curved cortical surface (rib) is seen with a dark acoustic shadow beneath it. Some irregular echogenicity is visible near the pleural line at the intercostal margins.

**Frames 6–10:** As the probe moves to an intercostal window, very wide, bright, vertical hyperechoic artifacts are seen arising from the pleural line and extending without fading all the way to the bottom of the screen. The artifacts shift slightly with respiration, consistent with lung sliding.

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Frames 6–10 demonstrate very prominent, wide, coalescing vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending uninterrupted to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts merge into a near-continuous bright "white sheet," obliterating any A-line reverberation pattern. They move with lung sliding.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = ground_glass**

The confluent, coalescing nature of the B-lines, which merge into a diffuse bright curtain obscuring all A-lines, is characteristic of the ground_glass (white lung) pattern, indicating significant alveolar-interstitial syndrome (e.g., pulmonary edema or diffuse alveolar disease).

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** There is no tissue-like hepatized lung parenchyma resembling liver texture. No irregular "shredded" deep border (shred sign) is identified. No punctate or linear hyperechoic air bronchograms are seen within the lung parenchyma. The bright structures in frames 3–5 are consistent with rib cortex and acoustic shadowing rather than consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
