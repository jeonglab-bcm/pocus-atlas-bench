# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: LT Lung Zone 2

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is identified at the top of the image with rib shadows bilaterally. Below the pleural line, there is a region of tissue-like (hepatized) echotexture. Multiple hyperechoic punctate and short linear foci are visible within this tissue. Vertical hyperechoic artifacts extend toward the bottom of the screen.

**Frames 4-6:** The consolidation pattern persists with clearly visible bright echogenic dots within the hepatized parenchyma (air bronchograms). B-lines continue to be seen, appearing confluent and coalescing in several regions.

**Frames 7-8:** Consistent findings with prior frames. The tissue-like parenchyma remains visible with scattered hyperechoic foci. B-lines are confluent in areas.

**Frames 9-10:** The consolidated tissue remains clearly visible with air bronchograms. The deep border of the consolidation appears somewhat irregular (possible shred sign). Confluent B-lines extend from the transition zone.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: ground_glass**

Multiple B-lines are present that coalesce and merge, creating a diffusely bright appearance below the pleural line. These are particularly visible at the transition between consolidated and aerated lung, creating a near-confluent white-out pattern in several zones.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: air_bronchogram**

Key findings:
- **Hepatization:** Lung parenchyma appears solid and tissue-like, resembling liver echotexture
- **Air bronchograms (predominant):** Multiple punctate and linear hyperechoic foci are clearly visible within the consolidated/hepatized lung, representing air-filled bronchi surrounded by non-aerated tissue
- **Shred sign:** An irregular, shredded deep border is visible in some frames at the boundary between consolidated and aerated lung

---
