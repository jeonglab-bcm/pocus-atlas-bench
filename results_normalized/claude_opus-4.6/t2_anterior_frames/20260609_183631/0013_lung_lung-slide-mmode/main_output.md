# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Anterior Zone

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-2 (B-mode + early M-mode):**
- B-mode shows well-defined pleural line at ~1.5-2 cm depth
- Horizontal A-line reverberation artifacts visible below the pleural line
- No vertical artifacts extending to screen bottom
- M-mode beginning to form; some granularity below pleural line

**Frames 3-6 (M-mode developing):**
- M-mode demonstrates the classic **"seashore sign"**: horizontal/stratified lines above the pleural line (chest wall = static), and a granular/sandy pattern below (lung parenchyma = moving)
- This confirms normal lung sliding
- B-mode consistently shows A-lines with no vertical artifacts

**Frames 7-10 (fully developed M-mode):**
- Mature M-mode confirms persistent seashore sign throughout the clip
- B-mode continues to show A-line dominant pattern
- A bright hyperechoic band at the bottom of the image represents A-line reverberations at depth, NOT consolidation
- No vertical laser-like artifacts arising from the pleural line

## Assessment Summary

### B-lines Assessment
**Observations:** Across all 10 frames, the lung field below the pleural line is dominated by **horizontal A-lines** (reverberation artifacts parallel to the pleural line). No hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the screen bottom are identified. The M-mode seashore sign confirms normal lung sliding.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **subtype = null**

### Consolidation Assessment
**Observations:** No tissue-like hepatization (no solid, liver-echogenic parenchyma) is seen. No shred sign (irregular border between consolidated and aerated lung) is present. No air bronchograms (punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within solid tissue) are identified. The bright areas at depth represent normal A-line reverberations.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

## Final Interpretation
This represents a **normal anterior lung zone** with an A-line predominant pattern and preserved lung sliding (seashore sign on M-mode), indicating normal lung aeration without interstitial syndrome or alveolar consolidation.
