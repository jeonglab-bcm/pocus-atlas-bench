## B-lines Assessment

**Observations:**
- Multiple frames (4, 8, 9, 10) demonstrate vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the screen
- These B-lines are discrete and individually identifiable in most frames
- Dark, aerated lung parenchyma is visible between the B-lines
- They appear to move with respiration across sequential frames
- Frame 4 shows a wider/brighter artifact, but overall the pattern is of separated discrete lines

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

The discrete, well-spaced B-lines with visible dark lung between them indicate interlobular septal thickening (septal rocket pattern).

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observations:**
- No tissue-like hepatization pattern identified in any frame
- No irregular "shredded" deep borders between consolidated and aerated lung
- No definitive punctate or linear hyperechoic air bronchograms within hepatized tissue
- Frame 2 shows some rounded hyperechoic foci but these are not definitively tissue-like consolidation; they may represent focal artifacts or very small subpleural consolidations but lack confirmatory features
- The lung parenchyma does not demonstrate solid organ-like appearance in any frame

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
