# Conclusions

## B-lines Assessment
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype: ground_glass**

In the transition zones between consolidated and aerated lung (particularly Frames 1-2 and 8-9), confluent vertical hyperechoic artifacts are present, coalescing into a diffuse bright sheet rather than being discrete and well-spaced.

## Consolidation Assessment
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "air_bronchogram"**

Frames 4–7 demonstrate clear **hepatization** (tissue-like, liver-resembling echogenicity) with **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** within the consolidated parenchyma, representing air-filled bronchi (air bronchograms). An irregular shredded deep border is also noted in some frames, but the predominant and most conspicuous finding is air bronchograms within hepatized lung.
