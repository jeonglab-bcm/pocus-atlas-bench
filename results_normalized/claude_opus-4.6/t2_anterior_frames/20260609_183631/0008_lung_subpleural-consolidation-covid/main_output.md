## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines are PRESENT (lung_rockets = true)**

Multiple bright, vertical, well-defined artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. They are clearly countable (approximately 2–3 per intercostal space) with **visible dark lung between them**.

**Subtype: Septal**

The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced with dark aerated parenchyma visible in between. This is consistent with thickened interlobular septa (early interstitial syndrome), rather than confluent/ground-glass coalescence.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: Consolidation is ABSENT (consolidation = false)**

- No tissue-like hepatization (no liver-echogenicity parenchyma) is seen.
- No shred sign (no irregular shredded deep border).
- No air bronchograms (no punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within solid tissue).

The dark areas visible are consistent with aerated lung between B-lines and rib shadowing, not alveolar consolidation.

**Consolidation type: null**
