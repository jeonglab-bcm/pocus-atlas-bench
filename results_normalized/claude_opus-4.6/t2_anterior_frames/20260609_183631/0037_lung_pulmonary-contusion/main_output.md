## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-10:** Across all sequential frames, I observe:
- **Superficial structures:** Chest wall soft tissue layers visible at the top
- **Ribs:** Two rib shadows creating dark acoustic shadows bilaterally
- **Pleural line:** A bright, hyperechoic horizontal line visible between the rib shadows
- **Subpleural space:** Multiple vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading
- **A-lines:** Largely obscured/absent due to the vertical artifacts
- **Lung sliding:** The slight variation between frames suggests preserved lung sliding at the pleural interface

The vertical artifacts are consistent across all frames, appearing as **discrete, well-separated laser-like lines** with dark (anechoic) lung parenchyma visible between them. I count approximately **2-3 B-lines per intercostal space**.

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

**Rationale:** The B-lines are:
- Clearly discrete and individually countable
- Well-spaced with dark lung visible between them
- Arising from the pleural line and extending to the far field without fading
- Moving synchronously (consistent across frames suggesting lung sliding)
- Not confluent or coalescing into a "white lung" appearance

This pattern is consistent with **thickened interlobular septa** (septal B-lines), which may be seen in early interstitial edema or mild interstitial lung disease.
