## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Throughout all 10 frames, multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts arise from the pleural line, extend to the bottom of the screen without fading, and appear to move with lung sliding. They are discrete and well-separated, with dark (aerated) lung parenchyma clearly visible between them. A-lines are not visible (erased by the B-lines).

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

The B-lines are individually identifiable and well-spaced, consistent with thickened interlobular septa (septal rockets), rather than confluent/coalescing ground-glass pattern.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** Below the pleural line, there is no tissue-like hepatized region. No solid, liver-like echogenicity is seen. No irregular shredded deep border is identified. No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci (air bronchograms) are present within the subpleural space. The artifacts below the pleural line are consistent with B-lines rather than consolidated parenchyma.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
