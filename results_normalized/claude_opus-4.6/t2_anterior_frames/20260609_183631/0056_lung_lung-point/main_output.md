# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1–3:** The pleural line is visible as a hyperechoic horizontal line at ~1 cm depth. A dark rib shadow occupies the left portion. Below the pleural line, I see discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending downward. Some A-line reverberation is faintly visible laterally.

**Frames 4–6:** The pleural line remains well-defined. Discrete vertical bright artifacts (1–2) arise from the pleural line and extend toward the bottom of the image. Dark lung parenchyma is visible between these artifacts. No tissue-like echotexture is identified below the pleural line.

**Frames 7–8:** A very prominent, well-defined vertical hyperechoic artifact arises from the pleural line and extends deep into the image — a classic B-line. It is laser-like and does not fade. Dark lung is clearly visible on either side.

**Frames 9–10:** Bright clustered foci are seen at the pleural level, but these represent the origin points of B-lines rather than consolidated tissue. The vertical artifacts persist. No hepatized tissue or shred sign is identified.

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Discrete, well-defined vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the far field without fading. They move with respiration. Dark, normally aerated lung is clearly visible between individual B-lines. The number per intercostal space appears to be approximately 2–3.

### Conclusion:
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

Rationale: The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced, with dark lung parenchyma clearly visible between them. They do not coalesce into a white-out/ground-glass pattern.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** No tissue-like hepatization pattern is identified below the pleural line. There is no irregular "shredded" deep border (shred sign). No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within a hepatized parenchymal background are seen. The sub-pleural lung maintains its normally aerated, dark appearance aside from the B-line artifacts.

### Conclusion:
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
