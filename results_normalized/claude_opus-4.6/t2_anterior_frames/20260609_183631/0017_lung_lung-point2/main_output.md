# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### B-mode (upper portion of each frame):
- **Frames 1-4**: Clear pleural line visible at approximately 1–1.5 cm depth. Below the pleural line, horizontal **A-lines** (reverberation artifacts) are seen repeating at regular intervals. No vertical artifacts extending to the bottom of the screen.
- **Frames 5-7**: Similar appearance with a well-defined bright pleural line. A-lines remain the dominant sub-pleural pattern. No hyperechoic vertical lines (B-lines) identified.
- **Frames 8-10**: Slight changes in brightness due to respiratory motion, but the fundamental pattern remains A-line dominant. No tissue-like echotexture or irregular borders suggestive of consolidation.

### M-mode (lower portion of each frame):
- **Superficial zone (0–~1 cm)**: Parallel horizontal lines representing the stationary chest wall ("stratosphere" portion).
- **Pleural line (~1 cm)**: Bright hyperechoic line.
- **Deep to pleural line (~1–3.5 cm)**: A combination of horizontal lines transitioning into a granular/sandy pattern with periodic dark columns synchronous with cardiac activity. This represents a **seashore sign with lung pulse**, confirming visceral-parietal pleural apposition and lung sliding.
