# Lung Ultrasound Frame-by-Frame Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1-2:** The pleural line is clearly identified as a bright hyperechoic horizontal line at approximately 1.5-2 cm depth. Superficial to this, I see well-defined tissue layers (skin, subcutaneous fat, intercostal muscles/fascia). Below the pleural line, I observe horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) at regular, equidistant intervals. A curved hyperechoic structure (rib) is seen at the deep margin with posterior acoustic shadowing.

**Frames 3-4:** Similar appearance. The pleural line remains well-defined. A-lines continue to dominate the subpleural space. No vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line are identified.

**Frames 5-6:** Slight variation in brightness due to respiratory motion (suggesting lung sliding is present). The A-line pattern persists. No discrete or confluent vertical hyperechoic lines extending to the screen's edge are seen.

**Frames 7-8:** Consistent with prior frames. A-line pattern remains dominant. The lung parenchyma below the pleural line appears dark/anechoic with only reverberation artifacts.

**Frames 9-10:** No change in pattern. A-lines are clearly visible, equidistant, and repeating. No tissue-like echotexture or irregularity at or below the pleural line.
