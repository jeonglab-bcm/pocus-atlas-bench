# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: RT H/T Zone 3 Low

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is visible between rib shadows (dark vertical bands). Multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend toward the bottom of the screen. These are discrete and well-separated with dark lung parenchyma visible between them. Some increased subpleural echogenicity is noted, particularly on the right lateral aspect.

**Frames 4-6:** The pattern continues with clearly defined B-lines between rib shadows. In frame 6, there is a more evident tissue-like area in the subpleural region, particularly to the right of center, with an irregular, "shredded" deep margin transitioning into aerated lung.

**Frames 7-9:** Discrete B-lines remain visible. The subpleural tissue-like echogenicity persists with irregular borders.

**Frame 10:** Shows the clearest evidence of subpleural consolidation — a tissue-like, hepatized area with an irregular, fragmented deep border (shred sign) adjacent to aerated lung.

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines = TRUE**

**Subtype: Septal**

Multiple discrete B-lines are visible in each intercostal space, arising from the pleural line and extending to the far field. They remain well-separated with dark lung parenchyma between them, consistent with a **septal** pattern (thickened interlobular septa). They do not coalesce into a confluent "white lung" sheet.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: Consolidation = TRUE**

**Type: Shred Sign**

In multiple frames (particularly 6 and 10), there is a subpleural area of tissue-like echogenicity with an **irregular, shredded deep border** where consolidated lung transitions into aerated lung. This irregular boundary is characteristic of the shred sign, indicating non-translobar alveolar consolidation in this dependent lower zone.
