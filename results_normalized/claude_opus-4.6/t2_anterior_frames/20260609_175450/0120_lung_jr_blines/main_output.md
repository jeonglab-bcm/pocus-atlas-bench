## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Multiple (≥3 per intercostal space) hyperechoic vertical artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. They move synchronously with lung sliding. Dark lung parenchyma remains visible between individual B-lines throughout most frames, though they are numerous.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

The B-lines are discrete and individually identifiable with dark intervals between them, consistent with thickened interlobular septa (interstitial syndrome). They do not coalesce into a diffuse white sheet characteristic of ground glass pattern.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** No tissue-like hepatization is seen below the pleural line. There is no irregular shredded deep border (shred sign). No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within solid-appearing lung parenchyma (air bronchograms) are identified. The sub-pleural pattern is dominated by B-line artifacts rather than solid tissue echotexture.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
