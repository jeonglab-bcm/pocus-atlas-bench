# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1–3:** The pleural line is visible at approximately 2–3 cm depth. Below the pleural line, there is a hyperechoic subpleural region with bright vertical artifacts extending toward the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are numerous and coalesce, creating a diffusely bright ("white lung") appearance that obliterates any A-line pattern.

**Frames 4–5:** The most striking frames. There is a clearly hepatized (tissue-like, echogenic) region immediately below the pleural line extending several centimeters deep. Within this consolidated zone, multiple punctate and linear hyperechoic foci are visible — these represent **air bronchograms** within non-aerated lung. The deep border of the consolidation is somewhat irregular.

**Frames 6:** Similar to earlier frames with confluent B-lines arising from the pleural line and extending deep, with a small subpleural hypoechoic consolidated region visible.

**Frames 7–8:** More lateral/tangential view. The pleural line appears irregular. Subpleural consolidation is less prominent here, but there are still hyperechoic areas along the pleural surface.

**Frames 9–10:** Show a wider field of view. A hyperechoic rounded structure is visible (~5–6 cm depth) surrounded by B-line artifacts. The overall pattern is one of reduced aeration with scattered bright artifacts.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: ground_glass**

Multiple confluent B-lines are visible in the majority of frames, merging into a diffuse white sheet that obscures any A-line pattern. The lung parenchyma below the pleural line appears uniformly bright in most frames, consistent with coalescent B-lines (ground glass pattern), indicating severe interstitial-alveolar disease.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: air_bronchogram**

Frames 4–5 demonstrate clear alveolar consolidation with:
- **Hepatization**: Tissue-like echogenicity of subpleural lung parenchyma resembling liver texture
- **Air bronchograms**: Multiple punctate hyperechoic foci within the hepatized lung, representing residual air within bronchi surrounded by non-aerated lung
- The deep border shows some irregularity (shred sign component), but the dominant finding is the air bronchograms within hepatized tissue

---

## Summary

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| B-lines (lung rockets) | **True** |
| B-line subtype | **Ground glass** |
| Consolidation | **True** |
| Consolidation type | **Air bronchogram** |

This pattern is consistent with severe loss of lung aeration, as seen in ARDS, pneumonia, or severe pulmonary edema with anterior consolidation.
