These are laser-like, narrow, and move with the pleural line. There are approximately 1–2 B-lines per intercostal space visible, with clearly dark (aerated) lung parenchyma between them. A-lines are not dominant.

**Conclusion:**

## Consolidation Assessment

No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within a consolidated region (air bronchograms) are present. The subpleural lung maintains an aerated pattern with artifact generation.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
