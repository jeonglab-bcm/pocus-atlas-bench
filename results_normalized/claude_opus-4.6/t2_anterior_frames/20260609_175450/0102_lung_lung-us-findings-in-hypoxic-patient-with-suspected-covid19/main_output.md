## B-lines Assessment

**Observations:** Across all 10 sampled frames, the predominant pattern is a dark lung field below the pleural line with horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) at regular intervals. One frame (5/51) shows a faint vertical streak centrally, but it does not clearly extend to the bottom of the screen uninterrupted, and it is not reproduced consistently across subsequent frames. This does not meet the criteria for true B-lines.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **subtype = null**

The A-line pattern dominates, consistent with normal lung aeration in this anterior zone.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observations:** Across all frames, there is no tissue-like hepatization (no solid, liver-like echotexture). No shred sign (no irregular deep border between consolidated and aerated lung). No air bronchograms (no punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within hepatized tissue). The lung parenchyma below the pleural line remains normally aerated throughout the clip.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
