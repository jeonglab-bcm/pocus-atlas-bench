# Lung Ultrasound Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Assessment

**Frames 1-2 (early):**
- **B-mode (bottom):** The pleural line is visible at approximately 1.5-2 cm depth. Below it, horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) are clearly seen at regular intervals. No vertical artifacts are identified.
- **M-mode (top):** Shows horizontal parallel lines above the pleural line (stratosphere) transitioning to a granular "sandy" pattern below — classic **seashore sign** confirming lung sliding.

**Frames 3-6 (middle):**
- **B-mode:** The pleural line remains well-defined. A-lines continue to dominate. The bright hyperechoic structure at the bottom of some frames represents deeper tissue/reverberation, not B-lines (they don't arise from the pleural line as discrete vertical rays).
- **M-mode:** Seashore sign persists with consistent granularity below the pleural line, confirming ongoing lung sliding throughout.

**Frames 7-10 (late):**
- **B-mode:** Same A-line dominant pattern. No vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending uninterrupted from pleural line to screen bottom.
- **M-mode:** Stable seashore sign throughout.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** All frames demonstrate a predominant **A-line pattern** with horizontal reverberation artifacts at regular intervals below the pleural line. No vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen are identified in any frame.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **subtype = null**

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** The lung parenchyma below the pleural line shows normal aeration artifacts (A-lines). There is no tissue-like hepatization, no irregular shredded deep borders (shred sign), and no punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within solid-appearing lung tissue.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

---

## Summary

| Finding | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Lung sliding | Present (seashore sign on M-mode) |
| B-lines | **Absent** |
| A-lines | Present and dominant |

**Interpretation:** This is a **normal anterior lung ultrasound** (A-profile). The presence of lung sliding with A-lines and absence of B-lines or consolidation indicates normally aerated lung parenchyma in this zone.
