## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**

Vertical hyperechoic artifacts are identified, most prominently in Frame 7 (L POST), arising from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the image. These are discrete and spaced, not confluent.

**Subtype: Septal**

The B-lines are discrete and well-separated with dark lung visible between them, consistent with a **septal** pattern. No coalescence into a diffuse white sheet is seen.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: Consolidation present (consolidation = true)**

The L POST frames (6–10) demonstrate clear evidence of subpleural consolidation:
- **Tissue-like hepatization** — echogenic, solid-appearing lung parenchyma
- **Irregular, shredded deep border** between consolidated and aerated lung (shred sign) — the dominant feature
- **Punctate hyperechoic foci** within consolidated tissue in Frames 8–9, suggestive of air bronchograms

**Consolidation Type: shred_sign**

The predominant pattern is the **shred sign** — an irregular, jagged transition zone between consolidated and aerated lung, visible across multiple sequential frames.
