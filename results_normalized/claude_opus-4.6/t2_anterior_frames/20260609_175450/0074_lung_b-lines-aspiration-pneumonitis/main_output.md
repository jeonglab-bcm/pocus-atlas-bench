## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines are PRESENT (lung_rockets = true)**

**Subtype: Septal**

**Reasoning:** Discrete, well-defined B-lines (1–3 per frame) arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. Dark lung parenchyma remains visible between individual B-lines. They do not coalesce into a confluent "white-out" pattern. This is consistent with thickened interlobular septa.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: Consolidation is PRESENT (consolidation = true)**

**Consolidation Type: Shred sign**

**Reasoning:** In multiple frames, a small subpleural area of tissue-like echogenicity is visible just below the pleural line. Its deep border is irregular and fragmented ("shredded"), transitioning abruptly into aerated lung below. There is no extensive hepatization or clear air bronchograms. This shred sign pattern indicates a small, focal area of alveolar consolidation surrounded by partially aerated lung, consistent with early or limited consolidation in the anterior zone.
