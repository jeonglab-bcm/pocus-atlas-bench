## B-lines Assessment

**Observations:** Across the clip, discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts are seen arising from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the screen. These are present in multiple frames (particularly Frames 1, 4–9). Between these vertical artifacts, dark lung parenchyma and A-lines remain visible. The B-lines do not coalesce or merge into a confluent white-out pattern. They are individually identifiable and well-spaced.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

The B-lines are discrete and well-separated with dark lung visible between them, consistent with thickened interlobular septa. They do not form a confluent ground-glass sheet.
