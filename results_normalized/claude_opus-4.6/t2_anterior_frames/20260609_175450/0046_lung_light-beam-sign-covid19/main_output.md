## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines are PRESENT (lung_rockets = true)**

Multiple frames (4, 8, 9, 10) demonstrate discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading. These are well-defined, laser-like, and clearly separated from each other with dark (aerated) lung parenchyma visible between them. They appear to move with respiration across frames.

**Subtype: Septal**

The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced, with clearly visible dark lung parenchyma between individual lines. They do not coalesce into a confluent white sheet. This pattern is consistent with thickened interlobular septa (≤3 B-lines per intercostal space).

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: Consolidation is ABSENT (consolidation = false)**

- No tissue-like hepatization is identified (no solid, liver-like parenchymal echogenicity).
- No shred sign (no irregular, shredded deep borders between consolidated and aerated lung).
- No air bronchograms (no punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within hepatized tissue).
- The small hyperechoic focus in frame 2 does not demonstrate surrounding hepatized tissue and is not consistent with consolidation.

**Consolidation type: null**
