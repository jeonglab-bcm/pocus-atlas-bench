---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines = TRUE**

**Subtype: ground_glass**

In frames 1–5, multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the far field without fading. These B-lines are numerous and coalesce in several regions, obscuring A-lines and creating a diffusely bright appearance below the pleural line — consistent with a ground-glass pattern indicating alveolar/interstitial disease.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: Consolidation = TRUE**

**Consolidation type: shred_sign**

In multiple frames (particularly frames 4–8), there is subpleural tissue-like echogenicity (hepatization) directly below the pleural line. The deep border of this consolidated area is **irregular and shredded**, with a jagged transition between consolidated (echogenic) and aerated (dark) lung. This "shred sign" is the predominant consolidation pattern. Scattered hyperechoic foci within the consolidated area may represent small air bronchograms, but the irregular border is the dominant feature.
