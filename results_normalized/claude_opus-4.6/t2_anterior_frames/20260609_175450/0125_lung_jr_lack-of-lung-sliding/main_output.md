# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1–10:** Each frame shows a consistent intercostal view with:
- A bright, hyperechoic **pleural line** at the superficial aspect of the image
- Two **rib shadows** creating dark vertical columns on either side
- Between the rib shadows, the sub-pleural space demonstrates **horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines)** running parallel to the pleural line at regular intervals
- The pleural line appears smooth and continuous across all frames, with subtle sliding motion between frames suggesting preserved lung sliding
- No vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen are identified in any frame
- No tissue-like echotexture or irregular borders are seen below the pleural line

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** All 10 frames demonstrate a predominant **A-line pattern**. The horizontal reverberation artifacts are clearly visible at regular intervals below the pleural line, indicating normal air-tissue interfaces. No vertical laser-like artifacts (B-lines) are identified in any frame.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **Subtype = null**

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** The lung parenchyma below the pleural line in all frames shows normal aeration artifacts. There is:
- No tissue-like hepatization (no liver-like echotexture)
- No shred sign (no irregular deep border)
- No air bronchograms (no punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within consolidated tissue)

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

## Summary

This is a **normal anterior lung ultrasound** demonstrating preserved lung sliding with an A-line dominant pattern, consistent with normal lung aeration. No B-lines or consolidation are present.
