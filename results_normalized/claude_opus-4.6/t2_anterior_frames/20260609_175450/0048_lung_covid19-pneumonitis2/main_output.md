**Frame 10:** Bright hyperechoic area at and below the pleural line with some vertical artifacts and an irregular deep boundary.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

Discrete B-lines are observed in several frames as hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the screen. They are individually identifiable and well-spaced, with dark lung parenchyma visible between them — consistent with a **septal** pattern.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: shred_sign**

Across multiple frames, there is subpleural tissue-like echogenicity (partial hepatization) with a distinctly **irregular, shredded deep border** separating the consolidated lung from the underlying aerated parenchyma. This "shred sign" is the predominant finding. Some scattered punctate hyperechoic foci suggestive of small air bronchograms are also noted (frames 7, 10), but the irregular deep boundary is the dominant pattern.

---

## Summary

| Finding | Result | Subtype |
|---------|--------|---------|
| B-lines | **Present** | Septal |
| Consolidation | **Present** | Shred sign |

This pattern is consistent with a **non-translobar, subpleural consolidation** — commonly seen in early ARDS, pneumonia, or atelectasis — with associated interstitial syndrome (septal B-lines) in the surrounding aerated lung.
