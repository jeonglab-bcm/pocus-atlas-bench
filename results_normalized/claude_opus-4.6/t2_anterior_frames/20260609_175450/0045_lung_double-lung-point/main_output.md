**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is clearly identified as a bright hyperechoic horizontal line. Below the pleural line, I observe discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending downward. A-lines (horizontal reverberation artifacts) are partially visible but somewhat interrupted by the vertical artifacts. No tissue-like echotexture is seen below the pleura.

**Frames 4-6:** The pleural line remains well-defined. A few discrete vertical artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend toward the bottom of the screen. Dark lung parenchyma is visible between these artifacts. No subpleural consolidation or hepatized tissue is identified.

**Frames 7-8:** Similar pattern with discrete vertical artifacts. The spacing between B-lines remains consistent. No coalescing of artifacts into a white sheet. No irregular tissue borders visible.

**Frames 9-10:** The pleural line is bright and continuous. Discrete vertical artifacts continue to be visible, arising from the pleural line and projecting inferiorly. The overall pattern remains consistent with the earlier frames.

## B-lines Assessment

**Observations:**
- Multiple discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line
- These artifacts extend toward the bottom of the screen
- Dark, normally-appearing lung parenchyma is visible between individual B-lines
- The B-lines do not coalesce into a confluent white sheet
- A-lines are partially visible but interrupted by B-lines
- Estimated ≤3 B-lines per intercostal space in most frames

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

The pattern is consistent with discrete, well-spaced B-lines indicating mildly thickened interlobular septa without diffuse alveolar flooding.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observations:**
- No tissue-like (hepatized) echotexture is identified below the pleural line
- No shred sign (irregular deep border) is present
- No air bronchograms (punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within solid-appearing lung) are seen
- The subpleural region maintains an artifactual pattern consistent with aerated lung

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
