## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

Multiple discrete, well-spaced B-lines are visible extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the image. Dark lung parenchyma is clearly visible between individual B-lines — they do not coalesce into a confluent white sheet. The B-lines appear to originate from the sub-pleural nodules, consistent with focal interstitial-alveolar involvement in miliary TB.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**consolidation_type = "hepatization"**

The sub-pleural nodules visible in frames 7–10 represent small peripheral consolidations. They appear as focal, tissue-like (hypoechoic) structures just beneath the pleural line, disrupting its normal smooth appearance. While small in size, they demonstrate tissue-like echogenicity consistent with hepatization pattern. No shred sign or air bronchograms are identified. These findings are characteristic of miliary tuberculosis, where hematogenous dissemination produces numerous small granulomas at the lung periphery.
