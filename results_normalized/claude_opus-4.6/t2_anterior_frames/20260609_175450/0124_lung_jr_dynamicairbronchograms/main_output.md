# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1-2:** A bright, curved hyperechoic pleural line is visible in the upper portion (~0.5-1 cm depth). Below it, a large anechoic (dark) area represents aerated lung. On the right side, discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts extend from the pleural line toward the bottom of the screen.

**Frames 3-5:** The pleural line remains well-visualized. Small bright foci are noted near the pleural line in Frame 3. The vertical artifacts on the right continue, appearing discrete and well-separated. Lung sliding appears present (subtle positional changes between frames).

**Frame 6:** The pleural line appears slightly irregular with some subpleural heterogeneous echogenicity, but no frank tissue-like consolidation pattern.

**Frames 7-10:** The vertical artifacts become slightly more prominent/clustered, particularly in Frames 9-10, but remain individually distinguishable with dark lung parenchyma visible between them. No coalescing into a "white-out" sheet is observed.

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines PRESENT (lung_rockets = true)**

**Subtype: Septal**

**Evidence:**
- Discrete, well-defined vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line
- They extend to the bottom of the screen without fading
- Dark (aerated) lung parenchyma remains visible between individual B-lines
- They show slight respiratory movement between frames
- No coalescence into a confluent white sheet (ruling out ground_glass)
- A-lines are not dominant, replaced by these vertical artifacts
