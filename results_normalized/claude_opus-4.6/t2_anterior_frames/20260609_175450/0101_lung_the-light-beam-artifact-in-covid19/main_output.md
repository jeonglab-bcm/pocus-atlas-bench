The dominant pattern is horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) at regular intervals below the pleural line, consistent with normally aerated lung.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **Subtype = null**

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observations:** No tissue-like hepatization (liver-like echogenicity) is seen in the sub-pleural lung. No shred sign (irregular deep border between consolidated and aerated lung) is identified. No punctate or linear hyperechoic air bronchograms are visible within the lung parenchyma. The lung appears normally aerated throughout the clip.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

## Summary

This anterior lung zone clip demonstrates a **normal A-line pattern** with preserved lung sliding, no B-lines, and no consolidation — consistent with normally aerated lung parenchyma.
