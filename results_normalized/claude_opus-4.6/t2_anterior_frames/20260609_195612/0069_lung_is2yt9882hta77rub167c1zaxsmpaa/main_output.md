## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Multiple frames (6–10) demonstrate broad, hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line, extending without fading to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are wide and coalesce, obscuring A-lines and creating a diffuse bright appearance in the far field. This pattern is consistent with confluent B-lines.

### Conclusion:
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = ground_glass**

The B-lines are broad and confluent, merging into a diffuse white sheet pattern rather than remaining discrete and well-separated. This suggests alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial disease.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** The dark areas seen in frames 3–5 are anechoic/hypoechoic with triangular geometry, consistent with **rib acoustic shadows** rather than true consolidation. There is:
- No tissue-like hepatization (no liver-like echogenicity)
- No definite shred sign (no irregular shredded border between consolidated and aerated lung)
- No air bronchograms (no punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within a solid-appearing parenchyma)

### Conclusion:
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
