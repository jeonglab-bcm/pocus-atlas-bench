## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

**Reasoning:** Across all frames, I observe discrete, well-separated vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the image without fading. Dark lung parenchyma is clearly visible between individual B-lines. They do not coalesce into a confluent "white-out" pattern. This is consistent with a **septal** B-line pattern indicating thickened interlobular septa.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: shred_sign**

**Reasoning:** In multiple frames (particularly frames 1, 6–8), I observe irregular subpleural areas of tissue-like echogenicity just below the pleural line. The deep border of these areas is ragged and irregular — the classic "shred sign" — representing the transition zone between consolidated and still-aerated lung. No definitive air bronchograms (punctate hyperechoic foci within hepatized tissue) are identified, and the consolidation is not extensive enough to demonstrate full hepatization.
