## B-lines Assessment

There is loss of normal A-line pattern. At the transition zones and deeper portions, confluent bright artifacts are present, consistent with B-lines merging into a ground-glass pattern in the areas surrounding the consolidation.

**Conclusion:** `lung_rockets = true`
**Subtype:** `ground_glass` (confluent artifacts at the periphery of consolidation, loss of all A-lines)

## Consolidation Assessment

The dominant finding is clear **alveolar consolidation**:
1. **Hepatization:** The subpleural lung parenchyma appears solid and tissue-like, resembling liver echotexture, with loss of all normal aeration artifacts.
2. **Air bronchograms:** Multiple scattered hyperechoic punctate and short linear foci within the hepatized lung, representing air trapped within bronchi surrounded by consolidated parenchyma.
3. **Shred sign:** The deep border of the consolidated area is irregular/shredded in several frames.

**Conclusion:** `consolidation = true`
**Predominant type:** `air_bronchogram` — The most prominent feature is the punctate and linear hyperechoic foci embedded within hepatized lung tissue, characteristic of dynamic or static air bronchograms in anterior zone consolidation.
