## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Throughout all frames, multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend without fading to the bottom of the screen. They move synchronously with respiration. In the majority of frames (particularly 1–8), B-lines are numerous (>3 per intercostal space) and coalescent, merging into broad areas of diffuse whiteness that obscure A-lines. In later frames, some discrete B-lines with visible dark spaces between them are also present.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = ground_glass** (predominantly confluent/coalescing B-lines creating a diffuse white sheet, with only intermittent discrete separation in later frames)

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** Despite the diffuse B-line pattern, there is no tissue-like hepatization (no solid, liver-like echogenicity of lung parenchyma). No shred sign (irregular deep border between consolidated and aerated lung) is seen. No air bronchograms (punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within solid-appearing tissue) are identified. The subpleural space maintains an artifact-predominant pattern rather than a solid tissue pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
