## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = false**

The dominant pattern across all frames is horizontal A-lines (reverberation artifacts). No convincing hyperechoic vertical artifacts are seen extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen without fading. The lung field below the pleural line is characterized by A-lines in the majority of frames.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**
**consolidation_type = "shred_sign"**

In several frames (1, 4–6, 10), there is a subpleural area of tissue-like echogenicity with an irregular, shredded deep border transitioning into normally aerated lung. This irregular margin between consolidated and aerated lung is characteristic of the **shred sign**. Some scattered hyperechoic punctate foci are noted within these tissue-like areas but the predominant feature is the ragged, irregular deep boundary rather than frank hepatization or dominant air bronchograms.
