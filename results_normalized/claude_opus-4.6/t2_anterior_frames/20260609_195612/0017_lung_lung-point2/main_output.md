## M-Mode Analysis

The M-mode (lower panel) demonstrates:
- **Superficial layers (0–1 cm):** Horizontal parallel lines (stationary chest wall)
- **Pleural line (~1 cm):** Bright, continuous line
- **Below pleural line (~1–1.5 cm):** Horizontal lines (A-line reverberations), confirming air-filled lung
- **Deeper (~1.5–3.5+ cm):** Regular periodic dark columnar interruptions at ~100–110/min — consistent with **lung pulse** (cardiac pulsations transmitted through non-sliding lung)

The overall M-mode pattern shows a stratosphere-like (barcode) pattern interrupted by lung pulse, indicating absent lung sliding with maintained lung-chest wall apposition.
