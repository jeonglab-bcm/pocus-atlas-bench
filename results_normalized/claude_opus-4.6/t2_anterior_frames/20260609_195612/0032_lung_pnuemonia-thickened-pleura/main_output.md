# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: RT H/T Zone 3 Low

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is visible between rib shadows (dark vertical bands). Multiple bright vertical artifacts (B-lines) arise from the pleural line and extend toward the bottom of the screen. In the central and right portions, several B-lines appear to coalesce, creating broad areas of hyperechogenicity. Some areas of subpleural tissue-like echogenicity are noted on the right side.

**Frames 4-6:** Persistent multiple B-lines are seen, many coalescing centrally. On the right lateral aspect, there is an area of irregular subpleural tissue-like echogenicity with a "shredded" deep border, distinct from the surrounding B-line pattern. The dark spaces between B-lines are diminishing in many areas.

**Frames 7-9:** Continued dense, confluent B-lines dominate the image. The subpleural irregular tissue-like areas remain visible on the right side with a ragged/irregular margin between consolidated and aerated parenchyma.

**Frames 10-11:** Similar pattern persists—dense coalescing B-lines centrally and irregular subpleural echogenicity laterally with ill-defined, shredded deep borders.

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: ground_glass**

Multiple B-lines are present in every intercostal space, and the predominant pattern is one of confluent/coalescing B-lines that merge into broad hyperechoic sheets, obscuring A-lines and creating a "white lung" appearance in many areas. This is consistent with significant alveolar-interstitial disease.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: shred_sign**

In multiple frames (particularly frames 6–11), on the right lateral subpleural region, there is an area of tissue-like echogenicity with an irregular, fragmented (shredded) deep border separating consolidated from aerated lung. This is distinct from the surrounding B-line artifacts and represents a small subpleural consolidation with a classic shred sign pattern, indicating partial alveolar filling/atelectasis at this dependent zone.
