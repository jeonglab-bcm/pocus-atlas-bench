No convincing air bronchograms (punctate hyperechoic foci within hepatized tissue) are identified. The R POST frames show milder sub-pleural irregularity.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "shred_sign"** (predominant irregular, shredded deep border pattern)
