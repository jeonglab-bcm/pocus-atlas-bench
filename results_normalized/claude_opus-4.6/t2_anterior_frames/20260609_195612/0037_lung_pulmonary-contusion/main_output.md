# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1–10:** Across all sequential frames, I observe:
- A clearly defined, bright **pleural line** at the top of the image
- **2–3 discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts** arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading
- These vertical artifacts are well-separated with **dark lung parenchyma visible between them**
- The vertical artifacts move subtly with respiration (consistent with lung sliding)
- No dominant horizontal A-line reverberation pattern
- No tissue-like echotexture or irregular borders below the pleural line

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

**Rationale:** The vertical artifacts clearly meet B-line criteria:
- They originate at the pleural line
- They extend to the bottom of the screen without fading
- They are laser-like and well-defined
- There are approximately 2–3 per intercostal space
- They remain discrete and well-spaced with clearly visible dark lung between them
- They do NOT coalesce into a white sheet (ruling out ground_glass)

This pattern is consistent with **thickened interlobular septa** (e.g., early pulmonary edema or mild interstitial disease).

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = false**

**consolidation_type = null**

**Rationale:** There is:
- No tissue-like hepatization (no liver-like echotexture)
