## B-lines Assessment

**Findings:** Across multiple frames, I observe discrete, well-spaced vertical hyperechoic artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the screen. A-lines remain visible between these artifacts, and dark lung parenchyma is seen between the B-lines. They are clearly separated and countable.

### Conclusion:
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

The discrete, well-spaced B-lines with preserved intervening dark lung parenchyma are consistent with the **septal** pattern, suggesting thickened interlobular septa (early/mild interstitial syndrome).
