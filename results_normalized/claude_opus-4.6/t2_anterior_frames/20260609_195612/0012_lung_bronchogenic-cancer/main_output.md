The probe sweeps slightly. The supradiaphragmatic tissue becomes more conspicuous — it demonstrates echogenicity similar to the liver (hepatization). No B-lines are visible.

The hepatized lung tissue above the diaphragm is most clearly visualized. It has its own distinct echotexture (not a perfect mirror of the liver), with subtle internal echogenic punctae suggesting air bronchograms. Associated anechoic fluid (pleural effusion) is visible.
