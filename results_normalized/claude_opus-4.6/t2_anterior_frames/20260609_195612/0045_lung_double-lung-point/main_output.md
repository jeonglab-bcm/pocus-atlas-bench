Dark, normally aerated lung parenchyma is visible between individual B-lines. A-lines are partially interrupted but still faintly visible in areas between the B-lines.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced (≤3 per intercostal space), consistent with thickened interlobular septa rather than diffuse alveolar flooding.
