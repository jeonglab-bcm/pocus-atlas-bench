**Reasoning:**
- **Sub-pleural nodules** are clearly identified as small focal tissue-like (hypoechoic) lesions at the pleural surface

- They appear as solid, tissue-density structures disrupting the normal hyperechoic pleural line
- No shred sign or air bronchograms are identified
