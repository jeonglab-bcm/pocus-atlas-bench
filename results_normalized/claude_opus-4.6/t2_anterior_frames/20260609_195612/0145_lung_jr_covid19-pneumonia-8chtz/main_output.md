# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1-3:** A bright, well-defined pleural line is visible at the top. Two to three distinct vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen. Dark lung parenchyma is visible between these artifacts. No obvious tissue-like echotexture below the pleural line.

**Frames 4-6:** The pattern remains consistent — discrete vertical bright lines originating from the pleural line project into the far field. The lines move slightly with respiration (consistent with lung sliding). A-lines are obscured in the regions where B-lines are present but may still be faintly visible between them.

**Frames 7-10:** B-lines persist, remaining well-separated and individually identifiable. No coalescence into a "white-out" pattern is seen. The sub-pleural region does not show any hepatized tissue or irregular borders.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

**Rationale:** Across all 10 frames, 2–3 well-defined, laser-like vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend without fading to the bottom of the image. They are clearly separated with dark, aerated lung visible between them. They do not coalesce into a confluent white sheet. This pattern is consistent with **septal B-lines** (thickened interlobular septa).

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = false**

**Consolidation type: null**

**Rationale:** Throughout all frames, there is no evidence of:
- Tissue-like hepatization (no liver-like echogenic parenchyma below the pleural line)
- Shred sign (no irregular, ragged deep border)
- Air bronchograms (no punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within solid tissue)

The lung parenchyma below the pleural line generates artifacts (B-lines) but does not appear solidified or de-aerated.
