## B-Lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = false**

No vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen are identified in any frame. The predominant sub-pleural pattern is **A-lines** (horizontal reverberation artifacts), indicating preserved aeration in the deeper lung zones.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**consolidation_type = "shred_sign"**

**Evidence:**
- A small subpleural area of tissue-like echogenicity is visible immediately below the pleural line across multiple frames
- The deep border between this consolidated zone and the aerated lung below is **irregular and shredded** — the hallmark shred sign
- The echogenicity of this subpleural zone is increased compared to normally aerated lung, suggesting partial loss of aeration
- No clear air bronchograms (punctate hyperechoic foci) are identified within the consolidated area
- The consolidation appears small/limited in extent, with preserved aeration (A-lines) in the deeper lung
