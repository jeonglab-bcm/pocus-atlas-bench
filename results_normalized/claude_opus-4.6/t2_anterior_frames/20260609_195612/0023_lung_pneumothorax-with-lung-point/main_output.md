Below it, I observe multiple horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) at regular equidistant intervals from the pleural line. Rib shadows are visible on the left side. The sub-pleural space is predominantly dark/hypoechoic.

**Frames 4-6:** The same pattern persists. The pleural line is well-defined, with A-lines repeating at regular intervals below. There are no convincing vertical artifacts extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen. Some faint scattered echogenicity is present but does not meet B-line criteria.

**Frames 7-8:** A-line dominant pattern continues. The pleural line appears to demonstrate sliding (subtle positional changes between frames). No vertical laser-like artifacts are identified.

**Frames 9-10:** Consistent with prior frames. A-lines remain the dominant sub-pleural artifact. The lung parenchyma below the pleural line does not demonstrate tissue-like echogenicity or irregular borders.
