## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines PRESENT (lung_rockets = true)**

**Subtype: Septal**

**Rationale:** Throughout the clip, discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend toward the bottom of the screen. They are clearly separated with intervening dark (normally aerated) lung parenchyma visible between them. There are approximately 1-3 B-lines per intercostal space. They do NOT coalesce into a confluent white sheet. A-lines are not dominant. This pattern is consistent with **septal B-lines**, suggesting thickened interlobular septa.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: Consolidation ABSENT (consolidation = false)**

**Consolidation type: null**

**Rationale:** Despite some hyperechoic foci at the pleural interface, there is:
- No tissue-like hepatization (no liver-like echogenic parenchyma)
- No shred sign (no irregular, shredded deep border)

The bright structures at the pleural level are consistent with the normal pleural line and the origins of B-line artifacts rather than true parenchymal consolidation. The sub-pleural space does not demonstrate a solid, tissue-like pattern.
