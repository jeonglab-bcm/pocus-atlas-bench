# Lung Ultrasound Frame-by-Frame Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frame 1 (1/51):** A well-defined hyperechoic pleural line is visible at approximately 55-60mm depth. Below the pleural line, the field is predominantly dark/anechoic. Faint horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) are suggested parallel to the pleural line. No vertical artifacts extending to the screen bottom.

**Frame 5 (5/51):** Pleural line again visible. A faint vertical streak is noted centrally, and a small bright dot is seen around 90mm depth on the right. This does not clearly extend to the bottom of the screen and likely represents a short-path artifact rather than a true B-line.

**Frame 9 (9/51):** Clear pleural line. Below it, the lung field is dark. Faint A-line reverberation pattern. No B-lines.

**Frame 14 (14/51):** Similar to prior frames. A faint central vertical artifact is noted but does not extend fully to the bottom of the screen and is not laser-like.

**Frames 18, 22, 27, 31, 35, 40:** Consistently show a well-defined pleural line with predominantly dark lung field below. A-line pattern (horizontal reverberations) is the dominant finding. No vertical artifacts meeting B-line criteria.

## B-lines Assessment

**Findings:** Across all 10 sampled frames, the dominant pattern is an **A-line pattern**. The lung field below the pleural line is dark with faint horizontal reverberation artifacts. No convincing hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading are identified.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **Subtype = null**

## Consolidation Assessment

**Findings:** In no frame is there evidence of:
- Tissue-like hepatization (no solid, liver-like parenchyma)
- Shred sign (no irregular deep border)
- Air bronchograms (no punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within consolidated lung)

The sub-pleural space appears normally aerated throughout the clip.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

## Summary
