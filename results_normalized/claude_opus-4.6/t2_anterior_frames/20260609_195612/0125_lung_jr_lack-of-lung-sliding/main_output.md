**Frames 4-6:** The pattern remains consistent. The pleural line is clearly identified, and A-lines continue at regular intervals. There is lung sliding suggested by subtle variations in the pleural line appearance between frames. No hyperechoic vertical artifacts are noted.

**Frames 7-9:** Similar pattern. Slightly more echogenicity is noted in the near-field (superficial tissues), but the subpleural pattern remains dominated by horizontal A-lines. No tissue-like echotexture is identified below the pleural line.

**Frame 10:** Consistent with prior frames. A-line dominant pattern persists with clean rib shadows and no pathological vertical artifacts.

## B-lines Assessment

- **subtype = null**

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** There is no tissue-like (hepatized) echogenicity below the pleural line. No shred sign or irregular deep borders are seen. No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci suggesting air bronchograms are present. The lung appears normally aerated.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
