# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-2:** The pleural line is identified as a bright hyperechoic horizontal line in the upper third of the image. Below it, there is heterogeneous echotexture with some scattered hyperechoic foci and subtle vertical artifacts extending downward.

**Frames 3-4:** The pleural line remains visible. Beneath it, there is an area of increased tissue-like echogenicity with an irregular, jagged deep margin transitioning to darker (aerated) lung below. Some discrete vertical artifacts (B-lines) are visible in adjacent areas.

**Frames 5-6:** The subpleural region shows a zone of solid-appearing tissue with a clearly irregular ("shredded") deep border. The consolidated area has echogenicity similar to soft tissue.

**Frames 7-8:** More prominent tissue-like consolidation is seen directly below the pleural line. The deep border remains irregular. Scattered hyperechoic punctate foci are visible within the consolidated region in some areas.

**Frames 9-10:** The consolidated area continues to be visible with an irregular deep border. Some discrete B-lines are seen emanating from areas where consolidation transitions to aerated lung.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

Discrete B-lines are visible in several frames, arising from the pleural line and edges of the consolidated region. They are well-spaced, with dark lung parenchyma between them, and do not coalesce into a confluent white-out pattern.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: shred_sign**

The dominant finding across these frames is a subpleural area of tissue-like echogenicity (hepatization) with a characteristically **irregular, shredded deep border** between consolidated and aerated lung. This "shred sign" is consistently visible across multiple frames. Some scattered hyperechoic foci within the consolidated area suggest small air bronchograms, but the predominant and most conspicuous pattern is the irregular deep margin.

---

## Summary

| Finding | Result | Subtype |
|---------|--------|---------|
| B-lines | **True** | Septal |
| Consolidation | **True** | Shred sign |

This pattern is consistent with **non-translobar alveolar consolidation** (e.g., early pneumonia, atelectasis, or inflammatory consolidation) in the anterior lung zone.
