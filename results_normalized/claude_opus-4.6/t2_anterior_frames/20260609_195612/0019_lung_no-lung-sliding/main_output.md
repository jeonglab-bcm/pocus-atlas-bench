# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1–10 (consistent findings throughout):**
- **Superficial layers:** Well-defined subcutaneous tissue and chest wall musculature visible in the near field
- **Ribs:** Dark, rounded structures with posterior acoustic shadowing visible on the left side of the image
- **Pleural line:** Bright, hyperechoic horizontal line clearly identified at approximately 1 cm depth
- **Sub-pleural space:** Multiple parallel, hyperechoic horizontal lines are seen at regular intervals below the pleural line — consistent with **A-lines** (reverberation artifacts)
- **Lung sliding:** Subtle changes in the pleural line position/texture across frames suggest preserved lung sliding
- **No vertical artifacts** extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen are identified in any frame

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** All 10 frames demonstrate a dominant **A-line pattern**. The horizontal reverberation artifacts are clearly visible, evenly spaced, and diminishing in brightness with depth. No hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending without fading to the screen edge are identified.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **Subtype = null**

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** Below the pleural line, normal aeration artifacts (A-lines) predominate. There is no tissue-like hepatized lung parenchyma, no irregular shredded border (shred sign), and no punctate or linear hyperechoic foci suggestive of air bronchograms. The dark areas are consistent with rib acoustic shadows, not consolidated lung.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

## Summary

This anterior lung zone demonstrates a **normal A-line pattern** with preserved lung sliding, indicating normal lung aeration. No pathological B-lines or consolidation are present.
