## B-lines Assessment

**Findings:**
- Multiple vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line
- They extend toward the bottom of the screen without fading
- They are individually identifiable with dark lung visible between them
- Present in multiple intercostal spaces (>3 per space in some areas)
- A-lines are not prominently visible (obscured by the B-lines)

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

The B-lines are discrete and individually separable, with visible dark lung parenchyma between them, consistent with thickened interlobular septa (interstitial syndrome). They do not coalesce into a confluent white sheet.
