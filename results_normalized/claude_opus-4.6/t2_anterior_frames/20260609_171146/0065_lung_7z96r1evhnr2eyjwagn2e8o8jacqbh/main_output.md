## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** In several frames (1, 2, 5, 8, 9), discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend toward the bottom of the screen. These are individually distinguishable with dark lung parenchyma visible between them. They do not coalesce into a confluent white sheet.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

Discrete, well-spaced B-lines are present in multiple frames, consistent with thickened interlobular septa. No confluent/ground-glass pattern is identified.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** In multiple frames (particularly 1, 3, 6, 7, 10), there is subpleural tissue-like echogenicity with:
- An irregular, fragmented ("shredded") deep border between consolidated and aerated lung
- Some punctate hyperechoic foci within the consolidated tissue (suggestive of air bronchograms, especially in frames 6–7)
- The consolidated areas are small/subpleural rather than large lobar consolidation

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "shred_sign"**

The predominant pattern is the shred sign — irregular, serrated deep margins between small subpleural consolidations and deeper aerated lung. Some scattered air bronchograms are also present but are secondary to the shred sign morphology.
