## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Across all 10 sampled frames, the predominant pattern below the pleural line is dark, aerated lung with horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines). While frame 5 shows a faint vertical artifact, it does not meet strict B-line criteria (hyperechoic, laser-like, extending to the bottom of the screen without fading, and moving with respiration).

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **subtype = null**

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** No tissue-like hepatization is seen in any frame. The lung parenchyma below the pleural line does not demonstrate liver-like echogenicity. There is no irregular shredded border (shred sign), and no punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within solid-appearing lung (air bronchograms). The pleural line appears smooth and regular throughout.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

---

## Summary

This anterior zone LUS clip demonstrates a **normal A-line pattern** consistent with adequately aerated lung. There are no B-lines and no signs of alveolar consolidation.
