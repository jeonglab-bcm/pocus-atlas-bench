## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines = true**

Multiple vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line, extend to the far field without fading, and are present across multiple frames. They are discrete and well-separated with visible dark lung between them.

**Subtype: septal**

The B-lines are individually identifiable, well-spaced, with clear dark intervals — consistent with thickened interlobular septa rather than confluent/ground-glass pattern.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

In the later frames (7–10), there is clear tissue-like echogenicity in the subpleural space with:
- Hepatized (liver-like) parenchyma
- An irregular, jagged deep border (shred sign)
- Punctate hyperechoic foci consistent with air bronchograms

**Consolidation type: shred_sign**

The most predominant and consistent finding across multiple frames is the **irregular, shredded deep boundary** between the consolidated lung and the remaining aerated parenchyma beneath it.
