# Lung Ultrasound Frame-by-Frame Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Assessment

**Frames 1–3:** A clearly defined pleural line is visible superficially. Below the pleural line, there is a heterogeneous, echogenic (tissue-like) subpleural area with an irregular, fragmented deep margin. Within this area, small punctate hyperechoic foci are visible, consistent with air bronchograms. The deep border between aerated and non-aerated lung is ragged/shredded.

**Frame 4:** The pleural line remains visible. The subpleural consolidation is still present but appears to be narrowing. Some hazy brightness extends below the consolidated area. A few discrete vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line are visible.

**Frames 5–7:** The consolidation becomes less prominent as the scanning plane shifts. The pleural line is clearly seen. A few scattered short vertical artifacts (B-lines) are noted arising from the pleural line, well-separated with dark lung between them.

**Frames 8–10:** The pleural line is well-defined. The subpleural consolidation reappears focally in frame 10 with a small tissue-like nodular echotexture. Scattered discrete B-lines are still visible in some frames, along with the overall reduction in consolidation size.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**

In several frames (particularly 4–9), discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend toward the bottom of the image. These are spaced apart with dark (aerated) lung visible between them.

**Subtype: septal**

The B-lines are discrete and well-separated (≤3 per intercostal space), consistent with thickened interlobular septa, likely reactive to the adjacent consolidation.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

The following signs are clearly demonstrated:

1. **Hepatization:** Frames 1–4 show tissue-like echogenicity subpleurally, resembling liver parenchyma with loss of normal aeration artifacts.
2. **Shred sign:** The deep border of the consolidated area is markedly irregular and "shredded," especially prominent in frames 1–3.
3. **Air bronchograms:** Punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within the hepatized lung in frames 1–3.

**Predominant consolidation type: shred_sign**

The irregular, fragmented deep margin between consolidated and aerated lung is the most consistently prominent feature across the clip, making the shred sign the predominant pattern.

---
