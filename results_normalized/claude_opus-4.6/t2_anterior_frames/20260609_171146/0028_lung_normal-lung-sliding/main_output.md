# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-10:** All frames demonstrate a consistent pattern:
- **Superficial layers:** Multiple hyperechoic horizontal lines representing subcutaneous tissue, fascia, and intercostal muscle layers
- **Pleural line:** A well-defined, bright hyperechoic horizontal line clearly identified at the expected depth
- **Sub-pleural space:** Predominantly dark (anechoic) with repeating horizontal reverberation artifacts at regular intervals below the pleural line
- **Lung sliding:** Subtle variation in the pleural line appearance between frames suggests preserved lung sliding
- **No vertical artifacts** extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen in any frame

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Across all 10 frames, the sub-pleural space is dominated by **A-lines** — horizontal, equidistant reverberation artifacts parallel to the pleural line. There are no hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line that extend to the bottom of the screen without fading.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **Subtype = null**
- The A-line pattern indicates normally aerated lung parenchyma at this anterior zone.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** There is no tissue-like (hepatized) echotexture below the pleural line in any frame. No shred sign (irregular deep border) is identified. No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci suggestive of air bronchograms are seen. The lung surface maintains its normal appearance with preserved aeration artifacts.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

## Summary

This anterior lung zone demonstrates a **normal A-line pattern** with preserved lung sliding and no pathological findings. No B-lines or consolidation are present, consistent with normally aerated lung.
