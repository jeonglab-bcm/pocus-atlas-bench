## B-lines Assessment

**Observations:**
- Vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen in virtually all frames
- In some frames (3–4), discrete, individually identifiable B-lines are seen with dark parenchyma between them (septal pattern)
- In other frames (5–6, 1–2), B-lines coalesce into broader confluent bright bands (approaching ground-glass pattern)
- No A-lines are visible in any frame

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = mixed** (both discrete septal-type and confluent ground-glass-type B-lines are present across different frames)

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observations:**
- No definitive tissue-like hepatization (liver-like echogenicity) is identified below the pleural line
- No clear irregular/shredded deep border (shred sign) is seen
- Hyperechoic spots near the pleural line in frame 10 are most consistent with B-line origins rather than air bronchograms within consolidated tissue
- The pleural line remains relatively smooth and intact across frames

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
