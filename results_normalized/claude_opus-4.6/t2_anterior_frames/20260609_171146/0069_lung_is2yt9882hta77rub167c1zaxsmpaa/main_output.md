## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines are PRESENT (lung_rockets = true)**

Multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading (frames 6–11). They are wide, coalescing, and merge into a near-continuous bright field, obliterating A-lines.

**Subtype: ground_glass**

The B-lines are confluent and coalescing rather than discrete and well-spaced. The lung surface appears diffusely bright in the affected regions, consistent with alveolar–interstitial edema or diffuse interstitial disease.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: Consolidation is PRESENT (consolidation = true)**

In frames 3–5, there is a subpleural hypoechoic region with loss of normal aeration pattern. The deep border between the consolidated area and the underlying aerated lung is irregular and fragmented — characteristic of the shred sign. No dominant air bronchograms or uniform hepatization pattern is seen.

**Consolidation type: shred_sign**

The predominant finding is an irregular, jagged deep margin of the consolidated area abutting aerated lung, consistent with a small subpleural consolidation.
