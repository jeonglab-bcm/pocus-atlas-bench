## B-lines Assessment

**Observations:** Across the clip, a few discrete, well-defined vertical hyperechoic artifacts are seen originating from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the image. They are isolated and well-separated, with dark (aerated) lung parenchyma visible between them. A-lines remain visible in most frames where B-lines are not dominant.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

Rationale: The B-lines are discrete, few in number (≤3 per intercostal space), and clearly spaced — consistent with a **septal** pattern indicating mildly thickened interlobular septa.

## Consolidation Assessment
