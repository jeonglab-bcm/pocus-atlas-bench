# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### M-mode (upper panel) observations:
- **Frames 1-2**: The M-mode shows horizontal parallel lines in the chest wall region with a transition zone at the pleural line. Below the pleural line, there is a granular/sandy pattern.
- **Frames 3-10**: A consistent pattern emerges — horizontal stratified lines above the pleural line (static chest wall) and a granular "sandy" pattern below the pleural line. This represents the **seashore sign**, confirming lung sliding is present.

### B-mode (lower panel) observations:
- **All frames**: A bright hyperechoic pleural line is consistently visible at approximately 1.5-2 cm depth.
- Below the pleural line, **horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines)** are visible at regular intervals, parallel to the pleural line.
- No vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen are identified.
- The bright areas at 3-4 cm depth appear to be deeper A-line reverberations, not B-lines.
- No tissue-like echotexture, shredded borders, or punctate hyperechoic foci within subpleural lung are seen.

## Conclusions

### B-lines Assessment:
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **Subtype = null**

The images demonstrate a classic **A-line dominant pattern** with clearly visible horizontal reverberation artifacts below the pleural line. No vertical artifacts meeting B-line criteria are present in any frame.

### Consolidation Assessment:
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

No hepatization, shred sign, or air bronchograms are identified. The lung parenchyma appears normally aerated throughout the clip.

### Summary:
This is a **normal anterior lung ultrasound** with an A-line pattern and preserved lung sliding (seashore sign on M-mode), indicating normally aerated lung at this zone.
