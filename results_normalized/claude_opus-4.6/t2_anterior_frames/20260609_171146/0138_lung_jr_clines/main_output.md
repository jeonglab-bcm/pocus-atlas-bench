# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Description

**Frames 1–2:** The pleural line is well-visualized as a bright hyperechoic line. Below the pleural line, there is a predominantly dark area (rib shadow centrally) with some scattered echogenicity at the lateral margins. Some subtle vertical artifacts are present adjacent to the rib shadow margins.

**Frames 3–6:** The probe sweeps through varying intercostal positions. In frames 4–5, irregular hyperechoic tissue-like material is seen immediately below the pleural line with bright punctate echoes within it. The deep border is ragged and irregular. Frame 6 returns to rib shadow dominance.

**Frames 7–8:** Clearly demonstrate tissue-like echogenicity below the pleural line with an **irregular, shredded deep border** where consolidated lung transitions to aerated lung. Hyperechoic dots/foci are visible within the consolidated region.

**Frames 9–10:** Frame 9 shows some scattered vertical artifacts at the consolidation margin. Frame 10 shows irregular subpleural consolidation with bright internal echoes and an uneven deep margin.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

At the margins of the consolidation, particularly visible in frames 1–2 and 9, there are discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line. These are sparse and well-separated, consistent with a **septal** pattern. They are not confluent or sheet-like.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: shred_sign**

Multiple frames (4, 5, 7, 8, 10) demonstrate:
- **Tissue-like (hepatized) echogenicity** immediately below the pleural line
- **Irregular, shredded deep border** (shred sign) — the transition from consolidated to aerated lung is ragged and serrated rather than smooth
- **Scattered hyperechoic foci** within the consolidated region suggestive of air bronchograms, though the predominant pattern is the irregular deep border

The predominant consolidation type is **shred_sign**, indicating a non-translobar, patchy subpleural consolidation pattern consistent with pneumonia or early ARDS.
