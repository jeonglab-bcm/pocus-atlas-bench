## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines = TRUE**

**Subtype: Septal**

**Reasoning:** Across multiple frames, I identify a small number of discrete, well-spaced vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the screen. Between these B-lines, dark lung parenchyma and A-lines remain clearly visible. This pattern of isolated, separated B-lines with preserved inter-line lucency is consistent with the **septal** subtype, suggesting thickened interlobular septa (early/mild interstitial process).

The lung parenchyma maintains its aerated appearance with artifact-generating pleural line and no sub-pleural tissue density.
