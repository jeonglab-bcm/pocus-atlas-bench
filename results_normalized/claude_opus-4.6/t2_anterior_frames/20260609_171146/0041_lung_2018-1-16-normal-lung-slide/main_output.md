**Frames 9-10:** The B-line artifacts return to prominence—bright vertical columns extending from the pleural line toward the bottom of the screen. The pattern remains discrete rather than confluent/coalescing.

## B-lines Assessment

**Findings:** Vertical hyperechoic artifacts are clearly identified arising from the pleural line, extending to the bottom of the screen without fading, and moving with respiration across frames. These are **discrete, well-spaced** B-lines with dark aerated lung visible between them. A-lines are partially obscured where B-lines are present but visible in adjacent areas.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

These are discrete, individually separable B-lines (likely ≤3 per intercostal space), consistent with thickened interlobular septa (early interstitial syndrome).

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Findings:** No tissue-like hepatization is identified. The subpleural lung does not demonstrate a solid, liver-like echogenicity. There is no irregular "shredded" deep border (shred sign), and no punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within a consolidated parenchyma (air bronchograms). The area deep to the pleural line maintains an aerated appearance with reverberation artifacts.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
