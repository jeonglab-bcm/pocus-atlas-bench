- Below the pleural line, **two prominent vertical hyperechoic artifacts** extend from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen — these are **B-lines**.

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: B-lines are PRESENT (lung_rockets = true)**

- Multiple well-defined, vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading.
- They are discrete and separated by visible dark lung parenchyma.
- **Subtype: septal** — distinct, well-spaced B-lines (approximately 2–3 per intercostal space) with clearly visible dark lung between them, indicating thickened interlobular septa rather than diffuse alveolar flooding.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: Consolidation is PRESENT (consolidation = true)**

- In frames 7–10, a small **subpleural hypoechoic region** with tissue-like echotexture is visible.
- Within this region, **punctate hyperechoic foci** are identified, representing air trapped within bronchi surrounded by non-aerated (consolidated) lung.
- **Consolidation type: air_bronchogram**

| Finding | Result |
