# 0033_lung_air-bronchograms-in-lll-pneumonia

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is visible at the top of the image between rib shadows. Below the pleural line, there is a large area of tissue-like echotexture (hepatization) occupying much of the visible field. Within this area, multiple punctate and linear hyperechoic foci are clearly seen, representing air bronchograms. The deep border of the consolidated area appears irregular.

**Frames 4-6:** The same consolidated pattern persists. The air bronchograms (bright punctate/linear dots) are particularly prominent in these frames, moving slightly with respiratory variation. The tissue retains its liver-like echogenicity.

**Frames 7-8:** Consolidation continues to dominate. The shredded, irregular deep border (shred sign) is appreciated at the transition between consolidated and partially aerated lung. Confluent B-lines extend from this boundary.

**Frames 9-10:** Similar pattern. The deep boundary shows confluent vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) extending toward the bottom of the screen from the edge of the consolidated region.

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: ground_glass**

Confluent B-lines are visible extending from the deep margin of the consolidation, coalescing into a near-white sheet pattern in the transition zone between consolidated and aerated lung. These obscure any A-line pattern.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = true**

**Consolidation type: air_bronchogram**

The dominant finding is a large area of hepatized (tissue-like, liver-echogenicity) lung parenchyma with multiple punctate and linear hyperechoic foci representing air bronchograms scattered throughout the consolidated tissue. A shred sign is also appreciable at the irregular deep border. The predominant and most conspicuous feature is the air bronchograms within hepatized lung.
