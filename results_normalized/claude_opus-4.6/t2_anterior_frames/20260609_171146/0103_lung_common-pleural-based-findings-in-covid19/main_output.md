# 0103_lung_common-pleural-based-findings-in-covid19

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1–4:** The pleural line is clearly identified as a bright hyperechoic horizontal line at approximately 1–1.5 cm depth. Below the pleural line, the lung field is predominantly dark/anechoic with repeating horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) at regular intervals corresponding to multiples of the skin-to-pleura distance. No vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending to the bottom of the screen are observed. Lung sliding appears present (slight shimmer/movement at the pleural line between frames).

**Frames 5–7:** The A-line pattern persists. The pleural line remains smooth and well-defined. Some slight increased echogenicity near the pleural line in frame 6 likely represents normal tissue/rib interfaces rather than pathologic findings. No vertical artifacts meeting B-line criteria are seen.

**Frames 8–10:** Similar appearance throughout. A-lines remain dominant. The hyperechoic curved structure at the deeper aspect (~4–5 cm) represents the rib cortex. No tissue-like echotexture, irregular borders, or punctate hyperechoic foci within the lung parenchyma are identified.

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Across all 10 frames, the predominant pattern below the pleural line consists of horizontal A-line reverberation artifacts. No vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading are identified.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **subtype = null**

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** The pleural line remains smooth and regular throughout. Below the pleural line, the lung appears normally aerated with no tissue-like (hepatized) echotexture. There is no irregular "shredded" deep border between consolidated and aerated lung. No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci suggestive of air bronchograms are seen within the subpleural space.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

---

## Summary

This is a **normal anterior lung ultrasound** demonstrating an A-line pattern consistent with normally aerated lung. No B-lines and no consolidation are present.
