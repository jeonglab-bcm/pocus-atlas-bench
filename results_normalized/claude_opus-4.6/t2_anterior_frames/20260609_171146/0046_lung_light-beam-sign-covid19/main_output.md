## B-lines Assessment

**Observations:** Multiple frames (4, 7, 8, 9, 10) demonstrate hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading. These are discrete, well-separated artifacts with clearly visible dark (aerated) lung parenchyma between them. The B-lines move with respiration across frames. They do not coalesce into a confluent white sheet.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal**

Rationale: Discrete, well-spaced B-lines (approximately 1–2 per intercostal space visible per frame) with preserved dark lung between them, consistent with thickened interlobular septa.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observations:** No tissue-like (hepatized) parenchyma is identified. The pleural line remains smooth without irregular deep borders (no shred sign). No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within solid-appearing lung tissue are seen (no air bronchograms). The small echogenic foci in Frame 2 do not meet criteria for consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
