## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** While there are subtle areas of mild subpleural heterogeneity in some frames (1, 4–6, 10), I do not identify:
- Clear **hepatization** (liver-like tissue echogenicity replacing aerated lung)
- Definitive **shred sign** (irregular, shredded deep margin)
- **Air bronchograms** (punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within consolidated lung)

The subpleural irregularities are minimal and inconsistent across frames. The A-line dominant pattern indicates preserved aeration.

### Conclusion:
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
