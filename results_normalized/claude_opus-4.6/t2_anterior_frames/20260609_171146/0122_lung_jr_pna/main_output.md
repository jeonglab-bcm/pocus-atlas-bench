## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** Across multiple frames, I identify discrete, well-spaced bright vertical artifacts arising from or near the pleural line. These are scattered (approximately 2–3 per field of view), with dark lung parenchyma visible between them. They do not coalesce or form a "white-out" pattern. A-lines are not clearly dominant.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal** — Discrete, well-spaced B-lines with preserved dark intervals between them, consistent with mildly thickened interlobular septa.

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** No region below the pleural line demonstrates tissue-like (hepatized) echogenicity resembling liver parenchyma. There is no irregular shredded deep border (shred sign). The scattered bright foci at depth are punctate but are not convincingly located within a consolidated/solid-appearing region — they lack the surrounding tissue-like echotexture that would characterize air bronchograms.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
