# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is clearly identified as a bright hyperechoic horizontal line near the top of the image. From the pleural line, I observe **2-3 distinct, well-defined vertical hyperechoic artifacts** extending to the bottom of the screen. Dark lung parenchyma is clearly visible between these vertical lines. No A-lines are seen (obscured by the B-lines).

**Frames 4-6:** The same pattern continues with discrete vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line. The B-lines move slightly with respiration (lung sliding present), confirming they are true B-lines rather than static artifacts. The spacing between B-lines remains consistent.

**Frames 7-10:** The pattern persists. The B-lines remain discrete and well-separated, with clearly visible dark inter-B-line spaces. No coalescence or "white-out" appearance is observed. The sub-pleural region does not demonstrate tissue-like density.

## B-lines Assessment

**Conclusion: lung_rockets = true**

**Subtype: septal**

**Rationale:** Multiple (≥3) discrete, well-spaced B-lines are present per intercostal space across all frames. They arise from the pleural line, extend to the far field without fading, and move with lung sliding. Importantly, dark lung parenchyma remains clearly visible between each B-line, indicating thickened interlobular septa rather than diffuse alveolar flooding.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Conclusion: consolidation = false**

**Consolidation type: null**

**Rationale:** 
- No tissue-like hepatization is seen — the subpleural area does not resemble liver parenchyma
- No shred sign — no irregular deep border between consolidated and aerated lung
- No air bronchograms — no punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within a hepatized field
- The lung architecture beneath the pleural line maintains an aerated (artifact-generating) pattern

## Summary
