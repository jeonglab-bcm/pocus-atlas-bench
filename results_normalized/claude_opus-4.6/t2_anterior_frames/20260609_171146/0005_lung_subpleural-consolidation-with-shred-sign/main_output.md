# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Anterior Zone

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is identified as a hyperechoic horizontal line in the near field. Below the pleural line, there is subpleural tissue-like echogenicity with irregular, hyperechoic structures. The deep portion of the image is mostly anechoic/dark. No clear A-line reverberations are visible.

**Frames 4-6:** Similar findings persist. The bright irregular structures below the pleural line appear to have a shredded, fragmented deep margin where they transition to the darker aerated lung below. Some bright punctate foci are visible within the echogenic tissue.

**Frames 7-10:** The pattern remains consistent. The irregularly-bordered consolidated tissue is visible subpleurally. Some narrow vertical artifacts can be seen at the lateral margins of the consolidated regions, consistent with B-lines arising from areas of partial aeration.

---

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** A-lines are absent throughout the clip. Some discrete vertical hyperechoic artifacts are seen at the margins of the consolidated regions, extending from the pleural line toward the far field. These are relatively few and well-separated.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **Subtype = septal** (discrete, spaced B-lines visible at the periphery of consolidation, with dark lung visible between them)

---

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:**
- **Hepatization:** Subpleural tissue-like echogenicity is present, with echogenicity resembling liver parenchyma replacing normally aerated lung.
- **Shred sign:** The deep margin of the consolidated area is irregular and shredded — a ragged, non-linear boundary separating consolidated from residual aerated lung.
- **Air bronchograms:** Bright punctate and short linear hyperechoic foci are visible within the hepatized region, representing residual air in small airways.

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "shred_sign"** (the irregular, fragmented deep border is the predominant and most consistent feature across all frames)

---

## Summary

| Finding | Result | Subtype |
|---------|--------|---------|
| B-lines | Present | Septal |
| Consolidation | Present | Shred sign |

**Interpretation:** This anterior lung zone demonstrates subpleural consolidation with a classic shred sign, indicating partial alveolar filling/collapse. The coexisting septal B-lines at the margins suggest surrounding interstitial involvement. This pattern is consistent with pneumonia, ARDS, or atelectasis with adjacent interstitial edema.
