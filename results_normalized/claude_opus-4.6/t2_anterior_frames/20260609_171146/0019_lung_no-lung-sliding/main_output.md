# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

# Lung Ultrasound Frame-by-Frame Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1–10 (consistent findings throughout):**
- **Probe:** Linear high-frequency (L14-6Ns), depth ~3.5 cm
- **Near field:** Well-defined layered subcutaneous tissue with normal echotexture
- **Pleural line:** Clearly visible as a bright hyperechoic horizontal line at approximately 1 cm depth
- **Rib shadows:** Two dark, rounded acoustic shadows visible (likely ribs in cross-section), creating the classic "bat sign"
- **Sub-pleural space:** Dominated by horizontal, regularly-spaced reverberation artifacts (A-lines) that repeat at intervals equal to the skin-to-pleura distance
- **Lung sliding:** Subtle frame-to-frame variation at the pleural line suggests preserved lung sliding
- **No vertical artifacts** extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen are identified in any frame

## B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** All 10 frames demonstrate a clear **A-line pattern** — horizontal reverberation artifacts repeating at regular intervals below the pleural line. No hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading are identified.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **Subtype = null**

## Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** The sub-pleural lung between the rib shadows demonstrates normal reverberation artifacts (A-lines). There is:
- No tissue-like hepatization (no liver-like parenchyma)
- No shred sign (no irregular deep border)
- No air bronchograms (no punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within solid-appearing tissue)

**Conclusion:**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

## Summary

This is a **normal anterior lung ultrasound** demonstrating:
- Intact pleural line with lung sliding
- Dominant A-line pattern indicating normal aeration
- No B-lines and no consolidation
- Consistent with "dry" lung (no interstitial or alveolar pathology)
