## Observations

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis:

**Frames 1-5:** The deeper field shows hyperechoic, tissue-like structures with irregular margins. There are bright punctate and linear echogenic foci within the consolidated parenchyma, consistent with **air bronchograms**. The lung tissue has lost its normal aeration pattern and demonstrates a **hepatized (tissue-like)** appearance resembling liver.

**Frames 6-7:** A transition becomes evident — an anechoic (dark/black) space begins to appear superficially, while consolidated, hepatized lung tissue is seen deeper. The irregular, shredded boundary between consolidated and partially aerated lung suggests the **shred sign**.

**Frames 8-10:** A clearly defined **anechoic space** (black fluid collection) is visible, bounded by the pleural line superiorly and a lung line/consolidated tissue inferiorly — consistent with the **quad sign**. Within this effusion, the consolidated/atelectatic lung appears to float and undulate, suggesting the **jellyfish sign**. The lung parenchyma beneath the fluid remains hepatized with visible air bronchograms.

### Key Findings:
- ✅ **Quad sign**: Anechoic fluid bounded by pleural structures
- ✅ **Jellyfish sign**: Atelectatic lung floating within effusion
- ✅ **Hepatization**: Tissue-like consolidated lung
- ✅ **Air bronchograms**: Hyperechoic foci within consolidated lung
- ✅ **Shred sign**: Irregular border of consolidation
