## Observations

### Sequential Frame Analysis:

**Superficial structures**: The top of each frame shows skin, subcutaneous tissue, and muscle layers with typical layered echogenic appearance.

**Anechoic fluid collection**: A large **anechoic (black) space** is clearly visible throughout all frames. This space is bounded:
- Superiorly by the parietal pleural line
- Laterally by rib shadows
- Inferiorly by a "lung line" → **Quad sign** is present

**Consolidated/atelectatic lung**: Within and adjacent to the fluid collection, there is:
- A tissue-like echogenic structure deep to the effusion that resembles liver parenchyma → **hepatization**
- Multiple small, round, hyperechoic foci (punctate bright dots) within the consolidated tissue → **air bronchograms** (particularly visible in frames 3–5, 8–10)
- The consolidated lung appears to float and undulate within the surrounding fluid across sequential frames → **Jellyfish sign**

**Diaphragm**: The bright hyperechoic diagonal line running through several frames represents the diaphragm/pleural reflection.
