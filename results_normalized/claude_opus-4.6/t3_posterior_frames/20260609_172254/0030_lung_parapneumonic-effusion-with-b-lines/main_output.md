1. **Large anechoic (black) space**: A prominent fluid collection occupies much of the imaging field, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib shadows, forming a clear **quad sign**.

4. **Spine sign**: The vertebral bodies are visible through the fluid collection in the far field (bottom of images), confirming the presence of fluid above the diaphragm.

5. **Dynamic movement**: The lung line moves cyclically toward and away from the pleural line across frames, consistent with a **sinusoid sign**.
