# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

## Observations

Analyzing these sequential frames from a PLAPS point ultrasound:

**Effusion findings:**
- **Quad sign**: A large anechoic (black) space is clearly visible, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by compressed lung/diaphragm, with rib shadows laterally. This forms a well-defined quadrilateral shape.
- **Jellyfish sign**: Across multiple frames (particularly frames 1-4), a structure is seen floating and undulating within the large fluid collection. This represents atelectatic lung tissue moving with respiration within the surrounding effusion — classic jellyfish appearance.

**Consolidation findings:**
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: In the dependent/inferior portion of the images (frames 4-9), compressed lung tissue appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver parenchyma.
- **Air bronchograms**: Small punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within the consolidated/atelectatic lung tissue in several frames, representing air-filled bronchi within non-aerated lung.

**Dynamic changes**: Across the sequence, the atelectatic lung structure moves cyclically toward and away from the chest wall, confirming respiratory motion within the fluid (sinusoid sign equivalent).
