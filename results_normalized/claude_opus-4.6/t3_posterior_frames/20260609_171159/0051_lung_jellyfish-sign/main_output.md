### Sequential Frame Analysis:

**Frames 1-3:** A large **anechoic (black) space** dominates the image, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by a visible lung line/compressed lung structure, with rib shadows laterally — forming a classic **quad sign**. **Frames 4-6:** The compressed lung tissue is clearly visible within the effusion. It appears to undulate and move toward the pleural line cyclically, consistent with a **jellyfish sign** — atelectatic lung floating within a large effusion.

Multiple **punctate hyperechoic foci** (bright dots) are visible within this consolidated tissue, consistent with **air bronchograms**. This represents compressive atelectasis/consolidation.
