## Observations

### Near Field (Chest Wall)
- The chest wall structures and pleural line are visible at the top of each frame.

### Mid-Field (Anechoic Space)
- A large **anechoic (black) space** is clearly present throughout the sequence, bounded superiorly by the parietal pleural line and inferiorly by a deeper echogenic structure. This forms a **quad sign**, indicating **pleural effusion**.

### Deep Field (Echogenic Structures within Fluid)
- Within and at the base of this fluid collection, there is an **irregular, echogenic, tissue-like structure** that changes shape and position across frames.
- In frames 6–9, this structure appears to **float and undulate** within the surrounding fluid, consistent with the **jellyfish sign** — atelectatic lung floating in effusion.
- The lung tissue itself demonstrates **hepatization** (tissue-like, liver-like echotexture), with loss of normal aeration artifacts.
- Scattered **hyperechoic punctate foci** within the consolidated tissue are consistent with **air bronchograms**.

### Dynamic Features
- Across sequential frames, the atelectatic lung moves cyclically toward and away from the chest wall, also suggestive of a **sinusoid sign** pattern.
