## Observations

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis:

**Frames 1-3:** A large **anechoic (black) space** dominates the image, clearly bounded superiorly by the parietal pleural line and inferiorly by a visible lung line. Within this fluid collection, a mobile, echogenic structure is seen floating and undulating — this is classic for the **jellyfish sign** (atelectatic lung floating freely in effusion).

**Frames 4-6:** The view shifts slightly, continuing to demonstrate the large effusion. The compressed lung is seen at the dependent border, appearing **tissue-like/hepatized** — resembling liver parenchyma in echogenicity. Small **punctate hyperechoic foci** (air bronchograms) are visible within this compressed lung tissue.

**Frames 7-10:** The effusion remains prominent. The atelectatic lung sinks toward the dependent zone and appears increasingly consolidated with a **shredded irregular border** between aerated and non-aerated lung.

### Key Signs Identified:
- ✅ **Quad sign**: Anechoic space bounded by pleural line, lung line, and rib shadows
- ✅ **Jellyfish sign**: Floating, undulating atelectatic lung within effusion
- ✅ **Hepatization**: Tissue-like appearance of compressed lung
- ✅ **Air bronchograms**: Punctate hyperechoic foci within consolidated lung
