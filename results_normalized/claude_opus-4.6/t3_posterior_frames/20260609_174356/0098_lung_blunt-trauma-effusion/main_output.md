# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

## Observations

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis:

**Frames 1-4:** A large **anechoic (black) space** is visible in the dependent region, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by a brighter lung line/tissue structure. This forms a clear **quad sign**, consistent with a significant **pleural effusion**.

**Frames 5-7:** Within and adjacent to the fluid collection, there is a **tissue-like structure** with echogenicity resembling liver parenchyma (hepatization). Scattered **hyperechoic punctate and linear foci** are visible within this tissue, consistent with **air bronchograms** within consolidated lung.

**Frames 8-9:** A striking image shows atelectatic, consolidated lung floating and undulating within the surrounding anechoic effusion — the classic **jellyfish sign**. The lung tissue moves freely within the fluid space.

**Frame 10:** Shows the consolidated lung with an irregular, **shredded deep border** (shred sign) where it interfaces with remaining aerated lung.

## Conclusion

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**

There is a **large pleural effusion** (quad sign, jellyfish sign) with **compressive atelectasis/consolidation** of the underlying lung (hepatization, air bronchograms, shred sign). The jellyfish sign indicates passive atelectasis of lung floating within a substantial effusion — a classic PLAPS-positive pattern seen in the posterior dependent zone.
