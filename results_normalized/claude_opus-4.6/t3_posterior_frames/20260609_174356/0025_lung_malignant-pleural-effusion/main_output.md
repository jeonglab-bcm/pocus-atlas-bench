## Observations

### Sequential Frame Analysis:

**Frames 1-3 (Early):** A large **anechoic (black) space** is visible in the dependent region, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by a distinct lung line — consistent with the **quad sign**. Within this fluid, a tissue-like structure is visible.

**Frames 4-7 (Mid):** The consolidated lung tissue becomes more prominent. It has a **hepatized (tissue-like/liver-like) echotexture** with scattered **punctate hyperechoic foci** consistent with **air bronchograms**. The atelectatic lung appears to undulate and float within the surrounding effusion — this represents the **jellyfish sign**.

**Frames 8-10 (Late):** The lung tissue moves cyclically toward and away from the chest wall within the fluid collection. The border between consolidated and aerated lung appears irregular (**shred sign**). The effusion remains clearly visible surrounding the consolidated lung.

### Signs Identified:
- ✅ **Quad sign** — anechoic bounded fluid space
- ✅ **Jellyfish sign** — floating, undulating atelectatic lung in effusion
- ✅ **Hepatization** — tissue-like echogenicity of lung parenchyma
- ✅ **Air bronchograms** — hyperechoic punctate/linear foci within consolidated lung
- ✅ **Shred sign** — irregular deep border of consolidation
