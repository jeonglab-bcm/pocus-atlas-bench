# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

## Observations

Across these sequential frames from the PLAPS point, I observe the following:

### Effusion Signs:
- **Quad sign**: A large **anechoic (black) space** is clearly visible, bounded superiorly by the parietal pleural line and inferiorly by a lung line, with rib shadows laterally. This represents a significant **pleural effusion**.
- **Jellyfish sign**: Within the effusion, there is a **floating, undulating tissue structure** that moves across the frames — this is atelectatic lung tissue suspended in the surrounding fluid, resembling a jellyfish in water. This is particularly well seen in frames 1–4 and 7–8.

### Consolidation Signs:
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: The floating/dependent lung parenchyma appears **solid and echogenic**, resembling liver texture. It has lost its normal aeration pattern.
- **Air bronchograms**: Within the hepatized lung tissue, there are **punctate hyperechoic foci** visible in several frames, representing air trapped within bronchi surrounded by consolidated lung.

### Summary:
A **large pleural effusion** is present with **atelectatic/consolidated lung** floating within it. The lung tissue demonstrates hepatization and air bronchograms, confirming both fluid and parenchymal consolidation.
