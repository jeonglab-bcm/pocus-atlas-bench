# 0029_lung_parapneumonic-effusion

## Observations

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis:

**Frames 1–2:** A large **anechoic (black) space** is clearly visible, representing a significant fluid collection. Within this fluid, a structure with tissue-like echogenicity is seen floating and undulating — consistent with atelectatic lung. Bright punctate/linear hyperechoic foci are visible within this tissue, suggesting **air bronchograms**.

**Frames 3–5:** The anechoic effusion remains prominent. The atelectatic lung tissue moves toward and away from the chest wall with respiration, demonstrating the **jellyfish sign** — a floating, undulating piece of lung within surrounding fluid.

**Frames 6–8:** The consolidated lung becomes more prominent as it moves within the effusion. It has a **hepatized (tissue-like) appearance**, resembling liver parenchyma. The deep border of the consolidated segment has an irregular, **shredded appearance (shred sign)** where it interfaces with any remaining aerated lung.

**Frames 9–10:** The lung continues its oscillatory motion within the effusion, confirming the dynamic jellyfish sign. Air bronchograms are visible as bright echogenic dots/lines within the hepatized tissue.

### Signs Identified:
- ✅ **Quad sign** — Anechoic bounded fluid space
- ✅ **Jellyfish sign** — Floating atelectatic lung in effusion
- ✅ **Hepatization** — Tissue-like consolidated lung
- ✅ **Air bronchograms** — Hyperechoic foci within consolidation
- ✅ **Shred sign** — Irregular deep border
