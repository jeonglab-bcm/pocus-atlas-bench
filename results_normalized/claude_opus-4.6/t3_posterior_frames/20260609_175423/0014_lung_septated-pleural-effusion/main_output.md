## Observations

Analyzing the sequential frames from this PLAPS point ultrasound:

### Effusion Signs:
- **Quad sign**: A large **anechoic (black) space** is clearly visible in the dependent zone, bounded superiorly by the parietal pleural line and inferiorly by a bright "lung line" (visceral pleura of the compressed/atelectatic lung), with rib shadows forming lateral boundaries.
- **Jellyfish sign**: Across the sequential frames, the echogenic atelectatic lung tissue is seen **floating and undulating** within the surrounding effusion. The movement pattern from frame to frame shows the lung swaying within the fluid, resembling a jellyfish in water.

### Consolidation Signs:
- **Hepatization (tissue-like pattern)**: The compressed lung tissue deep to the effusion appears **solid and echogenic**, resembling liver parenchyma in texture. The normal aeration pattern is completely lost.
- **Air bronchograms**: Within the hepatized lung, **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** are visible, representing residual air within bronchi surrounded by consolidated tissue.
- **Shred sign**: Some frames show an **irregular, shredded border** at the interface between the consolidated lung and any remaining aerated lung deeper in the field.

### Dynamic Assessment:
The sequential frames demonstrate respiratory variation in the position of the atelectatic lung within the effusion, confirming the dynamic nature of the jellyfish sign.
