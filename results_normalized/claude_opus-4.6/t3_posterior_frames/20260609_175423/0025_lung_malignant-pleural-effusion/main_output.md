- **Quad sign**: A large **anechoic (black) space** is clearly visible in the dependent region, bounded by the pleural line superiorly and a lung line inferiorly, forming a quadrilateral fluid collection. This is especially prominent in frames 9-10.

### Dynamic Changes:
Across the sequence, the atelectatic lung moves cyclically within the effusion, confirming the dynamic nature of both the fluid collection and the compressive atelectasis.
