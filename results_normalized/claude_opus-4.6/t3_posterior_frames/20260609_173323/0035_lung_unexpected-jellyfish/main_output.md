## Observations

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis:

Across all 10 sequential frames, I observe the following consistent findings:

1. **Large anechoic (black) space**: A significant fluid collection is present throughout the sequence, appearing as a well-defined dark region. This is bounded superiorly by the parietal pleural line and inferiorly by a visible lung line, forming a **quad sign**.

2. **Tissue-like/hepatized lung**: Adjacent to and partially surrounded by the effusion, there is echogenic solid-appearing tissue that resembles liver parenchyma in texture. This represents consolidated/atelectatic lung.

3. **Jellyfish sign**: In several frames (particularly frames 1, 3, 8, and 10), the atelectatic lung tissue appears to undulate and float within the surrounding effusion, giving the classic "jellyfish in water" appearance. The movement between frames confirms the dynamic, floating nature of compressive atelectasis.

4. **Air bronchograms**: Within the hepatized lung tissue, scattered hyperechoic punctate and linear foci are visible, representing air-filled bronchi within the consolidated parenchyma.

5. **Shred sign**: The deep border of the consolidated lung has an irregular, shredded margin where it interfaces with residual aerated lung.
