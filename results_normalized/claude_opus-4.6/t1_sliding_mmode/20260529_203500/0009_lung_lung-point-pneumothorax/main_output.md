## Overall Decision

There is a **clear spatial transition**: positions p00–p02 show seashore (sliding present) while positions p03–p08 show stratosphere (sliding absent). This coexistence of both patterns across different spatial positions indicates a **lung point** — the boundary between sliding and non-sliding lung.

**Overall Label: BOTH (Lung Point)**
