## Per-Position Classifications

### p00 (Image 1)
**Observation:** Clear horizontal parallel lines occupy the upper ~25% (chest wall). Below the pleural line, the image is predominantly dark with a distinctly granular/sandy texture. The pleural line appears slightly wavy.
**Classification:** **Seashore**

### p01 (Image 2)
**Observation:** Horizontal lines above, bright pleural line with some irregularity. Below: granular texture with 2-3 vertical dark columns (rib shadows over time). Background between columns is clearly sandy/speckled.
**Classification:** **Seashore**

### p02 (Image 3)
**Observation:** More prominent horizontal lines extending from above. Pleural line area shows irregularity/waviness. Below: A-lines are visible but the background texture between them retains granularity. Vertical columns persist.
**Classification:** **Seashore**

### p03 (Image 4)
**Observation:** Dense horizontal lines fill most of the frame from top to bottom. The pattern transitions through the pleural line level with minimal texture change. Between the lines, very little granularity is visible — mostly continuous linear structures. Pleural line is relatively straight.
**Classification:** **Stratosphere**

### p04 (Image 5)
**Observation:** Dense continuous parallel horizontal lines throughout the entire image. No obvious texture change at the pleural line — the barcode pattern continues unchanged from above to below. Minimal granularity anywhere.
**Classification:** **Stratosphere**

### p05 (Image 6)
**Observation:** Dense horizontal lines dominate throughout. Some subtle brightness variation but the background between lines appears linear, not granular. Pattern is essentially unchanged across the pleural line depth.
**Classification:** **Stratosphere**

### p06 (Image 7)
**Observation:** Dense horizontal parallel lines with dark vertical columns. The pattern of lines persists both above and below the pleural line. Between the bright lines, texture is linear/uniform rather than granular.
**Classification:** **Stratosphere**

### p07 (Image 8)
**Observation:** Well-organized horizontal lines in upper half. Below: structured rectangular dark regions bounded by bright horizontal lines. The overall appearance is barcode-like with organized horizontal structures throughout.
**Classification:** **Stratosphere**

### p08 (Image 9)
**Observation:** Horizontal lines above, identifiable pleural line. Below: predominantly dark with granular/sandy texture returning. Faint A-lines present but background between them is clearly granular. Vertical columns visible.
**Classification:** **Seashore**

### p09 (Image 10)
**Observation:** Very dark throughout with minimal signal. Only a bright line at the very top. Insufficient detail to determine pattern.
**Classification:** **Unclassifiable**
