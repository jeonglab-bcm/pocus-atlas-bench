## Overall Decision

There is a **spatial transition** from seashore (p00–p08) to stratosphere (p09) at the rightmost lateral position. This coexistence of both patterns across different positions indicates a **lung point** — the boundary between sliding and non-sliding lung.

**Overall label: both**
