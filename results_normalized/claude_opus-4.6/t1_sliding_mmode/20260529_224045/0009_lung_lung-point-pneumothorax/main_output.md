## Overall Decision

There is a clear **spatial transition**: positions p00–p03 show seashore (sliding present), while positions p04–p08 show stratosphere (sliding absent). This coexistence of both patterns across different spatial positions is the hallmark of a **lung point**.

## **Overall Label: BOTH (Lung Point)**
