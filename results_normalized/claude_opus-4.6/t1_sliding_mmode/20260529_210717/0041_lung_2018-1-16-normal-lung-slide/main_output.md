# Lung Ultrasound M-Mode Analysis

## Per-Position Classification

### p00 (~5% from left)
**Observation:** Clear horizontal parallel lines above the pleural line. Below, there is obvious granular/sandy texture with scattered bright echoes. The pleural line appears irregular/wavy.
**Classification: Seashore** ✓

### p01 (~15%)
**Observation:** Horizontal lines above. Below the pleural line, granular sandy texture is visible with a bright wavy pleural line. A-line visible but embedded in sandy background.
**Classification: Seashore** ✓

### p02 (~25%)
**Observation:** Horizontal lines above. Below the pleural line, two distinct dark vertical columns alternate with areas of granular texture. This represents periods of no-sliding alternating with sliding.
**Classification: Alternating (lung point)** ⚡

### p03 (~35%)
**Observation:** Horizontal lines above. Below, relatively uniform granular texture is maintained. No clear dark banding.
**Classification: Seashore** ✓

### p04 (~45%)
**Observation:** Similar to p03 — subtle granular texture below the pleural line with some horizontal structure. Granularity still present.
**Classification: Seashore** ✓

### p05 (~55%)
**Observation:** Granularity becoming subtler but still present. Horizontal lines more prominent but sandy background texture persists between them.
**Classification: Seashore** ✓

### p06 (~65%)
**Observation:** Distinct dark vertical bands emerging below the pleural line, alternating with textured/brighter areas. Pattern similar to p07 but less pronounced.
**Classification: Alternating (lung point)** ⚡

### p07 (~75%)
**Observation:** Very distinct dark vertical columns below the pleural line separated by brighter granular areas. Classic alternating seashore/stratosphere pattern.
**Classification: Alternating (lung point)** ⚡

### p08 (~85%)
**Observation:** Predominantly horizontal banding below the pleural line. Dark rectangular areas visible. Minimal granular texture — mostly organized parallel lines.
**Classification: Stratosphere** ✗

### p09 (~95%)
**Observation:** Very regular, evenly-spaced parallel horizontal lines throughout the entire image. No granular texture anywhere. Classic barcode/stratosphere pattern.
**Classification: Stratosphere** ✗

## Overall Decision: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

**Reasoning:** Multiple positions (p02, p06, p07) show alternating patterns, AND there is a clear spatial transition from seashore (left side: p00–p05) to stratosphere (right side: p08–p09). This confirms the presence of a lung point — the boundary where the lung intermittently contacts the chest wall, consistent with a pneumothorax with partial lung sliding.
