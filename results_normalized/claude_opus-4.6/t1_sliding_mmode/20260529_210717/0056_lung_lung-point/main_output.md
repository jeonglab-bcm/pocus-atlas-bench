**Classification: Stratosphere**

### p08 (~85% lateral)

**Classification: Stratosphere**

### p09 (~95% lateral)
**Observation:** Clear bright horizontal lines in upper ~35-40%, dark area below with faint non-linear texture. Absence of horizontal lines below the pleural line; faint granular/noisy texture visible.

## Overall Decision

There is a clear **spatial transition**: positions p00–p04 (left side) show seashore pattern (pleural sliding present), while positions p05–p08 (right side) show stratosphere/barcode pattern (pleural sliding absent). This coexistence of both patterns across different lateral positions indicates a **lung point**.

## **Overall Label: BOTH (Lung Point)**
