## Overall Decision

Multiple positions (p02, p06, p07, p08) show **alternating patterns**, AND there is a clear spatial transition from **seashore** (left/medial positions: p00-p05) to **stratosphere** (rightmost position: p09), with the alternating/lung point zones at the transition boundary (p02, p06-p08).

## **Overall Label: BOTH (Lung Point Present)**

This pattern is consistent with a **partial pneumothorax** where the lung point (the edge of the pneumothorax where visceral and parietal pleura intermittently contact) is located in the mid-to-lateral portion of the scanning field.
