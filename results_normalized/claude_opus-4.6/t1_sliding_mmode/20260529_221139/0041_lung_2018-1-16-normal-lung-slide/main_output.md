## Overall Decision

Multiple positions (p06, p07, p08) show **alternating patterns** (temporal cycling between seashore and stratosphere). Additionally, there is a clear **spatial transition** from seashore (p00–p05) to stratosphere (p09), with alternating patterns at the boundary positions (p06–p08).

**Overall Label: BOTH (Lung Point)**

The findings indicate a lung point where the lung intermittently contacts the chest wall at the boundary of what is likely a pneumothorax (right side/stratosphere) and normally sliding lung (left side/seashore).
