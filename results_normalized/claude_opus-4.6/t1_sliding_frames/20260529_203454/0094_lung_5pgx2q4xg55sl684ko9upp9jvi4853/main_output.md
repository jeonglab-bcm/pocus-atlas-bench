- A bright, curved diaphragmatic line is visible
- Above the diaphragm, there is tissue-like echotexture (hepatized lung)

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

This clip demonstrates **consolidated lung (hepatization) at the right base** with **dynamic air bronchograms**. The movement of the consolidated lung tissue and shifting of air bronchograms across frames confirms ongoing respiratory motion and pleural sliding. The dynamic nature of the air bronchograms also suggests this consolidation is more likely **pneumonia** rather than obstructive atelectasis.
