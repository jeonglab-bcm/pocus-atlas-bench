## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern, the granular/shimmering quality of the pleural line, and the intermittent comet-tail artifacts all confirm normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition with active sliding motion. This is consistent with a normally aerated, ventilated lung at this zone.
