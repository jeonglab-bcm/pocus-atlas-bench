## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Pleural Line Characteristics:

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is visible as a bright, hyperechoic interface approximately 3-4 cm deep. It appears **granular and textured** rather than smooth and sharply defined. Below it, there are horizontal reverberation lines (A-lines) with some variation in the speckle pattern between frames.

**Frames 4-6:** The pleural line maintains its shimmering, granular quality. The sub-pleural speckle pattern shows **subtle but definite changes** between consecutive frames. B-lines or short comet-tail artifacts can be seen originating from the pleural line.

**Frames 7-8:** Continued variation in the sub-pleural texture. The A-lines are present but are **not perfectly static** — there is slight variation in their brightness and definition between frames.

**Frames 9-10:** The pleural line continues to show a textured, bright interface. The sub-pleural pattern remains dynamic with frame-to-frame variation in the granular speckle below the pleura.

---

## Key Findings:

| Sign | Observation |
|------|------------|
| Pleural line texture | Granular/shimmering, not smooth or static |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation | Present between consecutive frames |
| B-lines/comet-tail artifacts | Present intermittently |
| Static A-lines | NOT seen — A-lines show subtle variation |
| Barcode/stratosphere pattern | Absent |

---

## Conclusion: **Pleural Sliding is PRESENT**

The evidence supports active pleural sliding: the pleural line demonstrates a granular, shimmering quality; there is clear frame-to-frame variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern; and B-lines are visible. There is no evidence of a fixed, smooth pleural line or static A-lines that would suggest absent sliding.
