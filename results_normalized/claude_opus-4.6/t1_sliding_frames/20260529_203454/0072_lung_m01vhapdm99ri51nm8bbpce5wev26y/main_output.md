## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The multiple B-lines originating from the pleural line, the granular/irregular pleural surface (with sub-pleural nodules), and frame-to-frame variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern all confirm active lung sliding. This is consistent with **miliary tuberculosis**, where disseminated sub-pleural micronodules create multiple B-lines while the visceral and parietal pleura remain in apposition with preserved sliding.
