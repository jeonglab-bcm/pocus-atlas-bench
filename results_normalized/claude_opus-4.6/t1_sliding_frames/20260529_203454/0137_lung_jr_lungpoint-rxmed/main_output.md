# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations:

**Frames 1-3:** A hyperechoic pleural line is visible in the upper portion of the field. The pleural line appears somewhat textured/granular. Below the pleural line, the sub-pleural speckle pattern shows variation between these frames, with shifting echogenic patterns.

**Frames 4-6:** The pleural line continues to appear bright with a slightly irregular/shimmering quality. The sub-pleural tissue architecture changes between consecutive frames — the arrangement of gray-scale speckle differs, indicating movement at the visceral-parietal pleural interface.

**Frames 7-8:** A-lines (horizontal reverberation artifacts) are visible below the pleural line. However, these are not perfectly static — subtle shifts in their intensity and the surrounding speckle pattern are noted between frames.

**Frames 9-10:** The pleural line maintains its textured appearance. The sub-pleural pattern continues to show frame-to-frame variation, consistent with ongoing lung movement beneath the transducer.

## Key Findings:

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| Pleural line texture | Granular/shimmering — not smooth or static |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation | Present between consecutive frames |
| Static A-lines | Not perfectly static across all frames |
| Comet-tail artifacts | Subtle short artifacts seen in some frames |

## Conclusion: **Pleural Sliding is PRESENT**

The evidence supports active pleural sliding based on:
1. **Shimmering/granular pleural line** appearance across multiple frames
2. **Frame-to-frame variation** in the sub-pleural speckle pattern (the most reliable B-mode sign)
3. Absence of a perfectly static, smooth pleural line or unchanging A-line pattern

No lung point or transition zone was identified.
