# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Pleural Sliding Assessment

**Frames 1-3 (Time series):**
- A bright, irregular, somewhat **granular pleural line** is visible at the top of the image
- Below the pleural line, there is a **tissue-like (hepatized) echotexture** rather than normal aerated lung with clean A-lines

- Sub-pleural tissue movement is appreciable across the sequence

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The images show **consolidated (hepatized) lung tissue** with preserved pleural sliding. The dynamic changes in sub-pleural speckle between frames, the granular/shimmering pleural line, and the absence of a barcode/stratosphere pattern all confirm lung movement against the chest wall. This pattern is consistent with **lung consolidation** (e.g., pneumonia) where the visceral and parietal pleura remain apposed and sliding is maintained.
