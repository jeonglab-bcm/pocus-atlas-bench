# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations:

**Frames 1-10:**
- A bright, well-defined **pleural line** is visible in the upper portion of each image
- The pleural line appears **smooth and sharply delineated** without significant shimmering or granular texture changes between frames
- Multiple **horizontal A-lines** are visible below the pleural line and remain essentially **unchanged** across all frames
- The **sub-pleural speckle pattern** shows minimal to no variation between consecutive frames
- The overall image composition remains remarkably **static** throughout the sequence

## Key Findings:

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| Pleural line appearance | Smooth, sharp, static — no shimmering |
| A-lines | Present and perfectly unchanged across frames |
| Sub-pleural speckle | No meaningful variation between frames |
| Comet-tail/B-lines | Absent |
| Transition point | Not identified |

## Conclusion: **Pleural Sliding is ABSENT**

The findings are consistent with a **stratosphere/barcode pattern equivalent** in B-mode:
- Static, well-defined pleural line
- Unchanging A-lines across all frames
- No variation in sub-pleural texture

This pattern raises concern for **pneumothorax** at this scanning location, or could indicate pleurodesis, absence of ventilation, or mainstem intubation on the contralateral side. Clinical correlation and identification of a **lung point** would help confirm the diagnosis.
