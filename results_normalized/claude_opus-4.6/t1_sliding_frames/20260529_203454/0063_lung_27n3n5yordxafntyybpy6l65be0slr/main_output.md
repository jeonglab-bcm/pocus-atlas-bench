## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The evidence supporting this includes:
1. **Confluent B-lines ("white lung")** — B-lines originate from sliding pleura
2. **Granular/shimmering pleural line** rather than a sharply static line
3. **Frame-to-frame speckle variation** below the pleural line, indicating visceral-parietal pleural movement
4. **Absence of stratosphere sign features** (no static, unchanging horizontal lines)

The "white lung" pattern seen here is pathological (suggesting interstitial syndrome/pulmonary edema) but confirms pleural apposition and active sliding.
