# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1–3:**
- A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is visible in the upper portion
- Below the pleural line, horizontal A-lines are present
- The **left portion** shows granular, shimmering sub-pleural texture with speckle variation between frames
- The **right portion** appears darker, more static, and uniform

**Frames 4–6:**
- The pleural line remains bright
- A clear **vertical boundary/transition zone** becomes apparent dividing the image
- Left side: more textured, granular pattern with frame-to-frame variation (suggesting movement)
- Right side: smoother, static pattern with minimal variation between frames

**Frames 7–9:**
- A-lines are prominently visible, especially on the left side
- Sub-pleural speckle continues to change on the left side between frames
- Right side remains relatively static and uniform

**Frame 10:**
- A distinct transition point is visible on the pleural line
- The left portion shows brighter, more echogenic tissue
- The right portion appears darker and motionless

## Key Findings

| Feature | Left Side | Right Side |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Pleural texture | Shimmering/granular | Smooth/static |
| Sub-pleural speckle | Varies between frames | Unchanged |
| A-lines | Present with variation | Rigidly fixed |

## Conclusion: **BOTH – Lung Point Present**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point**, characterized by:
- A visible **transition zone** where lung sliding is present (left) and absent (right)
- One segment of the pleural line shimmers while the adjacent segment remains fixed
- Sub-pleural speckle changes on one side but not the other

This is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax**, marking the exact boundary where the visceral pleura intermittently contacts the parietal pleura.
