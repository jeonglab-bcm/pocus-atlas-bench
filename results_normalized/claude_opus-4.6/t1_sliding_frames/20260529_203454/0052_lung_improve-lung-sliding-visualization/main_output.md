# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations:

### Early Frames (2/270 – 89/270):
- **Pleural line:** Appears bright, somewhat textured/granular
- **Sub-pleural pattern:** Heterogeneous speckle pattern with visible variation between consecutive frames
- **Artifacts:** Some comet-tail artifacts and scattered B-line-like artifacts visible originating from the pleural line
- **A-lines:** Present but with subtle textural variation between frames
- The field of view is wider, suggesting deeper penetration of structures

### Transition Frames (116/270 – 147/270):
- **Pleural line:** Begins to appear smoother and more sharply defined
- **Sub-pleural pattern:** Decreasing variation between frames
- A visible shift in the character of the image below the pleural line

### Late Frames (174/270 – 265/270):
- **Pleural line:** Smooth, sharply defined, and static-appearing
- **Sub-pleural pattern:** Horizontal, repetitive A-lines that appear unchanged across frames
- **No speckle variation** between consecutive frames
- Pattern is consistent with **stratosphere/barcode sign** equivalent (static, no sliding)

## Conclusion:

**BOTH (Lung Point)**

There is a clear transition visible across the sweep:
- **Sliding PRESENT** in earlier frames (granular pleural line, sub-pleural speckle variation, comet-tail artifacts)
- **Sliding ABSENT** in later frames (smooth static pleural line, unchanged A-lines, no speckle variation)

This transition point is characteristic of a **lung point**, which is pathognomonic for **pneumothorax** — representing the boundary where the visceral pleura intermittently contacts the chest wall (sliding present) versus where air separates the two pleural surfaces (sliding absent).
