# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Pleural Sliding Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is visible at approximately 1.5 cm depth as a bright hyperechoic interface. A rib shadow is seen on the left. The sub-pleural speckle pattern shows subtle variations between these frames, with a granular texture visible at the pleural interface.

**Frames 4-6:** The pleural line continues to demonstrate a textured, shimmering quality rather than a smooth, static line. Bright comet-tail artifacts/short B-lines are emerging from the pleural line. The sub-pleural pattern varies noticeably from previous frames.

**Frames 7-8:** A more prominent vertical hyperechoic artifact (B-line) is visible extending below the pleural line. The sub-pleural texture continues to differ from earlier frames.

**Frames 9-10:** Continued variation in sub-pleural speckle. Bright artifacts near the pleural line persist, and the pattern below the pleural line remains dynamic.

## Key Observations

| Sign | Finding |
|------|---------|
| Pleural line texture | Granular/shimmering, not smooth or static |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation | **Present** between consecutive frames |
| Comet-tail artifacts/B-lines | **Present** in multiple frames |
| Static A-lines | **Not observed** |
| Barcode/stratosphere sign | **Not observed** |

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The evidence supporting this includes: (1) variation in sub-pleural speckle between consecutive frames indicating lung movement along the chest wall, (2) comet-tail artifacts/B-lines originating from the pleural line, and (3) a granular, shimmering pleural line rather than a smooth, sharply defined static line.
