## Conclusion: **BOTH — Lung Point**

This sequence demonstrates a **lung point sign**, where the pleura intermittently slides (lung touching chest wall) and is absent (air interposed between lung and chest wall). This is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax** and indicates the border where the pneumothorax meets the partially collapsed lung.
