# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Pleural Sliding Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Pleural Line:**
- A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is visible in the near field across all frames
- The pleural line appears to have a **granular/shimmering texture** rather than being perfectly smooth and sharply defined
- There is subtle variation in the brightness and texture of the pleural line between consecutive frames

**A-lines:**
- Multiple horizontal reverberation lines (A-lines) are visible below the pleural line
- However, these A-lines show **subtle variations in intensity and pattern** between frames — they are not perfectly static and unchanged

**Sub-pleural Speckle Pattern:**
- Comparing consecutive frames (e.g., frames 1→2, 3→4, 5→6), there are **discernible differences in the sub-pleural speckle/texture pattern**
- The granular pattern below the pleural line shifts slightly between frames, consistent with lung parenchyma moving against the chest wall

**B-lines/Comet-tails:**
- No prominent B-lines are identified

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

**Supporting evidence:**
- ✅ Granular/textured pleural line (shimmering appearance)
- ✅ Frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern
- ✅ Subtle changes in A-line intensity between frames
- ❌ No perfectly static, smooth pleural line
- ❌ No barcode/stratosphere pattern

This pattern is consistent with a **normal A-line profile with lung sliding**, indicating aerated lung with visceral and parietal pleura gliding against each other during respiration.
