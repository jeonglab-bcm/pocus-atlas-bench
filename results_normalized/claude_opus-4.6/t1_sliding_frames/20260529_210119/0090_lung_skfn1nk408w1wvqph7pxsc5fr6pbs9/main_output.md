| Feature | Observation |

## Key Findings

**Signs of Sliding PRESENT:**
- ✅ Subtle but definite **changes in sub-pleural speckle pattern** between consecutive frames (compare frames 3-4 and 7-8 especially)
- ✅ Pleural line demonstrates some **granular/textured appearance** rather than a perfectly smooth, static line
- ✅ Slight variation in A-line brightness/sharpness across frames, consistent with moving pleural interface

**Signs of Sliding ABSENT:**
- ❌ Pleural line is not completely smooth and static
- ❌ A-lines are not perfectly unchanged across all frames

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The sub-pleural speckle pattern changes between consecutive frames, and the pleural line demonstrates subtle textural variation consistent with visceral-parietal pleural movement. The prominent A-lines with sliding represent a **normal lung aeration pattern** (A-profile). There is no evidence of a lung point or absent sliding.
