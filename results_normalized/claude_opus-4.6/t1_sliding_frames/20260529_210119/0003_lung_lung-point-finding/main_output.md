## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Pleural Line Assessment
Across all 10 frames, I observe:
- A **bright hyperechoic pleural line** visible beneath the subcutaneous tissue layers
- The pleural line has a **granular, shimmering texture** rather than appearing perfectly smooth and static
- There is subtle but noticeable **textural variation** at the pleural interface between consecutive frames

### Sub-pleural Region
- Horizontal **A-lines** are present below the pleural line
- Comparing consecutive frames, the **speckle pattern below the pleural line shows subtle changes** — the grainy texture shifts slightly between frames
- No prominent B-lines or comet-tail artifacts are identified

### A-line Behavior
- A-lines are visible but show **slight variation in intensity/clarity** across frames, rather than remaining perfectly static and unchanging

### Key Differentiating Observations
- The pleural line is **not perfectly smooth or sharply static** — it has a textured, granular quality
- Sub-pleural speckle **differs between consecutive frames**
- No barcode/stratosphere pattern characteristics
- No lung point (transition zone) identified

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The granular/shimmering pleural line and the frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern confirm active visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement. The presence of A-lines with sliding indicates a **normal aerated lung** (A-profile), which is reassuring against pneumothorax at this scanning location.
