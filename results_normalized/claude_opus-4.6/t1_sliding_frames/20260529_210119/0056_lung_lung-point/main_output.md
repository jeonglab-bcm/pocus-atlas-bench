# 0056_lung_lung-point

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Anatomical Orientation
Across all frames, I identify:
- Superficial soft tissue layers (skin, subcutaneous fat, muscle) in the upper portion
- A bright hyperechoic **pleural line** at approximately 1–1.5 cm depth
- A posterior acoustic shadow on the left (likely rib shadow)
- Sub-pleural lung tissue/artifacts below the pleural line

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1–3:** The pleural line is visible with a granular/textured appearance. Sub-pleural speckle pattern shows subtle variations between consecutive frames. Mild comet-tail artifacts are noted.

**Frames 4–6:** Progressive changes in the sub-pleural echogenicity are evident. Bright echogenic foci below the pleural line change position/configuration, indicating movement. The pleural line maintains a shimmering quality rather than a smooth, static appearance.

**Frames 7–8:** A prominent **comet-tail artifact/B-line** appears extending vertically from the pleural line, which is a hallmark of visceral pleura apposed to the chest wall and actively sliding.

**Frames 9–10:** Continued variation in sub-pleural speckle and artifact configuration. The bright structures below the pleural line shift in appearance compared to earlier frames.

## Key Findings

| Sign | Present? |
|------|----------|

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** 

The evidence supporting this includes: (1) clear variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames, (2) comet-tail artifacts/B-lines originating from the pleural line, and (3) a textured/granular pleural line rather than a smooth, fixed line. There is no evidence of a barcode pattern or lung point.
