# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Pleural Sliding Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations:

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is visible as a bright hyperechoic line approximately 1.5-2 cm deep. Superficial layers (subcutaneous tissue, intercostal muscles) are well-delineated above. Below the pleural line, the sub-pleural speckle pattern shows subtle but definite variation between these consecutive frames.

**Frames 4-6:** The pleural line maintains a granular, textured (shimmering) appearance rather than a smooth/static line. Some comet-tail-like artifacts are noted emanating from the pleural line, particularly in frame 5. The sub-pleural echotexture continues to vary.

**Frames 7-9:** Continued variation in sub-pleural speckle. Frame 8 shows notable change in the pattern below the pleural line compared to adjacent frames. The tissue architecture above the pleural line remains relatively constant while the area below changes.

**Frames 10:** The pleural line remains bright and textured. Sub-pleural variation persists.

## Key Findings:

| Sign | Observed? |
|------|-----------|
| Shimmering/granular pleural line | ✅ Yes |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation between frames | ✅ Yes |
| Comet-tail artifacts | ✅ Subtle, intermittent |
| Static/smooth pleural line | ❌ No |
| Perfectly unchanged A-lines | ❌ No |
| Lung point transition | ❌ No |

## Conclusion:

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The textured, shimmering pleural line and the clear variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames confirm lung sliding at the visceral-parietal pleural interface. This is consistent with a normal lung surface in contact with the chest wall.
