## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The evidence includes clearly visible B-lines originating from the pleural line, subtle inter-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern, and a textured (rather than static) pleural line. These findings are consistent with **miliary tuberculosis** — the sub-pleural nodules and confluent B-lines reflect diffuse interstitial/sub-pleural disease with preserved lung sliding (visceral pleura remains in contact with the parietal pleura and moves with respiration).
