## Conclusion: **BOTH — Lung Point Present**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point**, the pathognomonic sign of **pneumothorax**. There is a visible transition where:
- **Sliding is present** (visceral and parietal pleura apposed) on one side
- **Sliding is absent** (air separating pleural layers) on the other side

This finding is **100% specific** for pneumothorax and marks the boundary of the pneumothorax. Given the clinical indication of "CHEST PAIN," this is consistent with a partial pneumothorax at this intercostal space.
