## Conclusion: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point** — the pathognomonic ultrasound sign of pneumothorax. There is a visible transition where:
- **Sliding is PRESENT** on one side (granular, changing sub-pleural speckle between frames)
- **Sliding is ABSENT** on the other side (static, anechoic pattern with no inter-frame variation)

The alternating appearance of lung tissue intermittently coming into contact with the chest wall creates this characteristic finding, confirming a partial pneumothorax with the lung point marking its boundary.
