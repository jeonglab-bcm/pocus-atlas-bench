# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is clearly visible as a bright hyperechoic line in the upper third of the image. Above it, layered soft tissues (skin, subcutaneous fat, muscle) are well seen. Below the pleural line, the sub-pleural speckle pattern shows subtle granular variations between these frames — the echogenic texture shifts slightly in distribution and intensity.

**Frames 4-6:** The pleural line retains a granular, textured appearance rather than appearing as a smooth, perfectly defined static line. Sub-pleural tissue shows continued variation in speckle pattern compared to earlier frames. Some subtle vertical artifacts (possible comet-tails) appear intermittently.

**Frames 7-8:** Continued frame-to-frame variation in the sub-pleural region is observed. The pleural line maintains its shimmering/granular quality. The tissue below changes in echogenic pattern relative to prior frames.

**Frames 9-10:** The sub-pleural speckle continues to differ from preceding frames. The pleural line remains textured. There is mild increased echogenicity below the pleural line in frame 10, consistent with dynamic interference patterns generated by visceral-parietal pleural movement.

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The evidence supporting this includes:
1. **Shimmering/granular pleural line** rather than a sharp, smooth, fixed line
2. **Frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern**, indicating movement at the visceral-parietal interface
3. **Absence of static A-lines** or barcode-pattern characteristics
4. **No lung point** identified — sliding appears uniform across the visible pleural line

This pattern is consistent with normal visceral pleural movement against the parietal pleura during respiration.
