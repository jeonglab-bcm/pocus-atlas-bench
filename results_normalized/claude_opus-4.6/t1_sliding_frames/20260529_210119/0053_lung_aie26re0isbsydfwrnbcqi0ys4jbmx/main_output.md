## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The primary evidence supporting this conclusion is:
1. **Frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern** — the tissue architecture below the pleural line changes across sequential frames, indicating visceral pleural motion against parietal pleura
2. **Granular/textured pleural line** — rather than a smooth, static, sharply-defined line (which would suggest absent sliding)
3. **No static A-lines or barcode pattern** — which would be expected if sliding were absent (pneumothorax)

The sliding appears present throughout the clip without a clear transition point, making a lung point unlikely. This pattern is consistent with normal lung aeration with pleural apposition.
