# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations:

**Frames 1-3:**
- A well-defined, bright pleural line is visible at the top
- A large **anechoic (black) space** dominates below the pleural line

**Frames 8-10:**
- The echogenic tissue is more prominent in some frames, then recedes
- The transition point between the echogenic (sliding) and anechoic (non-sliding) regions shifts with respiration

| Sign | Present? |

## Conclusion: **BOTH — Lung Point**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point**, the pathognomonic sign of pneumothorax. The partially collapsed lung intermittently contacts the chest wall during respiration, creating a visible transition between:
- **Sliding present** (when lung contacts the pleura)
- **Sliding absent** (when only air is between the visceral and parietal pleura)

This confirms the presence of a **pneumothorax** with high specificity (~100%).
