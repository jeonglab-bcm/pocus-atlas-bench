# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations:

**Frames 1 (3:117) and 8-10 (91:117 to 116:117):**
- The sub-pleural region appears predominantly **dark/anechoic**
- The pleural line appears sharply defined and relatively static
- Minimal speckle variation is seen
- Pattern consistent with **absent pleural sliding** (air artifact/pneumothorax interface)

**Frames 2-7 (16:117 to 78:117):**
- The sub-pleural region fills with a **granular, echogenic pattern**
- Increased textural complexity below the pleural line
- Visible change in speckle pattern between consecutive frames
- Horizontal layering with sandy/shimmering texture
- Pattern consistent with **present pleural sliding** (lung tissue contacting chest wall)

## Key Findings:

| Feature | Observation |
|---------|-------------|
| Pleural line behavior | Alternates between static and shimmering |
| Sub-pleural pattern | Cycles between anechoic and granular |
| Temporal variation | Clear transition between "lung in" and "lung out" |
| A-lines | Static in some frames, disrupted in others |

## Conclusion: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

This sequence demonstrates a **lung point** — the pathognomonic transition where:
- The lung intermittently slides into the field of view (granular/echogenic pattern in frames 2-7)
- Then retracts, leaving a pneumothorax interface (anechoic/static pattern in frames 1, 8-10)

This cyclical, respiratory-phase-dependent alternation is diagnostic of a **partial pneumothorax** at its boundary with normal lung.
