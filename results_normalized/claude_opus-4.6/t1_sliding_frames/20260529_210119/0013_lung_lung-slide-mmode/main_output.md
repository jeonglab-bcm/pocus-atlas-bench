# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### B-mode (lower panels):
- **Pleural line:** Visible as a bright hyperechoic line at approximately 1.5-2 cm depth across all frames
- **Sub-pleural speckle pattern:** Shows **variation between consecutive frames** — the granular texture below the pleural line changes from frame to frame
- **A-lines:** Present but not perfectly static; they show subtle variation across frames
- **Pleural line texture:** Appears shimmering/granular rather than smooth and sharply defined

### M-mode (upper panels):
- **Above the pleural line:** Horizontal, linear stratified lines (representing the non-moving chest wall) — consistent across all frames
- **Below the pleural line:** A **granular, sandy texture** is clearly visible, particularly in frames 6–10 where sufficient temporal data has accumulated
- **Pattern identification:** This represents the **seashore sign** — horizontal "waves" (chest wall) above and sandy "shore" (moving lung) below the pleural line

## Key Findings

| Sign | Present? |
|------|----------|
| Seashore sign on M-mode | ✅ Yes |
| Granular sub-pleural variation between frames | ✅ Yes |
| Stratosphere/barcode sign | ❌ No |
| Static, unchanged A-lines | ❌ No |
| Lung point transition | ❌ No |

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The M-mode consistently demonstrates the **seashore sign** (granular pattern below the pleural line), and the B-mode shows inter-frame sub-pleural speckle variation — both classic indicators of normal visceral-parietal pleural movement. There is no evidence of stratosphere/barcode sign or lung point. This is a **negative FAST lung exam** (no pneumothorax detected).
