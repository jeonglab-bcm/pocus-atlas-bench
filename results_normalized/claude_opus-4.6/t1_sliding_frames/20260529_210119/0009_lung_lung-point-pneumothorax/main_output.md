# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Pleural Line Characteristics
Across all 10 frames, I observe:
- A **bright, echogenic pleural line** located at approximately 3-4 cm depth
- The pleural line appears **shimmering and granular/textured** rather than smooth and sharply static
- Multiple horizontal echogenic lines (A-lines) are visible below the pleural line

### Sub-pleural Pattern Changes Between Frames
- Comparing consecutive frames, there is **clear variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern**
- The granular texture below the pleural line shifts subtly from frame to frame
- The A-line echogenicity and surrounding speckle are **not perfectly identical** across frames — their appearance modulates slightly

### Artifacts
- There are **B-line-like artifacts / comet-tail artifacts** visible in several frames, originating from the pleural line and extending vertically
- The overall sub-pleural region shows a moderately echogenic pattern with some vertical streak artifacts consistent with interstitial involvement

### Signs Favoring Sliding PRESENT:
✅ Shimmering/granular pleural line  
✅ Comet-tail artifacts / B-lines originating from pleural line  
✅ Sub-pleural speckle pattern differs between consecutive frames  

### Signs Favoring Sliding ABSENT:
❌ No perfectly smooth, static pleural line  
❌ No unchanging A-lines across all frames  
❌ No barcode/stratosphere pattern  

---

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The textured, shimmering pleural line with frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern and the presence of B-lines/comet-tail artifacts all confirm active visceral-parietal pleural movement. This excludes pneumothorax at this scanning point.
