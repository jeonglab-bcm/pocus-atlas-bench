## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The evidence supports this conclusion based on:
1. Frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern
2. Granular, shimmering appearance of the pleural line (not a fixed, sharp static line)
3. Absence of stratosphere/barcode sign features
4. Subtle dynamic changes at the lung-pleural interface across sequential frames

The overall appearance also suggests significant interstitial pathology (confluent B-lines/white-out pattern), but lung sliding is maintained, indicating visceral and parietal pleura remain in contact and sliding against each other.
