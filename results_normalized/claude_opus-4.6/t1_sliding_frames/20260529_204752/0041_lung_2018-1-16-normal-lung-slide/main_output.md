# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Pleural Sliding Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1–3:** A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is visible in the upper third of the image. Below the pleural line, there is a granular/textured sub-pleural pattern with subtle variation in speckle between consecutive frames. Soft tissue layers (skin, subcutaneous fat, muscle) are seen above.

**Frames 4–6:** The pleural line maintains its hyperechoic appearance with some shimmering/granularity. Notably, there appear to be comet-tail artifacts or short B-lines extending below the pleural line in frame 5. The sub-pleural speckle pattern continues to change between frames.

**Frames 7–9:** More pronounced variation in the sub-pleural tissue pattern is observed. Frame 8 shows a brighter echogenic area extending vertically below the pleural line, consistent with a B-line artifact. The speckle pattern below the pleural line clearly differs from preceding frames.

**Frame 10:** The pleural line remains bright with a granular quality, and a bright artifact is visible below it. Sub-pleural pattern continues to show dynamic changes.

## Key Findings

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| Sub-pleural speckle variation | ✅ Present — clear differences between consecutive frames |
| Comet-tail / B-line artifacts | ✅ Present — seen in frames 5, 8, 10 |
| Granular/shimmering pleural line | ✅ Present — not smooth/static |
| Static A-lines unchanged across frames | ❌ Not seen |
| Barcode/stratosphere sign | ❌ Not seen |

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The dynamic variation in sub-pleural speckle patterns across consecutive frames, combined with comet-tail artifacts originating from the pleural line and its granular/shimmering appearance, are consistent with normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and sliding motion. There is no evidence of a static, barcode-like pattern or fixed A-lines to suggest absent sliding.
