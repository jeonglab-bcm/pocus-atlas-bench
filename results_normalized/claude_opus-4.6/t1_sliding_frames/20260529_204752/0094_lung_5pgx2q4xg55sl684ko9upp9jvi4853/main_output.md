## Conclusion: **Pleural Sliding is PRESENT**

The ultrasound at the right base demonstrates a **pleural effusion** with underlying **atelectatic lung** that moves freely with respiration (jellyfish/sinusoid sign). The significant frame-to-frame variation in the position and shape of the consolidated lung tissue within the effusion confirms that the visceral pleura is **mobile and not adherent**, indicating that pleural sliding (or its physiologic equivalent in the presence of effusion) is intact.
