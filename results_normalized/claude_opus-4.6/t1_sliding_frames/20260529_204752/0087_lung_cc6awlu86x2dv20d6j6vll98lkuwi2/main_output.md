## Conclusion:

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** 

The pleural line demonstrates a shimmering, granular texture with frame-to-frame variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern, and comet-tail artifacts (B-lines) are visible arising from the pleural interface. These findings are consistent with visceral and parietal pleura sliding against each other during respiration, indicating the lung is aerated and apposed to the chest wall at this location.
