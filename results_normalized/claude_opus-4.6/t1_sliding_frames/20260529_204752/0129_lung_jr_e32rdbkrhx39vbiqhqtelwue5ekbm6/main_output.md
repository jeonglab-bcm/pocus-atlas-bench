## Conclusion: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

There is a visible **transition point** along the pleural line where:
- One segment shows intermittent subtle granular variation (sliding present = lung in contact with chest wall)
- The adjacent segment remains static and smooth (sliding absent = air interposed)

This **lung point sign** is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax**, indicating the exact boundary where the partially collapsed lung intermittently touches the parietal pleura during respiration.
