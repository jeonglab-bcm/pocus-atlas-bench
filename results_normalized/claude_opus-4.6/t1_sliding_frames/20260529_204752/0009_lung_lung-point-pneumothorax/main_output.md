# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Pleural Sliding Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1-3:**
- A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is visible at approximately 3-4 cm depth
- Below the pleural line, I observe multiple closely-spaced horizontal lines (A-line reverberation artifacts)
- The sub-pleural speckle/texture pattern shows subtle differences between these frames
- The pleural line appears granular/textured rather than perfectly smooth

**Frames 4-6:**
- The pleural line maintains its bright, slightly shimmering appearance
- Sub-pleural pattern continues to show variation between consecutive frames
- The horizontal lines below the pleura shift subtly in intensity and pattern

**Frames 7-8:**
- Similar findings persist with ongoing variation in sub-pleural texture
- The pleural line retains its textured, granular quality

**Frames 9-10:**
- Continued frame-to-frame variation in the speckle pattern below the pleural line
- No segment of perfectly static, barcode-like pattern is identified

## Key Findings

| Sign | Observation |
|------|------------|
| Pleural line texture | Granular/shimmering — not sharply smooth or static |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation | **Present** — differs between consecutive frames |
| B-lines/comet-tail artifacts | Present in several frames |
| Static A-lines (unchanged) | **No** — subtle variation noted across frames |
| Lung point | Not identified |

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The evidence supports active visceral-parietal pleural movement: the pleural line has a shimmering, granular quality; the sub-pleural speckle pattern varies between consecutive frames; and B-line artifacts are visible originating from the pleural interface. There is no evidence of a barcode/stratosphere pattern or a lung point.
