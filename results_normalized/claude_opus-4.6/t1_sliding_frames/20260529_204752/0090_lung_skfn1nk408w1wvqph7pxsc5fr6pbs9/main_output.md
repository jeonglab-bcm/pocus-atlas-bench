## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The sub-pleural speckle pattern demonstrates frame-to-frame variation, and the pleural line shows a granular, textured quality rather than a smooth, fixed appearance. Although the sliding appears subtle (possibly due to the cardiac probe and limited resolution for lung imaging), the inter-frame variability in the sub-pleural region is inconsistent with absent sliding. No lung point or stratosphere/barcode pattern features are seen.
