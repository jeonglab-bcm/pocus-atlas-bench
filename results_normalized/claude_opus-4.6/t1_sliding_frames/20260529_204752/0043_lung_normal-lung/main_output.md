# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations:

**Frames 1-2:** A bright pleural line is visible near the top of the sector image. Multiple horizontal A-lines are seen below. The sub-pleural speckle pattern shows some texture.

**Frames 3-5:** The pleural line appears brighter/more granular in these frames. There is noticeable variation in the sub-pleural brightness and texture compared to earlier frames. Possible comet-tail artifacts originate near the pleural line.

**Frames 6-8:** Continued subtle variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern. The A-lines are present but their intensity/clarity varies slightly between frames.

**Frames 9-10:** The sub-pleural pattern continues to show frame-to-frame variation, though perhaps less pronounced than in mid-sequence frames.

## Key Findings:

| Feature | Observation |
|---------|-------------|
| Pleural line | Granular/shimmering appearance |
| A-lines | Present but show subtle inter-frame variation |
| Sub-pleural speckle | **Changes between consecutive frames** |
| B-lines/comet-tails | Possible short comet-tail artifacts in some frames |

## Conclusion: **Pleural Sliding is PRESENT**

The primary evidence is the **variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames**, combined with a shimmering/granular pleural line appearance. While A-lines are visible (an A-line pattern), they are not perfectly static across frames, and the tissue texture below the pleural line shifts — consistent with visceral pleura moving against parietal pleura during respiration.
