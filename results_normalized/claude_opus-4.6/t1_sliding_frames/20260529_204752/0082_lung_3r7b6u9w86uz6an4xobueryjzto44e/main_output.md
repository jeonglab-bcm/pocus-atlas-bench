## Conclusion: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point** — a visible transition where pleural sliding is **present** on one side and **absent** on the other, with the boundary shifting with respiratory movement. This is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax**, marking the exact border where the visceral pleura intermittently contacts the parietal pleura during breathing.
