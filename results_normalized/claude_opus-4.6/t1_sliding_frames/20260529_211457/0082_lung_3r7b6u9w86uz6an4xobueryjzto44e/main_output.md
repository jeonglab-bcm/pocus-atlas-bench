**Frames 1-3:** A bright pleural line is visible. Below the pleural line, there are hyperechoic clustered structures in the lower-central portion that show clear positional changes between frames. ## Conclusion: **BOTH — Lung Point Present**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point**, the pathognomonic sign of pneumothorax. There is a visible transition zone where:
- **Sliding is present** (left portion): granular sub-pleural changes, speckle variation between frames
- **Sliding is absent** (right portion): static, anechoic sub-pleural space

The lung edge intermittently enters the field of view during respiration, creating the characteristic alternating pattern at the boundary of the pneumothorax.
