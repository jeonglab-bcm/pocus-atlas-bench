# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations:

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is identified as a bright hyperechoic interface in the upper third of the image. Superficial tissue layers (subcutaneous tissue, muscle) are seen above. The sub-pleural speckle pattern shows subtle variation between these frames, with some textural changes in the region immediately below the pleural line.

**Frames 4-6:** The pleural line continues to appear with a somewhat shimmering/granular quality. Notably, there are changes in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames. The pleural line itself shows slight positional and textural variation. Some short comet-tail-like artifacts are intermittently visible.

**Frames 7-8:** The pleural line maintains its bright echogenic appearance. A-lines (horizontal reverberation artifacts) are visible below the pleural line, but they are not perfectly identical across frames—there is subtle variation in their intensity/clarity.

**Frames 9-10:** Similar findings with continued subtle variation in the sub-pleural region. The pleural line appears somewhat textured/granular rather than perfectly smooth and static.

## Key Findings:

| Feature | Observation |
|---------|------------|
| Pleural line texture | Granular/shimmering across frames |
| Sub-pleural speckle | Varies between consecutive frames |
| A-lines | Present but not perfectly static |
| Comet-tail artifacts | Intermittently seen |
| Static barcode pattern | Not identified |

## Conclusion:

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The variation in sub-pleural speckle between consecutive frames, the granular/shimmering quality of the pleural line, and the intermittent comet-tail artifacts all support active visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement, consistent with normal lung sliding.
