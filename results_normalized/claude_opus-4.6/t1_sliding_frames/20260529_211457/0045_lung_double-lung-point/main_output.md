# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Left Lung

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is identified as a bright hyperechoic interface. Below it, I note a granular/speckled pattern with subtle vertical artifacts (short comet-tail artifacts). The sub-pleural texture shows slight variation between these frames.

**Frames 4-6:** The pleural line maintains a shimmering, textured quality rather than appearing as a smooth, sharply defined static line. The speckle pattern below the pleural line continues to shift subtly between consecutive frames.

**Frames 7-8:** Similar findings—the pleural line has a granular appearance. A-lines (horizontal reverberation artifacts) are visible but are not perfectly static; there is slight variation in their intensity and the interposed speckle.

**Frames 9-10:** The sub-pleural pattern continues to demonstrate frame-to-frame variation. Short vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line change position slightly between frames.

## Key Findings

| Feature | Observation |
|---------|-------------|
| Pleural line texture | Granular/shimmering, not smooth or static |
| Sub-pleural speckle | Varies between consecutive frames |

| Barcode/stratosphere sign | Absent |

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The granular/shimmering pleural line, frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern, and subtle movement of short comet-tail artifacts all indicate normal visceral-parietal pleural interface motion. There is no evidence of a static pleural line or barcode sign to suggest absent sliding, and no lung point is identified.
