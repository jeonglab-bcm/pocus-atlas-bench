## Conclusion: **Pleural Sliding is PRESENT**

The evidence supports active pleural sliding:
1. **Variation in sub-pleural speckle** across consecutive frames confirms movement at the pleural interface
2. **Dynamic air bronchograms** (bright structures moving within consolidated tissue between frames) indicate the lung is ventilated and sliding against the chest wall
3. The consolidated (hepatized) lung at the right base with dynamic air bronchograms is most consistent with **pneumonic consolidation** rather than obstructive atelectasis or pneumothorax
