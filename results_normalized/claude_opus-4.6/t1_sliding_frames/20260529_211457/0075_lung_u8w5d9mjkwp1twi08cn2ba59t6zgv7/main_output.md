## Conclusion: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point** – a visible transition where pleural sliding is **present** on one side and **absent** on the other. The arrows annotate this transition zone. This is a pathognomonic sign of **pneumothorax**, marking the boundary between the pneumothorax (no sliding) and the normally apposed visceral-parietal pleura (sliding present). This is a highly specific finding (≈100%) for pneumothorax.
