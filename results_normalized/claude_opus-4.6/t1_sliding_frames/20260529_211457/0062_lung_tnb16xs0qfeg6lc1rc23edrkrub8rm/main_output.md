# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

**Frames 1-2 (3:117 - 16:117):**
- The sub-pleural region appears relatively dark/anechoic
- Minimal echogenic texture below the pleural line
- Pattern is consistent with air interface (no sliding) — smooth, static appearance

## Conclusion: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point** — a pathognomonic sign of **pneumothorax**. There is a clear cyclical alternation between:
- **Absent sliding** (dark, static, A-line pattern) when only air is present
- **Present sliding** (granular, textured, changing speckle) when the lung edge intermittently slides into the field of view with respiration

This transition corresponds to the boundary between pneumothorax and normally aerated lung, confirming the presence and approximate location of a pneumothorax border.
