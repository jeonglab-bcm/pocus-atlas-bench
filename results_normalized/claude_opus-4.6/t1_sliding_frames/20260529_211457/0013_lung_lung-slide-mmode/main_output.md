# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### B-mode (Lower Panels):
- A hyperechoic **pleural line** is consistently visible at approximately **2 cm depth**
- Horizontal **A-lines** are visible below the pleural line in most frames
- The **sub-pleural speckle pattern varies** between consecutive frames — the texture beneath the pleural line shifts and changes, indicating movement at the lung-pleura interface
- The pleural line itself appears to have a **shimmering, granular quality** rather than being smooth and static

### M-mode (Upper Panels):
- **Above the pleural line**: Smooth, horizontal, parallel lines representing the static chest wall — consistent across all frames
- **Below the pleural line**: A distinctly **granular/sandy texture** is visible, particularly in frames 3–10 as the M-mode tracing develops
- This creates the classic **"seashore sign"** — linear/wave-like pattern superficially with a sandy pattern deep to the pleural line
- No barcode/stratosphere sign (continuous horizontal lines through the pleural line) is identified
- No clear transition point between seashore and barcode patterns is seen

---

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

**Supporting evidence:**
1. ✅ **Seashore sign** on M-mode (granular pattern below the pleural line)
2. ✅ **Sub-pleural speckle variation** between consecutive frames
3. ✅ **Textured/shimmering pleural line** on B-mode
4. ❌ No stratosphere/barcode sign
5. ❌ No lung point identified

This is consistent with normal lung sliding, ruling out pneumothorax at this scanning location.
