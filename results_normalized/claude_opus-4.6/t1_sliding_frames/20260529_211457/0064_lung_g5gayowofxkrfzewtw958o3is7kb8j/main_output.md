# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations:

**Frames 1-3:** I observe a clearly defined pleural line with a large **anechoic (black) region** extending below it. The dark area occupies a significant portion of the image. The pleural line above the dark zone appears relatively smooth and static.

**Frames 4-6:** The **border of the anechoic region shifts** — the dark area changes in size/configuration. In some portions, echogenic tissue (granular speckle pattern) appears to move into the field, replacing the dark zone.

**Frames 7-10:** The pattern continues to **alternate** — the dark anechoic region expands and contracts. In frames where the echogenic tissue is visible, there appears to be subtle shimmering/textural change at the pleural line, while in frames dominated by the dark area, the pleural line appears more static.

## Conclusion: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point** — the pathognomonic transition between:
- **Absent sliding:** The anechoic region representing pneumothorax (no lung contact with parietal pleura)
- **Present sliding:** Intermittent appearance of lung tissue with granular speckle pattern

The rhythmic alternation of these two patterns with respiration confirms a **partial pneumothorax** with a visible lung point, indicating where the edge of the collapsed lung intermittently contacts the chest wall.
