## Conclusion: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

A lung point is identified, with **pleural sliding present** in one segment (left/central) and **absent** in the adjacent segment (right). The visible transition point where the sliding lung intermittently contacts the chest wall is characteristic of a **partial pneumothorax**. This finding is highly specific (~100%) for pneumothorax and indicates the boundary between normally aerated, sliding lung and the pneumothorax space.
