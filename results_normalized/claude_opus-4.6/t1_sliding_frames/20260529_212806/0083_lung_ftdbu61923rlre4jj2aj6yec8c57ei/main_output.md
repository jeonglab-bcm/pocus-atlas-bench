# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1-4:**
- A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is clearly visible
- The sub-pleural speckle pattern shows **notable variation** between consecutive frames
- There appears to be shimmering/granular texture at the pleural interface
- Some comet-tail artifacts appear to originate from the pleural line

**Frames 5-7:**
- A transition is observed: part of the pleural line appears to become more **static and sharply defined**
- The sub-pleural pattern shows reduced variation in one segment while continuing to change in another
- The position of this transition zone appears to shift across frames

**Frames 8-10:**
- The pleural line demonstrates a **clear demarcation point**
- On one side, the sub-pleural tissue continues to show speckle variation (sliding present)
- On the other side, the pattern appears fixed and unchanging (sliding absent)
- The transition point intermittently shifts position

## Key Findings

| Feature | Observation |
|---------|-------------|
| Pleural line texture | Shimmering in one segment, smooth/static in another |
| Sub-pleural speckle | Changes on one side, fixed on the other |
| A-lines | Static in one region, variable in the other |
| Transition point | Visible and shifting across frames |

## Conclusion: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point** — a visible transition zone where pleural sliding is **present** on one side and **absent** on the other. This alternating pattern is pathognomonic for **pneumothorax**, marking the exact boundary where the collapsed lung intermittently contacts the chest wall.
