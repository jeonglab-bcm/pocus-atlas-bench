# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations:

**Frames 1-3:** A bright pleural line is visible at the top. Below it, there is a large anechoic (black) area dominating the image. The left border of this dark zone appears relatively fixed, while the right side shows subtle tissue with speckle.

## Conclusion:

**BOTH — Lung Point Present**

This clip demonstrates a classic **lung point**, where partially collapsed lung intermittently contacts the chest wall. The shifting boundary between the pneumothorax (dark, static zone) and the aerated lung (echogenic, sliding zone) is pathognomonic for pneumothorax and marks its lateral extent.
