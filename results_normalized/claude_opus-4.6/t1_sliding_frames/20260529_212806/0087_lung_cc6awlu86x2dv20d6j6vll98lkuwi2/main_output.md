# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Pleural Line Characteristics
- Across all frames, the **pleural line appears bright, irregular, and textured** (shimmering/granular quality) rather than smooth and sharply defined
- The pleural line is located at the top of the sector, beneath a thin chest wall layer

### Sub-Pleural Pattern
- Below the pleural line, there is a **tissue-like echotexture** consistent with **lung consolidation (hepatization)**
- The consolidated lung appears as heterogeneous, liver-like parenchyma
- **Bright punctate foci** within the consolidation likely represent **air bronchograms**

### Frame-to-Frame Variation
- Comparing consecutive frames (particularly frames 1→2→3 and 4→5→6→7), there are **clear variations in the sub-pleural speckle pattern**
- The air bronchograms appear to shift slightly between frames, suggesting **dynamic air bronchograms** (air movement during respiration)
- The texture of the consolidated tissue changes subtly across frames, indicating motion at the pleural interface

### Artifacts
- **B-lines/comet-tail artifacts** are visible originating from the pleural line in several frames
- No static, perfectly unchanged A-lines are seen (which would suggest absent sliding)

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

Evidence supporting this:
1. ✅ Shimmering/granular pleural line
2. ✅ Sub-pleural speckle variation between frames
3. ✅ Dynamic air bronchograms within consolidated lung
4. ✅ B-line artifacts present
5. ❌ No barcode/stratosphere sign features
6. ❌ No static, unchanged A-lines

**Diagnosis:** This represents **consolidated lung with preserved pleural sliding**, likely indicating pneumonia (with hepatization and dynamic air bronchograms) rather than complete atelectasis or pneumothorax.
