# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Pleural Sliding Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1-3:** A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is identified at approximately 5-6 cm depth. Below this line, multiple confluent B-lines are visible extending to the far field, creating a "white lung" or dense interstitial pattern. The sub-pleural speckle pattern shows subtle but definite variation between these consecutive frames.

**Frames 4-6:** The pleural line remains bright and appears textured/granular rather than smooth and sharply static. The B-line pattern below the pleural line continues, with slight shifts in their distribution and intensity between frames.

**Frames 7-8:** The sub-pleural texture continues to change between frames. The horizontal A-lines that are faintly visible are not perfectly static — their appearance varies slightly.

**Frames 9-10:** The pattern is consistent with previous frames — a shimmering pleural line with ongoing variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern.

## Key Findings

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| Pleural line texture | **Granular/shimmering** — not smooth or static |
| B-lines | **Present** — multiple, originating from pleural line |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation | **Present** — changes between consecutive frames |
| Static A-lines / Barcode sign | **Absent** |

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The evidence includes: a shimmering/granular pleural line, abundant B-lines (suggesting interstitial syndrome — possibly pulmonary edema or pneumonitis), and clear variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern across frames. There is no lung point or area of absent sliding identified.
