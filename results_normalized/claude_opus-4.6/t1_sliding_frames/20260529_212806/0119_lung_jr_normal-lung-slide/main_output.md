# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Frame-by-Frame

**Frames 1-3:** A bright pleural line is visible with A-lines below. There is a noticeable difference between the **left portion** (appears darker, more static with well-defined horizontal lines) and the **right portion** (shows more granular texture and sub-pleural speckle variation).

## Conclusion: **LUNG POINT (Both Present and Absent)**

This clip demonstrates a **lung point** — the pathognomonic sign of **pneumothorax**. There is a visible transition zone where:
- **Left portion**: Absent sliding (static pleural line, unchanged A-lines, stratosphere-like pattern)
- **Right portion**: Present sliding (granular speckle variation, shimmering pleural interface)

This represents the boundary where the partially collapsed lung intermittently contacts the chest wall, confirming a non-tension pneumothorax.
