## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

This clip demonstrates **lung consolidation (hepatization) at the right base** with **dynamic air bronchograms** — bright echoes that move with respiration, confirming airway patency. This pattern is most consistent with **pneumonia** (rather than obstructive atelectasis, where air bronchograms would be static). The preserved lung sliding and dynamic bronchograms confirm ongoing ventilation of the consolidated segment.
