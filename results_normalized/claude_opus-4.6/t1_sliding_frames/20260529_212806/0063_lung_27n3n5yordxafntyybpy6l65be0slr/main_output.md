## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The ultrasound demonstrates a "white lung" pattern (confluent B-lines) with preserved lung sliding. The frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle and the granular appearance of the pleural line confirm visceral-parietal pleural apposition with movement. This pattern is consistent with significant **interstitial syndrome** (e.g., pulmonary edema, ARDS, or diffuse interstitial disease) but with no pneumothorax at this scanning location.
