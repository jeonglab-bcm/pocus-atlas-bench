# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Pleural Sliding Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frames 1-3:**
- A hyperechoic pleural line is visible at approximately 1-1.5 cm depth
- A rib shadow is present on the left, creating an acoustic shadow
- The sub-pleural speckle pattern shows subtle but visible variation between these frames
- The pleural line appears somewhat granular/textured rather than perfectly smooth

**Frames 4-6:**
- The pleural line continues to display a shimmering, granular quality
- Bright echogenic foci appear at the pleural line interface that shift slightly between frames
- Below the pleural line, the pattern texture changes compared to earlier frames
- Emerging comet-tail artifacts begin to appear

**Frames 7-10:**
- A prominent vertical hyperechoic artifact (comet-tail/B-line) is visible extending from the pleural line downward
- This artifact changes in appearance/intensity between frames
- The sub-pleural speckle pattern continues to vary from frame to frame
- The pleural line retains its granular, textured quality

## Key Findings

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| Pleural line appearance | Granular, shimmering — not smooth/static |
| Comet-tail artifacts | Present, originating from pleural line (frames 7-10) |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation | Present between consecutive frames |
| Static A-lines | Not observed |
| Barcode/stratosphere sign | Not observed |

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

Evidence supporting this includes: variation in sub-pleural speckle patterns across consecutive frames, a granular/shimmering pleural line rather than a sharply defined static one, and comet-tail artifacts originating from the pleural line that change between frames. These findings are consistent with normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement during respiration.
