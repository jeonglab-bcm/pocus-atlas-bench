## Conclusion:

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The evidence supporting this includes: (1) a textured, granular pleural line rather than a smooth, sharply defined static line; (2) clear frame-to-frame variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern indicating lung movement against the parietal pleura; and (3) absence of static A-lines or barcode/stratosphere sign features. This is consistent with normal lung aeration and visceral-parietal pleural apposition with respiratory movement.
