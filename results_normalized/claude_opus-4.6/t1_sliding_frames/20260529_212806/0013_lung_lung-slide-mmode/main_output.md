# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### B-mode (lower panels):
- The **pleural line** is visible at approximately 1.5–2 cm depth across all frames
- The pleural line appears **bright and textured/shimmering** rather than smooth and sharply static
- Sub-pleural speckle patterns show **subtle variation between frames**, with changes in the granular texture beneath the pleural line
- A hyperechoic structure (likely an A-line or posterior structure) is visible at ~3.5–4 cm depth

### M-mode (upper panels):
- **Above the pleural line**: Consistent horizontal, parallel, stratified lines representing the motionless chest wall
- **At the pleural line**: A bright, well-defined hyperechoic line at ~2 cm depth
- **Below the pleural line**: A distinctly **granular/sandy texture** that differs clearly from the stratified lines above — this is the **seashore sign**

As the M-mode tracing develops across frames (especially frames 3–10), the pattern becomes increasingly clear: waves (stratified chest wall) above and sand (granular moving lung) below.

## Key Findings

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| Seashore sign (M-mode) | ✅ Present — granular pattern below pleural line |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation | ✅ Changes between frames |
| Shimmering pleural line | ✅ Textured, not perfectly static |
| Barcode/stratosphere sign | ❌ Not seen |
| Static A-lines | ❌ Not seen |

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The M-mode demonstrates a classic **seashore sign** with clear horizontal lines (waves) above the pleural line and a granular sandy pattern below it. The B-mode frames confirm subtle frame-to-frame variation in the sub-pleural tissue, consistent with normal visceral-parietal pleural movement.
