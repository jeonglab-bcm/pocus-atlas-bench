# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Anatomical Orientation
- **Probe**: Linear high-frequency (L14-6Ns)
- **Preset**: EM FAST
- **Depth**: ~3-4 cm
- A **rib shadow** (dark anechoic structure) is visible on the left side of each frame
- The **pleural line** is identified as the bright hyperechoic horizontal line at approximately 1 cm depth
- Multiple **A-lines** (horizontal reverberation artifacts) are visible below the pleural line

### Dynamic Assessment (Frame-to-Frame Comparison)

**Superficial tissues (above pleural line):**
- Remain relatively stable across all 10 frames, indicating minimal probe motion

**Pleural line characteristics:**
- Appears with a slightly **granular/textured quality** rather than a perfectly smooth, sharply static line
- Shows subtle variation in brightness and texture between consecutive frames

**Sub-pleural region:**
- Frames 1→2→3: Subtle but visible changes in the speckle pattern below the pleural line
- Frames 4→5→6: Continued variation in sub-pleural echogenicity
- Frames 7→8→9→10: Ongoing textural changes in the sub-pleural area

**A-lines:**
- Present and prominent, but show **slight frame-to-frame variation** in intensity/sharpness — they are NOT perfectly static and identical across all frames

## Signs Identified

| Sign | Present? |
|------|----------|
| Granular/shimmering pleural line | ✅ Yes |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation between frames | ✅ Yes |
| Perfectly static, sharp pleural line | ❌ No |
| Static unchanging A-lines | ❌ No |
| Barcode/stratosphere sign | ❌ No |
| Lung point (transition zone) | ❌ No |

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The subtle but consistent frame-to-frame variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern, combined with the slightly textured (rather than perfectly smooth/static) pleural line, indicates normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement. The stability of superficial structures confirms these changes are not from probe motion but from actual lung sliding. This is a **normal lung ultrasound** appearance with A-lines and preserved sliding, effectively ruling out pneumothorax at this scanning location.
