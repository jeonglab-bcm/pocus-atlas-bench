## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Pleural Line Characteristics

**Frames 1-3:** The pleural line is visible as a bright hyperechoic line approximately 1-1.5 cm deep. It appears **textured and granular** rather than perfectly smooth. There is subtle variation in the brightness and micro-texture of the pleural line between these frames.

**Frames 4-6:** The pleural line maintains its bright, slightly irregular/shimmering quality. Sub-pleural speckle pattern shows subtle but detectable differences between consecutive frames.

**Frames 7-8:** Continued variation in the sub-pleural granular pattern. The pleural line continues to show a textured appearance.

**Frames 9-10:** Similar findings - the pleural line retains its granular quality, and the speckle pattern below the pleura continues to show frame-to-frame variation.

### Key Findings

| Feature | Observation |
|---------|-------------|
| Pleural line texture | Granular/shimmering, not perfectly smooth or static |
| Sub-pleural speckle | **Varies between consecutive frames** |
| A-lines | Present but show subtle variation across frames |

| Lung point | Not identified |

## Conclusion: **Pleural Sliding is PRESENT**

**Evidence supporting this conclusion:**
1. **Frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle** – the granular pattern below the pleural line changes between sequential frames, indicating visceral-parietal pleural movement
2. **Granular/textured pleural line** – consistent with dynamic sliding rather than a static, sharply defined line
3. **No barcode/stratosphere pattern** – no static horizontal lines dominating below the pleura
4. **Absence of a lung point** – no transition zone identified

This is consistent with a **normal left lung** with preserved lung sliding.
