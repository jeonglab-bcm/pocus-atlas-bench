## Conclusion: **BOTH (Lung Point)**

The key findings supporting this diagnosis:
1. **Visible transition point** where sliding is present on one side and absent on the other (highlighted by arrows)
2. **Alternating dynamic and static sub-pleural patterns** across the field of view
3. Part of the pleural line appears **granular/shimmering** while another segment remains **fixed and static**

This lung point is pathognomonic for **pneumothorax**, marking the boundary between visceral-parietal pleural apposition (sliding present) and air separation (sliding absent).
