# Lung Ultrasound Analysis: Sequential Frame Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1-3:** A bright pleural line is visible near the top of the field. Multiple horizontal A-lines (reverberation artifacts) are seen at regular intervals below the pleural line. The pleural line appears **smooth and sharply defined**. The sub-pleural speckle pattern shows minimal variation.

**Frames 4-6:** The same pattern persists. The A-lines maintain their position, spacing, and intensity with virtually no change. The pleural line remains static and well-delineated. No comet-tail artifacts or B-lines are identified.

**Frames 7-10:** The appearance remains remarkably consistent. A-lines are unchanged in position and echogenicity. The sub-pleural speckle pattern is essentially identical across these frames with no appreciable granular movement or textural change.

## Key Findings

| Feature | Observation |
|---------|-------------|
| Pleural line | Smooth, sharply defined, static |
| A-lines | Prominent, perfectly static, unchanged across all frames |
| Sub-pleural speckle | No meaningful variation between frames |
| B-lines/comet tails | Absent |
| Transition point | Not identified |

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.**

The evidence supporting this includes:
- **Static A-lines** that remain perfectly unchanged across all 10 frames
- A **smooth, sharply defined pleural line** without shimmering or granularity
- **No variation in sub-pleural speckle** between consecutive frames

This pattern (absent sliding + A-lines) is concerning for **pneumothorax** and warrants M-mode confirmation (expected stratosphere/barcode sign) and clinical correlation.
