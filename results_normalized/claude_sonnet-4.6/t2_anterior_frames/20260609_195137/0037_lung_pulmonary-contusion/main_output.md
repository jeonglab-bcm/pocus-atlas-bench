## Detailed Findings

### Pleural Line
- Well-defined, **smooth, continuous hyperechoic line** across all frames
- No irregularities or thickening

### Deep Field / Artifact Pattern
- The lung field below the pleural line is **predominantly hypoechoic/dark**
- **Horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines)** are the dominant pattern — appearing as equidistant parallel echogenic lines repeating at regular intervals below the pleural line
- No hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen are observed
- No "white lung" appearance or coalescing bright vertical streaks

### Consolidation Signs
- **No hepatization**: No liver-like echogenicity of lung parenchyma
- **No shred sign**: Deep border is not visible as irregular/shredded
- **No air bronchograms**: No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within lung tissue
