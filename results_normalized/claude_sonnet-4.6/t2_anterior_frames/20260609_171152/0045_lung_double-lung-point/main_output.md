## B-Lines Assessment

### What I Observe
- In **all 10 frames**, discrete **hyperechoic vertical artifacts** arise from the pleural line
- These streaks are **laser-like**, well-defined, and extend toward — and in several frames reach — the **bottom of the screen without fading**
- They **move synchronously** with the pleural line (consistent with lung sliding)
- Between each artifact, **dark lung parenchyma remains visible** — the artifacts are clearly **separated**, not merging or coalescing
- Count per intercostal space: typically **≤ 3 discrete B-lines**, never forming a continuous white sheet
- No obliteration of A-lines across the full intercostal space

### Conclusion
> **lung_rockets = `true`**
> **Subtype = `septal`**
> *(Discrete, well-spaced B-lines with preserved dark intervening parenchyma, consistent with thickened interlobular septa — no coalescence into a diffuse white "ground-glass" sheet)*

## Consolidation Assessment

### What I Observe
- The **pleural line is intact and smooth** across all frames — no subpleural disruption
- The deep field echogenicity represents **B-line artifact propagation**, not true parenchymal hepatization
- **No liver-like (hepatized) texture** is present in the near field or mid field
- **No irregular shredded deep border** (shred sign) is identifiable
- **No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci** within a hepatized zone (no air bronchograms)
- The deeper echogenic areas visible in frames 4–6 are continuous with B-line tails rather than forming independent solid-appearing tissue zones

### Conclusion
> **consolidation = `false`**
> **consolidation_type = `null`**
