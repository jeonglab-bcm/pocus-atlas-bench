## B-Lines Assessment

### Observations:
- The **pleural line is clearly visible**, hyperechoic, and smooth throughout all frames
- Below the pleural line, the dominant pattern consists of **horizontal, equally-spaced reverberation artifacts (A-lines)**, parallel to the pleural surface
- The central lung field is **predominantly anechoic/dark**
- In later frames (7–10), there is mild lateral echogenicity, but these do **not** originate sharply from the pleural line nor extend continuously to the bottom of the screen
- **No discrete, laser-like vertical hyperechoic artifacts** arising from the pleural line and reaching the screen bottom without fading are identified
- **No confluent/coalescing white "waterfall" pattern** is seen

### Conclusion:
> **lung_rockets = false**
> **subtype = null**
> A-line dominant pattern consistent with **normal anterior lung aeration**
