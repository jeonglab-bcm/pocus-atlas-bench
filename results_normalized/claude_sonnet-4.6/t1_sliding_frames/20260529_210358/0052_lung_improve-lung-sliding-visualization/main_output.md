## Key Diagnostic Finding

> The clip captures a **clear transition point** — the pleural line shimmers and shows speckle variation in early frames (sliding ✅), then becomes sharply defined and static in later frames (sliding ❌).

---

## ✅ Conclusion: **LUNG POINT IDENTIFIED**

This is the **pathognomonic sign of pneumothorax**:
- One segment of pleural line demonstrates **active sliding** (seashore pattern equivalent)
- An adjacent/later segment shows **absent sliding** (barcode/stratosphere pattern equivalent)
- The transition between these two zones = **lung point** — the precise boundary where the visceral and parietal pleura separate

> 🔺 **Diagnosis: Pneumothorax** — the lung point has ~100% specificity for pneumothorax and also allows estimation of its size.
