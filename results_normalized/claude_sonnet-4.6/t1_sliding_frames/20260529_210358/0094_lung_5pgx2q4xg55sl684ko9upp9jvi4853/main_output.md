## Conclusion

### ✅ **BOTH — Lung Point Identified**

This sequence demonstrates a **lung point** at the right base:

- 🔴 **Absent sliding** → smooth static pleural line + static A-lines (frames 2–4) = **pneumothorax zone**
- 🟢 **Present sliding** → shimmering irregular pleural line with heterogeneous sub-pleural speckle (frames 1, 5–10) = **aerated/consolidated lung re-entering the pleural space**
- 🔁 The **rhythmic alternation** between these two patterns across frames is the hallmark of a **lung point**

---

## ⚠️ Clinical Significance

> A **lung point is 100% specific for pneumothorax** (Lichtenstein, 2000). Its location at the **right base** suggests a **right-sided pneumothorax**, likely of moderate size (lung point at the base = larger pneumothorax). **Immediate clinical correlation and management are warranted.**
