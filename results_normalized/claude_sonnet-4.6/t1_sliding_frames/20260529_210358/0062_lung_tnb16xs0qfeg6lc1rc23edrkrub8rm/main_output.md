## Critical Diagnostic Signs Identified

### ✅ Signs Supporting Sliding PRESENT

1. **Respiratory excursion cycle**: The dramatic, rhythmic widening → narrowing of the ultrasound cone corresponds to lung expansion and retraction beneath the probe — this *is* the sliding phenomenon
2. **Changing sub-pleural speckle**: The granular texture below the pleural line varies significantly between frames (compare frame 1 vs. frames 5–6)
3. **Textured/shimmering pleural line**: In frames 4–7, the pleural interface is granular and bright rather than smooth and static
4. **No barcode/stratosphere sign**: There are no purely static horizontal lines throughout the sequence
5. **No static A-lines**: The reverberation artifacts below the pleural line change position and intensity with respiration

### ❌ No Signs of Absent Sliding
- No perfectly smooth, unchanging pleural line
- No static sub-pleural pattern across frames

### 🔵 No Lung Point
- No visible transition within a single frame between sliding and non-sliding zones

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding: PRESENT** ✅

The sequential frames demonstrate a complete respiratory cycle. The lung moves freely beneath the probe — expanding in frames 3–7 and retracting in frames 8–10 — producing the characteristic dynamic granularity of normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition. This pattern is **incompatible with pneumothorax** at this location.
