## Key Observations

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT
- **Pleural line**: Consistently **smooth and sharply defined** across all frames — no shimmering or granularity
- **A-lines**: Perfectly **parallel, equidistant, and static** — unchanged frame-to-frame
- **Sub-pleural speckle**: **No variation** between consecutive frames
- **No B-lines** or comet-tail artifacts originating from pleural interface

### ❌ Signs of Sliding PRESENT — Not Observed
- No granular/textured pleural line
- No dynamic speckle movement below pleura

### ❌ Lung Point — Not Identified
- No transitional zone detected within the visible field

---

## Conclusion

> ## 🔴 Pleural Sliding: **ABSENT**
>
> The ultrasound pattern across all 10 frames is consistent with the **"stratosphere/barcode sign"** equivalent in 2D:
> - Static, smooth pleural line
> - Fixed, non-dynamic A-lines
> - Zero sub-pleural speckle variation
>
> ### ⚠️ Clinical Correlation:
> **Absent lung sliding** raises concern for:
> 1. **Pneumothorax** *(most critical — must rule out)*
> 2. Mainstem intubation (contralateral side)
> 3. Severe hyperinflation/bullae
> 4. Pleurodesis/pleural adhesions
> 5. Apnea at time of scanning
>
> **Recommend M-mode confirmation** (barcode sign) and clinical correlation; if suspected pneumothorax, search for a **lung point** to confirm and estimate size.
