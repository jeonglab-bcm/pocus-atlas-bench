# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Structural Landmarks (All Frames)
- **Linear probe (L14-6Ns)** at ~2–3 cm depth
- **Rib acoustic shadow**: consistent dark rectangle on the **left** side of every frame
- **Pleural line**: bright hyperechoic horizontal interface visible throughout

---

### Sequential Frame Observations

| Frames | Left of center (near rib) | Right of center |
|--------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| 1–2 | Smooth, static, sharply defined pleural line; uniform A-lines below | Relatively uniform sub-pleural region, minimal change |
| 3–4 | Continues static/smooth | Faint increased sub-pleural echogenicity beginning to appear |
| 5–6 | Remains static, no sub-pleural speckle variation | **Distinct bright echogenic region appears** below the pleural line — suggestive of lung tissue making contact |
| 7–8 | Still static/barcode-like | Bright sub-pleural texture **persists and intensifies** — shimmering, granular pleural line |
| 9–10 | Unchanged static line | Sub-pleural echogenicity **peaks**, then partially recedes — oscillating pattern consistent with respiratory cycling |

---

## Key Signs Identified

### ❌ Sliding ABSENT (left/medial zone):
- Smooth, sharply demarcated, **non-moving** pleural line
- Horizontal A-lines with **no sub-pleural speckle variation** between frames
- Consistent across all 10 frames on this side

### ✅ Sliding PRESENT (right/lateral zone):
- **Shimmering, granular** pleural line texture
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation** between frames (especially frames 5–10)
- Echogenic lung parenchyma periodically reaches the pleural interface

### ⚡ Transition Point:
- Clear **left-to-right transition** visible mid-image where the static segment ends and the dynamic (sliding) segment begins

---

## Conclusion

> ### 🔴 LUNG POINT — Both present and absent sliding

This is the **pathognomonic sign of pneumothorax**. The left/medial portion shows absent sliding (pneumothorax side — collapsed lung away from chest wall), while the right/lateral portion shows present sliding (aerated lung still in contact). The **lung point** — the exact transition between these two zones — is visible within the field of view, indicating the **boundary of the pneumothorax**.

**Sensitivity for pneumothorax: ~79% | Specificity: ~100%**
