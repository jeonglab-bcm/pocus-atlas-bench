## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **shimmering, textured pleural line** with **inter-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle**, both hallmarks of normal visceral-parietal pleural sliding. The transient smoothing in frame 7 likely represents a brief phase of minimal respiratory movement rather than true absence of sliding. There is no evidence of a stratosphere/barcode pattern or a static, fixed pleural line.

In the context of **chest pain**, this finding effectively **argues against pneumothorax** at this location.
