## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is ABSENT**

The combination of a **smooth, static pleural line** with **unchanging A-lines** and **no sub-pleural speckle variation** across all 10 frames is characteristic of the **stratosphere/barcode sign** on M-mode equivalent. In the appropriate clinical context (left-sided, labeled view), this raises significant concern for **left-sided pneumothorax**, though absent sliding can also occur with:
- Pleurodesis
- Mainstem intubation (right-sided)
- Severe hyperinflation/apnea
- Complete pleural adhesion

⚠️ *Clinical correlation and additional views (lung point search) are recommended.*
