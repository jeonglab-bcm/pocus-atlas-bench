# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Frames 1–2
- Pleural line visible at ~2.5–3 cm depth
- Texture at the pleural interface appears **granular/shimmering**, not sharply smooth
- Sub-pleural speckle shows mild heterogeneity

### Frames 3–4
- Pleural line becomes **brighter and more textured**
- Multiple **echogenic foci** appear below the pleural line (~4–6 cm depth)
- Sub-pleural pattern is distinctly **heterogeneous** compared to frames 1–2

### Frames 5–6
- **Bright vertical artifacts** visible, consistent with **B-lines/comet-tail artifacts** originating at the pleural level
- High-intensity reflectors on the right side of frame 5 (~5–6 cm)
- Sub-pleural speckle is clearly **different** from earlier frames — consistent with respiratory movement

### Frames 7–8
- Image becomes **darker/more homogeneous** in deeper zones
- Pleural line still identifiable; brightness diminishes
- Sub-pleural variation continues between these frames

### Frames 9–10
- Return of **heterogeneous sub-pleural pattern**
- Bright areas reappear at ~4–5 cm, suggesting cyclical respiratory motion
- Speckle distribution **differs** from frames 7–8

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The shimmering/granular pleural line, the presence of B-line artifacts, and the frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern all indicate normal lung sliding. There is no evidence of a pneumothorax (no barcode sign, no static pleural line, no lung point).
