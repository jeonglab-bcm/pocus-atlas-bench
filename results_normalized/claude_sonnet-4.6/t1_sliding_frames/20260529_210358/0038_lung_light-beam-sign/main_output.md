# Lung Ultrasound Frame-by-Frame Analysis — R3 Zone

---

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Frames | Pleural Line Appearance | Sub-pleural Area | Artifacts |
|--------|------------------------|-----------------|-----------|
| **1–2** | Smooth, sharply defined, static | Predominantly anechoic/dark | No B-lines; no comet-tail artifacts |
| **3–4** | Slightly more textured; early shimmer | Vertical hyperechoic artifacts begin to appear | Possible B-lines emerging |
| **5–6** | Increasing granularity/brightness variation | Sub-pleural speckle begins to vary between frames | Minimal artifacts |
| **7–8** | Distinctly more textured, shimmering | Speckle pattern clearly differs from earlier frames | Increased sub-pleural activity |
| **9–10** | Clearly granular/active pleural interface | Definite vertical artifacts extending inferiorly | B-lines/comet-tails visible |

---

## Key Findings

### Signs of Sliding ABSENT (early frames):
- ✅ Smooth, sharply defined pleural line
- ✅ Static, unchanging sub-pleural appearance
- ✅ No B-lines or comet-tail artifacts
- ✅ Large anechoic sub-pleural zone (consistent with absent lung aeration/sliding)

### Signs of Sliding PRESENT (later frames):
- ✅ Shimmering, granular pleural line
- ✅ Sub-pleural speckle pattern changes between consecutive frames
- ✅ Vertical artifacts (B-lines/comet tails) originating at the pleural interface

---

## Conclusion

> ## ⚠️ LUNG POINT — **BOTH Present and Absent**
>
> The sequential frames demonstrate a **transition from absent pleural sliding (frames 1–2) to present sliding (frames 7–10)**, with a gradual transition zone in frames 3–6. This alternating pattern — a static/smooth pleural line in some frames versus a shimmering/granular line with artifacts in others — is the **lung point sign**, which carries **~100% specificity for pneumothorax**.
>
> The lung point represents the site where the partially collapsed lung intermittently contacts the parietal pleura during the respiratory cycle.

**Recommended next step:** M-mode confirmation (alternating seashore ↔ barcode pattern) and clinical correlation for pneumothorax management.
