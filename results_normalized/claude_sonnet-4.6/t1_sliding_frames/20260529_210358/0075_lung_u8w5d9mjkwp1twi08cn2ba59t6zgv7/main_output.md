- Granular, shimmering pleural interface

### ✅ Signs of BOTH — **Lung Point**
- The **arrows progressively track a transition point** along the pleural line
- One side of the arrow: sliding present (lung contacting chest wall)
- Other side of the arrow: sliding absent (pleural separation)
- This alternating pattern defines the **lung point**

## 🔺 Conclusion

> **BOTH — LUNG POINT IDENTIFIED**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **lung point (point pulmonaire)** — the precise location where pleural sliding transitions from present to absent. This sign is:

- **100% specific for pneumothorax** when identified
- Caused by the leading edge of the pneumothorax, where collapsed (non-sliding) lung meets the still-apposed (sliding) lung
- The arrow markers explicitly highlight this transition zone across frames

**Clinical significance:** This is pathognomonic for **pneumothorax**. The lung point also helps estimate pneumothorax size — a lateral lung point implies larger pneumothorax.
