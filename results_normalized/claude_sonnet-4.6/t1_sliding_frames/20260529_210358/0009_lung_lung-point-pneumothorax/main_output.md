## Key Signs Identified

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT:
- **Progressive brightening and granularity** of the pleural line across the sequence
- **Frame-to-frame sub-pleural speckle variation** — particularly evident in frames 5–10
- **Shimmering pleural interface** (not static or smooth)
- A-lines present but in a **dynamic** context, excluding pure stratosphere/barcode pattern

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT: *Not observed*
- No smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line maintained across all frames
- No barcode/stratosphere pattern
- Speckle pattern does change between consecutive frames

---

## Conclusion

> ## 🟢 Pleural Sliding: **PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **shimmering, granular pleural line** with **changing sub-pleural speckle** between frames — consistent with the **2D equivalent of the seashore sign**. The A-lines visible are a normal finding in well-aerated lung and do not indicate pneumothorax in this context. **No lung point or barcode sign** is identified.
