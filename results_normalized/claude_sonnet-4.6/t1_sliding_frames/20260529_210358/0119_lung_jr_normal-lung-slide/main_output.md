---

## Key Findings

### ✅ Signs Supporting Sliding PRESENT
- The pleural line is **bright and textured**, not razor-sharp and static
- **Sub-pleural speckle pattern visibly changes** between consecutive frames (most evident comparing frames 1–3 vs. frames 8–10)
- **No pure barcode/stratosphere pattern** — the horizontal layers below the pleural line are not perfectly static and identical across all frames
- A-lines are present but **not perfectly replicated** frame-to-frame (slight positional/intensity variation)

### ❌ Signs of Absent Sliding NOT convincingly present
- No frames show a perfectly smooth, glassy, unchanging pleural line
- Sub-pleural speckle is **not identical** across all 10 frames

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate the characteristic **shimmering pleural line** with **inter-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle** — the ultrasound equivalent of the "seashore sign." The combination of **A-lines + sliding present** is consistent with a **normally aerated lung** and effectively **rules out pneumothorax** at this location.
