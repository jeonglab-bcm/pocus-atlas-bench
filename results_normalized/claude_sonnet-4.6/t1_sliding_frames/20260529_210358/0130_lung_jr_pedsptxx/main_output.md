## Key Findings

### ✅ Signs Supporting Sliding PRESENT:
- The pleural line is **bright and granular/shimmering** across multiple frames — not smooth and static
- **Sub-pleural speckle pattern demonstrably varies** between consecutive frames (especially frames 3→4 and 7→8)
- No perfectly "frozen," unchanged pleural interface across the entire clip
- A-lines are present but show subtle frame-to-frame variation in the deeper field

### ❌ Signs of Absent Sliding:
- No barcode/stratosphere sign pattern observed
- No perfectly smooth, immobile pleural line sustained across all frames

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential variation in sub-pleural speckle and the granular/shimmering quality of the pleural line are consistent with **normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition during respiration**. No lung point or stratosphere sign is identified. This appearance is **inconsistent with pneumothorax** at this location.
