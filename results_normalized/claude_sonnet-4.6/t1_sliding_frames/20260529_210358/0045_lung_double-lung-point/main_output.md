### Key Observations

**Supporting Sliding PRESENT:**
- ✅ The pleural line demonstrates a **shimmering, granular texture** across multiple frames rather than a smooth, perfectly static line
- ✅ **Sub-pleural speckle pattern varies** between consecutive frames — notably between frames 1→3, 4→6, and 8→10
- ✅ **No stratosphere/barcode sign** — horizontal lines below the pleural line are not uniformly static; there is visible frame-to-frame texture change
- ✅ The **pleural line undulates subtly** in position/brightness across the sequence
- ✅ **A-lines present** but not perfectly "frozen" — their intensity varies slightly, consistent with dynamic lung interface

**Against Sliding ABSENT:**
- ❌ No frames show a completely smooth, featureless, motionless pleural line
- ❌ No barcode/stratosphere pattern identified
- ❌ Sub-pleural region is not uniformly static

**No evidence of a Lung Point:**
- ❌ No transition zone between sliding and non-sliding segments within any single frame

---

## Conclusion

> ### ✅ **Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**
>
> Across all 10 sequential frames of the **left lung**, the pleural line consistently demonstrates **granularity and shimmer**, with **frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle pattern**. These findings are characteristic of normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement during respiration (the "gliding sign"). There is **no evidence of pneumothorax** at this location.
