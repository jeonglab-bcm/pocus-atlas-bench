## Conclusion

> **BOTH — Lung Point Identified** 🫁

The sequential frames demonstrate a **lung point**: absent pleural sliding on the left portion of the field of view transitioning to present sliding on the right. This finding is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax**, representing the precise location where the collapsed lung re-contacts the parietal pleura during the respiratory cycle.

**Clinical implication:** Pneumothorax confirmed. The lung point location can help estimate the size of the pneumothorax (more lateral = larger).
