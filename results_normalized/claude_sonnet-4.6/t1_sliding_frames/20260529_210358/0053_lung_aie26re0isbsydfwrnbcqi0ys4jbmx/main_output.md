## Conclusion

> **🟢 Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **shimmering, granular pleural line** with consistent **sub-pleural speckle variation** between frames — the hallmark of the **"gliding sign."** There is no evidence of a stratosphere/barcode pattern or a fixed, motionless pleural interface.
