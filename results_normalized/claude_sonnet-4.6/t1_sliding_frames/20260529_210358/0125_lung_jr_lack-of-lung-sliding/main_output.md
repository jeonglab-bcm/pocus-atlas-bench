## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Pleural Line Characteristics
Across all 10 sequential frames, the **bright hyperechoic pleural line** (visible at the top of the dark acoustic shadow region) demonstrates:
- **Positional variation** between frames — the peak and contour of the bright reflector shifts notably from frames 1→4 and again in frames 7→10
- A **textured/shimmering quality** rather than a perfectly smooth, static line
- Subtle **undulation in curvature**, consistent with respiratory excursion

### Sub-Pleural Region
- The **speckle pattern below the pleural line changes** between consecutive frames
- Horizontal reverberation artifacts (**A-lines**) are visible but **not perfectly static** — their intensity and spacing show minor variation across frames
- No barcode/stratosphere pattern is identified (which would show completely static, equidistant horizontal lines)

### Deeper Structures
- A large **anechoic (dark) region** dominates the central field — consistent with acoustic shadowing from aerated lung
- Lateral echogenic tissue on both sides shows normal chest wall layering
