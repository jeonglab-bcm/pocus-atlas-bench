- No shimmering or granular texture at the pleural interface
- No B-lines or comet-tail artifacts propagating from the pleural line
- No frame-to-frame speckle diversity in the sub-pleural zone

### ❌ No lung point identified

## Conclusion

> **Lung sliding is ABSENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **static, smooth pleural line** with persistent **A-lines** and **no sub-pleural speckle variation** — findings consistent with the **stratosphere/barcode sign** on M-mode.

### Clinical Interpretation
In the appropriate clinical context, absent sliding with an A-line profile raises significant concern for:
- **Pneumothorax** (primary consideration)
- Other causes: mainstem intubation (contralateral side), severe bullous emphysema, prior pleurodesis, or pleural adhesions

> ⚠️ *Correlation with clinical history, M-mode confirmation, and lung point assessment is recommended before clinical decision-making.*
