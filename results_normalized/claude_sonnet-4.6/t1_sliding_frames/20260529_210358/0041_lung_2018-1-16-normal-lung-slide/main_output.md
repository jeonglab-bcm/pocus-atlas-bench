## Key Findings

### Signs of **ABSENT** sliding (dominant):
- ✅ Predominantly **smooth, sharply defined** pleural line throughout most frames
- ✅ Horizontal **A-line reverberation artifacts** visible and largely static in deeper field
- ✅ Minimal sub-pleural speckle variation across the **left segment** in all frames

### Signs of **PRESENT** sliding (focal):
- ✅ **Asymmetric brightening and granularity** of the right pleural segment in frames 3–5 and 8
- ✅ Sub-pleural speckle **does shift** on the right side between consecutive frames
- ✅ Brief comet-tail-like artifacts arise from the right pleural segment intermittently

### Signs of a **LUNG POINT**:
- ✅ A **visible transition zone** where the left pleural line remains static while the right side shimmers
- ✅ This transition alternates: right-side sliding appears → disappears → reappears across the clip
- ✅ Consistent with a **lung point** — the boundary between pneumothorax and re-expanded lung sliding in and out with respiration

---

## Conclusion

> **BOTH — Lung Point Present**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **lung point**: the **left portion** of the pleural line is **static with absent sliding** (smooth, A-line-dominant, barcode pattern equivalent), while the **right portion** exhibits **intermittent pleural sliding** (shimmer, speckle variation, comet-tail precursors). The alternating appearance across frames as the lung moves in and out is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax**, with this being the **single most specific ultrasound sign** (near 100% specificity) for that diagnosis.
