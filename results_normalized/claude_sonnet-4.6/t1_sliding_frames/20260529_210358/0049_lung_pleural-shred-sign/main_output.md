## Key Findings Across All Frames

### ❌ Signs of Sliding **ABSENT**:
- The pleural line remains **sharply defined and non-shimmering** throughout all 10 frames
- **No granular or textured quality** to the pleural interface
- **A-lines are present and perfectly static** — no frame-to-frame variation
- Sub-pleural speckle pattern shows **no meaningful variation** between consecutive frames
- No B-lines or comet-tail artifacts originating from the pleural line

### ✅ Signs of Sliding **NOT PRESENT**:
- No seashore-sign equivalent pattern
- No shimmer or movement artifact at the pleural line
- No sub-pleural "sandy" texture changes

---

## Conclusion

> ## 🔴 **ABSENT Pleural Sliding**
> This pattern — static, smooth pleural line + consistent A-lines + no sub-pleural speckle variation — is the **"stratosphere/barcode" equivalent in B-mode**, strongly suggesting **pneumothorax** or another cause of absent sliding (pleurodesis, severe consolidation, main-stem intubation).

**Recommended next step:** M-mode through the pleural line to confirm the **barcode/stratosphere sign**, and assess for a **lung point** (pathognomonic for pneumothorax) by scanning laterally.
