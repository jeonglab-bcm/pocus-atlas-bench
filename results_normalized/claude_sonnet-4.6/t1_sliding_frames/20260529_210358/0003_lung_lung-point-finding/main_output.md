## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Early Frames (1–3)
- Chest wall musculature is clearly layered above the pleural line
- Pleural line is **bright but shows early granularity/shimmer**
- Subpleural region displays horizontal A-lines that are relatively consistent, though not perfectly identical between frames
- Minimal but detectable speckle variation below the pleural line

### Mid Frames (4–6)
- The pleural line begins to show **increased textural variation** — subtle shimmer becomes more apparent
- The subpleural speckle pattern **starts to differ** between consecutive frames
- A progressively brighter, more echogenic zone appears in the deeper portions of the image
- A-lines shift subtly in depth/intensity

### Late Frames (7–10)
- Clear **progressive brightening and granularity** in the subpleural zone
- The deeper field shows a **dynamic, changing echogenic pattern** consistent with aerated lung parenchyma sliding into and out of the scan plane
- The pleural line itself shows visible shimmer — not a static, sharply defined line
- No persistent barcode/stratosphere pattern at any point across the sequence

---

## Conclusion: ✅ **Pleural Sliding PRESENT**

| Feature | Observation |
|---|---|
| Pleural line texture | Granular/shimmering — not smooth/static |
| Subpleural speckle | Changes between frames |
| A-lines | Shift in position/intensity across frames |
| Barcode sign | **Absent** |
| Deep echogenicity | Progressively dynamic (lung movement) |

The sequential brightening and granularity in the deeper field across frames 4–10 represents **aerated lung sliding beneath the parietal pleura**, confirming visceral-parietal pleural apposition — **no pneumothorax**.
