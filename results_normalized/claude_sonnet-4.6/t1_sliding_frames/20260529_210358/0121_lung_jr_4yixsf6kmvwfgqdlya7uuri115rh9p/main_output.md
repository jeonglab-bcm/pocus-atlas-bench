## Clinical Significance

| Finding | Interpretation |
|--------|---------------|
| Absent sliding (majority of frames) | Visceral-parietal pleural separation |
| Lung point (frames 8–10) | Lung re-contacts chest wall during respiration |
| **Overall diagnosis** | **Pneumothorax** — lung point confirms and localizes its boundary |

> ⚠️ The **lung point** is pathognomonic for pneumothorax with ~100% specificity. Its location helps estimate pneumothorax size.
