## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding: PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **textured, variably echogenic pleural line** with **changing sub-pleural speckle patterns** between frames — consistent with visceral pleural movement against the parietal pleura (lung sliding). The absence of a perfectly static, sharply defined pleural line and the lack of a barcode sign argue against pneumothorax at this location.

*Note: Formal M-mode confirmation (seashore sign) would strengthen this assessment.*
