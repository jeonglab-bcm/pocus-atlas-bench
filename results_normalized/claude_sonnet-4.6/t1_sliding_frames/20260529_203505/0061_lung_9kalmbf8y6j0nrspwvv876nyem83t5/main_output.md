## Conclusion

> ## 🔴 Pleural Sliding: **ABSENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **static, smooth pleural line** with **fixed repetitive A-lines** and **no sub-pleural dynamic variation** across all frames. This pattern is consistent with a **stratosphere/barcode sign** on M-mode.

### Differential Diagnosis for Absent Sliding (Left Side):
1. **Pneumothorax** *(primary concern)*
2. Right mainstem intubation
3. Pleurodesis / pleural adhesions
4. Complete atelectasis
5. Apnea at time of scanning

> ⚠️ **Clinical correlation is mandatory.** Identification of a **lung point** (transition between sliding and non-sliding) would confirm pneumothorax with near 100% specificity.
