## Signs Identified

### ✅ Sliding PRESENT
- **Subpleural speckle variation** between consecutive frames
- **Textured/shimmering pleural line** (not perfectly smooth and static)
- **A-lines present WITH sliding** → consistent with **A-profile** (normal lung)
- No barcode/stratosphere pattern identifiable

### ❌ Signs of Absent Sliding
- No perfectly static, mirror-smooth pleural line
- No barcode sign observed

### 🔄 Lung Point
- No transition zone identified; changes are **diffuse across the pleural line**

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT** throughout these frames.
> The combination of **A-lines + sliding** represents the classic **"A-profile"**, most consistent with **normal aeration** or obstructive lung disease (asthma/COPD). **Pneumothorax is effectively excluded** at this location.
