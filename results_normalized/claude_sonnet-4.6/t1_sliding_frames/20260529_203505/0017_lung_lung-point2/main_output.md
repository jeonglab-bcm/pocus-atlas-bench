## M-Mode Observations

The yellow triangle cursor progresses **left → right** across the temporal axis (−4 to 0 seconds), allowing assessment of the full M-mode sweep:

- **Early sweep (Frames 1–6):** Classic **Seashore Sign** — horizontal lines in the chest wall zone above the pleural line, with a **granular/sandy texture below** → consistent with pleural sliding **PRESENT**
- **Transitional zone (Frames 7–8):** Mixed pattern — partial granularity fading

## Conclusion

> ## ⚠️ LUNG POINT DETECTED

The clip demonstrates a **temporal transition** from:
- ✅ **Seashore sign** (sliding present) → to
- ❌ **Barcode/stratosphere sign** (sliding absent)

This alternating pattern on M-mode, combined with the visible change in pleural line texture on B-mode, is **pathognomonic for the Lung Point sign** — the boundary where aerated lung meets a non-sliding region (e.g., **pneumothorax**). This is the **most specific ultrasound sign for pneumothorax** (specificity ~100%).
