# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis – Left Lung Ultrasound

---

### Structural Landmarks (Consistent Across All Frames)
- **Rib shadows** are visible bilaterally and remain fixed (expected, as ribs don't move with respiration)
- A **bright hyperechoic pleural line** is identifiable between the rib shadows in all frames

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Frames | Pleural Line Appearance | Sub-pleural Speckle | Notable Features |
|--------|------------------------|---------------------|------------------|
| 1–2 | Bright, slightly textured | Moderate echogenicity | Initial baseline |
| 3–4 | Slightly shimmering | **Noticeably different** from frames 1–2 | Speckle shift visible |
| 5–6 | Granular, bright interface | Continuing variation | Sub-pleural texture changes |
| 7–8 | Textured, non-static | Clear inter-frame difference | Consistent with motion |
| 9–10 | Bright with shimmer | Speckle pattern continues to vary | No "freeze" of pattern |

---

### Key Signs Identified

✅ **Shimmering/granular pleural line** — the pleural line is bright and textured, not smooth and fixed  
✅ **Sub-pleural speckle variation** — the echogenicity pattern *below* the pleural line changes between consecutive frames, indicating visceral pleural movement  
✅ **No barcode/stratosphere sign** — horizontal static lines are absent below the pleural line  
✅ **No lung point** — no transition zone between sliding and non-sliding segments is identified  

---

### Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a shimmering, granular pleural line with dynamic sub-pleural speckle variation between frames — both hallmarks of normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement. This pattern is consistent with a **normal left lung**, effectively **ruling out pneumothorax** at this location.
