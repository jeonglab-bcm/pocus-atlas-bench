## Key Findings

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT
1. **Shimmering pleural line**: The pleural line progressively brightens and becomes granular/textured in frames 6–10 — a hallmark of lung sliding
2. **Comet-tail/B-line artifacts**: Vertical hyperechoic artifacts originating from the pleural line become clearly visible in frames 7–10
3. **Subpleural speckle variation**: Texture beneath the pleural line changes detectably between consecutive frames, confirming dynamic interface movement
4. **Respiratory phase correlation**: The cyclic pattern of brightening aligns with respiratory movement of visceral pleura against parietal pleura

### ❌ No Signs of Sliding ABSENT
- No persistent barcode/stratosphere pattern
- No completely static, perfectly smooth pleural line maintained across all frames
- A-lines, where present, do not remain perfectly unchanged

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate clear evidence of lung sliding: a dynamically brightening and shimmering pleural line, comet-tail artifacts originating from the pleural interface, and inter-frame subpleural speckle variation. No lung point or absent sliding pattern is identified. This is a **normal lung sliding pattern**, effectively ruling out pneumothorax at this site.
