| Finding | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Static, smooth pleural line (frames 1–7) | Absent sliding (air between pleural layers) |

> ⚠️ **The lung point is the only finding 100% specific for pneumothorax.** In the context of acute chest pain, this finding demands urgent clinical correlation and management.
