# Lung Ultrasound Frame-by-Frame Analysis

## Systematic Frame Review

### Pleural Line Assessment (All Frames)
| Feature | Observation |
|---------|-------------|
| Pleural line texture | **Smooth, sharply defined, hyperechoic** — minimal shimmering or granularity |
| Frame-to-frame variation | Pleural line position and brightness remain **largely static** |
| B-lines / comet-tails | **Absent** — no artifacts arising from the pleural line |

### Sub-Pleural Region (Frames 1–10)
- **Frames 1–4:** Sub-pleural area shows horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines); speckle pattern shows **minimal inter-frame variation**
- **Frames 5–8:** Below the pleural line, **increasingly prominent horizontal banding** — consistent with a **stratosphere/barcode sign**
- **Frames 9–10:** Pattern persists; A-lines remain static and equidistant with no granular variation

## Conclusion

> ## ⚠️ Pleural Sliding: **ABSENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **static, smooth pleural line** with **unchanging horizontal A-lines** and a **stratosphere/barcode pattern** in the sub-pleural zone — all classic indicators of **absent lung sliding**.

**Differential diagnosis to consider:**
- **Pneumothorax** (most urgent)
- Pleurodesis / pleural adhesion
- Severe consolidation/atelectasis
- Main-stem intubation (contralateral lung)

> No lung point was identified in this clip, though its absence does not exclude pneumothorax. Clinical correlation and M-mode confirmation are recommended.
