## Key Findings

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT:
- **Pleural line**: Smooth, sharply defined, and **non-dynamic** across all frames
- **A-lines**: Persistently present, horizontal reverberation lines — **perfectly static and replicated** throughout the clip
- **Sub-pleural speckle**: **No frame-to-frame variation** in the granular pattern below the pleural line
- **No B-lines** or comet-tail artifacts arising from the pleural interface
- **No shimmering or granular texture** at the pleural line

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT: **None identified**

### ⚡ Lung Point: **Not identified** — no transition between sliding/non-sliding zones visible

## Conclusion

> ## 🔴 **Absent Pleural Sliding**
> The findings are consistent with the **Stratosphere / Barcode sign** pattern on M-mode. Combined with static A-lines and absent sub-pleural speckle variation, this strongly suggests **absent lung sliding**.

### Differential Diagnosis:
1. **Pneumothorax** *(primary concern)*
2. Mainstem intubation (ipsilateral absence)
3. Severe bullous emphysema
4. Pleurodesis / pleural fibrosis
5. Apnea / breath-hold artifact

> ⚠️ **Clinical correlation is essential.** Identify a lung point to confirm pneumothorax with high specificity.
