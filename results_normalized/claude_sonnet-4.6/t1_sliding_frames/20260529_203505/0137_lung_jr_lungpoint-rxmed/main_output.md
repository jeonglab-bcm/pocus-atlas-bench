## Conclusion

> ## 🔴 LUNG POINT — Signs of BOTH Present and Absent Sliding

The sequential frames demonstrate an **alternating pattern** — certain frames show clear pleural shimmering and speckle variation (sliding present), while adjacent frames show a smooth, static pleural line with unchanging A-lines (sliding absent). This **cyclical alternation**, synchronized with the respiratory cycle at a **fixed transition zone**, is pathognomonic for a **lung point**.

### Clinical Significance:
The lung point represents the **boundary between collapsed (pneumothorax) and aerated lung**. Its presence confirms **pneumothorax** with near 100% specificity. The location of the lung point can help estimate pneumothorax size.
