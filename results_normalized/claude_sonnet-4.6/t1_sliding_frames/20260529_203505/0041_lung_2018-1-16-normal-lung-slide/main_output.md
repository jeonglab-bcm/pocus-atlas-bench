## Key Observations

### Signs of **ABSENT** sliding (dominant, central/left region):
- ✅ Smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line
- ✅ Well-preserved, repetitive **A-lines** unchanged across frames
- ✅ No sub-pleural speckle variation between frames
- ✅ No B-lines or comet-tail artifacts

### Signs of **PRESENT** sliding (right margin, intermittent):
- ✅ The right portion of the field shows **cyclic appearance/disappearance** of a darker region (lung re-entry)
- ✅ Subtle shimmering/textural change at that transition zone
- ✅ Speckle variation in that localized segment

---

## Conclusion

> **⚠️ LUNG POINT IDENTIFIED**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **lung point** — a transition zone where:
- The **left/central** pleural line is static with persistent A-lines → **absent sliding** (collapsed lung / pneumothorax)
- The **right margin** shows intermittent dynamic change → **sliding present** (lung re-contact with chest wall during inspiration)

This alternating pattern is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax** and represents the boundary of the collapsed lung.
