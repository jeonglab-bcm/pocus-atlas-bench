## Key Signs Identified

### ✅ Signs Supporting Sliding PRESENT:
- **Shimmering/granular pleural line**: The pleural interface lacks the smooth, "laser-sharp" static quality seen in absent sliding
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation**: The speckle pattern below the pleural line changes noticeably between consecutive frames (especially frames 1→5 and 7→10)
- **Comet-tail artifacts**: Vertical hyperechoic artifacts originating at the pleural line are visible in frames 3, 4, 9, and 10
- **Dynamic pleural line texture**: The echogenicity and texture of the pleural line fluctuates across the sequence, consistent with visceral-parietal pleural movement

### ❌ Signs of Absent Sliding (NOT observed):
- No smooth, perfectly static, "barcode-like" pleural line
- No perfectly preserved identical A-lines across all frames
- No fixed horizontal-only pattern in the deep field

---

## Conclusion

> **🟢 Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **dynamically changing, granular pleural line** with inter-frame sub-pleural speckle variation and intermittent comet-tail artifacts — all hallmarks of normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and sliding. There is **no evidence of a lung point or barcode pattern**, making pneumothorax at this location unlikely.
