## Key Observations

### ✅ Signs Supporting PRESENT Sliding
- **Shimmering, granular pleural line**: The pleural line is bright but **not static or perfectly smooth** — it shows textural variation frame-to-frame
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation**: The pattern immediately deep to the pleural line **changes between consecutive frames**, consistent with underlying lung movement
- **No barcode/stratosphere sign**: Horizontal static lines are **not seen** in the sub-pleural region
- **A-lines present but dynamic**: Reverberation artifacts are visible but shift subtly between frames

### ❌ No Signs of Absent Sliding
- No razor-sharp, motionless pleural line
- No completely static sub-pleural field
- No stratosphere (barcode) pattern

### ⚠️ No Lung Point Identified
- The sliding pattern appears **uniform** across the entire visible pleural line
- No transition zone between sliding and non-sliding segments is identifiable

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The pleural line demonstrates a **shimmering, granular texture** with **inter-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle**, consistent with **normal lung sliding**. This effectively **rules out pneumothorax** at this location.
