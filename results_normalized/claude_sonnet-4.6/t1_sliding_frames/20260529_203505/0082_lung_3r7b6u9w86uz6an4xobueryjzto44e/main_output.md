## Conclusion

> ## ⚠️ BOTH — Lung Point Identified

The sequential frames demonstrate a **lung point**: pleural sliding is **present** in the zone overlying the subpleural consolidation (left/central region) and **absent** in the adjacent anechoic zone (right/upper region). The transition is reproducible across multiple frames.

**This pattern is pathognomonic for pneumothorax.** The consolidated/atelectatic lung tissue represents the underlying lung edge that intermittently reaches the chest wall during inspiration, generating the sliding signal, while the remainder is separated by intrapleural air (absent sliding).

> **Recommended next step:** Correlate with clinical findings, confirm with M-mode (seashore ↔ barcode alternation at the transition point), and consider chest X-ray or CT if clinically indicated.
