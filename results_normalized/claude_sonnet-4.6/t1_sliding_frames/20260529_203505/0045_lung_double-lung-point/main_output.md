### Key Sign Assessment

#### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT:
- **Shimmering/granular pleural line**: The pleural interface is **not smooth and static** — it demonstrates brightness variation and textural irregularity across sequential frames
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation**: The echogenicity pattern immediately below the pleural line **changes between consecutive frames**, indicating lung tissue movement beneath the stationary chest wall
- **Pleural line positional shift**: Subtle but consistent craniocaudal displacement of the bright interface corresponds to respiratory excursion
- **No perfectly static A-lines**: The reverberation artifacts below show frame-to-frame variation, inconsistent with a fixed, non-sliding lung

#### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT — Not Observed:
- No **smooth, sharply defined, immobile** pleural line
- No **stratosphere/barcode pattern** characteristics
- No **identical sub-pleural texture** across all frames

#### ⚠️ Signs of Lung Point — Not Observed:
- The pleural line variation is **uniform across the visible segment**
- No transition zone where one portion slides and another is fixed
- No alternating shimmer/static pattern within a single frame

---

## Conclusion

> ### 🟢 **Lung Sliding: PRESENT**
>
> The sequential frames demonstrate consistent pleural line shimmering, sub-pleural speckle heterogeneity varying between frames, and respiratory displacement of the pleural interface — all hallmarks of intact visceral-parietal pleural apposition with normal lung sliding on the **left side**.
>
> **Clinical implication:** These findings make **left-sided pneumothorax unlikely** at the interrogated intercostal space. No lung point was identified to suggest partial pneumothorax.
