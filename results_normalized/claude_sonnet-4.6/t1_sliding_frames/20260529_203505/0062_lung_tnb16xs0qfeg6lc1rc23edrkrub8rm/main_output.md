## Conclusion

## 🔴 LUNG POINT — Both Present and Absent

This is the classic **lung point sign**, the pattern most consistent with **pneumothorax**:

- During **inspiration**: The partially collapsed lung re-expands and contacts the parietal pleura → **pleural sliding reappears** (wide, bright, shimmering fan)
- During **expiration**: The lung falls away from the chest wall → **pleural sliding disappears** (narrow, dark, static image)

> ⚠️ **The lung point sign carries ~100% specificity for pneumothorax** and represents the boundary between collapsed (airspace) and aerated lung. This finding warrants urgent clinical correlation and management.
