## Key Findings

✅ **Pleural line is NOT perfectly smooth or static** — it exhibits texture and shimmer variation across frames

✅ **Sub-pleural speckle changes between consecutive frames** — particularly notable in frames 7→8 and 9→10, indicating underlying lung movement

✅ **A-lines are present but NOT perfectly static** — subtle variation in intensity and definition between frames

❌ No barcode/stratosphere pattern evident

❌ No lung point transition identified

---

## Conclusion

> **Lung sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **shimmering/textured pleural line** with **inter-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle**, consistent with visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement during respiration. This pattern would correspond to a **seashore sign on M-mode**. There is **no evidence of pneumothorax** at this imaging location.
