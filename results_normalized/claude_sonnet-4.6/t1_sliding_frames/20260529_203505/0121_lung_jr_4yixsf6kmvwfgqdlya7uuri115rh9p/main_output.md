## Conclusion: **BOTH — Lung Point Present** 🔴

| Feature | Observation |
|---|---|
| Medial pleural segment | Shimmering (sliding present) |
| Lateral pleural segment | Static, smooth (sliding absent) |
| Transition zone | Visible across frames 8–10 |
| A-lines | Present in non-sliding zone |
| B-lines/comet tails | Absent |

> ⚠️ **The lung point** — the transition between a sliding and a non-sliding pleural segment — is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax**. This is the most specific ultrasound sign, with ~100% specificity.

**Clinical implication**: Pneumothorax should be confirmed clinically and managed accordingly.
