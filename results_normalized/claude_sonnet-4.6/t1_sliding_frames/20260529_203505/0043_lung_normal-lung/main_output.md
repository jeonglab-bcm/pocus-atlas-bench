## Key Signs Identified

### ✅ Sliding PRESENT:
- **Shimmering pleural line**: The pleural interface transitions from relatively smooth (frames 1–2) to clearly granular/textured (frames 7–10)
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation**: The speckle pattern beneath the pleural line clearly **differs between consecutive frames**, particularly frames 5→10
- **Bright mid-field fluctuation**: The expanding bright area in the middle of the image suggests lung moving into the near-field during inspiration (respiratory cycle motion)
- **A-line variation**: A-lines are present but show **variable intensity** across frames — consistent with a sliding lung rather than the perfectly static A-lines of a barcode sign

### ❌ Sliding ABSENT signs:
- No barcode/stratosphere sign observed
- No perfectly static, unchanging pleural line throughout the entire sequence
- A-lines do not remain identical frame-to-frame

### 🔄 Lung Point:
- No visible transition from sliding to non-sliding — not identified

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT** throughout this clip.

The progression from frames 1→10 likely represents the **respiratory cycle** (expiration to inspiration), with the lung sliding under the pleural line producing the characteristic shimmering interface and speckle variation. This pattern is consistent with a **normal aerated lung** and effectively **rules out pneumothorax** at this location.
