## Assessment of Sliding Signs

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT
- **Shimmering pleural line** visible across multiple frames (frames 2, 3, 6–8)
- **Subpleural speckle variation** clearly differs between consecutive frames — not static
- **Sinusoid sign equivalent**: lung repeatedly moves toward and away from the probe with respiration within the effusion
- **No barcode/stratosphere pattern** at any point
- **No perfectly static, unchanging A-lines** — the interface is dynamic

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT
- None identified

### 🔄 Signs of a Lung Point (BOTH)
- Not identified — no fixed transition between sliding and non-sliding zones

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **right pleural effusion** with an actively mobile lung (anechoic fluid + floating echogenic lung = **"jellyfish sign"**). The sinusoidal respiratory cycling of the lung within the effusion, combined with frame-to-frame variation in the pleural interface and subpleural speckle, confirms lung sliding is intact. This pattern is inconsistent with pneumothorax.

**Clinical correlation:** Right-sided pleural effusion with preserved visceral–parietal pleural contact/sliding.
