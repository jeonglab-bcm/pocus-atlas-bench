# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Structural Landmarks (All Frames)
- **Linear probe (L14-6Ns)**, EM FAST protocol
- Consistent **dark anechoic shadow on the left** = rib posterior acoustic shadow
- Bright horizontal echogenic line = **pleural line** at ~1 cm depth
- Horizontal reverberation lines below = **A-lines**

---

### Sequential Frame Observations

| Frames | Pleural Line Character | Sub-pleural Speckle | Notable Features |
|--------|----------------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| 1–4 | **Smooth, sharply defined** | Minimal variation | Static A-lines, no shimmer |
| 5–6 | **Subtle irregularity appears** mid-right | Slight variation begins | Possible interface change |
| 7–10 | **Convex echogenic structure** enters from right | Increasing variation right-sided | A structure appears/disappears dynamically |

---

## Critical Finding: **LUNG POINT**

### Evidence:

**Absent sliding (left/central zone):**
- ✅ Smooth, static, sharply defined pleural line
- ✅ Perfectly replicating horizontal A-lines
- ✅ No sub-pleural speckle variation

**Present sliding (right zone, frames 7–10):**
- ✅ Granular/irregular pleural line texture on right
- ✅ Sub-pleural speckle variation between frames
- ✅ Dynamic echogenic structure sliding into field

---

## Conclusion

> **LUNG POINT IDENTIFIED** — pathognomonic for **pneumothorax**

The transition point where **absent sliding transitions to present sliding** is visible in the right portion of the field. This alternating pattern (static → shimmering) across frames represents the lung edge moving in and out with respiration — the **lung point sign**, which has ~100% specificity for pneumothorax.
