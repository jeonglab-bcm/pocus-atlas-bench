## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT throughout the clip**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **shimmering, textured pleural line**, intermittent **comet-tail/B-line artifacts**, and consistent **frame-to-frame sub-pleural speckle variation** — all hallmarks of normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement with respiration.

**This pattern is inconsistent with pneumothorax in this zone.** The findings reflect normal lung aeration in the right middle field.
