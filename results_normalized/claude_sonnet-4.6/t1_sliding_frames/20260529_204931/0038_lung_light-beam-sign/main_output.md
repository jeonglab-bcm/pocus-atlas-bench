# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Frames 1–2
- Pleural line appears as a **smooth, sharply defined, bright linear structure**
- Sub-pleural field is predominantly **anechoic/dark**
- Horizontal reverberation artifacts (**A-lines**) are present below the pleural line
- No discernible variation in sub-pleural speckle between the two frames

### Frames 3–4
- Pleural line remains **smooth and static**
- A faint **vertical bright artifact** appears below the pleural line in one or both frames — potentially a comet-tail artifact, though it does not clearly "erase" A-lines as a true B-line would
- Sub-pleural speckle pattern shows **minimal change** between frames

### Frames 5–8
- Near-field (chest wall tissue) becomes progressively **more heterogeneous and echogenic** — indicating respiratory chest wall motion
- Despite this chest wall movement, the pleural line itself remains **relatively unchanged in texture and brightness**
- No shimmering or granular character to the pleural line
- A-lines below remain **parallel, equidistant, and static** — classic reverberation pattern

### Frames 9–10
- Recurrence of a **bright vertical streak** below the pleural line (similar to frames 3–4)
- Pleural line still appears **sharp and non-granular**
- Sub-pleural speckle continues to show **no meaningful inter-frame variation**

---

## Summary of Key Findings

| Feature | Observation |
|---|---|
| Pleural line texture | Smooth, sharply defined — **not granular or shimmering** |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation | Absent — pattern is static across frames |
| A-lines | Present, static, equidistant — **unchanged between frames** |
| B-lines | No definite B-lines; vertical artifacts seen in 2–3 frames are equivocal |
| Lung point | No clear transition from sliding to non-sliding segment identified |

---

## Conclusion

> ### ⚠️ Pleural Sliding: **ABSENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate the classic **B-mode correlate of the stratosphere/barcode sign**:
- A **smooth, non-textured, static pleural line**
- **Unchanged sub-pleural speckle** across all frames
- **Static, parallel A-lines** without B-line disruption

**Absent pleural sliding** is the primary ultrasound criterion for **pneumothorax** (in the appropriate clinical context), though other etiologies must be considered (pleural adhesions, pleurodesis, severe hyperinflation, apnea, mainstem intubation).
