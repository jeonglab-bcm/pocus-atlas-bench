# 0056_lung_lung-point

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Frames 1–2 (Baseline)
- Pleural line appears as a **bright, moderately textured interface**
- Sub-pleural region is largely anechoic/dark
- Soft tissue/muscle layers above show fibrillar echogenic lines
- Minimal sub-pleural speckle variation at this stage

---

### Frames 3–4 (Early Motion)
- A **rounded hyperechoic structure** begins to emerge from the left — consistent with a rib shadow moving into field
- Small **bright foci begin appearing** just below the pleural line (~1.5–2 cm depth)
- Sub-pleural speckle pattern starts to differ from earlier frames

---

### Frames 5–6 (Peak Activity)
- **Multiple clustered hyperechoic foci** clearly visible below the pleural line
- These bright spots have characteristics of **comet-tail artifacts / B-lines** arising from the pleural interface
- The pleural line itself appears **shimmering and irregular** rather than smooth and static
- Significant **inter-frame variation** in sub-pleural speckle is evident

---

### Frames 7–10 (Return Phase)
- The bright foci **progressively disperse** as respiration continues
- The pleural line retains a **granular, textured quality** (not a smooth, sharply defined line)
- Sub-pleural speckle continues to change between consecutive frames
- No segment of the pleural line remains perfectly fixed or static throughout

---

## Summary of Findings

| Sign | Observed? |
|------|-----------|
| Shimmering/granular pleural line | ✅ Yes |
| Comet-tail artifacts / B-lines from pleural line | ✅ Yes (frames 5–6) |
| Inter-frame sub-pleural speckle variation | ✅ Yes |
| Smooth, static, sharply defined pleural line | ❌ No |
| Fixed, unchanging A-lines | ❌ No |
| Transition zone (lung point) | ❌ Not identified |

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate:
- A **shimmering, textured pleural line** (not smooth or static)
- **Comet-tail/B-line artifacts** arising from the pleural surface in multiple frames
- Clear **inter-frame variation** in the sub-pleural speckle pattern consistent with visceral pleura moving against parietal pleura during respiration

This pattern is consistent with **normal lung sliding**, effectively ruling out pneumothorax at this location.
