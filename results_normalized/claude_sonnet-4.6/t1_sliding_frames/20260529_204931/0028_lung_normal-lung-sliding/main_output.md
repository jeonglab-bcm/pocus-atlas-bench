## Key Findings

### Signs of ABSENT sliding:
- ✅ **Smooth, sharply defined pleural line** — no shimmering or granularity throughout all frames
- ✅ **Static A-lines** — horizontal reverberation artifacts remain perfectly replicated across every frame
- ✅ **No sub-pleural speckle variation** — the region below the pleural line is essentially identical between consecutive frames
- ✅ **No B-lines** — no comet-tail artifacts arising from the pleural interface
- ✅ M-mode equivalent pattern: consistent with a **stratosphere/barcode sign**

### Signs of sliding PRESENT: ❌ None identified
### Signs of a lung point: ❌ No transition zone observed

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is ABSENT**

The appearance across all 10 frames is consistent with a **pneumothorax** (or alternative causes of absent sliding: pleurodesis, mainstem intubation, phrenic nerve palsy). Clinical correlation is essential for definitive diagnosis.
