## Key Findings

### Left/Center Segment (near rib shadow):
- ✅ Smooth, static pleural line
- ✅ No sub-pleural speckle variation
- ✅ Static, repeating A-lines → **Sliding ABSENT**

### Right Segment:
- ✅ Intermittent granular shimmer at pleural interface
- ✅ Speckle pattern changes between consecutive frames
- ✅ Periodic brightening consistent with lung contact → **Sliding PRESENT**

---

## Conclusion: **BOTH — Lung Point Identified** ⚠️

> The images demonstrate a **lung point** — the most specific ultrasound sign for **pneumothorax** (specificity ~100%). There is a visible transition zone where:
> - The **left/central** pleural segment is **static** (barcode pattern equivalent, pneumothorax zone)
> - The **right** pleural segment shows **intermittent sliding** (lung periodically re-apposing the chest wall with respiration)

This oscillating pattern of absent-then-present sliding at a fixed anatomical point is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax**.
