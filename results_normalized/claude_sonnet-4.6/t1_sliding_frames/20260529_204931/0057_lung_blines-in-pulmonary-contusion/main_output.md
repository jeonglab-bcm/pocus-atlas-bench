## Key Findings Summary

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT:
- **Granular/shimmering pleural line** — The pleural interface is bright and textured across most frames rather than sharply smooth and static
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation** — The echo texture immediately below the pleural line demonstrably changes between consecutive frames (especially F1→F2, F4→F5, F5→F6)
- **Comet-tail/B-line artifacts** — Frames 3–6 show vertically oriented echogenic artifacts arising from the pleural line, consistent with moving lung interface
- **A-lines are NOT perfectly static** — While A-lines are visible, their prominence varies frame-to-frame, inconsistent with a barcode sign

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT:
- No sustained stratosphere/barcode pattern identified
- No segment of the pleural line remains completely fixed across all frames

### ⚠️ Signs of Lung Point (BOTH):
- Frame 8 momentarily shows a smoother, less active pleural line — could represent a partial transition — but insufficient evidence for a definitive lung point

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate consistent hallmarks of normal visceral-parietal pleural movement: a shimmering, granular pleural line with frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle and the presence of comet-tail/B-line artifacts. No sustained barcode pattern or fixed static pleural line is identified across the clip.
