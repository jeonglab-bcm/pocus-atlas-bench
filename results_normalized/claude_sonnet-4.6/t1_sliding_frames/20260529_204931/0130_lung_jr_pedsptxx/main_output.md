## Conclusion: **Pleural Sliding PRESENT** ✅

The combination of:
- **Granular/textured pleural line** (not smooth/static)
- **Subtle but detectable sub-pleural speckle variation** between frames

is consistent with **present pleural sliding**, effectively **excluding pneumothorax** at this location.
