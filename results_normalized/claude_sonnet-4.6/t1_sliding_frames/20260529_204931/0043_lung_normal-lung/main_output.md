- ❌ **No B-lines** (comet-tail artifacts) arising from the pleural interface
- ❌ **No inter-frame speckle variation** in the sub-pleural zone
- ✅ **Smooth, hyperechoic, sharply defined pleural line** — consistent across all frames
- ✅ **Static, perfectly horizontal A-lines** — no disruption or respiratory modulation
- ✅ **Stratosphere/barcode equivalent** in B-mode: unchanging horizontal pattern throughout

---

## Conclusion

> ### ⚠️ **PLEURAL SLIDING: ABSENT**

All 10 frames demonstrate the **B-mode equivalent of the stratosphere/barcode sign** — a motionless pleural line with perfectly replicated, static A-lines and no sub-pleural speckle change. There is **no evidence of sliding in any frame**, and **no lung point transition** is identified.

**Clinical correlation:** Absent sliding raises concern for **pneumothorax**, but may also be seen with apnea, pleural adhesions, mainstem intubation, or severe consolidation. Confirmation with M-mode and clinical context is advised.
