> ### ✅ **Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**
>
> The sequential frames demonstrate clear **frame-to-frame variation** in pleural line texture and sub-pleural speckle, cycling between a shimmering/granular appearance and a smoother appearance — consistent with the **normal respiratory gliding motion** of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura.
