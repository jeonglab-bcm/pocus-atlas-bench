# Lung Ultrasound — Sequential Frame Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Frames | Pleural Line | Sub-pleural Speckle | A-lines | B-lines |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|---------|---------|
| 1–3 | Smooth, sharply defined, bright | Uniform, static | Present, horizontal, regular | None |

---

## Key Findings

- ❌ **No shimmering or granularity** at the pleural interface across all frames
- ❌ **No comet-tail artifacts or B-lines** originating from the pleural line
- ❌ **No sub-pleural speckle variation** between consecutive frames
- ✅ **Smooth, static, sharply defined pleural line** throughout
- ✅ **A-lines are perfectly static and equidistant** — unchanged across all 10 frames
- ✅ Would correspond to a **Stratosphere/Barcode sign** on M-mode

---

## Conclusion

> ## ⚠️ Pleural Sliding: **ABSENT**

The combination of a **static smooth pleural line + unchanging A-lines + absent sub-pleural speckle variation** is consistent with **absent lung sliding**, raising strong concern for:

- **Pneumothorax** (most critical diagnosis)
- Alternatively: mainstem intubation (contralateral lung), pleural adhesions, apnea, or phrenic nerve palsy

**Recommended next step:** Assess for a **lung point** (transition zone) to confirm pneumothorax and estimate its size.
