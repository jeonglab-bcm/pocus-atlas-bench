## Conclusion

> **Lung sliding is PRESENT**

The most compelling evidence is the **cyclical expansion and contraction of the acoustic window** (cone widening then narrowing), which reflects the lung moving toward and away from the probe during respiration — the 2D equivalent of the seashore sign.
