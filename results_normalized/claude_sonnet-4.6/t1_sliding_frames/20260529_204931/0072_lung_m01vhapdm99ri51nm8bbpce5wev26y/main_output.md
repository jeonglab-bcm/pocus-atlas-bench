## Sliding Assessment

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT:
1. **B-lines** — originate exclusively from the visceral-parietal pleural interface; their generation **requires pleural contact and sliding**
2. **Nodular/irregular pleural line** — not the smooth, featureless line of absent sliding
3. **Sub-pleural speckle variation** between consecutive frames (frames 1–6)

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT:
- No stratosphere/barcode sign demonstrated
- No perfectly static, smooth pleural line
- No frozen A-line pattern

### ⚡ Signs of Lung Point (BOTH):
- None identified — no focal transition between sliding and non-sliding segments

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The cardinal evidence is the **presence of multiple B-lines** (frames 7–10), which are physically impossible without visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement. Combined with **sub-pleural nodular irregularities** consistent with miliary granulomas, this ultrasound pattern represents:

- **Diffuse interstitial pattern** (B-lines) → miliary infiltration
- **Sub-pleural granulomas** → characteristic of **Miliary TB**
- **Preserved lung sliding** → no pneumothorax

This is a classic **"white lung with nodular pleural line"** pattern in miliary tuberculosis with intact pleural sliding.
