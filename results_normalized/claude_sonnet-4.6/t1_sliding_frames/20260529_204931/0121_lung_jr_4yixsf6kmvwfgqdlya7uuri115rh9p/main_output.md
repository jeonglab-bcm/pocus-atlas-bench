## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Frames 1–4 (Early sequence)
- Pleural line appears **smooth, sharply defined, and linear**
- Sub-pleural speckle pattern shows **minimal variation** between frames
- **A-lines visible**, appearing static and parallel below the pleural interface
- No comet-tail/B-line artifacts detected
- → **Signs of ABSENT sliding**

---

### Frames 5–7 (Mid sequence)

- → **Transitional zone**

### Frames 8–10 (Late sequence)

---

## Conclusion: **BOTH — Lung Point Present** 🔴

| Feature | Observation |
|---|---|
| Sliding absent zone | Frames 1–4: smooth, static pleural line |
| Sliding present zone | Frames 8–10: shimmering line, speckle variation |
| Transition | Visible cyclical shift across the sequence |

> **This pattern is pathognomonic for pneumothorax.**
> The lung point — where aerated lung intermittently contacts the chest wall during respiration — creates this alternating appearance of sliding present/absent within the same clip. M-mode at this location would show **alternating seashore and barcode (stratosphere) patterns**.
