## ⚠️ Conclusion: **LUNG POINT IDENTIFIED**

> The sequential transition from **absent sliding** (stratosphere/barcode pattern) → **present sliding** (granular, shimmering pleural line with sub-pleural texture) within the **same scanning location** is the definition of a **lung point**.

### Clinical Significance:
A lung point is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax** (100% specificity). This represents the boundary where:
- **Free pleural air** displaces the lung laterally (frames 1–7: no sliding)
- During **inspiration**, the lung re-expands into this zone (frames 8–10: sliding returns)

**In the clinical context of acute chest pain**, this finding mandates immediate evaluation for pneumothorax.
