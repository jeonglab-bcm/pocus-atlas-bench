## Key Diagnostic Features

### Signs Supporting Sliding **PRESENT** ✅
- Subtle but **reproducible frame-to-frame variation** in pleural line brightness and texture
- **Sub-pleural speckle pattern shifts** between consecutive frames
- Pleural line position changes slightly (consistent with respiratory motion)
- **No A-lines** visible deep to the pleural interface (rules against pneumothorax pattern)

### Signs Against Absent Sliding ❌
- No perfectly static, sharply fixed pleural line throughout the entire sequence
- Absence of classic barcode/stratosphere sign appearance
- The deep field is uniformly **anechoic** — not the repetitive A-line reverberation expected in pneumothorax

---

## Interpretation of the Deep Anechoic Field

> The large, echo-free area below the pleural line — **without A-line reverberation** — is most consistent with a **pleural effusion**, not pneumothorax.

In pneumothorax, you expect:
- A-lines (horizontal reverberations)
- Static bright pleural line
- No anechoic fluid collection

---

## Conclusion

### ✅ **Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**

These frames are consistent with **lung ultrasound demonstrating a pleural effusion**, with **preserved pleural sliding** evidenced by:

1. Progressive curvature of the pleural line across frames (respiratory motion)
2. Frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle
3. Mild but consistent shimmer of the pleural interface
4. Large anechoic fluid collection displacing the lung — classic effusion morphology

> **No lung point is identified** — sliding appears uniformly present across the visible pleural segment.
