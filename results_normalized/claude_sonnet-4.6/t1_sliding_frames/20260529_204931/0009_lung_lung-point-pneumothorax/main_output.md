## Conclusion

> ### ✅ **Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **shimmering/granular pleural line** with **progressive inter-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle**, consistent with normal visceral-parietal pleural movement during respiration. The A-lines present are not static artifacts — they fluctuate, which is consistent with the **seashore sign** equivalent in B-mode. There is **no evidence of pneumothorax** (barcode/stratosphere sign pattern) in this clip.
