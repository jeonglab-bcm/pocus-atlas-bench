### Key Findings

✅ **Signs of Sliding PRESENT:**
- **Shimmering/granular pleural line** — visible in frames 3–9
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation** — speckle pattern differs between consecutive frames
- **Cyclic brightening** of the pleural interface matching respiratory rhythm (frames 1→10 show oscillation)
- **No barcode/stratosphere sign** — horizontal line pattern is NOT static
- A-line intensity fluctuates between frames (inconsistent with purely absent sliding)

❌ **Signs of Sliding ABSENT NOT present:**
- No perfectly smooth, immobile pleural line sustained across all frames
- No static A-lines with zero inter-frame variation
- No stratosphere pattern

---

## Conclusion

> ### ✅ **Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**
> These sequential frames demonstrate **normal lung sliding** with a cyclically shimmering pleural line, inter-frame sub-pleural speckle variation, and fluctuating A-line intensity consistent with visceral-parietal pleural movement during respiration. There is **no lung point** identified — sliding appears uniform across the visible pleural segment. This pattern argues **against pneumothorax** at this location.
