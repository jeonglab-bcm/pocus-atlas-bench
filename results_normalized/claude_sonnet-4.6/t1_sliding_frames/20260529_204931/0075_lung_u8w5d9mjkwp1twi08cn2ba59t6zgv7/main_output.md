## Conclusion

> ### ⚠️ BOTH — **Lung Point Identified**
> The sequential frames demonstrate a **classic lung point**: a discrete transition where **pleural sliding is present on one side** and **absent on the other**, with the boundary **moving dynamically** across frames with respiration (tracked by the orange arrows).

### Clinical Significance
The lung point is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax** (specificity ~100%). The lung is partially collapsed; where it still contacts the chest wall, sliding is preserved — and that contact point shifts with each breath, producing the visible moving transition.
