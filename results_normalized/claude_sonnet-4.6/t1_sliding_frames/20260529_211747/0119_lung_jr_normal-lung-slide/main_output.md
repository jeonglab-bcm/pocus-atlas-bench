---

## Key Signs Identified

### 🔴 Signs of Sliding **ABSENT** (dominant pattern, frames 1–8):
- Smooth, sharply demarcated, **non-shimmering** pleural line
- **Horizontal, static A-lines** that replicate without change across frames
- No sub-pleural speckle variation between consecutive frames
- No B-lines or comet-tail artifacts

### 🟡 Signs of **BOTH** — Lung Point (frames 9–10):
- A **visible lateral transition** where one segment of the pleural line becomes textured/shimmering while the adjacent segment remains smooth and fixed
- Sub-pleural speckle variation **only on one side** of the transition zone
- Alternating pattern consistent with intermittent lung re-contact

---

## Conclusion

> **⚠️ LUNG POINT IDENTIFIED — HIGH SPECIFICITY FOR PNEUMOTHORAX**

The predominant pattern is **absent pleural sliding** (static pleural line + A-lines = "stratosphere/barcode" equivalent on B-mode), with a **lung point transition** emerging in the final frames. A lung point has **~100% specificity for pneumothorax** and indicates the boundary between collapsed lung (no sliding) and partially aerated lung (sliding present). Urgent clinical correlation and chest imaging are warranted.
