# Lung Ultrasound — Frame-by-Frame Analysis

## Structural Landmarks (All Frames)
- **Linear probe (L14-6Ns)** at ~3.5 cm depth
- A **rounded anechoic structure** on the left = intercostal vessel (landmark)
- A **bright horizontal pleural line** visible ~1 cm deep in all frames

---

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Frames | Pleural Line Character | Sub-pleural Zone | A-lines |
|--------|----------------------|------------------|---------|
| 1–3 | Smooth, sharply defined, static | Minimal speckle variation | Faint, parallel, consistent |
| 4–5 | Slightly more defined, still smooth | Minimal change | A-lines persist |
| 6 | Transition — slight granularity begins on right | Subtle speckle change | Partially disrupted |
| 7–8 | **Shimmer/granularity appears on right side** | Increasing sub-pleural texture change | Replaced by brighter artifacts right-of-center |
| 9–10 | **Active shimmer right-of-center; left remains smooth** | Clear speckle variation right; static left | Right side disrupted; left retained |

---

## Key Findings

### 🔴 Left/Central Zone — Sliding ABSENT
- Smooth, sharply defined pleural line
- Unchanging horizontal A-lines
- No sub-pleural speckle variation
- → **Barcode/stratosphere pattern** equivalent

### 🟢 Right/Lateral Zone (Frames 7–10) — Sliding PRESENT
- Granular, shimmering pleural interface
- Comet-tail precursors emerging
- Sub-pleural speckle visibly changes between frames
- → **Seashore sign** equivalent

---

## ⚡ Conclusion: **LUNG POINT — Both Present and Absent**

> There is a **visible transition** across the horizontal span of the pleural line: the left/central segment remains fixed (absent sliding with preserved A-lines) while the right/lateral segment develops shimmer and comet-tail artifacts (sliding present). This **oscillating appearance** — alternating between seashore and barcode patterns across the respiratory cycle — is the hallmark of a **lung point**.

### Clinical Significance
A lung point is **pathognomonic for pneumothorax** (specificity ~100%), marking the boundary between:
- Collapsed lung (medial/left) → no sliding
- Aerated lung re-apposing the chest wall (lateral/right) → sliding returns

**Recommended next step:** M-mode at both zones to confirm alternating seashore/barcode patterns; clinical correlation for pneumothorax management.
