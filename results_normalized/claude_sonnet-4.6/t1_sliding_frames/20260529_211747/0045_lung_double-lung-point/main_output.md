## Key Findings Summary

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT:
- **Pleural line** is consistently **smooth, sharply defined, and static** across all 10 frames
- **No shimmering or granular texture** to the pleural interface
- **A-lines are perfectly horizontal and unchanged** between consecutive frames
- **No B-lines or comet-tail artifacts** arising from the pleural line
- **Sub-pleural speckle pattern is nearly identical** across all frames — characteristic of a "frozen" pleural line
- Overall pattern consistent with a **barcode/stratosphere sign** on M-mode

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT:
- None clearly identified

### Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):
- No visible transition zone detected across the field of view

---

## Conclusion

> ### 🔴 **Pleural Sliding: ABSENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **static, smooth pleural line** with no detectable sub-pleural movement, no B-lines, and fixed A-lines — all consistent with **absent lung sliding**.

### Differential Diagnosis to Consider:
1. **Pneumothorax** *(most critical — must rule out first)*
2. Pleural adhesions / pleurodesis
3. Severe hyperinflation / bullous disease
4. Main-stem intubation (contralateral lung)
5. High PEEP reducing tidal volume

> ⚠️ **Clinical correlation and M-mode confirmation (barcode sign) are strongly recommended. If pneumothorax is suspected, a lung point sign should be actively sought to confirm.**
