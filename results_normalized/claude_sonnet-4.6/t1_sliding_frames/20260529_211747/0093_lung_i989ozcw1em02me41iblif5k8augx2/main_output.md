## Conclusion

> **🔴 Pleural Sliding: ABSENT**

The dominant pattern across these 10 sequential frames demonstrates:
- A **smooth, static, hyperechoic pleural line**
- **Unchanged A-line reverberation** in the deep field
- **No meaningful frame-to-frame variation** in sub-pleural speckle

This is consistent with the **"stratosphere/barcode" equivalent in B-mode** — highly suspicious for **pneumothorax** at this imaging window.

The rare bright artifacts in isolated frames are insufficient to confirm sliding and may represent **edge/side-lobe artifacts** rather than true B-lines or comet tails. If these represent intermittent sliding, a **lung point** should be sought by scanning adjacent intercostal spaces to identify a transition zone.
