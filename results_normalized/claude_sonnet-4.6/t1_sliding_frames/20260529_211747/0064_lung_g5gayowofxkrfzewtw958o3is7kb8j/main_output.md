- The deep **echogenic structure (atelectatic/compressed lung)** clearly **changes position and morphology** across frames — this is the **sinusoid sign**, indicating the lung is freely moving within the effusion

## Conclusion

> **Pleural Sliding: PRESENT** ✅

**Evidence:**
- ✅ **Sinusoid sign** — atelectatic lung oscillates within the effusion with respiration
- ✅ Sub-pleural echogenic structure changes position across consecutive frames
- ✅ No barcode/stratosphere pattern (no pneumothorax)
- ✅ Absence of rigid, static A-lines

**Diagnosis: Pleural Effusion with preserved lung mobility** — the lung is not adherent to the chest wall, ruling out significant pleurodesis or pneumothorax.
