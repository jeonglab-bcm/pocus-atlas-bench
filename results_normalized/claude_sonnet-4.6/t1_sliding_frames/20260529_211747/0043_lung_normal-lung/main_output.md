## Key Signs Identified

### ✅ Signs of ABSENT Sliding:
- **Pleural line**: Smooth, sharply defined, **no shimmering or granular texture** across all 10 frames
- **A-lines**: Present, horizontal, **perfectly static and replicated** between frames
- **Sub-pleural speckle**: **No inter-frame variation** in the immediate sub-pleural zone
- **No B-lines** or comet-tail artifacts originating from the pleural interface
- Pattern consistent with **Stratosphere/Barcode sign** on M-mode correlate

### ❌ Signs of PRESENT Sliding:
- None identified

### ❌ Signs of Lung Point (BOTH):
- No transition zone visible
- No alternating zones of shimmer vs. static line

---

## Conclusion

> ## 🔴 Pleural Sliding: **ABSENT**

The pleural line is **uniformly smooth and static** across all frames, with **unchanged A-lines** and **no sub-pleural speckle variation**. This is the classic **"barcode/stratosphere sign"** pattern on M-mode.

### Clinical Interpretation:
Absent sliding raises concern for:
1. **Pneumothorax** (most critical diagnosis)
2. Pleurodesis/pleural adhesions
3. Mainstem intubation (contralateral lung)
4. Apnea at time of acquisition

> ⚠️ **Clinical correlation required** — absent sliding alone is not diagnostic of pneumothorax without a lung point or supporting clinical context.
