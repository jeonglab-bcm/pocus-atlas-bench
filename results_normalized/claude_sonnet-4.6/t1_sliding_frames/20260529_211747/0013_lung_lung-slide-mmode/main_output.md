# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### M-Mode (Upper Panel) — All Frames

| Zone | Appearance |
|------|-----------|
| Above pleural line | Horizontal parallel striations — normal chest wall |
| Pleural line (~2–2.5 cm) | Bright, hyperechoic transition band |
| **Below pleural line** | **Granular, heterogeneous, "sandy" texture — progressive across frames** |

- Frames 1–3: Early granularity beginning to develop below the pleural line
- Frames 4–7: Increasingly coarse, speckled sub-pleural pattern
- Frames 8–10: Well-established sandy/grainy texture below pleural line

→ **This constitutes a classic Seashore Sign**

---

### B-Mode (Lower Panel) — All Frames

- Pleural line appears **textured/shimmering** rather than perfectly smooth and static
- **Sub-pleural speckle pattern varies between consecutive frames**
- No pure stratosphere/barcode pattern observed
- No fixed, completely immobile pleural line

---

## Summary of Signs Identified

| Sign | Present? |
|------|----------|
| Seashore sign on M-mode | ✅ Yes |
| Sub-pleural speckle variation between frames | ✅ Yes |
| Shimmering/textured pleural line | ✅ Yes |

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The M-mode consistently demonstrates the **seashore sign**, and B-mode confirms dynamic sub-pleural variation across frames. This effectively **rules out pneumothorax** at this imaging location.
