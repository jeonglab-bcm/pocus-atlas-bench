## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding: ABSENT**

The pleural line is **smooth, static, and non-shimmering** throughout all frames, with **unchanging A-lines** below it — the 2D equivalent of the **stratosphere/barcode sign**. This pattern is consistent with **absent lung sliding**, raising strong concern for **pneumothorax** at the R3 location. M-mode confirmation would show horizontal parallel lines throughout (barcode sign).
