## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Frames 1–3
- The pleural line appears **relatively irregular and textured** in frame 1, with heterogeneous sub-pleural content suggesting some movement/sliding
- By frames 2–3, the pleural line becomes **progressively smoother and more sharply defined**
- Sub-pleural speckle is relatively **static** and A-lines begin to appear consistent

### Frames 4–6
- The pleural line develops **bilateral bright focal reflections** at its lateral edges — a flickering pattern
- The central portion appears **fixed and smooth** with no sub-pleural speckle variation
- This is a **transition zone**: one side appears active while the central segment is static

### Frames 7–10
- The pleural line returns to a **bright, continuous, uninterrupted linear structure**
- A-lines are **static and unchanged** between frames
- **No B-lines or comet-tail artifacts** originate from the pleural line
- Sub-pleural speckle is **frozen** — no frame-to-frame granular variation

---

## Conclusion

> ### ⚠️ BOTH — **Lung Point Identified**
> This sequence demonstrates a **lung point**: a transition where pleural sliding is **present in early frames** (shimmering, irregular pleural line, dynamic sub-pleural content) and **absent in later frames** (smooth, static pleural line; unchanged A-lines; barcode/stratosphere pattern).
>
> 🔴 **This finding is pathognomonic for pneumothorax.**
