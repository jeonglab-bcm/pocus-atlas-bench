---

## Key Findings

### Evidence of Sliding PRESENT (early & late frames):
- ✅ Shimmering, granular pleural line texture
- ✅ Dynamic sub-pleural speckle variation between frames
- ✅ Bright, textured interface consistent with visceral-parietal pleural contact

### Evidence of Sliding ABSENT (mid-sequence frames):
- ✅ Smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line
- ✅ Regular, parallel, equidistant A-lines (stratosphere/barcode pattern)
- ✅ No inter-frame speckle variation beneath pleural line

### Evidence of LUNG POINT:
- ✅ **Alternating pattern** of sliding → absent → sliding across respiratory cycle
- ✅ The transition corresponds to the lung intermittently reaching the probe position with inspiration, then retracting with expiration
- ✅ This oscillating pattern in a sequential series is the **dynamic equivalent of a lung point**

---

## Conclusion

> ## ⚠️ LUNG POINT IDENTIFIED
> 
> These frames demonstrate the **lung point sign** — the pathognomonic finding for **pneumothorax**. The lung intermittently contacts the chest wall at this probe location (during inspiration), producing sliding in some frames, while retracting away (during expiration) to produce the stratosphere/barcode pattern in others.
>
> **This is diagnostic of pneumothorax** with 100% specificity. The probe is positioned precisely at the boundary between the air-filled pleural space and the re-expanded lung.
