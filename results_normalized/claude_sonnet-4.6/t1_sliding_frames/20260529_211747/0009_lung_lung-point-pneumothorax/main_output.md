## Key Findings Across All Frames

### ✅ Signs of Sliding ABSENT:
- **Pleural line is smooth and sharply defined** — no shimmering or granular texture
- **Dominant A-line pattern** — parallel horizontal reverberation artifacts at equidistant depths, unchanged across frames
- **No B-lines / comet-tail artifacts** originating from the pleural interface
- **Sub-pleural speckle virtually static** — no meaningful frame-to-frame variation in the tissue beneath the pleural line
- Pattern is consistent with a **"stratosphere/barcode sign"** equivalent in static frames

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is ABSENT**

The combination of:
1. A smooth, static pleural line
2. Predominant A-lines
3. No sub-pleural speckle variation

...constitutes the **A'-profile**, which carries **high specificity for pneumothorax** in the appropriate clinical context (BLUE protocol). Confirmation with M-mode would be expected to show the **stratosphere/barcode sign**.
