## Key Findings

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT
- **Shimmering pleural line**: Frames 3–6 show a bright, focal artifact originating at the pleural interface that **changes position and intensity** between frames — consistent with a sliding visceral/parietal pleural interface
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation**: The granular pattern deep to the pleural line is **not identical between consecutive frames**, most notably in frames 3–6 and 8–10
- **Comet-tail/B-line precursor artifacts**: Bright foci arise from the pleural line in frames 3–5, characteristic of visceral pleural movement
- **Respiratory variation**: Frame 7 (smoother, more static) likely represents an **end-expiratory pause**, not true absence of sliding

### ❌ Signs Suggesting Absent Sliding — NOT Observed
- No perfectly static, razor-sharp pleural line across all frames
- No unchanging, identical A-lines across all frames
- No stratosphere/barcode pattern on M-mode (not shown, but image texture does not support it)

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **dynamic, shimmering pleural interface** with **inter-frame sub-pleural speckle variation** and **moving comet-tail artifacts**, all consistent with normal lung sliding. The transient smoothing in frame 7 represents a physiologic respiratory pause, not a lung point.

**Clinical implication**: These findings effectively **rule out pneumothorax** at this intercostal space. The chest pain in this patient is unlikely to be due to pneumothorax (at least at the imaged location).
