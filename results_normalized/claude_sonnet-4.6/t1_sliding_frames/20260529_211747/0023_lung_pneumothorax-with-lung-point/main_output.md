### Signs Assessed

**Signs of Sliding PRESENT ✅**
- The pleural line demonstrates a **shimmering, granular texture** across multiple frames rather than a uniformly smooth, static line
- **Sub-pleural speckle pattern varies** between consecutive frames — particularly evident comparing frames 1→2, 6→7, and 9→10
- **No fixed, perfectly static pleural line** is maintained throughout the clip
- No persistent barcode/stratosphere pattern visible
- Possible faint **comet-tail artifacts** in frames 7–9

**Signs of Sliding ABSENT ❌**
- No purely horizontal, perfectly repeating static A-lines maintained across all frames
- No barcode sign pattern identified
- Pleural line is not uniformly smooth throughout

**Signs of Lung Point (BOTH) ❌**
- No visible transition zone where one segment slides and another does not
- No focal region of alternating shimmer/stasis

---

## ✅ Conclusion: **Pleural Sliding PRESENT**

This left posterior lung ultrasound demonstrates **normal pleural sliding**, evidenced by:
1. A **shimmering, textured pleural line** (not smooth and static)
2. **Frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle**, reflecting visceral-parietal pleural movement during respiration
3. Absence of the barcode/stratosphere sign

> **Clinical Implication:** Pneumothorax is effectively **ruled out** at this imaging location on the left lung.
