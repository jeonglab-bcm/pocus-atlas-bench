## Conclusion

### ⚠️ **Pleural Sliding: ABSENT** (with possible Lung Point on the left)

The predominant pattern across these frames is **absent pleural sliding**, characterized by:
- A **static, smooth, sharply defined pleural line**
- **Unchanged A-lines** below the pleural interface
- **No sub-pleural speckle variation** between consecutive frames (consistent with the **stratosphere/barcode sign** pattern on M-mode)

The subtle left-sided variation in frames 6–8 raises suspicion for a **lung point** — the pathognomonic sign of pneumothorax where sliding abruptly resumes at the lung's edge.

> **Clinical Correlation:** This pattern is highly concerning for **pneumothorax**. The lung point, if confirmed, would be **100% specific** for pneumothorax. M-mode confirmation would be expected to show alternating **seashore** (sliding present) and **barcode** (sliding absent) patterns at the transition zone.
