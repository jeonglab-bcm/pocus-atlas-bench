## Conclusion

### ✅ **Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate characteristic **shimmering and progressive granularity of the pleural interface**, with clear **inter-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle**. This dynamic behavior is consistent with **normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement** (lung sliding), effectively **ruling out pneumothorax** at this location.

> On M-mode, one would expect a **Seashore Sign** (granular sandy pattern below the pleural line) at this site.
