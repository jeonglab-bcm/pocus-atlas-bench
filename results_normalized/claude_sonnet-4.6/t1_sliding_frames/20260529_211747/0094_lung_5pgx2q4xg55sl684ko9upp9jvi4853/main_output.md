## Sonographic Signs Identified

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT

> **"Jellyfish Sign" (Lung Flapping)**
- A **right-sided pleural effusion** (anechoic space) is clearly visible
- Within it, **consolidated/atelectatic lung tissue** is seen **changing position and morphology** across frames — moving passively with respiration
- Sub-pleural speckle and structural pattern **differ significantly between consecutive frames**
- No barcode/stratosphere sign; dynamic motion confirms lung movement

## Conclusion

### 🟢 Pleural Sliding: **PRESENT**

**Primary evidence:** The **Jellyfish Sign** — atelectatic/consolidated lung tissue is seen **floating and moving within a pleural effusion** across sequential frames, confirming active lung motion with respiration.

**Associated findings:**
- **Right pleural effusion** (anechoic space)
- **Compressive/obstructive atelectasis** (echogenic consolidated tissue within effusion)
- No pneumothorax (absence of barcode sign, dynamic motion confirmed)

> ⚠️ **Clinical correlation:** This pattern (effusion + jellyfish sign at the right base) is consistent with a moderate-to-large pleural effusion causing compressive atelectasis. Thoracentesis guidance may be indicated.
