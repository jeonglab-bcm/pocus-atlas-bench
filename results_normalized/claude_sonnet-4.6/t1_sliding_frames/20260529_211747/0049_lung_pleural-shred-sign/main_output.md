## ⚠️ Conclusion: **LUNG POINT SIGN**

This clip demonstrates the **lung point** — the alternating transition between:
- **Absent sliding** (pneumothorax zone) in early frames
- **Present sliding** (normal aerated lung) in later frames

> 🔴 **The lung point sign is pathognomonic for pneumothorax** (specificity ~100%). It represents the anatomical border where the collapsed lung re-meets the chest wall during the respiratory cycle.
