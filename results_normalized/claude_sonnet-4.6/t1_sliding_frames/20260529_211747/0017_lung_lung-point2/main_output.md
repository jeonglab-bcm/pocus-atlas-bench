## Conclusion: **BOTH — Lung Point Present** 🫁

> The M-mode sweep demonstrates a **classic Lung Point** — a transition from a **seashore sign** (frames 1–7, sliding present) to a **barcode/stratosphere sign** (frame 10, sliding absent), with an intermediate transition zone in between.

**Clinical significance:**
- The **lung point is pathognomonic for pneumothorax**
- It represents the boundary where the collapsed lung re-contacts the chest wall during respiration
- This finding has **~100% specificity** for pneumothorax
- The location of the lung point can estimate pneumothorax size
