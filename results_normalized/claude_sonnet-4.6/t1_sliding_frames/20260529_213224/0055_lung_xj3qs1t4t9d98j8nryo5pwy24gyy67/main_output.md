## Conclusion

> **Lung sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate:
1. **Shimmering quality** of the pleural line (particularly frames 7–10)
2. **Sub-pleural speckle variation** between frames — the tissue immediately below the pleural line changes between consecutive images
3. **Absence of a sustained barcode pattern** — the pleural line never appears perfectly static across the entire sequence
4. Subtle but consistent **respiratory excursion** visible in the overlying soft tissue layers

In the clinical context of **chest pain evaluation**, the presence of lung sliding at this zone effectively **rules out pneumothorax** at this site, as pneumothorax would produce the absent-sliding (barcode) pattern throughout all frames.
