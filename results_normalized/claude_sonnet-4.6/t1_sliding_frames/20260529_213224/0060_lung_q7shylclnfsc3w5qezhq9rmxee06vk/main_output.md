## Key Findings

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT
1. **Shimmering pleural line** — The pleural interface fluctuates in brightness and texture across frames (most evident in frames 1, 6–10)
2. **Comet-tail artifacts / B-lines** — Hyperechoic vertical streaks arise from the pleural line, particularly on the right margin of the beam, and vary in prominence between frames
3. **Sub-pleural speckle variation** — The granular pattern immediately below the pleural line clearly differs between consecutive frames (e.g., frames 3→4 vs. 8→9), indicating visceral pleural movement
4. **Dynamic pleural line brightness** — Cyclical brightening and textural change consistent with respiratory lung excursion

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT
- No consistently smooth, static, "laser-line" pleural appearance across all frames
- No barcode/stratosphere pattern identified
- A-lines are present in some frames but are **not perfectly static** — they vary in number and intensity

---

## Conclusion

> ### 🟢 **Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **shimmering, dynamically changing pleural line** with **varying sub-pleural speckle** and **comet-tail/B-line artifacts** that change in prominence across the respiratory cycle. These findings are consistent with **intact lung sliding**, indicating normal apposition and movement of the visceral against the parietal pleura.

**No lung point** is identified — the findings appear uniform across the field without a clear transition zone between sliding and non-sliding regions.

> **Clinical implication:** Pneumothorax at this location is effectively **ruled out**.
