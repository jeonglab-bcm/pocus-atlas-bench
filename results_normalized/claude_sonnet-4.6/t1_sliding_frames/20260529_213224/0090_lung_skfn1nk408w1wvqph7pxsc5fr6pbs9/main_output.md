## Conclusion

### **Pleural sliding: PRESENT**

The sequential variability in pleural line texture (granular/shimmering in mid-cycle frames) and the frame-to-frame changes in sub-pleural speckle pattern are consistent with **visceral-parietal pleural movement**, confirming lung sliding. The A-lines seen are consistent with **normal aerated lung** (A-lines + sliding = normal or no pneumothorax).

> ⚠️ **No lung point identified** in this clip. No definitive transition between sliding and non-sliding zones is observed.
>
> **Clinical implication:** Pneumothorax is effectively **ruled out** at this probe location, as the presence of lung sliding alone excludes pneumothorax with high sensitivity (~95%).
