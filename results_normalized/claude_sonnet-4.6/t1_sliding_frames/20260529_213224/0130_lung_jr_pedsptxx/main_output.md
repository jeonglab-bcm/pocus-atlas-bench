## Conclusion

### ✅ Pleural Sliding: **PRESENT**

**Supporting evidence:**
1. **Shimmering/granular pleural line** — not the smooth, fixed line of absent sliding
2. **Sub-pleural speckle variation** between consecutive frames — classic indicator of visceral–parietal pleural movement
3. **Dynamic comet-tail artifacts** arising from the pleural interface
4. **No barcode/stratosphere pattern** — A-lines shift subtly across frames
5. **No lung point** — the entire visible pleural segment appears to slide uniformly

> **Interpretation:** This is consistent with **normal lung sliding**, effectively ruling out pneumothorax at this imaging window.
