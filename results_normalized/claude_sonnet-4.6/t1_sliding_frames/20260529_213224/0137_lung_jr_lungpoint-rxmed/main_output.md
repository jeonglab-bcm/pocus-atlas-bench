## Key Observations

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT:
- **Shimmering/granular pleural line** across multiple frames (especially 1, 5–8)
- **Frame-to-frame variation** in sub-pleural speckle pattern — the deep field is **not identical** between frames
- **Changing pleural line texture** cycling through the respiratory phases (bright/complex → smooth → bright again)
- No perfectly static, laser-sharp, unchanging pleural line reproducible across all frames

### ❌ Signs of Absent Sliding NOT dominant:
- The pleural line is **never uniformly smooth and static across all frames**
- No barcode/stratosphere sign identifiable
- A-lines, where present, do **not remain perfectly fixed**

---

## Conclusion

> ## 🟢 **Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **respiratory-phase–dependent shimmering of the pleural line** with **inter-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle** — classic features of **normal lung sliding**. The cycling between more granular (inspiratory) and smoother (expiratory) pleural line appearances reflects visceral pleura moving against parietal pleura, confirming **no pneumothorax** at this location.
