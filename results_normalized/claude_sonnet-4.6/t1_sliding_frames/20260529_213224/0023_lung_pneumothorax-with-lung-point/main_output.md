### Key Sign Assessment

#### ✅ Signs Supporting Sliding PRESENT:
- **Pleural line texture**: Across all 10 frames, the pleural line is **bright, granular, and textured** — not the smooth, laser-sharp, static line typical of absent sliding
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation**: The echogenic pattern **below the pleural line changes between consecutive frames**, indicating visceral-parietal pleural movement
- **No dominant static A-lines**: A-lines are not perfectly locked and unchanging across frames
- **No barcode/stratosphere pattern**: The deep tissue does not show purely horizontal, parallel, static lines

#### ❌ Signs of Absent Sliding NOT Present:
- No smooth, static, sharply defined pleural line
- No fixed, perfectly repeating A-line stack
- No stratosphere sign

#### ❌ Signs of Lung Point NOT Present:
- No visible transition zone where one side slides and the other is fixed
- The entire visible pleural line behaves uniformly across all frames

---

## 🔷 Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT bilaterally along the entire visible left pleural line.**

The sequential frames demonstrate a consistently **bright, granular, shimmering pleural interface** with **frame-to-frame sub-pleural speckle variation**, both hallmarks of normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement. There is **no evidence of pneumothorax** (absent sliding) and **no lung point** to suggest a partial pneumothorax.
