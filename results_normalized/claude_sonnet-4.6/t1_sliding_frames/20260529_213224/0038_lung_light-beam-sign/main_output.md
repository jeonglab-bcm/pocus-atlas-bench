# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Pleural Line Character
| Frames | Observation |
|--------|-------------|
| 1–2 | Pleural line appears **smooth, sharply delineated, linear** — minimal texture or shimmer |
| 3–4 | Line remains **static in position**; faint, fixed bright artifact noted just below the line |
| 5–6 | Chest wall soft tissue echogenicity increases (respiratory motion), yet the **pleural line itself does not shimmer or granulate** |
| 7–8 | More chest wall movement visible; pleural interface remains **fixed and non-textured** |
| 9–10 | Pleural line persists as a **rigid, non-sliding interface**; no comet-tail/B-line artifacts arising from it |

---

### Sub-Pleural Field
- **Predominantly anechoic (black)** below the pleural line in all frames
- **Faint horizontal reverberation lines** (A-lines) visible in several frames — static and equidistant
- **No variation in sub-pleural speckle** between consecutive frames (a hallmark of absent sliding)

---

### Key Negative Findings
- ❌ No shimmering or granular pleural line texture
- ❌ No B-lines or comet-tail artifacts
- ❌ No seashore sign pattern
- ❌ No sub-pleural speckle change between frames

---

## Conclusion

> ### ⚠️ **Pleural Sliding: ABSENT**
>
> The ultrasound demonstrates the classic **"stratosphere/barcode" equivalent pattern** in B-mode:
> - A **static, smooth pleural line**
> - **Fixed A-lines** without respiratory variation
> - **No sub-pleural speckle movement**
>
> This is consistent with **absent lung sliding**, raising strong concern for **pneumothorax** at R3. Clinical correlation (e.g., lung point identification, M-mode confirmation of barcode sign) is recommended.
