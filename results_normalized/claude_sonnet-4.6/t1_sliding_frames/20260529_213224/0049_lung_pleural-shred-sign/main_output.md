## Key Findings Across All Frames

- ❌ **No shimmering or granular texture** at the pleural line
- ❌ **No B-lines or comet-tail artifacts** arising from the pleural interface
- ❌ **No sub-pleural speckle variation** between consecutive frames
- ✅ **Static, smooth, sharply defined pleural line** throughout
- ✅ **A-lines are perfectly static and repeated** — consistent with a **"barcode/stratosphere sign"** pattern (M-mode correlate)
- ❌ No visible lung point transition

---

## Conclusion

> ## ⚠️ Pleural Sliding: **ABSENT**
> This is consistent with **pneumothorax**.
> The static pleural line, absent speckle variation, and unchanging A-lines all point to loss of visceral-parietal pleural apposition and absence of lung sliding.
