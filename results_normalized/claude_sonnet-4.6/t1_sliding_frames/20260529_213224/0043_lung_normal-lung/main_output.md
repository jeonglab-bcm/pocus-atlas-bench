---

## Key Findings

### ✅ Signs of Sliding **PRESENT**
- **Pleural line has granular/shimmering texture** — not the sharp, smooth, static line seen in pneumothorax
- **Progressive and cyclic variation in sub-pleural speckle** between consecutive frames — consistent with respiratory motion transmitted through an apposed parietal-visceral pleura
- **No perfectly static A-line repetition** — the horizontal bands shift in brightness and depth across the sequence
- No lung point transition zone identified

### ❌ Signs of Sliding **Absent** — NOT observed
- No stratosphere/barcode pattern
- No frozen, perfectly smooth pleural line
- No frame-to-frame identity in the near-field speckle

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a shimmering, textured pleural interface with progressive sub-pleural speckle variation — the classic **"seashore" pattern equivalent in B-mode**. This confirms visceral-parietal pleural apposition and effectively **rules out pneumothorax** at this location.
