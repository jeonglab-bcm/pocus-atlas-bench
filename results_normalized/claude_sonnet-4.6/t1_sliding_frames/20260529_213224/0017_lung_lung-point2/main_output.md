## M-mode Analysis (Critical Finding)

Tracking the yellow triangle marker progressing **left (−4) → right (0)** across the timeline:

### Left portion of M-mode (frames 1–4):
- Predominantly **parallel horizontal lines** below the pleural interface
- Classic **stratosphere/barcode sign**
- → **Pleural sliding ABSENT**

### Right portion of M-mode (frames 7–10):
- Transition to a **granular/sandy texture** below the pleural line
- Classic **seashore sign**
- → **Pleural sliding PRESENT**

### The Transition Zone (frames 5–6):

- This is the **LUNG POINT**

## Conclusion

> ## ⚠️ LUNG POINT — Both Signs Present

This study demonstrates the **pathognomonic lung point** — the precise anatomical location where:
- **Collapsed lung** (no visceral–parietal pleural contact) → barcode sign
- **Aerated lung** (normal contact restored during inspiration) → seashore sign

### Clinical Significance:
| Finding | Interpretation |
|---------|---------------|
| Lung point present | **Specificity ~100% for pneumothorax** |
| Partial sliding absence | Likely **partial/incomplete pneumothorax** |
| Alternating M-mode patterns | Lung swinging in and out of the probe field with respiration |

**This is diagnostic of pneumothorax at this intercostal site.**
