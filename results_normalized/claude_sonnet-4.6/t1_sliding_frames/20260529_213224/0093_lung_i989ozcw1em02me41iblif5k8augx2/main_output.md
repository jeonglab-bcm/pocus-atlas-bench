## Conclusion

> ## ⚠️ PLEURAL SLIDING: **ABSENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **static, smooth, sharply demarcated pleural line** with **persistent A-lines** and **no inter-frame sub-pleural speckle variation** — the classic **"stratosphere/barcode sign"** equivalent on 2D imaging.

### Clinical Correlation:
This pattern is consistent with:
1. **Pneumothorax** (most urgent concern)
2. Contralateral mainstem intubation
3. Apnea/breath-hold
4. Pleurodesis or severe pleural adhesions
5. Complete consolidation with absent aeration

**Recommend M-mode confirmation** (barcode sign) and clinical correlation. If pneumothorax is suspected, assess for a **lung point** (transition to sliding) to confirm and localize.
