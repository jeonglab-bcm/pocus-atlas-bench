## Conclusion

> ## ✅ Pleural Sliding: **PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate clear evidence of pleural sliding:
1. The **sub-pleural speckle pattern changes substantially** between early (frames 1–4) and late (frames 7–10) frames
2. The **pleural line is textured and shimmering**, not smooth and static
3. The **progressive brightening of the near-field** in frames 7–10 reflects the lung moving into the field of view with inspiration — the classic **"lung pulse/glide" phenomenon**
4. Suggestion of **B-line/comet-tail artifacts** arising from the pleural surface in later frames

**Clinical interpretation:** Normal pleural apposition with respiratory excursion — no pneumothorax in this region. The findings are consistent with **normal aerated lung** with intact visceral-parietal pleural contact.
