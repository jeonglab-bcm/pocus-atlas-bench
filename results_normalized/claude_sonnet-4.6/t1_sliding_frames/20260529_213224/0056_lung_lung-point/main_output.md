# Lung Ultrasound Sequential Frame Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Frames | Key Findings |
|--------|-------------|
| **1–2** | Pleural line is bright but relatively smooth; sub-pleural region is largely anechoic; minimal speckle variation |
| **3–4** | Bright echogenic structure emerges on the **left margin** (likely rib shadow or cortex); small hyperechoic foci begin appearing near the pleural line |
| **5–6** | **Multiple punctate bright foci** cluster near/at the pleural line — consistent with **comet-tail / early B-line artifacts**; pleural line takes on a **granular, shimmering texture** |
| **7–8** | Bright foci partially redistribute; a bright structure now appears on the **right margin**; pleural line continues to show textural variation; sub-pleural speckle differs from frames 1–2 |
| **9–10** | Bright foci persist near the pleural line with continued shimmer; sub-pleural speckle pattern has clearly **changed** relative to early frames |

---

## Key Signs Identified

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT
- **Shimmering/granular pleural line** — evident from frames 3 onward; not static or razor-sharp
- **Comet-tail artifacts / nascent B-lines** — visible as bright punctate and short vertical foci at the pleural interface (frames 5–7 most prominent)
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation** — the echogenicity pattern below the pleural line changes between early and late frames
- **Progressive lateral shift of echogenic structures** — consistent with respiratory-driven pleural motion

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT — Not Observed
- No perfectly smooth, fixed, motionless pleural line
- No "barcode/stratosphere" pattern (no horizontal static lines below pleural interface)
- A-lines, while present in the near field, are not perfectly static and identical across all frames

### 🔄 Lung Point — Not Identified
- No clear transition zone where one segment slides and an adjacent segment is fixed
- No alternating seashore/barcode pattern

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a shimmering, granular pleural line with comet-tail artifacts and inter-frame sub-pleural speckle variation — all hallmarks of normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement. **Pneumothorax is not suggested** at this imaging site.
