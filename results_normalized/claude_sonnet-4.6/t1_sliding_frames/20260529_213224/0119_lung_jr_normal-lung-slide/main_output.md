# 0119_lung_jr_normal-lung-slide

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Frames 1–3
- Pleural line: **Bright, smooth, sharply defined** — minimal textural variation
- Below pleural line: Horizontal **A-lines** that are well-organized and largely static
- Sub-pleural speckle: Virtually **unchanged** between consecutive frames
- No B-lines visible

### Frames 4–6
- Pleural line remains **fixed in position**, smooth contour
- A-lines persist with **no discernible change** in spacing or intensity
- Sub-pleural granularity: **Static** — no shimmer or speckle shift

### Frames 7–8
- Pleural line begins showing **subtle positional variation**
- Sub-pleural speckle pattern starts to show **mild heterogeneity** between frames
- A-lines remain predominantly static but slight disruption appears

### Frames 9–10
- **Notable change**: Pleural line shifts **inferiorly** in frame 10
- Sub-pleural region shows a distinctly **different echo texture** compared to earlier frames
- This transition suggests **renewed sliding** appearing at this location

---

## Summary of Findings

| Feature | Observation |
|---|---|
| Pleural line | Smooth, static (frames 1–8); shifting (frames 9–10) |
| A-lines | Present and static throughout most frames |
| Sub-pleural speckle | Largely unchanged early; variation in late frames |
| B-lines | Absent |

---

## Conclusion: **BOTH — Lung Point Present**

> The early frames demonstrate **absent pleural sliding** (smooth static pleural line, unchanging A-lines, barcode/stratosphere pattern equivalent in 2D). In the final frames, the pleural line shifts inferiorly and sub-pleural texture changes, indicating **sliding resumes** — consistent with a **lung point**. This is pathognomonic for **pneumothorax**, representing the boundary between collapsed (non-sliding) and aerated (sliding) lung.
