## Conclusion

> ## ⚠️ BOTH — **Lung Point Identified**

The alternating pattern — lung tissue expanding into the sector (sliding present) then retracting away (sliding absent) — at a **fixed transition zone** is the **pathognomonic lung point sign**.

This finding is **100% specific for pneumothorax**, representing the boundary between the collapsed lung (absent sliding laterally) and the aerated lung (sliding present medially).
