## ✅ Conclusion: **Pleural Sliding — PRESENT**

The dominant evidence is:
1. **Multiple B-lines** (frames 7–10) — B-lines are pathognomonic of pleural sliding; they cannot exist without visceral-parietal pleural contact and movement
2. **Shimmering, granular, irregular pleural line** (frames 1–6) rather than a smooth, static interface
3. **Variable sub-pleural speckle** across consecutive frames

The irregular pleural line and sub-pleural nodules are characteristic of **Miliary TB** — disseminated granulomas producing multiple tiny sub-pleural lesions and B-lines from interstitial involvement — but **pneumothorax is effectively excluded** by the presence of B-lines.
