---

## Key Observations

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT (left/medial segment):
- Shimmering, granular pleural line with brightness variation
- Sub-pleural speckle pattern **differs between consecutive frames**
- Comet-tail/B-line precursor artifacts visible

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT (right/lateral segment):
- Smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line segment
- A-lines appear static and unchanged in that region
- No sub-pleural speckle variation beneath this segment

### ⚠️ Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):
- A **visible transition zone** where the active (sliding) segment meets the static (non-sliding) segment
- This transition **oscillates with respiration** across sequential frames
- The dark anechoic region appearing laterally in frames 3–4 and 8–9 represents the **lung edge intermittently entering/exiting** the field

---

## Conclusion

> **LUNG POINT IDENTIFIED — Pathognomonic for PNEUMOTHORAX**

The sequential frames demonstrate a classic **lung point**: pleural sliding is **present medially** and **absent laterally**, with a transition point that shifts with respiratory phase. This is the **only ultrasound finding specific (nearly 100%) for pneumothorax** and should prompt immediate clinical action.

**Recommended next step:** M-mode through the transition zone to confirm alternating **seashore ↔ barcode** pattern.
