## Key Signs Identified

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT:
- **Shimmering/textured pleural line**: The pleural interface is not uniformly smooth — it shows a granular, bright, flickering quality, particularly in frames 4–8
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation**: The echogenic texture *below* the pleural line clearly **differs between consecutive frames**, indicating lung movement against the chest wall
- **No static lock**: The pleural line and sub-pleural zone do not remain frozen across frames

### ❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT: Not identified
- No barcode/stratosphere pattern behavior
- A-lines are present but not perfectly static — they vary subtly with breathing

---

## Conclusion

> **✅ Pleural Sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **shimmering, granular pleural line** with **inter-frame sub-pleural speckle variation**, consistent with **normal visceral-parietal pleural apposition and movement**. This pattern is consistent with the **seashore sign** on M-mode (sandy pattern below the pleural line).

**No lung point or absent sliding is identified** — **pneumothorax at this location is effectively excluded.**
