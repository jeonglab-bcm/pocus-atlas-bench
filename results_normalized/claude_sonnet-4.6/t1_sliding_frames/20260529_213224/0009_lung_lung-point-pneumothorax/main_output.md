# Lung Ultrasound – Sequential Frame Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Frames | Pleural Line Appearance | Sub-pleural Speckle | A-lines |
|--------|------------------------|---------------------|---------|
| 1–2 | Moderately bright, slight texture | Minimal variation | Prominent, horizontal |
| 3–4 | Brighter, begins to shimmer | Increasing variation | Slightly shifting |
| 5–6 | Clearly shimmering, granular texture | Noticeable frame-to-frame change | Less perfectly static |
| 7–8 | Bright, textured interface | Continued sub-pleural speckle shift | Variable intensity |
| 9–10 | Granular, active-appearing | Clear change in speckle distribution | Not identical across frames |

---

## Key Findings

### ✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT:
- **Shimmering pleural line**: The pleural interface progressively gains a brighter, granular, textured appearance from frame 3 onward
- **Sub-pleural speckle variation**: The echo texture *below* the pleural line clearly differs between consecutive frames — the hallmark of sliding
- **A-line instability**: While A-lines are present, they are **not perfectly static** — their intensity and configuration shift across frames
- **No barcode/stratosphere pattern** detected

### ❌ No Signs of Absent Sliding:
- No smooth, perfectly static, sharply defined pleural line sustained throughout
- No fixed, unchanging A-line pattern

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

This sequence demonstrates **normal lung sliding** — consistent with the "seashore" pattern on M-mode. The progressive shimmer of the pleural line and frame-to-frame sub-pleural speckle variation confirm visceral-parietal pleural movement, effectively **ruling out pneumothorax** at this intercostal site.
