# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

### Pleural Line Characteristics

| Frames | Pleural Line Appearance | Sub-pleural Pattern |
|--------|------------------------|---------------------|
| 1–2 | Relatively smooth, bright hyperechoic line | Minimal speckle variation |
| 3–4 | Beginning texture/granularity emerges | Subtle inter-frame speckle changes |
| 5–6 | Increasing shimmer; line texture becomes heterogeneous | Moderate sub-pleural variation |
| 7–10 | Clearly granular/textured pleural line; arch contour changes | Distinct frame-to-frame speckle differences; evolving echogenicity below line |

---

## Key Observations

**✅ Signs of Sliding PRESENT:**
- The pleural line **progressively becomes more granular and shimmering** across frames (particularly frames 6–10)
- The **curvature and prominence of the pleural line arc changes** between frames, indicating movement
- **Sub-pleural speckle pattern varies** between consecutive frames — not static
- **A-lines**, while present, show subtle shifting rather than being perfectly fixed

**❌ Signs of Sliding ABSENT: NOT observed**
- No barcode/stratosphere sign
- No completely static, razor-sharp pleural line throughout the entire sequence
- No fixed, unchanging A-line pattern

---

## Conclusion

> **Pleural sliding is PRESENT**

The sequential frames demonstrate a **shimmering, granular pleural line** with **frame-to-frame variation in sub-pleural speckle**, consistent with normal visceral-parietal pleural movement during respiration. **No lung point is identified** — sliding appears uniform across the visible pleural segment.
