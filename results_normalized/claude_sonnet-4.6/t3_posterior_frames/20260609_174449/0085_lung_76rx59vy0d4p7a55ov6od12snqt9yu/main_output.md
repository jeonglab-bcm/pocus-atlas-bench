## Signs Identified

### ✅ Quad Sign (Present)
Across all frames, a **large anechoic (black) space** is clearly visible in the dependent posterior zone, bounded:
- **Superiorly** by the pleural line
- **Inferiorly** by the lung line
- **Laterally** by rib/soft tissue margins
This forms a classic quadrilateral fluid pocket.

### ✅ Sinusoid Sign (Suggested)
Sequential frames show **subtle cyclical displacement** of the bright lung line/interface toward the pleural line — consistent with respiratory movement of fluid-lung interface.

### ✅ Jellyfish Sign (Partial)
In frames 5–10, a **faint, echogenic structure** is visible floating within the anechoic collection, suggesting atelectatic lung tissue undulating within the effusion.

### ⚠️ Hepatization / Consolidation
There is mildly **increased echogenicity** in the parenchyma adjacent to the effusion in later frames, which may represent **compressive atelectasis**, but no definitive shred sign or air bronchograms are confidently identified.
