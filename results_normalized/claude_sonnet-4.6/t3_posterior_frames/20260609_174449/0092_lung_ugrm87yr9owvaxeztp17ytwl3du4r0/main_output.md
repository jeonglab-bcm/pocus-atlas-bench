**Interpretation:**
There is a **moderate-to-large right pleural effusion** (quad sign, jellyfish sign, sinusoid sign) with **underlying compressive atelectasis / consolidation** of the right lower lobe (hepatization + static air bronchograms). The floating atelectatic lung within the effusion (jellyfish sign) is the dominant dynamic feature. This pattern is highly characteristic of **PLAPS-positive** findings in the context of decompensated heart failure, pneumonia with parapneumonic effusion, or malignant effusion with lobar collapse.
