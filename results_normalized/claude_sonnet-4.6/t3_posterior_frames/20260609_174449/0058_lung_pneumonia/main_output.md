# 0058_lung_pneumonia

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Systematic Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### **Chest Wall / Pleural Line**
- Clearly identifiable echogenic pleural line in the near field across all frames.
- Rib shadows visible laterally.

#### **Anechoic Space (Frames 2–225)**
- A **prominent anechoic (black) region** occupies the dependent (lower) portion of the image throughout the clip.
- This space is bounded:
  - **Superiorly** by the pleural/visceral interface
  - **Laterally** by rib acoustic shadows
- This forms a clear **Quad Sign** → consistent with **pleural effusion**

#### **Echogenic Tissue Mass (Mid-field)**
- Above the anechoic fluid, there is a **heterogeneous, echogenic structure** with a **liver-like (hepatized) texture** — loss of normal aeration artifacts.
- This tissue shows **irregular margins** at its deep border → **Shred Sign**
- **Punctate/linear hyperechoic foci** are visible within this structure across multiple frames (especially 48–175) → consistent with **Air Bronchograms**

#### **Dynamic Assessment Across Frames**
- The echogenic consolidated tissue appears to **shift/undulate** relative to the effusion between early and late frames → suggestive of **Jellyfish Sign** (atelectatic lung floating within effusion)

### Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

There is clear evidence of a **posterior pleural effusion** (quad sign, jellyfish sign) **with associated lung consolidation/atelectasis** (hepatization, air bronchograms, shred sign) in the dependent posterolateral zone — a classic **PLAPS pattern**, most commonly seen in pneumonia with parapneumonic effusion, heart failure, or post-obstructive atelectasis.
