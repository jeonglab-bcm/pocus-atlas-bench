# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Posterior Zone)

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Structural Landmarks
- **Pleural line**: Identifiable as a hyperechoic horizontal line near the top of each frame
- **Rib shadows**: Bilateral shadowing artifacts defining the lateral boundaries

---

### Effusion Signs

| Sign | Finding |
|------|---------|
| **Quad Sign** | ✅ Present — Large **anechoic (black) space** bounded superiorly by pleural line, with rib shadows laterally, forming a clear quadrilateral fluid pocket |
| **Jellyfish Sign** | ✅ Present — An **echogenic, undulating structure** (consolidated/atelectatic lung) is seen **floating and shifting position** within the anechoic effusion across sequential frames (most prominent in frames 3–5) |
| **Sinusoid Sign** | Suggestive — respiratory-related movement of the deep lung border toward pleural line is implied by the dynamic positional changes |

---

### Consolidation Signs

| Sign | Finding |
|------|---------|
| **Tissue-like hepatization** | ✅ Present — The floating structure within the effusion demonstrates **liver-like echogenicity**, consistent with atelectatic/consolidated lung |
| **Air bronchograms** | Possible — Small punctate hyperechoic foci noted within the echogenic tissue in several frames |
| **Shred sign** | ✅ Suggested — Irregular, shredded deep border between consolidated tissue and surrounding fluid |

---

## Synthesis

The sequential frames demonstrate:
1. A **large anechoic pleural effusion** (Quad sign)
2. A **floating echogenic consolidated/atelectatic lung lobe** shifting within the effusion across frames (Jellyfish sign)
3. **Hepatized lung texture** within the floating structure

---

## Conclusion

```
plaps   = true
type    = "both"
```

> **Posterior pleural effusion with compressive/atelectatic lung consolidation** — classic PLAPS pattern combining free fluid and hepatized lung (Jellyfish sign). This pattern is commonly associated with heart failure, parapneumonic effusion, or malignant effusion with lobar atelectasis.
