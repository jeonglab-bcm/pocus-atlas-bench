# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Sequential Frames)

## Systematic Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Frames 1–3 (Early sequence):
- A **curved hyperechoic line** (diaphragm) is identified inferiorly
- **Anechoic (black) space** is visible above the diaphragm, bounded superiorly by the pleural line → consistent with the **Quad Sign**
- No significant internal echoes in this space initially

### Frames 4–6 (Mid sequence):
- The anechoic space **persists and appears to expand** slightly
- Respiratory variation in the position of the deep echogenic interface suggests a **Sinusoid Sign** (lung line oscillating toward the pleural line)
- Upper field shows normal-appearing pleural line without B-lines

### Frames 7–10 (Late sequence):
- A **rounded, echogenic floating structure** emerges within the anechoic collection
- This structure undulates with respiratory motion → classic **Jellyfish Sign**
- The floating structure itself shows **tissue-like echogenicity** (hepatization) with some internal bright foci suggesting **air bronchograms**
- The border between this structure and surrounding fluid is irregular → **Shred Sign** elements present

## Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

**Moderate-to-large pleural effusion** (quad sign, sinusoid sign) with **floating consolidated/atelectatic lung** (jellyfish sign) demonstrating tissue-like hepatization and air bronchograms. This pattern is consistent with a **compressive atelectasis within a significant pleural effusion**, a hallmark PLAPS finding in critically ill patients.
