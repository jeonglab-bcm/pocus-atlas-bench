## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Sequential Frames)

---

### Observations Across All 10 Frames

#### **1. Pleural Line**
A bright hyperechoic pleural line is consistently visible in the **upper portion** of each frame, with bilateral rib shadows flanking it — forming the superior boundary of the zone of interest.

#### **2. Large Anechoic Space (Effusion)**
A prominent **anechoic (black) collection** occupies the central-to-inferior field across all frames, bounded by:
- The pleural line **superiorly**
- A curvilinear echogenic lung interface **inferiorly** (lung line)
- Rib acoustic shadows **laterally**

→ ✅ **Quad Sign: POSITIVE** — Classic quadrilateral fluid space confirmed.

#### **3. Lung Line Behavior (Respiratory Variation)**
Across sequential frames, the lung line shows **subtle positional variation** (closer to pleural line in some frames, more distant in others), consistent with respiratory-phase fluid dynamics.

→ ✅ **Sinusoid Sign: LIKELY POSITIVE** (static frames; would be confirmed in M-mode)

#### **4. Floating Structure Within Effusion**
Beginning in frames 4–6 and becoming more prominent in frames 7–10, a **lobulated, echogenic soft-tissue structure** is visible **floating/undulating within the anechoic collection**. It has a rounded, irregular morphology inconsistent with a simple artifact.

→ ✅ **Jellyfish Sign: POSITIVE** — Atelectatic lung lobe suspended within surrounding fluid

#### **5. Tissue-Like Density (Consolidation / Compressive Atelectasis)**
The floating structure demonstrates:
- **Hepatized (liver-like) echogenicity** — solid, homogeneous gray texture
- **Punctate hyperechoic foci** within the parenchyma in frames 8–10 → consistent with **air bronchograms**
- A **shredded/irregular deep border** where the consolidated tissue interfaces with residual aeration

→ ✅ **Tissue-like Hepatization: POSITIVE**
→ ✅ **Air Bronchograms: PRESENT**
→ ✅ **Shred Sign: PRESENT at deep margin**
