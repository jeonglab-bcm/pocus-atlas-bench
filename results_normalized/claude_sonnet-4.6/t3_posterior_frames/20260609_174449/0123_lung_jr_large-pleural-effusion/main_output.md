# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Sequential Frame Observations

#### Frames 1–5 (Early frames)
- A **predominantly anechoic (black) region** is visible below the pleural line
- The space is bounded superiorly by the **bright pleural line** and flanked by **rib acoustic shadows** laterally
- This forms a clear **Quad sign** — consistent with **pleural effusion**
- No significant echogenic material visible within the fluid at this stage

#### Frames 6–10 (Later frames)
- A **bright, echogenic, heterogeneous mass** emerges from below, floating within the anechoic fluid space
- This structure demonstrates a **tissue-like, liver-textured (hepatized) appearance**
- It appears to **undulate** slightly across frames — consistent with the **Jellyfish sign** (atelectatic/consolidated lung floating within effusion)
- Within the echogenic tissue, there are **punctate/linear hyperechoic foci** suggestive of **air bronchograms**
- The deep border of the echogenic tissue is **irregular** — consistent with the **Shred sign**
