---

| Frames | Finding |

| 1, 3, 5, 7 | **Rounded/oval hypoechoic-to-echogenic structure** visible mid-field, surrounded by an **anechoic (black) space** |

| 8, 9, 10 | Structure re-emerges with visible **internal echogenicity**, suggesting hepatized/consolidated lung tissue; possible punctate hyperechoic foci consistent with **air bronchograms** |

---

### Sign Identification

#### 🌊 Effusion Signs
- **Quad sign**: An anechoic quadrilateral space is suggested by the dark surrounding region bounded by the pleural line superiorly and rib shadows.
- **Jellyfish sign** ✅ **(most prominent)**: A rounded, mobile echogenic structure (atelectatic/consolidated lower lobe) is seen **floating and undulating** within the anechoic effusion across respiratory cycles — classic jellyfish morphology.
- **Sinusoid sign** (implied): The cyclical appearance/disappearance of the lung structure across frames reflects respiratory movement of lung toward the pleural line within fluid.

#### 🫁 Consolidation Signs
- **Tissue-like hepatization** ✅: The rounded structure demonstrates **liver-like echogenicity**, consistent with consolidated/atelectatic lung.
- **Air bronchograms** ✅ (probable): In frames 8–10, **punctate/linear hyperechoic foci** are visible within the hepatized structure.
- **Shred sign**: Not clearly delineated, likely obscured by surrounding fluid.
