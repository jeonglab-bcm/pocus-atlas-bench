# 0143_lung_jr_pleuraleffusion

# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

## Sequential Frame Observations

### Structural Landmarks
- **Chest wall / soft tissue**: Bright superficial layers visible at the top of each frame
- **Pleural line**: Hyperechoic line forming the superior boundary of the deep field
- **Deep field**: Dominated by a large **anechoic (black) region** extending through most of the imaging depth

---

## Identified Findings

### ✅ Effusion Signs

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| **Quad sign** | Large anechoic quadrilateral space bounded superiorly by pleural line, with lateral rib shadows — **clearly present** |
| **Sinusoid sign** | Across sequential frames, the deep echogenic boundary shifts cyclically toward the pleural line, consistent with respiratory variation within fluid |
| **Jellyfish sign** | In mid-to-late frames (frames 7–10), a floating, undulating echogenic structure appears within the anechoic fluid — consistent with **atelectatic lung floating in effusion** |

### ✅ Consolidation Signs

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| **Tissue-like hepatization** | Echogenic, liver-textured tissue visible at the deep border in later frames |
| **Shred sign** | Irregular interface between the echogenic tissue and surrounding fluid visible in frames 8–10 |

---

## Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

A **large pleural effusion** is the dominant finding (quad sign + sinusoid sign). A **jellyfish sign** indicates floating atelectatic lung within the fluid. Additionally, hepatized/consolidated lung tissue with a shred sign is visible at the deep margin, indicating **associated compressive atelectasis or consolidation**.
