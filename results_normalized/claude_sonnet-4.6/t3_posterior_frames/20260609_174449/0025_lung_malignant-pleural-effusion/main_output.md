# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Systematic Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### **Effusion Signs**

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| **Quad Sign** | A **large anechoic (black) space** is clearly visible, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib shadows — forming a classic quadrilateral fluid pocket |
| **Jellyfish Sign** | In multiple frames (frames 7–10), an **echogenic structure appears to undulate/float** within the anechoic fluid — consistent with atelectatic lung suspended in effusion |
| **Sinusoid Sign** | Cyclical approximation of the lung line toward the pleural line is suggested across sequential frames |

#### **Consolidation Signs**

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| **Tissue-like Hepatization** | The deeper lung parenchyma demonstrates **solid, liver-like echogenicity** — loss of normal aeration artifacts |
| **Air Bronchograms** | **Punctate/linear hyperechoic foci** are visible within the hepatized region across multiple frames |
| **Shred Sign** | The deep border of consolidated tissue is **irregular and fragmented**, demarcating consolidated from residually aerated lung |

---

### Conclusion

```
plaps  = true
type   = "both"
```

**Rationale:** The posterior zone demonstrates a **large pleural effusion** (quad sign + jellyfish sign of floating atelectatic lung) **combined with underlying consolidation** (hepatization + air bronchograms + shred sign). This is a classic **PLAPS-positive** pattern indicative of posterior alveolar and pleural pathology.
