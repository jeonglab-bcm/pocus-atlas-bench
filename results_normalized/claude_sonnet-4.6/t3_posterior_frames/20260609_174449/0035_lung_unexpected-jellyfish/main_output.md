# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### **Frames 1–3 (Early systole/diastole phase)**
- A **distinct anechoic (black) space** is visible superiorly, bounded above by the **pleural line** and below by an echogenic interface — consistent with the **Quad Sign**
- Below this fluid space, echogenic tissue begins to emerge, suggesting a tissue-like structure beneath the effusion
- The fluid collection appears **free-flowing** and dependent

#### **Frames 4–6 (Mid-sequence)**
- The anechoic space **persists** but becomes partially compressed
- The underlying lung tissue transitions into a **heterogeneous, liver-like echogenicity** — classic **hepatization pattern**
- **Hyperechoic punctate foci** are visible within the echogenic tissue → consistent with **air bronchograms**
- Deep border of the consolidated tissue appears **irregular and shredded** → **Shred Sign**

#### **Frames 7–10 (Late sequence)**
- **Hepatized lung** is the dominant finding — solid, echogenic parenchyma with loss of normal aeration artifacts
- **Air bronchograms** (static, linear/punctate bright foci) are clearly visible within consolidated tissue
- The **lung-fluid interface** shows slight respiratory movement suggestive of a residual **Sinusoid Sign**
- A floating echogenic structure within the effusion may suggest early **Jellyfish Sign**

> **Interpretation:** This PLAPS-point ultrasound demonstrates a **moderate pleural effusion** (quad sign, sinusoid sign, jellyfish sign) with **underlying pulmonary consolidation** (hepatization, air bronchograms, shred sign). This pattern is consistent with a **parapneumonic effusion with lobar/segmental pneumonia** or post-obstructive atelectasis with reactive effusion. Clinical correlation (fever, leukocytosis, CXR) is recommended.
