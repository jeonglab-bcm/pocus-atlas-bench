# Lung Ultrasound — PLAPS Point Analysis

## Sequential Frame Observations

### Frames 1–2 (Early Sweep)
- Predominantly **anechoic (black) space** beneath the pleural line
- Clear upper boundary (pleural line) with lateral rib shadow margins
- Consistent with **Quad sign** morphology — free fluid collection

### Frames 3–5 (Mid Sweep — Transition Zone)
- A large **echogenic, tissue-like structure** emerges from the inferior-lateral aspect
- Texture closely resembles liver parenchyma → **hepatization pattern**
- The anechoic fluid space remains visible **superior to** the solid tissue
- The consolidated lung appears to "float" within the fluid → **Jellyfish sign**

### Frames 6–10 (Later Sweep — Full Consolidation)
- The hepatized region **dominates** the field
- **Hyperechoic punctate/linear foci** within the solid tissue → **Air bronchograms**
- Deep border is **irregular and shredded** → **Shred sign**
- Residual anechoic fluid is visible at the margins
