# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

## Sequential Frame Observations

### Frames 1–3 (Early Sequence)
- A **large anechoic (black) space** dominates the field, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by an echogenic structure — classic **Quad Sign**
- A small **hyperechoic punctate focus** is visible within the echogenic tissue (**air bronchogram**)
- The deep lung border shows **hepatized (liver-like) echogenicity** — tissue-like consolidation/atelectasis

### Frames 4–6 (Mid Sequence)
- The echogenic consolidated/atelectatic lung is clearly **floating freely within the effusion**, undulating with respiratory motion
- This is the classic **Jellyfish Sign** — atelectatic lung suspended in surrounding fluid
- The hepatized lung texture confirms **consolidation/compressive atelectasis**

### Frames 7–10 (Late Sequence)
- The echogenic lung gradually **descends toward the dependent field**, consistent with **sinusoid-type respiratory movement**
- The posterior fluid collection remains large with an **irregular deep lung border** (possible **Shred Sign** at the fluid-lung interface)
- The effusion progressively occupies more of the posterior zone
