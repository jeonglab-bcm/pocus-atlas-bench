## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Structural Landmarks
- **Pleural line**: Clearly visible as a bright hyperechoic line at the superior aspect
- **Rib shadows**: Present laterally, defining the borders of the acoustic window

---

### Key Findings Across Sequential Frames

#### 1. 🟦 Quad Sign — **PRESENT**
A large **anechoic (black) quadrilateral space** is consistently visible, bounded by:
- Pleural line (superiorly)
- Visceral pleural/lung line (inferiorly)
- Rib acoustic shadows (laterally)
→ Indicates **free pleural effusion**

#### 2. 🪼 Jellyfish Sign — **PRESENT**
Across sequential frames, a **bright, echogenic, mobile structure** is seen:
- **Floating and undulating** within the anechoic effusion
- Changes shape and position with each frame (respiratory variation)
- Represents **collapsed/atelectatic lung** tethered at the hilum, drifting in fluid

#### 3. 🫁 Hepatization — **PRESENT**
The floating structure exhibits:
- **Liver-like (hepatized) echogenicity**
- Loss of normal aeration artifacts
- Tissue-like solid appearance → **consolidation/atelectasis**
