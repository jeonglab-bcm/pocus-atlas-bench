- Relatively homogeneous echogenic parenchyma in the posterior zone
- No obvious anechoic (black) quadrilateral space → **no quad sign**

- No posterior acoustic shadowing from ribs enclosing an anechoic space → **no quad sign confirmed**

### Conclusion

```
plaps   = true
type    = "consolidation"
```

**Rationale**: The posterior left basal lung zone demonstrates classic features of **pulmonary consolidation**:
- **Hepatization**: Solid, liver-like echogenicity replacing normal lung parenchyma
- **Air bronchograms**: Hyperechoic punctate/linear foci within the consolidated tissue
- **Shred sign**: Irregular interface at the deep border of consolidation

There is **no convincing anechoic effusion** forming a quad sign or sinusoid sign. This pattern at the PLAPS point is consistent with **posterior basal consolidation** (e.g., pneumonia, atelectasis), without accompanying significant pleural effusion.
