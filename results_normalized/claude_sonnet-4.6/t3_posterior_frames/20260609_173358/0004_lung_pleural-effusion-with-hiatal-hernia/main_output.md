# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

### Sequential Frame Observations

#### Structural Landmarks
- **Curvilinear (convex) probe** used, appropriate for deep structure assessment
- **Diaphragm** visible as a bright, curvilinear hyperechoic structure on the **right side** of each frame
- Depth scale indicates structures visualized to ~8–9 cm

---

### Findings Identified Across All Frames

#### ✅ Tissue-Like Hepatization
The lung parenchyma in the central/posterior field demonstrates **heterogeneous, echogenic texture resembling liver parenchyma** — loss of normal aeration pattern, consistent with **alveolar consolidation**

#### ✅ Air Bronchograms
Scattered **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** are visible throughout the hepatized tissue across multiple frames, representing **air-filled bronchi** within consolidated alveoli

#### ✅ Shred Sign
The deep margin of the consolidated zone shows an **irregular, non-smooth border** between the consolidated and potentially aerated lung

#### ⚠️ Possible Small Effusion
A small anechoic component may be present adjacent to the consolidation, though not dominant

---

### Conclusion

| Parameter | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| **PLAPS** | ✅ `true` |
| **Type** | `"consolidation"` (dominant) |
| **Key signs** | Hepatization + Air bronchograms + Shred sign |

> **Interpretation**: This pattern is consistent with **posterior alveolar consolidation** (e.g., pneumonia, atelectasis). Clinical correlation recommended.
