# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — RT H/T Zone 2 Low (PLAPS Point)

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Finding | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Anechoic space** | Dark, fluid-filled region visible above the diaphragm in multiple frames, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by the diaphragm |
| **Hepatized tissue** | Rounded, liver-textured echogenic structure visible in alternating frames (frames 1, 3, 5, 7, 9–10), representing consolidated/atelectatic lung |
| **Cyclic movement** | The hepatized mass **appears and disappears** across frames in synchrony with respiration — the hallmark dynamic finding |
| **Air bronchograms** | Punctate hyperechoic foci visible within the echogenic mass in later frames (especially frame 10) |
| **Diaphragm** | Bright curvilinear reflector at bottom-right, confirming subdiaphragmatic location of liver |

---

### Sign Identification

> 🪼 **Jellyfish Sign** ✅
The consolidated/atelectatic lung lobe **floats and undulates** within surrounding anechoic pleural fluid across respiratory cycles — the defining jellyfish sign.

> ⬛ **Quad Sign** ✅
Anechoic fluid bounded by pleural line superiorly, diaphragm/lung line inferiorly, and rib shadows laterally.

> 🫁 **Tissue-like Hepatization** ✅
Solid, liver-echotextured mass = consolidated lung parenchyma.

> 💨 **Air Bronchograms** ✅ (subtle, frame 10)
Punctate hyperechoic foci within the hepatized region.

---

### Conclusion

```
plaps  = true
type   = "both"
```

**Rationale:** This clip demonstrates a **moderate-to-large right pleural effusion** (anechoic quad sign) containing a **floating consolidated/atelectatic lung lobe** (jellyfish sign) with internal air bronchograms — consistent with **compressive atelectasis within an effusion**, a classic PLAPS pattern involving both effusion and consolidation.
