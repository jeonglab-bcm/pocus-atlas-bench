# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — Right Posterior Zone (PLAPS Point)

## Sequential Frame Observations

### Frames 1–5 (Early Phase)
- A predominantly **anechoic (black) space** is seen in the dependent region
- The **pleural line** is visible superiorly, with rib shadows laterally
- This anechoic quadrilateral space is consistent with a **Quad Sign** → **pleural effusion**
- The deep boundary (lung line) is relatively flat and smooth

### Frames 6–10 (Late Phase / Respiratory Movement)
- A large **echogenic, liver-textured mass** progressively enters the field of view from deep within the effusion
- The texture demonstrates **tissue-like hepatization** (echogenicity resembling hepatic parenchyma)
- **Punctate/linear hyperechoic foci** are visible within this tissue → consistent with **air bronchograms**
- The structure undulates with respiration, floating within the surrounding anechoic fluid → **Jellyfish Sign**
- The deep border of the consolidated tissue is irregular → **Shred Sign**
