Across **all 10 frames**, a prominent **anechoic (black) quadrilateral space** is consistently visible in the right posterior base:
- **Superior border**: Bright pleural line
- **Inferior border**: Diaphragm / lung interface
- **Lateral borders**: Rib acoustic shadows
- This constitutes a clear **Quad Sign**, indicative of **pleural effusion**

---

#### **Jellyfish Sign**
In frames **1, 5–10**, an **echogenic, tissue-like structure** is seen **floating and undulating** within the anechoic effusion:
- Its position **shifts progressively** across the respiratory cycle
- It appears tethered at the hilum and freely mobile distally
- Movement is consistent with respiratory-driven buoyancy
- This constitutes a **Jellyfish Sign** — atelectatic/collapsed lung suspended within surrounding fluid

---

#### **Tissue Hepatization / Consolidation**
The floating structure itself displays:
- **Homogeneous, solid-appearing echogenicity** resembling liver parenchyma
- Loss of normal aeration artifacts within its substance
- Suggestive of **compressive atelectasis with hepatization** of the dependent lung

#### **Sinusoid Sign (Dynamic)**
The lung line demonstrably **changes distance from the pleural line** across sequential frames (compare frames 2–3 vs. 6–10), consistent with **sinusoidal respiratory movement** within the fluid — supporting a **Sinusoid Sign**
