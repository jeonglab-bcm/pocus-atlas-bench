### Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Frames | Key Findings |
|--------|-------------|
| 1–4 | Hyperechoic pleural line; **tissue-like, hepatized echogenicity** below it — solid parenchymal texture resembling liver |

---

### Signs Identified

- ✅ **Tissue-like hepatization** — throughout all frames, the lung parenchyma has lost aeration and resembles liver texture
- ✅ **Shred sign** — irregular deep border between consolidated and residual aerated lung (frames 7–10)
- ✅ **Air bronchograms** — punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within the consolidated tissue
- ⚠️ **Possible quad sign / jellyfish sign** — anechoic region in frames 5–6 suggests a small associated effusion

---

### Conclusion

plaps  = true

**Dominant finding: posterior lung consolidation (hepatization + shred sign + air bronchograms), with a likely small associated pleural effusion component.**
