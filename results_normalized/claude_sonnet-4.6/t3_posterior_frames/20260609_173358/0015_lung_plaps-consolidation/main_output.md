# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

## Sequential Frame Observations

### Frame 1
- **Dominant large anechoic (black) space** fills the majority of the image
- Bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib shadows
- Classic **Quad sign** is present — a clear quadrilateral anechoic fluid collection

### Frames 2–10 (Progressive)
| Finding | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Anechoic fluid** | Persists in superior portion — large free-flowing pleural effusion |
| **Echogenic mass-like structure** | Appears in mid-to-deep field; tissue-like (hepatized) texture, resembling liver parenchyma |
| **Floating motion** | Structure changes position across frames, undulating within fluid — **Jellyfish sign** |
| **Hyperechoic foci** | Punctate/linear bright spots within echogenic tissue — **Air bronchograms** |
| **Irregular deep border** | Shredded interface between echogenic lung and aerated tissue — possible **Shred sign** |

## Interpretation

The sequential frames demonstrate **atelectatic/consolidated lung floating freely within a large pleural effusion**, with internal air bronchograms confirming residual aeration.
