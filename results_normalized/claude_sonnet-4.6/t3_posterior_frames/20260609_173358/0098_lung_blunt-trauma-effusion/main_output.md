# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

## Sequential Frame Observations

### Frames 1–5 (Early Sweep)
- Moderately echogenic tissue in the near/mid field with **irregular, heterogeneous texture**
- Hyperechoic streaks and foci visible within the parenchyma
- Frame 4 shows a relatively homogeneous mid-gray zone, suggesting transitional imaging plane
- No clearly dominant A-lines; no normal aeration pattern

### Frames 6–10 (Later Sweep)
| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Hepatization** | Lung parenchyma is **solid and echogenic**, resembling liver texture — loss of normal aeration |
| **Air bronchograms** | Multiple **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** within the consolidated tissue |
| **Shred sign** | Deep border of consolidated lung is **irregular and shredded**, not smooth |
| **Jellyfish sign** | Echogenic consolidated tissue appears to **undulate/float** within a surrounding anechoic space in frames 8–10 |
| **Quad sign** | Anechoic space partially bounded by pleural and lung lines visible in later frames |

---

## Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

Both **alveolar consolidation** (hepatization + air bronchograms + shred sign) and **pleural effusion** (anechoic surrounding space + jellyfish sign) are present at the PLAPS point. Consolidation appears predominant, with effusion serving as the surrounding medium in which the consolidated lobe floats.
