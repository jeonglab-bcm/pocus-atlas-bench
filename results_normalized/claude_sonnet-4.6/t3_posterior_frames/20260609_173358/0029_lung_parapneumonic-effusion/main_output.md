# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Effusion Evidence
| Feature | Observation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Anechoic space** | Large, uniformly black region occupying the upper/mid field across nearly all frames |
| **Quad sign** | Anechoic space bounded superiorly by pleural line, inferiorly by echogenic lung interface, laterally by rib acoustic shadows — **present** |
| **Jellyfish sign** | Echogenic tissue mass seen floating and undulating within the anechoic collection across sequential frames (frames 1–2, 9–10) — **present** |

### Consolidation Evidence
| Feature | Observation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Hepatization** | Echogenic, liver-textured parenchymal mass visible within/below effusion — **present** |
| **Air bronchograms** | Focal punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within the consolidated tissue, notably prominent in frames 7–8 — **present** |
| **Shred sign** | Irregular, ill-defined deep border of the consolidated tissue — **present** |

## Summary of Findings
- **Large posterior pleural effusion** with classic Quad sign geometry
- **Atelectatic/consolidated lung** floating within the effusion → Jellyfish sign
- **Hepatized lung parenchyma** with internal air bronchograms
- Suggests **compressive atelectasis ± consolidation** (e.g., pneumonia, infarct) surrounded by effusion
