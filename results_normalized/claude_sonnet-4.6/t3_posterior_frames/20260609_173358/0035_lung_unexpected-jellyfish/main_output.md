# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Feature | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| **Anechoic space** | Large black/anechoic region in the dependent posterior zone, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib shadows — **Quad sign present** |
| **Deep border** | Bright echogenic mass visible within/adjacent to the anechoic fluid — consistent with **atelectatic/consolidated lung** |
| **Dynamic floating structure** | Across multiple frames, the echogenic lung tissue appears to shift position within the fluid — consistent with **Jellyfish sign** |
| **Tissue texture** | The echogenic lung mass demonstrates **hepatization** — solid, liver-like parenchymal echogenicity with loss of aeration artifacts |
| **Hyperechoic foci** | Punctate/linear bright spots within the consolidated tissue visible in several frames — consistent with **air bronchograms** |

---

### Signs Identified

- ✅ **Quad sign** (pleural effusion)
- ✅ **Jellyfish sign** (floating atelectatic lung within effusion)
- ✅ **Hepatization** (consolidation/compressive atelectasis)
- ✅ **Air bronchograms** (within consolidated tissue)

---

### Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

There is a **moderate-to-large pleural effusion** with a **floating atelectatic/consolidated lung** (jellyfish sign), along with evidence of **pulmonary consolidation/compressive atelectasis** showing hepatization and air bronchograms — a classic PLAPS pattern.
