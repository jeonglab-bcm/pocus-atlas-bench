# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point Assessment

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Frames 1–3 (Early Sequence)
- A **bright pleural line** is visible superficially
- A clearly **anechoic (black) space** is present between the pleural line and a deeper reflective interface
- **Rib shadows** bound the space laterally
- This constitutes a classic **Quad Sign** — confirming free pleural fluid

### Frames 4–6 (Mid Sequence)
- The anechoic space **persists** with respiratory variation
- The deeper lung interface (lung line) shows **oscillatory movement** toward the pleural line — consistent with a **Sinusoid Sign**
- No A-lines visible in this zone (normal aeration pattern absent)

### Frames 7–10 (Late Sequence)
- A **rounded, echogenic structure** emerges in the dependent portion of the fluid collection
- This structure exhibits **undulating/floating motion** within the effusion — consistent with a **Jellyfish Sign** (atelectatic/consolidated lung lobe floating in fluid)
- The tissue demonstrates **hepatization-like echotexture** suggesting associated consolidation/atelectasis
