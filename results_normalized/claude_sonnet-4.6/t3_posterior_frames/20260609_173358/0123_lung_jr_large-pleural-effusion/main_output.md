# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### **Frames 1–5 (Early sequence)**
- A distinct **anechoic (black) space** is visible in the dependent/posterior zone, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib shadows
- This forms a clear **Quad Sign** — a quadrilateral anechoic collection consistent with **pleural effusion**
- No significant internal echogenicity within the fluid space at this point

#### **Frames 6–10 (Later sequence)**
- A **rounded, hyperechoic (bright) structure** progressively appears within the anechoic effusion on the right mid-lower field
- This structure **changes position across sequential frames**, demonstrating respiratory motion
- Its appearance — echogenic, irregular, mobile, floating within surrounding fluid — is consistent with the **Jellyfish Sign** (atelectatic/consolidated lung floating within effusion)
- The tissue exhibits **hepatization** (liver-like echogenicity), indicating **pulmonary consolidation/atelectasis**
