# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Sequential Frames)

---

### Observations

#### Anechoic Space (Effusion)
- A **large anechoic (black) region** occupies the dependent/posterior zone across all frames
- It is bounded superiorly by the **pleural line** — consistent with a **Quad sign**
- The fluid collection is homogeneous and anechoic, confirming **free pleural effusion**

#### Dynamic Lung Structure Within Fluid
- A **hyperechoic, tissue-like structure** is visible floating within the effusion
- Across sequential frames, this structure **changes position and orientation**, showing clear **undulating movement**
- This is the classic **Jellyfish sign** — atelectatic/consolidated lung floating freely in surrounding effusion

#### Lung Parenchyma
- The floating lung tissue demonstrates **hepatization** (solid, liver-like echogenicity)
- Loss of normal aeration artifacts within the structure
- Possible **punctate hyperechoic foci** suggesting **air bronchograms** within the consolidated segment
