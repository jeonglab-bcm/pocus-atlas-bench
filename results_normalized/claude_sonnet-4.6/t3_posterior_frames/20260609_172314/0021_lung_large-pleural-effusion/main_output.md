# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Sequential Frames)

---

### Observations

**Pleural Line:**
- Hyperechoic pleural line is identifiable near the top of each frame
- No normal A-line reverberation pattern in the posterior zone

**Parenchymal Echotexture:**
- The posterior lung zone demonstrates **tissue-like (hepatized) echogenicity** — the lung parenchyma appears solid, with a texture resembling liver
- This pattern is consistent and persistent across all 10 frames

**Deep Border:**
- The inferior/deep margin of the consolidated region appears **irregular and shredded**, consistent with a **Shred Sign** — representing the interface between consolidated and partially aerated lung

**Air Bronchograms:**
- **Punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** are visible within the hepatized tissue across multiple frames, consistent with **air bronchograms**

**Effusion Assessment:**
- No clearly defined anechoic (black) quadrilateral space (no Quad Sign)
- No floating/undulating lung tissue (no Jellyfish Sign)
- No evidence of significant free pleural fluid

---

### Conclusion

| Parameter | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| **PLAPS** | ✅ `true` |
| **Type** | `consolidation` |
| **Key Signs** | Hepatization + Shred Sign + Air Bronchograms |

> **PLAPS = TRUE — Consolidation pattern** without significant associated effusion.
