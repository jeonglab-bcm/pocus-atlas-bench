# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

---

### **Frames 1–2 (Cephalad sweep)**
- Hyperechoic, **liver-like (hepatized) tissue** visible in the near-to-mid field
- Irregular deep border suggesting a **shred sign**
- Punctate and linear bright foci within the tissue consistent with **air bronchograms**

---

### **Frames 3–4 (Transition zone)**
- Emergence of a **homogeneous hypoechoic/anechoic region** below the pleural line
- Bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib shadows → **Quad sign forming**
- Loss of normal A-line pattern

---

### **Frames 5–10 (Caudal sweep — most diagnostic)**

| Finding | Detail |
|--------|---------|
| **Quad sign** | Anechoic quadrilateral space = **pleural effusion** confirmed |
| **Jellyfish sign** | Echogenic, hepatized lung tissue floating/undulating within anechoic fluid across respiratory frames |
| **Hepatization** | Solid, liver-textured parenchyma — loss of aeration |
| **Air bronchograms** | Hyperechoic punctate/linear foci within consolidated tissue |
| **Shred sign** | Irregular posterior border between consolidated and deeper aerated lung |

---

### Conclusion

```
plaps = true
type  = "both"
```

> **Pleural effusion** (quad sign, anechoic space) with **atelectatic/consolidated lung floating within it** (jellyfish sign) **+** underlying **consolidation** (hepatization, air bronchograms, shred sign).

This is a classic **PLAPS-positive** pattern representing a **parapneumonic effusion with associated pulmonary consolidation/atelectasis**, highly consistent with pneumonia with reactive effusion or lobar collapse-consolidation.
