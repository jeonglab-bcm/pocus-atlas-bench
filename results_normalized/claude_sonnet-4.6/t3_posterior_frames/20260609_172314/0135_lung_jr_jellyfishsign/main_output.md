- Prominent **anechoic (black) region** below the pleural line

- Echogenic, solid tissue begins to **emerge and undulate** within the anechoic space

- Echogenicity resembles liver texture (**tissue-like hepatization**)
- Hyperechoic punctate/linear foci within the solid tissue — consistent with **air bronchograms**
- Deep border remains irregular (shred sign confirmed)
