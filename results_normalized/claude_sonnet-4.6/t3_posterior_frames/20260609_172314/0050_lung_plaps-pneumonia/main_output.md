# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Sequential Frames)

---

### Observations by Sign

#### 🔷 Effusion Signs

| Sign | Finding |
|------|---------|
| **Quad Sign** | ✅ Present — A clear **anechoic (black) quadrilateral space** is visible bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by a curved hyperechoic lung line, with rib shadows laterally (frames 1–6) |
| **Sinusoid Sign** | ✅ Suggested — Sequential frames show the lung line **cyclically approaching the pleural line**, consistent with respiratory movement within fluid |
| **Jellyfish Sign** | ✅ Present — In **frames 7–10**, a rounded, undulating **echogenic structure floats** within the anechoic effusion, representing atelectatic lung suspended in fluid |

#### 🔶 Consolidation Signs

| Sign | Finding |
|------|---------|
| **Tissue-like hepatization** | ✅ Present — The floating structure in later frames shows **liver-like echogenicity**, consistent with atelectatic/consolidated lung |
| **Shred sign** | ⚠️ Partially visible — Irregular deep border between the consolidated tissue and surrounding fluid |
| **Air bronchograms** | ❌ Not clearly identified in these frames |

### Sequential Pattern Summary

```
Frames 1–3:   Large anechoic effusion dominant → Quad sign
Frames 4–6:   Lung line movement toward pleural line → Sinusoid sign
Frames 7–10:  Rounded echogenic mass floating in fluid → Jellyfish sign

```

---

### Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

**Rationale:** The images demonstrate a **moderate-to-large pleural effusion** (quad sign, sinusoid sign) with a **floating atelectatic/consolidated lung lobe** (jellyfish sign + hepatization). This is a classic PLAPS-positive pattern representing **dependent effusion with compressive atelectasis** of the posterior basal lung segment.
