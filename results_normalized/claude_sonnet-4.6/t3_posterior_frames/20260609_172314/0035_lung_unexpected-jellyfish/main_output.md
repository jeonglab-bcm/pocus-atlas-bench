# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Frame | Key Findings |
|-------|-------------|
| 1–2 | Large **anechoic (black) space** bounded by pleural line superiorly and a deep echogenic interface — consistent with **Quad sign** |
| 3–4 | Anechoic fluid collection persists; a **hepatized (liver-like echogenic)** tissue mass becomes visible at the deep margin |
| 5–6 | Consolidated lung tissue appears with **irregular/shredded deep border** (Shred sign); hyperechoic foci within suggest **air bronchograms** |
| 7–8 | A structure appears to **float and undulate** within the anechoic fluid — consistent with **Jellyfish sign** (atelectatic lung in effusion) |
| 9–10 | Both components clearly visible: anechoic effusion anteriorly + consolidated/hepatized lung posteroinferiorly |

---

### Signs Identified

- ✅ **Quad sign** — anechoic quadrilateral pleural fluid space
- ✅ **Jellyfish sign** — floating atelectatic lung within effusion
- ✅ **Tissue-like hepatization** — echogenic solid-appearing lung
- ✅ **Shred sign** — irregular deep border of consolidation
- ✅ **Air bronchograms** — punctate hyperechoic foci within hepatized tissue
