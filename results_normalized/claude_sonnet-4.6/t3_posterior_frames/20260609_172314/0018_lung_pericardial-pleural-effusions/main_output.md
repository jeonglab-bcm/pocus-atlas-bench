## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Sequential Frames)

---

### Observations Across Frames

#### 🔲 Pleural Line
A hyperechoic pleural line is consistently visible at the near field across all frames, with normal rib shadow artifacts on either side.

---

#### 🌊 Anechoic Space — **Quad Sign: PRESENT**
In frames 3–10, a prominent **anechoic (black) quadrilateral space** is clearly visible:
- Bounded **superiorly** by the pleural line
- Bounded **inferiorly** by a distinct lung line
- Bordered laterally by rib/rib-shadow margins
- This is a **classic Quad Sign**, confirming **free pleural effusion**

---

#### 🪼 Floating Structure — **Jellyfish Sign: PRESENT**
Within the anechoic effusion, frames 4–9 demonstrate a **mobile, undulating echogenic structure** that:
- Floats freely within the fluid
- Changes shape and position across sequential frames
- Represents **atelectatic/consolidated lung** swaying within the effusion
- Consistent with the **Jellyfish Sign**

---

#### 🫁 Consolidated Lung Tissue — **Hepatization: PRESENT**
The floating lung structure itself exhibits:
- **Tissue-like (hepatized) echo texture** — echogenicity resembling liver/spleen
- Loss of normal aeration artifacts (no A-lines or B-lines within the structure)
- Possible **punctate hyperechoic foci** suggesting **air bronchograms** within the consolidated parenchyma
