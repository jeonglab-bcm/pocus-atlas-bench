# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### Chest Wall / Pleural Line (Near Field)
- Rib shadows and hyperechoic pleural line are consistently visible in the upper portion of all frames
- Some frames show a **small anechoic (dark) region** just beneath the pleural line, suggestive of **free fluid**

#### Deep Field Findings (Across All Frames)

| Feature | Observation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Tissue echogenicity** | Dense, solid, liver-like (hepatized) texture — markedly echogenic parenchyma |
| **Air bronchograms** | Punctate and linear hyperechoic foci scattered within the consolidated tissue, visible in frames 2–10 |
| **Deep border** | Irregular, shredded margin between consolidated and presumably aerated lung (**Shred sign**) |
| **Anechoic space** | Dark region superior/adjacent to hepatized tissue in multiple frames (**Quad sign** elements — effusion) |
| **Motion dynamics** | Tissue undulates across frames suggesting a **Jellyfish sign** component (atelectatic consolidated lung floating in fluid) |

---

### Signs Identified

- ✅ **Hepatization** (tissue-like consolidation)
- ✅ **Air bronchograms** (dynamic/static)
- ✅ **Shred sign**
- ✅ **Quad sign / anechoic pleural fluid**
- ✅ **Jellyfish sign** (motion across frames)

---

### Conclusion

```
plaps  = true
type   = "both"
```

**Rationale:** There is evidence of **posterior pleural effusion** (anechoic space forming a quad/jellyfish appearance) **combined with alveolar consolidation** (hepatization + air bronchograms + shred sign) in the dependent PLAPS zone — a pattern consistent with **parapneumonic effusion with underlying pneumonic consolidation**, or post-obstructive/compressive atelectasis with associated effusion.
