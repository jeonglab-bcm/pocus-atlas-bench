# Lung Ultrasound — PLAPS Point Analysis

## Sequential Frame Observations

### Frames 1–3 (Early phase)
- Clear **anechoic (black) space** is visible deep to the pleural line in the dependent zone
- The space is bounded superiorly by the **pleural line** and laterally by **rib acoustic shadows**
- This forms a classic **Quad sign**, consistent with **pleural effusion**
- No complex echoes or floating structures yet visible within the fluid

### Frames 4–6 (Transition)
- The anechoic effusion space persists
- A **hyperechoic, tissue-like structure** begins appearing within the fluid collection
- The structure has **echogenicity resembling liver parenchyma** (hepatization pattern)

### Frames 7–10 (Late phase)
- The **echogenic solid structure** is now fully visible, **floating and undulating** within the surrounding anechoic effusion
- Movement across frames shows characteristic **oscillating motion** with respiration
- This is consistent with the **Jellyfish sign** — atelectatic/consolidated lung suspended within effusion
- The tissue demonstrates **solid, hepatized echogenicity** suggesting consolidation/atelectasis
