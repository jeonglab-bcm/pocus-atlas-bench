### Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Anechoic Space:**
- A **large anechoic (black) region** is consistently present across all frames, occupying a substantial portion of the image — consistent with a **significant pleural effusion**
- This space is clearly bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib/chest wall structures (**Quad sign ✓**)

**Floating Structure:**
- A **mobile, echogenic tissue mass** is visible undulating within the anechoic fluid across sequential frames
- This structure moves rhythmically, swinging and shifting position with each frame (respiratory cycle) — classic **Jellyfish sign ✓**
- The floating tissue demonstrates **hepatization** (liver-like echogenicity), consistent with **compressive atelectasis / consolidation**

**Consolidation Features:**
- The floating lung parenchyma shows **tissue-like echogenicity** (hepatization ✓)
- Punctate/linear hyperechoic foci are visible within the echogenic tissue, suggesting **air bronchograms ✓**
