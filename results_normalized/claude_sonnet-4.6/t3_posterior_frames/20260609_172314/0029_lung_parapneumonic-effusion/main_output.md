# 0029_lung_parapneumonic-effusion

# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Right Posterior Zone)

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Effusion Findings
| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| **Quad Sign** | ✅ Clear anechoic (black) space bounded superiorly by the pleural line, inferiorly by the lung line, and laterally by rib shadows — forming a classic quadrilateral fluid pocket |
| **Sinusoid Sign** | ✅ The lung line appears to oscillate toward the pleural line across sequential frames, consistent with respiratory modulation within a fluid collection |
| **Jellyfish Sign** | ✅ An echogenic structure is visible **floating and undulating** within the anechoic effusion across frames (most evident in frames 7–10), consistent with atelectatic lung suspended in fluid |

### Consolidation Findings
| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| **Tissue-like Hepatization** | ✅ The floating echogenic structure demonstrates **liver-like solid echotexture**, consistent with atelectatic/consolidated lung parenchyma |
| **Air Bronchograms** | ✅ Punctate/linear hyperechoic foci are visible **within** the hepatized tissue in several frames, representing air-filled bronchi |
| **Shred Sign** | ✅ Irregular, non-geometric deep border between the consolidated tissue and surrounding fluid/deeper aerated lung |

---

## Summary of Findings

> A **moderate-to-large right pleural effusion** is present, forming a well-defined anechoic quad pattern. Within this effusion, **atelectatic/consolidated lung** (hepatized texture with internal air bronchograms) is seen floating freely — producing the **jellyfish sign** across sequential frames. The deep border of the consolidation is shredded and irregular.

```
plaps  = true
```

**Dominant pattern**: Pleural effusion with secondary compressive/obstructive atelectasis and consolidation of the dependent right lower lobe — a classic PLAPS-positive pattern.
