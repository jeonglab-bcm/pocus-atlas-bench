---

### Systematic Observations

#### Near Field / Pleural Line
- A bright hyperechoic pleural line is consistently visible in the near field across all frames.
- No significant sliding artifact disruption noted, but the focus is the deep zone.

#### Deep Field — Lung Parenchyma
| Finding | Observation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Tissue sign / Hepatization** | The lung parenchyma below the pleural line shows **solid, liver-like echogenicity** — loss of normal aeration pattern, replacing the expected black (aerated) lung |
| **Air bronchograms** | Multiple **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** scattered throughout the consolidated area (best seen frames 48–175/229), consistent with air-filled bronchi within solid lung |
| **Shred sign** | The **deep border of the consolidated region is irregular and jagged** — not smooth — indicating interface between consolidated and partially aerated lung |
| **Quad sign / Effusion** | **No discrete anechoic (black) quadrilateral space** is identified; no clear fluid stripe separating pleural and lung lines |
| **Jellyfish / Sinusoid** | Not observed |

---

### Conclusion

> **plaps = true**
> **type = "consolidation"**

The posterior lung zone demonstrates **alveolar consolidation** with hepatization, air bronchograms, and a shred sign at the deep border. There is **no dominant pleural effusion** identified. This pattern is consistent with a **PLAPS-positive consolidation** (e.g., pneumonia, atelectasis).
