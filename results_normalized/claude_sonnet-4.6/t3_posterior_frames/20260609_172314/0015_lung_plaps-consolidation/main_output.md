# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frame 1:**
- Large **anechoic (black) space** dominating the field — consistent with a significant pleural effusion
- Bright curved line at the inferior margin representing the lung/pleural line
- Rib shadows visible laterally → **Quad sign present**

**Frames 2–10 (sequential):**
- The large anechoic effusion persists throughout
- A **bright, echogenic, tissue-like structure** emerges in the dependent field, clearly surrounded by anechoic fluid
- This structure shows:
  - **Hepatized (liver-like) echogenicity** — loss of normal aeration artifacts
  - **Punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** within it → **Air bronchograms**
  - **Irregular/shredded deep border** → **Shred sign**
  - **Floating/undulating motion** within the effusion → **Jellyfish sign**

### Conclusion

A **large pleural effusion** with **floating compressive/passive atelectasis** displaying hepatization and air bronchograms. This is a classic **PLAPS-positive** finding indicating posterior alveolar and pleural syndrome.
