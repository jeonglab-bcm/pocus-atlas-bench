# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

## Sequential Frame Observations

### Frames 1–3 (Early sequence)
- A **large anechoic (black) space** dominates the field, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib acoustic shadows → **Quad sign ✓**
- Within the anechoic collection, a **hyperechoic structure with punctate bright foci** is visible centrally — consistent with **air bronchograms** within collapsed/consolidated lung tissue

### Frames 4–6 (Mid sequence)
- The echogenic lung tissue is seen **floating and undulating** within the surrounding anechoic effusion
- The tissue moves cyclically with respiration → **Jellyfish sign ✓**
- The floating structure has a **hepatized (tissue-like) echotexture**, consistent with compressive atelectasis or consolidation

### Frames 7–10 (Late sequence)
- The consolidated/atelectatic lung becomes more prominent as it moves in and out of the scan plane
- The **shredded/irregular deep border** of the echogenic tissue is partially visible → **Shred sign ✓** (partial)
- The effusion remains visible surrounding the tissue
