### Sign Identification

#### 🔵 Effusion Signs
- **Quad Sign** ✅ — Anechoic quadrilateral space bounded by pleural line, lung line, and rib shadows is clearly visible (frames 2, 4, 6)
- **Sinusoid Sign** ✅ (implied) — The lung line moves toward the pleural line cyclically with respiration
- **Jellyfish Sign** ✅ — A rounded, consolidated lung segment **floats and undulates** within the surrounding effusion, appearing and partially retracting across sequential frames — classic jellyfish morphology

#### 🔴 Consolidation Signs
- **Tissue-like Hepatization** ✅ — The floating structure exhibits **liver-like echogenicity**, consistent with compressive atelectasis ± consolidation
- **Air Bronchograms** ✅ — Small punctate/linear **hyperechoic foci** are visible within the hepatized tissue (frames 3, 5, 8–10), representing air-filled bronchi

---

### Synthesis

> The sequential frames show a **moderate pleural effusion** with a **compressively atelectatic/consolidated lung lobe** floating freely within it. The structure's movement across frames represents real-time **jellyfish sign dynamics**. The hepatized texture with internal air bronchograms confirms the lung parenchyma is **consolidated/atelectatic**, not simply collapsed.
