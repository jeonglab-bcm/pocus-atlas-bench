# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Sequential Frames)

---

### Observations

#### 1. Pleural Line & Near Field
- The pleural line is identifiable but **lacks normal sliding artifacts** and **no A-lines** are generated deep to it — indicating loss of aeration in the underlying lung.

#### 2. Central Lung Zone — **Hepatization**
- The lung parenchyma occupying the majority of the field demonstrates a **liver-like (hepatized) echogenicity** — intermediate gray, homogeneous solid texture, indistinguishable in echogenicity from the liver/spleen.
- This is the hallmark **tissue sign** of alveolar consolidation.

#### 3. Hyperechoic Foci Within the Consolidated Tissue
- Scattered **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** are visible within the hepatized zone across multiple frames.
- These represent **air bronchograms** — air trapped within airways of consolidated lung — confirming true consolidation (vs. atelectasis alone, though both can coexist).

#### 4. Deep Border
- The deep margin of the consolidated zone is **irregular and shredded**, consistent with the **shred sign** — the interface between consolidated and partially aerated lung.

#### 5. Bright Wedge (Right Side of Frames)
- A **hyperechoic triangular/wedge structure** is consistently visible on the right side — representing the **diaphragm** in this posterolateral view, serving as a useful anatomical landmark.

#### 6. Effusion Assessment
- **No clearly defined anechoic quadrilateral** (quad sign) is identified.
- No sinusoid or jellyfish sign is present.
- There is **no significant free fluid** separating the pleural layers.

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"consolidation"`**

The posterior lung zone demonstrates **alveolar consolidation** with hepatization, air bronchograms, and a shred sign — consistent with **pneumonia, aspiration, or lobar collapse** in the dependent lung region. **No significant pleural effusion** is identified in these frames.
