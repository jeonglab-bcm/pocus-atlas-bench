### Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Frames | Findings |
|--------|----------|
| 1–3 | Chest wall and pleural line visible superiorly; hypoechoic/anechoic space emerging in dependent zone |

| Sign | Present | Notes |

| **Jellyfish sign** | ✅ | Echogenic atelectatic/consolidated lung floating and undulating within effusion across sequential frames |
| **Hepatization** | ✅ | Echogenic parenchymal tissue visible within fluid |

| **Shred sign / Air bronchograms** | Indeterminate | Resolution limits definitive characterization |

### Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

A **pleural effusion** (quad sign) is present with a **floating consolidated/atelectatic lung lobe** (jellyfish sign), indicating both fluid and parenchymal abnormality in the posterior dependent zone — a classic PLAPS pattern.
