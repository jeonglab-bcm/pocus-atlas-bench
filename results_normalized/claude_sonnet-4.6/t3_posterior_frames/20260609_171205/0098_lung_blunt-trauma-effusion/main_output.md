# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Sequential Frame Observations

#### **Frames 1–4 (Early frames):**
- Predominantly echogenic structures with irregular architecture
- Relatively homogeneous "tissue-like" echotexture in the near-to-mid field
- Faint suggestion of a dependent anechoic region beginning to emerge
- Hyperechoic foci visible within the tissue — early suggestion of **air bronchograms**

#### **Frames 5–10 (Later frames):**
- A **distinct anechoic (black) region** becomes clearly visible in the dependent zone → **Quad sign** (bounded superiorly by pleural line, inferiorly by lung/tissue interface, laterally by rib shadows)
- Within/adjacent to this anechoic collection: **echogenic, floating tissue mass** with irregular borders → **Jellyfish sign** (atelectatic/consolidated lung buoyant within effusion)
- The floating tissue demonstrates **hepatization** (liver-like echotexture, loss of normal aeration)
- **Punctate/linear hyperechoic foci** within the consolidated tissue → **Air bronchograms**
- Deep border of the consolidated lung shows irregular shredding → **Shred sign**
