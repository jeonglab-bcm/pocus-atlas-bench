# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

## Systematic Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Pleural Line & Superficial Layer
- A clear **pleural line** is visible superficially across all frames
- Rib shadows are identifiable laterally, providing anatomical landmarks

### Anechoic Space (Frames 1–10)
- A **large, predominantly anechoic (black) space** is consistently visible in the upper-to-mid field
- It is bounded:
  - **Superiorly** by the pleural line
  - **Inferiorly** by a distinct lung line
  - **Laterally** by rib/tissue shadows
- This forms a classic **Quad Sign** ✅ → **Pleural effusion confirmed**

### Deep Tissue Findings (Progressive across frames 3–10)
- Below/within the anechoic space, **echogenic (gray/white) tissue** is visible with:
  - **Hepatization pattern**: texture resembling liver parenchyma → **Consolidation** ✅
  - **Punctate/linear hyperechoic foci** within the tissue → **Air bronchograms** ✅
  - **Irregular deep border** between consolidated and deeper aerated lung → **Shred sign** ✅

### Dynamic Assessment
- In mid-sequence frames (5–8), the echogenic consolidated lung appears to **float/undulate** within the surrounding effusion → Suggestive of **Jellyfish Sign** ✅

**Interpretation**: This is a **positive PLAPS** with a **moderate-to-large pleural effusion** (Quad + Jellyfish signs) combined with **adjacent lung consolidation** (hepatization, air bronchograms, shred sign).
