# 0073_lung_2017-10-9-dove-in-pleural-fluid

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### Structural Findings (consistent across all 10 frames):

**1. Tissue-like Hepatization (Consolidation)**
The lower-mid field demonstrates **solid, echogenic tissue** with a liver-like echotexture — loss of normal aeration pattern, appearing as a gray homogeneous mass. This is the dominant finding across all frames.

**2. Shred Sign**
The **deep border** of the consolidated tissue is **irregular and fragmented** — a classic shred sign — representing the interface between hepatized (consolidated) and residually aerated lung parenchyma.

**3. Air Bronchograms**
Within the consolidated tissue, **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** are visible (best seen in frames 1–3, 7–8), consistent with **air-filled bronchi** trapped within consolidation.

**4. Possible Small Effusion (Frames 4–5)**
A relatively **hypoechoic/anechoic zone** is visible adjacent to the consolidated tissue, suggesting a **small pleural effusion**. The consolidated tissue appears to float or undulate slightly — raising the possibility of a **jellyfish sign** (atelectatic lung within fluid).

### Conclusion

| Parameter | Finding |

| **PLAPS** | ✅ `true` |
