# 0029_lung_parapneumonic-effusion

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Finding | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Anechoic space** | A prominent black (anechoic) region is consistently visible across all frames, bounded superiorly by the pleural line, inferiorly by a lung line, and laterally by rib shadows — **Quad Sign ✓** |
| **Floating structure** | A hyperechoic, tissue-like mass is seen undulating within the anechoic space across sequential frames — **Jellyfish Sign ✓** |
| **Hepatization** | The floating lung tissue demonstrates solid, liver-like echogenicity with loss of normal aeration artifacts — **Tissue-like hepatization ✓** |
| **Air bronchograms** | Punctate/linear hyperechoic foci are visible within the echogenic floating tissue — **Air bronchograms ✓** |

---

### Interpretation

- A **large pleural effusion** is present (anechoic quadrilateral space, quad sign)
- Within the effusion, **collapsed/consolidated lung** is floating and undulating with respiration (jellyfish sign)
- The floating lung parenchyma shows **compressive atelectasis/consolidation** with hepatization and internal air bronchograms

---

### Conclusion

```
plaps  = true
type   = "both"
```

> **Pleural effusion** (dominant finding) with **compressive atelectasis/consolidation** (jellyfish sign + hepatization + air bronchograms) — a classic PLAPS-positive pattern.
