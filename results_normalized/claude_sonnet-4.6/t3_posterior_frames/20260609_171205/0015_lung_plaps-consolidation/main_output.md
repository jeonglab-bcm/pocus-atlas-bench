# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### **Frame 1**
- A **large anechoic (black) space** dominates the upper/mid field
- Bordered inferiorly by bright echogenic rib structures with posterior acoustic shadowing
- Minimal internal echoes — consistent with **free pleural fluid**

#### **Frames 2–10 (Sequential)**
- The anechoic space persists across all frames (stable fluid collection)
- A **bright, echogenic, tissue-like structure** progressively appears within/deep to the anechoic zone
- This structure displays:
  - **Hepatized texture** (echogenicity resembling liver parenchyma)
  - **Irregular, shredded deep border** (shred sign)
  - Punctate/linear hyperechoic foci internally — consistent with **air bronchograms**
  - The structure appears to **undulate/shift** between frames → **Jellyfish sign**

---

### Signs Identified

| Sign | Present? |
|------|----------|
| **Quad sign** | ✅ Yes — anechoic quadrilateral bounded by pleural line, lung line, rib shadows |
| **Jellyfish sign** | ✅ Yes — floating atelectatic/consolidated lung within effusion |
| **Tissue hepatization** | ✅ Yes — solid echogenic lung parenchyma |
| **Air bronchograms** | ✅ Yes — hyperechoic foci within consolidated tissue |
| **Shred sign** | ✅ Yes — irregular deep border |

---

### Conclusion

> **PLAPS = `true`**
> **Type = `"both"`**

A **large pleural effusion** (quad sign, jellyfish sign) is present with underlying **pulmonary consolidation/atelectasis** (hepatization, air bronchograms, shred sign). The consolidated lung floats within the effusion, producing the classic jellyfish appearance. This is a **positive PLAPS pattern** indicating posterolateral alveolar and pleural pathology.
