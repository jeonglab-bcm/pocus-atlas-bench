# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — RT H/T Zone 2 Low (PLAPS Point)

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Feature | Observation |
|---|---|
| **Pleural line** | Visible superiorly, relatively smooth |
| **Anechoic space** | Present — dark (hypoechoic/anechoic) region representing free fluid |
| **Echogenic floating structure** | A rounded, lobulated, echogenic (hepatized-appearing) mass intermittently appears and disappears within the anechoic space across frames |
| **Respiratory dynamics** | The echogenic structure **oscillates in and out of view** cyclically with respiration |
| **Deep border** | Irregular, consistent with shred sign at lung-fluid interface |
| **Internal echogenicity** | Punctate bright foci within the solid tissue suggesting **air bronchograms** |

---

### Sign Identification

| Sign | Present? |
|---|---|
| **Quad sign** | ✅ Anechoic quadrilateral fluid pocket visible |
| **Jellyfish sign** | ✅ Atelectatic/consolidated lung **floating and undulating** within effusion across frames |
| **Hepatization** | ✅ Solid liver-like echotexture within floating tissue |
| **Air bronchograms** | ✅ Punctate hyperechoic foci within consolidated segment |
| **Shred sign** | ✅ Irregular deep border |

---

### Conclusion

```
plaps = true
type  = "both"
```

**Rationale:** The sequential frames demonstrate a classic **jellyfish sign** — a consolidated/atelectatic lung segment with hepatized echotexture and internal air bronchograms **floating freely** within a surrounding **pleural effusion**. This constitutes simultaneous evidence of both **effusion** (anechoic fluid, quad sign, jellyfish dynamics) and **consolidation** (hepatization, air bronchograms, shred sign), confirming a **PLAPS-positive** finding of the **"both"** subtype.
