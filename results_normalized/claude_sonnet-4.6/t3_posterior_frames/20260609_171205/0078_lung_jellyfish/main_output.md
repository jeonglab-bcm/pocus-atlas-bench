# 0078_lung_jellyfish

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — Right Posterior Zone (PLAPS Point)

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### **Frames 1–5 (Early Sequence)**
- The **dependent/far-field region** displays a relatively **anechoic (black) space** bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib/soft tissue interfaces
- This forms a **Quad sign** — a quadrilateral anechoic fluid collection
- The overlying lung parenchyma appears relatively unremarkable in early frames
- Depth markers (~10–15 cm) confirm this is a posterior dependent zone finding

#### **Frames 6–10 (Late Sequence)**
- A **large, echogenic, liver-textured structure** progressively enters the field — classic **tissue-like hepatization**
- The echogenic mass has:
  - **Irregular, shredded deep border** → **Shred sign** ✓
  - **Punctate/linear hyperechoic foci** within the echogenic parenchyma → **Air bronchograms** ✓
- The consolidated lung visibly **undulates and swings** into the anechoic fluid space with respiratory motion → **Jellyfish sign** ✓

### Conclusion

```
plaps = true
type  = "both"
```

**Interpretation:** This is a **classic PLAPS pattern** demonstrating a **moderate pleural effusion** with a **compressive/atelectatic consolidated lung segment** floating within it (jellyfish sign). The consolidated lung shows hepatization, air bronchograms, and a shredded deep margin — indicating **both free fluid and adjacent lung consolidation/atelectasis** in the right posterior dependent zone.

> **Clinical correlation:** This pattern is highly consistent with **parapneumonic effusion with adjacent consolidation**, or **malignant/transudative effusion with compressive atelectasis**. Integration with clinical context (fever, dyspnea, bilateral assessment) is recommended.
