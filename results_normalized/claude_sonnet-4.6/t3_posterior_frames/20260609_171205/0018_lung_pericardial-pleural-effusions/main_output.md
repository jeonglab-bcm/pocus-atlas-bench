# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point Assessment

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

**Frames 1–2 (Early systole/diastole):**
- A prominent **anechoic (black) space** is visible in the dependent posterior zone, bounded superiorly by the pleural line — consistent with the **Quad sign** (pleural effusion).
- Within the fluid, an **echogenic, irregular structure** is beginning to appear.

**Frames 3–4 (Mid-cycle):**
- The anechoic collection is well-defined.
- A **hyperechoic, tissue-dense structure** with liver-like echotexture becomes clearly visible within the fluid — consistent with **hepatization (tissue sign)**.
- The deep margin of this structure is **irregular and shredded** — consistent with the **Shred sign**, suggesting an interface between consolidated and aerated lung.

**Frames 5–10 (Late cycle / respiratory variation):**
- The echogenic, hepatized lung structure appears to **undulate and shift position** within the surrounding anechoic effusion across sequential frames — consistent with the **Jellyfish sign** (atelectatic/consolidated lung floating within effusion).
- No significant A-lines or B-lines are identified in this zone.
- The fluid collection appears moderate in size with clear respiratory dynamics.

### Conclusion

```
plaps  = true
type   = "both"
```

**Interpretation:** There is a **moderate pleural effusion** (Quad sign confirmed) with a **floating atelectatic/consolidated lung segment** (Jellyfish sign). The echogenic lung tissue demonstrates **hepatization** and an **irregular shredded deep border**, indicating underlying **compressive atelectasis or consolidation** within the effusion. This is a classic **PLAPS-positive** posterior zone finding, combining both fluid and solid pathology.
