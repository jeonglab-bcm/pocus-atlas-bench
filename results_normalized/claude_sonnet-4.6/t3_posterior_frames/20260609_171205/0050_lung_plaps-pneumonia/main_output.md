# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Sequential Frames)

---

### Observations

#### Frames 1–2 (Early Respiratory Phase)
- A clear **anechoic (black) space** is visible above the bright curvilinear diaphragm
- This space is **bounded superiorly by the pleural line**, inferiorly by the diaphragm/lung interface, and laterally by rib shadows
- Classic **Quad Sign** morphology is present → **Pleural effusion confirmed**

#### Frames 3–6 (Mid Respiratory Phase)
- The anechoic collection **varies in depth** with respiratory motion
- The deep lung line shows **cyclical movement toward the pleural line** → suggestive of **Sinusoid Sign** (dynamic respiratory variation within fluid)
- No A-lines in the posterior zone

#### Frames 7–10 (Late Phase / Deep Inspiration)
- A **rounded-to-lobulated echogenic structure** appears floating/undulating within the anechoic fluid space
- This echogenic mass has **tissue-like (hepatized) echotexture**, consistent with atelectatic/consolidated lung
- The floating behavior within surrounding fluid = **Jellyfish Sign**
- The border of this structure appears **irregular/shredded** → compatible with **Shred Sign**

### Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

There is a **moderate pleural effusion** (quad + sinusoid signs) with **floating consolidated/atelectatic lung** (jellyfish sign + hepatization), consistent with a **PLAPS-positive** posterior zone — indicating dependent alveolar and pleural pathology.
