# 0100_lung_hemothorax

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### **Dominant Finding: Large Anechoic Space**
Across all frames, there is a **large, black (anechoic) region** occupying the majority of the image — consistent with a **pleural effusion**. The space is bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib shadows, forming the **Quad Sign**.

#### **Mobile Echogenic Structure — Jellyfish Sign**
A **bright, hyperechoic, tissue-like mass** is visible *floating and undulating* within the anechoic effusion. Tracking across sequential frames:

| Frames | Observation |
|--------|------------|
| 1–3 | Echogenic structure positioned mid-field, beginning to shift |
| 4–6 | Structure visibly moves/rotates within the fluid |
| 7–10 | Continued undulation — classic pendular movement |

This movement pattern is the **Jellyfish Sign** — atelectatic/collapsed lung floating freely within surrounding pleural fluid.

#### **Tissue Hepatization**
The floating structure demonstrates **liver-like echogenicity** (tissue hepatization), consistent with **compressive atelectasis** or consolidation. Possible faint **linear hyperechoic foci** within the tissue may represent **air bronchograms**.
