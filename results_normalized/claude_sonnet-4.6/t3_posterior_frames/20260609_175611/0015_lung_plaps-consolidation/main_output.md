# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

# Lung Ultrasound — PLAPS Point Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Frame 1 (Initial Frame)
- **Predominantly anechoic (black) space** filling the majority of the image
- Bounded superiorly by a pleural line and laterally by rib shadow artifacts
- Classic **Quad Sign** morphology — strongly indicative of **pleural effusion**

### Frames 2–10 (Sequential Frames)
| Feature | Observation |
|---|---|
| **Hepatization** | Central structure with **liver-like, solid echogenicity** — loss of normal aeration |
| **Air Bronchograms** | Multiple **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** within the hepatized tissue |
| **Shred Sign** | **Irregular, jagged deep border** between consolidated and aerated lung |
| **Jellyfish Sign** | Consolidated lung appears to **float and undulate** within surrounding anechoic fluid |

---

## Conclusion

> **PLAPS = `true`**
> **Type = `"both"`**

### Reasoning:
1. **Effusion**: Frame 1 demonstrates a clear **Quad Sign** (anechoic quadrilateral space). The consolidated lung floats within it (**Jellyfish Sign**).
2. **Consolidation**: Frames 2–10 show **hepatization** with **air bronchograms** (bright echogenic foci) and a **shred sign** at the deep border — confirming alveolar consolidation (likely pneumonia with parapneumonic effusion or atelectasis with reactive effusion).
