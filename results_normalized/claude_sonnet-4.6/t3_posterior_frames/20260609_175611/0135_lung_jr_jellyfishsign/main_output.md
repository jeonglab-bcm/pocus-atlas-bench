# Lung Ultrasound PLAPS Point Analysis

## Sequential Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Frames 1–2 (Early):
- **Large anechoic (black) space** occupying most of the field below the pleural line
- Minimal echogenic tissue visible — consistent with **free pleural fluid**
- The quadrilateral shape bounded by the pleural line superiorly and rib shadows laterally → **Quad Sign ✓**

### Frames 3–5 (Mid):
- An **echogenic, heterogeneous tissue mass** begins entering the field from the lateral margin
- The tissue has a **hepatized (liver-like) texture** — loss of normal aeration artifacts
- Irregular, shredded deep border visible → **Shred Sign ✓**

### Frames 6–10 (Late):
- The consolidated lung tissue **floats and undulates** within the surrounding anechoic effusion
- This dynamic movement resembles a jellyfish swimming → **Jellyfish Sign ✓**
- Within the hyperechoic parenchyma, punctate/linear bright foci suggest **Air Bronchograms ✓**
