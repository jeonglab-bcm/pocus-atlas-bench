# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — RT H/T Zone 2 Low (PLAPS Point)

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Finding | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Anechoic space** | A dark, fluid-filled region is visible in multiple frames, bounded superiorly by the pleural line — consistent with **Quad Sign** |
| **Floating echogenic structure** | A rounded, tissue-like echogenic mass appears and disappears cyclically across frames (frames 1, 3, 5, 7–10), oscillating within the anechoic space — classic **Jellyfish Sign** |
| **Hepatization** | The floating structure displays solid, liver-like echotexture with loss of normal aeration artifacts — **tissue-like hepatization** |
| **Air bronchograms** | Hyperechoic punctate/linear foci are visible within the hepatized tissue in later frames — **air bronchograms present** |
| **Respiratory cycling** | The echogenic structure moves in and out of view in synchrony with respiration, confirming dynamic floating within fluid |

---

### Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

**Reasoning:**
- The **anechoic surrounding space** = pleural effusion (Quad sign confirmed)
- The **oscillating echogenic structure** = atelectatic/consolidated lung floating within the effusion (**Jellyfish sign**)
- Internal **hepatization + air bronchograms** confirm co-existing **pulmonary consolidation**

This is a **PLAPS-positive** finding representing a **combined posterior pleural effusion with compressive/absorptive atelectasis/consolidation** of the dependent right lower lobe.
