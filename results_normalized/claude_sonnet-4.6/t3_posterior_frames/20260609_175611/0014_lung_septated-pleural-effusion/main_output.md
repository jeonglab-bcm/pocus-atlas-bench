# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

## Sequential Frame Observations

### Structural Findings

**1. Tissue-like Hepatization**
Across all frames, a large region of the posterior lung zone demonstrates **solid, echogenic parenchyma with liver-like (hepatized) texture** — loss of normal aeration artifacts is consistent throughout the sequence.

**2. Air Bronchograms** ✅
Within the consolidated tissue, **hyperechoic branching/linear foci** are clearly visible — these represent air-filled bronchi surrounded by airless (fluid-filled) alveoli. These structures persist and are reproducible across frames.

**3. Shred Sign** ✅
The **deep border of the consolidated region is irregular and "shredded"** — indicating the transition zone between consolidated and still-aerated lung, rather than a smooth boundary.

**4. Effusion Component**
In several frames, a **hypoechoic/anechoic layer** is visible adjacent to the consolidation, suggesting a **small to moderate parapneumonic or reactive pleural effusion** (partial quad sign).
