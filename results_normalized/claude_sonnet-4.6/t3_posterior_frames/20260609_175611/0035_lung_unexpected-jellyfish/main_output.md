### Frame-by-Frame Observations

| Frames | Findings |
|--------|----------|
| 1–3 | Large **anechoic (black) space** dominates the near/mid field, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by echogenic lung tissue → **Quad sign** strongly present |

### Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

A **large pleural effusion** (quad sign + jellyfish sign) is present alongside **dependent lung consolidation/atelectasis** (hepatization), a classic PLAPS pattern — commonly seen in pneumonia with parapneumonic effusion or heart failure with compressive atelectasis.
