## Sign-by-Sign Assessment

### 🔵 Effusion Signs

**Quad Sign — ✅ PRESENT**
- A clearly demarcated **anechoic (black) space** is visible in the dependent posterior zone across all frames
- Bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by the lung line, forming a characteristic quadrilateral fluid pocket

**Sinusoid Sign — ✅ LIKELY PRESENT (dynamic)**
- Sequential frames demonstrate **cyclical variation** in the depth of the lower (lung) boundary
- The lung line appears to migrate toward the pleural line across the respiratory cycle, consistent with sinusoid movement

**Jellyfish Sign — ✅ PRESENT**
- Several frames (particularly frames 5–9) show **floating echogenic material** within the anechoic fluid
- This material undulates across frames, consistent with **atelectatic lung floating within the effusion**

---

### 🟠 Consolidation Signs

**Tissue-like Hepatization — ✅ PRESENT (partial)**
- The echogenic floating material shows **liver-like texture** in multiple frames
- Suggests dependent compressive atelectasis or early consolidation

**Shred Sign — ⚠️ POSSIBLE**
- The interface between the echogenic tissue and fluid appears irregular in some frames
- Not definitively confirmed without full dynamic review

**Air Bronchograms — ❌ NOT CLEARLY IDENTIFIED**
