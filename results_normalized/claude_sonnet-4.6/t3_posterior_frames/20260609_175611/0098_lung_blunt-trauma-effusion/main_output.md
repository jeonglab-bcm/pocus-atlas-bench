# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### **Frames 1–2**
- Hyperechoic irregular structures with **punctate/linear bright foci** embedded within echogenic tissue
- Suggests **air bronchograms** within hepatized (consolidation) lung
- **Shred sign**: irregular, frayed deep border visible

#### **Frames 3–4**
- More homogeneous echogenic zone with **liver-like texture** (tissue hepatization)
- Loss of normal aeration artifacts (no A-lines)
- Deeper zone becomes relatively hypoechoic/anechoic

#### **Frames 5–6**
- Transition frames: a **rounded echogenic mass** begins to emerge
- Surrounding **anechoic (black) space** becomes more apparent — consistent with free fluid
- Appearance begins to resemble the **Jellyfish sign**

#### **Frames 7–10**
- Clear **rounded/lobulated echogenic structure** surrounded by an **anechoic halo**
- This floating, undulating atelectatic lung within effusion = **Jellyfish sign** ✓
- The anechoic space bounded by pleural and lung lines = **Quad sign** ✓
- Consolidated lung with air bronchograms persists within the floating tissue

### Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

**Rationale:** The images demonstrate **anechoic pleural effusion** (Quad sign, Jellyfish sign with floating atelectatic lung) coexisting with **alveolar consolidation** (hepatization, air bronchograms, shred sign). This is a classic PLAPS-positive finding consistent with a **parapneumonic effusion with adjacent lung consolidation**, most commonly seen in pneumonia or atelectasis with reactive effusion.
