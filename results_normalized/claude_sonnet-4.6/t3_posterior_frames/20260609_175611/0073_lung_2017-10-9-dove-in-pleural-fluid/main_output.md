# 0073_lung_2017-10-9-dove-in-pleural-fluid

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Sequential Frames)

---

### Observations

#### 🔵 Effusion Signs
- **Quad sign**: In several mid-sequence frames (frames 4–6), a **relatively anechoic (dark) quadrilateral space** is visible, bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by a tissue interface — consistent with a **pleural fluid collection**.
- The fluid layer appears **small-to-moderate**, with a clear demarcation from underlying tissue.

#### 🟠 Consolidation Signs
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: Deep to the fluid (and in adjacent areas), the lung parenchyma demonstrates **echogenicity resembling liver texture** — loss of normal aeration artifacts.
- **Air bronchograms**: Scattered **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** are visible within the hepatized tissue across multiple frames, consistent with **air-filled bronchi within consolidated lung**.
- **Shred sign**: The deep border of the consolidated zone is **irregular and fragmented**, indicating the transition between consolidated and partially aerated lung.

---

### Conclusion

| Parameter | Finding |
|-----------|---------|
| **PLAPS** | ✅ `true` |
| **Type** | `"both"` |
| **Effusion** | Small-moderate pleural effusion (Quad sign present) |
| **Consolidation** | Hepatization + air bronchograms + shred sign |

> **Interpretation**: This is a **PLAPS-positive** posterior zone with **combined pleural effusion and underlying lung consolidation** — a pattern consistent with pneumonia with parapneumonic effusion, or atelectasis with associated effusion.
