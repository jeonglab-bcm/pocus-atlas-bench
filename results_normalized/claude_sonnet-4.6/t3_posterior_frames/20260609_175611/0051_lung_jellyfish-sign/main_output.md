# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

# PLAPS Point — Sequential Lung Ultrasound Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Frames 1–3 (Early Sequence)
- A **large anechoic (black) space** dominates the field, consistent with a pleural fluid collection
- **Quad sign present**: The fluid is bounded superiorly by the pleural line, inferiorly by the lung line, and laterally by rib shadows
- A small hyperechoic punctate focus is visible within adjacent echogenic tissue in Frame 1 — suggestive of an **air bronchogram**

### Frames 4–6 (Mid Sequence)
- A curved, echogenic structure is visible **floating and undulating** within the anechoic effusion
- This represents the **Jellyfish sign** — atelectatic lung suspended within surrounding fluid
- The shape of the fluid space shifts subtly across frames, consistent with respiratory cycling (**Sinusoid sign**)

### Frames 7–10 (Late Sequence)
- The probe angle reveals increasingly echogenic, **liver-textured (hepatized)** lung parenchyma adjacent to the fluid
- The deep border of this tissue is irregular — consistent with a **Shred sign**
- Punctate bright foci within the echogenic parenchyma = **Air bronchograms**
