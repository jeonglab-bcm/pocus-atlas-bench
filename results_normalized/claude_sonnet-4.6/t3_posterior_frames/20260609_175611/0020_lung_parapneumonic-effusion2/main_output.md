### Key Findings

#### 🔲 Quad Sign — ✅ PRESENT
A large **anechoic (black) space** is clearly visible, bounded by:
- Pleural line **superiorly**
- Rib shadows **laterally**
- A moving deep border **inferiorly**
→ Classic quadrilateral fluid space = **pleural effusion**

#### 🪼 Jellyfish Sign — ✅ PRESENT
Across sequential frames, a **bright echogenic structure** (consolidated/atelectatic lung) is seen:
- **Floating and undulating** within the anechoic effusion
- Changing shape and position between frames
- Resembling a jellyfish suspended in water

#### 🫁 Tissue-like Hepatization — ✅ PRESENT
The floating structure demonstrates:
- **Liver-like echogenicity** (loss of aeration)
- Solid parenchymal texture = **atelectatic/consolidated lung**
