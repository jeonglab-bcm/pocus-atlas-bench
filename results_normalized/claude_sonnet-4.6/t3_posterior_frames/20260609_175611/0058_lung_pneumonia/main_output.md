# Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Posterior Zone)

## Sequential Frame Observations

### Pleural Interface (Superficial Zone)
- A **bright hyperechoic pleural line** is consistently visible in the upper portion of all frames
- Rib shadows casting posterior acoustic shadowing are present, confirming appropriate probe positioning

### Deep Lung Parenchyma (Frames 2–125)
- The posterior lung tissue appears **solid and heterogeneously echogenic**, with a texture closely resembling liver parenchyma → **hepatization pattern**
- Multiple **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** are distributed throughout the echogenic tissue, consistent with **air bronchograms** (static type)
- The deep border of the echogenic region is **irregular and shredded** → **shred sign**

### Transition Frames (148–225)
- Progressive appearance of a **hypoechoic/anechoic region** in the inferior/dependent portion of the field
- In frames 198–225, a relatively **dark (anechoic) crescent-shaped space** appears below the consolidated tissue, suggesting a **small associated pleural effusion** (partial quad sign morphology)
- No classic jellyfish sign or sinusoid sign clearly identified
