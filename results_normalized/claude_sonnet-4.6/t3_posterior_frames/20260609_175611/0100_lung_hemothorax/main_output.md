# 0100_lung_hemothorax

## PLAPS Point — Sequential Frame Analysis

---

### Observed Findings

#### 🖤 Anechoic Space (Frames 1–6)
- A **large, black (anechoic) quadrilateral space** is clearly visible in the early frames, bounded by:
  - The **pleural line** superiorly
  - The **lung line** inferiorly
  - **Rib shadows** laterally
- This constitutes a **positive Quad Sign** → **Pleural effusion confirmed**

#### 🪼 Floating Echogenic Structure (Frames 4–7)
- Within the anechoic fluid, an **undulating, echogenic mass** appears to float and shift position across frames
- Its shape and dynamic movement are consistent with **atelectatic lung suspended in fluid**
- This constitutes a **positive Jellyfish Sign**

#### 🫁 Hepatized Lung (Frames 7–10)
- The lower field progressively fills with **tissue-like, liver-textured echogenicity**
- The deep margin appears **irregular and shredded** → **Shred Sign**
- Scattered **punctate/linear hyperechoic foci** within the echogenic area → **Air Bronchograms**
