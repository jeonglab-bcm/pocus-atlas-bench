# Lung Ultrasound PLAPS Analysis — Sequential Frame Review

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Frames 1–3 (Early)
- A **curved bright line** (diaphragm/pleural line) is visible in the mid-field
- **Above this**: a relatively **anechoic (black) space** bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferiorly by a deeper reflective boundary
- Classic **Quad Sign geometry** is forming — rectangular anechoic collection

### Frames 4–6 (Mid-sequence)
- The anechoic space **persists and expands** slightly, consistent with free pleural fluid
- Respiratory variation in the depth of the deep boundary suggests a **Sinusoid Sign**
- No A-lines are visible — normal aeration artifacts are absent

### Frames 7–10 (Late)
- A **rounded, lobular echogenic structure** emerges within the anechoic effusion
- This structure has **liver-like (hepatized) texture** — consistent with **consolidation/atelectasis**
- The structure appears to **undulate within the fluid** → **Jellyfish Sign** ✓
- Hyperechoic punctate foci within the echogenic mass suggest **Air Bronchograms**
