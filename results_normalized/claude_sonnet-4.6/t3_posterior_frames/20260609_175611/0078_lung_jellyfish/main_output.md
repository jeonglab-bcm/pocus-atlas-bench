# 0078_lung_jellyfish

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — Right Posterior Zone (PLAPS Point)

---

### Frame-by-Frame Observations

#### **Frames 1–4 (Early Sequence)**
- A **large anechoic (black) space** is visible in the dependent posterior zone
- The pleural line is identifiable superiorly; the deep boundary is relatively flat
- This anechoic region forms the **Quad Sign** — bounded by the pleural line above, rib shadows laterally, and a deeper lung line
- No significant internal echoes in this phase → **free pleural fluid**

#### **Frames 5–7 (Mid Sequence)**
- An **echogenic, irregular structure begins to appear** rising from the deep portion of the anechoic fluid
- The structure is **buoyant and undulating** within the surrounding fluid
- This is the classic **Jellyfish Sign** — atelectatic/consolidated lung floating within pleural effusion

#### **Frames 8–10 (Late Sequence)**
- The echogenic structure becomes **fully visible and prominent**
- It demonstrates **liver-like (hepatized) echotexture** — solid, homogeneous, similar to hepatic parenchyma
- Scattered **hyperechoic foci** within the mass consistent with **air bronchograms**
- The deep border shows an **irregular, shredded interface** — the **Shred Sign** — between consolidated and residually aerated lung

### Conclusion

```
plaps = true
type  = "both"
```

**Interpretation:** This is a **PLAPS-positive** study demonstrating a **moderate-to-large right pleural effusion** with **compressive/atelectatic consolidation** of the posterior lung. The sequential frames capture the dynamic **Jellyfish Sign**, with the consolidated lung visibly floating and undulating within the effusion. Additional features of true consolidation (hepatization, air bronchograms, shred sign) confirm parenchymal involvement beyond simple compressive atelectasis — suggesting this may represent **pneumonia with parapneumonic effusion**, or **compressive atelectasis with reactive effusion** in the appropriate clinical context.
