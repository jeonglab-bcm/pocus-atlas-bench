# Lung Ultrasound — PLAPS Point Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Pleural Interface
- A **bright hyperechoic pleural line** is consistently visible in the near-field across all frames.
- Rib shadows provide lateral boundaries in multiple frames, forming the lateral walls of a potential quadrilateral space.

### Anechoic (Fluid) Region
- Several frames (especially frames 1–2, 4, 7–9) demonstrate a **dark/anechoic space** between the pleural line superiorly and a deeper echogenic structure inferiorly, bounded laterally by rib shadows.
- This geometry is consistent with a **Quad Sign**, indicating **pleural effusion**.

### Deep Echogenic Tissue
- Frames 6 and 10 most clearly show **tissue-like, hepatized echogenicity** in the dependent posterior lung — the parenchyma loses normal aeration artifacts and takes on a solid, liver-like texture.
- Small **punctate and linear hyperechoic foci** are visible within this echogenic tissue in frame 6, consistent with **air bronchograms**.
- The deep margin of the consolidated tissue has an **irregular, shredded border** in several frames, consistent with a **Shred Sign** (interface between consolidated and aerated lung).
