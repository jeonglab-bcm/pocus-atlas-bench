# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis — PLAPS Point (Sequential Frames)

---

### Systematic Observations

#### 🔲 Pleural/Chest Wall Layer (Near Field)
- Hyperechoic rib shadows are visible bilaterally at the top of each frame
- The **pleural line** is clearly identified superficially
- Normal soft tissue layers visible above

---

#### 🌑 Anechoic Space (Mid-to-Deep Field)
Across **all 10 frames**, a **large, anechoic (black) fluid collection** occupies a substantial portion of the image:
- Bounded **superiorly** by the pleural line
- Bounded **laterally** by rib acoustic shadows
- Forms a clear **quadrilateral/triangular space**
- ✅ **→ Quad Sign: PRESENT** — confirms **pleural effusion**

---

#### 🫁 Deep Structure (Lung Tissue)
Within and adjacent to the anechoic fluid, there is a **hyperechoic, solid-appearing structure** with:

| Feature | Observation |
|---|---|
| **Texture** | Liver-like (hepatized), echogenic, loss of aeration artifacts |
| **Shape** | Rounded/lobular, irregular deep border |
| **Position** | Partially surrounded by anechoic fluid |
| **Motion across frames** | Subtle floating/undulating movement relative to fluid |
| **Internal foci** | Punctate hyperechoic spots consistent with **air bronchograms** |
| **Deep border** | Irregular, ragged interface with deeper aerated lung |

✅ **→ Hepatization: PRESENT** — consolidated lung parenchyma  
✅ **→ Jellyfish Sign: PRESENT** — consolidated lung floating within effusion  
✅ **→ Shred Sign: PRESENT** — irregular consolidated-aerated lung interface  
✅ **→ Air Bronchograms: PRESENT** — hyperechoic foci within consolidated tissue  

---

### Progressive Frame Analysis
| Frame Sequence | Key Change |
|---|---|
| Frames 1–3 | Effusion dominates; consolidated tissue barely visible deep |
| Frames 4–6 | Consolidated lung migrates superiorly into view; Jellyfish pattern emerging |
| Frames 7–10 | Full hepatized lobe visible; maximal Jellyfish sign; air bronchograms more apparent |

This progressive appearance is consistent with **respiratory cycling** — the consolidated lung rises and falls within the effusion (Jellyfish sign dynamics).

---

### 🏁 Conclusion

```
plaps = true
type  = "both"
```

**Findings**: Large **posterior pleural effusion** (Quad sign) with **compressive/obstructive atelectasis and/or consolidation** (hepatization, Jellyfish sign, Shred sign, air bronchograms) of the adjacent dependent lung lobe.

> **Clinical Correlation**: This pattern at the PLAPS point is highly characteristic of a **moderate-to-large parapneumonic effusion or transudative/exudative effusion** with associated **lobar consolidation** (e.g., pneumonia with parapneumonic effusion, malignant effusion with atelectasis, or heart failure with dependent consolidation). Further clinical history and additional imaging zones are recommended.
