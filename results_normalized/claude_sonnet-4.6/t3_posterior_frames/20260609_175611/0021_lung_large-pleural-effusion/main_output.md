## Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

**Reasoning:**
- A **moderate pleural effusion** is identified (quad sign, jellyfish sign, likely sinusoid sign)
- **Underlying lung consolidation/atelectasis** is present, evidenced by hepatization and air bronchograms
- The consolidated lung floats within the effusion, producing the classic **jellyfish appearance** across sequential respiratory frames

This pattern is consistent with **PLAPS-positive** posterior lung pathology — likely **parapneumonic effusion with associated compressive/obstructive atelectasis**, or consolidating pneumonia with reactive pleural effusion.
