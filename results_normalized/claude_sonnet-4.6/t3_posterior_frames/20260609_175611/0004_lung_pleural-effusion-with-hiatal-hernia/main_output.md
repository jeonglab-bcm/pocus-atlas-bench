# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

# Lung Ultrasound — PLAPS Point Analysis

## Frame-by-Frame Observations

### Near-Field / Pleural Interface
- A pleural line is identifiable in the upper portion of the fan
- No normal A-line reverberation artifacts are present in the deeper field — this alone indicates abnormality

---

### Central Finding: Hepatized Lung Mass
Across **all 10 sequential frames**, a large, **solid-appearing, heterogeneous structure** occupies the central-to-left mid-field:

| Sign | Observation |
|------|-------------|
| **Hepatization** | Parenchyma echogenicity mirrors liver texture — loss of aeration artifacts, solid tissue-like appearance |
| **Air bronchograms** | Scattered punctate and linear hyperechoic foci visible within the consolidated tissue (frames 4–9 especially) |
| **Shred sign** | Irregular, non-linear deep border between consolidated and residually aerated lung |
| **Respiratory mobility** | The consolidated mass shifts slightly across frames, confirming it is intrathoracic lung tissue |

---

### Right-Lateral Finding: Effusion Evidence
- A **bright, wedge-shaped/triangular hyperechoic structure** on the right (likely the **diaphragm**) is present throughout
- In several frames (3, 4, 8, 9), a **hypoechoic/anechoic space** is visible between the consolidated lung and this structure — consistent with **pleural fluid**
- In frames 5–7, the consolidated lung appears **rounded and partially floating**, suggesting a **Jellyfish sign** — atelectatic lung bobbing within surrounding effusion

---

## Dynamic Assessment Across Frames

```
Frames 1–2:  Consolidation dominant; minimal fluid visible
Frames 3–4:  Triangular anechoic space emerges laterally → Quad sign elements
Frames 5–7:  Mass appears more rounded, partially suspended → Jellyfish sign
Frames 8–10: Effusion space again visible; consolidation borders irregular (shred sign)
```

---

## Conclusion

> **plaps = `true`**
> **type = `"both"`**

**Dominant finding**: Posterior lung **consolidation** (hepatization + air bronchograms + shred sign), consistent with **lobar/segmental pneumonia or atelectasis**

**Secondary finding**: **Pleural effusion** surrounding the consolidated lobe (anechoic space, jellyfish sign), typical of a **parapneumonic effusion**

This constitutes a **positive PLAPS** — posterior alveolar consolidation with associated pleural fluid collection.
