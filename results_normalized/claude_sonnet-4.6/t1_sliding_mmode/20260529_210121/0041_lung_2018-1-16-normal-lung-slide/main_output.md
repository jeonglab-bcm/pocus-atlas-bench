## Step 2 — Overall Decision

There is a **clear spatial transition**: positions p00–p08 (left-to-right) all demonstrate seashore pattern (sliding present), while p09 (rightmost position) demonstrates a definitive stratosphere/barcode pattern (sliding absent). This spatial coexistence of both patterns across lateral positions is the hallmark of a **lung point**.

> **Overall Label: `BOTH` — Lung Point Identified**
>
> Pleural sliding is present across most of the scanned region (p00–p08) and absent at the rightmost lateral position (p09), indicating the lung point lies near the right edge of this ultrasound field.
