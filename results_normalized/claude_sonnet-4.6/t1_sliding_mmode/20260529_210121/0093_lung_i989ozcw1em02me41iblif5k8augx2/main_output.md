**Classification: SEASHORE**

### p09 (Position 10)

## Step 2 — Overall Decision

**Spatial pattern observed:**
- **p00–p04 → STRATOSPHERE** (no pleural sliding on the left)
- **p05 → ALTERNATING** (transitional lung point)
- **p06–p09 → SEASHORE** (pleural sliding present on the right)

There is both an explicitly **alternating position (p05)** AND a **clear spatial transition** from stratosphere to seashore across the probe positions.

> ## ✅ Overall Label: **"BOTH" — LUNG POINT IDENTIFIED**
> Pleural sliding is **absent** at the left lateral positions and **present** at the right lateral positions, with the lung point located approximately at **p05–p06**. This confirms a **lung point sign**, which is pathognomonic for **pneumothorax**.
