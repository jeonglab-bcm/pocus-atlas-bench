## Step 2 — Overall Decision

There is a **clear spatial transition**: positions p01–p08 all demonstrate seashore (pleural sliding **present**), while p09 at the rightmost lateral position demonstrates stratosphere (pleural sliding **absent**). This coexistence of both patterns across different spatial positions is the definition of a **lung point**.

> ## ✅ Overall Label: **BOTH** (Lung Point Identified)
> Pleural sliding is present medially (p01–p08) and absent at the far lateral position (p09), indicating a lung point near the right edge of the scanned region.
