## Step 2 — Overall Decision

There is a **clear spatial transition** across the active ultrasound field:
- Positions **p01 through p08** (left to right-center): **Seashore** — pleural sliding present
- Position **p09** (rightmost): **Stratosphere/Barcode** — pleural sliding absent

This coexistence of sliding (seashore) and non-sliding (stratosphere) zones at different lateral positions is the definition of a **lung point**.

### ➤ Overall Label: **"BOTH" — Lung Point Identified**

The lung point lies at the lateral transition between p08 and p09, marking the boundary between aerated and non-aerated lung.
