### p08
**Observations:** Upper zone shows classic parallel A-line pattern. Below the pleural line there is a wide hypoechoic/dark band (likely consolidation or deep shadowing), then further below faint horizontal echoes reappear. Despite the dark band, the upper zone pattern is characteristic of lung sliding and the image structure does not fulfill barcode criteria (no dense horizontal lines throughout).
**Classification: Seashore**

---

### p09
**Observations:** Strikingly different from all prior images. Multiple very bright, evenly-spaced, full-width horizontal white bands span the entire image from top to bottom. Between these bright lines: completely dark bands with **zero granular texture** — no sandy background anywhere. The pleural line is not identifiable as distinct from the other lines (all look identical), consistent with a stationary, non-sliding pleura generating only pure reverberations. This image is not "nearly black" (it has maximal signal), so it is classifiable.
**Classification: Stratosphere/Barcode**
