## Step 2 — Overall Decision

There is a **clear spatial transition** across the ultrasound field:
- **Positions p00–p04** → Seashore (pleural sliding PRESENT)
- **Positions p05–p09** → Stratosphere/Barcode (pleural sliding ABSENT)

This spatial coexistence of seashore and stratosphere patterns across adjacent lateral positions is the hallmark of a **lung point** — the anatomical boundary between an apposed (sliding) lung region and a pneumothorax (or adhesion) zone.

> ### ✅ Overall Label: **BOTH (Lung Point)**
> Pleural sliding is **present** on the left side of the scan field and **absent** on the right side, indicating a **lung point** at approximately the p04–p05 transition zone.
