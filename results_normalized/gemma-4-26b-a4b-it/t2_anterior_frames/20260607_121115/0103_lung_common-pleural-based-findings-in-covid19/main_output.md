# 0103_lung_common-pleural-based-findings-in-covid19

The lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip of the anterior lung zone shows the following:

**B-lines Assessment:**
Throughout the frames, discrete, hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines/lung rockets) are visible, arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are well-spaced and do not coalesce into a continuous white sheet, which is characteristic of a **septal** pattern. This indicates thickened interlobular septa.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the visualized area. The lung parenchyma does not exhibit a tissue-like hepatized appearance, a shred sign, or internal air bronchograms. The presence of B-lines indicates that the lung remains aerated.
