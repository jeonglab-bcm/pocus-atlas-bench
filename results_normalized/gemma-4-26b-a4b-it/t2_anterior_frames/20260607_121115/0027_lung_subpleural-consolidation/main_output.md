# 0027_lung_subpleural-consolidation

The lung ultrasound video clip shows an anterior view of the right hemithorax (Zone 1).

**B-lines Assessment:**
Throughout the video clip, there are no hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line that extend to the bottom of the screen. Therefore, B-lines are absent.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The ultrasound image shows a large, anechoic (dark) oval structure in the anterior region, which is consistent with a large blood vessel. There is no evidence of lung tissue appearing solid and echogenic (hepatization), no irregular "shred sign" at the border of aerated and non-aerated lung, and no punctate or linear hyperechoic air bronchograms within the parenchyma. Therefore, consolidation is absent.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **consolidation = false**
