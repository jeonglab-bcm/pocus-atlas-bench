# 0043_lung_normal-lung

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip of the anterior lung zone:

**Observation:**
The ultrasound shows multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are confluent and coalescing, merging into a diffuse, bright, "white" appearance that obscures the normal horizontal A-line pattern. This represents a ground-glass pattern, which is indicative of alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial lung disease. There is no evidence of consolidation, such as hepatization, a shred sign, or air bronchograms; the lung appears aerated, albeit with significant interstitial/alveolar fluid.
