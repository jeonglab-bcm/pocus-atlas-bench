# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

The video clip shows a lung ultrasound scan of an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
In the initial frames (frames 1-5), there are numerous, dense, and coalescing hyperechoic vertical artifacts that extend from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts merge into a bright, almost uniform appearance, which is characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern. This indicates diffuse alveolar or interstitial involvement.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
In the later frames (frames 6-10), a large, hypoechoic/anechoic area is visible, representing non-aerated lung. The border between this non-aerated area and the aerated lung (the bright, artifact-filled region) is irregular and "jagged," which is the characteristic **shred sign**. This finding is indicative of alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **B-line subtype**: ground_glass
- **consolidation**: true
- **consolidation_type**: "shred_sign"
