# 0138_lung_jr_clines

The provided video clip shows a lung ultrasound of an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
Upon analyzing the video, no B-lines (vertical, hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen) are observed. The aerated portions of the lung primarily show horizontal A-line reverberation artifacts. Therefore, **lung_rockets = false**.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The ultrasound shows a large area of acoustic shadowing, which is a dark region where the ultrasound signal is blocked. This shadowing is caused by a dense area just below the pleural line, which is characteristic of a consolidation. The interface between the aerated lung (showing A-lines) and this consolidated/shadowing region is irregular and jagged, which is consistent with the **shred sign**. There is no clear evidence of tissue-like hepatization or distinct air bronchograms visible due to the prominent acoustic shadowing. Therefore, **consolidation = true** and the predominant pattern is the **shred_sign**.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = false**
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "shred_sign"**
