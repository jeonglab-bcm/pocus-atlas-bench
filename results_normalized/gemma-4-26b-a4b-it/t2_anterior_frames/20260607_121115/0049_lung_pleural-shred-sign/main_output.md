# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip of the anterior lung zone:

**Observation:**
The ultrasound image reveals a bright, horizontal pleural line. Extending from this pleural line, there are intense, hyperechoic vertical artifacts that coalesce and merge into a diffuse, bright white sheet. This pattern obscures the normal horizontal A-line reverberations and gives the appearance of a "white lung." There is no evidence of tissue-like consolidation, such as hepatization, a shred sign, or air bronchograms; the bright area is due to confluent B-line artifacts rather than solid lung tissue.
