# 0046_lung_light-beam-sign-covid19

The video clip shows an ultrasound of an anterior lung zone.

## B-lines Assessment
Upon analyzing the frames, there are prominent vertical, hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen, which are characteristic of **B-lines (lung rockets)**. As the video progresses, these B-lines become highly confluent and coalesce, forming a diffuse, bright, and white appearance that obscures the normal lung architecture. This pattern is diagnostic of **ground-glass** B-lines, which typically indicates diffuse interstitial or alveolar edema.

## Consolidation Assessment
The lung parenchyma does not exhibit signs of **consolidation**. There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization (liver-like appearance), a shredded border sign, or the presence of air bronchograms (punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within a solid-looking area). The predominant finding is the artifactual pattern caused by coalescing B-lines rather than a solid, aerated-loss tissue mass.

**Conclusion:**
- **B-lines:** Present; subtype is **ground-glass**.
- **Consolidation:** Absent.
