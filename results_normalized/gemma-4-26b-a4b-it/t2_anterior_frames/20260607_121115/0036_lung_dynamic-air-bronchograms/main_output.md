# 0036_lung_dynamic-air-bronchograms

The lung ultrasound video clip shows the following findings:

**B-lines Assessment**
The ultrasound image displays numerous hyperechoic, vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are highly confluent and coalesce to form a diffuse, bright, "white" appearance, which is characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern. This pattern is indicative of diffuse interstitial or alveolar disease, such as pulmonary edema.

**Consolidation Assessment**
In the deeper portion of the ultrasound image, there is an area of lung parenchyma that appears solid and echogenic, with a texture highly resembling liver tissue. This loss of normal aeration and the appearance of tissue-like echogenicity is characteristic of **hepatization**, a form of alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusions**
* **lung_rockets:** true
* **subtype:** ground_glass
* **consolidation:** true
* **consolidation_type:** hepatization
