# 0031_lung_pneumoniashred-sign

The provided ultrasound images show sequential frames of the right lung's anterior zone.

**Frame-by-frame analysis:**
In all frames (1-7), there is a high density of hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are so numerous and coalescent that they merge into a diffuse, bright, white field, which completely obscures the horizontal A-line reverberation artifacts. This confluent pattern gives the appearance of a "white sheet" or "ground glass." There is no evidence of consolidated tissue-like (hepatized) lung, nor are there signs of a shredded border (shred sign) or air bronchograms within the lung parenchyma.

**Conclusions:**
- **B-lines Assessment:** B-lines are present (`lung_rockets = true`) and follow a **ground_glass** pattern, characterized by coalescing, confluent vertical artifacts that obscure A-lines.
- **Consolidation Assessment:** Consolidation is absent (`consolidation = false`).
