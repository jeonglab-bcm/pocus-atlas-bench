## B-lines Assessment
The video shows numerous hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading. These artifacts move in synchrony with the pleural line, confirming they are **B-lines**. These B-lines are very dense and frequently coalesce, creating a diffuse, hazy, and bright white appearance that obscures the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines, which typically indicates diffuse alveolar or interstitial disease.

## Consolidation Assessment
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the scanned area. No tissue-like hepatized lung, shred sign, or air bronchograms are visible. The lung parenchyma is dominated by the diffuse ground-glass B-line pattern rather than solid-appearing tissue.
