# 0017_lung_lung-point2

The ultrasound video clip shows an anterior lung zone scan.

**B-lines Assessment**
The B-mode image (top) reveals multiple, hyperechoic vertical artifacts, known as B-lines, arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are numerous and appear to coalesce, creating a diffuse, bright, and somewhat uniform "white" appearance of the lung parenchyma, which obscures the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass B-lines**, indicating diffuse interstitial or alveolar involvement. The M-mode image (bottom) confirms these findings with dense vertical striations throughout the captured depth.

**Consolidation Assessment**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the imaged area. The lung parenchyma, while appearing bright due to the presence of ground-glass B-lines, does not exhibit tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign, or internal air bronchograms.

**Conclusions**
*   **B-lines**: present (subtype: **ground_glass**)
*   **Consolidation**: absent (**consolidation = false**)
