# 0026_lung_pneumonia-b-lines

The ultrasound video displays an anterior view of the left hemithorax (Zone 2). 

Upon examination of the sequential frames, multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts are visible, originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are highly confluent and coalesce, creating a diffuse, bright, white pattern that obscures the underlying lung parenchyma and A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of ground-glass B-lines, which often indicate interstitial syndrome or alveolar edema.

There are no signs of lung consolidation in this view. I do not observe any tissue-like hepatization, irregular "shred signs," or air bronchograms within the lung parenchyma.

**Conclusions:**
* **B-lines:** Present (`lung_rockets = true`), with a `ground_glass` subtype.
* **Consolidation:** Absent (`consolidation = false`).
