# 0101_lung_the-light-beam-artifact-in-covid19

The provided lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip shows an anterior lung zone view.

**B-lines Assessment:**
Throughout the sequence of frames, multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts, known as B-lines (lung rockets), are clearly visible. These artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen. The B-lines are very dense and appear to coalesce, merging into a diffuse, bright white area that obscures the normal horizontal A-lines. This confluent pattern is characteristic of the **ground-glass** subtype of B-lines, which is often associated with diffuse interstitial or alveolar disease.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the visualized lung zone. The lung parenchyma does not exhibit a tissue-like (hepatized) appearance, nor are there any visible shred signs or air bronchograms. The predominant finding is the presence of extensive, coalescing B-lines.

**Conclusion:**
- **B-lines:** Present (subtype: **ground_glass**)
- **Consolidation:** Absent
