# 0122_lung_jr_pna

The sequential ultrasound frames show the following findings in the anterior lung zone:

**B-lines Assessment**
Vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (lung rockets) are clearly visible, originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the image. As the sequence progresses, these B-lines become increasingly dense and coalesce, creating a diffuse, bright, and hazy appearance that obscures the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of a **ground-glass** subtype of B-lines, indicating diffuse interstitial or alveolar involvement.

**Consolidation Assessment**
There is no evidence of lung consolidation in the visualized area. The parenchyma does not exhibit a tissue-like (hepatized) appearance, there is no irregular "shred sign" at the lung border, and no air bronchograms are seen within a solid-appearing area. The lung remains aerated, albeit with significant B-line artifacts.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **subtype**: ground_glass
- **consolidation**: false
- **consolidation_type**: null
