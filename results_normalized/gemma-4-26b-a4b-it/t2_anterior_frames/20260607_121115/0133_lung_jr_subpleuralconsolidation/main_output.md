# 0133_lung_jr_subpleuralconsolidation

The sequential frames from this lung ultrasound (LUS) of the right upper lung zone reveal the following:

## B-lines Assessment
**B-lines are present** and exhibit a **ground-glass** subtype. There are numerous, highly confluent hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line that merge into a diffuse, bright, white appearance, obscuring the normal horizontal A-lines. This pattern is indicative of significant interstitial or alveolar-space involvement.

## Consolidation Assessment
**Consolidation is present** in the visualized area. The ultrasound shows a large area of lung parenchyma with a tissue-like, solid appearance. 
* **Shred sign**: There is a very prominent, irregular, and "shredded" deep border between the consolidated lung and the adjacent aerated/artifact-heavy lung area.
* **Air bronchograms**: Within the consolidated area, small, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible, representing air trapped within the bronchi.

**Conclusions:**
* **lung_rockets**: true
* **subtype**: ground_glass
* **consolidation**: true
* **consolidation_type**: shred_sign (with visible air bronchograms)
