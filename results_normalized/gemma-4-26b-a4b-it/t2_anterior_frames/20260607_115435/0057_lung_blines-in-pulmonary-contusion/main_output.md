# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

The video shows a sequence of lung ultrasound images of an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
In the first five frames, there is a high density of hyperechoic vertical artifacts that are so numerous they merge into a diffuse, bright white sheet, obscuring the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines, indicating diffuse interstitial or alveolar disease. In the final two frames, the ultrasound signal is lost in a large area, and no B-lines are visible in that region.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
Throughout the video, there is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign, or air bronchograms. The bright, confluent pattern observed is better characterized as ground-glass B-lines rather than solid alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusions:**
- **B-lines:** present (subtype: **ground_glass**)
- **Consolidation:** absent (**false**)
