# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound video clip of the left anterior lung zone:

### B-lines Assessment
The ultrasound images show multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading. These artifacts are characteristic of B-lines. These B-lines are very numerous and appear to coalesce, creating a diffuse, bright appearance in the lung parenchyma. This pattern is consistent with **ground-glass B-lines**, which typically indicate alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial disease.

### Consolidation Assessment
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the imaged area. I do not observe any tissue-like hepatized lung, any irregular "shred sign" at a consolidated border, or any internal air bronchograms. The subpleural area is dominated by B-line artifacts rather than solid-appearing tissue.

### Conclusions
* **B-lines:** Present (subtype: **ground-glass**)
* **Consolidation:** Absent (**false**)
