# 0005_lung_subpleural-consolidation-with-shred-sign

The lung ultrasound video clip shows the following findings:

**B-lines Assessment:**
There are prominent vertical, hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts appear to coalesce in several areas, creating a diffuse bright appearance that partially obscures the underlying lung structure. This is characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern of B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
A clear area of **consolidation** is visible, particularly on the left side of the image. This region exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma appears solid and has an echogenicity similar to liver tissue. Within this consolidated area, small, hyperechoic punctate foci are visible, which are characteristic of **air bronchograms**. Additionally, the border between this consolidated lung and the adjacent aerated lung is irregular, demonstrating the **shred sign**.

**Conclusions:**
* **B-lines:** present (ground_glass)
* **Consolidation:** present (air_bronchogram)
