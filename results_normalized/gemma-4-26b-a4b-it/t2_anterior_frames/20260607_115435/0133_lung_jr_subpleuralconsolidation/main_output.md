# 0133_lung_jr_subpleuralconsolidation

The video clip shows an ultrasound of the right upper anterior lung zone.

### B-lines Assessment
The ultrasound shows multiple, confluent, hyperechoic vertical artifacts that arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts merge together to form a diffuse, bright white sheet, obscuring the underlying A-lines and lung parenchyma. This appearance is characteristic of **ground-glass B-lines**.

* **lung_rockets**: true
* **subtype**: ground_glass

### Consolidation Assessment
A significant area of the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, mimicking the texture of liver tissue, which is known as **hepatization**. Within this hepatized lung, small, bright, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible, representing **air bronchograms**. Furthermore, the border between the consolidated area and the adjacent lung is irregular and "shredded," demonstrating the **shred sign**.

* **consolidation**: true
* **consolidation_type**: "hepatization"
