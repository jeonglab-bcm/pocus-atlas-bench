These artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. The B-lines are numerous and appear to coalesce, creating a confluent, bright, and uniform hyperechoic area that obscures the normal A-line pattern. This pattern is characteristic of a **ground-glass** subtype of B-lines, which is often associated with diffuse interstitial or alveolar processes like pulmonary edema or pneumonia.
