# 0131_lung_jr_bronchiolitis

The sequential ultrasound frames of the anterior lung zone demonstrate the following:

*   **B-lines Assessment**: Vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) are clearly visible arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. In the earlier frames, these B-lines appear somewhat discrete and well-spaced. As the sequence progresses (especially in frames 5 through 7), these artifacts coalesce and merge into a confluent, bright white appearance that obscures the horizontal A-line artifacts. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass B-lines**.
*   **Consolidation Assessment**: No signs of alveolar consolidation are identified. There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatized lung, a shred sign at the lung border, or internal air bronchograms. The findings are consistent with aerated lung with significant interstitial/alveolar fluid.

**Conclusions:**
*   **B-lines**: present (**lung_rockets = true**); subtype is **ground_glass**.
*   **Consolidation**: absent (**consolidation = false**).
