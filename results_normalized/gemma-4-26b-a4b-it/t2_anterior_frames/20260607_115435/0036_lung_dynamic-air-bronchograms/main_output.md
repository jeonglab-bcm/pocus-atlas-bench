# 0036_lung_dynamic-air-bronchograms

The lung ultrasound video clip shows the following findings:

**B-lines Assessment**
The ultrasound image displays numerous hyperechoic vertical artifacts that originate from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are highly confluent and coalescent, merging into a diffuse, bright white area that obscures the typical horizontal A-line pattern. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines, indicating diffuse interstitial or alveolar involvement.

**Consolidation Assessment**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the visualized anterior zone. The lung parenchyma does not exhibit a tissue-like (hepatized) appearance, there is no irregular "shred sign" at the lung border, and no air bronchograms (punctate or linear hyperechoic foci) are visible within a consolidated region.

**Conclusions**
- **B-lines present**: true
- **B-line subtype**: ground_glass
- **Consolidation present**: false
- **Consolidation type**: null
