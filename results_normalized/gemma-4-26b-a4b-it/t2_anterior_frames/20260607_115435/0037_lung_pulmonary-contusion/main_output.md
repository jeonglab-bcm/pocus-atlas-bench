# 0037_lung_pulmonary-contusion

The video clip shows a lung ultrasound of an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
The images demonstrate numerous, confluent, hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are coalesced, creating a diffuse, bright, "white-out" appearance that obscures the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground glass** B-lines, which are often seen in conditions like alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial disease.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in this view. I do not observe tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign at the lung border, or air bronchograms within the lung parenchyma. The lung appears aerated, although with significant interstitial/alveolar fluid represented by the confluent B-lines.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **subtype**: ground_glass
- **consolidation**: false
