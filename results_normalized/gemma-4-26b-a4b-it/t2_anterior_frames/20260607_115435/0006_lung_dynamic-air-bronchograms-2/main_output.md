# 0006_lung_dynamic-air-bronchograms-2

The lung ultrasound video clip shows an anterior lung zone view.

**B-lines Assessment**
In the image, multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts can be seen originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the scanning field without fading. These are characteristic of B-lines (lung rockets). These B-lines appear as discrete, well-spaced vertical lines with visible dark lung parenchyma between them, which is consistent with the **septal** subtype.

**Consolidation Assessment**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in this view. The lung parenchyma shows normal aeration characterized by the presence of pleural sliding (implied) and B-line artifacts, without any signs of tissue-like hepatization, a shredded border, or air bronchograms.

**Conclusions**
- **B-lines**: present (**septal**)
- **Consolidation**: absent
