# 0034_lung_covid19-pneumonia

The video shows a lung ultrasound of an anterior lung zone.

**Observations:**

*   **B-lines Assessment:** The video demonstrates numerous, confluent, hyperechoic vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts merge together, creating a diffuse, bright appearance that obscures the normal horizontal A-line pattern. This pattern is characteristic of **ground glass** B-lines.
*   **Consolidation Assessment:** There is no evidence of lung tissue appearing solid or liver-like (hepatization), nor is there a visible "shred sign" or internal air bronchograms in this view. The lung parenchyma, while appearing very bright due to the confluent B-lines, does not show the characteristic features of alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusions:**

*   **lung_rockets:** true
*   **B-line subtype:** ground_glass
*   **consolidation:** false
