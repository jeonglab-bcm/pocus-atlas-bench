# 0148_lung_jr_pediatric-consolidation

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames of the anterior lung zone, here is the analysis:

### B-lines Assessment
The images demonstrate numerous hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line. These artifacts are very dense and coalesce into a diffuse, bright white sheet, which obscures the normal A-line pattern. This appearance is characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern.
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **subtype = ground_glass**

### Consolidation Assessment
There is clear evidence of alveolar consolidation in the visualized area. The lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, with a texture resembling liver tissue (**hepatization**). The border between the aerated (ground-glass) lung and the consolidated region is irregular and "shredded" (**shred sign**). Additionally, small, bright hyperechoic spots are visible within the consolidated tissue, which are indicative of **air bronchograms**.
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "hepatization"**

### Conclusion
The lung ultrasound shows a **ground-glass pattern** of B-lines (confluent B-lines) and a prominent area of **hepatized consolidation** characterized by a shredded border and air bronchograms.
