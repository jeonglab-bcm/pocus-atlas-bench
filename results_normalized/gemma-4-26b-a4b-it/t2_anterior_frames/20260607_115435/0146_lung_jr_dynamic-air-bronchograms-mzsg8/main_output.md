# 0146_lung_jr_dynamic-air-bronchograms-mzsg8

The lung ultrasound video shows an anterior view of the lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment**
The video demonstrates multiple hyperechoic, vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts move synchronously with the pleural line, indicating lung sliding. The B-lines are very numerous and appear to coalesce, creating a diffuse, bright appearance that obscures the A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines, which is often associated with diffuse interstitial or alveolar processes.

**Consolidation Assessment**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the visualized area. There is no tissue-like hepatized lung, no irregular "shred sign" at the border of aerated and non-aerated lung, and no punctate or linear air bronchograms within a solid-appearing lung.

**Conclusions**

- **B-line subtype**: ground_glass
- **consolidation**: false
