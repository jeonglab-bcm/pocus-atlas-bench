# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

The video clip shows a lung ultrasound of the left anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
Vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) are clearly visible arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are numerous and appear to coalesce, forming a diffuse, bright white appearance that obscures the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground glass** B-lines, which can indicate conditions such as pulmonary edema or diffuse interstitial disease.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung parenchyma does not show tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign, or any internal air bronchograms. The lung appears to be aerated, albeit with interstitial involvement as evidenced by the B-lines.

**Conclusions:**
- **B-lines:** Present (ground glass subtype)
- **Consolidation:** Absent
