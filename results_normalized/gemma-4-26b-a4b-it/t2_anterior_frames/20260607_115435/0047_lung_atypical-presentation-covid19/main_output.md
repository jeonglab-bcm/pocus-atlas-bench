# 0047_lung_atypical-presentation-covid19

The sequential frames of this lung ultrasound (LUS) image of an anterior lung zone show the following:

* **B-lines Assessment:** There are numerous hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are confluent and coalesce to create a diffuse, bright white appearance that obscures the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines, which typically indicates alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial disease.
* **Consolidation Assessment:** There is no evidence of lung consolidation. The lung parenchyma does not show tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign, or air bronchograms.

**Conclusions:**
- **B-lines present:** true
- **B-line subtype:** ground_glass
- **Consolidation present:** false
- **Consolidation type:** null
