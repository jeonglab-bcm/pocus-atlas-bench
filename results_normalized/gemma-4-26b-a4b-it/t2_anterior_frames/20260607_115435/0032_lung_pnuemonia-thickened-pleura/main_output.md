# 0032_lung_pnuemonia-thickened-pleura

The ultrasound video shows the right anterior lung zone in a low position. 

**Observations:**

These artifacts are very numerous and appear to coalesce or merge, creating a diffuse, bright white area that obscures any underlying horizontal A-lines. This confluent pattern is characteristic of a ground-glass appearance. There is no evidence of lung hepatization, shredded borders, or air bronchograms, which would indicate consolidation.

**B-lines Assessment:**
B-lines are present (`lung_rockets = true`). Due to their confluence and the diffuse white appearance they create, they are classified as the **ground_glass** subtype.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
No signs of consolidation (hepatization, shred sign, or air bronchograms) are observed (`consolidation = false`).
