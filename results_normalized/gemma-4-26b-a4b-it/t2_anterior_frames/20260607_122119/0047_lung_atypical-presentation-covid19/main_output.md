# 0047_lung_atypical-presentation-covid19

The lung ultrasound video shows an anterior lung zone with the following findings:

### B-lines Assessment
The video demonstrates the presence of numerous, confluent hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are coalescing, creating a bright, diffuse appearance that obscures any underlying A-lines, which is characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern. This pattern is often associated with conditions like interstitial syndrome or alveolar edema.

### Consolidation Assessment
The pleural line appears smooth and continuous, and the lung parenchyma below shows prominent aeration artifacts (B-lines). There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization, shredded borders, or air bronchograms. Therefore, **consolidation is absent**.

**Conclusion:**
- **B-lines:** Present, subtype: **ground_glass**
- **Consolidation:** **false**
