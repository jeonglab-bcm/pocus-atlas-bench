# 0036_lung_dynamic-air-bronchograms

The video shows a lung ultrasound (LUS) of the anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
The ultrasound images reveal the presence of numerous, highly confluent B-lines. These hyperechoic vertical artifacts are so densely packed that they merge into a continuous, bright white area, obscuring the A-lines and creating a "white lung" appearance. This is characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern, which typically indicates diffuse interstitial disease or alveolar edema.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
A significant area of alveolar **consolidation** is observed in the deeper portions of the image. This area exhibits a **hepatized** appearance, where the lung parenchyma loses its normal aeration and instead shows a solid, echogenic texture resembling liver tissue. The border between this consolidated region and the more aerated/ground-glass zone is irregular, demonstrating a **shred sign**. Within the consolidated area, multiple bright, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible, which are characteristic of **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusions:**
* **B-lines:** Present; subtype is **ground-glass**.
* **Consolidation:** Present; the predominant features are **hepatization** and **air bronchograms**.
