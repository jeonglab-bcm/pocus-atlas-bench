## B-lines Assessment
The ultrasound images demonstrate several vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines or "lung rockets") that arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. These B-lines appear as discrete, well-spaced lines, with visible lung parenchyma (represented by A-line patterns or the dark areas between them) between the artifacts. This pattern is characteristic of septal B-lines.

## Consolidation Assessment
