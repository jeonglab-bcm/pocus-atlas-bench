# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

The provided lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip shows an anterior lung zone scan.

## B-lines Assessment
In the video, multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) are clearly visible. These artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. They move in synchrony with the pleural line, indicating the presence of lung sliding. The B-lines are dense but remain largely discrete, with some dark lung parenchyma still visible between them. This pattern is characteristic of **septal B-lines**, which typically indicate thickened interlobular septa.

## Consolidation Assessment
Upon inspection of the lung parenchyma, there are no signs of alveolar consolidation. Specifically, I do not observe any tissue-like hepatization (liver-like echogenicity), a shred sign (irregular border between aerated and consolidated lung), or air bronchograms (hyperechoic foci within consolidated lung). The presence of B-lines confirms that the lung remains aerated.

## Conclusion
- **B-lines**: Present (**lung_rockets = true**)
- **B-line subtype**: **septal**
- **Consolidation**: Absent (**consolidation = false**)
