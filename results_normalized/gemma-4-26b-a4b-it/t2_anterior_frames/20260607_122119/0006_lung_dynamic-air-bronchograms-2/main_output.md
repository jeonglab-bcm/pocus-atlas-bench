# 0006_lung_dynamic-air-bronchograms-2

The video shows a lung ultrasound of the anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
The ultrasound image demonstrates multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These are B-lines (lung rockets). The B-lines are numerous and appear to coalesce or merge, creating a diffuse, bright "white lung" appearance that obscures the normal dark lung parenchyma. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines, which typically indicate diffuse interstitial or alveolar disease.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the observed area. There is no tissue-like hepatization (lung appearing solid like liver), no irregular "shred sign" at the border of aerated and non-aerated lung, and no visible air bronchograms within a consolidated area. The lung appears normally aerated, albeit with significant B-line artifacts.

**Conclusions:**
* **lung_rockets**: true
* **subtype**: ground_glass
* **consolidation**: false
* **consolidation_type**: null
