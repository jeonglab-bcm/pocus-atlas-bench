# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

The lung ultrasound video clip shows an anterior lung zone view.

**B-lines Assessment:**
The ultrasound image reveals numerous hyperechoic vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts do not fade and move with the lung sliding. The B-lines are highly dense and coalesce into a continuous, bright, hazy area that obscures the underlying horizontal A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of lung consolidation in this view. Specifically, there is no hepatized tissue (liver-like appearance), no shredded border between aerated and non-aerated lung (shred sign), and no punctate or linear air bronchograms within a solid-appearing lung parenchyma. The bright appearance of the lung below the pleural line is due to the confluent B-line artifacts rather than solid consolidation.

**Conclusions:**
*   **lung_rockets:** true
*   **subtype:** ground_glass
*   **consolidation:** false
