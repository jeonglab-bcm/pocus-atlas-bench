# 0039_lung_hepatization-of-lung

The video clip shows an anterior lung zone ultrasound.

**B-lines Assessment**
There is an absence of B-lines (lung rockets). No hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen are visible. The lung is not aerated enough to produce these artifacts.
- **lung_rockets = false**

**Consolidation Assessment**
There is clear evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung parenchyma shows a **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic with a texture resembling liver tissue. Within this hepatized lung, there are multiple small, bright, punctate hyperechoic foci, which are characteristic of **air bronchograms**. The border between the consolidated lung and the adjacent dark area (likely pleural effusion) appears irregular, consistent with a **shred sign**.
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "hepatization"**

**Conclusions**
- **lung_rockets: false**
- **consolidation: true**
- **consolidation_type: "hepatization"**
