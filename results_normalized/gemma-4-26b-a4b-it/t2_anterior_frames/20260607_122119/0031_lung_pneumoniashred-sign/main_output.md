# 0031_lung_pneumoniashred-sign

The sequential frames of this lung ultrasound (LUS) of the anterior right lung zone show the following:

**B-lines Assessment:**
There are no discrete, vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen that characterize B-lines (lung rockets). Therefore, **lung_rockets = false**.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The image shows a prominent area of lung parenchyma that has lost its normal aerated appearance and instead exhibits a solid, echogenic texture resembling liver tissue, which is characteristic of **hepatization**. This consolidated region also displays an irregular, jagged deep border where it meets the more aerated lung, known as the **shred sign**. Additionally, within the hepatized lung, there are small, bright, punctate hyperechoic spots, representing **air bronchograms**. Given the predominant tissue-like appearance of the lung, the findings are consistent with consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
* **lung_rockets**: false
* **consolidation**: true
* **consolidation_type**: "hepatization"
