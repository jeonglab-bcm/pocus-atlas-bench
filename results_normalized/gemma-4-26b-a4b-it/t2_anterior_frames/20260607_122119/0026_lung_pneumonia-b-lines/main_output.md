**B-lines Assessment:**

These artifacts are not discrete or well-spaced; instead, they are confluent and coalesce into a diffuse, bright, white appearance that obscures the underlying lung parenchyma. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung does not exhibit tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign, or air bronchograms. The appearance is dominated by the diffuse ground-glass B-line pattern rather than solid-appearing lung tissue.
