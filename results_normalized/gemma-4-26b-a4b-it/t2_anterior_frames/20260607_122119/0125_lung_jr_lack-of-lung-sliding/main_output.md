# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

The provided images consist of several identical frames from a lung ultrasound (LUS) of an anterior lung zone.

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis
All frames show the same findings:
*   **Pleural Line:** A bright, hyperechoic horizontal line is visible at the top of the lung field.
*   **Acoustic Shadow:** A prominent dark vertical band in the center indicates the shadow of a rib.
*   **B-lines:** On both sides of the rib shadow, there are numerous, dense, hyperechoic vertical artifacts extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are coalescing, which obscures the underlying A-lines and gives a bright, hazy appearance to the lung parenchyma.
*   **Consolidation:** There is no evidence of solid, tissue-like lung parenchyma (hepatization), irregular borders (shred sign), or air bronchograms. The lung appears to be aerated, albeit with significant interstitial/alveolar fluid indicated by the B-lines.

### B-lines Assessment
*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**: Numerous vertical hyperechoic artifacts are clearly visible.
*   **Subtype (ground_glass)**: The B-lines are highly dense and coalesce into a diffuse, bright pattern, characteristic of a ground-glass appearance.

### Consolidation Assessment
*   **Consolidation (consolidation = false)**: No signs of alveolar consolidation (hepatization, shred sign, or air bronchograms) are observed in the imaged area.

### Conclusions
*   **lung_rockets: true**
*   **subtype: ground_glass**
*   **consolidation: false**
*   **consolidation_type: null**
