# 0133_lung_jr_subpleuralconsolidation

The sequential frames of this lung ultrasound (LUS) video from the right upper anterior lung zone show the following findings:

**B-lines Assessment**
The video demonstrates the presence of multiple, confluent B-lines (lung rockets). These B-lines are not discrete but rather coalesce into a diffuse, bright, "white-out" appearance that obscures the normal horizontal A-line pattern. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines, indicating significant interstitial or alveolar involvement.

**Consolidation Assessment**
The video also shows clear evidence of alveolar consolidation. A large area of the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic with a granular, tissue-like texture, which is highly suggestive of **hepatization** (the lung resembling the appearance of liver tissue). Within this consolidated area, small hyperechoic punctate foci can be seen, which are consistent with **air bronchograms**. Some areas also exhibit an irregular, "shredded" border where the consolidated tissue meets more aerated or shadowed areas (**shred sign**).

**Conclusion**
- **B-lines**: `lung_rockets = true`, `subtype = ground_glass`
- **Consolidation**: `consolidation = true`, `consolidation_type = hepatization`
