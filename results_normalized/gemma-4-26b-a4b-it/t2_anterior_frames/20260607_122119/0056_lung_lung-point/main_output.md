# 0056_lung_lung-point

The video shows a lung ultrasound of an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
There are several hyperechoic, vertical artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are discrete and well-spaced, allowing some degree of underlying lung parenchyma to remain visible between them. This pattern is characteristic of septal B-lines, which typically indicate thickened interlobular septa.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the observed area. The lung parenchyma does not exhibit a tissue-like hepatized appearance, there is no irregular "shred sign" at the border of aerated/consolidated lung, and no air bronchograms are visible within a solid-appearing mass.
