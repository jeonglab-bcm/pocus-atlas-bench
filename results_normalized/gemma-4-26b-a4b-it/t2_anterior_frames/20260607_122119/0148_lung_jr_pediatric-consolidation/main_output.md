# 0148_lung_jr_pediatric-consolidation

The sequential ultrasound frames of the anterior lung zone show the following findings:

**Frame-by-frame analysis:**
*   **Frames 1 & 2:** Show a large, hypoechoic (dark) area located deep to the pleural line, which represents non-aerated lung tissue (consolidation). No vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) are seen.
*   **Frames 3 & 4:** Within the hypoechoic consolidated area, small, punctate hyperechoic (bright) foci are visible, which are characteristic of **air bronchograms**. Additionally, an irregular, jagged border between the aerated lung and the consolidated region starts to become apparent on the left side of the image, indicating the **shred sign**.
*   **Frames 5 & 6:** The **shred sign** becomes even more prominent, with a highly irregular and "shredded" interface visible between the aerated lung and the consolidated, hypoechoic tissue.

**B-lines Assessment:**
No vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending to the bottom of the screen are observed.
- **lung_rockets = false**

**Consolidation Assessment:**
A large area of non-aerated lung is clearly visible. The irregular border between the aerated and consolidated lung is a hallmark of the shred sign, and small bright spots within the consolidation indicate air bronchograms.
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "shred_sign"** (as this feature is most prominent in the final frames)

**Conclusion:**
The ultrasound images demonstrate **consolidation** in the anterior lung zone, characterized predominantly by a **shred sign** (irregular border) and the presence of **air bronchograms**. There are **no B-lines** present.
