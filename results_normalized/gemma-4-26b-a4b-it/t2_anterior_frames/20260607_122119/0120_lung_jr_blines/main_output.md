# 0120_lung_jr_blines

The lung ultrasound images show a sequence of frames from an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
The images reveal multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are numerous and confluent, merging together to create a diffuse, bright white appearance that obscures any horizontal A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass B-lines**, indicating significant interstitial or alveolar-interstitial involvement.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The bright area below the pleural line is due to the confluence of B-lines rather than solid, tissue-like hepatization of the lung parenchyma. No shred sign or air bronchograms are observed.

**Conclusions:**

* **b_line_subtype**: "ground_glass"
* **consolidation**: false
