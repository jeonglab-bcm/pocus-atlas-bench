# 0005_lung_subpleural-consolidation-with-shred-sign

The sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) show the following:

**B-lines Assessment**
There are no hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line that extend to the bottom of the screen. Therefore, B-lines (lung rockets) are absent.

**Consolidation Assessment**
The lung parenchyma in the anterior zone exhibits clear signs of alveolar consolidation. The lung tissue appears solid and echogenic, with a texture that closely resembles liver tissue, a phenomenon known as **hepatization**. Additionally, within this hepatized area, small, bright hyperechoic foci can be seen, which are characteristic of **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusions:**
* **lung_rockets:** false
* **consolidation:** true
* **consolidation_type:** "hepatization"
