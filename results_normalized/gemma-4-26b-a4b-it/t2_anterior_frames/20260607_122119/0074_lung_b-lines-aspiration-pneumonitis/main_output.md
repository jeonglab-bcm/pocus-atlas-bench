# 0074_lung_b-lines-aspiration-pneumonitis

The ultrasound video displays a scan of an anterior lung zone, showing a dynamic change in the B-line pattern.

As the probe moves, these B-lines become increasingly dense and coalesce into a continuous, bright, and confluent white area that obscures the A-lines, indicating a **ground glass** pattern.
