# 0005_lung_subpleural-consolidation-with-shred-sign

The lung ultrasound video shows an anterior lung zone with the following findings:

**B-lines Assessment:**
The images reveal hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are not discrete or well-spaced; instead, they rapidly coalesce and merge, creating a diffuse, bright, white appearance that obscures the normal A-line pattern. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung parenchyma does not exhibit a solid, tissue-like (hepatized) appearance, nor is there a visible shred sign or air bronchograms. The increased echogenicity is due to B-line artifacts from aerated (but likely fluid-filled) lung, not from consolidated lung tissue.

**Conclusions:**
* **lung_rockets**: true
* **ground_glass**: true
* **consolidation**: false
* **consolidation_type**: null
