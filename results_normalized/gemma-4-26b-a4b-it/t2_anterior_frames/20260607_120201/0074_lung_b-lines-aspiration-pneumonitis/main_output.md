These artifacts are not discrete or well-spaced; instead, they are highly confluent and coalesce to form a diffuse, bright, white sheet that obscures the underlying lung parenchyma and A-line pattern. There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the visualized anterior lung zone. The lung appears aerated, with the bright appearance being due to the confluent B-line artifacts rather than consolidated lung tissue.
