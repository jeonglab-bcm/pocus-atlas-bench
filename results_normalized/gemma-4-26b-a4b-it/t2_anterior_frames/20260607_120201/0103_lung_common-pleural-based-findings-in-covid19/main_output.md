# 0103_lung_common-pleural-based-findings-in-covid19

Based on the analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) images of the anterior lung zone:

**Observations:**
- **Pleural Line:** A distinct, bright hyperechoic horizontal line is visible, representing the pleural line.
- **B-lines Assessment:** There are no vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines/lung rockets) arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. Instead, the image shows multiple horizontal reverberation artifacts, known as **A-lines**, which are characteristic of a normally aerated lung.
- **Consolidation Assessment:** The lung parenchyma below the pleural line does not exhibit a solid, liver-like (hepatized) appearance. There is no evidence of an irregular "shred sign" at the border of aerated and non-aerated tissue, nor are there any visible air bronchograms (punctate or linear hyperechoic foci) within the lung tissue.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets:** false
- **consolidation:** false
