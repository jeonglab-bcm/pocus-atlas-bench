# 0131_lung_jr_bronchiolitis

Based on the sequential analysis of the lung ultrasound video frames:

## B-lines Assessment
- **Initial frames (1-2):** Predominantly show horizontal A-lines, indicating normal aeration.
- **Middle frames (3-4):** Discrete, hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) begin to appear, originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These are characteristic of the **septal** subtype.
- **Later frames (5-7):** The B-lines increase in number and coalesce, merging into a diffuse, bright white sheet that obscures the A-lines. This represents the **ground-glass** pattern.
- Since both discrete (septal) and confluent (ground-glass) B-line patterns are observed throughout the clip, the classification is **mixed**.

## Consolidation Assessment
- Throughout the sequence, there is no evidence of lung tissue appearing solid or hepatized (resembling liver texture).
- There is no irregular "shred sign" at the border of aerated and non-aerated lung.
- No punctate or linear air bronchograms are visible within the bright areas. The bright appearance in the later frames is due to the coalescence of B-line artifacts rather than solid tissue replacement.
- Therefore, **consolidation is absent**.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **subtype = mixed**
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
