# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

The pleural line is visible as a bright, horizontal hyperechoic line. Beneath the pleural line, numerous hyperechoic vertical artifacts are observed. These artifacts are confluent and coalesce, creating a diffuse, bright, and hazy appearance in the lung parenchyma, which is characteristic of **ground-glass B-lines**.
