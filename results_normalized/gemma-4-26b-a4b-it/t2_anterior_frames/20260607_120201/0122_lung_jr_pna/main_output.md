# 0122_lung_jr_pna

The provided lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence shows an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
The ultrasound images reveal the presence of numerous B-lines. These hyperechoic vertical artifacts originate from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen. The B-lines are confluent and coalescing, merging into a diffuse, bright, white sheet that obscures the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of the **ground-glass** subtype of B-lines, which often indicates alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial lung disease.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of lung consolidation in this sequence. The lung parenchyma does not exhibit a tissue-like hepatization appearance, an irregular shred sign at the border, or internal air bronchograms. The bright appearance of the lung is due to confluent B-line artifacts rather than solid, consolidated lung tissue.

**Conclusions:**
* **B-lines:** present (subtype: **ground-glass**)
* **Consolidation:** absent
