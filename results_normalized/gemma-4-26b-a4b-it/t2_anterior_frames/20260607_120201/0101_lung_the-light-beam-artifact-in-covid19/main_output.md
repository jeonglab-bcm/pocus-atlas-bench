# 0101_lung_the-light-beam-artifact-in-covid19

The sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video show the following:

## B-lines Assessment
Throughout the video clip, there is a prominent, diffuse white appearance below the pleural line. Instead of seeing discrete, individual vertical artifacts (septal B-lines), the artifacts are confluent and coalescing, merging into a continuous bright sheet that obscures the normal A-line horizontal artifacts. This pattern is highly characteristic of **ground-glass B-lines**, often referred to as "white lung."

## Consolidation Assessment
Upon inspection, there is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung does not exhibit tissue-like hepatization (liver-like texture), a shredded border (shred sign), or internal air bronchograms. The intense echogenicity is due to the confluence of B-lines rather than the presence of solid, consolidated lung tissue.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **subtype**: ground_glass
- **consolidation**: false
