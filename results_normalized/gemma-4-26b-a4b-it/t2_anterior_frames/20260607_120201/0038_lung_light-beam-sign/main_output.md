# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

The ultrasound video clip shows an anterior lung zone. 

Initially, some discrete, hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) can be seen arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. As the clip progresses, these B-lines become increasingly numerous and begin to coalesce, merging into a confluent, bright, hazy appearance that obscures the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of a **ground-glass** subtype of B-lines, indicating diffuse interstitial or alveolar processes.

There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in this view. No tissue-like hepatization, shred sign, or air bronchograms are observed; instead, the lung parenchyma is dominated by the confluent B-line artifacts.

**Conclusions:**
* **B-lines:** Present; subtype is **ground-glass**.
* **Consolidation:** Absent.
