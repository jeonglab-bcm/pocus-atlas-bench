# 0046_lung_light-beam-sign-covid19

The video shows sequential ultrasound frames of an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
The images reveal numerous hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line that extend to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are confluent and coalescing, creating a diffuse, bright, "white sheet" appearance that obscures the normal A-line pattern. This pattern is characteristic of ground-glass B-lines, which is typically indicative of diffuse interstitial or alveolar disease, such as pulmonary edema or pneumonia.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in these views. The lung parenchyma does not exhibit tissue-like hepatization, irregular shredded borders (shred sign), or internal air bronchograms. The lung remains aerated, although with significantly increased echogenicity due to the ground-glass B-line pattern.

- **subtype**: ground_glass
- **consolidation**: false
