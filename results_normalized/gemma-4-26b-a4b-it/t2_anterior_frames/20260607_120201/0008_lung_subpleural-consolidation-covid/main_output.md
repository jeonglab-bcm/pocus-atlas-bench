# 0008_lung_subpleural-consolidation-covid

The lung ultrasound video shows an anterior view of a lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
The image shows prominent, confluent hyperechoic vertical artifacts that merge into a continuous, bright white sheet, obscuring any A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass B-lines** (also known as coalescing B-lines), which indicate interstitial edema or diffuse interstitial lung disease.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is a distinct area of hypoechoic (dark) lung tissue adjacent to the aerated lung. The border between this dark, non-aerated region and the bright, aerated region with B-lines is irregular and jagged. This is the classic **shred sign**, which is a diagnostic indicator of **consolidation**.

**Conclusions:**
- **B-lines:** present (**lung_rockets = true**), subtype: **ground_glass**
- **Consolidation:** present (**consolidation = true**), type: **shred_sign**
