# 0026_lung_pneumonia-b-lines

The sequential frames of this lung ultrasound (LUS) of the left hemithorax (Zone 2) show the following:

### B-lines Assessment
The images demonstrate the presence of numerous, prominent vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line. These artifacts are very dense and, in most frames (especially frames 3, 4, and 5), they coalesce to form a diffuse, bright white sheet that obscures any horizontal A-line artifacts. This pattern is characteristic of a **ground-glass** subtype of B-lines, typically indicating diffuse interstitial or alveolar disease.

### Consolidation Assessment
The lung parenchyma does not show signs of alveolar consolidation. There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization (which would appear echogenic/bright), a shred sign (an irregular, shredded border), or air bronchograms (punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within a consolidated area). The large, dark vertical band in the center is an acoustic shadow, most likely caused by a rib, rather than a consolidated area of lung tissue.

### Conclusion
* **B-lines**: present (subtype: **ground_glass**)
* **Consolidation**: **false**
