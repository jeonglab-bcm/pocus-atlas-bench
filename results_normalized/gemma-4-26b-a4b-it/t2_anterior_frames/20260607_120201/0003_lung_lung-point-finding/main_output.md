# 0003_lung_lung-point-finding

The video shows a lung ultrasound of an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
Upon frame-by-frame analysis, multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts (lung rockets) can be seen arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading. These artifacts move in synchrony with the pleural line (indicating lung sliding). The B-lines are discrete, well-spaced, and do not coalesce into a single confluent white sheet. This pattern is characteristic of **septal B-lines**, which are often associated with thickened interlobular septa.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung parenchyma does not exhibit a tissue-like hepatization, a shredded border, or internal air bronchograms. The presence of B-lines indicates that the lung is still significantly aerated.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **B-line subtype**: septal
- **consolidation**: false
- **consolidation_type**: null
