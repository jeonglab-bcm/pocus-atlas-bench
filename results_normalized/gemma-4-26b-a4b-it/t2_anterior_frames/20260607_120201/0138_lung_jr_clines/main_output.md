# 0138_lung_jr_clines

The video displays a lung ultrasound (LUS) of an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
Upon systematic review of the frames, there is an absence of discrete, hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line. The characteristic patterns of septal or ground-glass B-lines are not observed in this clip. Therefore, **lung_rockets = false**.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The ultrasound images clearly demonstrate areas of lung consolidation. The following findings are present:
*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** Large portions of the lung parenchyma exhibit a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue, indicating a loss of normal aeration.
*   **Shred sign:** The interface between the consolidated lung and the aerated lung is irregular and "shredded."
*   **Air bronchograms:** Small, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within the hepatized lung tissue, representing trapped air within the bronchi.

Given these findings, **consolidation = true**. The most predominant feature is the tissue-like appearance of the lung, so the **consolidation_type is "hepatization"**.

**Conclusion:**
The lung ultrasound reveals significant consolidation characterized by hepatization, the shred sign, and air bronchograms, with no B-lines identified.
