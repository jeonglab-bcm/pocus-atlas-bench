# 0001_lung_lung-cavitary-lesion

The lung ultrasound video shows the following:

**B-lines Assessment:**
No B-lines (lung rockets) are visible in this clip. The lung appears predominantly consolidated rather than showing patterns of interstitial or alveolar edema that would produce B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is prominent lung consolidation in the anterior zone. The consolidated tissue has a solid, echogenic appearance, characteristic of **hepatization** (the lung tissue resembling the texture of the liver). A very clear and irregular, jagged border is visible where the consolidated lung meets the aerated lung above it, which is a classic **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **lung_rockets:** false
* **consolidation:** true
* **consolidation_type:** "shred_sign"
