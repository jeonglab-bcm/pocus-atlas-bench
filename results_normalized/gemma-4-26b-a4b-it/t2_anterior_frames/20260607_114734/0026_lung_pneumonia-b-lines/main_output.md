# 0026_lung_pneumonia-b-lines

The lung ultrasound video clip shows an anterior lung zone (LT H/T ZONE 2).

**B-lines Assessment:**
In the lateral portions of the image, flanking the central hypoechoic area, there are dense, confluent, vertical hyperechoic artifacts that extend to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts merge into a diffuse bright area, characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern of B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is a large, central, hypoechoic area that lacks normal aeration artifacts (like A-lines). The border between this dark, consolidated area and the surrounding B-line-rich (aerated) lung is irregular and "shredded," which is consistent with the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **B-lines:** Present, **ground-glass** subtype.
* **Consolidation:** Present, **shred sign**.
