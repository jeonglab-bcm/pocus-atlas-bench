# 0101_lung_the-light-beam-artifact-in-covid19

The ultrasound video clip shows an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
Upon analysis of the sequential frames, B-lines (lung rockets) are present. Initially, a few discrete, vertical hyperechoic artifacts are visible. As the clip progresses, these B-lines become increasingly numerous and coalesce, merging into a diffuse, bright white pattern that obscures the underlying A-lines. This confluent pattern is characteristic of a **ground-glass** subtype of B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The image does not show signs of alveolar consolidation, such as tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign, or internal air bronchograms. The bright appearance is due to confluent B-line artifacts rather than solid lung parenchyma. Therefore, consolidation is absent.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **b_line_subtype**: "ground_glass"
- **consolidation**: false
- **consolidation_type**: null
