# 0147_lung_jr_acute-chest-syndrome

The sequential lung ultrasound frames demonstrate the following findings:

## B-lines Assessment
The images show the presence of numerous vertical hyperechoic artifacts. These artifacts are confluent and coalescing, merging into a diffuse white sheet that obscures the normal horizontal A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines, indicating diffuse interstitial or alveolar disease.

## Consolidation Assessment
In the later frames (specifically frames 4 through 7), a region of lung consolidation is visible, particularly in the lower right portion of the scan area. This area exhibits a tissue-like, solid appearance compared to the surrounding aerated lung. A highly characteristic **shred sign** is present, represented by the irregular, jagged border between the consolidated (solid-looking) lung and the adjacent aerated lung area characterized by ground-glass B-lines.

**Conclusions:**
- **B-lines:** Present, with a **ground-glass** pattern.
- **Consolidation:** Present, with a predominant **shred sign**.
