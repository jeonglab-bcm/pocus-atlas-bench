# 0017_lung_lung-point2

The provided video clip shows a lung ultrasound (LUS) of an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment**
The B-mode image (top) displays multiple vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are very dense and appear to coalesce, creating a bright, diffuse appearance that obscures the normal horizontal A-line pattern. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines, which often indicates diffuse interstitial or alveolar disease (such as pulmonary edema). The M-mode image (bottom) shows a "seashore sign" (horizontal lines above the pleural line and a granular "sea-grass" pattern below), which confirms the presence of lung sliding.

**Consolidation Assessment**
There is no evidence of lung consolidation in the anterior zone. The ultrasound image is dominated by aeration artifacts (B-lines), and there is no visualization of tissue-like hepatized lung, a shred sign, or air bronchograms.

**Conclusions**
- **B-lines**: Present; subtype is **ground-glass**.
- **Consolidation**: Absent.
