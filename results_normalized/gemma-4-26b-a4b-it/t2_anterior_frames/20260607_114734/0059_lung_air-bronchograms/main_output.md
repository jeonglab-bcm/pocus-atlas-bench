# 0059_lung_air-bronchograms

nt.The sequential frames of this lung ultrasound show the following findings:

**B-lines Assessment**
The images show multiple vertical, hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the sector. These artifacts are B-lines, and they move in synchrony with the pleural line (indicating lung sliding). The B-lines appear as relatively discrete, well-spaced vertical lines in several areas of the view, which is characteristic of the **septal** subtype. Although they are bright and frequent, they do not merge into a completely continuous, diffuse white sheet that would define a ground-glass pattern.

**Consolidation Assessment**
There is no evidence of lung consolidation in the visualized anterior zone. The lung parenchyma does not exhibit a solid, tissue-like (hepatized) appearance, there is no identifiable "shred sign" (irregular deep border), and no punctate or linear air bronchograms are seen within any consolidated-appearing areas. The bright areas at the bottom of the images are consistent with intense B-line activity rather than consolidation.

**Conclusions**
* **B-lines**: present (subtype: septal)
* **Consolidation**: absent
