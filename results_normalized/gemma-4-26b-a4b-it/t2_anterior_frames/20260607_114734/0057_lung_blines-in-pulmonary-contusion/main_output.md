* **Frames 3-5:** As the sequence progresses, the B-lines become increasingly numerous and begin to coalesce or merge. This results in a diffuse, bright, white-sheet-like appearance that obscures the underlying A-lines, characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern.

**B-lines Assessment:**
B-lines are present throughout the clip. While they begin as discrete lines, they rapidly coalesce into a confluent pattern, which defines the **ground-glass** subtype.

There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation (e.g., tissue-like hepatization, shred sign, or air bronchograms) visible in these frames. The prominent bright areas are due to confluent B-line artifacts, and the large dark area is an anechoic space consistent with pleural effusion.
