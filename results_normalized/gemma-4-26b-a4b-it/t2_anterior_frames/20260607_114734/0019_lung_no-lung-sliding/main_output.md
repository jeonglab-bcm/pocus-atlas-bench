# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

The ultrasound video clip shows an anterior lung zone with the following findings:

**B-lines Assessment:**
The video demonstrates the presence of numerous hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines or lung rockets) arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are confluent and coalescing, creating a diffuse bright area that obscures the underlying A-lines, which is characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the visualized anterior lung zone. The lung parenchyma does not show tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign, or internal air bronchograms; instead, it is characterized by the aforementioned B-line artifacts, indicating it remains aerated.

**Conclusions:**
* **lung_rockets:** true
* **B-line subtype:** ground_glass
* **consolidation:** false
