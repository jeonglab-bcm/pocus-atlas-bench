# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

The provided lung ultrasound video clip shows an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment**
Upon analysis of the video frames, a prominent vertical hyperechoic artifact is observed. This artifact originates from the pleural line and extends to the bottom of the screen without fading, which is characteristic of a B-line (lung rocket). The B-line is discrete and well-defined, with the lung parenchyma appearing relatively dark between any potential adjacent lines, rather than forming a confluent, diffuse white sheet. Therefore, B-lines are present and categorized as the **septal** subtype.

**Consolidation Assessment**
The lung parenchyma below the pleural line appears largely hypoechoic, which is consistent with normally aerated lung. There is no evidence of hepatization (tissue-like appearance), a shred sign (irregular border), or air bronchograms (hyperechoic foci within consolidated lung). Consequently, **no consolidation** is identified in this view.

**Conclusions**
- **B-lines**: present (subtype: **septal**)
- **Consolidation**: absent
