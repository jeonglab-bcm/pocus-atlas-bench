# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

The video displays an ultrasound of the anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
The ultrasound shows the presence of B-lines. These are hyperechoic vertical artifacts that arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen. The B-lines in this clip appear to be confluent, merging into a diffuse, bright area that obscures the horizontal A-lines, which is characteristic of a ground glass pattern.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in the viewed lung zone. No hepatization, shredded borders, or air bronchograms are observed; the lung parenchyma appears aerated, albeit with a significant B-line pattern.

**Conclusions:**
- **B-lines:** Present (ground_glass)
- **Consolidation:** Absent
