# 0138_lung_jr_clines

The lung ultrasound video of the anterior lung zone shows the following:

* **B-lines Assessment**: There are multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line that extend to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are confluent and merge into a diffuse, bright "white" appearance, obscuring the normal A-line pattern. This characterizes a **ground-glass** B-line pattern, which typically indicates diffuse interstitial or alveolar disease.
* **Consolidation Assessment**: There is no evidence of hepatized lung tissue, shred sign, or air bronchograms in the visualized lung area. The bright appearance is due to confluent B-lines rather than solid consolidation. Therefore, **consolidation is absent**.

**Conclusions:**
* **lung_rockets**: true
* **B-line subtype**: ground_glass
* **consolidation**: false
* **consolidation_type**: null
