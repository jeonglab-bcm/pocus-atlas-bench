# 0046_lung_light-beam-sign-covid19

The lung ultrasound video clip displays an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
Throughout the clip, particularly from the middle frames onwards, there is a prominent appearance of multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts that originate from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. These artifacts are coalescing and confluent, creating a diffuse, bright, "white-out" appearance that obscures the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass B-lines**, which typically indicate diffuse alveolar or interstitial disease (such as pulmonary edema).

**Consolidation Assessment:**
No evidence of alveolar consolidation is observed. There is no tissue-like hepatization (solid-looking lung with liver-like echogenicity), no shred sign (irregular border between aerated and consolidated lung), and no air bronchograms (hyperechoic foci within consolidated lung) visible. The bright area is due to confluent B-line artifacts rather than solid lung tissue.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets:** true
- **b_line_subtype:** ground_glass
- **consolidation:** false
- **consolidation_type:** null
