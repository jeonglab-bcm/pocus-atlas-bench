# 0133_lung_jr_subpleuralconsolidation

The ultrasound video clip shows a view of the right upper lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
In the provided frames, there is a prominent, bright, and confluent area of hyperechoic signal below the pleural line. These signals are not discrete, well-spaced lines, but rather appear to merge into a diffuse, bright area that obscures the typical horizontal A-line artifacts. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass B-lines**, which often indicate interstitial edema or other diffuse lung pathologies.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
More significantly, the bright, echogenic area has a very irregular and jagged border where it meets the darker, non-aerated regions of the image (likely the adjacent rib shadow or non-consolidated lung). This irregular, "shredded" appearance at the interface between the aerated and non-aerated lung is a classic **shred sign**, which is highly specific for **alveolar consolidation**. While the area is quite bright (resembling hepatization), the most distinguishing feature is this irregular border.

**Conclusion:**
- **B-lines present:** true; **subtype:** ground_glass
- **Consolidation present:** true; **consolidation_type:** shred_sign
