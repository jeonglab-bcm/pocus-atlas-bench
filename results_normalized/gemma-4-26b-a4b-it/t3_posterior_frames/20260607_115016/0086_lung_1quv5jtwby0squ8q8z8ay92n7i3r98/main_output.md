The ultrasound video shows a posterior lung zone scan with several characteristic features:

*   **Effusion signs**: There is a large anechoic (black) area representing a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, a consolidated lung fragment is seen moving or floating, which is a classic **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs**: The lung tissue within the effusion appears echogenic and solid, resembling liver tissue, which is known as **tissue-like hepatization**.

Because both a significant pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are present, the pattern is classified as follows:
