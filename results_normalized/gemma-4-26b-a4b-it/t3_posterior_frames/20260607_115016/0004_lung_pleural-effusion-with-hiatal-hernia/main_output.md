# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

In these lung ultrasound frames, the following features are observed in the posterior/lateral lung zone:

* **Effusion signs**: There is a prominent anechoic (black) space located between the rib shadow and the lung parenchyma, consistent with a **pleural effusion** (suggestive of the quad sign).
* **Consolidation signs**: The lung tissue within this zone lacks normal aeration and exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic like liver parenchyma. Small hyperechoic foci within this area are consistent with **air bronchograms**.

Because both pleural effusion and lung consolidation are visible, the findings indicate a PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
