# 0100_lung_hemothorax

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound, the following observations can be made:

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** There is a significant anechoic (black) space in the dependent posterior lung zone, indicating a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, the lung tissue moves in an undulating, floating manner during the respiratory cycle, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue seen within the effusion appears solid and echogenic, demonstrating **tissue-like hepatization** (resembling liver texture), which indicates alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = both**
