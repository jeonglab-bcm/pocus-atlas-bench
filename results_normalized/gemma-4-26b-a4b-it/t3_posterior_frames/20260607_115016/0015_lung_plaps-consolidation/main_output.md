# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

The ultrasound video demonstrates a clear PLAPS pattern in the posterolateral lung zone.

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) area representing a significant pleural effusion. The consolidated lung appears to be floating within this fluid collection, which is highly suggestive of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma within the dependent zone is no longer aerated; instead, it exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic with a texture very similar to liver tissue. The interface between the consolidated lung and the effusion shows an irregular border, consistent with a **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
