The ultrasound images show a large anechoic (black) area in the posterior lung zone, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a piece of echogenic, solid-appearing lung tissue that resembles the texture of the liver (hepatization). This lung tissue appears to float within the surrounding effusion, which is a classic representation of the **jellyfish sign**.

Because both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung are visible, the following classification applies:

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
