# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The ultrasound video shows a large anechoic (black) space in the posterior/lateral lung zone, which is diagnostic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, a consolidated, hyperechoic (bright) lung mass can be seen moving and undulating, which is the characteristic **"jellyfish sign"**. The appearance of the lung itself is solid and echogenic, consistent with **tissue-like hepatization**.

Because both significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the classification is as follows:

* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
