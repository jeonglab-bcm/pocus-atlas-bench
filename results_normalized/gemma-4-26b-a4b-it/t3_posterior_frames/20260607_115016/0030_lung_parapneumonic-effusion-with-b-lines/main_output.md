# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

The ultrasound images show a view of the right posterior lung zone (PLAPS point). 

In the dependent portion of the image, there is a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, a solid, echogenic mass of lung tissue is visible. This mass has a texture similar to liver tissue, a finding known as **hepatization**, which indicates **alveolar consolidation**. The way this consolidated lung tissue appears to float and move within the surrounding fluid is highly suggestive of the **jellyfish sign**.

Based on these observations:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
