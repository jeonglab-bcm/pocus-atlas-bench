# 0126_lung_jr_loculated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound video demonstrates findings characteristic of a PLAPS point (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) involving both effusion and consolidation:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) space representing a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, the consolidated lung tissue is seen moving, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue within the effusion appears solid and echogenic, indicating **hepatization** (consolidation). The irregular, non-smooth border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid is indicative of the **shred sign**.

Because both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
