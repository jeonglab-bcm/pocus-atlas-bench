# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

The ultrasound video shows a prominent **PLAPS pattern** in the posterior lung zone.

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) space representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a floating, undulating, echogenic structure is clearly visible that moves with respiration, which is a classic representation of the **jellyfish sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The "jellyfish" itself represents consolidated, hepatized lung tissue floating within the surrounding effusion.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
