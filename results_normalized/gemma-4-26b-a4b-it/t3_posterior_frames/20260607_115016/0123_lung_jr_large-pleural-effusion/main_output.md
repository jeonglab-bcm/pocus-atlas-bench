# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

Based on the provided ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone, here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large **anechoic (black) area** occupying a significant portion of the image, which is diagnostic of a **pleural effusion**. The way the echogenic lung tissue is seen floating within this fluid is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue visible within the fluid appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of liver tissue, which is known as **tissue-like hepatization**. The irregular, "shredded" appearance of the border between the consolidated lung and the fluid is indicative of the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"** (Both significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible).
