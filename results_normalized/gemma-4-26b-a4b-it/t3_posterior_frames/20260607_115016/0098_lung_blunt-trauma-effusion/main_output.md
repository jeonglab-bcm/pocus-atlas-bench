# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

The sequential frames of the lung ultrasound at the PLAPS point show the following:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) area indicating a pleural effusion. The **quad sign** is visible, where the fluid is bounded by the pleural line, the lung mass, and rib shadows. Additionally, the appearance of a solid, echogenic mass floating within this fluid collection is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The floating mass exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic similar to liver parenchyma, which signifies alveolar consolidation. The border of this mass against the fluid is irregular, consistent with a **shred sign**.

Because both significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
