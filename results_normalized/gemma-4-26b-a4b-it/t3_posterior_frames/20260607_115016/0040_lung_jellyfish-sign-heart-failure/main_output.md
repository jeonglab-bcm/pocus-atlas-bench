# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

Based on the provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** There is a large, prominent **anechoic (black) space** occupying the central portion of the view, which is characteristic of a significant **pleural effusion**. The shape of this fluid collection, bounded by the pleural surfaces and lung tissue, is consistent with the **quad sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue visible on the periphery of the effusion appears hyperechoic and solid, resembling liver texture, which is indicative of **tissue-like hepatization**. The border between the consolidated lung and the effusion/aerated lung is irregular, displaying a **shred sign**. Small, bright, punctate echoes within the consolidated lung suggest the presence of **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"** (Both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visualized).
