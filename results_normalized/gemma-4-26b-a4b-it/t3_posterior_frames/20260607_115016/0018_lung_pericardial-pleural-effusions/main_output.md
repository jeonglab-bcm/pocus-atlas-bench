# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), the following observations can be made:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) space representing a large pleural effusion. Within this fluid, the consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and undulating, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The floating lung tissue exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic, which indicates alveolar consolidation.

Because both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are clearly visible, the assessment is as follows:

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
