# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

In this lung ultrasound video clip of the PLAPS point, I observe the following:

*   **Effusion signs:** A large, anechoic (black) collection is visible, representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, an echogenic, solid-appearing piece of lung tissue is seen floating and moving, which is a classic representation of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The floating piece of lung exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic like liver tissue. The border between this consolidated lung and the surrounding effusion is irregular, demonstrating the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
