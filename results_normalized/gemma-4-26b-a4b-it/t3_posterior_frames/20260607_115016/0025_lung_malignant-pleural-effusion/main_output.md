# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

The video displays a lung ultrasound of a posterior lung zone, which is highly characteristic of a **PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome)** pattern.

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There are large, irregular anechoic (black) areas that represent **pleural effusion**. The presence of consolidated lung tissue "floating" within these fluid-filled spaces is consistent with the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The bright (hyperechoic), irregular structures within the dark areas show **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration and resembles solid organ tissue (like the liver). The irregular, jagged borders between the consolidated tissue and the surrounding areas are characteristic of the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
