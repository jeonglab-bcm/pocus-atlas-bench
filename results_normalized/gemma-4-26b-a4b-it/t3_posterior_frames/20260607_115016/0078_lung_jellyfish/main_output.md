# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The ultrasound frames from the right posterior lung zone show a significant collection of anechoic (black) fluid, indicating a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a large, solid-appearing, and highly echogenic mass of lung tissue. This tissue demonstrates **tissue-like hepatization**, meaning it has lost its normal aerated appearance and now resembles the texture of liver parenchyma, which is a hallmark of **lung consolidation**. The appearance of this consolidated lung segment floating within the surrounding effusion is characteristic of the **"jellyfish sign."**

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
