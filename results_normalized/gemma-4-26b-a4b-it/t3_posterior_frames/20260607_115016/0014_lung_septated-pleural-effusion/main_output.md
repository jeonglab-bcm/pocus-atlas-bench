# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

The lung ultrasound video shows a large anechoic (black) area in the dependent posterior zone, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, a piece of echogenic, consolidated lung tissue is seen moving and undulating, which is a classic representation of the **jellyfish sign**. The lung tissue itself appears solid and hepatized within the effusion.
