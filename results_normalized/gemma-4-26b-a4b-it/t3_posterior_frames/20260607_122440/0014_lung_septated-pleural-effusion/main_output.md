# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound video shows a large anechoic (black) area in the posterior/lateral dependent lung zone, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, a hyperechoic (bright), irregular structure is seen floating, representing atelectatic/consolidated lung tissue. This appearance is known as the **jellyfish sign**. The consolidated nature of the lung tissue within the effusion is also evident (tissue-like hepatization).

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
