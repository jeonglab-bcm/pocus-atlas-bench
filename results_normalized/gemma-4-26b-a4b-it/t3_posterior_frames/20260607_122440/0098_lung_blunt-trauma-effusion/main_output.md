# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

Based on the sequential ultrasound images provided, here is an analysis of the findings:

### **Observations**
*   **Effusion:** There is a large, prominent anechoic (black) area in the posterior lung zone, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**.
*   **Consolidation:** Within this fluid collection, there is a visible, irregular, and highly echogenic structure. This structure has a solid, "tissue-like" appearance, which is indicative of **hepatization** (lung consolidation).
*   **Jellyfish Sign:** The presence of this consolidated, hepatized lung tissue floating within a surrounding pleural effusion is a classic representation of the **jellyfish sign**.

### **Conclusion**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"** (as both a significant effusion and lung consolidation are present)
