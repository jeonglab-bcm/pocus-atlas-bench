# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

In these sequential ultrasound frames of the PLAPS point, the following features are observed:

*   **Effusion signs:** A large anechoic (black) area is visible, representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, the consolidated lung mass appears to move or float, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** Below the fluid, there is a solid, echogenic area with a texture very similar to liver tissue, indicating **tissue-like hepatization**. Within this consolidated lung, small hyperechoic (bright) spots can be seen, representing **air bronchograms**. The border between the consolidated lung and the fluid is irregular.

Because both a significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the findings are classified as follows:

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
