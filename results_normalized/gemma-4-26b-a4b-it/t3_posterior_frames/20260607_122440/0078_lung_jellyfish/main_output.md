# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The provided ultrasound sequence of the right posterior lung zone displays several key findings characteristic of a PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) pattern:

*   **Effusion signs:** A large anechoic (black) area is visible, representing a pleural effusion. In the latter part of the sequence, the hepatized lung tissue can be seen moving and undulating within this fluid collection, which is the classic **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma itself appears highly echogenic and solid, exhibiting **tissue-like hepatization**, where it resembles the texture of liver tissue rather than normal aerated lung.

Because both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
