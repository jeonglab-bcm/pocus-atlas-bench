# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

The ultrasound video shows a large anechoic (black) area, which is indicative of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, a piece of lung tissue is seen floating and undulating with respiratory movement, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**. The lung tissue itself has a solid, echogenic appearance resembling liver parenchyma, indicating **tissue-like hepatization** (consolidation). The irregular border where the consolidated lung meets the fluid can also be described as a **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
