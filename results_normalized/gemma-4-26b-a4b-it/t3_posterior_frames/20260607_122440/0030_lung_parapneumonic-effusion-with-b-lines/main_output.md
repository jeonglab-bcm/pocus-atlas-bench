# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames, the following observations can be made:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) space in the dependent portion of the lung, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. The appearance of a consolidated, solid-looking lung structure floating within this fluid collection is highly suggestive of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue within the effusion shows **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic, similar to the texture of the liver. Additionally, small hyperechoic (bright) foci can be seen within this consolidated area, which likely represent **air bronchograms**.

Because both an effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the findings represent a combination of both patterns.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
