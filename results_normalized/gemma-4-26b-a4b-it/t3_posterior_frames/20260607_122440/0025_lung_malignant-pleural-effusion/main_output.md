# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

Based on the sequential lung ultrasound frames provided, here is the assessment:

**Observations:**
* **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue in the posterior zone shows significant **tissue-like hepatization**. The lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration and appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of liver tissue. There are also small, punctate hyperechoic foci within the consolidated areas, likely representing **air bronchograms**.
* **Effusion signs:** Multiple large, **anechoic (black) spaces** are visible surrounding and interspersed with the consolidated lung tissue, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. The movement of the consolidated lung pieces within these fluid-filled spaces is consistent with the **jellyfish sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = both**
