# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

Based on the provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** A large, prominent anechoic (black) area is visible in the central part of the image, which is highly characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. The shape and position are consistent with signs like the **quad sign**, where fluid collects in the dependent portions of the pleural space.
* **Consolidation signs:** On either side of the large fluid collection, the lung tissue appears highly echogenic and lacks the normal aeration patterns (like A-lines). This solid, granular appearance is characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**, indicating alveolar consolidation. The irregular border between the consolidated lung and the effusion area also suggests a **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both a significant fluid collection and consolidated, hepatized lung tissue confirms a PLAPS pattern.
