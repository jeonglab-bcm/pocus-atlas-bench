# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The video shows a posterior lung zone ultrasound scan. A large, anechoic (black) area is visible, representing a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, a piece of consolidated, atelectatic lung tissue is seen floating and undulating with respiration. This characteristic appearance is known as the **jellyfish sign**.

Based on these observations:
- **plaps = true** (both effusion and consolidation/atelectasis are present)
- **type = "both"**
