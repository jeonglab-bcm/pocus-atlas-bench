# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

The provided ultrasound frames show a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, irregularly shaped echogenic (bright) structure. This movement of consolidated lung tissue within a large effusion is highly indicative of the **jellyfish sign**. The irregular border of this structure also suggests the **shred sign**, and its appearance is consistent with **tissue-like hepatization** of the lung.

Because both a significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the findings are classified as follows:

* **plaps = true**
* **type = both**
