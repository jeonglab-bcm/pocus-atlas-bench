# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The video shows a lung ultrasound examination of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point).

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** There is a significant anechoic (black) space in the dependent region, indicating a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, the consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and moving, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, displaying **tissue-like hepatization**. The border between the aerated and consolidated lung is irregular, representing the **shred sign**. Additionally, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within the consolidated lung, suggestive of **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
