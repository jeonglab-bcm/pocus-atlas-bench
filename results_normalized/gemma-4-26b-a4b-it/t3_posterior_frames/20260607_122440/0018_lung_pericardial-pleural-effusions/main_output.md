# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames, the following observations can be made:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, prominent anechoic (black) area representing a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, a piece of consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating, which is the characteristic **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue floating in the effusion exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic rather than aerated.

Because both a significant pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are clearly visible, the findings are classified as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
