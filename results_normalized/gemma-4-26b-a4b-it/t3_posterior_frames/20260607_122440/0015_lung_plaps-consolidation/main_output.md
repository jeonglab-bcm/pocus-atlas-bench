# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

The sequential ultrasound frames show a view of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point).

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Effusion:** There is a significant anechoic (black) area, particularly visible in the middle of the sequence, representing a pleural effusion.
* **Consolidation (Hepatization):** Adjacent to the fluid, the lung parenchyma exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**. The lung appears solid and has an echogenic, granular texture that closely resembles liver tissue.
* **Shred Sign:** The border between the consolidated, hepatized lung and the surrounding anechoic fluid/aerated area appears irregular and "shredded."
* **Air Bronchograms:** Small, hyperechoic (bright) punctate foci are visible within the hepatized lung tissue, which are characteristic of air bronchograms within consolidated lung.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
