# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The ultrasound video demonstrates findings in the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) consistent with both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation:

* **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) area representing a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a piece of consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and moving, which is a classic presentation of the **jellyfish sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The floating piece of lung tissue exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing as a solid, echogenic mass that resembles liver texture rather than aerated lung.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
