# 0096_lung_ecrc29rbuigumwcs1dakjt6c9osenx

In these ultrasound frames of a posterior lung zone, there is a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, an irregular, hyperechoic (bright), and tissue-like structure is visible, representing **consolidated (atelectatic) lung**. The appearance of this lung tissue floating and undulating within the surrounding fluid is the **jellyfish sign**.
