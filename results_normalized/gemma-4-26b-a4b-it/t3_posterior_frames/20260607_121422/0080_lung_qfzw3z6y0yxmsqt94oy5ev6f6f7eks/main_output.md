# 0080_lung_qfzw3z6y0yxmsqt94oy5ev6f6f7eks

The ultrasound images of the left lung base demonstrate several classic signs of a **PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome)** pattern:

*   **Consolidation signs:**
    *   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The lung parenchyma in the dependent zone appears solid and echogenic, with a texture very similar to liver tissue.
    *   **Shred sign:** The interface between the hepatized (consolidated) lung and the adjacent aerated lung is irregular and "shredded."
    *   **Air bronchograms:** Small, punctate, hyperechoic (bright) foci are visible within the consolidated lung, representing trapped air within the bronchi.
*   **Effusion signs:**
    *   **Jellyfish sign:** The consolidated, hepatized lung tissue appears to be "floating" within a surrounding anechoic (black) space, which represents a pleural effusion.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"** (both consolidation and pleural effusion are present)
