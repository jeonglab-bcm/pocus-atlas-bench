# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

The video shows an ultrasound scan of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point).

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) area representing a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, the lung tissue is seen floating and undulating with respiratory movement, which is a classic **jellyfish sign**. 
* **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue within the effusion appears solid and echogenic, exhibiting **tissue-like hepatization** (resembling liver texture). The irregular border of this lung tissue is also visible, consistent with a **shred sign**.

Since both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the classification is as follows:

* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
