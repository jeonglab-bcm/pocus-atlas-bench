# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

In this lung ultrasound (LUS) video, the following findings are observed in the posterior lung zone:

* **Effusion signs:** There is a large, prominent **anechoic (black) space** characteristic of a **pleural effusion**.
* **Consolidation signs:** Adjacent to the effusion, the lung tissue appears solid and echogenic, exhibiting **tissue-like hepatization**. Furthermore, there is a highly irregular, jagged interface between the consolidated lung and the surrounding area, which is the **shred sign**. Small hyperechoic foci within the consolidated area are suggestive of **air bronchograms**.

Because both pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
