# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

The lung ultrasound images demonstrate a large anechoic (black) space in the posterior dependent zone, which is characteristic of a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, irregular hyperechoic (white) structures representing lung tissue are seen floating and undulating with respiratory movement. This specific appearance, where consolidated lung tissue floats within an effusion, is known as the **jellyfish sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
