# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

The lung ultrasound video clip demonstrates a clear **PLAPS pattern** in the right lower posterior lung zone.

**Observations:**
* **Consolidation signs**: The lung parenchyma exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic, similar to the texture of the liver. Within this consolidated area, there are small, punctate hyperechoic foci that are suggestive of **air bronchograms**.
* **Effusion signs**: A prominent anechoic (black) area is visible, representing a pleural effusion.
* **Jellyfish sign**: The consolidated, hepatized lung tissue appears to be floating within the surrounding anechoic fluid collection, creating a characteristic **jellyfish sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
