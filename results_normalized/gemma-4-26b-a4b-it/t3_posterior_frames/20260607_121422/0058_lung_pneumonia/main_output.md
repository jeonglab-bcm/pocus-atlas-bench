# 0058_lung_pneumonia

The ultrasound video displays a posterior lung zone scan (PLAPS point) showing the following features:

* **Effusion signs:** A significant anechoic (black) space is visible, representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, an echogenic, consolidated lung segment is seen floating and moving dynamically with respiration, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The floating lung segment exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic similar to liver tissue. Within this hepatized lung, small, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible, representing **air bronchograms**. An irregular border where the lung meets the fluid can also be seen, consistent with the **shred sign**.

Because both a large pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly present, the classification is:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
