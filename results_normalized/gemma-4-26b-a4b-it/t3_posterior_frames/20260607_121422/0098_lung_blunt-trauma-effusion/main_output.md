# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames provided, here is the assessment of the PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) point:

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) area present in the dependent zone, which is characteristic of a significant pleural effusion.
* **Consolidation signs:** Within the anechoic fluid collection, there is a floating, heterogeneous, and echogenic mass of lung tissue. This tissue lacks the typical aerated appearance of normal lung and instead shows **tissue-like hepatization** (it looks solid and similar to liver tissue).
* **Integrated sign:** The movement and appearance of this consolidated lung tissue floating within the surrounding fluid is a classic presentation of the **jellyfish sign**. Additionally, the border between the consolidated tissue and the fluid/aerated lung appears irregular, consistent with the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
