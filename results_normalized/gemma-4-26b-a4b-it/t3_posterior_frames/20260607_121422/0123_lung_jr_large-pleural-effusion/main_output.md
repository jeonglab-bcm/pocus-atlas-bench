# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

The sequential ultrasound frames demonstrate a large, anechoic (black) area in the posterior lung zone, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. 

Superior to this fluid collection, the lung parenchyma appears echogenic and solid, resembling the texture of liver tissue. This finding is indicative of **lung hepatization**, a sign of **alveolar consolidation**. Additionally, the presence of consolidated lung tissue floating within the anechoic fluid in some frames may represent a **jellyfish sign**.

Because both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the findings are classified as follows:

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
