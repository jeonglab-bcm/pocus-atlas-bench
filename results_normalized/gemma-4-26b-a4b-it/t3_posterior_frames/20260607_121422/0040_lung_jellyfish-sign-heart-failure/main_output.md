# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

The video displays a lung ultrasound at the PLAPS point, showing the following features:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, prominent **anechoic (black) area** occupying the central portion of the image, which is characteristic of a significant **pleural effusion**. This fluid collection creates a clear space bounded by the pleural line and the lung tissue.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue visible at the lateral edges of the effusion appears bright and solid, exhibiting **tissue-like hepatization**. This indicates that the lung parenchyma has lost its aeration and is behaving like solid tissue, a clear sign of **alveolar consolidation**.

Since both a large pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are clearly visible, the findings are classified as follows:

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
