# 0143_lung_jr_pleuraleffusion

The video clip shows a posterior lung ultrasound view with the following findings:

*   **Effusion signs**: There is a significant anechoic (black) space, indicating a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, the consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and undulating, which is a classic **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs**: The lung tissue within the effusion appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver texture (**tissue-like hepatization**). Small, bright hyperechoic spots within this tissue are visible, representing **air bronchograms**. The irregular border between the consolidated lung and the aerated lung (as it moves) represents the **shred sign**.

Because both pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the findings are classified as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
