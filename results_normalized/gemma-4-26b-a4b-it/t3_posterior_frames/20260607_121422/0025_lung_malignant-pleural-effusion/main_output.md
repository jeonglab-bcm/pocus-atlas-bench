# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

The sequential ultrasound frames show a highly heterogeneous and echogenic area in the dependent lung zone, which is characteristic of a **PLAPS pattern**.

**Observations:**
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic with a texture resembling liver tissue. The borders of this consolidated area are irregular and "ragged," which is consistent with the **shred sign**. The presence of multiple anechoic (dark) spaces within the consolidated mass may represent small areas of trapped fluid or air-fluid interfaces, though they do not clearly show classic hyperechoic air bronchograms in these specific frames.
*   **Effusion signs:** There is no clear evidence of a large, free-flowing pleural effusion forming a "quad sign" or a "jellyfish sign" (a lung mass floating in a large pool of fluid). The anechoic areas appear to be part of the complex, consolidated lung structure itself.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "consolidation"**
