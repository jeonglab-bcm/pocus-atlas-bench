# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** A large anechoic (black) area is visible, representing a pleural effusion. Most notably, the **jellyfish sign** is present, where the undulating, solid-looking consolidated lung is seen "floating" within the surrounding pleural fluid.
* **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue demonstrates prominent **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic, similar to the texture of the liver, which indicates loss of normal aeration.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
