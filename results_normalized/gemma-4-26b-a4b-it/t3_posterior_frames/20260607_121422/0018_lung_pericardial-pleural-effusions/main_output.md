# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames at the PLAPS point:

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) space in the dependent portion of the image, characteristic of a **pleural effusion** (consistent with a **quad sign**).
* **Consolidation signs:** Within the anechoic fluid, there is a visible, echogenic mass of lung tissue that appears to be floating. This undulating appearance of consolidated/atelectatic lung within an effusion is highly characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**. The lung tissue itself appears solid and echogenic, consistent with **hepatization**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
