# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The ultrasound video shows a posterior lung zone scan at the PLAPS point.

A large anechoic (black) space is visible, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, a piece of lung tissue is seen floating and undulating rhythmically with respiration, which is the classic **jellyfish sign**. This sign itself is indicative of both a pleural effusion and underlying lung consolidation (atelectasis). The consolidated lung tissue appears echogenic and "hepatized" (resembling liver tissue), and its irregular border against the fluid is consistent with a **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
