# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames provided, here is the analysis of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point):

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** A large anechoic (black) area is visible, which is consistent with a **pleural effusion**. This fluid collection occupies a significant portion of the dependent zone.
* **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma within the view demonstrates **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic with a texture very similar to the liver. Within this consolidated tissue, small hyperechoic (bright) dots are visible, which are characteristic of **air bronchograms**. Additionally, an irregular, "shredded" border is visible between the consolidated lung and the adjacent effusion, representing the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
