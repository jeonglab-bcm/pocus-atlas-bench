# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

The ultrasound video shows a clear **PLAPS pattern** in the right hemithorax (zone 2 low).

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion:** There is a large anechoic (black) area, representing a pleural effusion.
*   **Consolidation:** Within this effusion, a solid, echogenic mass is visible. This mass exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma appears solid and similar to the texture of the liver.
*   **Jellyfish sign:** The hepatized lung appears to be floating within the anechoic fluid collection, which is characteristic of the "jellyfish sign."

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
