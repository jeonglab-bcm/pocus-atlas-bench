# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

The ultrasound video shows a view of the posterior/lateral lung zone (PLAPS point). 

**Observations:**
- **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) area representing a pleural effusion. The collection is bounded by the ribs laterally and the pleural line, demonstrating the **quad sign**. Within this fluid, the consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and undulating, which is the characteristic **jellyfish sign**.
- **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue within the effusion appears solid and echogenic, showing **tissue-like hepatization**. The interface between this consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid is irregular, exhibiting the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
