# 0126_lung_jr_loculated-pleural-effusion

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames, the following observations can be made:

*   **Effusion signs**: A large anechoic (black) area is visible in the dependent posterior zone, representing a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, the echogenic lung tissue is seen floating and undulating, which is the characteristic **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs**: The lung tissue within the fluid appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue, which is indicative of **tissue-like hepatization** (consolidation).

Because both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the assessment is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
