# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

Based on the provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the assessment:

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a significant anechoic (black) space visible, which represents a **pleural effusion**. The consolidated lung tissue appears to be floating within this fluid, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma shows **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic with a texture resembling liver tissue rather than normal aerated lung. There is an irregular, "shredded" border between the consolidation and the surrounding fluid (**shred sign**), and small hyperechoic punctate spots within the consolidated area suggest **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"** (as both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible)
