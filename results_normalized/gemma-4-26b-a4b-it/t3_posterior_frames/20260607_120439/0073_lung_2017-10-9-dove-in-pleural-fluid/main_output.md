# 0073_lung_2017-10-9-dove-in-pleural-fluid

The video shows a lung ultrasound at the PLAPS point (posterolateral alveolar and/or pleural syndrome).

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) area visible, which represents a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, a hyperechoic, tissue-like structure is seen moving rhythmically and undulating with respiration. This is the classic **"jellyfish sign"**, where consolidated lung tissue floats within the pleural fluid.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The floating structure itself exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing as solid lung parenchyma rather than an aerated lung with A-lines.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
