# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The sequential ultrasound frames of the right posterior lung zone show the following:

* **Effusion signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) area representing a pleural effusion. In the later frames, the consolidated lung is seen floating and moving within this fluid collection, which is the **jellyfish sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The lung within the effusion shows **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic with a texture similar to liver tissue. The border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid is irregular, consistent with a **shred sign**. Additionally, small, hyperechoic (bright) punctate areas within the consolidated lung are visible, representing **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
