# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound video clip shows a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a visible, echogenic structure representing consolidated or atelectatic lung tissue. As the sequence progresses, this lung tissue can be seen moving or undulating within the surrounding fluid, which is highly characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**. 

Because both a large effusion and consolidated lung tissue are present, the findings are as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
