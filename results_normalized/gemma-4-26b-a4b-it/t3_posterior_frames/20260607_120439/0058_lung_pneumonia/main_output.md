# 0058_lung_pneumonia

The sequential ultrasound frames show the following findings at the PLAPS point:

*   **Effusion signs:** A large anechoic (black) space is visible, bounded by the pleural line and lung tissue, representing a pleural effusion (**Quad sign**). Within this fluid, a piece of lung tissue is seen floating and moving with respiration, characteristic of the **Jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma below the effusion appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver texture (**tissue-like hepatization**). Within this consolidated lung, multiple bright, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible, which are **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
