# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The ultrasound clip demonstrates a significant amount of anechoic (black) space, which represents a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a piece of echogenic, hepatized lung tissue that is moving and undulating rhythmically with respiration. This characteristic finding is known as the **jellyfish sign**, which occurs when atelectatic/consolidated lung floats within an effusion.
