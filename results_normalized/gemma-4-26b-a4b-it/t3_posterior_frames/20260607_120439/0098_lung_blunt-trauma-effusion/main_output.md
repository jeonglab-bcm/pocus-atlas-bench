# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

The ultrasound images demonstrate a classic **PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome)** pattern.

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** There is a significant anechoic (black) area representing a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, the consolidated lung tissue appears to be floating, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue within the effusion is echogenic and appears solid, exhibiting **tissue-like hepatization** (resembling the texture of liver). The irregular, non-smooth border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid/pleural space is consistent with a **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
