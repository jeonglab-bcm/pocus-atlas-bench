# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images show a large, anechoic (black) area in the dependent posterior lung zone, which is highly indicative of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a well-defined, highly echogenic (white), solid-looking area of lung tissue. This appearance of solid lung tissue within the fluid is consistent with lung consolidation (hepatization). The appearance of this consolidated lung tissue "floating" within the surrounding effusion is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.

Therefore, the findings are as follows:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
