# 0078_lung_jellyfish

In this lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence of the right posterior zone (PLAPS point), the following findings are observed:

*   **Effusion signs:** A large anechoic (black) space is visible, representing a pleural effusion. In the later frames, the consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and undulating within this fluid, which is a classic **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue appears solid and echogenic with a **tissue-like hepatization** pattern, characteristic of lung consolidation. There is also evidence of air bronchograms (small hyperechoic foci) within the consolidated lung.

Because both a significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present, the findings are classified as follows:

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
