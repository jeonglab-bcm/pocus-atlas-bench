# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The provided ultrasound video clip shows a view of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point).

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) space representing a **pleural effusion**. The effusion is bounded by the pleural line superiorly and rib shadows laterally, consistent with the **quad sign**. Within the fluid, an echogenic, solid-appearing structure is seen to move and undulate with respiration, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The structure floating within the effusion shows **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing as solid, echogenic lung parenchyma rather than aerated lung. Its irregular border against the fluid/aerated lung is indicative of the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
