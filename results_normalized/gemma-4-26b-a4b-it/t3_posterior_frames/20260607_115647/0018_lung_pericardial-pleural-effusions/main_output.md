# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

In the sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), a large anechoic (black) area is clearly visible, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. 

Within this fluid collection, the lung tissue is seen as a consolidated, echogenic mass that appears to float within the effusion, a finding known as the **"jellyfish sign"**. This consolidated lung tissue shows **tissue-like hepatization** and contains small, bright hyperechoic foci, which are indicative of **air bronchograms**.

Because both significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present, the classification is as follows:

- **plaps = true**
- **type = both**
