# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

The lung ultrasound video shows a large anechoic (black) space, which indicates a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a solid, echogenic structure that is undulating and floating, which is the classic **jellyfish sign**. This sign represents atelectatic, consolidated lung tissue floating within an effusion. The appearance of the lung tissue itself is hepatized (solid-like), confirming alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
