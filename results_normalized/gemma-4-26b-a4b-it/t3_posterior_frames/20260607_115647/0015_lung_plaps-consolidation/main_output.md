# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

The sequential ultrasound frames show the following findings at the PLAPS point:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Effusion:** A large, anechoic (black) space is visible in the dependent portion of the image, indicating a significant pleural effusion. This collection forms a quadrilateral shape bounded by the pleural line, lung line, and rib shadows, consistent with the **quad sign**.
* **Consolidation/Atelectasis:** Within the anechoic fluid, there is a floating, echogenic (bright), solid-appearing mass of lung tissue. This tissue has a solid, granular texture resembling liver parenchyma, which is known as **tissue-like hepatization**.
* **Jellyfish Sign:** The visual pattern of the consolidated lung tissue floating and undulating within the surrounding pleural effusion is a classic representation of the **jellyfish sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
