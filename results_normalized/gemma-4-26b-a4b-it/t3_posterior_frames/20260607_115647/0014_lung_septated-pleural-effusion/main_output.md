# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound video displays a large anechoic (black) area in the posterior lung zone, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, the lung tissue is seen floating and undulating, which is a classic manifestation of the **jellyfish sign**. Additionally, the lung tissue itself appears echogenic and solid, resembling liver tissue (**hepatization**), with irregular, non-smooth borders against the fluid collection, indicating the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
