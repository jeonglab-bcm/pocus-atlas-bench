# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames, the following observations are made:

*   **Consolidation signs:** There is clear evidence of **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of liver tissue. Within this hepatized lung, small hyperechoic (bright) punctate foci are visible, representing **air bronchograms**. Furthermore, the border between the consolidated lung and the more aerated portion is irregular, exhibiting the **shred sign**.
*   **Effusion signs:** The consolidated lung tissue appears to be floating within a surrounding hypoechoic/anechoic space, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**, indicating the presence of a pleural effusion.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = both**
