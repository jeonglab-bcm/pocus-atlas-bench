# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

Based on the provided ultrasound video clip of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) area visible, which represents a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, a mobile, echogenic, and irregularly shaped structure is seen moving or "floating" with respiration. This is the classic **jellyfish sign**, indicating compressed, atelectatic lung tissue within a surrounding effusion.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The moving structure within the effusion exhibits **tissue-like hepatization** (appearing solid and echogenic like liver tissue) and shows an irregular, "shredded" border at its interface with the fluid, known as the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"** (Both an effusion and consolidated/atelectatic lung tissue are clearly visible).
