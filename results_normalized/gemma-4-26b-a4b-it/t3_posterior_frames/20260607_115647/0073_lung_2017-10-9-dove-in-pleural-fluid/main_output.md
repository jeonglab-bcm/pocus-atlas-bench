# 0073_lung_2017-10-9-dove-in-pleural-fluid

The video shows a lung ultrasound scan of a posterior lung zone, demonstrating a classic **PLAPS pattern** (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome).

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) area representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, a piece of lung is seen floating and undulating with respiratory movement, which is a characteristic **jellyfish sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The floating lung fragment appears more echogenic and solid than normal aerated lung, exhibiting **tissue-like hepatization**. The irregular border where it meets the fluid/air is suggestive of a **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"**
