# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The video clip shows a lung ultrasound view at the PLAPS point. 

In the posterior-lateral dependent zone, there is a large anechoic (black) area representing a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, a piece of consolidated, hepatized-looking lung tissue is visible. During respiration, this piece of lung tissue moves and undulates within the fluid, which is the classic **jellyfish sign**. This sign is a combination of both effusion and lung consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
