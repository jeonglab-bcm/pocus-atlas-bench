# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

The ultrasound video shows a posterior lung zone scan (PLAPS point) with several characteristic findings:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) area representing a significant pleural effusion. Most notably, the consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and undulating within this fluid, which is a classic representation of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue within the effusion shows **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic, similar to the texture of the liver. The irregular border of this consolidated tissue against the surrounding fluid or aerated lung can be interpreted as a **shred sign**.

Because both a significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, the findings are classified as follows:

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
