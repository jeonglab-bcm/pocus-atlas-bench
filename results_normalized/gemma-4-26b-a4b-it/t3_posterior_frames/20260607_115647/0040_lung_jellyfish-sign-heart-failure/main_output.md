# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

The provided video clip shows a lung ultrasound scan at the PLAPS point (posterolateral alveolar and/or pleural syndrome point).

**Observation:**
- **Effusion signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) area occupying the central portion of the scan, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. The lung tissue appears to be adjacent to or partially submerged in this fluid collection.
- **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue visible at the periphery (left and right sides) appears echogenic and solid, resembling liver-like texture, which is indicative of **tissue-like hepatization**. Furthermore, the border between this consolidated lung tissue and the adjacent pleural fluid is irregular and jagged, displaying a clear **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
