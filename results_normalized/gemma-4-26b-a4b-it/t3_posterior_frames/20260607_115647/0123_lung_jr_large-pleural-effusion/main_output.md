# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, a piece of lung tissue is seen floating and moving, which is the classic **"jellyfish sign"**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The floating lung tissue itself appears echogenic and solid, demonstrating **tissue-like hepatization**. The border between this consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid/aerated lung appears irregular (**shred sign**).

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
