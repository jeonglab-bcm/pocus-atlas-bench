# 0058_lung_pneumonia

Based on the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the assessment:

**Observations:**
* **Effusion signs:** A large, anechoic (black) space is clearly visible, representing a pleural effusion. The consolidated lung tissue appears to be floating within this fluid, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
* **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue within the effusion shows **hepatization** (it appears solid and similar to liver tissue) and contains punctate hyperechoic foci, which are **air bronchograms** (air trapped within the bronchi of the consolidated lung).

**Conclusion:**
* **plaps = true**
* **type = "both"** (Both significant pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are present).
