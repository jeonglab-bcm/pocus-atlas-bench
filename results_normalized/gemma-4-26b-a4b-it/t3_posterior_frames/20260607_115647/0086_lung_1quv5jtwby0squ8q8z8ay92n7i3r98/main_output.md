 ultrasound video clip shows a classic presentation of a **PLAPS (posterolateral alveolar and/or pleural syndrome)** pattern.

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) space indicating a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a fragment of consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and undulating, which is a characteristic **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The floating lung fragment shows **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic like liver tissue. The border where the consolidated lung meets the fluid is irregular, demonstrating a **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"** (as both pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible)
