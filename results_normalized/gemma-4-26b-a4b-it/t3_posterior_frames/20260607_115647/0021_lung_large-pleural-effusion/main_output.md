# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone:

*   **Observation of Effusion Signs**: There is a large **anechoic (black) space** that represents a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, the lung tissue is seen floating and undulating, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Observation of Consolidation Signs**: The lung tissue within the effusion appears echogenic and lacks normal aeration, displaying **tissue-like hepatization**. This indicates that the lung is consolidated or atelectatic.

Since both a significant pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are clearly visible, the findings satisfy the criteria for a PLAPS pattern involving both components.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
