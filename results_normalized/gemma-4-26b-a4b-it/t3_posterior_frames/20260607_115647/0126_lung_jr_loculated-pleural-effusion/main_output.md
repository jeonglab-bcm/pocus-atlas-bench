# 0126_lung_jr_loculated-pleural-effusion

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames, the following observations can be made:

*   **Effusion signs:** A large anechoic (black) area is visible in the dependent posterior region, representing a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, a piece of lung tissue is seen floating and undulating, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The floating lung tissue exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic rather than aerated. This indicates lung consolidation.

Because both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are clearly visible, the findings are diagnostic of a PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
