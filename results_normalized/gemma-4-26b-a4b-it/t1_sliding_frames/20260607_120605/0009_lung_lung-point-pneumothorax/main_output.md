# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames, here is the evaluation:

**Observation:**
The ultrasound images show a bright, continuous pleural line at the top of the field. Below this line, there is a dense pattern of numerous, vertical, bright comet-tail artifacts known as **B-lines**. These B-lines are confluent, filling much of the lung field, which is characteristic of an interstitial syndrome (often seen in conditions like pulmonary edema). 

When examining the sequence of frames, the pattern of these B-lines is not static. There is a visible **shimmering and slight variation in the speckle pattern** of the B-lines between consecutive frames, indicating movement of the underlying lung tissue against the chest wall.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
