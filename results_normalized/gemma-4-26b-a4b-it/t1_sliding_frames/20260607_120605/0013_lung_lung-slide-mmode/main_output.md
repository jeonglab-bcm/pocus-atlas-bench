# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on the analysis of the provided ultrasound frames, here is the assessment:

**Observations:**

* **M-mode Trace:** The M-mode trace (top portion of each frame) shows a distinct transition at the depth of the pleural line (approximately 2.5 cm). 
    * **Above the pleural line:** There is a pattern of straight, horizontal, parallel lines. This represents the static chest wall, often referred to as the "barcode" or "stratosphere" part of the sign.
    * **Below the pleural line:** There is a granular, speckled, or "sandy" pattern. This is the characteristic "seashore" part of the sign.
* **B-mode Image:** The pleural line is visible as a bright, relatively horizontal interface. Below this line, the lung tissue displays a granular, speckled appearance.

**Conclusion:**

The presence of the **seashore sign** on the M-mode trace (the transition from horizontal lines above the pleura to a granular pattern below it) is a definitive indicator that **pleural sliding is present**.
