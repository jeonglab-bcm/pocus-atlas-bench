# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the analysis of the video frames:

*   **Pleural Line:** A bright, horizontal, and slightly textured/granular line is visible, which represents the pleural line.
*   **Sub-pleural Artifacts:** There are multiple, prominent, vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (known as **B-lines** or comet-tail artifacts) originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the image.
*   **Movement:** Upon reviewing the sequence, there is a visible shimmering and dynamic change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern and the B-lines between consecutive frames, indicating movement of the lung surface.

According to the provided signs, the presence of prominent B-lines originating from the pleural line and the shimmering sub-pleural pattern are indicators of sliding.

**Conclusion: Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
