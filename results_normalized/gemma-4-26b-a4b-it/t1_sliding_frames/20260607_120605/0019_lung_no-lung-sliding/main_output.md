# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the provided ultrasound frames:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line Appearance:** The pleural line (the bright, horizontal interface at approximately 1.5 cm depth) exhibits a **shimmering and granular texture** rather than being a perfectly smooth, static line.
* **Sub-pleural Speckle Pattern:** As the frames progress, the **speckle pattern in the region immediately below the pleural line shows subtle variations** and movement between consecutive frames. This lack of a static, frozen appearance is a key indicator of lung movement against the chest wall.
* **Absence of Static Signs:** There is no evidence of a completely static, sharp, and immobile pleural line, which would indicate a lack of sliding (e.g., as seen in a pneumothorax). Additionally, no single transition point (lung point) is visible where sliding stops, suggesting the presence of sliding is uniform across the scanned area.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
