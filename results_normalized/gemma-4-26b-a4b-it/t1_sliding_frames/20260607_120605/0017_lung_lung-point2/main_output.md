# 0017_lung_lung-point2

Based on the provided sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS) M-mode image:

**Frame-by-frame analysis:**
Across all the provided frames, the M-mode trace shows a consistent pattern.
- **Above the pleural line (from 0 to approximately 1.5 cm depth):** There is a series of parallel, horizontal lines. This represents the stationary chest wall tissue.
- **At the pleural line (approximately 1.5 cm depth):** There is a distinct interface between the static tissue above and the dynamic lung tissue below.
- **Below the pleural line (from 1.5 cm depth downwards):** Instead of continuing with horizontal lines, the image displays a repeating pattern of vertical columns containing a granular, speckled, or "sand-like" texture. This texture corresponds to the movement of the lung during the respiratory cycle.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of a granular pattern below the static horizontal lines of the chest wall is characteristic of the **"seashore sign"** on M-mode. This sign is a definitive indicator that lung movement is being captured.

Therefore, **pleural sliding is present**.
