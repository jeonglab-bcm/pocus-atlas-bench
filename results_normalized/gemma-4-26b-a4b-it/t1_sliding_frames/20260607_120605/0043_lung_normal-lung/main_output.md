# 0043_lung_normal-lung

Based on the sequential analysis of the lung ultrasound video, here is the assessment:

**Frame-by-Frame Observation:**
* **Pleural Line:** The bright, horizontal interface at the top of the lung field (the pleural line) is not a static, sharp line. Instead, it exhibits a shimmering, granular, and textured appearance.
* **Artifacts:** Multiple vertical, hyperechoic (bright) lines, known as **B-lines** (comet-tail artifacts), are clearly visible. These lines originate from the pleural line and extend deep into the image.
* **Movement:** These B-lines and the granular speckle pattern below the pleural line show dynamic movement between consecutive frames, indicating they are moving in synchrony with the breathing cycle.

**Assessment of Signs:**
* **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
    * **Shimmering pleural line:** Observed. The line has a granular/shimmering texture rather than being perfectly smooth and static.
    * **Comet-tail artifacts (B-lines):** Observed. Numerous B-lines are present and they exhibit movement across the frames.
    * **Speckle pattern variation:** Observed. The sub-pleural texture changes between frames.
* **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** Not observed. The line is not static, and the artifacts are not fixed.
* **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** Not observed. The entire visualized area shows signs of movement.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
