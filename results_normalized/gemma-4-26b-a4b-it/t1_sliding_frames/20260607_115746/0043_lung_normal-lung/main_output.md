# 0043_lung_normal-lung

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames in the lung ultrasound video:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line:** The pleural line, located at the top of the lung field, is bright, textured, and shows a shimmering quality.
* **Sub-pleural Pattern:** Below the pleural line, there is a prominent granular, "sandy" speckle pattern.
* **Dynamic Changes:** When comparing consecutive frames, there is a subtle but visible movement and variation in the speckle pattern and the texture of the tissue immediately beneath the pleura. This indicates that the visceral and parietal pleura are moving against each other during the respiratory cycle.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **present**.
