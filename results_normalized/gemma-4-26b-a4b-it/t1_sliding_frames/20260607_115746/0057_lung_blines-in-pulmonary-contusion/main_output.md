# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

*   **Frames 1-5:** The pleural line is visible, and there is a noticeable shimmering, granular texture in the subpleural space. This granular pattern and the variation in the speckle pattern between frames are characteristic of **pleural sliding being present**.
*   **Frames 6-7:** A large, anechoic (black) area appears on the left side of the image, indicating a lack of ultrasound signal from the lung tissue, which is characteristic of a pneumothorax where **pleural sliding is absent**. However, on the right side of the image in these same frames, a pleural line with subpleural texture remains visible, indicating that **sliding is still present** in that specific region.

The observation of a clear transition within the same frame/sequence—where sliding is present on one side and absent on the other—is the definitive sign of a **lung point**.

**Conclusion: Both (lung point)**
