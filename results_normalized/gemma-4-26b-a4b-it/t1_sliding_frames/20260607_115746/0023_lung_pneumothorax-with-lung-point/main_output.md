# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames in the lung ultrasound video:

1.  **Pleural Line Observation:** A bright, hyperechoic horizontal line representing the pleural line is clearly visible across the image. This line appears to have a textured, shimmering quality rather than being perfectly smooth and static.
2.  **Artifacts:** Several vertical, comet-tail-like artifacts, known as **B-lines**, can be seen originating from the pleural line (particularly on the left side of the screen). The presence of B-lines is a strong indicator of lung sliding.
3.  **Sub-pleural Pattern:** The area below the pleural line shows a granular, "noisy" speckle pattern. This pattern is not static; the specific arrangement of these granules changes between consecutive frames, which is characteristic of the "seashore sign" seen in B-mode during active lung sliding.
4.  **Transition Point:** There is no visible transition area (lung point) where sliding stops; the entire visible field of view shows these signs of movement.

**Conclusion: Pleural sliding is present.**
