# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound, here is my analysis:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line:** A bright, horizontal pleural line is clearly visible.
* **Sub-pleural Movement:** The most striking feature is the granular speckle pattern located directly beneath the pleural line. As the frames progress, this speckle pattern is not static; it constantly shifts, changes texture, and moves. This "shimmering" effect is highly characteristic of the visceral pleura sliding against the parietal pleura.
* **Absence of Static Features:** There is no evidence of a static, "barcode-like" appearance or a perfectly smooth, unchanging pleural line that would suggest a lack of movement. Furthermore, there is no visible transition point between a sliding area and a non-sliding area (no lung point).

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.** The primary indicator is the dynamic and constantly changing speckle pattern beneath the pleural line across the consecutive frames.
