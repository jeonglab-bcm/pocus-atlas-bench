# 0041_lung_2018-1-16-normal-lung-slide

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames, here is the assessment:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line:** A bright, horizontal interface (the pleural line) is visible in the upper portion of the image.
* **Sub-pleural Texture:** Below the pleural line, there is a prominent granular, "sandy" texture of the lung parenchyma.
* **Dynamic Speckle Pattern:** When comparing consecutive frames, the speckle pattern (the bright white dots) within the lung tissue below the pleural line is constantly changing. The dots appear, move, and disappear from one frame to the next, creating a shimmering effect.
* **Absence of static A-lines:** The image does not show perfectly static, unchanging horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) that would indicate a lack of movement.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The dynamic and shifting speckle pattern below the pleural line is a key indicator of lung sliding in B-mode ultrasound.
