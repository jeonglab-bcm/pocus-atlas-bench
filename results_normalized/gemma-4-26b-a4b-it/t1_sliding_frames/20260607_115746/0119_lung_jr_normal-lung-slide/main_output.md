# 0119_lung_jr_normal-lung-slide

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
* **Pleural Line:** In all frames, a distinct, bright, and smooth horizontal line is visible, representing the pleural line.
* **Artifacts:** Below the pleural line, there are several horizontal, equidistant lines known as A-lines (reverberation artifacts), which are characteristic of air in the lung.
* **Temporal Observation:** Comparing the frames in sequence, the pleural line remains perfectly static. There is no perceptible shimmering, granular texture changes, or movement of the line. Furthermore, no B-lines (vertical comet-tail artifacts) are observed originating from the pleural line. The A-lines also remain constant and stationary across all frames.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.** 

The lack of any movement or shimmering of the pleural line across the frames, combined with the presence of stable A-lines, is a key indicator of absent pleural sliding, which is commonly associated with a pneumothorax.
