# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames in the lung ultrasound video:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line:** A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is clearly visible. As the frames progress, this line exhibits a subtle shimmering or granular texture.
* **B-lines:** Multiple, prominent vertical comet-tail artifacts (B-lines) are seen originating directly from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the screen. 
* **Dynamic Changes:** Upon comparing consecutive frames, the pattern of the B-lines and the sub-pleural speckle pattern show continuous, subtle variations rather than remaining perfectly static. This "shimmering" effect indicates movement between the visceral and parietal pleura.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.** The presence of a shimmering pleural line and the dynamic variation of the B-line artifacts across the frames are characteristic signs of active pleural sliding.
