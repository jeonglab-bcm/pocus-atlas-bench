Based on the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) frames, here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **M-mode Analysis:** The M-mode image displays two distinct patterns. Above the pleural line (approximately at a depth of 1.5 cm), there are parallel, horizontal lines, which represent the static chest wall. Below the pleural line, there is a grainy, speckled, or "sandy" texture.
*   **Sign Identification:** This specific combination of a horizontal pattern above the pleural line and a granular pattern below it is known as the **"seashore sign."**
*   **B-mode Analysis:** The B-mode image shows a pleural line that appears to have some texture, consistent with movement.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.** The presence of the "seashore sign" on M-mode is a definitive indicator of normal pleural sliding.
