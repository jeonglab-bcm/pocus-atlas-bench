# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

Based on the sequential frames of this left lung ultrasound, here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** A bright, hyperechoic horizontal line is clearly visible. The line itself shows a subtle shimmering or textured quality rather than being perfectly smooth and static.
*   **Artifacts:** Numerous vertical, comet-tail artifacts (known as **B-lines**) originate from the pleural line and extend deep into the image.
*   **Sub-pleural Speckle:** The pattern of echoes below the pleural line is granular and dynamic. When comparing consecutive frames, there is visible movement and variation in the speckle pattern, which indicates that the visceral and parietal pleura are sliding against each other.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
