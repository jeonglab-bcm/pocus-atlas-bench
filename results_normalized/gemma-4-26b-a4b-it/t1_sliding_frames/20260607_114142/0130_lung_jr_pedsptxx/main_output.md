# 0130_lung_jr_pedsptxx

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears as a bright, textured, and somewhat shimmering interface. It is not a perfectly smooth, static line.
* **Subpleural Speckle Pattern:** When comparing the consecutive frames, there is subtle variation in the position and pattern of the granular "speckles" immediately below the pleural line. This dynamic, "dancing" appearance of the subpleural speckles is a hallmark of pleural sliding in B-mode ultrasound.
* **Absence of Barcode/Stratosphere Signs:** There is no evidence of a static, horizontal-only pattern that would indicate an absence of sliding.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
