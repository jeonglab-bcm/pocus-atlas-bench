# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the ultrasound video frames, here is the assessment:

**Observation:**
* **Pleural Line Appearance:** The pleural line exhibits a subtle shimmering or "gliding" motion rather than being a perfectly smooth, static line.
* **Sub-pleural Speckle Pattern:** The grainy texture (speckle pattern) immediately beneath the pleural line changes dynamically between consecutive frames, which is characteristic of movement.
* **Lack of Static Signs:** The pleural line does not appear fixed or unchanging, nor do the A-lines stay perfectly static throughout the respiratory cycle.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
