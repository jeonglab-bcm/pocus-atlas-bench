# 0041_lung_2018-1-16-normal-lung-slide

Based on the analysis of the ultrasound video frames:

**Observations:**
* **Shimmering Pleural Line:** The hyperechoic pleural line exhibits a shimmering, granular texture as the frames progress.
* **Moving B-lines:** Multiple bright, vertical comet-tail artifacts (B-lines) are clearly visible originating from the pleural line. These artifacts show slight dynamic changes in position and intensity between frames.
* **Dynamic Speckle Pattern:** The granular, "sandy" appearance of the lung parenchyma below the pleural line varies from frame to frame, rather than remaining perfectly static.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **present**.
