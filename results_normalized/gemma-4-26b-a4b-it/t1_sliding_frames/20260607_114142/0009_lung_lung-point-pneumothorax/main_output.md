# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound video, here is my analysis:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line Appearance:** The pleural line, located near the top of the ultrasound field, exhibits a shimmering and granular texture rather than being perfectly smooth and static.
* **Artifacts:** There are numerous, prominent vertical, bright artifacts (comet-tail artifacts) known as **B-lines** that originate from the pleural line and extend toward the bottom of the image.
* **Movement:** Throughout the video sequence, there is a subtle, granular movement in the speckle pattern of the tissue immediately below the pleural line, indicating dynamic interaction.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **PRESENT**. The combination of a shimmering pleural line and the presence of B-lines originating from that line are hallmark signs of pleural sliding.
