# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound, here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **Left and central portions of the pleural line:** The pleural line is bright and exhibits a highly granular, "shimmering" texture. There is a prominent speckle pattern below this line that appears to change slightly between frames, which are characteristic signs of **pleural sliding being present**.
*   **Right portion of the pleural line:** As the pleural line moves toward the right side of the image, the texture becomes noticeably smoother, thinner, and less granular, appearing more static. This represents the sign of **pleural sliding being absent**.
*   **Transition:** There is a clear, visible transition point where the shimmering, textured segment meets the smooth, static segment.

**Conclusion:**
**Both** signs are present, indicating a **lung point**. This finding is diagnostic of a pneumothorax, marking the specific area where the lung re-expands and meets the chest wall.
