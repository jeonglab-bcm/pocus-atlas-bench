# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

1.  **Frames 1-5:** A bright, relatively continuous pleural line is visible. Below this line, there is a granular, shimmering pattern of speckles that changes in shape and position between consecutive frames. This indicates that **pleural sliding is present**.
2.  **Frames 6-8:** A large, black area (signal dropout) appears on the left side of the image. In this region, no pleural line is discernible, and there is no visible movement or speckle pattern. This absence of the pleural line and sliding is characteristic of a pneumothorax, where **sliding is absent**.
3.  **Overall Assessment:** The sequence shows a clear transition between an area where pleural sliding is seen (the granular area) and an area where it is absent (the large black area). This transition zone is known as a **lung point**.

**Conclusion: Both (lung point)**
