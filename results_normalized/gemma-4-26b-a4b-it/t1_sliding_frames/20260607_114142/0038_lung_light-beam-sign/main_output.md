# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video, here is the analysis:

### **Frame-by-Frame Analysis**
*   **Frames 1-2:** The pleural line is visible as a bright, hyperechoic (white) interface. A prominent vertical, comet-tail artifact (a B-line) is seen originating from the pleural line and extending deep into the image. The area just beneath the pleura shows a granular, speckle pattern.
*   **Frames 3-4:** There is visible shimmering and subtle movement of the pleural line. The position and texture of the B-line fluctuate slightly between frames, indicating that the lung is moving against the chest wall.
*   **Frames 5-6:** The pleural line maintains a textured, non-static appearance. The granular speckle pattern in the subpleural region changes slightly from one frame to the next.
*   **Frame 7:** The shimmering effect on the pleural line remains consistent throughout the sequence.

### **Assessment of Signs**
*   **Shimmering/granular pleural line:** **Present.** The pleural line is not a smooth, static line; it exhibits a fine, moving, and granular texture.
*   **Comet-tail artifacts (B-lines):** **Present.** A clear B-line is visible originating from the pleural line.
*   **Speckle pattern variation:** **Present.** The subpleural speckle pattern shows subtle variations between consecutive frames.
*   **Signs of absence (static line/barcode sign):** **Absent.** The line is not perfectly smooth or static.
*   **Signs of both (lung point):** **Absent.** The entire visible pleural line demonstrates movement; there is no clearly defined transition to a static, non-sliding segment.

### **Conclusion**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
