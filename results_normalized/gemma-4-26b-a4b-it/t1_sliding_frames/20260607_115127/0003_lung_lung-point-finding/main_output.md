# 0003_lung_lung-point-finding

Based on the analysis of the ultrasound video frames, here are the observations and conclusion:

**Observations:**
* **Shimmering Pleural Line:** The bright, hyperechoic pleural line exhibits a continuous, granular, and shimmering motion rather than remaining static and smooth.
* **Dynamic Speckle Pattern:** The pattern of speckles/granularity immediately beneath the pleural line changes visibly from one frame to the next, indicating movement.
* **B-line Movement:** Vertical artifacts (B-lines) are visible and move in synchronization with the shimmering pleural line.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **present**.
