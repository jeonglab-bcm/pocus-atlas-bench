# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

Based on the provided sequence of lung ultrasound frames, here is the analysis:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frames 1–5:** The pleural line is visible, and there is a noticeable shimmering, granular texture with subpleural speckle patterns that vary slightly from frame to frame. These are classic indicators that **pleural sliding is present**.
*   **Frames 6–7:** A large, dark, anechoic area appears on the left side of the image. In this specific region, the shimmering pleural line and granular subpleural texture are lost, and no ultrasound signal is returned from the lung tissue. This indicates that **pleural sliding is absent** in this area (often due to air in the pleural space, as in a pneumothorax).

**Conclusion:**

Because the video shows a clear transition where sliding is present in some areas and absent in others, it demonstrates a **lung point**.

**Final Assessment: Both (Lung Point)**
