# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the provided ultrasound images, here is the analysis:

*   **Pleural Line:** A bright, continuous, and slightly textured (granular) pleural line is visible across the upper portion of the image.
*   **Artifacts:** On the left side of the image, there are several prominent, vertical, bright artifacts originating from the pleural line. These are **B-lines** (comet-tail artifacts).
*   **Movement/Signs:** The presence of B-lines is a strong indicator that the lung is moving against the pleural line. The pleural line itself has a shimmering, granular appearance rather than being a perfectly smooth, static line.

Because there is a shimmering pleural line and clear B-lines, the findings are characteristic of active lung movement.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
