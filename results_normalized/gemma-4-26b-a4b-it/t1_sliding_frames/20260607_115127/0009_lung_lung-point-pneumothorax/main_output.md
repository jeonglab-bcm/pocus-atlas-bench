# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video, here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line Appearance:** The pleural line (the bright interface at the top of the lung field) exhibits a shimmering, granular texture rather than being perfectly smooth and static.
* **Sub-pleural Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line is not uniform or static; it shows subtle variations in brightness and position between consecutive frames, which is characteristic of the "seashore sign" (granular texture) in B-mode.
* **Artifacts:** There is a visible granular pattern of speckle below the pleural line, and the vertical artifacts (B-lines) appear to fluctuate slightly with the respiratory motion, further indicating lung movement against the chest wall.
* **Absence of Static Signs:** There is no evidence of a purely static, sharply defined line or a lack of sub-pleural speckle variation, which would be indicative of absent sliding (e.g., in pneumothorax).

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
