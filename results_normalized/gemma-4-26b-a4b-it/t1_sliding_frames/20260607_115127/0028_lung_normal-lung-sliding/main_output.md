# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the ultrasound frames:

**Frame-by-frame analysis:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright, horizontal interface between the chest wall and the lung) is not a single, static, smooth line. Instead, it exhibits a shimmering and textured appearance.
*   **Sub-pleural Area:** Below the pleural line, there is a prominent, granular, "sandy" speckle pattern. This pattern is dynamic; the individual speckles and their positions shift and change from one frame to the next, which is characteristic of normal lung movement.
*   **Artifacts:** There are fine, vertical, comet-tail-like artifacts originating from the pleural line, which are indicative of lung sliding.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
