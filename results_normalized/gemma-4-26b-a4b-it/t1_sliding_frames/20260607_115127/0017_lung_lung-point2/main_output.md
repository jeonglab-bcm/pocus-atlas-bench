# 0017_lung_lung-point2

Based on the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) M-mode images, here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **M-mode Pattern:** The M-mode image displays a classic **"seashore sign."** 
    *   **Above the pleural line** (from depth 0 to approximately 1.5 cm), there is a pattern of continuous, parallel horizontal lines. This represents the stationary chest wall tissue.
    *   **At the pleural line** (approximately 1.5 cm depth), there is a bright, horizontal interface.
    *   **Below the pleural line** (below 1.5 cm), the pattern transitions into a granular, "sandy" or "speckled" appearance. This granular pattern represents the movement of the lung sliding against the chest wall during respiration.
*   **B-mode image:** The top B-mode image shows a visible pleural line with some sub-pleural speckle.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The presence of the seashore sign on M-mode is a definitive indicator of lung sliding at the scanned location.
