# 0119_lung_jr_normal-lung-slide

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound, here is the analysis:

**Frame-by-frame Observation:**
* **Frame 1:** A bright, hyperechoic (white) horizontal line representing the pleural line is visible. Below this line, there is a textured, granular, or speckled pattern.
* **Frames 2-4:** The granular pattern beneath the pleural line shows subtle, continuous changes in the distribution and position of the speckles between consecutive frames. The pleural line itself appears to have a slight "shimmering" quality.
* **Frames 5-7:** The shimmering and granular appearance remains consistent. The pattern below the pleura is not static; it exhibits dynamic movement, where the speckles shift slightly with each frame, characteristic of the lung sliding against the parietal pleura.

**Assessment of Signs:**
* **Shimmering/Granular pattern:** Present. There is a distinct granular texture below the pleural line that is not static.
* **Dynamic speckle pattern:** Present. The sub-pleural speckle pattern differs slightly between consecutive frames, indicating movement.
* **Absence of static A-lines/Barcode sign:** The area below the pleura is not characterized by perfectly static, unchanging horizontal lines, which would suggest a lack of sliding.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
