# 0003_lung_lung-point-finding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames in the lung ultrasound video, here is the assessment:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
* **Pleural Line Appearance:** Throughout the video, the pleural line (the bright horizontal line near the top) is not static. Instead, it exhibits a shimmering, granular, and slightly irregular texture.
* **Sub-pleural Patterns:** Below the pleural line, there is a dynamic, "sandy" speckle pattern that changes slightly from frame to frame, indicating movement of the lung against the chest wall.
* **Artifacts:** Multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts, known as **B-lines**, are clearly visible originating from the pleural line and extending deep into the image. The presence of B-lines is a strong indicator that the lung is in contact with the pleura, which occurs during pleural sliding.
* **Absence of Lung Point:** There is no identifiable transition point within the field of view where the sliding pattern changes to a static "barcode" pattern. The sliding appears consistent across the imaged area.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The combination of a shimmering pleural line and the presence of dynamic B-lines confirms active sliding.
