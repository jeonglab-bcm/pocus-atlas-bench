# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames in the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

*   **Observations:** The image shows a bright, hyperechoic (white) pleural line. Directly beneath this line, there is a granular, "sandy" texture composed of small speckles. As the sequence of frames progresses, the specific pattern and position of these sub-pleural speckles change slightly from frame to frame. This dynamic variation in the texture below the pleural line is a key indicator of movement at the interface. There is no area along the visible pleural line that appears perfectly smooth, static, or unchanging.
*   **Conclusion:** **Pleural sliding is present.**
