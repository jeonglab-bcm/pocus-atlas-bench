# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

 on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound video:

**Observations:**
* **Shimmering Pleural Line:** The bright interface representing the pleural line exhibits a shimmering, granular texture rather than being a static, smooth line.
* **B-line Artifacts:** Vertical, bright, comet-tail-like artifacts (B-lines) are visible originating from the pleural line.
* **Dynamic Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern in the sub-pleural area changes subtly between consecutive frames, which is a characteristic sign of the lung sliding against the parietal pleura.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
