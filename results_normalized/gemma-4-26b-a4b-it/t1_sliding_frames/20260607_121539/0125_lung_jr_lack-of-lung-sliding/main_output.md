* **Pleural Line:** A bright, somewhat granular-textured pleural line is visible beneath the chest wall layers.
* **Artifacts:** Multiple, prominent, vertical, hyperechoic (bright white) lines are clearly visible, originating from the pleural line and extending deep into the lung field. These are characteristic **B-lines** (also known as comet-tail artifacts).
* **Temporal Analysis:** Although the frames provided are identical, the presence of these specific artifacts is a key diagnostic indicator.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

This conclusion is based on the presence of **comet-tail artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line**, which is one of the specified signs of pleural sliding in the provided criteria.
