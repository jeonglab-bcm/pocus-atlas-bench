# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the video frames, here is the assessment of the lung ultrasound:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright, horizontal interface in the middle of the image) is not static; it exhibits a shimmering, textured, or granular appearance as the frames progress.
* **Artifacts:** Multiple vertical, hyperechoic (bright) lines, known as **B-lines**, are clearly visible originating from the pleural line and extending towards the bottom of the screen.
* **Sub-pleural Texture:** The area beneath the pleural line shows a dynamic, "sandy" or granular speckle pattern that changes slightly between frames, which is characteristic of a moving lung.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.** The presence of a shimmering pleural line and B-lines are definitive signs of lung sliding.
