# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

Based on an analysis of the provided frames, here is the assessment of the lung ultrasound:

**Frame-by-Frame Observation:**
* **Frames 1–5:** The pleural line is visible, and there is a shimmering, granular texture (speckle pattern) visible in the sub-pleural space. This indicates the presence of pleural sliding.
* **Frames 6–8:** A significant change occurs. A large, dark (anechoic) area appears on the left side of the image. In this area, the pleural line and the granular lung texture are no longer visible, which is characteristic of the absence of pleural sliding (likely due to air in the pleural space, or pneumothorax).
* **Transition:** There is a clear boundary where the textured, sliding lung meets the dark, non-sliding area.

**Conclusion:**
The video demonstrates a visible transition point where pleural sliding is present on one side and absent on the other. This phenomenon is known as a **lung point**, which is a highly specific sign for a pneumothorax.

**Final Assessment:**
Signs of **BOTH** (lung point) are present.
