# 0043_lung_normal-lung

Based on an analysis of the provided lung ultrasound video clip:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
- Throughout the sequential frames, the pleural line (the bright horizontal line at the top of the field) exhibits a subtle shimmering effect.
- The region immediately below the pleural line shows a highly dynamic, granular, and speckle-like pattern.
- This sub-pleural speckle pattern is not static; it changes its appearance and distribution from one frame to the next, indicating movement.
- The ultrasound field does not show a repetitive, static "barcode" pattern of horizontal lines, which would instead indicate a lack of movement.

**Signs Observed:**
- **Shimmering pleural line:** The interface of the pleura shows texture and movement.
- **Variable sub-pleural speckle pattern:** The granular texture below the pleura differs between consecutive frames, which is a key indicator of pleural sliding in B-mode.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
