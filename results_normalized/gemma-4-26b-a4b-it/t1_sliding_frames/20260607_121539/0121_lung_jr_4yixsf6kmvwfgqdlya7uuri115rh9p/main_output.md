Based on the analysis of the sequential M-mode ultrasound frames:

*   **Observations:** The M-mode trace shows a distinct pattern. Above the bright pleural line, there is a series of static, horizontal lines representing the stationary superficial tissues. *   **Conclusion:** The presence of the seashore sign on M-mode is a definitive indicator that **pleural sliding is PRESENT**.
