* **p00: Seashore** — The image shows bright horizontal lines above the pleural line and a distinct granular/sandy texture below the pleural line, indicating the presence of pleural sliding.
* **p01: Seashore** — Similar to p00, there is a clear granular texture below the pleural line.
* **p02: Stratosphere** — The pattern consists of continuous, dense, parallel horizontal lines both above and below the pleural line with no visible granularity, indicating an absence of pleural sliding.
* **p03: Stratosphere** — Similar to p02, the image shows a continuous "barcode" pattern throughout, indicating no pleural sliding.

### **Step 2 — Overall decision:**

**Overall label: both (lung point)**

**Reasoning:** There is a clear spatial transition across the examined positions, moving from a **seashore** pattern (p00, p01) to a **stratosphere** pattern (p02, p03). This transition between sliding and non-sliding lung indicates a lung point.
