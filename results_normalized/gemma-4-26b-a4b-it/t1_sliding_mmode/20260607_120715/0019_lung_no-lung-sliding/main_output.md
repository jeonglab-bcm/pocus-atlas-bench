# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on the provided M-mode images, here is the step-by-step analysis:

**Step 1 — Per-position classification:**

* **p00 (Image 1):** Above the pleural line, there are horizontal parallel lines. Below the pleural line, there is a visible granular/mottled "sandy" texture rather than continuous, sharp lines. The pleural line itself shows some irregularity. **Classification: Seashore**
* **p01 (Image 2):** Similar to p00, the region below the bright pleural line displays a grainy, mottled texture characteristic of lung sliding. **Classification: Seashore**
* **p02 (Image 3):** This image shows dense, continuous, and perfectly parallel horizontal lines both above and below the pleural line. There is no granular texture present, and the pleural line is very straight and smooth. **Classification: Stratosphere/Barcode**
* **p03 (Image 4):** Like p02, this strip consists entirely of continuous parallel lines with a smooth, straight pleural line and no granularity below it. **Classification: Stratosphere/Barcode**

*(Note: Only 4 images were provided for analysis; the classification is based on the available data.)*

**Step 2 — Overall decision:**

There is a clear spatial transition across the analyzed positions: some positions (p00, p01) exhibit the **Seashore** pattern, while others (p02, p03) exhibit the **Stratosphere/Barcode** pattern. This coexistence of both patterns across different lateral positions indicates the presence of a lung point.

**Overall Label: both (lung point)**
