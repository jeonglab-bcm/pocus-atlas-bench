# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

**Observations:**
The image shows a lung ultrasound frame from the posterior lung zone. The area appears largely anechoic (black), suggesting fluid. The upper border is defined by a pleural line (horizontal hyperechoic line), and the lower border is less distinct but appears to be defined by the lung line (hyperechoic line). The lateral borders are defined by the rib shadows. This configuration forms a quadrilateral shape. There is no clear sign of lung sliding (dynamic movement of the lung line relative to the pleural line), air bronchograms, or tissue-like consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
Based on the presence of the anechoic space bounded by the pleural line, lung line, and rib shadows, the image shows signs consistent with pleural effusion (specifically, the quad sign). There are no clear signs of consolidation. Therefore, the PLAPS pattern is present, and it is predominantly due to effusion.

**Classification:**
- **plaps = true** (Presence of effusion signs)
- **type = "effusion"** (Predominantly fluid collection signs)
