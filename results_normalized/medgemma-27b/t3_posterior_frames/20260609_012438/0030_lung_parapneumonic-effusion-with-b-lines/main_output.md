# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

**Analysis:**

1.  **Image Quality:** The image is a B-mode ultrasound frame from the posterior lung zone (RT H/T ZONE 2 LOW).
2.  **Pleural Line:** A distinct pleural line is visible superiorly.
3.  **Lung Line:** A lung line is visible inferior to the pleural line.
4.  **Fluid Collection:** Below the lung line, there is a large, anechoic (black) area. This suggests a fluid collection (pleural effusion).
5.  **Quad Sign:** The anechoic area is bounded by the pleural line, the lung line, and the rib shadow laterally, forming a clear quadrilateral shape. This is characteristic of the Quad sign, strongly indicating pleural effusion.
6.  **Lung Parenchyma:** The lung parenchyma superior to the pleural line appears relatively normal, showing some lung sliding (though not perfectly captured in this single static frame). There are no clear signs of consolidation (tissue-like hepatization, shred sign, air bronchograms) within the visible lung parenchyma.
7.  **Movement:** The image is static, so dynamic signs like sinusoid sign or jellyfish sign cannot be assessed.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the presence of the Quad sign (anechoic space bounded by pleural line, lung line, and rib shadow), there is clear evidence of pleural effusion in the posterior lung zone. There are no definite signs of consolidation. Therefore, the pattern is consistent with effusion.

**Final Classification:**

*   **plaps = true** (Presence of effusion signs)
*   **type = "effusion"** (Predominantly fluid collection signs)
