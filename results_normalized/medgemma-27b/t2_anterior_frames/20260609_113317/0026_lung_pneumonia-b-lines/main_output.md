# 0026_lung_pneumonia-b-lines

**Image Analysis:**

The ultrasound image shows the anterior lung zone. The probe is oriented with the right side of the image being superior and the left side inferior.

1.  **B-lines Assessment:**
    *   **Presence:** There are multiple vertical hyperechoic lines originating from the pleural line and extending towards the bottom of the image. These lines appear to move with respiration (though this is a static image, the pattern is characteristic).
    *   **Type:** The B-lines appear relatively close together, and in some areas, they seem to merge or coalesce, obscuring the A-lines (horizontal reverberations) in the intercostal spaces. This pattern is consistent with a ground-glass appearance or a mixed pattern with coalescent septal lines. However, the image quality is somewhat limited, making precise differentiation difficult. Given the density and merging nature, it leans towards ground-glass or mixed.
    *   **Conclusion:** B-lines are present. Classifying them definitively as septal vs. ground-glass vs. mixed is challenging due to image quality, but they are clearly present and confluent in areas.

2.  **Consolidation Assessment:**
    *   **Signs:** The lung parenchyma below the pleural line appears relatively homogenous and somewhat hyperechoic compared to the expected aerated lung. There isn't a clear demarcation or shredded appearance ("shred sign") at the edge of the visualized lung field. There are no obvious punctate or linear hyperechoic foci ("air bronchograms") within the parenchyma. The texture doesn't strongly resemble liver ("tissue-like hepatization"). The overall appearance is more suggestive of interstitial changes or edema rather than frank consolidation.
    *   **Conclusion:** No clear signs of consolidation (hepatization, shred sign, air bronchograms) are evident. The parenchyma appears somewhat dense but not typical for consolidation.

**Final Classification:**

*   **lung_rockets:** true (B-lines are present)
*   **consolidation:** false (No signs of consolidation are clearly present)
*   **B-line subtype:** mixed (due to the presence of coalescing lines)
*   **consolidation_type:** null (since consolidation is false)

**Summary:** The image shows prominent B-lines, indicating interstitial edema or thickening. There are no definitive signs of alveolar consolidation.
