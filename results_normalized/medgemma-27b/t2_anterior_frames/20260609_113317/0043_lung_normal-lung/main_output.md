# 0043_lung_normal-lung

**Frame Analysis:**

The image displays a lung ultrasound (LUS) frame from an anterior lung zone. The scan is in B-mode, showing the pleural line at the top and the lung parenchyma below. A-lines (vertical hyperechoic artifacts from the probe) are visible in the aerated lung. The image quality is good, allowing for assessment of lung texture and artifacts.

**B-lines Assessment:**

*   **Presence:** There are vertical hyperechoic artifacts extending from the pleural line towards the bottom of the image. These artifacts appear to arise from the pleural line and move with the lung surface (although motion assessment is not directly possible from a static image, their appearance is consistent with lung sliding artifacts). They are relatively numerous.
*   **Classification:** The B-lines are not well-spaced and discrete (septal pattern). Instead, they appear more confluent, merging together in some areas, obscuring the A-lines. This pattern is consistent with a ground-glass appearance. There are no clear regions with discrete, separated B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**

*   **Presence:** The lung parenchyma below the pleural line does not exhibit a solid, homogeneous, liver-like echotexture (hepatization). There is no clear evidence of a shredded deep border (shred sign). Nor are there distinct punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within a consolidated area (air bronchograms). The lung parenchyma appears somewhat heterogeneous but not uniformly echogenic like consolidated lung.

**Conclusion:**

*   **lung_rockets = true** (B-lines are present)
*   **B-line subtype = ground_glass** (Confluent B-lines obscuring A-lines)
*   **consolidation = false** (No signs of hepatization, shred sign, or air bronchograms)
*   **consolidation_type = null** (Since consolidation is false)
