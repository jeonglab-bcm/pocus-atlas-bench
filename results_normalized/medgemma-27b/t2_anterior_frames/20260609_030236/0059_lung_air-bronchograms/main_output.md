# 0059_lung_air-bronchograms

**Frame Analysis:**

**B-lines Assessment:**

*   **Subtype:** The B-lines appear relatively discrete, with some space visible between them, and they do not seem to merge into a continuous bright sheet obscuring the A-lines completely, although they are numerous. The pattern is more suggestive of septal thickening than confluent ground-glass appearance.

There's a loss of the typical A-line pattern, suggesting some degree of fluid or increased tissue density. There's no clear evidence of tissue-like hepatization, shred sign, or distinct air bronchograms within a clearly defined consolidated area. The image quality is not optimal, and the pattern is not clearly diagnostic of consolidation.
