# 0046_lung_light-beam-sign-covid19

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frame 1:** The image displays a normal lung appearance. A horizontal hyperechoic line representing the pleura is visible at the top. Below it, horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) are faintly visible, and the deeper tissue is relatively anechoic/dark, indicating normal aeration.
*   **Frame 2:** Similar to Frame 1, showing normal lung parenchyma with A-lines and no vertical artifacts.
*   **Frame 3:** A distinct, vertical hyperechoic artifact arises from the pleural line and extends downwards. It appears as a discrete line with dark lung parenchyma visible to its left. This is characteristic of a **septal B-line**.
*   **Frame 4:** There is a prominent, broad vertical hyperechoic band originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. The area to the right of the pleura is bright and somewhat homogeneous, obscuring underlying structures. This appearance of confluent, coalescing B-lines forming a "white sheet" is characteristic of **ground-glass** pattern.
*   **Frame 5:** The image returns to a normal appearance similar to Frame 1, with horizontal A-lines and no vertical artifacts.
*   **Frame 6:** Normal lung appearance.
*   **Frame 7:** Similar to Frame 4, a broad vertical hyperechoic band is visible on the right side, consistent with the **ground-glass** pattern.
*   **Frame 8:** Similar to Frame 7, showing the vertical hyperechoic band.

**B-lines Assessment:**
Vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) are clearly present in frames 3, 4, 7, and 8.
- In **Frame 3**, the B-line is discrete and well-spaced from the rest of the lung field, fitting the **septal** pattern.
- In **Frames 4, 7, and 8**, the B-lines are numerous and coalescing, merging into a diffuse vertical sheet of hyperechogenicity that obscures A-lines. This fits the **ground_glass** pattern.
- Since both discrete (septal) and confluent (ground_glass) patterns are visible in different frames (time points), the subtype is **mixed**.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
- There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization (liver-like texture) in the non-artifact regions.
- There are no irregular "shred signs" visible as a border between aerated and non-aerated lung; the borders of the bright artifacts are consistent with the propagation of ultrasound artifacts (B-lines).
- There are no distinct air bronchograms (punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within solid tissue).
- Therefore, consolidation is absent.

**Conclusions:**

*   **lung_rockets:** true
*   **B-lines subtype:** mixed
*   **consolidation:** false
