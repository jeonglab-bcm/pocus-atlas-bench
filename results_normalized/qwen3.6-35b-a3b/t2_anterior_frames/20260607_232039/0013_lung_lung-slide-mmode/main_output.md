**B-lines Assessment**

In the sequential frames, the ultrasound images display the following features:
- **Top Panel (M-mode):** The M-mode tracing (indicated by the green line in the B-mode below) shows parallel horizontal lines extending across the screen. This is known as the "barcode sign" or "stratosphere sign," which indicates the absence of lung sliding.
- **Bottom Panel (B-mode):** The lung window shows a distinct, bright horizontal pleural line. Deep to the pleural line, there are multiple, parallel, horizontal hyperechoic artifacts. These are A-lines (reverberation artifacts), which indicate normal, aerated lung.
- **Vertical Artifacts:** There are no vertical hyperechoic laser-like lines arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. The "landmarks" for B-lines (lung rockets) are completely absent.

**Conclusion:** B-lines are absent.
