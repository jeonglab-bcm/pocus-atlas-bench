# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**B-lines Assessment**
The images display vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line. These artifacts extend to the bottom of the screen without fading and are distinct from the horizontal A-lines. They appear as discrete, well-spaced vertical columns ("lung rockets") rather than a confluent sheet of white. Dark lung parenchyma (A-lines) is clearly visible between these vertical artifacts.
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **B-line subtype: septal**

**Consolidation Assessment**
The lung parenchyma appears normally aerated. There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization, irregular shredded borders, or air bronchograms. The normal reverberation pattern (A-lines) is preserved in the intercostal spaces not occupied by the B-lines.
- **consolidation = false**

**Conclusion**
The ultrasound shows a normal lung pattern with discrete B-lines (septal pattern), suggestive of interstitial thickening but not alveolar consolidation. There is no evidence of consolidation.
