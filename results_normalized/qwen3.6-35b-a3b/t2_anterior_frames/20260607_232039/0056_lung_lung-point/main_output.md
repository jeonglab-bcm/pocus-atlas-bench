**B-lines Assessment:**
Vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. They move with lung sliding (implied by the nature of the clip). Thus, **lung_rockets = true**.
Regarding the subtype: The clip displays both discrete, well-spaced B-lines (typical of the **septal** pattern, seen in frames 1, 2, 8) and areas where the lines are dense, coalescing, and obscuring A-lines (typical of the **ground-glass** pattern, seen in frames 4, 5, 6, 7). The presence of both patterns in different regions or frames classifies this as **mixed**.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization (liver-like texture) or a "shred sign" (irregular border). The presence of A-lines and B-lines indicates that the lung parenchyma is aerated. Therefore, **consolidation = false**.

**Conclusions:**

*   **lung_rockets:** true
