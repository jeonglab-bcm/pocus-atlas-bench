# 0005_lung_subpleural-consolidation-with-shred-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip:

**B-lines Assessment:**
In the provided frames, particularly evident in the first, second, and fifth images, there are distinct hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line. These artifacts extend downwards to the bottom of the screen without fading. They appear relatively discrete and well-spaced, with some degree of darker lung parenchyma visible between them, rather than merging into a complete white sheet. This pattern is consistent with **septal** B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is clear evidence of alveolar consolidation. In several frames (notably the fourth and sixth), a significant portion of the lung field displays increased echogenicity with a tissue-like texture, resembling the liver (**hepatization**). Within this echogenic, solid-appearing area, there are distinct punctate and linear hyperechoic foci. These bright spots are characteristic of **air bronchograms** (air-filled bronchi visible within the consolidated lung tissue). The border of the consolidation is somewhat irregular, but the air bronchograms are a prominent feature.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **B-lines subtype: septal**
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type: "air_bronchogram"**
