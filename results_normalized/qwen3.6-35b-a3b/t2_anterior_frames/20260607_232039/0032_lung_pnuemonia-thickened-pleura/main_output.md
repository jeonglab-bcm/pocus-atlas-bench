# 0032_lung_pnuemonia-thickened-pleura

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**B-lines Assessment:**
- **Presence:** The images clearly show hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading. These are characteristic of B-lines (lung rockets).
- **Subtype:**
    - In the central region of the scan, the B-lines are numerous, confluent, and coalescing, merging into a diffuse, bright "white lung" appearance that obscures the underlying A-lines. This fits the **ground_glass** description.
    - In the peripheral regions (particularly on the left side), there are more discrete, well-spaced vertical lines with dark lung parenchyma visible between them. This fits the **septal** description.
    - Since both patterns are visible in different regions, the subtype is classified as **mixed**.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
- **Findings:** There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization (liver-like texture) that is distinct from the B-line artifacts. The bright vertical streaks are artifacts (B-lines) rather than solid tissue. There are no clear "shred signs" (irregular borders between consolidated and aerated lung) or "air bronchograms" (punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within consolidated lung) visible. The hyperechogenicity is due to the confluent B-lines.
- **Conclusion:** Consolidation is absent.

**Conclusions:**
