# 0036_lung_dynamic-air-bronchograms

**B-lines Assessment**
- **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**: The images, particularly the first few frames (1-5) and later frames (7-8), show hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line. These artifacts extend downwards towards the bottom of the screen. They are distinct and separate from one another in the earlier frames, with dark, aerated lung parenchyma visible between them. This fits the description of septal B-lines.
- **Subtype**: The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced (typically visible as a few distinct lines per intercostal space in the aerated regions), which corresponds to the **septal** subtype. There is no diffuse white sheet obscuring the entire field of view characteristic of ground-glass B-lines, although the area of consolidation is bright.

**Consolidation Assessment**
- **Consolidation present (consolidation = true)**: In the later frames (6-8), there is a distinct area of lung parenchyma that appears solid and echogenic (tissue-like), consistent with hepatization.
- **Subtype**: Within this tissue-like consolidated area, there are distinct punctate and linear hyperechoic foci (bright spots). These are characteristic of **air bronchograms**, which represent air-filled bronchi within the fluid-filled or solidified alveoli. The border of the consolidated area appears somewhat irregular, but the presence of air bronchograms is the most prominent feature defining the consolidation type.

**Conclusion**
