# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**B-lines Assessment:**
The images clearly show the presence of **B-lines** (lung rockets). These appear as hyperechoic, vertical laser-like lines that arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. They move with lung sliding (implied by the sequence). The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced, with dark lung parenchyma visible between them. They do not merge into a confluent white sheet. This pattern is characteristic of thickened interlobular septa.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung parenchyma deep to the pleural line appears predominantly anechoic (dark), consistent with normal aeration, interrupted only by the vertical B-line artifacts. There is no "tissue-like hepatization" (solid, liver-like echogenicity), no "shred sign" (irregular border), and no visible "air bronchograms" within a consolidated area.

**Conclusions:**
