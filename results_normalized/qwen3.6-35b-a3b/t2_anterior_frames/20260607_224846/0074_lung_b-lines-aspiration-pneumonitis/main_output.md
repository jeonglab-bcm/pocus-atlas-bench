# 0074_lung_b-lines-aspiration-pneumonitis

**B-lines Assessment**
- **Presence:** The images show prominent, hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts do not fade and obscure the underlying A-lines. Thus, **B-lines are present**.
- **Subtype:**
    - In the earlier frames (e.g., frames 1-2), there are thick, somewhat distinct vertical artifacts.
    - In the later frames (e.g., frames 6-8), the artifacts have coalesced into a very dense, diffuse white sheet ("white lung" appearance) that obscures most of the underlying field. This merging and confluence of artifacts is characteristic of a **ground_glass** pattern. While there is some variation, the dominant and most striking feature, especially in the later frames, is the confluent nature of the lines. Therefore, the classification is **ground_glass**.

**Consolidation Assessment**
- **Findings:** I am looking for tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign, or air bronchograms.
    - The primary finding is the vertical B-line artifacts.
    - In frames 3-5, there is a large anechoic (black) area to the right of the lung tissue. This is consistent with a pleural effusion, not aerated lung.
    - The echogenic area adjacent to the effusion (frames 3-5) and filling the screen in frames 6-8 exhibits vertical streaking, which is characteristic of B-line artifacts (interstitial syndrome) rather than the homogeneous "liver-like" texture of hepatization.
    - There are no clear air bronchograms (punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within consolidated tissue).
    - The border between the echogenic lung and the anechoic fluid is visible, but since the black area is fluid (not aerated lung), this is not a "shred sign" (which is the irregular border between consolidated and *aerated* lung).
- **Conclusion:** There are no definitive signs of alveolar consolidation. The findings are consistent with severe interstitial syndrome (confluent B-lines) and likely a pleural effusion.

**Conclusions**
- **lung_rockets:** true
- **consolidation:** false
