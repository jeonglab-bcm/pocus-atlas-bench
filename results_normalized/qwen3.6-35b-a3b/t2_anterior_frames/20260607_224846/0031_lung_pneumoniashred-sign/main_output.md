# 0031_lung_pneumoniashred-sign

**B-lines Assessment**
The image does not show the classic features of B-lines (lung rockets). There are no distinct vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. The dominant feature is a solid, echogenic area rather than vertical lines. Therefore, B-lines are assessed as absent.

**Consolidation Assessment**
The images clearly display alveolar consolidation in the anterior zone.
*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The lung parenchyma in the upper/middle portion of the sector appears solid, homogeneous, and hyperechoic, resembling liver tissue (hepatization). This area occupies a significant portion of the image.
*   **Air bronchograms:** In the later frames (e.g., frames 6, 7, 8), distinct linear and punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within the consolidated tissue. These are characteristic of air bronchograms, which represent air-filled bronchi surrounded by fluid-filled/alveoli-consolidated lung.
*   **Shred sign:** The border between the consolidated tissue and the underlying structures (or aerated lung, if present deeper down) appears irregular and jagged in some frames (e.g., frame 1), consistent with the shred sign.

Given the solid, liver-like appearance of the parenchyma, the predominant type of consolidation is hepatization, though air bronchograms are a key feature.

**Conclusions**
- **lung_rockets:** false
- **consolidation:** true
- **consolidation_type:** hepatization
