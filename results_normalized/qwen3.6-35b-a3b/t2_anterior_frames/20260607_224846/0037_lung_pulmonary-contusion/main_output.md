# 0037_lung_pulmonary-contusion

**B-lines Assessment**
The image sequence displays prominent vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line. These artifacts, known as B-lines or "lung rockets," extend from the pleura to the bottom of the screen without fading. In this clip, the B-lines are numerous, wide, and appear to merge or coalesce, creating a diffuse, bright vertical column that obscures the underlying lung parenchyma. This confluent appearance is characteristic of severe interstitial syndrome, often seen in alveolar edema. While there are discrete elements, the overall pattern is dominated by coalescing lines that create a "white lung" appearance.
