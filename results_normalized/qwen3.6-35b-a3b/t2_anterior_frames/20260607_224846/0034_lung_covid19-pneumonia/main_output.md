**B-lines Assessment:**
*   **Presence:** **True**. There are clear vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading. These are B-lines (lung rockets).
*   **Subtype:** **mixed**. The clip exhibits characteristics of both subtypes. Frames 1, 4, and 7 show distinct, relatively well-separated vertical lines, which is characteristic of **septal** B-lines. However, frames 2, 3, 5, 6, and 8 show a much higher density of these lines, appearing confluent and creating a dense vertical texture that resembles a "white sheet," which is characteristic of **ground_glass** B-lines. Since both patterns are visible in different frames, the classification is mixed.

**Conclusions:**
