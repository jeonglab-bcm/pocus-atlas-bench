# 0103_lung_common-pleural-based-findings-in-covid19

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**B-lines Assessment**
The images display a distinct, hyperechoic pleural line. Below this line, the dominant feature is the presence of horizontal reverberation artifacts known as A-lines. There are no vertical, hyperechoic laser-like beams arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. Therefore, **B-lines are absent**.

**Consolidation Assessment**
The lung parenchyma beneath the pleural line appears normally aerated, characterized by the presence of A-lines and the absence of solid, tissue-like echogenicity. There is no evidence of "hepatization" (liver-like texture), an irregular "shred sign" at the lung interface, or hyperechoic foci indicative of air bronchograms. Therefore, **consolidation is absent**.

**Conclusion**
- **lung_rockets**: false
- **consolidation**: false
