# 0148_lung_jr_pediatric-consolidation

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frames 1-3:** These frames display a normal lung ultrasound pattern. There is a clear, hyperechoic pleural line. Below the pleural line, there are horizontal reverberation artifacts known as A-lines, indicating normal aeration of the lung parenchyma. No vertical B-lines are visible.
*   **Frames 4-7:** These frames show a significant change in the lung appearance. There is a large area of subpleural consolidation. The lung parenchyma in this region appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue (hepatization). The border between this consolidated area and the adjacent aerated lung (visible in the upper right) is irregular and jagged, which is characteristic of the "shred sign". Within the consolidated tissue, there are punctate and linear hyperechoic foci, representing air bronchograms (air-filled bronchi surrounded by consolidated lung).
*   **Frame 8:** This frame shows a different view or time point. Distinct, vertical hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. These are B-lines (lung rockets). They appear discrete and well-spaced, not confluent.

**B-lines Assessment:**
*   **Presence:** B-lines are present, specifically visible in the final frame.
*   **Subtype:** The B-lines visible in the final frame are discrete and well-spaced. This fits the description of **septal** B-lines (lung_rockets = true, subtype = septal). While there are multiple frames, the B-lines in the final frame are the primary evidence, and they are septal in nature.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
*   **Presence:** Consolidation is clearly present in frames 4 through 7.
*   **Type:** The predominant feature is the tissue-like, echogenic appearance of the lung parenchyma, which mimics the liver. This is **hepatization**. The presence of the "shred sign" (irregular border) and air bronchograms further confirms this, but the tissue-like texture is the most defining characteristic of the parenchymal change itself.

**Conclusions:**
*   **lung_rockets:** true
*   **lung_rockets_subtype:** septal
*   **consolidation:** true
*   **consolidation_type:** hepatization
