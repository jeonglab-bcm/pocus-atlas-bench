- **Subtype:** In the central and left portions of the image, the B-lines appear as discrete, well-spaced vertical lines with the darker lung parenchyma visible between them. **Consolidation Assessment:**
- **Presence:** On the right side of the image, there is an area of lung parenchyma that appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of the liver (tissue-like hepatization). The border between this area and the adjacent aerated lung is irregular and jagged, known as the **shred sign**.
- **Type:** Within this consolidated area, there are punctate and linear hyperechoic foci, which represent air-filled bronchi surrounded by fluid-filled alveoli. These are **air bronchograms**. While hepatization and the shred sign are present, the presence of distinct air bronchograms is a key specific sign of alveolar consolidation.
