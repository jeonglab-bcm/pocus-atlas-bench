# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

**B-lines Assessment**
- **Observation:** The ultrasound frames display the typical appearance of a normal, aerated lung. Deep to the hyperechoic pleural line, there are regular, horizontal reverberation artifacts known as A-lines. There is a notable absence of vertical, laser-like hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) that would arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen.
- **Conclusion:** **B-lines absent (lung_rockets = false)**.

**Consolidation Assessment**
- **Observation:** The lung parenchyma below the pleural line appears dark and anechoic with horizontal A-lines, indicating normal air content. There is no evidence of tissue-like echogenicity (hepatization), an irregular deep border (shred sign), or hyperechoic foci (air bronchograms).
- **Conclusion:** **Consolidation absent (consolidation = false)**.
