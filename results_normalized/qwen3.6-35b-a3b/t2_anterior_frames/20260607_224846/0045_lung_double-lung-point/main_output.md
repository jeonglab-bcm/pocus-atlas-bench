# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound video clip of the anterior left lung zone:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
- **Pleural Line:** A distinct, hyperechoic horizontal line representing the pleura is visible in all frames. It appears regular and intact.
- **Subcutaneous/Interstitial Tissue:** Above the pleural line, layers of subcutaneous tissue and intercostal muscles are visible.
- **Artifacts:** In the lung field below the pleural line, vertical hyperechoic artifacts are visible. Specifically, in frames 1, 4, and 5, a prominent vertical streak can be seen originating from the pleural line and extending downwards. These artifacts do not appear to be horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines).
- **Lung Parenchyma:** The area below the pleural line does not show a homogeneous, tissue-like texture (hepatization). The texture is somewhat heterogeneous due to the vertical artifacts. There are no visible air-filled bronchi (air bronchograms) or irregular borders suggesting consolidation.

**B-lines Assessment:**
- **Presence:** Vertical hyperechoic artifacts are seen arising from the pleural line and extending downwards. They meet the criteria for B-lines (lung rockets).
- **Subtype:** The B-lines appear as discrete, distinct vertical lines (particularly visible in the center of the field of view in frames like frame 4). They are not merging into a diffuse, confluent white sheet that obscures the underlying lung. This pattern is characteristic of **septal** B-lines, which typically represent thickened interlobular septa and are usually ≤3 per intercostal space.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
- **Signs:** There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung tissue does not appear "hepatized" (solid and liver-like). There is no "shred sign" (irregular border between consolidated and aerated lung) and no visible air bronchograms. The presence of discrete B-lines usually suggests interstitial involvement rather than alveolar consolidation.
- **Conclusion:** Consolidation is absent.

**Conclusions:**
- **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**: The image shows discrete vertical artifacts.
- **Subtype**: **septal**
- **Consolidation present (consolidation = false)**: No signs of hepatization, shred sign, or air bronchograms.
- **Consolidation type**: **null**
