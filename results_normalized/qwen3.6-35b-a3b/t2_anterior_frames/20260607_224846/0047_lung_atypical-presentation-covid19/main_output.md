Instead, there is a confluent, hyperechoic area that appears as a "white sheet," obscuring the underlying lung structures and A-lines. This appearance is consistent with **confluent B-lines (ground-glass pattern)**.
*   **Frames 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8:** These frames return to the appearance seen in Frame 1. They display discrete, vertical, hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. The artifacts are separated by dark vertical spaces, indicating aerated lung between the lines. This confirms the presence of **septal B-lines**.

**B-lines Assessment:**
*   **Presence:** B-lines are clearly present throughout the clip (lung_rockets = true).
*   **Subtype:** The clip displays two distinct patterns. Frames 1 and 3-8 show discrete, well-spaced vertical artifacts, fitting the description of **septal** B-lines. Frame 2 shows a confluent pattern where the lines merge into a diffuse bright sheet, fitting the description of **ground_glass** B-lines. Since both patterns are visible in the sequence, the subtype is **mixed**.
