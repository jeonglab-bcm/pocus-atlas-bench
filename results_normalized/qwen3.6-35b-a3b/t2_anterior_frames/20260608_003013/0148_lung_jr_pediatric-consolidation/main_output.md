# 0148_lung_jr_pediatric-consolidation

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**B-lines Assessment:**
- **Presence:** B-lines are present. In frames 1, 2, and 8, distinct hyperechoic vertical artifacts are visible originating from the pleural line and extending downwards. In frame 8, they are clearly visible in the aerated lung tissue adjacent to the consolidated area.
- **Subtype:** The pattern appears to be **mixed**. In frame 1, there are relatively discrete, spaced vertical lines (septal pattern). In frame 2 and the upper portion of frame 3, the vertical artifacts become more confluent and bright, creating a "white sheet" appearance (ground-glass pattern). Frame 8 shows both distinct lines and the edge of a consolidated area.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
- **Presence:** Alveolar consolidation is present. From frame 3 onwards, a large area of tissue-like echogenicity is visible.
- **Type:** The predominant appearance is **hepatization**. The lung parenchyma in frames 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7 has a solid, liver-like texture with loss of normal aeration artifacts. Additionally, hyperechoic punctate and linear foci (air bronchograms) are visible within the consolidated tissue (especially in frames 4 and 5). An irregular, shredded border (shred sign) is also evident at the interface between the consolidated and aerated lung (e.g., in frame 8). However, the fundamental tissue appearance is hepatization.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets:** true
- **B-line subtype:** mixed
- **consolidation:** true
- **consolidation_type:** hepatization
