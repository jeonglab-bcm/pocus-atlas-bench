**B-lines Assessment**

There are no distinct vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen (which would be B-lines or "lung rockets"). The background remains dark (anechoic) between the horizontal lines, further supporting the presence of air and absence of significant interstitial syndrome.
