# 0032_lung_pnuemonia-thickened-pleura

The ultrasound images show a longitudinal view of the anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
There are prominent, hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line. These artifacts extend to the bottom of the screen without fading and appear to move with lung sliding (implied by the sequence). They are numerous and closely spaced. In the central portion of the image, the B-lines are confluent and coalesce, creating a diffuse, bright "white" appearance that obscures the underlying lung parenchyma and normal A-lines. This pattern fits the description of coalescing B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization (liver-like texture of the lung). The bright vertical columns are reverberation artifacts (B-lines), not solid consolidated lung tissue. There are no clear air bronchograms (static or dynamic bright spots within a dark mass) visible, nor is there an irregular "shred sign" border. The lung appears to have severe interstitial involvement rather than alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **B-line subtype = ground_glass** (due to the confluent, coalescing nature of the artifacts creating a white sheet appearance).
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
