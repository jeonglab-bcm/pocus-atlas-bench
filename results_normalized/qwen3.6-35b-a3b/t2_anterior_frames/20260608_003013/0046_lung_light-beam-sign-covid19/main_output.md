# 0046_lung_light-beam-sign-covid19

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound frames:

**B-lines Assessment**
*   **Presence:** B-lines (lung rockets) are **present**. In frames 3, 4, 7, and 8, distinct hyperechoic vertical artifacts are visible arising from the pleural line and extending downwards.
*   **Subtype:** The subtype is **septal**.
    *   In frames 3, 7, and 8, the B-lines appear as discrete, well-spaced vertical lines. Frame 7 and 8 clearly show two distinct lines with dark lung parenchyma visible between them.
    *   While Frame 4 shows a prominent, bright vertical artifact, the overall pattern across the clip (especially with the presence of clear A-lines in frames 1, 2, 5, and 6) suggests focal, discrete interstitial thickening rather than a diffuse "white out" or ground-glass sheet. The artifacts do not merge into a continuous, diffuse sheet obscuring the entire field in a way characteristic of ground-glass opacity.

**Consolidation Assessment**
*   **Presence:** Consolidation is **absent** (false).
    *   There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization (solid liver-like texture).
    *   There is no irregular "shred sign" visible at the interface of aerated and non-aerated lung.
    *   There are no distinct air bronchograms (punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within a solid mass). The bright vertical lines are artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleura, not air-filled bronchi within consolidated tissue. The presence of A-lines in other frames confirms the lung is aerated.

**Conclusion**
*   **lung_rockets:** true
*   **subtype:** septal
*   **consolidation:** false
*   **consolidation_type:** null
