# 0027_lung_subpleural-consolidation

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**B-lines Assessment:**
Upon reviewing the frames, particularly the areas adjacent to the hypoechoic lesions, there are no distinct hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. The background lung parenchyma (where visible) does not show the characteristic "lung rockets". Therefore, B-lines are absent.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The images reveal distinct, well-circumscribed, hypoechoic areas located in the subpleural region (just deep to the pleural line). This appearance is characteristic of subpleural consolidation.
- The consolidations are hypoechoic (darker than surrounding tissue) and appear solid/tissue-like.
- The deep borders of these consolidations are irregular, jagged, and ill-defined, contrasting with the surrounding tissue. This irregular interface is known as the **"shred sign"**.
- There is no clear evidence of distinct, linear hyperechoic foci (air bronchograms) within the consolidations, although there are some internal echoes. The most prominent feature is the irregular border.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets**: false
- **consolidation**: true
- **consolidation_type**: "shred_sign"
