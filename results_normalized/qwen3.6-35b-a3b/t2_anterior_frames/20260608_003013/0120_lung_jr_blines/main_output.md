# 0120_lung_jr_blines

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound frames:

**B-lines Assessment:**
The images display distinct, vertical, hyperechoic (bright white) artifacts arising from the pleural line. These artifacts extend from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen without fading, which is the characteristic appearance of B-lines (lung rockets).
- **Presence:** B-lines are clearly present.
- **Subtype:** The B-lines appear discrete and well-spaced. There is visible separation between the vertical columns, and they do not merge into a single, confluent white sheet that obscures the entire field. This pattern corresponds to **septal** B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
- **Observation:** The lung parenchyma shows vertical artifacts but does not exhibit the solid, tissue-like texture (hepatization) seen in pneumonia. There is no irregular, "shredded" border between consolidated and aerated lung, nor are there any visible air bronchograms (punctate or linear bright spots within solid tissue). The vertical lines are artifacts of interstitial thickening, not consolidation of the alveoli themselves.
- **Conclusion:** No signs of consolidation are present.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets:** true
- **B-lines subtype:** septal
- **consolidation:** false
- **consolidation_type:** null
