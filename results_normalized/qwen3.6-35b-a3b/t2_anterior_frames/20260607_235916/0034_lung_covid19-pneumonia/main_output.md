# 0034_lung_covid19-pneumonia

The ultrasound images display a standard anterior lung view.

**B-lines Assessment**
The images clearly demonstrate the presence of B-lines (lung rockets). Vertical, hyperechoic artifacts arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. These artifacts are distinct and relatively well-spaced, with dark lung parenchyma (and horizontal A-lines) visible between them in several frames. They do not merge into a solid, confluent "white lung" sheet. Therefore, the B-lines are classified as **septal**.

**Consolidation Assessment**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung parenchyma beneath the pleural line does not exhibit a tissue-like "hepatization" (resembling liver texture). There is no irregular "shred sign" border, nor are there any visible air bronchograms (hyperechoic foci within solid lung). The pleural line remains visible and distinct.

**Conclusion**
- **B-lines present**: true
- **B-line subtype**: septal
- **Consolidation**: false
- **Consolidation type**: null
