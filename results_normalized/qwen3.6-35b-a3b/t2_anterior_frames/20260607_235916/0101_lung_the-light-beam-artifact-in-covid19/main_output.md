# 0101_lung_the-light-beam-artifact-in-covid19

**B-lines Assessment**
- **Observation:** The images display a pleural line from which multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts arise. In the initial frames (e.g., frame 1 and 2), these vertical lines are somewhat discrete and spaced apart, though numerous. As the sequence progresses (frames 3-8), these vertical artifacts become extremely dense and coalesce, merging into a diffuse, bright white sheet that obscures the underlying lung parenchyma and A-lines.
- **Conclusion:** **B-lines are present (lung_rockets = true)**.
- **Subtype Classification:** The sequence shows both discrete, spaced B-lines in the earlier frames and confluent, coalescing B-lines forming a white sheet in later frames. This fits the definition of **mixed** (both septal and ground_glass patterns visible at different time points). Alternatively, given the high density, it could be classified as **ground_glass** (confluent), but the visible separation in early frames supports **mixed**.

**Consolidation Assessment**
- **Observation:** While the "white lung" appearance of confluent B-lines can mimic consolidation, there is no distinct tissue-like hepatization (liver texture) separate from the artifacts. The vertical streaks are characteristic of reverberation artifacts (B-lines) rather than a static solid mass. There is no clear "shred sign" (irregular border between consolidated and aerated lung) distinct from the B-line field.
- **Conclusion:** **Consolidation is absent (consolidation = false)**.

**Final Conclusions**
- **B-lines present:** true
- **B-line subtype:** mixed
- **Consolidation present:** false
- **Consolidation type:** null
