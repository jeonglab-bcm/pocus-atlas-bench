# 0120_lung_jr_blines

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound frames:

**B-lines Assessment:**
The images clearly display vertical, hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line. These artifacts, known as B-lines or "lung rockets," extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. They appear as discrete, separate lines rather than a confluent white sheet. There is visible dark lung parenchyma between these vertical artifacts, which is characteristic of the septal pattern. Therefore, B-lines are present.

*   **lung_rockets:** true
*   **lung_rocket_sub:** septal

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The lung parenchyma visible between the B-lines appears to have normal aeration (it is dark/anechoic), lacking the solid, liver-like appearance (hepatization) seen in consolidation. There are no signs of a shred sign (irregular border between consolidated and aerated lung) or air bronchograms (air-filled bronchi within consolidated tissue).
