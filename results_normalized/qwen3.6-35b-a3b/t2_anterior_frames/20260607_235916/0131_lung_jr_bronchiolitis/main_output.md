# 0131_lung_jr_bronchiolitis

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frame 1 & 2:** The image displays normal lung parenchyma. The pleural line is visible as a bright, horizontal structure. Below the pleura, there are horizontal reverberation artifacts known as A-lines, which indicate normal aeration. No vertical artifacts (B-lines) are seen.
*   **Frame 3 & 4:** A distinct, vertical hyperechoic line appears on the right side of the intercostal space, arising from the pleural line and extending downwards. This is a B-line (lung rocket). It appears discrete and well-spaced, characteristic of a septal pattern.
*   **Frame 5:** The probe moves, and the view shifts.
*   **Frame 6, 7, & 8:** A large area of hyperechogenicity (brightness) is visible on the left side of the screen, extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the image. The vertical artifacts have coalesced into a diffuse "white sheet," obscuring the underlying A-lines. This appearance is characteristic of confluent B-lines, or "ground glass" pattern. The transition between the normal lung (with A-lines) on the right and the abnormal zone on the left is relatively vertical, typical for the edge of a B-line zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
*   **Presence:** B-lines are clearly present in multiple frames (3, 4, 6, 7, 8).
*   **Subtype:** The clip shows both discrete, well-spaced B-lines (seen in frames 3 and 4) and confluent, coalescing B-lines forming a white sheet (seen in frames 6, 7, 8). Therefore, the pattern is **mixed**.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
*   **Presence:** There are no signs of alveolar consolidation. The bright areas observed are vertical artifacts (B-lines) extending to the bottom of the screen, not solid tissue. There is no "tissue-like hepatization" (which would resemble liver texture), no "shred sign" (irregular deep border), and no distinct "air bronchograms" (punctate/linear hyperechoic foci within consolidated tissue). The bright area in frames 6-8 fits the description of ground-glass B-lines rather than consolidation.
*   **Classification:** **consolidation = false**.

**Conclusions:**
*   **lung_rockets = true**
*   **subtype = mixed**
*   **consolidation = false**
*   **consolidation_type = null**
