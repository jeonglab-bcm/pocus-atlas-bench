# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound frames:

**B-lines Assessment**
The images show a distinct, hyperechoic pleural line. Deep to the pleura, there are multiple horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) parallel to the pleural line. There are no vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines or "lung rockets") arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. The vertical artifacts are absent, and the A-lines are preserved.

**Consolidation Assessment**
The lung parenchyma exhibits a normal aerated pattern characterized by the pleural line and A-lines. There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization (solid, liver-like echogenicity), the "shred sign" (irregular border), or air bronchograms. The lung appears normally aerated.

**Conclusions**
- **lung_rockets**: false
- **consolidation**: false
