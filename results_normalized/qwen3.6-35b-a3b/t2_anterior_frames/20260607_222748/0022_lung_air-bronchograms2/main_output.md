# 0022_lung_air-bronchograms2

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound frames:

**B-lines Assessment**
The image does not display classic B-lines (lung rockets) arising from the pleural line into an aerated lung field. While there are vertical hyperechoic streaks, they appear embedded within the echogenic tissue rather than extending as distinct, fading artifacts from a normal pleural line into a dark, aerated background. Therefore, B-lines are considered absent in the context of the primary pathology present.

**Consolidation Assessment**
The images demonstrate a significant area of increased echogenicity in the lung parenchyma. The tissue appears solid and homogeneous, resembling the texture of the liver (hepatization). There is a loss of normal aeration artifacts (A-lines). Within this consolidated tissue, there are small, punctate hyperechoic foci visible, which are consistent with air bronchograms (air-filled bronchi within the fluid-filled/solid alveoli). The deep border appears somewhat irregular against the adjacent structures (likely the diaphragm or liver, given the bright horizontal interface at the bottom), but the primary feature is the solid, tissue-like appearance.

**Conclusions**
- **lung_rockets**: false
- **consolidation**: true
- **consolidation_type**: hepatization
