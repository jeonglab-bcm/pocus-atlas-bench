# 0122_lung_jr_pna

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound frames:

**B-lines Assessment:**
In the initial frames (1-3), distinct, hyperechoic vertical artifacts are seen arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are discrete and well-spaced, with dark lung parenchyma (A-lines) visible between them. This pattern is characteristic of **septal** B-lines. While the later frames show more echogenic tissue, the initial presence of discrete rockets confirms the presence of B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
In the later frames (4-8), the lung parenchyma beneath the pleural line loses its normal aeration and takes on a tissue-like, echogenic appearance similar to the liver (**hepatization**). Within this consolidated tissue, there are distinct punctate and linear hyperechoic foci. These foci are characteristic of **air bronchograms** (air-filled bronchi surrounded by fluid-filled alveoli). The presence of these specific signs indicates alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **subtype**: septal
- **consolidation**: true
- **consolidation_type**: air_bronchogram
