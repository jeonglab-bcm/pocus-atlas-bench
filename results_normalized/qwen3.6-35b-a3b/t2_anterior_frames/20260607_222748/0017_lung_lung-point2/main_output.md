# 0017_lung_lung-point2

The ultrasound images display an anterior lung zone assessment.

**B-lines Assessment:**
In the B-mode images (top), the pleural line is visible as a bright, horizontal line. Deep to the pleural line, there are horizontal reverberation artifacts known as A-lines. There are no vertical, hyperechoic, laser-like beams arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. The M-mode image (bottom) confirms this, showing a pattern of horizontal lines (the "stratosphere sign" or prominent A-lines) below the pleural line, rather than the vertical streaks characteristic of B-lines (which would appear as vertical lines moving with the pleural line in M-mode). Therefore, B-lines are absent.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The lung parenchyma deep to the pleural line appears anechoic (dark) with horizontal A-lines, which is characteristic of normally aerated lung. There is no evidence of tissue-like echogenicity (hepatization), no irregular "shred sign" border, and no air bronchograms. Therefore, consolidation is absent.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets**: false
- **consolidation**: false
