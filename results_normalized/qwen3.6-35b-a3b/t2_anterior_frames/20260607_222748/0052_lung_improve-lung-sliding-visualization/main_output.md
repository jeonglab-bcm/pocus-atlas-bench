The lung parenchyma appears normally aerated. There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization (loss of aeration artifacts), an irregular deep border (shred sign), or hyperechoic foci within the parenchyma (air bronchograms). The presence of A-lines further supports the absence of consolidation.
