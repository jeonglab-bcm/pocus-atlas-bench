# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

**Analysis:**

*   **B-lines Assessment:** The ultrasound images reveal a normal lung pattern. The pleural line is clearly visible as a hyperechoic horizontal line. Below this line, there are distinct horizontal reverberation artifacts known as A-lines. There is a complete absence of vertical, hyperechoic "lung rocket" artifacts that would arise from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. The M-mode view (indicated by the green line and the top-left inset) demonstrates the "seashore sign," with the "waves" of A-lines over the "sand" of the deep tissues, confirming the absence of B-lines and the presence of lung sliding.
    *   **Result:** B-lines are absent.

*   **Consolidation Assessment:** The lung parenchyma appears normally aerated, characterized by the presence of A-lines. There is no evidence of a tissue-like, liver-like echogenicity (hepatization) that would indicate alveolar consolidation. No irregular borders (shred sign) or punctate linear hyperechoic foci (air bronchograms) are visible within the lung field.
    *   **Result:** Consolidation is absent.

**Conclusions:**

*   **lung_rockets**: false
*   **consolidation**: false
