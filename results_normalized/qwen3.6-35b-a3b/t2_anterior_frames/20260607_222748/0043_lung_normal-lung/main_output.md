# 0043_lung_normal-lung

The ultrasound images display a standard view of the anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment**
The images are dominated by horizontal, equidistant, hyperechoic lines parallel to the pleural line. These are **A-lines**, which are reverberation artifacts caused by the reflection of sound waves between the transducer and the highly reflective pleural interface in aerated lung. There are no vertical, laser-like hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. Therefore, B-lines are not present.
- **lung_rockets = false**

**Consolidation Assessment**
The lung parenchyma below the pleural line shows the characteristic pattern of aerated lung (A-lines). There is no area of tissue-like echogenicity (hepatization) that would suggest alveolar consolidation. The border between the lung and the chest wall is regular, and there are no air bronchograms or irregular "shred signs" visible.
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**
