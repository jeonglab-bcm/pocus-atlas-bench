**Consolidation Assessment**

Furthermore, the presence of color Doppler signals (red and blue spots) distributed throughout this tissue indicates vascularity within the consolidated lung parenchyma (the "static lung" sign), which is a hallmark of alveolar consolidation. The border of the consolidated area is somewhat irregular but the primary finding is the tissue-like texture.
