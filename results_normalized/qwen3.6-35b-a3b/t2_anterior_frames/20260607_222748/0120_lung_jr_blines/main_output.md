# 0120_lung_jr_blines

**B-lines Assessment:**
- **lung_rockets = true**: Vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line are clearly visible in all frames. They extend downwards towards the bottom of the screen.
- **subtype = "mixed"**:
    - In frames 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, and 8, the B-lines are discrete, distinct, and well-spaced with dark lung parenchyma visible between them. This fits the **septal** subtype description.
    - In frames 4 and 5, the B-lines appear to coalesce, creating a diffuse, bright "white sheet" that obscures the underlying lung texture and A-lines. This fits the **ground_glass** subtype description.
    - Since both patterns are visible across the sequence, the subtype is **mixed**.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
- **consolidation = false**:
    - There is no clear evidence of **hepatization** (tissue-like echogenicity). The vertical artifacts persist, indicating the lung is still aerated enough to generate them, rather than being solidified with fluid.
    - There is no clear **shred sign** (irregular border between consolidated and aerated lung). The transition between bright and dark areas in frames 4-5 appears to be due to the coalescence of B-lines rather than a pathological border of consolidation.
    - There are no distinct **air bronchograms** (punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within consolidated lung). The vertical lines arise from the pleura and extend to the bottom, characteristic of B-lines, not air-filled bronchi within consolidated tissue.
- **consolidation_type = null**: As consolidation is not present.

**Conclusion:**
The lung ultrasound shows a mixed pattern of B-lines, ranging from discrete septal B-lines to confluent ground-glass B-lines, without evidence of alveolar consolidation.
