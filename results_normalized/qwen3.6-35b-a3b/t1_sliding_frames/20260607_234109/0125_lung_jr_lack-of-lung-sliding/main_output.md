# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the provided sequential frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Observation:** Across all frames (1 through 8), the ultrasound image displays a consistent, static pattern.
*   **Structures:** We can clearly see the hyperechoic (bright) pleural line near the top. Beneath it, there are distinct, horizontal, parallel hyperechoic lines known as A-lines (reverberation artifacts). There are also vertical anechoic (dark) shadows cast by the ribs.
*   **Motion:** When comparing the frames, the position of the A-lines and the rib shadows remains completely unchanged. The pleural line appears smooth and static, lacking the "shimmering" or granular appearance associated with the lung sliding against the chest wall. There are no vertical B-lines (comet-tail artifacts) originating from the pleura, and the sub-pleural texture (speckle) does not vary between frames. The pattern is essentially frozen.

**Conclusion:**
The absence of motion in the sub-pleural region, combined with the presence of static A-lines and a lack of the "spindle sign" (which indicates lung sliding), points to **absent** pleural sliding. This pattern is characteristic of a pneumothorax.

**Assessment:** absent
