# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Pleural Line:** In all frames, a distinct, hyperechoic (bright white) linear structure is visible at a depth of approximately 4-5 cm. This represents the pleural interface.
*   **Sub-pleural Artifacts:** Below the pleural line, there are multiple parallel horizontal hyperechoic lines. These are A-lines (reverberation artifacts).
*   **Motion Comparison:** When comparing Frame 1 through Frame 8, there is no discernible change in the position of the pleural line or the horizontal A-lines. The structures appear perfectly static. The speckle pattern in the lung parenchyma below the pleural line does not vary between frames. There is no "shimmering" effect associated with the breathing movement of the lung against the chest wall.

**Assessment:**
The observed features align perfectly with the **Signs of sliding ABSENT**:
*   The pleural line is **smooth, sharply defined, and static**.
*   The **A-lines remain perfectly static and unchanged** across all frames.
*   There is **no variation in sub-pleural speckle** between frames.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**. (This pattern is typically associated with pneumothorax, where air in the pleural space prevents the lung from sliding against the chest wall, often resulting in the "barcode" or "stratosphere" sign on M-mode).
