# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
- **Pleural Line:** A distinct, bright hyperechoic line is visible in the upper portion of the ultrasound field, representing the pleural interface.
- **Sub-pleural Texture:** Below the pleural line, the echotexture (speckle pattern) is not static. Comparing consecutive frames (e.g., Frame 1 to Frame 3), there is a visible variation in the grey-scale patterns deep to the pleura. This "shimmering" or granular appearance indicates motion.
- **Artifacts:** In several frames (notably frames 3 and 4), vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (consistent with B-lines or comet-tail artifacts) are seen originating from the pleural line and extending downwards. These artifacts move with the breathing cycle.
- **Dynamic Change:** The texture and artifacts in the region immediately below the pleural line change position and intensity from one frame to the next, which is the hallmark of the lung moving against the chest wall.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The evidence includes the variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between frames and the presence of vertical artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line, which are consistent with the "Seashore sign" typically seen in M-mode, indicating a healthy, moving lung.
