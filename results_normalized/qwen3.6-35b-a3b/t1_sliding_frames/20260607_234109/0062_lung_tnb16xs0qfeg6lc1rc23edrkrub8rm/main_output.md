**Assessment of Signs:**

*   **Signs of Sliding PRESENT:** The granular, "shimmering" texture observed in Frames 3 and 7 (particularly on the right side) indicates movement of the lung tissue relative to the chest wall. Additionally, the speckle pattern changes significantly between consecutive frames (e.g., from Frame 2 to 3), which confirms movement.
*   **Signs of Sliding ABSENT:** Frames 4, 5, and 6 display prominent horizontal lines (A-lines) that resemble the **barcode sign**. In Frame 8 and the left side of Frame 7, the signal is static or absent.
*   **Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):** The sequence demonstrates a transition between active sliding and static tissue. Specifically, in Frame 3 and Frame 7, there appears to be a lateral transition: the right side shows active, granular sliding (seashore sign), while the left side shows static horizontal lines or darkness (barcode sign/absence). This vertical demarcation between moving lung tissue and static air/chest wall is the hallmark of a **lung point**.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the observation of a transition between sliding tissue (granular/seashore appearance) and static tissue (horizontal lines/barcode appearance) across the frames, particularly visible in Frames 3 and 7, the assessment is **Signs of BOTH**. This pattern is characteristic of a **lung point**, which is pathognomonic for pneumothorax.
