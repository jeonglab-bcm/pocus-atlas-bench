# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
Across all eight frames, the bright, hyperechoic pleural line remains in a fixed position. There is no visible "shimmering" or granular movement of the lung tissue beneath the pleura (which would indicate the lung moving against the chest wall). The horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) extending deep into the image remain perfectly parallel and static. The sub-pleural region appears uniform and lacks the speckle pattern variation that would indicate lung expansion and collapse.

**Conclusion:**
**Absent**

The image displays the classic signs of **absent pleural sliding** (often referred to as the "barcode" or "stratosphere" sign in M-mode, though here visible as static horizontal A-lines in 2D). The pleural line is smooth, sharply defined, and stationary throughout the sequence. This is a hallmark sign of pneumothorax.
