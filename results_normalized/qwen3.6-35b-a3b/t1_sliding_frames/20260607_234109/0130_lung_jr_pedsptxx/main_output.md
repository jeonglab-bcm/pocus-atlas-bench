# 0130_lung_jr_pedsptxx

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
*   **Static Pleural Line:** Across all eight frames, the bright, hyperechoic pleural line remains in a fixed position. There is no visible to-and-fro movement (shimmering) of the visceral pleura relative to the parietal pleura, which would normally occur with respiration.
*   **Static Sub-pleural Speckle:** The texture and horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) below the pleural line do not change their position or pattern between consecutive frames. The "speckle" pattern is frozen.
*   **Lack of Motion:** There is no displacement of the lung tissue relative to the chest wall layers visible at the top of the image.

**Conclusion:**
The signs observed (smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line; no variation in sub-pleural speckle between frames) indicate that **pleural sliding is absent**. This finding is consistent with conditions such as pneumothorax or severe lung consolidation/atelectasis where the lung does not glide against the chest wall.
