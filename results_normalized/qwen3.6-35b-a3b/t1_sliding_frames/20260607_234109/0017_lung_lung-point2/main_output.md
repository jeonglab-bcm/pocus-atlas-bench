# 0017_lung_lung-point2

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frames 1-8:** The top portion of the ultrasound images (B-mode) displays a bright, horizontal echogenic line representing the pleura. Below this line, there is a hypoechoic region.
*   **M-mode (Bottom Portion):** In all frames, the M-mode tracing (Depth vs. Time) reveals a distinct pattern.
    *   **Above the pleural line:** There are horizontal striations representing the static chest wall tissues.
    *   **Below the pleural line:** Instead of a granular, "sandy" texture (which would indicate lung tissue moving with respiration), there are continuous, parallel horizontal lines extending downwards. These are A-lines (reverberation artifacts).
    *   **Comparison across frames:** The pattern of horizontal lines below the pleural line is perfectly static. There is no variation in the position or texture of these lines from frame to frame. The dark vertical bands visible in the lower section are stationary features, not moving lung tissue.

**Assessment:**

*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** None observed. There is no "seashore sign" (granular texture below the pleural line) and no movement of the speckle pattern.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The M-mode clearly demonstrates the **Stratosphere sign** (also known as the **Barcode sign**). This is characterized by horizontal lines that continue uninterrupted below the pleural line, mirroring the chest wall lines above. The A-lines remain perfectly static and unchanged across all frames.

**Conclusion:**

Pleural sliding is **ABSENT**. The presence of the Stratosphere/Barcode sign on M-mode is a classic indicator of pneumothorax (air in the pleural space preventing the visceral and parietal pleura from sliding against each other).
