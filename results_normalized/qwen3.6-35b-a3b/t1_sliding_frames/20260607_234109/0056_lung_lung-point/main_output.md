# 0056_lung_lung-point

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frames 1-8:** The ultrasound images display a consistent view of the pleural interface.
    *   **Superficial layers:** At the top of the image, we see the subcutaneous tissue and muscle layers, characterized by alternating hyperechoic and hypoechoic horizontal bands.
    *   **Pleural Line:** Below the muscle layers, there is a distinct, bright (hyperechoic) horizontal line representing the visceral and parietal pleura. This line appears relatively sharp and continuous across the visible area.
    *   **Deep structures:** Deep to the pleural line (below it), there are multiple, parallel, horizontal hyperechoic lines. These are reverberation artifacts known as **A-lines**. They are equally spaced and mirror the pleural line.
    *   **Motion:** Comparing Frame 1 to Frame 8, there is **no observable change** in the position of the pleural line or the A-lines. The "speckle" or texture of the lung tissue that normally appears granular and moves (shimmers) during respiration is not visible or changing. The pattern is completely static. There are no vertical, laser-like beams (B-lines) originating from the pleural line.

**Assessment of Signs:**

*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** None. There is no granular "shimmering" of the pleural line, no B-lines, and no change in the sub-pleural texture between frames.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
    *   **Static A-lines:** The horizontal lines deep to the pleural line remain in the exact same position throughout the sequence.
    *   **Static Pleural Line:** The pleural interface does not exhibit the dynamic movement associated with lung sliding.
    *   **No speckle variation:** The pattern below the pleural line is identical in every frame, indicating the lung is not expanding and sliding against the chest wall.
*   **Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):** None. There is no visible transition zone where sliding appears in one area and is absent in another. The entire field of view shows the same static pattern.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the complete lack of movement in the pleural line and the sub-pleural tissue, along with the presence of static A-lines, **pleural sliding is absent**. This pattern is consistent with the "Stratosphere" or "Barcode" sign (typically seen on M-mode), which is a strong indicator of pneumothorax.
