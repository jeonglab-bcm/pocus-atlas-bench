# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Anatomy:** The images display the chest wall layers at the top and a distinct, bright, horizontal hyperechoic line below, which represents the **pleural line**. Below this line, horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) are visible.
*   **Motion Assessment:** Comparing the frames from the first to the last, the pleural line remains in the exact same position. The horizontal A-lines below the pleura do not shift or change their vertical spacing. The texture (speckle pattern) of the tissue below the pleural line is identical across all frames.
*   **Signs Observed:**
    *   **Smooth, static pleural line:** The bright line is well-defined and does not exhibit the subtle up-and-down motion associated with respiration.
    *   **Static A-lines:** The horizontal lines below the pleura are fixed in place.
    *   **No speckle variation:** The sub-pleural texture does not change between frames, indicating a lack of movement between the visceral and parietal pleura.

**Conclusion:**
The pleural sliding is **Absent**. The lack of movement of the pleural line and the static nature of the sub-pleural artifacts across the sequence suggest that the lung is not sliding against the chest wall, which is a sign often associated with pneumothorax or lung adhesions.
