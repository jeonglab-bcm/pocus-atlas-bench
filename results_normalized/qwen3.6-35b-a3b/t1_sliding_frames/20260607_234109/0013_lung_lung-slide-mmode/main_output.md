**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frames 1-4:**
    *   **B-mode (bottom):** The image displays a distinct, hyperechoic pleural line. Below this line, there are multiple parallel horizontal lines, which are A-lines (reverberation artifacts). These lines appear static and do not change position between frames.
    *   **M-mode (top left):** The tracing shows the "Stratosphere" or "Barcode" sign. Above the bright pleural line (chest wall), there are horizontal lines. Below the pleural line, the pattern remains horizontal and uniform, lacking the granular "sand" texture.
    *   **Conclusion for these frames:** The presence of static A-lines and the Stratosphere sign indicates **absent pleural sliding**. This is characteristic of a pneumothorax.

*   **Frames 5-8:**
    *   **B-mode (bottom):** The texture below the pleural line changes. The sharp, distinct A-lines are less visible or replaced by a more heterogeneous, granular texture consistent with lung parenchyma.

    *   **Conclusion for these frames:** The presence of the granular "sand" pattern in the M-mode (Seashore sign) and the change in B-mode texture indicates **present pleural sliding**.

**Assessment:**

The video clip captures a transition. Initially (Frames 1-4), the signs of **absent** sliding are visible (Barcode sign, static A-lines). Subsequently (Frames 5-8), the signs of **present** sliding become visible (Seashore sign). The M-mode trace itself shows an alternation from horizontal lines (Barcode) to a granular pattern (Seashore).

**Conclusion:**
Both signs of sliding PRESENT and ABSENT are observed in the sequence. This corresponds to the **Signs of BOTH** category (often indicative of a lung point or a resolving pneumothorax where the lung edge moves into/out of the field of view or the lung re-expands).
