# 0041_lung_2018-1-16-normal-lung-slide

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Pleural Line:** In all frames, a distinct, hyperechoic (bright) horizontal line is visible near the top, representing the pleural line.
*   **Artifact Pattern:** Below the pleural line, there are multiple horizontal, parallel, hyperechoic lines. These are characteristic **A-lines** (reverberation artifacts).
*   **Movement Observation:** When comparing the frames from start to finish, the pleural line remains in a fixed vertical position. It does not move back and forth (shimmer).
*   **Sub-pleural Texture:** The area below the pleural line, including the A-lines and the deeper tissue texture (speckle), appears identical in every frame. There is no change in the pattern or position of the artifacts or tissue echoes, indicating a "frozen" image.

**Assessment:**
The observed features align with the **Signs of sliding ABSENT**:
*   The pleural line is static and sharply defined.
*   The A-lines remain perfectly static and unchanged across all frames.
*   There is no variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between frames.

This pattern is consistent with the **Stratosphere (or Barcode) sign**, which indicates the absence of lung sliding, typically seen in conditions like pneumothorax.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.**
