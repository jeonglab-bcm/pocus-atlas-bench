*   **Comparison:** When comparing the frames from the beginning to the end of the sequence, there is no discernible vertical movement of the pleural line relative to the chest wall structures (muscle/ribs) above it. The horizontal A-lines deep to the pleura remain perfectly aligned and stationary in every frame. The texture of the tissue below the pleural line does not change, indicating a lack of lung expansion against the chest wall.
*   **Signs:**

    *   There are no vertical B-lines originating from the pleura.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the static nature of the pleural line and the horizontal A-lines, which show no variation between consecutive frames, **pleural sliding is absent**. This finding is consistent with the "barcode" or "stratosphere" sign often seen in pneumothorax.
