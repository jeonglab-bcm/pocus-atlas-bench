This "foamy" or granular appearance is characteristic of the aerated lung parenchyma moving against the parietal pleura. This contrasts with the anechoic (black) space seen in pneumothorax or the static horizontal lines seen in the "barcode" sign.

The granular texture is consistent across the visible field, ruling out a pneumothorax in this specific window. The pattern observed is the B-mode equivalent of the "Seashore sign" (though M-mode is not provided, the granular appearance below the line is the B-mode correlate).

**Conclusion:**

Based on the granular, "sandy" appearance of the lung tissue below the pleural line and the subtle rhythmic movement of the structures between frames, **pleural sliding is present**.
