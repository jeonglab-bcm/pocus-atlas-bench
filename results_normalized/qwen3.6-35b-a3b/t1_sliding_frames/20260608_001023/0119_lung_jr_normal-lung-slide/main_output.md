# 0119_lung_jr_normal-lung-slide

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Pleural Line:** In all frames, there is a distinct, hyperechoic (bright) horizontal line near the top of the image, which represents the pleural line.
*   **Sub-pleural Region:** Below the pleural line, there are horizontal reverberation artifacts known as A-lines. More importantly, the tissue immediately beneath the pleural line exhibits a granular, "sandy" texture (lung parenchyma).
*   **Motion/Speckle Change:** When comparing consecutive frames (e.g., Frame 1 vs. Frame 2 vs. Frame 5), the fine granular speckle pattern in the sub-pleural region shifts slightly. The texture is not rigidly static; the arrangement of the echoes changes subtly from one frame to the next. This indicates movement of the lung surface.

**Conclusion:**
The observation of a shifting, granular speckle pattern below the pleural line confirms the presence of lung movement.

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
