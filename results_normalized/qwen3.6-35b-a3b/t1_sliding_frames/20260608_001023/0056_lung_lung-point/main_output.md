# 0056_lung_lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Pleural Line:** In all frames, there is a distinct, bright, horizontal echogenic line representing the pleura. This line remains in a fixed vertical position throughout the sequence. It appears as a sharp, thin, and static interface rather than a "shimmering" or granular structure.
*   **Sub-pleural Region:** Below the pleural line, there are multiple parallel, horizontal hyperechoic lines. These are A-lines (reverberation artifacts).
*   **Motion:** Comparing consecutive frames (e.g., Frame 1 to Frame 8), there is no detectable vertical movement of the pleural line or the A-lines. The pattern of horizontal lines remains perfectly static.
*   **Vertical Artifacts:** There are vertical dark shadows extending downwards, which correspond to rib shadows (acoustic shadowing), rather than moving B-lines.
*   **Overall Pattern:** The combination of a static pleural line and static, horizontal A-lines creates a pattern consistent with the "Stratosphere" or "Barcode" sign (typically seen on M-mode, but evidenced by static A-lines on B-mode).

**Conclusion:**
The pleural line is static, and the A-lines do not move or change between frames. There is no evidence of the "sea" texture (lung parenchyma) moving beneath the pleura. These findings are consistent with the **absent** sliding sign.

**Assessment: Absent**
