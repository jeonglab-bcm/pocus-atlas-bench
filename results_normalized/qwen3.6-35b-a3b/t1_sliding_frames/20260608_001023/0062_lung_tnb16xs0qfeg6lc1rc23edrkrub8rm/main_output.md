4.  **Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):**
    *   The sequence demonstrates a transition. In the middle frames (5-7), we see a region with a granular/bright texture (center) which is changing/appearing (indicating sliding), adjacent to regions with static A-lines (left) or darkness (right) which are not changing.
    *   This coexistence of a moving lung (sliding present) and a static air space (sliding absent/A-lines) defines a **Lung Point**. The transition point is likely located at the boundary between the bright central area and the peripheral static areas.

**Conclusion:**
Both signs of sliding (movement in the center) and signs of no sliding (static A-lines on the sides) are visible in the sequence, particularly in the middle frames. This indicates the presence of a **Lung Point**.
