# 0130_lung_jr_pedsptxx

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** A distinct, bright (hyperechoic), horizontal line is visible representing the pleura. It remains in a fixed position relative to the chest wall structures (skin, subcutaneous tissue, muscle) throughout all frames.
*   **A-lines:** Deep to the pleural line, there are multiple horizontal, parallel hyperechoic lines. These are reverberation artifacts known as A-lines.
*   **Motion/Slide:** Comparing the frames from the beginning to the end, there is no detectable movement. The pleural line does not "shimmer" or shift. The A-lines remain perfectly static and do not change their spacing or position. The speckle pattern in the region below the pleura (the lung parenchyma) does not vary between frames.

**Assessment:**
The lack of movement in the pleural line and the static nature of the A-lines and sub-pleural tissue indicate that the lung is not moving against the chest wall. This corresponds to the **Stratosphere / Barcode sign** (if viewed on M-mode) and the lack of variation in sub-pleural speckle.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**.
