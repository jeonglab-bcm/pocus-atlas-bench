    *   If they are different spots, and one has sliding (7-8) and one doesn't (1-6), then it's a Lung Point.
    *   So "Both".

    *   Frames 1-6 show static horizontal A-lines and a static pleural line, indicative of absent sliding (Stratosphere sign).
    *   Frames 7-8 show vertical B-lines and sub-pleural nodules. B-lines move with lung sliding, indicating present sliding.
