# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observations:**
- **Pleural Line:** The bright, horizontal line representing the pleura appears static across all frames. It does not exhibit the "shimmering" or jagged motion associated with lung sliding.
- **Sub-pleural Area:** Deep to the pleural line, the image is dominated by horizontal, parallel echogenic lines (A-lines) at regular intervals. There is a lack of the granular, textured appearance of lung parenchyma immediately below the pleura.
- **Static Nature:** The position of the pleural line and the A-lines remains identical from frame to frame. There is no variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern.

**Conclusion:**
The consistent presence of the static pleural line and the vertical series of horizontal A-lines (resembling a barcode) indicates the **absence** of pleural sliding. This pattern is characteristic of the "Barcode sign" or "Stratosphere sign," typically seen in pneumothorax.

**Assessment: Absent**
