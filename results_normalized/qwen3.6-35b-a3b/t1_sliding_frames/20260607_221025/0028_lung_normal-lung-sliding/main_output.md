# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Frame-by-frame observation:**
- In all frames (1 through 8), a distinct, bright, horizontal hyperechoic line is visible, representing the pleural line.
- Beneath this line, there are multiple parallel horizontal echogenic lines, which are characteristic of A-lines (reverberation artifacts).
- Comparing the frames, the position of the pleural line and the underlying A-lines remains completely static. There is no "shimmering" or granular texture change at the pleural interface, nor is there any variation in the speckle pattern of the tissue below the pleural line. The structures appear frozen in time across the sequence.

**Conclusion:**
The visual evidence points to **pleural sliding being ABSENT**.

This is indicated by the smooth, sharply defined, and static pleural line and the static A-lines, which are consistent with the absence of lung movement (potential pneumothorax or lung consolidation/atelectasis where sliding is lost).
