# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Pleural Line:** In all frames, a distinct, hyperechoic (bright white) horizontal line is visible, representing the pleural interface. This line separates the chest wall layers (above) from the lung tissue (below).
*   **Sub-pleural Area (Lung):**
    *   In **Frames 1, 2, 6, 7, and 8**, the area immediately below the pleural line displays horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) and a relatively dark, consistent granular texture.
    *   In **Frames 3, 4, and 5**, a distinct vertical hyperechoic artifact (a B-line or comet-tail artifact) appears originating from the pleural line. It is most prominent in Frame 4.
*   **Comparison:** Comparing the frames, particularly the transition from Frame 2 to Frame 3 and Frame 5 to Frame 6, there is a clear variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern. Specifically, the appearance and intensification of the vertical B-line artifact in the middle frames indicates movement or change in the lung tissue relative to the chest wall.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of the vertical artifact (B-line) and the variation in the sub-pleural texture between consecutive frames correspond to the criteria for **Signs of sliding PRESENT** ("Comet-tail artifacts or B-lines originating from the pleural line" and "Speckle pattern below the pleural line differs between consecutive frames"). The lung tissue is moving against the chest wall.

**Assessment: Pleural sliding is present.**
