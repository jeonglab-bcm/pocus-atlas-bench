# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

**Analysis of Frames:**

*   **B-mode (Bottom Panel):** Throughout the sequence, the B-mode image displays a hyperechoic pleural line. Below the pleural line, there are multiple horizontal reverberation artifacts known as **A-lines**. This pattern typically suggests the presence of air in the pleural space (pneumothorax), which is a sign of **absent sliding**. However, there is a distinct vertical hyperechoic artifact labeled with a blue **"B"** circle. This is a **B-line** (comet-tail artifact), which originates from the pleural line and extends to the bottom of the screen without fading. B-lines indicate the presence of fluid or thickened interlobular septa in an expanded lung, and their presence usually correlates with **present sliding**.
*   **M-mode (Top Panel):**
    *   In the early frames (e.g., Frame 1), the zoomed-in crop in the top-left corner shows the area below the pleural line with a granular, "sandy" texture. This is the **Seashore sign**, which indicates **present sliding** (movement of the lung against the chest wall).
    *   In the later frames (e.g., Frame 4 onwards), the full M-mode view shows horizontal striations both above and below the pleural line. The pattern below the line resembles the pattern above it. This is the **Stratosphere sign** (or Barcode sign), which indicates **absent sliding** (static lung tissue, typical of pneumothorax).

**Assessment:**

The image displays a classic mix of findings:
1.  **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The presence of A-lines in the B-mode and the Stratosphere/Barcode sign in the main M-mode view.
2.  **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** The presence of a B-line (labeled "B") and the granular texture (Seashore sign) visible in the zoomed section/corresponding to the B-line's location.

The coexistence of A-lines (indicating pneumothorax/no sliding) and B-lines (indicating expanded lung/sliding) in the same field of view is the hallmark of a **Lung Point**. The Lung Point is the transition zone between the area of pneumothorax (no sliding) and the area of lung contact (sliding present). The M-mode likely captures this transition, showing Stratosphere in the pneumothorax region and Seashore in the sliding region.

**Conclusion:**

**Signs of BOTH (lung point)**
