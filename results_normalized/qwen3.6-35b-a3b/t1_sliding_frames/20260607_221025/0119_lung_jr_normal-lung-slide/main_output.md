# 0119_lung_jr_normal-lung-slide

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Frame 1:** A bright, horizontal echogenic line representing the pleura is visible near the top of the screen. Below this line, there are multiple horizontal reverberation artifacts known as A-lines. The tissue texture below the pleura appears static.
*   **Frames 2-8:** Comparing these subsequent frames to the first one, there is no discernible movement. The pleural line remains in the exact same position. The horizontal A-lines below it are perfectly aligned and do not shift, expand, or contract. The speckle pattern (the grainy texture) in the sub-pleural space is identical in every frame, indicating a complete lack of motion.

**Assessment:**
The key observation is the complete lack of movement of the pleural line and the sub-pleural structures between consecutive frames. The A-lines remain perfectly static. This corresponds to the sign of **absent** pleural sliding. In a dynamic view with sliding, one would expect to see the "seashore sign" (granular pattern) or the lung tissue moving slightly relative to the chest wall (shimmering). Here, the image is static, resembling the "stratosphere" or "barcode" sign.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**.
