**Analysis of Frames:**

*   **Frame 1-8:** In all consecutive frames, the hyperechoic pleural line appears as a smooth, sharply defined, horizontal band. Deep to the pleural line, there are horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) that are parallel to the pleural line.
*   **Movement Check:** Comparing the frames from top to bottom, there is no visible displacement of the pleural line or the underlying lung tissue. The texture and position of the "speckle" pattern below the pleural line remain identical across all images. There is no "shimmering" or granular appearance that would indicate the visceral and parietal pleura sliding past each other.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the lack of motion in the pleural line and the deep tissues, as well as the perfectly static A-lines, the signs of pleural sliding are not seen.

**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.**
