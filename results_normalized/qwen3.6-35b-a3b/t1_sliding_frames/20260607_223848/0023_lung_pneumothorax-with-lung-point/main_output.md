# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Frame 1:** A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is visible near the top of the sector. Below it, horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) are present. The tissue just below the pleural line has a granular appearance.
*   **Frame 2 - 8:** As we progress through the frames, there is a subtle but observable downward shift of the pleural line and the A-lines, indicating movement away from the transducer (consistent with inspiration). Furthermore, the granular speckle pattern of the lung parenchyma just beneath the pleural line changes slightly between frames (e.g., compare the texture on the left side in Frame 1 vs. Frame 5). The bright pleural line itself exhibits a "shimmering" quality as it moves.

**Assessment of Signs:**
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
    *   **Speckle pattern variation:** The texture of the lung tissue (sub-pleural region) shifts and changes slightly between consecutive frames, indicating motion.
    *   **Movement of the pleural line/A-lines:** The entire interface and the reverberation artifacts drift slightly downwards across the sequence, rather than remaining perfectly static.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
    *   There is no evidence of the "stratosphere" or "barcode" sign (which would appear as perfectly static, parallel lines without movement).
    *   The A-lines are not perfectly static; they move with the pleural line.

**Conclusion:**
The observation of shifting speckle patterns and the subtle motion of the pleural line and A-lines confirms that the lung is moving against the chest wall.

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
