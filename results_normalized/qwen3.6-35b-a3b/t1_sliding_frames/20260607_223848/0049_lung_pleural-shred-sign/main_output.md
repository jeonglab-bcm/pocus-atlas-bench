# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
Throughout the video frames, the bright, horizontal pleural line exhibits a subtle but consistent movement relative to the chest wall layers above it. The tissue interface is not perfectly static; it shifts position slightly in each frame. Furthermore, the granular, speckled pattern in the sub-pleural region (the deep shadow below the bright line) changes texture and position from frame to frame. This fluctuation indicates that the lung is expanding and contracting, causing the visceral pleura to glide over the parietal pleura.

**Conclusion:**
