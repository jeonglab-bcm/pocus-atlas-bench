# 0130_lung_jr_pedsptxx

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
*   **Pleural Line:** A distinct, hyperechoic (bright) line is visible, representing the visceral-parietal pleural interface.
*   **Sub-pleural Texture:** Immediately deep to (below) this bright line, there is a granular, textured appearance. This area is often described as having a "sandy" appearance.
*   **Motion:** When comparing the frames (e.g., Frame 1 vs. Frame 4), there is a subtle but visible shift in the speckle pattern and texture of the lung parenchyma just below the pleural line. The granular area does not remain perfectly static; it exhibits a "shimmering" effect.
*   **Artifacts:** Deeper in the image, horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) are visible, but the presence of the granular zone above them confirms movement.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The presence of the granular "sandy" pattern below the pleural line and the visible motion of this pattern between frames are classic signs of normal lung sliding. This rules out pneumothorax (which would show a static barcode/stratosphere sign).
