*   **Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):** There is a visible vertical transition point in Frames 1-7. To the left of this point is the anechoic area (sign of absent sliding/pneumothorax), and to the right is the lung tissue (sign of present sliding). The appearance of the lung tissue in the previously anechoic area in Frame 8 confirms that this boundary is dynamic, consistent with the **Lung Point** sign, where sliding is present on one side of the transition and absent on the other.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the visible transition between an anechoic area (absent sliding) and echogenic lung tissue (present sliding), along with the dynamic change in the field of view by the final frame, the assessment is:
