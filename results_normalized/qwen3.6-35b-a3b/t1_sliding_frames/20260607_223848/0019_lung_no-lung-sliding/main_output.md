# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Observation:** In all frames, the chest wall layers are visible at the top. Deep to the chest wall, a bright, echogenic pleural line is clearly visible between the two hyperechoic structures (likely ribs).
*   **Deep Structures:** Deep to the pleural line, there are distinct, horizontal, parallel reverberation artifacts known as A-lines. These lines are equidistant from each other.
*   **Motion Analysis:** Comparing Frame 1 through Frame 8, there is no discernible movement of the pleural line relative to the chest wall. The horizontal A-lines remain perfectly fixed in position and orientation. There is no "shimmering" or granular motion (which would indicate the lung sliding back and forth). The texture of the sub-pleural region does not change; it remains a static pattern of horizontal lines.

**Conclusion:**
The absence of the "shimmering" pleural line and the presence of perfectly static, horizontal A-lines indicate that lung sliding is not occurring. This static appearance corresponds to the "Stratosphere" or "Barcode" sign often seen in M-mode.

**Assessment: Absent**
