# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
Across all eight frames, the pleural line (the bright, hyperechoic horizontal band separating the chest wall from the lung) remains in a completely fixed position. The horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) below the pleural line are also perfectly static and do not change position or intensity between frames. There is no visible "shimmering" or movement of the pleural interface that would indicate the visceral and parietal pleura moving against each other during respiration. The tissue below the pleural line shows a static speckle pattern rather than dynamic movement.

**Conclusion:**
The signs of sliding are **ABSENT**. The static nature of the pleural line and A-lines indicates no pleural sliding is occurring in this view.
