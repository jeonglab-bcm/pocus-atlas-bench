# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Anatomy:** The images display a longitudinal view of the thoracic wall. Two hyperechoic (bright) structures with posterior acoustic shadowing (dark vertical bands) are visible on the left and right sides, representing ribs. Between them lies the intercostal space.
*   **Pleural Line:** A bright, horizontal line is visible between the ribs. This is the pleural line (visceral and parietal pleura interface).
*   **Sub-pleural Region:** Below the pleural line, there are multiple parallel, horizontal hyperechoic lines. These are A-lines, which are reverberation artifacts indicating the presence of air in the lung (or lack of lung tissue/sliding).
*   **Motion:** Comparing Frame 1 through Frame 8, there is no discernible movement.
    *   The pleural line remains in the exact same vertical position relative to the ribs.
    *   The horizontal A-lines remain perfectly static and unchanged in depth and intensity.
    *   There is no "shimmering" or granular texture movement just beneath the pleural line. The interface looks sharp and fixed.

**Assessment of Signs:**
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** None observed. There is no shimmering pleural line, no B-lines, and no changing speckle pattern.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
    *   **Smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line:** The pleural line is distinct and does not move.
    *   **Stratosphere / barcode sign:** The presence of static horizontal A-lines creates a pattern analogous to the "barcode sign" seen on M-mode (though these are B-mode frames, the static horizontal nature is the key indicator).
    *   **Static A-lines:** The reverberation artifacts do not shift position.
    *   **No variation in speckle:** The texture below the pleura is static.

**Conclusion:**
The analysis reveals a static pleural line with no movement and static A-lines. This indicates that the lung is not expanding and moving against the chest wall.

**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.**
