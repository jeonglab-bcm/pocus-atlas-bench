# 0056_lung_lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
The images display the layers of the chest wall (skin, subcutaneous tissue, and intercostal muscles) at the top, followed by the hyperechoic (bright) pleural line. Below the pleural line, horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) are visible.

**Analysis:**
- **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** When comparing the frames, there is a subtle but discernible change in the position and texture of the pleural line and the underlying lung parenchyma. The pleural line exhibits a "shimmering" or granular appearance rather than being a thin, static hairline. The speckle pattern in the region immediately below the pleural line varies slightly between consecutive frames, indicating movement of the lung tissue relative to the chest wall during respiration.
- **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** There is no evidence of a perfectly static, sharp pleural line, nor is there a "barcode" or "stratosphere" pattern where the sub-pleural texture is frozen. The A-lines are present, but the structures above them are not fixed.
- **Signs of BOTH:** There is no visible transition point where the sliding stops; the motion appears consistent across the visible pleural line.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **present**.
