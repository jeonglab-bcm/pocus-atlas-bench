# 0041_lung_2018-1-16-normal-lung-slide

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frames 1-8:** Across the sequence, a distinct, hyperechoic (bright) pleural line is visible near the top of the lung field, separating the chest wall layers above from the lung tissue below.
*   **Artifacts:** Originating from the pleural line and extending downwards to the bottom of the screen are vertical, laser-like bright artifacts. These are B-lines (comet-tail artifacts). They are non-homogenous and appear to move or change intensity slightly between frames.
*   **Sub-pleural Texture:** Below the pleural line, the lung parenchyma shows a granular texture. Comparing consecutive frames (e.g., Frame 1 vs. Frame 4 vs. Frame 8), the specific pattern of the vertical artifacts and the grey-scale texture of the lung tissue shifts slightly. This indicates that the lung tissue is moving relative to the chest wall probe.
*   **Absence of Static Signs:** There is no evidence of a static "barcode" or "stratosphere" pattern. The horizontal A-lines typically seen in a static lung (pneumothorax) are not the dominant feature; instead, the vertical B-lines and dynamic tissue texture dominate.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the presence of **B-lines (comet-tail artifacts)** originating from the pleural line and the observation that the **speckle pattern below the pleural line differs between consecutive frames**, the lung is moving.

**Pleural sliding is present.**
