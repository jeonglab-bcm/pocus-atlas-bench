Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
- The images show a region of the lung with a distinct hyperechoic line at the top (the pleural line), marked by the red overlay. Above it are superficial tissues marked by green.
- Below the pleural line, there are prominent horizontal, parallel echogenic lines. These are **A-lines**, which are reverberation artifacts caused by the reflection of sound waves off the pleural surface.
- Comparing the frames from 1 through 8, the position of the pleural line and the A-lines remains **completely static**. There is no "shimmering" or oscillation of the pleural line that would indicate respiratory movement.
- The sub-pleural speckle pattern does not change between frames.
- The visual appearance of a static pleural line with horizontal A-lines corresponds to the 2D equivalent of the "stratosphere" or "barcode" sign seen on M-mode, which indicates a lack of lung sliding.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.**
