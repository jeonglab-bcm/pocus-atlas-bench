# 0119_lung_jr_normal-lung-slide

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
The ultrasound images display a standard intercostal view.
*   **Pleural Line:** There is a distinct, hyperechoic (bright white) horizontal line near the top of the lung window, representing the pleural interface.
*   **Artifacts:** Below the pleural line, there are regularly spaced, horizontal hyperechoic lines. These are A-lines (reverberation artifacts), which are characteristic of normal, aerated lung or pneumothorax.
*   **Motion Analysis:** Comparing the frames from start to finish, the position of the pleural line and the A-lines remains completely fixed. There is no vertical movement of the pleural line relative to the chest wall. The "speckle" or granular texture in the sub-pleural region does not change or shimmer between frames. The horizontal lines do not expand or contract.

**Conclusion:**
The features observed align with the signs of **sliding ABSENT**. Specifically, the pleural line is smooth and static, and the A-lines remain perfectly unchanged across all frames, suggesting a lack of lung sliding (often seen in pneumothorax).

**Pleural sliding: Absent**
