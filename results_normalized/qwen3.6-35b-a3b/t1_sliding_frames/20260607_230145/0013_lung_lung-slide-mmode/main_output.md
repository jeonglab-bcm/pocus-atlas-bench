# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **M-mode (Top Panel):** Across all frames, the M-mode cursor (indicated by the green line in the B-mode view) traces a region where the image consists of parallel, horizontal lines both above and below the bright pleural line. This pattern is known as the **Stratosphere sign** or **Barcode sign**. The characteristic "sandy" or granular appearance (seashore sign) seen in the lung area below the pleural line is missing.
*   **B-mode (Bottom Panel):** The image shows a distinct, hyperechoic (bright) pleural line. Below this line, there are multiple horizontal, hyperechoic lines extending deep into the field. These are **A-lines** (reverberation artifacts). The tissue immediately beneath the pleura is static and lacks the granular, moving speckle pattern that indicates lung sliding.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of the Barcode sign on M-mode and the static A-lines on B-mode indicate that the lung is not moving against the chest wall. Therefore, pleural sliding is **absent**. This finding is highly suggestive of a pneumothorax.
