# 0137_lung_jr_lungpoint-rxmed

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
- The images display a horizontal, echogenic (bright) line near the top, which represents the pleural line.
- Below this line, there are parallel horizontal hyperechoic lines, characteristic of A-lines (reverberation artifacts).
- Comparing all eight frames, the position of the pleural line and the structures beneath it (the A-lines and deeper tissues) remain completely stationary. There is no "shimmering," no granular movement of the sub-pleural lung tissue, and no change in the speckle pattern between consecutive frames.
- The pleural line appears smooth and sharply defined throughout the sequence.

**Conclusion:**
The lack of motion between the pleural line and the underlying lung tissue indicates that **pleural sliding is absent**. This pattern is consistent with the "stratosphere" or "barcode" sign often seen in M-mode ultrasound, suggesting a condition such as pneumothorax where air separates the pleural layers.
