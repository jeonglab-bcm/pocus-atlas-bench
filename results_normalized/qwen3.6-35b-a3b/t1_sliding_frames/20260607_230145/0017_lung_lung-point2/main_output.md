# 0017_lung_lung-point2

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Analysis of Frames:**
*   **2D Mode (Top Panel):** The bright horizontal line represents the pleura. Below this line, the tissue appears somewhat textured, distinct from the acoustic shadowing seen in pneumothorax.
*   **M-Mode (Bottom Panel):** This is the key diagnostic view.
    *   **Above the pleural line (0 - 1 cm depth):** There are horizontal, parallel lines. This represents the static soft tissues of the chest wall (muscles, subcutaneous tissue).
    *   **Below the pleural line (> 1.5 cm depth):** There is a granular, "sandy" appearance. This pattern is distinct from the horizontal lines above. This specific pattern is known as the **"Seashore sign"**.
*   **Frame-to-Frame Comparison:** As you move from Frame 1 to Frame 8, the granular pattern in the lower M-mode section changes and shifts. This indicates movement of the lung tissue relative to the chest wall.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of the **Seashore sign** (granular pattern below the pleural line) and the dynamic change in the speckle pattern between frames confirm that the lung is moving against the chest wall.

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
