# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

**Step 1 — Per-position classification:**

*   **p00 (Image 1):** The image is almost entirely black with very faint horizontal lines at the very top. This indicates the probe is outside the active scanning region or signal is lost.
    *   Classification: **Unclassifiable**

*   **p01 (Image 2):** There are horizontal lines at the top. A bright pleural line is visible. Below the pleural line, the image is dark with very faint horizontal structures, lacking the granular "sandy" texture of lung parenchyma. This pattern of continuous lines (or dark space) below the pleura without lung sliding is characteristic of the stratosphere sign.
    *   Classification: **Stratosphere**

*   **p02 (Image 3):** Similar to p01. Horizontal lines above. Bright pleural line. Below, the texture is dark and streaky/linear, not granular.
    *   Classification: **Stratosphere**

*   **p03 (Image 4):** Horizontal lines above. Bright pleural line. Below, there is a gray band, but it appears to be composed of faint horizontal streaks (A-lines) rather than the distinct granular "sand" seen in later images.
    *   Classification: **Stratosphere**

*   **p04 (Image 5):** This image shows dense, continuous parallel horizontal lines extending from the top all the way down, including through and below the pleural line. There is no granular texture. This is the classic "Barcode" or Stratosphere sign.
    *   Classification: **Stratosphere**

*   **p05 (Image 6):** Horizontal lines are present at the top. A bright pleural line is visible. Crucially, below the pleural line, there is a distinct granular, "sandy" texture (lung parenchyma). This indicates the presence of lung tissue and sliding.
    *   Classification: **Seashore**

*   **p06 (Image 7):** Similar to p05. Clear granular/sandy texture below the pleural line.
    *   Classification: **Seashore**

*   **p07 (Image 8):** Similar to p05. Granular texture below the pleural line.
    *   Classification: **Seashore**

*   **p08 (Image 9):** Similar to p05. Granular texture below the pleural line.
    *   Classification: **Seashore**

*   **p09 (Image 10):** Similar to p05. Granular texture below the pleural line.
    *   Classification: **Seashore**

**Step 2 — Overall decision:**

*   **Excluded positions:** p00 (Unclassifiable).
*   **Classifiable positions:**
    *   Stratosphere: p01, p02, p03, p04
    *   Seashore: p05, p06, p07, p08, p09
*   **Observation:** There is a clear spatial transition from Stratosphere pattern (positions p01-p04) to Seashore pattern (positions p05-p09). This indicates the presence of a lung point, where the lung slides in one area (Seashore) and does not slide (pneumothorax) in another (Stratosphere).

**Overall label:** both
