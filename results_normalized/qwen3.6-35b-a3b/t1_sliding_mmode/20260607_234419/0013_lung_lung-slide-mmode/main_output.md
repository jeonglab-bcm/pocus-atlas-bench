# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on the visual analysis of the 10 synthetic M-mode images:

**Step 1 — Per-position classification:**

*   **p00 (Image 1):** The image shows horizontal parallel lines in the upper region (above the pleural line) and a **granular, sandy texture** in the lower region (below the pleural line). This is the classic **Seashore** sign.
*   **p01 (Image 2):** Similar to p00, horizontal lines above and granular texture below. **Seashore**.
*   **p02 (Image 3):** Horizontal lines above, granular texture below. **Seashore**.
*   **p03 (Image 4):** Horizontal lines above, granular texture below. **Seashore**.
*   **p04 (Image 5):** Horizontal lines above. A bright A-line is visible below the pleural line, but the background texture remains **granular/sandy**, not continuous parallel lines. **Seashore**.
*   **p05 (Image 6):** Horizontal lines above. A bright A-line is visible, but the surrounding texture is granular. **Seashore**.
*   **p06 (Image 7):** Horizontal lines above, granular texture below. **Seashore**.
*   **p07 (Image 8):** Horizontal lines above, granular texture below. **Seashore**.
*   **p08 (Image 9):** Horizontal lines above, granular texture below. **Seashore**.
*   **p09 (Image 10):** Horizontal lines above, granular texture below. **Seashore**.

*Note: No images appear "nearly black" or lack sufficient structure to be considered unclassifiable. All show clear M-mode signals.*

**Step 2 — Overall decision:**

All 10 classifiable positions exhibit the **Seashore** pattern. There are no regions of Stratosphere/Barcode pattern, nor are there alternating bands indicating a lung point. The presence of the seashore pattern in all positions indicates that pleural sliding is present throughout the scanned region.

**Overall label: present**
