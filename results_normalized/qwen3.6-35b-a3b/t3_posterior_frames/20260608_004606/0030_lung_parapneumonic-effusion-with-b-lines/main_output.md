# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

The ultrasound images show the posterior lateral lung zone (PLAPS point).
- **Effusion signs:** In frames 3, 5, and 7, there is a large anechoic to hypoechoic space. Within this space, a rounded, echogenic structure is visible, resembling a "Jellyfish" floating in fluid. This represents atelectatic lung floating in a pleural effusion. The "Quad sign" is suggested by the anechoic space bounded by the chest wall/ribs.
- **Consolidation signs:** The floating tissue (the "body" of the jellyfish) and the surrounding parenchyma in other frames appear tissue-like and echogenic (hepatization), indicating a loss of aeration. Hyperechoic foci, consistent with **air bronchograms**, are visible within the consolidated tissue (e.g., in frame 3 and 5). The border of the consolidated area is irregular.

**Conclusion:**
The image displays signs of both a pleural effusion (Jellyfish sign, anechoic space) and lung consolidation (hepatization, air bronchograms).
