# 0058_lung_pneumonia

The ultrasound images display the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point). The most prominent feature is a large anechoic (black) space, which indicates a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a solid, echogenic mass of lung tissue that appears "hepatized" (resembling the texture of the liver), indicating alveolar consolidation. Inside this consolidated tissue, there are multiple hyperechoic (bright) punctate and linear structures, which are air bronchograms. The appearance of the consolidated lung floating within the surrounding pleural fluid is characteristic of the "Jellyfish sign."
