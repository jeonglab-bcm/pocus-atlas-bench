# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

The images display a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. This space is bounded by the chest wall and ribs, creating a **Quad sign**. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, echogenic structure that appears to be atelectatic lung tissue, consistent with the **Jellyfish sign**. The tissue itself appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver texture (**tissue-like hepatization**), and has an irregular border against the surrounding structures, suggestive of the **Shred sign**. These findings indicate the presence of both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation (or significant atelectasis which appears consolidated).
