# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

Based on the visual evidence in the sequential frames:

**Observations:**
-   **Effusion:** In the first frame, there is a prominent anechoic (black) region, indicative of a pleural effusion. The shape and location suggest a significant fluid collection in the dependent posterior zone.
-   **Consolidation:** In the subsequent frames (2 through 8), a large area of the lung parenchyma is visible as echogenic and solid, resembling the texture of the liver ("tissue-like hepatization"). This loss of normal aeration artifacts (A-lines) and the presence of a solid, granular texture are hallmarks of alveolar consolidation.
-   **Combination:** The presence of the anechoic fluid space alongside the solid, hepatized lung tissue indicates that both pathologies are present. The first frame potentially hints at the "jellyfish sign" (atelectatic lung floating in effusion) or simply a large consolidation adjacent to a large effusion.

**Conclusion:**
-   **plaps**: true
-   **type**: "both"
