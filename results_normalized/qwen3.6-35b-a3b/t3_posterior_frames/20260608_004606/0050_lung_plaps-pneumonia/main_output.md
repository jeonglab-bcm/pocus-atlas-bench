# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

Based on the provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point):

**Observation:**
The images reveal a significant anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. This fluid collection creates a "Quad sign" appearance, particularly visible in the earlier frames (1-6), where the fluid is bounded by the chest wall/pleura and rib shadows. Within this fluid collection, there is an echogenic, tissue-like structure that appears to be floating and undulating, consistent with the **"Jellyfish sign"**. This structure represents atelectatic or consolidated lung parenchyma (tissue-like hepatization) suspended in the effusion. The presence of the fluid and the solid, non-aerated lung tissue within it indicates both pathological processes.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
