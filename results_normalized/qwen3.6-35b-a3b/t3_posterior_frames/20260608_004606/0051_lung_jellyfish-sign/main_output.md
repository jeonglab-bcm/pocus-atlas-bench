# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The images display a large anechoic (black) space in the posterior lung zone, which corresponds to the pleural space. This fluid collection creates a **quad sign**, where the anechoic fluid is bounded by the ribs (lateral), the diaphragm (inferior), and the pleural line/lung (medial/superior).

Furthermore, within this fluid collection, a distinct, echogenic, undulating structure is visible (particularly clear in frames 4, 5, and 6). This represents an atelectatic (collapsed) lung lobe floating in the pleural fluid, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**. The presence of this sign confirms a significant pleural effusion. While the floating lung is atelectatic (a form of consolidation), the primary sonographic features described (Quad sign, Jellyfish sign) fall under the effusion category in the provided definitions.
