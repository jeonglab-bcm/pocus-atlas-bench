# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The image sequence displays a posterior lung zone ultrasound.

**Observations:**
1.  **Effusion Signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) area occupying a significant portion of the image, indicative of a pleural effusion. In the initial frames (1-3), a distinct, linear, hyperechoic structure is visible floating within or adjacent to this fluid collection. This undulating structure represents atelectatic lung tissue floating in the fluid, characteristic of the **"Jellyfish sign"**. The boundaries of the fluid collection appear to be formed by rib shadows laterally and the chest wall/pleura superiorly, suggestive of the **"Quad sign"**.
2.  **Consolidation Signs:** Adjacent to and beneath the fluid collection, there is a large region of homogeneous, echogenic tissue that resembles the texture of the liver. This is **"tissue-like hepatization"**, indicating lung consolidation (pneumonia). The border of this consolidated area is somewhat irregular.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both significant pleural fluid (effusion) and solid, hepatized lung tissue (consolidation) is evident.
