# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

The ultrasound images display a classic appearance of the PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) point.

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion Signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) space indicative of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, particularly visible in the earlier frames, there is an echogenic, floating structure that resembles a jellyfish in water. This is the **Jellyfish sign**, representing atelectatic lung tissue floating within the pleural fluid.
*   **Consolidation Signs:** Adjacent to and surrounded by the fluid is a large area of lung parenchyma that appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue. This is **tissue-like hepatization**. Within this consolidated area, there are punctate and linear hyperechoic foci, which represent **air bronchograms** (air-filled bronchi surrounded by fluid-filled alveoli).

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both a large pleural effusion and significant lung consolidation (hepatization) with air bronchograms confirms the PLAPS pattern.
