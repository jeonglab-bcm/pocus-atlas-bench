# 0143_lung_jr_pleuraleffusion

The ultrasound images reveal a large anechoic (black) space within the pleural cavity, which is characteristic of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, a distinct, echogenic, irregular structure is visible floating in the fluid. This appearance corresponds to the **Jellyfish sign**, which represents atelectatic (collapsed) or consolidated lung tissue floating within the pleural fluid. The presence of this sign, along with the large fluid collection, confirms pathology in the dependent posterior lung zone (PLAPS point).

**plaps = true**
**type = "effusion"**
