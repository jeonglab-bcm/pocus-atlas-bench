# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

The ultrasound images show a posterior lung zone (PLAPS point). The most prominent finding is a large, triangular or wedge-shaped area of hyperechoic tissue that resembles the texture of the liver (hepatization). This indicates **alveolar consolidation**. The border of this consolidated lung is relatively sharp against the surrounding anechoic (dark) area.

The anechoic area surrounding the consolidated lung represents a **pleural effusion**. The appearance of the consolidated lung floating or suspended within the fluid is characteristic of the **Jellyfish sign** (or potentially the **Quad sign** if looking at the fluid pocket adjacent to the chest wall, though the boundaries are less distinct here). The presence of the solid, tissue-like lung parenchyma confirms consolidation, and the surrounding fluid confirms effusion.

**Conclusion:**
**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
