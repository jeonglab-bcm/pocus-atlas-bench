# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

The ultrasound images demonstrate a significant pathological finding in the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point).

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion:** There is a large, prominent anechoic (black) space visible in the center of the field. This represents a fluid collection. The geometry of this space, appearing as a quadrilateral shape bounded by the chest wall/pleura superiorly and potentially rib shadows laterally (visible as bright vertical lines), is consistent with the **Quad sign**. Additionally, the echogenic tissue adjacent to the fluid appears to be suspended within it, suggestive of the **Jellyfish sign** (atelectatic lung floating in effusion).
*   **Consolidation:** Adjacent to the anechoic fluid, there is a large area of hyperechoic, tissue-like parenchyma. This "hepatized" texture, resembling the liver, indicates loss of aeration and is characteristic of alveolar **consolidation**. The border between this consolidated tissue and the fluid/air is irregular.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both a significant pleural effusion (Quad sign/Jellyfish sign) and adjacent lung consolidation (tissue-like hepatization) confirms the PLAPS pattern.
