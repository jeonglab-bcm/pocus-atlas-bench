# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

The ultrasound images display the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point). The sequence reveals a significant anechoic (black) space, indicative of a pleural effusion. Adjacent to this fluid collection, the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of the liver (hepatization), which signifies alveolar consolidation. The border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding structures is somewhat irregular. The presence of both the fluid collection and the tissue-like consolidated lung is characteristic of the PLAPS pattern.
