# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

Based on the visual analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Observation:**
The images show a sector scan of the posterior lung zone. The most prominent feature is a large, anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a fluid collection. Within this fluid, there is a distinct, linear echogenic structure that appears to be floating or undulating slightly between frames. This represents the visceral pleura of the lung (visceral pleura line) suspended within the pleural fluid, which corresponds to the **Jellyfish sign**. The overall configuration of a fluid-filled space with the lung edge visible is also consistent with the **Quad sign** for pleural effusion. There are no obvious signs of extensive parenchymal hepatization (solid liver-like texture of the lung) or air bronchograms that would suggest a primary consolidation pattern distinct from the compressive atelectasis associated with the effusion.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"**
