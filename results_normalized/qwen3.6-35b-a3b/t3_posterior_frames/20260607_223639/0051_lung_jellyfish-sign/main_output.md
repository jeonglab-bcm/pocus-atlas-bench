# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

Based on the visual evidence in the sequential frames:

1.  **Effusion Signs:** The images prominently display a large anechoic (black) space, which corresponds to the **Quad sign** of a pleural effusion. This space is bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally by rib shadows. Within this fluid collection, particularly in the first few frames, there is a linear, hyperechoic structure that appears to be undulating or floating, consistent with the **Jellyfish sign** (atelectatic lung floating in the effusion).

2.  **Consolidation Signs:** As the probe angle shifts in the later frames (particularly frames 4-8), a large area of echogenic, tissue-like material is visible adjacent to the fluid. This represents **tissue-like hepatization** of the lung parenchyma. In the final frames (7 and 8), distinct punctate and linear hyperechoic foci are visible within this consolidated tissue, which are characteristic of **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusion:**
The image sequence demonstrates clear signs of both pleural effusion (Quad sign, Jellyfish sign) and lung consolidation (hepatization, air bronchograms) in the posterior dependent lung zone.
