# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

Based on the visual evidence in the ultrasound frames:

1.  **Effusion Signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) space occupying a significant portion of the image, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. This fluid collection creates a space where structures can be seen floating.
2.  **Consolidation/Atelectasis Signs:** Within the anechoic fluid, there is a distinct, echogenic (grey/white) structure that appears solid and tissue-like. This resembles the texture of the liver (hepatization), indicating that the lung parenchyma has become consolidated or atelectatic (collapsed). This floating, solid lung tissue within the fluid is a classic presentation of the **"Jellyfish sign"**.
3.  **Interface:** The border between the fluid and the solid tissue appears somewhat irregular, consistent with the **"Shred sign"** or the irregular interface between effusion and consolidated lung.

The presence of both a significant fluid collection (effusion) and solid, hepatized-appearing lung tissue (consolidation/atelectasis) floating within it confirms the pathology.
