# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The ultrasound images display a large anechoic (black) area, which indicates a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is an echogenic, undulating structure that appears to be floating. This is characteristic of the "Jellyfish sign," where atelectatic or consolidated lung tissue floats in the pleural fluid. The floating lung tissue appears solid and echogenic (tissue-like hepatization), indicating alveolar consolidation or compressive atelectasis. The combination of a large fluid collection and floating, solid-appearing lung tissue confirms the presence of both effusion and consolidation in the posterior dependent zone.
