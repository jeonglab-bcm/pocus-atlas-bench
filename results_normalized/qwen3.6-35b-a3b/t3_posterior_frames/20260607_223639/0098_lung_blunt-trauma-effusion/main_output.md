# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Observation:**
The ultrasound images display a posterior dependent lung zone (PLAPS). There is a significant anechoic (black) fluid collection visible in the upper/middle portion of the scan, consistent with a pleural effusion. Deep to this fluid, the lung parenchyma appears solid, echogenic, and homogeneous, resembling liver tissue (tissue-like hepatization). Within this consolidated area, there are distinct, punctate, and linear hyperechoic foci, which represent air bronchograms. The interface between the fluid and the lung suggests the lung tissue is atelectatic and potentially floating (suggestive of the jellyfish sign). The combination of the fluid collection and the consolidated lung tissue is clearly visible across the frames, particularly in frames 6 and 7.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
