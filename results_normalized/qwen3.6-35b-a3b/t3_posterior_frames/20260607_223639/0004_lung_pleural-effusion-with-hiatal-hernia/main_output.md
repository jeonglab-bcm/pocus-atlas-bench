# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

Based on the visual analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Observations:**
The images display a large area of increased echogenicity within the posterior lung zone. The texture of this area is homogeneous and solid, resembling liver parenchyma, which is characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**. Within this consolidated region, there are multiple punctate and linear hyperechoic foci visible, consistent with **air bronchograms** (static). The deep border of this consolidation appears somewhat irregular, potentially representing the **shred sign**. There is no clear evidence of a significant anechoic fluid collection forming a "quad sign" or a "jellyfish sign" (floating atelectatic lung) in the adjacent pleural space, although the view is focused primarily on the parenchymal changes.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of hepatization and air bronchograms confirms alveolar consolidation.

**Classification:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "consolidation"**
