# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

Based on the visual evidence in the provided ultrasound frames:

**Observation:**
The images display a large, anechoic (black) region, which is characteristic of a fluid collection, consistent with a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid-filled space, particularly visible in the earlier frames, there are echogenic (bright) structures that appear to be floating. This corresponds to the **Jellyfish sign**, representing atelectatic lung tissue suspended in the pleural fluid. Superior to the fluid, there is a region of echogenic, homogeneous tissue that resembles liver parenchyma, indicative of **tissue-like hepatization** (lung consolidation). The presence of rib shadows on the lateral aspect helps define the boundaries.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both a significant fluid collection (effusion) and solid, hepatized lung tissue (consolidation) in the posterior dependent zone confirms the PLAPS pattern.
