# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

The images reveal a significant pathological pattern in the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point).

**Observations:**
*   **Consolidation:** The majority of the visualized lung field displays "tissue-like hepatization," where the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling a sponge rather than aerated lung. This "sponge" appearance is due to the presence of numerous small, punctate, anechoic (black) spaces within the tissue, which represent **air bronchograms** (air-filled bronchi within consolidated lung).
*   **Effusion:** Surrounding this consolidated tissue is a large anechoic (black) space, indicative of a significant pleural effusion.
*   **Composite Signs:** The image exhibits the **Jellyfish sign**, where the consolidated, atelectatic lung appears to be floating within the surrounding pleural fluid. Additionally, there is a **Shred sign**, characterized by the irregular, jagged interface between the deep border of the consolidated lung and the adjacent fluid/aerated lung.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of extensive hepatization with air bronchograms alongside a surrounding fluid collection confirms pathology in the dependent posterior zone.
