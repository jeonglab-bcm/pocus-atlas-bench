# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

The ultrasound images display a posterior lung zone (PLAPS point).

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion:** There is a large anechoic (black) space visible, particularly in the upper and left portions of the image. This fluid collection is bounded superiorly by the chest wall, inferiorly by the lung tissue, and laterally by rib shadows, forming the characteristic **"quad sign"** of a pleural effusion.
*   **Consolidation:** Beneath the effusion, the lung parenchyma appears solid, heterogeneous, and echogenic, resembling the texture of the liver (**tissue-like hepatization**).
*   **Air Bronchograms:** Within this consolidated, hepatized lung tissue, there are punctate and linear hyperechoic (bright) foci. These represent air-filled bronchi surrounded by fluid-filled alveoli (**air bronchograms**).

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both a significant pleural effusion (quad sign) and lung consolidation (hepatization with air bronchograms) in this posterior zone indicates a PLAPS pattern involving both pathologies.
