# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

The ultrasound sequence displays a large area of lung pathology in the dependent posterior zone.

**Observations:**
- **Consolidation:** The majority of the images (especially frames 2-8) show a large, homogeneous, echogenic region that lacks the normal A-lines and sliding of aerated lung. This "tissue-like hepatization" resembles the texture of the liver, indicating significant lung consolidation (pneumonia or atelectasis). Within this consolidated tissue, there are punctate hyperechoic foci, suggestive of **air bronchograms**.
- **Effusion:** In the first frame, and partially visible in subsequent frames as a dark anechoic space surrounding the solid tissue, there is a significant fluid collection. This is a pleural effusion. The presence of the echogenic consolidated lung floating or adjacent within this anechoic space is consistent with the **Jellyfish sign** (atelectatic lung floating in effusion) or simply a large effusion associated with consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both significant pleural fluid (anechoic space) and lung parenchyma that has become solid and liver-like (hepatization) confirms the PLAPS pattern involving both types of pathology.
