# 0029_lung_parapneumonic-effusion

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Observations:**
*   **Consolidation:** The images prominently display a large area of lung parenchyma that appears echogenic and solid, resembling liver tissue (tissue-like hepatization). This indicates a loss of aeration and alveolar consolidation.
*   **Effusion:** Adjacent to the consolidated lung tissue, there is a distinct anechoic (black) space, which represents a pleural effusion. In the later frames (7-8), a more localized fluid pocket is clearly visible adjacent to the consolidated tissue.
*   **Interface:** There is a clear, somewhat irregular interface between the solid, consolidated lung and the fluid collection.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both significant pleural fluid (anechoic space) and tissue-like lung consolidation (hepatization) in the posterior dependent zone confirms a PLAPS pattern.
