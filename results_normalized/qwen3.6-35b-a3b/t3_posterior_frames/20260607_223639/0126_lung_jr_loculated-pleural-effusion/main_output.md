**Observation:**
The ultrasound images display a large anechoic (black) space in the upper portion of the scan, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a distinct, echogenic (brighter) structure that appears to be floating and undulating. This specific appearance is known as the **Jellyfish sign**, representing atelectatic or consolidated lung tissue floating within the pleural fluid. The presence of this tissue indicates consolidation (or severe atelectasis) alongside the effusion.
