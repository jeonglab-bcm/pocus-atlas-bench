# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

The ultrasound images demonstrate a large anechoic (black) space, which is indicative of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection or immediately deep to it, there is a region of lung tissue that appears echogenic and solid, resembling the texture of the liver (hepatization). This is a sign of alveolar consolidation. Additionally, there are hyperechoic foci within this consolidated tissue, suggestive of air bronchograms. The combination of a substantial fluid collection and consolidated, non-aerated lung tissue in the dependent posterior zone is characteristic of the PLAPS pattern.
