# 0007_lung_pleural-effusion-with-compressed-lung-and-spine-sign

Based on the visual evidence from the ultrasound frames:

**Observation:**
The images demonstrate findings in the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point). There is a large anechoic (black) area consistent with a **pleural effusion**. Adjacent to this fluid collection, the lung parenchyma appears echogenic and solid, lacking the normal aeration artifacts (like A-lines), which is characteristic of **alveolar consolidation** (often described as "hepatization" because it resembles liver tissue). Within this consolidated tissue, faint hyperechoic foci are visible, suggestive of **air bronchograms**. The interface between the fluid and the consolidated lung is visible.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
