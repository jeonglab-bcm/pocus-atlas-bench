# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

The images display a significant pathological pattern in the posterior lung zone.

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion:** There is a large, predominantly anechoic (black) area visible, particularly in the upper and right portions of the sector, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion.
*   **Consolidation:** Adjacent to and within the fluid collection, there is a large area of echogenic, tissue-like material. This lung parenchyma has lost its normal aerated appearance and resembles the texture of the liver (hepatization), indicating alveolar consolidation.
*   **Jellyfish Sign:** In the later frames (especially images 4-8), the consolidated lung tissue appears to be floating within the surrounding fluid collection. This undulating, floating appearance of the atelectatic lung is known as the "jellyfish sign" or "plankton sign."
*   **Air Bronchograms:** There are punctate and linear hyperechoic (bright) structures visible within the consolidated tissue, consistent with air bronchograms.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both a significant pleural effusion and underlying lung consolidation (hepatization) with floating atelectatic lung confirms the diagnosis.
