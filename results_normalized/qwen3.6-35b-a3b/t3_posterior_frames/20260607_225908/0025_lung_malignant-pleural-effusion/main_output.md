# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images display a classic appearance of severe pathology in the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point).

**Observations:**
*   **Consolidation:** The lung parenchyma has lost its normal aerated appearance and appears "tissue-like" or solid (hepatization), resembling the texture of the liver.
*   **Air Bronchograms:** Within this consolidated, echogenic tissue, there are numerous distinct, irregular, hyperechoic (bright white) linear and punctate structures. These are air bronchograms, representing air-filled bronchi surrounded by fluid-filled or consolidated alveoli.
*   **Effusion:** There are large, irregular anechoic (black) areas surrounding and interdigitating with the consolidated lung tissue. This indicates the presence of a pleural effusion.
*   **Dynamic signs:** The appearance of the consolidated lung floating within the anechoic fluid is suggestive of the "Jellyfish sign" (atelectatic lung floating in fluid). The interface between the consolidated lung and the aerated lung (if visible at the periphery) or the effusion appears irregular, suggestive of the "Shred sign."

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both significant pleural fluid (anechoic spaces) and lung tissue consolidation (hepatization with air bronchograms) indicates a complex pleuropulmonary process, likely pneumonia with a parapneumonic effusion.
