# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

The ultrasound sequence reveals significant pathology in the posterior lung zone. There is a large area of tissue-like, hyperechoic parenchyma, which represents lung consolidation (hepatization), where the lung has lost its air content and resembles the texture of the liver. Adjacent to this consolidated area, there are anechoic (black) regions consistent with a pleural effusion. The interface between the consolidated lung and the fluid/aerated lung appears somewhat irregular, suggestive of the "shred sign." The presence of both significant fluid collection and consolidated lung tissue in the dependent posterior zone is characteristic of a PLAPS pattern.
