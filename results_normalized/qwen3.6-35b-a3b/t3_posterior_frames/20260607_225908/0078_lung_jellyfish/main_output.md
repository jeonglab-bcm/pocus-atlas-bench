*   **Consolidation:** Deep to the fluid, there is a large area of echogenic, tissue-like parenchyma that resembles the liver (hepatization), indicating alveolar consolidation. The normal air-filled lung pattern is absent.
*   **Air Bronchograms:** Within the consolidated (hepatized) lung tissue, there are distinct, hyperechoic (bright white) linear and punctate structures. These are air bronchograms, which are pathognomonic for lung consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
The image shows signs of both pleural effusion (the anechoic fluid collection) and alveolar consolidation (the hepatized lung with air bronchograms) in the dependent posterior zone.
