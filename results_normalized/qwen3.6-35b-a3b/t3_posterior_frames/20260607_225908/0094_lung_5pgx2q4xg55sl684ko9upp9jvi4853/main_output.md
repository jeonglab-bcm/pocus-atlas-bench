*   **Air Bronchograms:** Within the consolidated lung tissue, distinct hyperechoic linear and punctate structures are visible (particularly evident in the second and third frames). These represent static air bronchograms, confirming the solid nature of the lung tissue.

**Conclusion:**
The images demonstrate both lung consolidation (hepatization with air bronchograms) and a pleural effusion in the dependent posterior zone.
