Based on the sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point):

**Observation:**
The images display a region of lung parenchyma that has lost its normal aerated appearance. Instead of the typical horizontal A-lines or vertical B-lines, there is a large area of tissue-like, echogenic material that resembles the texture of the liver (hepatization). This indicates alveolar consolidation. Within this consolidated area, there are punctate hyperechoic foci, which are consistent with air bronchograms (air-filled bronchi surrounded by fluid-filled/alveoli-filled alveoli). There is no clear evidence of a large anechoic (black) fluid collection surrounding the lung (quad sign) or a fragment of lung floating freely in fluid (jellyfish sign). The primary pathology is the solidification of the lung tissue.

**Conclusion:**
**plaps = true**
**type = "consolidation"**
