# 0078_lung_jellyfish

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Observations:**
The images show a view of the "RT POSTERIOR" lung zone.
1.  **Pleural Effusion:** There is a large, anechoic (black) space visible, particularly at the top and right side of the sector. This fluid collection creates boundaries consistent with the **Quad sign** (though the rib boundaries are not perfectly distinct, the fluid pocket is significant). The presence of this fluid is a primary indicator of effusion.
2.  **Lung Consolidation:** Below and adjacent to the fluid, there is a large area of tissue-like, echogenic parenchyma. This texture resembles the liver, known as **tissue-like hepatization**, indicating that the lung alveoli are filled with fluid/pus rather than air.
3.  **Air Bronchograms:** Within this consolidated (hepatized) area, there are distinct hyperechoic (bright) linear and punctate foci. These represent air-filled bronchi surrounded by consolidated tissue.
4.  **Shred Sign:** The interface between the consolidated lung tissue and the surrounding aerated lung or fluid appears irregular and jagged, characteristic of the **shred sign**.
5.  **Jellyfish Sign:** In the initial frames, a mobile-appearing structure (the atelectatic/consolidated lung) is seen within or floating near the fluid collection, resembling a jellyfish.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both a significant anechoic fluid collection (effusion) and tissue-like, echogenic lung parenchyma with air bronchograms (consolidation) confirms a positive finding for the PLAPS point.
