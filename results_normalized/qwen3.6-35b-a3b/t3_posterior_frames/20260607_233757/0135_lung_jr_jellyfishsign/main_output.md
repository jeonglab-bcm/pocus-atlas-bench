# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

Based on the visual evidence in the ultrasound frames:

**Observation:**
The images display a posterior lung view characterized by two distinct findings. First, there is a large, deep area of hyperechoic (bright), solid-appearing tissue that resembles the texture of the liver. This represents **tissue-like hepatization**, indicative of alveolar consolidation. Second, superior to this consolidated lung tissue, there is a distinct anechoic (black) space. This dark area represents a **pleural effusion**. The combination of a fluid collection overlying consolidated lung in the posterior dependent zone is a classic presentation of the PLAPS point.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
