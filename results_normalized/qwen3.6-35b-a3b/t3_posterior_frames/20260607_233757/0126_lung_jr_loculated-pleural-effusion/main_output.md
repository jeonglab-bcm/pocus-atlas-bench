# 0126_lung_jr_loculated-pleural-effusion

The images display a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there are echogenic structures that appear to be floating and undulating. This is the **"Jellyfish sign,"** where atelectatic or consolidated lung tissue is suspended in the pleural fluid. The lung tissue itself appears solid and echogenic (resembling liver texture), which indicates **tissue-like hepatization** or **consolidation**. The combination of a large fluid collection and the floating, solid-appearing lung tissue confirms the presence of both pathology types.
