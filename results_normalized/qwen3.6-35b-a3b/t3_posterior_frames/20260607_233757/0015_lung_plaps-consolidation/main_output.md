# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

The ultrasound sequence displays the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point).

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion:** The first frame clearly shows a large, anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a significant pleural effusion. This fluid collection is consistent with the "Quad sign" seen in dependent lung zones.
*   **Consolidation:** In the subsequent frames (especially 2-4), the lung parenchyma adjacent to the spine appears solid and echogenic, losing its normal airy texture. This "tissue-like hepatization" indicates alveolar consolidation.
*   **Air Bronchograms:** Within the consolidated (hepatized) lung tissue, there are visible hyperechoic (bright) linear and punctate foci. These are air bronchograms, representing air-filled bronchi surrounded by fluid-filled or consolidated alveoli.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both the anechoic fluid collection (effusion) and the solid, hepatized lung tissue with air bronchograms (consolidation) confirms the diagnosis.
