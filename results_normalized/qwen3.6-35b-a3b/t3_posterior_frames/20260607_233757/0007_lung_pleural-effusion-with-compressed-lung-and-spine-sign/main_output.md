# 0007_lung_pleural-effusion-with-compressed-lung-and-spine-sign

The images display a large anechoic (black) space, particularly evident on the left side of the sector in multiple frames (e.g., images 1, 4, 6). This represents a significant **pleural effusion**. Adjacent to this fluid collection is a region of lung parenchyma that appears solid, homogeneous, and echogenic, resembling the texture of the liver. Within this consolidated area, there are punctate and linear hyperechoic structures, which are consistent with **air bronchograms** (air-filled bronchi surrounded by fluid-filled alveoli). The combination of a large fluid collection compressing the lung and the lung tissue itself appearing solid indicates both pathology types are present.
