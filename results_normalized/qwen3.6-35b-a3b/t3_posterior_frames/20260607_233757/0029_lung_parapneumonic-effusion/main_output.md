# 0029_lung_parapneumonic-effusion

**Observation:**
The sequential frames display a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a mobile, echogenic (solid-appearing) structure that shifts position between frames (e.g., compare frame 4 and frame 8), exhibiting the **Jellyfish sign**. This represents atelectatic lung tissue floating within the pleural fluid. The solid structure itself has a homogenous, echogenic texture resembling liver parenchyma, which corresponds to **tissue-like hepatization**, a sign of alveolar consolidation. The presence of rib shadows on the left side and the interface between the fluid and the consolidated lung creates a configuration consistent with the **Quad sign**.
