# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Observation:**
The images reveal a significant pathological pattern in the posterior lung zone.
1.  **Effusion:** There is a large, anechoic (black) space occupying the superior and middle portions of the scan. This represents a pleural effusion.
2.  **Consolidation:** Deep to the fluid collection, there is a large area of echogenic, tissue-like parenchyma that resembles the liver (hepatization), indicating lung consolidation. This area has lost the normal horizontal A-line artifacts.
3.  **Air Bronchograms:** Within the consolidated, echogenic tissue, there are punctate and linear hyperechoic foci. These represent air bronchograms, confirming that the solid area is lung tissue rather than a solid mass or abscess.
4.  **Jellyfish Sign:** The appearance of the atelectatic/consolidated lung floating within the pleural fluid collection is suggestive of the "jellyfish sign."

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both significant pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation (with air bronchograms) in the posterior zone confirms a PLAPS pattern.
