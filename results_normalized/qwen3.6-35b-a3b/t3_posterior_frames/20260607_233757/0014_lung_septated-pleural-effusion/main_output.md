# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

Based on the visual analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Observation:**
The images display a large anechoic (black) space, which indicates the presence of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a distinct, linear, and hyperechoic (white) structure that appears to be floating and undulating. This structure represents the lung parenchyma, which is atelectatic (collapsed) and non-aerated, suspended in the pleural fluid. This specific appearance of lung tissue floating in fluid is known as the **Jellyfish sign**. The movement of this "tongue" of lung tissue within the fluid across the frames also suggests the **Sinusoid sign**, indicating dynamic movement with respiration. There is no clear evidence of a "shred sign" at an interface with aerated lung, nor is there a large area of static hepatization separate from the fluid (though the floating lung is consolidated/atelectatic).

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**: The presence of the Jellyfish sign and the large anechoic space confirms pathology in the PLAPS point.
*   **type = "effusion"**: The primary finding is the large fluid collection containing the floating lung (Jellyfish sign), which is explicitly categorized under effusion signs in the provided criteria. The fluid is the predominant feature.
