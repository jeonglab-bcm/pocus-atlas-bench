# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

The ultrasound images demonstrate a large anechoic (black) space in the posterior lateral zone, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. This fluid collection creates a distinct quadrilateral shape bounded by the pleural line superiorly, the diaphragm/lung base inferiorly, and the rib shadows laterally, known as the **Quad sign**. Additionally, within this fluid collection, there appears to be an echogenic, undulating structure that resembles a **Jellyfish sign**, representing atelectatic lung tissue floating within the effusion. While there is echogenic tissue visible, the dominant feature is the fluid collection, and the signs present (Quad sign, Jellyfish sign) are classified under effusion.
