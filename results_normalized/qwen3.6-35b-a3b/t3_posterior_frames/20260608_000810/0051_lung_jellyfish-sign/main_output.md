# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

Based on the provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point):

**Observations:**
The images display a large, anechoic (black) space within the pleural cavity. This space is bounded by the chest wall (ribs) laterally and the lung surface inferiorly, creating a distinct quadrilateral shape known as the **Quad sign**. Within this anechoic fluid collection, there is a hyperechoic, linear structure that appears to be floating and undulating. This structure represents the atelectatic lung collapsing into the fluid, resembling a jellyfish in water, which corresponds to the **Jellyfish sign**. The presence of significant free fluid in the dependent posterior zone is the dominant feature.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "effusion"**
