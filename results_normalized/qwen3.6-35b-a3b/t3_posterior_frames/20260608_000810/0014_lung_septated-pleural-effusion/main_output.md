# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Observation:**
The images reveal a large anechoic (black) area in the posterior zone, which is indicative of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, a thin, hyperechoic line is visible floating freely. This line represents the visceral pleura of an atelectatic (collapsed) lung. The appearance of the lung tissue undulating and floating within the fluid collection is characteristic of the **"Jellyfish sign"**. While there may be underlying atelectasis (consolidation) of the floating lung tissue, the predominant visual feature defining the pathology is the large fluid collection containing the mobile lung line. There is no extensive static "tissue-like hepatization" filling the primary field of view separate from the effusion.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"**
