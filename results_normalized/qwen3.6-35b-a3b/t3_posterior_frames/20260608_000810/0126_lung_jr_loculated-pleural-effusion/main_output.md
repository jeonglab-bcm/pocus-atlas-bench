# 0126_lung_jr_loculated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images display a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a distinct echogenic structure that appears to be consolidated lung tissue floating within the fluid. This appearance is known as the "Jellyfish sign," where the atelectatic or consolidated lung floats like a jellyfish in the surrounding effusion. The floating tissue shows a solid, tissue-like texture consistent with hepatization (consolidation), rather than aerated lung. There are no clear A-lines visible in the dependent zone, and the normal lung sliding is obscured by the fluid and consolidation.
