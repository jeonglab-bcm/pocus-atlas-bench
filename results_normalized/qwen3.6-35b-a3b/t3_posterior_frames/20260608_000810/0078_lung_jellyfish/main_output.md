# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The ultrasound images display a significant pathology in the right posterior lung zone.

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) space at the top of the image, indicating the presence of fluid. This fluid collection creates a separation between the chest wall and the underlying lung tissue.
*   **Consolidation:** Below the fluid collection, there is a large area of lung parenchyma that appears solid, heterogeneous, and echogenic, resembling the texture of the liver. This is known as "tissue-like hepatization."
*   **Air Bronchograms:** Within the consolidated, solid-appearing lung tissue, there are distinct hyperechoic (bright white) linear and punctate structures. These represent air-filled bronchi within the consolidated lung, known as static air bronchograms.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both a pleural effusion (the anechoic space) and lung consolidation (the hepatized tissue with air bronchograms) in the dependent posterior zone confirms the PLAPS pattern.
