# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

Based on the provided ultrasound frames, the following observations are made:

1.  **Consolidation:** There is a large, triangular area of tissue-like echogenicity (hepatization) in the posterior aspect. The texture resembles liver parenchyma rather than normal aerated lung, indicating alveolar consolidation.
2.  **Effusion & Jellyfish Sign:** In the later frames (particularly frames 4 through 7), a distinct, linear, hyperechoic structure is visible floating within an anechoic (black) space. This structure represents atelectatic lung tissue floating within a pleural effusion. This dynamic appearance is characteristic of the **"Jellyfish sign"**. The presence of the anechoic space surrounding this floating lung confirms a significant pleural effusion.

The combination of hepatized lung tissue and a pleural effusion containing floating atelectatic lung confirms the presence of the PLAPS pattern.

**Classification:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
