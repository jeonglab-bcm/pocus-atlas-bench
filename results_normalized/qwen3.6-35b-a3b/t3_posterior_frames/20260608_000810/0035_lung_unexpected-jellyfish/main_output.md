# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

Based on the visual evidence in the sequential frames:

1.  **Observation:** The ultrasound images reveal a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within and adjacent to this fluid collection, there is a large, echogenic (bright) area with a tissue-like texture, resembling the liver parenchyma. This appearance is known as "tissue-like hepatization," indicating lung consolidation or severe atelectasis. The consolidated lung tissue appears to be floating within or surrounded by the fluid collection, which is suggestive of the "jellyfish sign" (atelectatic lung floating in effusion). The borders of the consolidation are somewhat irregular, potentially indicating the "shred sign" at the interface with aerated lung (though aerated lung is not clearly dominant here). The combination of significant fluid and consolidated lung tissue in the posterior dependent zone is the hallmark of the PLAPS pattern.

2.  **Conclusion:**
    *   **plaps = true**
    *   **type = "both"**
