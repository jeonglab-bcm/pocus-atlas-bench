# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

**Frame by Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frame 1:** The pleural line is visible as a bright, textured interface rather than a sharp, static line. The sub-pleural region exhibits a granular, "sandy" pattern.
*   **Frame 2:** The pleural line remains dynamic. There is observable variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern compared to the previous frame, indicating movement.
*   **Frame 3:** The shimmering characteristic of the pleural line persists. The texture below the pleural line continues to change with each frame.
*   **Frame 4:** The line maintains its shimmering appearance, confirming the dynamic nature of the interface.
*   **Frame 5:** The entire visible pleural line is dynamic, exhibiting consistent movement and texture across the captured area.

**Conclusion:**

The pleural sliding is **Present**.

**Justification:**
The primary evidence for sliding is the **shimmering or granular pleural line** observed across all frames. Instead of a smooth, sharp, static line (which would indicate the absence of sliding), the interface is bright and textured, and the sub-pleural speckle patterns demonstrate variation between consecutive frames, characteristic of the "Seashore sign."
