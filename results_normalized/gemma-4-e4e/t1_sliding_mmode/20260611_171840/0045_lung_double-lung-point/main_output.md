# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis

### Frame-by-Frame Observation

Across all five sequential frames (Frame 1 through Frame 5), the ultrasound image displays a distinct, bright, horizontal line representing the pleural interface. Critically, the region immediately below this pleural line (the sub-pleural space) does not appear smooth or uniformly black (anechoic). Instead, it exhibits a highly dynamic, heterogeneous pattern of speckles and granular texture. This sub-pleural texture remains consistent and active across all five frames, indicating motion and tissue interaction at the lung-chest wall interface.

### Analysis of Pleural Sliding

**Observation based on signs:**
*   **Seashore Sign:** The presence of a granular, "sand-like" pattern immediately beneath the pleura is strongly evident in all frames. This pattern is characteristic of the sound waves reflecting off the moving lung tissue.
*   **Dynamic Pleura:** The interface is not static or sharply defined in a rigid manner; it is surrounded by active, textured sound.

**Conclusion:**
Based on the consistent presence of a highly dynamic, granular sub-pleural pattern (the Seashore Sign), **Pleural Sliding is Present.**
