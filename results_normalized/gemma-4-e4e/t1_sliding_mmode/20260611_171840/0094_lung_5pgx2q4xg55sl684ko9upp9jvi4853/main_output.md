*   **Assessment:** These features are classic indicators of a static, non-moving pleural interface.

### Conclusion

**Overall Assessment:**
In all three frames, the ultrasound displays a smooth, static pleural line and the characteristic horizontal "barcode" or "stratosphere" pattern of perfectly static A-lines. There is no evidence of shimmering, B-lines, or granular speckle variation between frames.

**Final Conclusion:**
Based on the signs observed, **Pleural Sliding is Absent** in these frames.
