# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

**Observation of Ultrasound Frames:**

Across all sequential frames (Frame 1 through Frame 4), the interface between the lung and the chest wall (the pleural line) is not a smooth, sharply defined, static line. Instead, it exhibits a **textured, granular, and somewhat shimmering appearance**.

Below this pleural line, the speckle pattern is **dynamic and non-static**. The pattern does not remain perfectly fixed or uniform across the frames, indicating that the sub-pleural interface is moving. This dynamic, granular pattern is consistent with the "Seashore Sign" often seen in sliding. There are no signs of a smooth, static barcode pattern, which would indicate absent sliding.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the granular/shimmering appearance of the pleural line and the dynamic, non-static speckle pattern in the sub-pleural region, **pleural sliding is present**.
