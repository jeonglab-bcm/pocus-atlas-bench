## Assessment of Pleural Sliding

Since the images are static, the gold-standard definitive proof of sliding (movement) cannot be provided. However, we must evaluate the *pattern* displayed:

*   **Sign Present (Seashore Sign):** The presence of the granular, "sandy" speckle pattern below the pleural line is the classic sonographic representation of the **Seashore sign**.
*   **Sign Absent (Barcode Sign):** The image does *not* show the pure, static, perfectly horizontal lines (the Barcode or Stratosphere sign), which would indicate no movement or lung collapse/pneumothorax.

**Interpretation:**
In Lung Ultrasound, the **Seashore sign** is the indicator that the lung tissue is normally aerated, and therefore, the mechanical function of the pleura (sliding) is expected and occurring normally.

## Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is present (normal).**

The sonographic pattern observed (Seashore sign) strongly indicates normal aeration and active pleural sliding, even though the movement cannot be independently verified due to the frames being static duplicates.
