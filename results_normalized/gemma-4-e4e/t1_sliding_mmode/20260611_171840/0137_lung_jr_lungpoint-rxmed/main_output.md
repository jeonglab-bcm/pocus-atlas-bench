This analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound clip indicates a distinct pattern.

---

### 1. Frame-by-Frame Observation

The frames show a consistent view of a pleural line (the bright, horizontal white line at the top of the image).

**Observation across all frames:**
*   The **pleural line** itself remains perfectly static, smooth, and sharply defined. There is no visible granular texture or shimmering motion.
*   The **sub-pleural area** (the tissue below the pleural line) appears as a fixed, medium-gray speckled texture.
*   Critically, **no discernible change or variation** in the sub-pleural speckle is observed when comparing Frame 1 to Frame 7. The tissue does not exhibit the "sandy granular" pattern characteristic of movement.

### 2. Assessment Against Specific LUS Signs

*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** None of these signs are observed. There is no shimmering, no B-line artifacts originating from the line, no seashore pattern, and no change in the sub-pleural speckle between frames.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** Several of these signs are present. The pleural line is smooth, sharply defined, and completely static. The sub-pleural speckle pattern is fixed and shows no variation.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** No transition or alternating patterns are present in the field of view.

### 3. Conclusion

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames, the evidence strongly indicates that **pleural sliding is ABSENT.**

The presence of a smooth, static pleural line combined with the complete lack of dynamic changes or variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern (which would normally generate a "seashore sign") are the definitive signs of **absent sliding**.
