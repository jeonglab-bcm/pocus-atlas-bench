# 0041_lung_2018-1-16-normal-lung-slide

**Analysis of Ultrasound Frames:**

*   **Frame 1:** The pleura is visualized as a sharp, hyperechoic line. The area immediately below the pleura (sub-pleural space) exhibits a consistent, structured speckle pattern.
*   **Frame 2 - Frame 5:** As the frames are compared, the pleural line remains perfectly stable and does not appear to shimmer or change position. The sub-pleural pattern does not show any variation in speckle or change to a granular pattern.
*   **Observations:** There is no evidence of "shimmer" (dynamic movement), no comet-tail artifacts, and no observable transition between static and dynamic features. The pleural line and the sub-pleural pattern are static and unchanging across all sequential frames.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the presence of a **smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line** and the complete **lack of variation in sub-pleural speckle** between consecutive frames, the signs point to the **absence of pleural sliding.**
