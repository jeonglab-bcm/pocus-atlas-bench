**Analysis of Frames:**

*   **Frame 1-4 Observation:** In all four frames, a bright, sharply defined, horizontal line (the pleural line) is visible at the top of the image. The line itself remains perfectly static and does not show any signs of movement, shimmering, or granular texture (shimmer/comet tail). The sub-pleural area (below the line) shows a consistent, static speckle pattern (resembling a series of uniform A-lines) that does not change between frames.

**Assessment:**

*   **Sliding PRESENT:** None of the signs (shimmer, comet tails, pattern change, seashore sign) are observed.
*   **Sliding ABSENT:** The primary signs of absent sliding are present: the pleural line is smooth, sharply defined, and static throughout the sequence. The sub-pleural speckle pattern remains perfectly unchanged.
*   **Signs of BOTH:** No transition point or alternating pattern is visible.

**Conclusion:**

Pleural sliding is **absent**. The sonographic features (static pleural line, lack of speckle variation) are characteristic of the "barcode" or "static A-line" pattern.
