# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

This analysis examines the provided sequential frames from the lung ultrasound.

***

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Observation:**
All five sequential frames presented are highly consistent. There is no discernible change in the sub-pleural region or the pleural line itself between frames.

*   **Pleural Line:** In every frame, the pleural line at the top of the image appears **smooth and sharply defined**. It does not exhibit any granular texture or "shimmer" that would indicate micro-movements.
*   **Sub-Pleural Pattern:** Below the pleural line, the pattern consists of vertical, highly reflective lines (consistent with B-lines). These lines are **perfectly static**. The texture and spacing of these B-lines do not change or shift across the frames.
*   **Movement/Variation:** There is **no variation in sub-pleural speckle or dynamic movement** of the pleural line or the underlying tissue between Frame 1 and Frame 5.

***

### Assessment of Pleural Sliding

**Signs of Sliding PRESENT:**
*   None of the criteria are met. There is no shimmering, no comet-tail artifacts, and no indication of the dynamic "seashore" pattern.

**Signs of Sliding ABSENT:**
*   **Met:** The pleural line is smooth, sharply defined, and static.
*   **Met:** The sub-pleural pattern is highly consistent and does not change between frames (no variation in sub-pleural speckle).
*   This static pattern is consistent with the appearance of a "Stratosphere" or "barcode" sign, indicating a lack of movement.

**Signs of BOTH (lung point):**
*   Not present. There is no transition point or alternating pattern observed.

### Conclusion

Based on the lack of movement of the pleural line and the absence of any change in the sub-pleural speckle across all sequential frames, **pleural sliding is absent**. The visual evidence suggests a static lung surface.
