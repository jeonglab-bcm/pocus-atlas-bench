# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

This analysis examines the five sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip to assess the presence or absence of pleural sliding.

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frame 1:**
*   **Observation:** The pleural line is visible as a bright, continuous interface at the top of the image. The line itself appears smooth and static. The tissue immediately below the line (the lung parenchyma) shows a uniform speckled pattern. There is no visible shimmering, granularity, or dynamic change in the sub-pleural area.
*   **Assessment:** Suggestive of static/absent sliding.

**Frame 2:**
*   **Observation:** The pleural line remains sharply defined and continuous. Compared to Frame 1, there is no observable change in the line's texture or position. The sub-pleural speckle pattern appears consistent and unchanging across the frame.
*   **Assessment:** Suggestive of static/absent sliding.

**Frame 3:**
*   **Observation:** The line remains smooth and fixed. No signs of movement, B-lines, or the granular "sandy" texture associated with the Seashore sign are visible.
*   **Assessment:** Suggestive of static/absent sliding.

**Frame 4:**
*   **Observation:** The interface is continuous and appears fixed. The ultrasound image shows a stable, uninterrupted pattern below the pleural line, consistent with a stationary interface rather than a dynamic one.
*   **Assessment:** Suggestive of static/absent sliding.

**Frame 5:**
*   **Observation:** The pleural line is present, smooth, and static. No dynamic movement or variation in the sub-pleural speckle is observed.
*   **Assessment:** Suggestive of static/absent sliding.

***

### Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.**

Across all five frames, the pleural line remains smooth, continuous, and static. There is a total lack of shimmering, granular texture, or variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern (i.e., the Seashore sign is not present). This indicates that the interface between the pleura and the lung is stationary.
