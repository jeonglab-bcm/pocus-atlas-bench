# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

**Frame by Frame Analysis:**

**Image 1 (M-mode):** The initial M-mode display shows a textured, somewhat granular interface at the pleural line, suggesting a dynamic state rather than a perfectly static interface.

**Frame 1 (Bottom-Left):**
*   **Pleural Line:** A distinct, bright, and sharp pleural line is visible.
*   **Sub-Pleural Area:** The area below the pleural line is heterogeneous. It is not composed solely of uniform, static horizontal lines (A-lines). Instead, there is a mixture of horizontal speckles and vertical/angled echoes, creating a complex, granular appearance. This morphology is consistent with a "seashore sign" pattern.

**Frame 2 (Bottom-Center):**
*   The image appears identical to Frame 1. The pleural line remains fixed, and the complex speckle pattern in the sub-pleural space is maintained.

**Frame 3 (Bottom-Right):**
*   The image appears identical to Frame 1. The pleural line remains fixed, and the complex speckle pattern in the sub-pleural space is maintained.

**Assessment of Pleural Sliding:**

*   **Pleural Sliding Present?** Yes. The presence of a complex, heterogeneous pattern (mixture of speckled texture, vertical echoes, and horizontal lines) in the sub-pleural space is indicative of the **Seashore Sign**. The Seashore Sign is the classic ultrasound indicator of normal pleural sliding. The lack of a uniformly static A-line pattern rules out the "Barcode Sign" (which signifies lack of sliding).
*   **Pleural Sliding Absent?** No. The pattern is clearly dynamic/textured, not static.
*   **Both?** No evidence of a transition point is visible in these frames.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the visual evidence, particularly the complex, textured, and dynamic appearance of the sub-pleural space (consistent with a **Seashore Sign**), **pleural sliding is present.**
