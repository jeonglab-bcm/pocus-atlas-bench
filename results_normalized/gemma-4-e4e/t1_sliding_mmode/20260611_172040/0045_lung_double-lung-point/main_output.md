# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

This is an analysis of five sequential frames from a lung ultrasound clip.

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frame 1:**
*   The pleural line is a bright, sharp, horizontal interface at the top of the image.
*   Numerous vertical artifacts, consistent with B-lines, extend deep into the lung field.
*   The pleural line appears static in this single frame.

**Frame 2:**
*   The pleural line remains perfectly horizontal and fixed.
*   The pattern of B-lines below the pleural line is identical to Frame 1.
*   There is no observable change in the location or texture of the pleural line or the sub-pleural artifacts compared to the previous frame.

**Frame 3:**
*   The pleural line remains static.
*   The sub-pleural speckle pattern (B-lines) is unchanged from Frame 1 and Frame 2.

**Frame 4:**
*   The pleural line remains perfectly fixed.
*   No discernible variation or movement is observed in the pleural interface or the tissue beneath it.

**Frame 5:**
*   The pleural line is static.
*   The sub-pleural pattern is consistent and unchanging across the sequence.

## Conclusion

Based on the analysis of the five sequential frames:

**Signs observed:**
*   **Pleural Line:** Smooth, sharp, and completely static.
*   **Sub-pleural Speckle:** No variation or change in the pattern of B-lines or the texture of the sub-pleural tissue between frames.
*   **Sliding Indicators:** No shimmering, comet-tail artifacts, or movement is visible.

**Assessment:**
Pleural sliding is **ABSENT**.

The findings are consistent with the absence of lung movement at the interface, which is typically seen in conditions such as a pneumothorax or when the lung is fixed due to underlying pathology (e.g., severe consolidation).
