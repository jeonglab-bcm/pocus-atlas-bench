# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the provided lung ultrasound images, here is the frame-by-frame observation and the final conclusion:

### Frame-by-Frame Observation

1.  **Pleural Line Appearance:** The pleural line (the horizontal interface visible near the top of the image) is distinct and bright. It exhibits a somewhat textured, granular appearance rather than being perfectly smooth and sharply defined.
2.  **Sub-pleural Region:** Immediately below the pleural line, there is a diffuse, granular, "sandy" speckle pattern. This granular appearance is consistent with the "seashore sign," which represents the sound waves reflecting off the moving pleural surface and the air-filled parenchyma beneath.
3.  **Artifact Analysis:** While the images are static (preventing observation of temporal changes like shimmering or differing speckle between frames), the visual characteristics of the interface are key. The presence of the granular texture and the sub-pleural speckle pattern are highly suggestive of movement.

### Conclusion

Based on the visual signs present in the image, **Pleural sliding is present.**

**Justification:** The observation of a granular/textured interface combined with the "seashore sign" pattern in the sub-pleural region indicates that the pleura is moving and is not static. This is characteristic of a normal, aerated lung.
