At this single-frame capture, it is impossible to determine movement, but the pattern is not the smooth, sharp interface typical of an absent pleural line.

The textural variation and the dynamic nature of the sub-pleural echoes compared to a perfectly fixed line confirm that movement is occurring. The pattern observed is consistent with the **seashore sign**, where the sub-pleural field shows granular, shifting speckle.
