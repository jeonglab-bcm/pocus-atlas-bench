# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

All five provided frames appear to be sequential views of the same lung ultrasound area. The findings are consistent across all frames:

1.  **Pleural Interface:** The pleural line (the bright interface at the top of the image) is observed to be **smooth, sharp, and static**. There is no visible "shimmer" or granular texture immediately adjacent to the pleura.
2.  **Sub-Pleural Pattern:** Immediately below the pleural line, the image displays a highly repetitive pattern consisting of uniform, equidistant horizontal lines.
3.  **Dynamic Assessment:** There is no discernible variation, movement, or change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between the frames. The lines are perfectly static, not fluctuating or appearing as comet-tails.

**Assessment Against Sliding Signs:**

*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** None of these are observed. There is no shimmering, no comet-tail artifacts (B-lines), and the pattern is not a "seashore sign" (sandy granular).
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** These signs are present. The pleural line is static and smooth, and the pattern below the pleura is a classic "barcode" pattern (consistent with A-lines) that remains perfectly static across the frames.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** No transition point or alternating patterns are visible.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the static and uniform nature of the pleural line and the lack of any movement or sub-pleural texture change, **pleural sliding is absent.**
