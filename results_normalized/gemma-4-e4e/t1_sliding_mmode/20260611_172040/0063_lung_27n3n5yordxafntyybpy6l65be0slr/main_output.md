**Observation:**

The image displays a typical lung ultrasound (LUS) scan. The pleura (the bright line at the top of the image) is visible. Below the pleural line, the sub-pleural field does not exhibit the perfectly flat, smooth, parallel horizontal lines (A-lines/stratosphere sign) characteristic of absent sliding or complete collapse. Instead, the sub-pleural region presents a textured, granular, and somewhat speckled pattern. While true dynamic "shimmering" requires observing the frames in motion, the visual appearance of the echoes below the pleura is heterogeneous rather than static.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the granular and textured appearance of the echoes below the pleural line, the signs of pleural sliding are **present**.
