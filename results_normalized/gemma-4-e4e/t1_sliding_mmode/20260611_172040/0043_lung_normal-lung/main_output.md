# 0043_lung_normal-lung

**Analysis of the Frames:**

**Frame-by-Frame Observation:**
In all five frames, the image displays a highly uniform and structured pattern. A bright, horizontal interface (the pleural line) is visible at the top of the lung field. Below this interface, there is a repeating pattern of multiple, parallel, horizontal, hypoechoic lines. The echoes in the sub-pleural field are linear and highly organized, not scattered or granular. The pleural line itself appears sharp and unchanging between consecutive frames.

**Application of Ultrasound Signs:**
1. **Shimmering/Granular Pleural Line:** **ABSENT.** The pleural line remains static and smooth across all frames.
2. **Comet-tail Artifact/B-lines (Dynamic):** The pattern consists of linear echoes, but there is no dynamic motion-related streak or "comet tail" artifact that would indicate friction or movement.
3. **Seashore/Barcode Sign:** The pattern seen is the **Barcode (or Stratosphere) sign**. The presence of parallel, static, horizontal lines indicates a lack of air-filled lung interfaces moving against the pleura, which is characteristic of consolidation or fluid (no dynamic speckle/seashore pattern is present).
4. **A-lines:** The sub-pleural lines are static and unchanging across the sequence.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the static nature of the pleural line, the lack of dynamic shimmering, and the presence of the **Barcode/Stratosphere sign** (linear, static, horizontal patterns), the conclusion is that **Pleural Sliding is ABSENT**.
