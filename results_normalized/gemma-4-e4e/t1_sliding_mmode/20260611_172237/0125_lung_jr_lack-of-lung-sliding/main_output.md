# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

**Analysis of the Ultrasound Frames:**

*   **Frame 1 to Frame 5:** In all five sequential frames, the pleural line is consistently visible as a smooth, sharply defined interface.
*   **Pleural Motion/Shimmer:** There is no visible shimmering or granular motion observed across the pleural line. The line remains static.
*   **Sub-pleural Pattern (B-lines/Speckle):** The speckle pattern below the pleural line is uniform and does not exhibit any change in intensity, texture, or pattern (such as B-lines or comet tails) between frames.
*   **Static Features:** The overall appearance is static, consistent with a fixed line and uniform sub-pleural field.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the consistent presence of a smooth, static pleural line and the absence of any change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern across the sequence, the signs of pleural sliding are **absent**.
