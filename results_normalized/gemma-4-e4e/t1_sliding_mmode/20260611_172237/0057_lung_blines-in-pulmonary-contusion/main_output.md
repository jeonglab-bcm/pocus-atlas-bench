# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

**Analysis of Frames:**

*   **General Observation:** In all 11 frames, a distinct pleural line is visible at the top of the image.
*   **Sliding Signs Observed:**
    *   **B-lines/Comet-tails:** In every frame, there are numerous, prominent, vertical artifacts extending downward from the pleural line. These are classic B-lines (comet-tail artifacts), which indicate interstitial fluid/edema and are strongly associated with lung aeration and motion.
    *   **Seashore Sign:** The subpleural region (the area between the pleura and the ribs) consistently displays a granular, speckled, and textured pattern. This appearance is consistent with the "Seashore Sign," which indicates that the speckle pattern varies between consecutive frames, confirming that the lung tissue is moving.
*   **Sliding Signs Absent:**
    *   There is no evidence of a smooth, sharp, static pleural line (Stratosphere/Barcode sign). The pleura is clearly interacting with the sub-pleural tissue in a dynamic manner.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the persistent presence of prominent **B-lines** and the characteristic **granular/speckled texture** of the subpleural field across all sequential frames, the signs of pleural sliding are **present**.
