# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frame 1:** The pleural line (the bright interface at the top) exhibits a slightly shimmering or granular quality. The speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line is textured and appears dynamic rather than static, suggesting potential movement.
*   **Frame 2:** Similar to Frame 1, the pleural line is bright, and the sub-pleural field shows significant graininess and textural variation, which are hallmarks of pleural sliding.
*   **Frame 3:** There is a noticeable change in presentation. The pleural line appears smooth and sharply defined. Below the line, the echoes are structured into relatively static, horizontal patterns (resembling A-lines or a barcode sign), indicating a lack of motion or sliding.
*   **Frame 4:** The presentation remains static. The pleural line is fixed, and the underlying echoes maintain a highly uniform and unchanging pattern, characteristic of a lack of pleural sliding.

**Conclusion:**
The sequence of frames demonstrates **both** signs of sliding and signs of stasis. The initial frames (1 and 2) show features suggesting the presence of pleural sliding (shimmer/granular texture), while the latter frames (3 and 4) show features suggesting the absence of sliding (static, sharp line/A-lines).

Therefore, the conclusion is **Both (Lung Point).**
