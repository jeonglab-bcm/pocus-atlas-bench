# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

**Observation Analysis:**

*   **Pleural Line:** In all five frames, a distinct, sharp, bright horizontal line (the pleural line) is visible at the top of the image. Across all frames, the position and sharpness of this line remain static.
*   **Sub-Pleural Field:** Below the pleural line, the sonographic field exhibits a consistent, granular, or speckled pattern. This pattern maintains its overall texture and density across all frames.
*   **Dynamic Change:** There is no observable shimmering, granular movement of the pleural line, nor is there a visible difference in the speckle pattern in the sub-pleural field between consecutive frames that would indicate the movement of lung tissue relative to the transducer. The pattern is fixed and static.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the static nature of the interface and the lack of discernible change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern across the sequence, **Pleural Sliding is Absent.**

The presence of a static pleural line and the absence of the characteristic "seashore sign" (which requires a moving interface) are the key indicators of lack of sliding in this sequence.
