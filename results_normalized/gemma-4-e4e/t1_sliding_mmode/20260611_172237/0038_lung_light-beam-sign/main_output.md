# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

## Detailed Analysis

### Frame 1 (Top Left)
**Observation:** The pleural line appears bright and exhibits a noticeable degree of granularity and textural variation (shimmer) immediately adjacent to it. The speckle pattern below the pleural line is heterogeneous and slightly active.
**Conclusion:** **Sliding Present.** The shimmering and textured nature of the pleural interface suggest movement, consistent with a Seashore sign.

### Frame 2 (Top Right)
**Observation:** The pleural line remains visible and appears textured and granular. Although possibly slightly less dynamic than Frame 1, the interface is still complex and does not appear perfectly smooth or static.
**Conclusion:** **Sliding Present.** The sub-pleural texture and the appearance of the line suggest normal lung sliding.

### Frame 3 (Bottom Left)
**Observation:** The pleural line is extremely smooth, sharply defined, and static. The sub-pleural region immediately below the line appears highly uniform with minimal speckle variation across the frame.
**Conclusion:** **Sliding Absent.** The line is static and smooth, fitting the criteria of a Stratosphere/Barcode pattern.

### Frame 4 (Bottom Right)
**Observation:** The pleural line is smooth and stable, maintaining a fixed position relative to the surrounding tissue. There is no evidence of shimmering or motion-induced variation in the sub-pleural tissue.
**Conclusion:** **Sliding Absent.** The static nature of the line and the uniform speckle pattern indicate a lack of movement.

---

## Overall Assessment

**Conclusion: Both (Lung Point/Intermittent).**

The sequence captures a transition from a dynamic lung interface (Frames 1 and 2, consistent with **Seashore/Sliding Present**) to a static interface (Frames 3 and 4, consistent with **Stratosphere/Sliding Absent**). The transition between these two states indicates that the appearance of pleural sliding is variable or that the specific point captured in the sequence moved from a sliding area to a static area, or vice versa.
