# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

**Frame Analysis:**

The provided frames display a lung ultrasound view. Upon careful examination:

1.  **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the interface at the top of the image) appears smooth and static. There is no evidence of "shimmering" or a "textured/granular" appearance that would indicate movement (sliding).
2.  **Sub-pleural Pattern:** Immediately below the pleural line, the pattern consists of distinct, horizontal, parallel echoes. This pattern is characteristic of **A-lines** (horizontal artifacts caused by sound reflecting off the smooth pleural surface), which indicates a very static interface.
3.  **Movement:** In these static frames, there is no observable variation in the speckle pattern between frames, nor are there any signs of "comet-tail" artifacts or "seashore" granular patterns that would suggest the movement of lung tissue against the chest wall.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the observation that the pleural line is static, the sub-pleural pattern consists of fixed A-lines, and there are no signs of shimmering or granular motion, **pleural sliding is absent**.
