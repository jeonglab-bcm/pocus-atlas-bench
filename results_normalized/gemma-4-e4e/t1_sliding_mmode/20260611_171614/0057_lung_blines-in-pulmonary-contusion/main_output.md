**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frame 1:** The pleural line appears somewhat textured or shimmering, and the sub-pleural area exhibits a granular speckle pattern. This suggests dynamic movement or **pleural sliding is present**.
*   **Frame 2:** The pleural line appears smooth and sharply defined. The sub-pleural field is more uniform and static compared to Frame 1. This suggests **pleural sliding is absent** (a static "barcode" appearance).
*   **Frame 3:** The pleural line is highly defined and perfectly smooth. There is a lack of dynamic speckle in the sub-pleural field, indicating a static field consistent with the **absence of sliding**.
*   **Frame 4:** The pleural line shows evidence of shimmering, and the sub-pleural region appears granular and speckled, indicating dynamic movement. This suggests **pleural sliding is present**.
*   **Frame 5:** The pleural line remains visible, and the sub-pleural area retains a textured/granular appearance consistent with dynamic variation. This suggests **pleural sliding is present**.

**Conclusion:**

The sequential frames demonstrate an alternation between signs of active pleural movement (granularity/shimmer in Frames 1, 4, and 5) and signs of a static pleura (smooth, static lines in Frames 2 and 3). Therefore, the overall finding is that **Pleural Sliding is BOTH present and absent** across the field of view, indicative of a "lung point."
