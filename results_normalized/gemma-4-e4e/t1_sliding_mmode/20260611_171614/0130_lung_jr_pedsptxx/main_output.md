**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

**Frame 1:**
*   **Observation:** Below the bright, horizontal pleural line, the sub-pleural area shows a speckled, somewhat granular pattern.
*   **Assessment:** This pattern is suggestive of the **Seashore sign**, indicating the presence of sliding.

**Frame 2:**
*   **Observation:** The speckled texture below the pleural line remains granular and textured, similar to Frame 1.
*   **Assessment:** This confirms the presence of sliding (consistent with the **Seashore sign**).

**Frame 3:**
*   **Observation:** The sub-pleural pattern appears more organized than the first two frames, showing a dense, uniform pattern of speckles, but it is still textured.
*   **Assessment:** This represents a transition in the pattern, but the characteristics of movement are still visible.

**Frame 4:**
*   **Observation:** The pattern below the pleural line appears highly uniform, dense, and remarkably static (unchanging) across the field. It resembles a series of fine, parallel horizontal lines.
*   **Assessment:** This static, uniform pattern is consistent with the **A-line sign** or lack of sliding.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the sequential analysis, the images show signs of both sliding and no sliding. Frames 1 and 2 exhibit a granular texture consistent with the **Seashore sign (Sliding Present)**, while Frame 4 exhibits a highly organized and static pattern consistent with **A-lines (Sliding Absent)**. Therefore, the findings demonstrate **both** pleural sliding and static characteristics across the presented frames.
