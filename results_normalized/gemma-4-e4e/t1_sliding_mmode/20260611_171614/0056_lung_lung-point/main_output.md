### Final Assessment

Based on the analysis of all four frames, the dominant finding is the presence of a **smooth, sharply defined, and static pleural line** across the entire duration of the clip. There is no visible shimmering, no dramatic change in the sub-pleural pattern that would indicate tissue movement (seashore sign), and no comet-tail artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleura.

**Conclusion:** **Pleural sliding is absent.** The static nature of the pleural line suggests either the absence of movement or the presence of pathology that prevents the typical movement seen in normal lung function.
