*   **Frame 2:** The observation is consistent with Frame 1. The pleural line remains static, and there is no discernible change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between this frame and the previous one.
*   **Frame 3:** The pleural line remains fixed and smooth. The pattern below the line shows no signs of movement or granular change.
*   **Frame 4:** The pleural line is static. The absence of movement or variation in the sub-pleural texture is clear.
*   **Frame 5:** All frames exhibit the same unchanging appearance. The pleural line is fixed, and the sub-pleural pattern is uniform.

**Summary of Observations:**

*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line is smooth, sharply defined, and perfectly static across all five frames.
*   **Sub-pleural Pattern:** There is no evidence of speckle variation or shimmering. The pattern strongly suggests the "A-line" or "Stratosphere/Barcode sign" pattern, indicating an absence of respiratory movement.
*   **Signs of Sliding Present:** None were observed (No shimmering, no comet tails, no difference in sub-pleural speckle between frames).
*   **Signs of Sliding Absent:** Multiple signs were observed (Smooth, static pleural line; perfect consistency in the sub-pleural pattern).
*   **Signs of Both:** No transition point or alternating pattern was seen.

**Conclusion:**

Pleural sliding is **ABSENT**.
