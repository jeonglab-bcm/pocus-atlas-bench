# 0078_lung_jellyfish

**Observation:**
The provided images are sequential frames from a Right Posterior Lung Ultrasound (RT Posterior).
The lung parenchyma in the posterior zone is markedly abnormal across all frames. The tissue exhibits a dense, homogeneous, and bright (hyperechoic) texture, resembling the appearance of solid liver parenchyma (**tissue-like hepatization**). This signifies consolidation.
There is no clear evidence of a large, anechoic quadrilateral fluid collection (Quad sign) or a distinct "jellyfish" fragment floating in significant free fluid. While dynamic movement (Sinusoid sign) cannot be assessed on static images, the lung appears consolidated rather than primarily filled with free fluid.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "consolidation"**

**Summary:**
The ultrasound demonstrates extensive posterior lung consolidation characterized by tissue-like hepatization, consistent with a parenchymal process rather than a primarily pleural effusion.
