# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

**Observation:**
The provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone demonstrate significant parenchymal changes. The lung parenchyma is markedly echogenic and appears solid, characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**. Crucially, within this dense, consolidated tissue, there are multiple punctate and linear hyperechoic foci visible (clearly seen in frames 5 and 6). These foci represent air-filled bronchi within the fluid/consolidated alveoli, which is the definition of **air bronchograms**. Normal A-lines (which indicate normal air-filled lung) are absent throughout the field of view. No clear anechoic fluid collections (quad signs) are demonstrated.

**Conclusion:**
**plaps = true**
**type = "consolidation"** (Alveolar consolidation is evident via hepatization and air bronchograms without primary evidence of large pleural effusion.)
