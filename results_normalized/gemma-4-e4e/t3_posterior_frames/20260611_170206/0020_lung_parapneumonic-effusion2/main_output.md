# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

**Image Assessment:**
All five frames display a consistent finding in the posterior/lateral dependent lung zone (PLAPS area). There is a large, distinct, anechoic (black) space occupying the majority of this region.

**Effusion Signs:**
*   **Quad sign:** In all frames, an anechoic space is clearly visible. This space is bounded superiorly by a visible pleural line and inferior/laterally by the lung line/rib shadows, creating the characteristic appearance of a substantial pleural fluid collection. This is a definitive sign of **pleural effusion**.
*   **Sinusoid/Jellyfish signs:** These dynamic or specific textural signs are not clearly identifiable in these static images.

**Consolidation Signs:**
*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** There is no evidence of the lung parenchyma appearing solid, echogenic, or resembling liver texture.
*   **Shred sign/Air bronchograms:** No irregular borders or punctate air-filled bronchi are visible within any consolidated areas.

**Conclusion:**
The images consistently demonstrate a prominent, anechoic pleural fluid collection (Quad sign). There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation.

**Final Classification:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"**
