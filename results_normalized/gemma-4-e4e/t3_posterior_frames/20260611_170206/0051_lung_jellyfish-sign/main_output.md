# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

**Observation:**

The provided sequence of lung ultrasound frames demonstrates pathology in the posterior lung zone.

1.  **Effusion Assessment:** There is a clear, anechoic (black) space visible deep to the pleural line across all frames, which is characteristic of fluid accumulation (pleural effusion).
2.  **Consolidation Assessment:** Within and immediately adjacent to this fluid collection, especially in frames 3 and 4, the lung parenchyma appears solid, heterogeneous, and markedly echogenic. This tissue-like appearance is consistent with alveolar consolidation or exudate (hepatization). There appears to be an irregular or "shredded" interface between the aerated/consolidated tissue and the fluid-filled space.
3.  **Synthesis:** The images clearly display both an anechoic collection (effusion) and an underlying, solid, echogenic process (consolidation) within the dependent posterior zone.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true**
