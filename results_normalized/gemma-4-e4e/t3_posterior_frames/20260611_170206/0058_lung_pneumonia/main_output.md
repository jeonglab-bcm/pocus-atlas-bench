# 0058_lung_pneumonia

**Observation:**
The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone demonstrate severe pathology. The lung parenchyma appears diffusely and densely echogenic, exhibiting a texture highly suggestive of consolidated tissue (hepatization). There is a profound loss of normal aeration, and no typical A-lines are visible. Within this dense, solid-like tissue, there are irregular, dark (hypoechoic) structures that are consistent with air bronchograms (air-filled bronchi within consolidated lung).

Crucially, there is no clear, anechoic (black) space bounded by the ribs or pleura. Therefore, signs of significant free pleural effusion (Quad sign, Jellyfish sign) are not present.

**Assessment of PLAPS Signs:**
*   **Effusion signs:** Absent (plaps = false for effusion).
*   **Consolidation signs:** Present (Tissue-like hepatization and evidence of air bronchograms).

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true** (Consolidation is present).
*   **type = "consolidation"** (The pathology is dominated by alveolar filling/consolidation without significant free fluid).
