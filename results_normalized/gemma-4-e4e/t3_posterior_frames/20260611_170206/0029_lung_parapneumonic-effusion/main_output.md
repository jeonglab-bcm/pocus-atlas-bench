# 0029_lung_parapneumonic-effusion

**Observation:**
The sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone demonstrate significant pathology.
*   **Effusion Signs:** Clear anechoic (black) pleural pockets, Quad signs, Sinusoid signs, or Jellyfish signs are **not** present.
*   **Consolidation Signs:** The lung parenchyma immediately deep to the pleura is uniformly, densely, and intensely hyperechoic across all four frames. This "tissue-like" appearance is consistent with alveolar filling (consolidation) and loss of normal aeration. The texture is solid and echogenic, strongly suggestive of hepatization.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**: One or more consolidation signs (tissue-like hepatization) are present.
- **type = "consolidation"**: The primary pathology observed is dense, tissue-like consolidation within the lung parenchyma, with no prominent evidence of large, free anechoic fluid collections.
