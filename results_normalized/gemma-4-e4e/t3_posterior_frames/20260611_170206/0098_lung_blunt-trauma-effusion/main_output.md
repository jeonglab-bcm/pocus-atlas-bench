# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

**Observation:**

In all sequential frames of the lung ultrasound, a distinct, well-demarcated anechoic (black) collection is visible in the posterior/lateral dependent lung zone. This collection is bounded superiorly by a discernible pleural line and inferior/laterally by the lung/rib shadow. This morphology is characteristic of the **Quad Sign**, indicating a localized pleural effusion.

No signs of alveolar consolidation (such as tissue-like hepatization, the shred sign, or air bronchograms) are identified within the visible lung parenchyma. The dominant finding is the presence of free fluid.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "effusion"
