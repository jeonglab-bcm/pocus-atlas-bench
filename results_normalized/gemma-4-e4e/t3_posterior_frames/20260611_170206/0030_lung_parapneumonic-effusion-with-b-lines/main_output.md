# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

**Observation:**
The sequential lung ultrasound frames demonstrate a posterior and lateral zone in the right hemithorax with an abnormal echotexture. The expected normal pattern of clear A-lines or regular lung sliding is absent in this region. Instead, the lung parenchyma appears filled with a heterogeneous, granular, and highly echogenic material. This texture exhibits features consistent with **tissue-like hepatization** (parenchyma appearing solid and echogenic, resembling liver texture). Furthermore, within this consolidated area, there are numerous punctate hyperechoic foci, which are consistent with **air bronchograms** seen within the consolidated lung tissue. There are no clear, large, anechoic pockets bounded by a distinct pleural line that would indicate a dominant free-flowing pleural effusion (quad sign, sinusoid sign, or jellyfish sign).

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true** (Alveolar consolidation is present)
*   **type = "consolidation"**
