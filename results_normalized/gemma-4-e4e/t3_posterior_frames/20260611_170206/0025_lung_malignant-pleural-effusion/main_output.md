# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

**Observation:**
The ultrasound images of the posterior/lateral lung zone show a heterogeneous and markedly echogenic appearance of the lung parenchyma. The lung tissue does not exhibit the normal clear A-line pattern characteristic of air-filled alveoli. Instead, the parenchyma appears dense, solid, and exhibits an echotexture that is consistent with solid organ tissue, similar to the liver (tissue-like hepatization). This indicates alveolar filling/consolidation. While the interface is complex, the dominant finding is the loss of normal aeration and the replacement of air by fluid/cells within the alveoli. Definitive signs like the Quad sign or distinct Air Bronchograms are not clearly visible in these static frames, but the pervasive density strongly points to consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "consolidation"
