# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

**Observations:**

The sequential lung ultrasound frames of the posterior zone demonstrate a clear, well-aerated lung pattern. Characteristic **A-lines** (horizontal reverberation artifacts) are consistently visible, which indicate normal aeration. The pleural line is visible, and the pattern is consistent with normal lung sliding.

There is **no evidence** of the following:
1.  **Effusion signs:** No anechoic (black) quadrilateral space, no fluid-lung interface (lung line) indicative of fluid accumulation, and no floating, undulating atelectasis (jellyfish sign).
2.  **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma does not appear solid or uniformly echogenic (no tissue-like hepatization), and no abnormal air bronchograms or shred signs are observed.

**Conclusion:**

The criteria for PLAPS (Posterior/Lateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) are not met.
