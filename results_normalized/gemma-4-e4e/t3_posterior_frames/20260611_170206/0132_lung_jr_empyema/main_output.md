# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

**Observations:**

The lung ultrasound frames demonstrate significant abnormality in the posterior lung zone. The parenchyma does not display normal clear lung sliding or crisp A-lines. Instead, the lung tissue appears dense, heterogeneous, and "solid" or "tissue-like," consistent with alveolar filling. Multiple punctate hypoechoic structures are visible within this denser, grey tissue, which are consistent with air bronchograms. These findings suggest filling of the airspaces with exudate or fluid.

**Conclusion:**

The presence of tissue-like texture (hepatization) and air bronchograms confirms the presence of alveolar consolidation.
