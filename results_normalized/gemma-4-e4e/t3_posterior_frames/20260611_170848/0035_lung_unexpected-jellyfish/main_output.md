# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

**Observation:**
The sequential frames consistently demonstrate a prominent finding in the posterior/lateral dependent zone. There is a clear, dark (anechoic) space situated along the peripheral lung wall. This anechoic area is bounded by the pleural line superiorly and the rib shadows/lung interface inferior/laterally, forming a quadrilateral appearance. This configuration is characteristic of the **Quad sign**, which indicates the presence of free fluid (pleural effusion). While the surrounding lung parenchyma is visible, there are no distinct signs of alveolar consolidation, such as diffuse hepatization or prominent air bronchograms within a solid mass. The predominant feature across all frames is the fluid collection.

**Conclusion:**
**plaps = true**
**type = "effusion"**
