# 0073_lung_2017-10-9-dove-in-pleural-fluid

**Observations:**
The sequential frames demonstrate the presence of a significant, anechoic (black) collection in the posterior/lateral lung zone. This collection is clearly bounded superiorly by the pleural line. The presence of this distinct anechoic fluid collection confirms a pleural effusion. While the lung parenchyma visible beneath the fluid appears altered, it does not exhibit the clear, solid, homogeneous echogenicity characteristic of tissue-like hepatization. No definitive shred sign or air bronchograms are clearly identified.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of a substantial anechoic space adjacent to the lung, bounded by the pleural line, is a hallmark of fluid accumulation (effusion).
