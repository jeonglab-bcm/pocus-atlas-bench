# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

**Observations:**

The ultrasound images demonstrate a large, clear, anechoic (black) space occupying the posterior/lateral dependent region of the lung. This space is bounded by a visible pleural line superiorly and the lung parenchyma inferiorly, forming a distinct separation between the fluid collection and the lung tissue.

**Sign Assessment:**
*   **Effusion signs:** The presence of this distinct, anechoic space bounded by the pleural line and the lung interface is consistent with the **Quad sign** (a quadrilateral space formed by pleural line, lung line, and rib shadows).
*   **Consolidation signs:** No signs of alveolar consolidation (such as tissue-like hepatization, air bronchograms, or the shred sign) are visible in the surrounding lung parenchyma.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the consistent presence of a large anechoic pocket bounded by the pleural line and the lung tissue (Quad sign), the diagnosis is:

**plaps = true**
**type = "effusion"**
