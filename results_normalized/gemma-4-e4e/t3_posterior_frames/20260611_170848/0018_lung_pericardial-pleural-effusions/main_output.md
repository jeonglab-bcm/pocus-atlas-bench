# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

**Observation:**
The provided ultrasound frames demonstrate a classic pattern of pleural effusion. In all four frames, there is a large, anechoic (black) fluid collection occupying the posterior/lateral lung zone. This fluid collection is clearly bounded superiorly by the pleural line (the thin white line at the top of the fluid) and inferior/laterally by the lung interface. This configuration constitutes a definitive **quad sign**. There is no evidence of tissue-like echogenicity, shredded borders, or air bronchograms, ruling out significant alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"**
