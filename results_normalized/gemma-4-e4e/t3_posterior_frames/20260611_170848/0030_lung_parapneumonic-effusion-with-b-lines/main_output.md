# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

**Observations:**

The provided lung ultrasound images of the right posterior zone (RT H/T ZONE 2 LOW) demonstrate a large, uniform, anechoic (black) area in the dependent portion of the lung. This space is bounded superiorly by a distinct, linear pleural line. The lack of normal lung markings (A-lines) within this large anechoic space is characteristic of fluid.

*   **Effusion Signs:** The presence of a large, fluid-filled, anechoic collection in the pleural space strongly suggests a **pleural effusion**. While the full quadrilateral shape (quad sign) may require more contextual views, the fluid-air/fluid-lung interface is clear and extensive. The lung parenchyma below the fluid line appears compressed or consolidated due to the external pressure from the fluid.
*   **Consolidation Signs:** There is no evidence of the lung parenchyma appearing solid or uniformly echogenic (tissue-like hepatization). No air bronchograms or a prominent shredded interface consistent with alveolar consolidation are visible within the dominant fluid collection.

**Conclusion:**

The primary and overwhelming finding in this posterior lung zone is a significant pleural fluid collection.
