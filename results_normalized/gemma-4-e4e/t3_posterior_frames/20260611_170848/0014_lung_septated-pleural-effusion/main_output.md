# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

**Observation:**

The sequential images display the lung interface, initially showing a discernible pleural line. As the sequence progresses (especially in the final image), a distinct, large, anechoic (black) space develops in the dependent, posterior region of the lung. This dark space represents fluid. The fluid collection is bounded superiorly by the pleural line and laterally/inferiorly by the lung/rib shadows, forming a characteristic dependent space.

**Assessment against PLAPS Criteria:**

1.  **Effusion signs:** The presence of a large anechoic space in the dependent posterior zone strongly suggests a **Quad sign** (fluid bounded by pleural line superiorly and lung lines laterally/inferiorly). This indicates a significant pleural effusion.
2.  **Consolidation signs:** There is no evidence of diffuse lung tissue appearing solid, echogenic, or hepatized. No shredded borders or visible air bronchograms are present within the affected area.

**Conclusion:**

The analysis demonstrates the clear presence of a significant dependent fluid collection (pleural effusion) in the posterior lung zone. Consolidation is not evident.
