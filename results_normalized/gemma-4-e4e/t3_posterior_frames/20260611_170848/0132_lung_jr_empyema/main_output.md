# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

**Description of Observations:**

The ultrasound images show a posterior lung zone where the normal, aerated appearance of the lung (characterized by A-lines) is absent. The lung parenchyma exhibits marked, diffuse hyper-echogenicity and a solid, dense texture, which is characteristic of tissue-like hepatization. This texture resembles the solid liver parenchyma. Furthermore, there are small, punctate, dark areas (hypoechoic foci) visible within the hyper-echogenic lung tissue, suggesting the presence of air within consolidated bronchi (air bronchograms). There is no evidence of a large, anechoic fluid collection (absence of a definitive Quad Sign).

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "consolidation"**
