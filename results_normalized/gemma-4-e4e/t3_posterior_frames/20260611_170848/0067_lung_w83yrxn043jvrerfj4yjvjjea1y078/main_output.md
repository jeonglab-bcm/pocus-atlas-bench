**Assessment of PLAPS Signs:**

*   **Effusion Signs:**
    *   **Quad Sign:** The presence of a large, anechoic (black) dependent space bounded superiorly by the pleural line, and extending into the peripheral zone (lateral rib shadows), strongly suggests a **Pleural Effusion**. The anechoic collection is the primary finding.
    *   **Sinusoid Sign/Jellyfish Sign:** Cannot be confirmed with static frames, but the characteristic appearance of fluid collection is evident.
*   **Consolidation Signs:**

    *   **Shred Sign/Air Bronchograms:** Not present. The border between lung and fluid is smooth (the pleural line), indicating a separation rather than consolidation.

**Classification:**

*   **plaps:** true (Significant fluid collection is present).
*   **type:** "effusion" (The primary and dominant pathology observed is a free fluid collection/pleural effusion).

**Conclusion:**
The images demonstrate clear signs of a pleural effusion in the posterior lung zone, consistent with the Quad Sign. No significant signs of alveolar consolidation are present.
