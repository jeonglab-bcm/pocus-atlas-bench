# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

**Image Analysis and Observations:**

**Effusion Signs:**
*   **Quad sign:** Not clearly identified. There is no distinct, large anechoic (black) space with a well-defined pleural line and lung line forming a quadrilateral.
*   **Sinusoid/Jellyfish signs:** These dynamic signs cannot be definitively confirmed from static frames but are not the primary feature.

**Consolidation Signs:**
*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** **Present.** The lung parenchyma is uniformly echogenic (bright) and dense, appearing solid and granular, which is highly characteristic of consolidated tissue. There is a marked loss of the normal air artifacts.
*   **Shred sign:** The irregular and disrupted deep borders suggest consolidation, although a formal "shred" (a sharp, jagged line separating zones) is not perfectly isolated, the overall texture is consistent with infiltrative processes.
*   **Air bronchograms:** **Present.** Numerous punctate and linear hyperechoic areas are visible within the dense, consolidated tissue, representing air within the bronchi.

**Summary of Findings:**
The images demonstrate widespread and severe infiltration of the posterior lung zone. The primary finding is the dense, solid, echogenic texture (hepatization) of the lung, accompanied by evidence of air trapped in consolidated airways (air bronchograms).

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true**: The presence of clear signs of consolidation (hepatization, air bronchograms) fulfills the criteria for PLAPS.
*   **type = "consolidation"**: While minor dependent fluid may be present, the overwhelmingly dominant and pathognomonic finding is the solid, tissue-like consolidation.
