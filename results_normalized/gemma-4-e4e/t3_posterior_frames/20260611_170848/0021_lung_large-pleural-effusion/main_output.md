# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

**Assessment of Lung Ultrasound Frames:**

**Observation:**
The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone demonstrate abnormal findings in the dependent area.

1.  **Effusion Signs:** There is a prominent hypoechoic (black/fluid-filled) area visible in the dependent portion of the posterior lung. This appearance is consistent with a collection of fluid, suggesting a component of pleural effusion.
2.  **Consolidation Signs:** The lung tissue within the dependent zone appears significantly echogenic (bright) and solid, losing the characteristic normal aeration and A-line patterns of healthy lung. This solid, dense appearance is consistent with **tissue-like hepatization** (alveolar consolidation). The overall interface between the dense and clearer zones appears irregular, suggesting the involvement of both airspace filling and fluid accumulation.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true** (Signs of both effusion and consolidation are present.)
*   **type = "both"** (The findings represent a mix of dependent fluid accumulation and consolidated/hepatized lung tissue.)
