# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

**Description of Observations:**

The sequential images demonstrate a significant area of increased echogenicity in the posterior lung zone. This area appears dense and solid, displaying a texture highly characteristic of consolidation. The lung parenchyma exhibits a **tissue-like hepatization**, resembling the homogeneous gray appearance of liver tissue. The normal fine, striped A-lines (representing air-filled alveoli) are absent in this zone, indicating loss of aeration. A discernible pleural line is visible superiorly, but the primary pathology is not characterized by a large anechoic effusion (no clear Quad sign) but rather by the altered parenchymal texture.

**Assessment against PLAPS Criteria:**

*   **Effusion signs:** Not definitively present or dominant.
*   **Consolidation signs:** **Tissue-like hepatization** is clearly present.

**Classification:**

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "consolidation"**
