# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

**Observation:**

The sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS) from the posterior zone clearly demonstrate the presence of a significant, anechoic (black) area filling the posterior dependent lung zone.

*   **Effusion Signs:** The large, dark, anechoic space is bounded superiorly by the visible pleural line and laterally by the chest wall/rib shadows. This morphology strongly suggests the presence of free fluid, meeting the criteria for the **Quad sign**.
*   **Consolidation Signs:** No signs of alveolar consolidation (e.g., hepatization, shred signs, or air bronchograms) are identified in the visualized lung tissue. The lung appears compressed or shifted by the fluid but does not exhibit the solid, homogenous texture of consolidation.

**Conclusion:**

The PLAPS pattern is **true** due to the clear presence of free fluid.
