# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

**Observation:**

The lung ultrasound frames consistently show a massive, uniformly echogenic, dense structure occupying the dependent posterior/lateral lung zone. This structure exhibits a highly organized, solid appearance that mimics the texture of the liver (echogenic parenchyma). This pattern is characteristic of alveolar consolidation rather than simple fluid accumulation (pleural effusion), which would appear anechoic (black). There is a clear absence of the typical A-line pattern seen in normal aeration. While the dense material represents fluid within the alveoli, the primary ultrasound pattern observed is that of widespread tissue-like hepatization.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "consolidation"** (Predominantly tissue-like/hepatized lung without dominant free fluid).
