# 0058_lung_pneumonia

**Description of Observations:**
The sequential frames from the posterior lung zone demonstrate a dense, highly hyperechoic (bright) appearance occupying the entire posterior lung base. This homogeneous echogenicity is consistent with **alveolar consolidation (hepatization)**, where the lung parenchyma appears solid and lacks the normal aeration artifacts (A-lines). The usual demarcation between the aerated lung and the dependent zone is obliterated by this consolidated appearance. There is no clearly identifiable anechoic (black) space in the posterior zone that would suggest a free pleural effusion (i.e., the "quad sign" is not present).

**Assessment:**
*   **Effusion signs:** Absent.
*   **Consolidation signs:** Present (tissue-like hepatization).

**Conclusion:**
**plaps = true**
**type = "consolidation"**
