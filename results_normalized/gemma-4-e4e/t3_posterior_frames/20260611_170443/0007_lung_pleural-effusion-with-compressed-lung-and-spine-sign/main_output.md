# 0007_lung_pleural-effusion-with-compressed-lung-and-spine-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observation:**
The lung ultrasound images consistently demonstrate a large, dark (anechoic) space occupying the dependent posterior/lateral zone across all frames (Frames 2, 3, 4, and 5). This space is sharply demarcated superiorly by a pleural line and inferior/laterally by a lung line. This configuration fulfills the requirements for a pleural fluid collection.

**Assessment for PLAPS Signs:**
*   **Effusion Signs:** The **Quad sign** is clearly present in all frames. The anechoic fluid is bounded by a clear pleural line superiorly and a lung line inferior/laterally, forming a quadrilateral pattern. The fluid collection is substantial.
*   **Consolidation Signs:** No signs of alveolar consolidation (such as tissue-like hepatization, shredded deep borders, or air bronchograms) are identified in the visible lung parenchyma.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"** (The finding is dominated by the pleural effusion, specifically the quad sign.)
