# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

**Observation:**

The ultrasound image shows a massive, predominantly anechoic (black) area occupying the posterior/dependent lung zone. This area represents a significant accumulation of fluid in the pleural space. A clear, bright hyperechoic linear structure, consistent with the **pleural line**, is visible superiorly, clearly delineating the boundary of the fluid collection. The configuration of this anechoic area, bounded by the pleural line and the lung/rib shadows, is consistent with the **quad sign** (or a large pleural effusion). There are no clear signs of widespread alveolar consolidation, such as tissue-like hepatization, shredded borders, or visible air bronchograms within the dependent zone.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"**
