# 0100_lung_hemothorax

**Observation:**
The provided sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone do not exhibit the classic signs associated with a Posterior Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome (PLAPS).

*   **Effusion signs:** There is no clear, large anechoic space forming a quad sign bounded by the pleural and lung lines, nor are there distinct features of the sinusoidal or jellyfish sign.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma does not show signs of tissue-like hepatization (appearing solid and echogenic like liver tissue), nor is there evidence of a shredded interface or prominent air bronchograms indicative of dense consolidation.
*   The visible lung architecture and interface suggest the absence of significant pleural effusion or alveolar consolidation in these specific zones.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = false
