# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

**Observation:**
The sequential frames display the posterior/lateral lung zone. The dependent lung parenchyma in this zone appears markedly abnormal. Instead of the normal, dark, highly aerated appearance with clear A-lines (lung sliding artifacts), the lung tissue is intensely echogenic (bright) and appears solid. This pattern is consistent with **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung texture resembles solid liver parenchyma. Within this consolidated, echogenic tissue, there are irregular, dark, punctate foci visible. These dark foci represent air-filled bronchi trapped within the fluid/exudate-filled alveoli, which is a classic **air bronchogram** sign. No clear, anechoic quadrilateral fluid collection (quad sign) is visible, and the lung does not appear to be floating in surrounding free fluid (no jellyfish sign).

**Conclusion:**
**plaps = true**
**type = "consolidation"**
