# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

**Description of Observations:**
The provided sequential frames demonstrate a clear and large anechoic (black) space occupying the posterior/lateral dependent portion of the lung fields across all frames. This space represents a fluid collection. The lung interface is visible adjacent to this collection. The features of this finding strongly align with the criteria for a pleural effusion. No clear, widespread signs of diffuse alveolar consolidation (such as massive tissue-like hepatization or numerous air bronchograms) are dominant in these frames; the primary pathology is the fluid accumulation.

**Assessment of Signs:**
*   **Effusion signs:** The presence of a large anechoic collection in the posterior zone meets the criteria suggestive of a **quad sign** (anechoic space bounded by pleural/lung lines and rib shadows).
*   **Consolidation signs:** No definitive consolidation signs are clearly visible as the primary finding is the fluid.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"** (The predominant finding is the large pleural fluid collection.)
