# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

**Observations:**
The ultrasound frames show the posterior lung zone. A distinct pleural line is visible. Immediately deep to the pleural line, a significant anechoic (black) space is observed, indicating the presence of fluid. The underlying lung tissue (parenchyma) appears somewhat attenuated but does not exhibit the diffuse, solid, or "tissue-like" texture (hepatization) typical of widespread alveolar consolidation.

**Analysis of Signs:**

*   **Effusion Signs:** The prominent, anechoic space superior to the lung tissue is consistent with a **pleural effusion**. This fluid collection is the primary feature observed.
*   **Consolidation Signs:** There is no clear evidence of **tissue-like hepatization**, **shred sign**, or distinct **air bronchograms**. The lung parenchyma, while possibly diseased, does not show the hallmarks of dense alveolar consolidation in these images.

**Conclusion:**
Based on the presence of an anechoic fluid collection, the pattern is classified as:

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"**
