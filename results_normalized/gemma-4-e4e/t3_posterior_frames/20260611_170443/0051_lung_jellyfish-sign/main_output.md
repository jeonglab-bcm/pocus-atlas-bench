# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

**Observations:**

The sequential frames show large areas of anechoic (black) space in the dependent zones of the lung field. This is consistent with the presence of significant fluid accumulation, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion.

*   **Effusion Signs:** The large anechoic zones exhibit characteristics consistent with a pleural effusion, which could potentially present as a **Quad sign** (dependent fluid collection bounded by the chest wall and lung) or a large area of accumulated fluid. While a dynamic assessment is required to confirm the **Sinusoid sign**, the static images strongly suggest significant fluid accumulation.
*   **Consolidation Signs:** The areas of visible lung parenchyma do not display the characteristic dense, solid, or "hepatized" texture associated with extensive alveolar consolidation. Therefore, consolidation signs are not the primary finding.
