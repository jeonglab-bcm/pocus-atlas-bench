# 0143_lung_jr_pleuraleffusion

**Image Analysis and Observation:**

The sequence of lung ultrasound frames demonstrates a large, anechoic (black) area occupying the posterior/lower lung zone. This fluid collection is bounded superiorly by a distinct pleural line (lung line) and inferiorly/laterally by the imaging boundaries and rib shadows.

*   **Effusion Signs:**
    *   **Quad Sign:** The anechoic space formed between the pleural line and the lung line, confined by the lateral rib shadows, is characteristic of a significant pleural effusion (Quad sign).
    *   **Sinusoid/Jellyfish:** No definitive evidence of dynamic sinusoidal movement or a floating "jellyfish" structure is visible in these static frames.
*   **Consolidation Signs:**
    *   **Hepatization:** The lung parenchyma visible deep to the fluid interface does not exhibit the characteristic solid, "tissue-like" texture of consolidation.
    *   **Shred Sign/Air Bronchograms:** These signs are not identifiable in the presented images.

**Conclusion:**

The ultrasound findings are consistent with a prominent pleural effusion.

*   **type = "effusion"**
