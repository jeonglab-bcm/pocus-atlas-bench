# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

**Observation:**

The ultrasound images consistently display a posterior/lateral lung field that exhibits a marked increase in echogenicity, appearing dense and homogeneous. The normal pattern of lung aeration (visualized by the characteristic A-lines, which are absent here) has been replaced by a texture that resembles solid parenchyma, similar to the liver (**Tissue-like hepatization**). There is no evidence of a distinct, anechoic (black) space or clear boundary defining a free fluid collection (i.e., no **Quad sign** or **Sinusoid sign** is visible). Instead, the entire visualized dependent zone is characterized by this solid, echogenic appearance, which is pathognomonic for alveolar filling/consolidation.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true** (Alveolar consolidation is clearly present)
*   **type = "consolidation"** (Consolidation is the dominant finding, and no significant pleural effusion is observed).
