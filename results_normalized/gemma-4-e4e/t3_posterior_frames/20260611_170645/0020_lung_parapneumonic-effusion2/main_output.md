# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

**Observation:**

The sequential frames consistently demonstrate a large, well-defined, anechoic (black) space in the posterior lung zone. This space is clearly bounded superiorly by a horizontal/curved pleural line and inferiorly by the visible lung line (the interface between the fluid and the underlying lung parenchyma). This appearance is highly characteristic of a fluid collection.

There is no evidence of the lung parenchyma appearing solid or highly echogenic ("tissue-like hepatization"), nor are there signs of irregular deep borders or air bronchograms. The dominant finding is the accumulation of fluid in the pleural space.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true** (The presence of a clear pleural effusion constitutes a PLAPS pattern).
*   **type = "effusion"** (The findings are predominantly characterized by a fluid collection, matching the criteria for the Quad Sign).
