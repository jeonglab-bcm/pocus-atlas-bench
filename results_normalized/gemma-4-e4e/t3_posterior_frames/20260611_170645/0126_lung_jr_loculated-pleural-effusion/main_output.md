# 0126_lung_jr_loculated-pleural-effusion

**Observation:**

The sequential frames from the posterior/lateral lung zone demonstrate a large, anechoic (black) space filling the dependent portion of the lung. This represents a significant fluid collection (pleural effusion). Within this fluid collection, the lung parenchyma is visible, exhibiting internal branching structures and speckled echogenicity, contrasting sharply against the surrounding anechoic fluid.

Specifically, the **Jellyfish sign** is clearly evident (most pronounced in frames 3 and 4): the lung appears suspended and undulating within the surrounding anechoic fluid, much like a jellyfish in water. No classic features of alveolar consolidation (like tissue-like hepatization) are observed in the dependent zone; instead, the primary pattern is that of free fluid accumulation.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"** (The presence of the Jellyfish sign confirms a predominant fluid collection/effusion surrounding the lung tissue).
