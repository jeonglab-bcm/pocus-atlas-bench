# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

**Observation:**

The provided sequence of lung ultrasound images displays clear signs of both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation in the posterior lung zone.

1.  **Effusion Signs:** Multiple frames demonstrate a large, anechoic (black) space accumulating between the pleura and the chest wall/lung base. This anechoic collection represents free pleural fluid, which is the primary sign of the **Quad Sign** (or large-scale fluid collection).
2.  **Consolidation Signs:** Intermixed with the fluid, particularly visible in frames 4 and 5, the lung parenchyma shows a highly echogenic, dense appearance. This texture is characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**, where the normally black (anechoic/aerated) lung appears solid and bright, mimicking the texture of liver tissue.

**Conclusion:**

The presence of both free pleural fluid (effusion) and consolidated/hepatized lung tissue in the posterior zone confirms a PLAPS pattern.

*   **type = "both"**
