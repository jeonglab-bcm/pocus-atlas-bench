# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

**Description of Observations:**

The sequential lung ultrasound frames consistently demonstrate a large, clearly defined anechoic (black) space in the posterior/lateral dependent lung zone. This fluid collection is separated from the surrounding lung parenchyma. The appearance of this anechoic space, bounded by the pleural line superiorly and the tissue boundaries laterally/inferiorly, is highly suggestive of a significant fluid collection. There is no evidence of parenchymal consolidation (echogenicity resembling liver tissue) in this region.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"**
