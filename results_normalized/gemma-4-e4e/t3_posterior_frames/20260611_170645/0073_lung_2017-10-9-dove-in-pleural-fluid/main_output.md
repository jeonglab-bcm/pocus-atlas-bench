# 0073_lung_2017-10-9-dove-in-pleural-fluid

**Observation:**
The ultrasound frames display the posterior lung zone. The most prominent finding is a large, uniformly anechoic (black) space occupying the dependent area of the lung. This fluid collection is clearly bounded superiorly by a distinct pleural line and laterally/inferiorly by rib shadows. This configuration strongly suggests a pleural effusion. There is no evidence of dense, tissue-like "hepatization" or air bronchograms within the parenchyma, ruling out primary alveolar consolidation. The clear delineation of anechoic space within the lung field meets the criteria for a large-scale fluid accumulation.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true** (due to the presence of a clear pleural effusion).
*   **type = "effusion"** (the dominant pathological process observed).
