# 0078_lung_jellyfish

**Observation:**
The lung parenchyma in the posterior zone displays a highly echogenic, solid appearance, which is characteristic of alveolar consolidation or tissue-like hepatization. There is an absence of the typical A-lines associated with normal aeration. The interface between the consolidated lung and potentially remaining aerated lung appears irregular, consistent with the **shred sign**. While some areas may suggest an interface, there is no clear, large anechoic (black) space bounded by a distinct pleural line forming a classic quad sign of effusion.

**Conclusion:**
**plaps = true**
**type = "consolidation"**
