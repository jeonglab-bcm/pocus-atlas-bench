# 0007_lung_pleural-effusion-with-compressed-lung-and-spine-sign

**Image Analysis:**

Across all five frames, the posterior/lateral lung zone consistently demonstrates a large, anechoic (black) space.

*   **Effusion Signs:** The prominent anechoic area is sharply bounded. It is contained superiorly by the pleural line and inferior/laterally by the ribs/lung interface, forming a characteristic quadrilateral shape. This is a clear demonstration of the **Quad sign**, which indicates the presence of a significant pleural effusion in the posterior/dependent lung zone.
*   **Consolidation Signs:** There are no features of consolidation (no "tissue-like hepatization," "shred signs," or air bronchograms) visible in the parenchyma.

**Conclusion:**

The presence of the Quad sign confirms the presence of an effusion.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "effusion"** (Predominantly fluid collection).
