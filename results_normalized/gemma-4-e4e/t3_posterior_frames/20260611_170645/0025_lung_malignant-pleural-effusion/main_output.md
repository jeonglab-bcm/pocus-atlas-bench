# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

**Observation:**
The provided sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) demonstrate widespread and striking abnormalities in the lung parenchyma.

1.  **Consolidation:** The lung fields exhibit a dense, heterogeneous, and solid-appearing echogenicity, which is highly characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**. The normal clear, aerated appearance of the lung is completely replaced by a pattern resembling liver tissue.
2.  **Structural Changes:** The parenchyma appears consolidated, with irregular interfaces and patchy, reticular/nodular echogenic patterns, consistent with alveolar filling (consolidation).
3.  **Effusion:** While there are signs of interstitial/pleural thickening, a clear, dominant quadrangular or sinusoidal pleural effusion (free fluid collection) is not the primary or most prominent feature. The pattern is dominated by the solid, consolidated texture.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true** (Consolidation is clearly present).
*   **type = "consolidation"** (The dominant finding is diffuse alveolar consolidation/hepatization, rather than predominant free fluid collection).
