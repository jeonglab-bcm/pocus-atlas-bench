# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

**Observation:**
The lung ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone demonstrate marked abnormal echogenicity of the lung parenchyma. The tissue appears diffusely bright and solid, resembling liver texture (hepatization). The typical aeration pattern of normal lung tissue is lost. Within this highly echogenic field, there are irregular interfaces and scattered areas of decreased echogenicity, consistent with air-filled structures traversing the dense tissue, suggesting the presence of air bronchograms. No clear, large, anechoic space corresponding to a classic free pleural effusion (e.g., a defined Quad sign) is visible; however, the predominant pathology observed is consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
The primary pattern observed is **alveolar consolidation** (tissue-like hepatization).
