# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

**Observation:**
The ultrasound images consistently demonstrate a large, anechoic (black) area occupying the dependent/posterior lung zone. This space is bounded superiorly by the pleural line and inferior/laterally by the rib cage/diaphragm. This large, uniform, anechoic collection is characteristic of a pleural effusion. The lung tissue visible superior to this collection appears aerated. There is no evidence of diffuse parenchymal opacification, solid, "liver-like" echogenicity, or visible air bronchograms within the dependent zone.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true** (The presence of a significant fluid collection suggests a PLAPS pattern, specifically the pleural component).
*   **type = "effusion"** (The dominant and unmistakable finding is the accumulation of free fluid, consistent with the quad sign or a substantial pleural effusion).
