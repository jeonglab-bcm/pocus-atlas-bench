# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

**Observation:**
The sequential lung ultrasound images of the posterior zone demonstrate a prominent, anechoic (black) collection of fluid filling the dependent space. This collection is bounded superiorly by a distinct pleural line (the visceral pleura) and laterally and inferiorly by the rib shadows and lung tissue. The remaining lung parenchyma does not display the solid, echogenic appearance characteristic of consolidation.

**Assessment against PLAPS Criteria:**
*   **Effusion signs:**
    *   **Quad sign:** **Present**. A clear, anechoic quadrilateral of fluid is seen, bounded by the pleural line superiorly and the ribs/lung line inferior/laterally.
    *   Sinusoid sign: Cannot be definitively confirmed with static images, but the presence of significant free fluid is evident.
    *   Jellyfish sign: Not observed.
*   **Consolidation signs:**
    *   Tissue-like hepatization: Not present. The pathology is fluid, not solid tissue.
    *   Shred sign / Air bronchograms: Not present.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of the definitive Quad sign indicates a significant pleural effusion.
