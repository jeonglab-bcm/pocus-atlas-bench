# 0100_lung_hemothorax

**Observation:**
The provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone demonstrate parenchymal thickening and increased echogenicity. The lung parenchyma appears dense and "solid," lacking the normal black (anechoic) appearance of well-aerated lung. This appearance is consistent with a tissue-like, hepatized texture. While the posterior zone shows significant density, a large, anechoic free fluid collection (like the quad sign) is not the predominant feature.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**: The presence of dense, hepatized lung parenchyma indicates a pathological process (consolidation/atelectasis) in the posterior zone.
*   **type = "consolidation"**: The primary visible sign is the tissue-like appearance of the lung tissue, without dominant free fluid collection signs.
