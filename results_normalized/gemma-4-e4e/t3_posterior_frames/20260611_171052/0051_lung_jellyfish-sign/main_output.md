# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

**Description of Findings:**

The provided sequence of lung ultrasound images shows a significant and consistent finding of an anechoic (black) area occupying the lower portion of the posterior lung zone. This anechoic space is clearly demarcated superiorly by a distinct, bright horizontal line, which represents the pleural line.

The presence of this large, fluid-filled pocket is consistent with a **pleural effusion**. The underlying lung tissue visible through and beneath the fluid appears denser and lacks the characteristic uniform, fine, parallel A-lines of normal aeration.

While the underlying lung tissue itself may be affected by atelectasis or consolidation, the most prominent and unequivocal feature visible across all frames is the accumulation of free fluid, which is the primary sign of a pleural effusion.

**Conclusion:**

**plaps = true**
**type = "effusion"**
