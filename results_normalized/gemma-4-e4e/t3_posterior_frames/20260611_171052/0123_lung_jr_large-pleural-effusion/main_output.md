# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

**Observation:**
The provided lung ultrasound frames demonstrate a large, clearly defined, anechoic (black) space occupying the posterior and dependent lung zone across all frames. A distinct, thin, bright line (the pleural line) is visible superiorly, separating the fluid collection from the remaining lung parenchyma. The anechoic collection is characteristic of free fluid. The lung tissue itself does not exhibit the solid, echogenic texture required for consolidation (hepatization); rather, the dependent space is filled with fluid. This pattern strongly suggests a pleural effusion.

**Conclusion:**
**plaps = true**
**type = "effusion"**

*(Rationale: The presence of a large anechoic dependent space bounded by a pleural line is the classic presentation of a pleural effusion, fitting the "effusion" criteria within the PLAPS classification.)*
