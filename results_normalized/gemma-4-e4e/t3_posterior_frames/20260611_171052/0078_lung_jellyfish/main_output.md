# 0078_lung_jellyfish

**Observation:**

The ultrasound images of the RT Posterior zone demonstrate a pattern of widespread, dense, and homogeneous echogenicity across the visualized lung parenchyma. This appearance is markedly different from a normal, air-filled lung (which would show A-lines and distinct pleural/lung interfaces). The lung parenchyma appears solid, dense, and echogenic, exhibiting **tissue-like hepatization** (solid appearance, similar to liver texture). No distinct anechoic areas (which would indicate free pleural fluid) or clear A-lines are visible. The dominant pathology observed is the dense, consolidative change within the lung tissue.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true** (Alveolar consolidation is clearly present).
*   **type = "consolidation"** (The findings are predominantly characterized by tissue-like hepatization, not by a free fluid collection).
