# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

**Description of Observations:**

The sequential frames demonstrate a large, persistent, and predominantly **anechoic (dark/black) area** in the posterior/lateral dependent zone of the lung. This anechoic space is bounded superiorly by a clear, distinct pleural line. Below this fluid collection, the normal lung architecture is obscured or compressed. The absence of typical A-lines in the dependent zone, combined with the significant anechoic space, is highly suggestive of a large pleural effusion. No clear signs of tissue-like hepatization, air bronchograms, or a distinct "shred sign" (typical of dense consolidation without fluid) are definitively visible in these static frames; the dominant pathology is the fluid collection. The overall appearance is consistent with a posterior pleural effusion.

**Conclusion:**

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "effusion"**
