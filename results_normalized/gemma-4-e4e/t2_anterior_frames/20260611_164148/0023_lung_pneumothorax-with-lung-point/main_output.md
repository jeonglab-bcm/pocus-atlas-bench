**B-lines Assessment**

**Assessment:** B-lines are present. Hyperechoic, vertical, artifact-like structures are visible arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen.

**Subtype Classification:** The B-lines are **confluent and coalescing**, merging into a dense, diffuse white sheet that obscures the underlying lung architecture (A-lines). This pattern is characteristic of a **ground\_glass** pattern.

**Consolidation Assessment**

**Assessment:** **Consolidation is present.** The lung parenchyma exhibits a uniformly dense and highly echogenic texture that lacks the typical dark, aerated appearance of healthy lung. This widespread, solid-like infiltration suggests alveolar filling.
