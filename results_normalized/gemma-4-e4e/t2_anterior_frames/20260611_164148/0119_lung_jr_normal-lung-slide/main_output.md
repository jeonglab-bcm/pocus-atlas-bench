## Analysis of Lung Ultrasound Frames

**Frame by Frame Observation:**

**B-lines:**
In every frame, vertical, hyperechoic artifacts are visible extending from the pleural line towards the bottom of the image. These artifacts are discrete, meaning they are well-separated, and they do not merge or coalesce into a diffuse white sheet. They are classic examples of lung rockets (B-lines).

**Consolidation:**
The lung parenchyma appears generally clear and well-aerated between the vertical B-lines. There is no evidence of homogeneous, liver-like echogenicity (hepatization) across the field of view. No clear air bronchograms (air within solid parenchyma) or an irregular, shredded border (shred sign) are visible.
