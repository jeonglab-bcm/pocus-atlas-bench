# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

## B-lines Assessment
**Observation:**
In all sequential frames, multiple, hyperechoic, vertical artifacts are clearly visible originating from the pleural line and extending deep into the visualized lung field. These artifacts are not discrete; rather, they are confluent and appear to form a dense, diffuse white sheet, effectively obscuring the underlying A-lines. This pattern is characteristic of diffuse alveolar or interstitial pathology.

**Conclusion:**
*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**
*   **B-lines subtype = "ground_glass"**

## Consolidation Assessment
**Observation:**
The lung parenchyma demonstrates a diffuse, high-level echo pattern dominated by B-lines. While this indicates significant lung pathology (likely severe pulmonary edema or diffuse interstitial disease), there is no clear evidence of the specific criteria for alveolar consolidation:
*   The parenchyma does not appear solid or tissue-like (no hepatization).
*   No distinct, irregular, shredded border between aerated and consolidated lung is visible (no shred sign).
*   No characteristic punctate or linear hyperechoic foci (air bronchograms) are discernible within an opacified area.

**Conclusion:**
*   **consolidation = false**
*   **consolidation_type = null**

***

**Overall Summary:**
The sequential frames consistently demonstrate a pattern of extensive, confluent B-lines, classifying the finding as a **ground_glass** pattern. There is **no evidence** of distinct alveolar consolidation (hepatization, shred sign, or air bronchogram).
