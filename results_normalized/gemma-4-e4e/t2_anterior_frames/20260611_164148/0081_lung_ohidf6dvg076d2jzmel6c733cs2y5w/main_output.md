### Consolidation Assessment
The lung parenchyma below the pleural line appears uniformly dense and homogenous. It has lost its normal, dark, aerated appearance and instead exhibits a solid, bright, echogenic texture, which is highly characteristic of fluid or cellular material replacing air. This solid, liver-like texture is known as hepatization. While the screen is very bright, the overall dense appearance meets the criteria for consolidation.
**Assessment:** The dominant sign is **tissue-like hepatization**.
