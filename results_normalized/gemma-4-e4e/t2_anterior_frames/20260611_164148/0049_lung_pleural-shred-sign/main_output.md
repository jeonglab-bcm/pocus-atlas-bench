*   **Consolidation:** Not present.

### Summary and Assessment

**B-lines Assessment:**
*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**
*   **Classification:** mixed. The sequence shows a transition from discrete, well-spaced **septal** B-lines (Frames 1-2) to a dense, coalescing, **ground_glass** pattern (Frames 3-5).

**Consolidation Assessment:**
*   **consolidation = false**. While the lung is markedly opacified due to the presence of B-lines (indicating diffuse interstitial/alveolar disease), there are no signs of classic alveolar consolidation such as tissue-like hepatization, air bronchograms, or a definitive shredded border between aerated and consolidated tissue.

**Conclusion:**
The lung ultrasound sequence demonstrates significant pulmonary disease characterized by a **mixed B-line pattern**, progressing from septal to ground-glass appearance, consistent with diffuse interstitial or alveolar filling (likely pulmonary edema or interstitial syndrome), but **without evidence of alveolar consolidation**.
