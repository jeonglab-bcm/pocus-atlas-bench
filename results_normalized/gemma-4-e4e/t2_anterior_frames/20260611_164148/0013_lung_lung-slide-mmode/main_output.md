# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

**B-lines Assessment**

*   **Observation:** In frames 4, 5, and 6, there are distinct, hyperechoic vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending deep into the lung parenchyma. These artifacts are clearly separated by dark, anechoic (aerated) lung tissue.
*   **Classification:** **B-lines present (lung\_rockets = true)**.
*   **Subtype:** **septal** (discrete, well-spaced B-lines).

**Consolidation Assessment**

*   **Observation:** The lung parenchyma, while exhibiting interstitial changes (B-lines), does not show the characteristic solid, echogenic texture of hepatization. There is no visible "shred sign" or punctate/linear hyperechoic air bronchograms within a consolidated region.
*   **Classification:** **consolidation = false**.

**Conclusion**

*   The ultrasound demonstrates **septal B-lines**, indicating interstitial thickening.
*   There is **no evidence of alveolar consolidation** (hepatization, shredded border, or air bronchograms).
