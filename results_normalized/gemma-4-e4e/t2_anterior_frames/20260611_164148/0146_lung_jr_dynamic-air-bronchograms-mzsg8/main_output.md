## B-lines Assessment

**B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**

**B-line subtype:** ground_glass
*Rationale:* Across all frames, the B-lines are not discrete and well-spaced (septal); instead, they are numerous, dense, and coalesce into a thick, diffuse white sheet that merges across the entire field of view.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Consolidation = false**
*Rationale:* While the lung exhibits severe, dense opacification (which is highly abnormal), the images do not show the classic defining signs of alveolar consolidation:
*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The pattern is dominated by B-lines (edema/interstitial filling) rather than a true solid, liver-like tissue appearance.
*   **Shred sign:** No irregular, shredded border between consolidated and aerated lung is visible.
*   **Air bronchograms:** No punctate or linear hyperechoic foci (representing air-filled bronchi) are identifiable within the dense opacified lung.

***

## Summary of Observations

**B-lines:** **true** (ground_glass)
**Consolidation:** **false**

**Description:** The lung ultrasound video shows extensive, confluent, and dense B-lines (lung rockets) that merge into a uniform, diffuse white sheet. This pattern is characteristic of ground-glass opacification, typically indicating significant alveolar or interstitial edema (pulmonary edema). Definitive signs of alveolar consolidation (hepatization, shred sign, air bronchograms) were not present in this sequence.
