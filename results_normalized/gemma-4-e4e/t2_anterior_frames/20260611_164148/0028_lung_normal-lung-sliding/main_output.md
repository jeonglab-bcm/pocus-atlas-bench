# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

**B-lines Assessment**

Based on the analysis of all frames, B-lines are present. The artifacts are not discrete and well-spaced; instead, they are confluent and form a dense, diffuse white sheet that extends from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen, completely obscuring any discernible A-lines or normal lung architecture.

*   **lung_rockets = true**
*   **B-lines Subtype = ground_glass**

**Consolidation Assessment**

Consolidation is clearly present. The lung parenchyma is uniformly hyper-echogenic, exhibiting a dense, solid appearance that lacks the normal aeration seen in healthy lung tissue. This appearance closely mimics the texture of the liver on ultrasound.

*   **consolidation = true**
*   **consolidation_type = "hepatization"**

**Conclusion**

The lung ultrasound images demonstrate a pattern of severe pulmonary pathology characterized by dense, confluent B-lines (ground glass pattern) and evidence of alveolar consolidation (hepatization). This combination of findings is classic for severe pulmonary edema or diffuse alveolar damage.
