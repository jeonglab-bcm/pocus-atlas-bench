# 0026_lung_pneumonia-b-lines

## Image Analysis

### B-lines Assessment

**Frame by Frame Observation:**
Across all five frames, a clear pleural line is visible at the top. Below the pleural line, multiple vertical, hyperechoic (bright) artifacts are seen extending downwards towards the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are distinct, well-defined, and separated by dark, anechoic areas which represent air-filled lung parenchyma. They do not coalesce into a diffuse sheet.

**Assessment:**
- **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**: Yes.
- **Subtype:** The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced, with clear evidence of lung tissue visible between them.
- **Classification:** **septal**

### Consolidation Assessment

**Frame by Frame Observation:**
The lung parenchyma does not exhibit the characteristics of airspace filling. There is no generalized solid, liver-like echogenicity (hepatization). There are no irregular, shredded interfaces visible, nor are there any punctate, bright foci within a consolidated background that would indicate air bronchograms. The dark areas beneath the pleura confirm adequate aeration.

**Assessment:**
- **Consolidation (consolidation = false)**: No signs of alveolar consolidation are present.

## Conclusion

**B-lines Assessment:**
The lung ultrasound displays multiple discrete, vertical, hyperechoic artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending into the lower lung fields. The separation between these artifacts indicates a pattern consistent with thickening of the interlobular septa.
*   **lung_rockets = true**
*   **classification = septal**

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The lung parenchyma is well-aerated and does not show any evidence of alveolar consolidation or tissue-like changes.
*   **consolidation = false**
