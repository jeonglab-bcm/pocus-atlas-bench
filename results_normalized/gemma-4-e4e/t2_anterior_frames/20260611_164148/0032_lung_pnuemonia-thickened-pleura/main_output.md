## Lung Ultrasound Analysis

**B-lines Assessment**
B-lines are clearly present throughout the entire clip. Initially (Frames 1-2), the artifacts appear discrete and well-spaced, suggesting a septal pattern. However, as the clip progresses (Frames 3-5), these artifacts coalesce and merge into a dense, confluent sheet, characteristic of a ground-glass pattern. Since both discrete (septal) and coalescing (ground-glass) patterns are observed, the overall pattern is classified as mixed.
**lung_rockets = true**
**B-line Subtype:** mixed

**Consolidation Assessment**
The lung parenchyma appears highly echogenic and is dominated by B-lines, which is typical of interstitial or alveolar edema, not true alveolar consolidation. There is no evidence of the solid, tissue-like echogenicity characteristic of hepatization. Furthermore, no irregular, shredded borders (shred sign) or distinct air-filled bronchi (air bronchograms) are visible within any area of increased echogenicity. Therefore, the lung parenchyma does not meet the criteria for consolidation.

**Summary of Observations and Conclusions:**

**B-lines:**
*   **Observation:** Multiple, hyperechoic vertical artifacts are present across all frames. They transition from discrete, well-spaced lines (septal pattern) to a confluent, dense white sheet (ground-glass pattern) over the duration of the clip.

*   **Conclusion:** Consolidation is absent.
