# 0010_lung_pulmonary-shunt-in-pneumonia

## LUS Analysis

### B-lines Assessment
In all sequential frames, multiple, distinct, and well-spaced hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) are clearly visible extending from the pleural line down the lung parenchyma. The areas of lung parenchyma between these distinct B-lines remain dark (hypoechoic/anechoic), indicating that the lung is not uniformly consolidated but is displaying thickened septal structures.

**B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**

**Classification: septal**

### Consolidation Assessment
The lung parenchyma does not exhibit the characteristic signs of alveolar consolidation. There is no evidence of a uniformly echogenic, solid tissue-like appearance (hepatization), no irregular shred signs, and no visible punctate or linear hyperechoic air bronchograms within consolidated tissue. The lung remains aerated despite the presence of B-lines.

**consolidation = false**
**consolidation_type = null**

## Conclusions

**B-lines:** Septal
