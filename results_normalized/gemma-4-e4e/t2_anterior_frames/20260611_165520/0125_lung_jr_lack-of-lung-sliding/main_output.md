# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

**B-lines Assessment**
Across all frames, the images exhibit discrete, vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line and extending downwards. These lines are clearly spaced, and the lung parenchyma between them remains visible and appears aerated. This pattern is characteristic of **septal** B-lines, indicating thickening of the interlobular septa.

*   **lung_rockets = true**
*   **B-line subtype = "septal"**

**Consolidation Assessment**
None of the frames display the signs of alveolar consolidation. The lung parenchyma does not have the uniform, liver-like echogenicity (hepatization), nor are there any discernible air bronchograms or an irregular, shredded border between consolidated and aerated tissue. The lung remains aerated despite the presence of B-lines.

*   **consolidation = false**
*   **consolidation_type = null**

**Conclusion**
The lung ultrasound demonstrates a clear pattern of diffuse interstitial disease characterized by septal B-lines. There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation.
