**Consolidation Classification:** hepatization

### Frame 6
**B-lines Assessment:** The pattern of diffuse, coalescing B-lines (ground-glass appearance) persists.
**B-line Classification:** ground_glass
**Consolidation Assessment:** The parenchymal texture is solidly uniform and echogenic, consistent with solid tissue replacement (hepatization).
**Consolidation Classification:** hepatization

## Summary of Observations and Conclusions

**B-lines:** The clip shows a progression from a clear **septal** pattern (discrete, well-spaced B-lines) in the initial frames (Frames 1-3) to a diffuse **ground_glass** pattern (coalescing B-lines forming a dense white sheet) in the later frames (Frames 4-6).

**Consolidation:** Consolidation is not initially present. However, as the B-lines shift to a ground-glass pattern (Frame 4), the lung parenchyma progresses rapidly through a phase of dense, uniform opacification, achieving a **hepatization** appearance by Frames 5 and 6.

**Overall Conclusion:** The LUS video demonstrates a progression of disease from interstitial edema/thickening (septal B-lines) to diffuse alveolar damage/filling of airspaces (ground-glass B-lines) followed by solid tissue replacement or alveolar hemorrhage/fill (hepatization).
