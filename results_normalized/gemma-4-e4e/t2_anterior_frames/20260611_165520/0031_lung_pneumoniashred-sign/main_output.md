The characteristic black/dark gray areas of normal aeration are absent throughout the visible lung tissue. This homogeneous, solid-like appearance closely mimics the echogenicity of a liver or solid tissue.
*   **Conclusion:**
    *   **consolidation = true**
    *   **consolidation\_type = "hepatization"** (due to the uniform, tissue-like, highly echogenic appearance).

### Summary

The clip demonstrates a severe pattern of **ground-glass B-lines** combined with extensive **alveolar consolidation presenting as hepatization**. This pattern suggests severe alveolar filling, often associated with conditions like acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS) or severe pneumonia, where the lung tissue appears dense and solid on ultrasound.
