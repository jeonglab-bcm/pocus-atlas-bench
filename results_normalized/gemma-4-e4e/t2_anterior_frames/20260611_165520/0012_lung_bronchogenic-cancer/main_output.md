# 0012_lung_bronchogenic-cancer

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames (Frames 1 through 4), the following observations are made:

## B-lines Assessment

**lung_rockets = true**
**ground_glass**

**Description:** In all frames, there is a dense presence of hyperechoic, vertical streaks originating from the pleural line and extending deep into the lung field. These artifacts are confluent and merge into a diffuse, bright, white sheet, indicating severe alveolar filling or interstitial edema. The lack of distinct spaces between these bright artifacts classifies the pattern as ground-glass.

## Consolidation Assessment

**consolidation = true**
**consolidation_type = "hepatization"**

**Description:** The lung parenchyma displays a high degree of density and uniform echogenicity. The tissue has lost its normal aeration and appears solid and uniformly bright (echogenic), resembling the texture and appearance of the liver (hepatized lung). There is no clear evidence of air bronchograms or a distinct shredded border, but the overwhelming evidence points to diffuse alveolar consolidation.

### Summary of Findings

The analysis across all four frames consistently reveals **ground-glass** B-lines and evidence of diffuse parenchymal **consolidation** characterized by a **hepatization** pattern.
