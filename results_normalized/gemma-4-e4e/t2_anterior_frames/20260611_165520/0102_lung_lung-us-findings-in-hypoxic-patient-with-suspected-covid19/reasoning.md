**General LUS Principles for the analysis:**
1.  **B-lines (Lung Rockets):** These are hyperechoic vertical lines that extend from the pleura to the rib cage/bottom of the screen, indicating airless alveoli (interstitial edema).
    *   **Septal:** Discrete, well-separated B-lines.
    *   **Ground Glass:** Coalescing/confluent B-lines, obscuring underlying architecture.
    *   **Mixed:** Both patterns present.
2.  **Consolidation:** This indicates filling of alveoli with fluid/cells, losing aeration.
    *   **Hepatization:** Solid, liver-like texture, loss of lung sliding/aeration pattern.
    *   **Air Bronchograms:** Visible air-filled bronchi within the consolidated (opacified) area.
    *   **Shred Sign:** Irregular boundary between consolidated and aerated lung.
