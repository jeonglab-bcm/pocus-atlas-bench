# 0022_lung_air-bronchograms2

## Lung Ultrasound Analysis

### B-lines Assessment

**Observation:**
The images consistently show numerous hyperechoic, vertical artifacts extending from the pleural line into the lung fields. While discrete B-lines are initially visible, the most prominent feature across the sequential frames is that these B-lines are **confluent and coalescing**. They merge into a dense, diffuse, white sheet that uniformly obscures the underlying lung parenchyma and any potential A-lines.

**Conclusion:**
B-lines are present. They are classified as a **ground_glass** pattern.

---

### Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:**
The lung fields throughout the sequential frames appear highly dense and homogeneous. The normal black/dark appearance associated with air-filled lung is absent. The texture of the consolidated lung is uniform, bright, and dense, giving it a solid, tissue-like echogenicity. There are no clearly defined, irregular "shred" borders visible between aerated and consolidated regions, nor are there distinct punctate hyperechoic foci typical of air bronchograms in this view.

**Conclusion:**
Consolidation is present. The predominant type of consolidation is **hepatization**.
