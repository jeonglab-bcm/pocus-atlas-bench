# 0027_lung_subpleural-consolidation

## B-lines Assessment

The images show numerous vertical, hyperechoic artifacts extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen across all frames. These artifacts meet the definition of B-lines (lung rockets).

**B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**.

**Classification:**
The B-lines are highly frequent and closely spaced. While they are not perfectly discrete, their high density and the fact that they appear to be merging into a diffuse, bright field throughout the visualized zone suggests a confluent pattern. This pattern is characteristic of diffuse alveolar filling or alveolar edema.

**Type:** **ground_glass**

---

## Consolidation Assessment

Upon close inspection of all frames, the lung parenchyma does not demonstrate the signs of alveolar consolidation:

*   The lung field is not uniformly solid or echogenic in a way that mimics liver tissue (no hepatization).
*   No irregular, shredded border is visible between aerated and consolidated lung.
*   There are no visible dark, air-filled bronchi within a bright, solid area (no air bronchograms).

The overall pattern is consistent with diffuse interstitial/alveolar fluid (ground glass opacification visualized via B-lines), not tissue-like alveolar collapse or consolidation.

**consolidation = false**
**consolidation_type = null**

---

## Conclusion

**B-line Summary:** B-lines are present and are classified as **ground_glass**, indicating a diffuse interstitial or alveolar process, most commonly pulmonary edema.
**Consolidation Summary:** No evidence of alveolar consolidation is identified in the imaged zone.

**Overall Assessment:** Diffuse ground glass pattern (likely representing pulmonary edema).
