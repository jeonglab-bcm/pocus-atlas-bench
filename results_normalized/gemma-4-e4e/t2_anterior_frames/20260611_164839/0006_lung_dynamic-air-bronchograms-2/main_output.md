# 0006_lung_dynamic-air-bronchograms-2

**B-lines Assessment:**
The image consistently shows numerous, overlapping, hyperechoic vertical lines (B-lines) that originate from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen. These lines are not discrete and spaced (septal); instead, they are confluent and merge into a diffuse, bright white sheet that completely obscures the normal A-line pattern.

*   **Result:** B-lines are present, and the pattern is **ground_glass**.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The lung parenchyma exhibits an overall solid, dense, and highly echogenic appearance. The texture is not that of normally aerated lung tissue but resembles the dense, uniform texture of solid organ tissue (hepatization). The lack of distinct normal aeration and the uniformly bright, opaque appearance confirm widespread alveolar involvement.

*   **Result:** Consolidation is present, and the predominant type is **hepatization**.

**Conclusion:**

*   **lung_rockets = true**
