# 0008_lung_subpleural-consolidation-covid

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames, here are the observations and conclusions:

### B-lines Assessment

**B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**: Yes.
**Subtype: ground_glass**

**Observation:** In all six frames, there is a prominent presence of hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising immediately from the pleural line. These artifacts are confluent and merge into a dense, diffuse white sheet, completely obscuring the underlying A-lines (the horizontal reverberation artifacts). This appearance is characteristic of **ground glass** pattern.

### Consolidation Assessment

**Consolidation = false**

**Observation:** While the lung parenchyma is markedly affected by widespread opacities, there is no clear evidence of alveolar consolidation. Specifically:
*   No **tissue-like hepatization** (solid, liver-like echogenicity) is observed.
*   No **air bronchograms** (punctate or linear hyperechoic foci within an otherwise echogenic solid lung) are visible.
*   The **shred sign** (irregular, shredded border between consolidated and aerated tissue) is not evident.

### Summary and Conclusion

The sequential lung ultrasound frames demonstrate widespread interstitial/alveolar pathology characterized by prominent, confluent, and merging vertical artifacts.

**Final Classification:**
*   **lung_rockets = true**
*   **B-lines_subtype = "ground_glass"**
*   **consolidation = false**
