# 0130_lung_jr_pedsptxx

## B-lines Assessment

**B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**

The images show a widespread pattern of hyperechoic vertical artifacts starting at the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the frame. These artifacts are **confluent** and merge into a dense, uniform white sheet, obscuring the visibility of normal A-lines (horizontal reverberations).

*   **Subtype:** ground_glass
*   **Justification:** The presence of coalescing B-lines that merge into a diffuse sheet is characteristic of ground-glass opacities, indicating alveolar or interstitial edema.

## Consolidation Assessment

**consolidation = true**

The lung parenchyma exhibits a highly echogenic, solid appearance, lacking the normal, dark aeration expected in healthy lung tissue.

*   **Predominant Type:** hepatization
*   **Justification:** The lung tissue appears uniformly bright and dense, resembling the texture and echogenicity of solid tissue (like the liver) rather than air-filled lung. There is no clear demonstration of a distinct shred sign or air bronchograms.

### Conclusion

*   **B-lines:** lung_rockets = **true**
*   **B-line subtype:** ground_glass
*   **Consolidation:** consolidation = **true**
*   **Consolidation type:** hepatization

**Overall Description:** The lung ultrasound shows diffuse, confluent B-lines consistent with a ground-glass pattern, coupled with overall lung density indicative of tissue-like hepatization. This pattern strongly suggests diffuse alveolar damage or significant pulmonary edema.
