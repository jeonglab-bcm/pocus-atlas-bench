# 0001_lung_lung-cavitary-lesion

## B-lines Assessment

**B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**.
The images consistently show multiple, dense, vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending deep into the field. These artifacts are confluent (merging into a diffuse white sheet) and obscure the definition of the underlying lung tissue.

**B-lines Classification:**
**ground_glass**. The B-lines are numerous and coalescing into a diffuse opacification, which is characteristic of ground-glass change.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Consolidation = true**.
The lung parenchyma appears significantly abnormal. The overall echogenicity is high and diffuse, lacking the typical dark (anechoic/black) areas of normal air-filled lung.

**Consolidation Classification:**
**hepatization**. The diffuse, solid-appearing, uniformly bright echogenicity of the parenchymal tissue gives it a solid, liver-like (hepatized) texture, indicating widespread alveolar filling. Air bronchograms are not clearly identifiable due to the widespread consolidation, and a defined shred sign is not the dominant feature.
