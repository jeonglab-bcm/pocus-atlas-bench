# 0117_lung_jr_normal-lung-sliding-during-fast-exam

## B-lines Assessment

Based on the analysis of all frames (1 through 6), there is a pervasive presence of numerous, highly reflective vertical artifacts. These artifacts are not discrete but instead coalesce and merge across the ultrasound field, obscuring the normal horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines).

*   **B-lines present (lung\_rockets = true)**
*   **B-lines subtype:** ground\_glass (The B-lines are confluent and form a diffuse white sheet, indicating alveolar edema/diffuse interstitial disease, rather than discrete septal thickening).

## Consolidation Assessment

The lung parenchyma throughout the sequence appears significantly affected. The normally air-filled, dark regions of the lung are replaced by a uniformly bright, solid-appearing texture.

*   **consolidation = true**
*   **consolidation\_type:** hepatization (The parenchyma exhibits a dense, uniform, tissue-like echogenicity, resembling the texture of the liver, indicating diffuse alveolar filling).

***

## Summary of Observations

**B-lines:** The images show extensive **ground-glass** B-lines. The artifacts are numerous and confluent, filling the entire field of view.
**Consolidation:** The images show evidence of **hepatization**. The lung tissue is uniformly echogenic and solid, suggesting widespread filling of the alveoli with fluid or exudate.
