**Consolidation Assessment**

The lung parenchyma shows diffuse interstitial opacities represented by the B-lines, which is characteristic of interstitial disease (e.g., pulmonary edema). However, there is no evidence that the alveoli themselves have filled with fluid to create a solid, tissue-like appearance (hepatization), nor are there clear signs of air within bronchi surrounded by consolidated lung (air bronchograms). The primary abnormality is interstitial/interlobular.
