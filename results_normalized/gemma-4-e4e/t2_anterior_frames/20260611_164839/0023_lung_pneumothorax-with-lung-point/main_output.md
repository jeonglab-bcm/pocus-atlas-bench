# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

## B-lines Assessment

**Analysis:**
In all five frames, there are distinct, hyperechoic, vertical artifacts extending from the pleural line deep into the lung parenchyma. These artifacts are well-defined and do not merge or coalesce into a solid white sheet.

**Conclusion:**
*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**
*   **B-line subtype:** **septal** (The lines are discrete and well-spaced, indicating thickened interlobular septal fluid/fibrosis).

## Consolidation Assessment

**Analysis:**
The lung parenchyma consistently shows the presence of A-lines (horizontal reverberation artifacts), which confirms normal aeration of the underlying alveoli. There are no areas demonstrating the dense, homogenous, tissue-like echogenicity of hepatization. No irregular, shredded borders or air-filled structures (air bronchograms) are visible.

**Conclusion:**
*   **consolidation = false**
*   **consolidation_type = null**

## Summary

The lung ultrasound demonstrates **septal B-lines** across the visualized anterior field. There is **no evidence of alveolar consolidation** or signs of hepatization, shred sign, or air bronchograms.
