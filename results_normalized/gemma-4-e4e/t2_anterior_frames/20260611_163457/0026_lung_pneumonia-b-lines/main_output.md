# 0026_lung_pneumonia-b-lines

## B-lines Assessment
The analysis of the sequential frames reveals variations in the pattern of vertical artifacts:
- **Frame 1 & 2:** Show widespread, confluent, and highly hyperechoic vertical artifacts, consistent with a **ground_glass** pattern where the lung surface appears uniformly bright, obscuring A-lines.
- **Frame 4 & 5:** Show numerous, distinct, and separated vertical hyperechoic artifacts with visible dark lung parenchyma between them, consistent with a **septal** pattern.
Since both discrete (septal) and confluent/coalescing (ground-glass) patterns are observed within the sequence, the overall classification is mixed.
