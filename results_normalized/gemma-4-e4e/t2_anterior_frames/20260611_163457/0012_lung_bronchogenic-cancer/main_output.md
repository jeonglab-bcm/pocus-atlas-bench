# 0012_lung_bronchogenic-cancer

**B-lines Assessment**
The image shows multiple, discrete, well-spaced, hyperechoic vertical artifacts (lung rockets) originating from the pleural line and extending deep into the lung parenchyma. The areas of lung tissue between these artifacts remain dark, allowing for the visualization of air. This pattern is characteristic of septal thickening.
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **subtype = "septal"**

**Consolidation Assessment**
The lung parenchyma exhibits clear air/tissue interfaces (the distinct septal B-lines). The overall texture of the lung does not resemble solid tissue (hepatization), nor is there a visible shred sign or air bronchograms within consolidated lung. The lung is clearly aerated but shows signs of interstitial disease.
- **consolidation = false**
- **consolidation_type = null**

**Conclusions:**
The ultrasound findings are dominated by **septal B-lines**, indicating pulmonary interstitial thickening or alveolar edema, but there are no signs of alveolar consolidation.
