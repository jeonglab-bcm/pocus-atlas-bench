## Conclusion

**B-lines:** B-lines are **absent** throughout the sequence. The predominant imaging feature is dense lung consolidation, not the typical vertical artifacts of alveolar edema.

**Consolidation:** Consolidation is **present** in all frames. The primary characteristics observed are:
1.  **Tissue-like hepatization:** The lung parenchyma appears uniformly dense and solid, resembling the echogenicity of the liver.
2.  **Air Bronchograms:** Numerous punctate and linear hyperechoic foci within the dense lung tissue represent air-filled bronchi.
3.  **Shred Sign:** Irregular, dense borders are observed, particularly in Frame 4, consistent with severe alveolar filling.

**Predominant Consolidation Type:** Hepatization with significant air bronchography.
