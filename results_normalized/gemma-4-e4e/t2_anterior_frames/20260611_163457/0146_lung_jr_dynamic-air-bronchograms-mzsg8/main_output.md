## Lung Ultrasound Analysis

### B-lines Assessment

**Observation:** In all five frames, the lung parenchyma exhibits multiple, discrete, highly echogenic (bright) vertical lines originating from the pleural line and extending towards the bottom of the screen. These lines are distinct and well-spaced, meaning there is dark lung tissue visible between the bright lines. The artifacts are consistent and persist throughout the sequence.

**Classification:**
*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**
*   **Subtype: septal** (The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced, indicating thickened interlobular septa.)

### Consolidation Assessment

**Observation:** The lung parenchyma is dominated by the presence of numerous B-lines. There is no area of uniform, solid, highly echogenic tissue resembling the liver (no hepatization). No irregular, dense border patterns (shred sign) are visible. Furthermore, there are no obvious air-filled bronchi visible within a dense, consolidated area (no air bronchograms). The lung pattern is consistent with diffuse interstitial disease or pulmonary edema, rather than alveolar consolidation.

**Classification:**
*   **consolidation = false**
*   **consolidation_type = null**
