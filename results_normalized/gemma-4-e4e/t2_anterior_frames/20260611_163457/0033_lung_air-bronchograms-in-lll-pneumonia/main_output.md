# 0033_lung_air-bronchograms-in-lll-pneumonia

## Analysis of Lung Ultrasound (LUS) Frames

### B-lines Assessment

**Assessment:**
The images show multiple, well-defined, hyperechoic vertical artifacts (lung rockets) originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are discrete and maintain clear space between them throughout the visible parenchyma, rather than merging into a continuous white sheet.

**Conclusion:**
*   **B-lines present (lung\_rockets = true)**
*   **Subtype: septal** (The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced, consistent with thickened interlobular septa.)

### Consolidation Assessment

**Assessment:**
The lung parenchyma displayed in these frames appears extremely dense, highly echogenic, and homogeneous. It lacks the normal "speckled" appearance of an aerated lung. The overall texture of the parenchyma is consistent with solid, non-aerated tissue, which is characteristic of hepatization (lung appearing solid and uniform, similar to the texture of the liver). There is no distinct "shred sign" visible, and while the density is high, the overwhelming impression is one of diffuse, dense, solid parenchymal filling.

**Conclusion:**
*   **consolidation = true**
*   **consolidation\_type = "hepatization"** (The parenchyma displays a solid, dense, liver-like echogenicity.)
