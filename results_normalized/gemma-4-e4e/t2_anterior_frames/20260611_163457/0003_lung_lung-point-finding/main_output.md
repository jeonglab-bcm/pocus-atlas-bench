# 0003_lung_lung-point-finding

**B-lines Assessment**

**Observations:**
Across all five frames, numerous hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) are observed originating from the pleural line and extending down the scan. These lines are discrete and distinct; the dark, aerated lung parenchyma remains visible between individual lines. They have not coalesced into a confluent white sheet that would obscure the normal lung texture.

**Classification:**
*   **lung_rockets = true**
*   **Classification:** **septal** (The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced, indicating thickening of the interlobular septa.)

**Consolidation Assessment**

**Observations:**
The lung parenchyma shows a pattern of diffuse interstitial thickening (evidenced by the B-lines) but does not exhibit the characteristic density of consolidation. There is no area where the lung tissue appears solid, uniform, or resembles liver tissue (hepatization). No irregular shredded borders or internal air bronchograms are visible within the visualized lung fields.

**Classification:**
*   **consolidation = false**
*   **consolidation_type = null**

***

**Summary of Findings:**
The ultrasound demonstrates a pattern of **septal B-lines**, indicative of thickening of the interlobular septa, consistent with interstitial lung process (such as pulmonary edema or interstitial disease). There is **no evidence of alveolar consolidation** in the anterior lung zone.
