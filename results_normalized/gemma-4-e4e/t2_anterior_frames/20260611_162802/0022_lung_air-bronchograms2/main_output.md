## B-lines Assessment

**Assessment:**
B-lines are **present (lung_rockets = true)** in all frames.

**Classification:**
The B-lines are classified as **mixed**.
*   **Initial Frames (1-2):** Discrete, well-spaced B-lines are visible, fitting a septal pattern (interstitial thickening).

Since both discrete (septal) and confluent (ground-glass) patterns are clearly visible throughout the sequence, the overall pattern is classified as mixed.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Rationale:**
While the overall lung parenchyma appears significantly hyper-echogenic (increased whiteness) due to the overwhelming presence of confluent B-lines (a sign of edema/interstitial disease), the criteria for definite alveolar consolidation (like pneumonia) are not met. Specifically:
*   No distinct air bronchograms (air-filled bronchi visible within the opacified tissue) can be identified.
*   The increased echogenicity is best attributed to the dense collection of vertical artifacts (B-lines) rather than a true, solid, liver-like texture (hepatization) independent of the artifacts.
*   There are no clearly definable "shred signs" marking a transition between aerated and consolidated zones.
