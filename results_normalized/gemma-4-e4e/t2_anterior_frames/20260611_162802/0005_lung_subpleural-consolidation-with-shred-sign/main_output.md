**Consolidation Summary:** Consolidation is first absent and interstitial, but quickly progresses to be present in the subsequent frames. The dominant pattern observed is a **hepatization** (tissue-like/solid appearance), indicating significant alveolar filling across the zone.

**Overall Clinical Impression:** The sequential frames depict a rapid transition from early interstitial lung disease (septal B-lines) to diffuse alveolar filling (consolidation/hepatization), consistent with an acute, widespread process such as severe pulmonary edema or diffuse pneumonia.
