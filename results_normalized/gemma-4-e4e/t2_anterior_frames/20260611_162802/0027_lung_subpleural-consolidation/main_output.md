## Final Assessment

### B-lines Assessment
*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**
*   **B-line Classification: ground_glass**
    *   *Justification:* In all four frames, the lung parenchyma is filled with dense, confluent vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) that merge into a diffuse white sheet, obscuring the normal lung appearance and indicating diffuse alveolar or interstitial disease (pulmonary edema/diffuse interstitial syndrome).

### Consolidation Assessment
*   **Consolidation = false**
    *   *Justification:* While B-lines indicate increased lung pathology (edema/interstitial thickening), there is no evidence of airspace filling (consolidation). The lung does not exhibit the tissue-like echogenicity (hepatization), irregular boundaries (shred sign), or internal air-filled tubes (air bronchograms) associated with true alveolar consolidation.
