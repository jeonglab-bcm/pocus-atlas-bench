# 0006_lung_dynamic-air-bronchograms-2

## Analysis

### B-lines Assessment
Across all four frames, distinct, bright, hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) are visible originating from the pleural line and extending deep into the lung parenchyma. These artifacts are separate and do not merge into a continuous white sheet. This pattern indicates thickening of the interlobular septae.

**Assessment:**
- **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**

**B-line Subtype:**
The B-lines are discrete and well-spaced, meaning the normal (though thickened) dark lung parenchyma is visible between the streaks.
- **septal**

### Consolidation Assessment
The lung parenchyma exhibits a solid, dense, and highly echogenic appearance. It has a coarse, granular, and uniform texture that mimics the echogenicity of liver tissue when viewed on an abdominal ultrasound. The normal airy, black appearance of an aerated lung is completely absent. While specific air bronchograms are not overwhelmingly clear due to the uniform density, the overall appearance is characteristic of alveolar filling (consolidation).

**Assessment:**
- **consolidation = true**

**Consolidation Type:**
The predominant feature is the solid, liver-like echogenicity.
- **consolidation_type = "hepatization"**

***

### Conclusion

*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets):** true
*   **B-line subtype:** septal
*   **Consolidation:** true
*   **Consolidation type:** "hepatization"
