## B-lines Assessment

**B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**

**B-line subtype: septal**

**Observation:**
In the deep lung regions shown in all frames, there are multiple distinct, hyperechoic, vertical artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line and extending towards the bottom of the screen. These lines are clearly separated and do not coalesce into a diffuse white sheet. The dark, anechoic lung parenchyma remains visible between these well-defined lines, consistent with a septal pattern.

## Consolidation Assessment

**Consolidation = false**

**Observation:**
There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung parenchyma does not appear as a solid, uniformly echogenic mass (hepatization). No air bronchograms are visible, and there is no evidence of a shredded or irregular border pattern typical of consolidation. The primary finding is interstitial thickening rather than alveolar filling.

## Conclusion

**B-lines:** Septal
**Consolidation:** None

**Summary of Findings:**
The lung ultrasound demonstrates multiple, discrete, and well-spaced **septal B-lines**. There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The pattern suggests an interstitial process involving the thickening of the interlobular septa.
