## Conclusion
The lung ultrasound demonstrates a progression of interstitial changes, starting with discrete septal B-lines in Frames 1 and 2, transitioning to a mixed pattern in Frame 3, and culminating in a dense, confluent ground-glass pattern by Frame 5. Despite the increased brightness and density of the lung parenchyma, there is no clear evidence of classic alveolar consolidation (such as hepatization or air bronchograms). The predominant pathology is consistent with diffuse alveolar/interlobular interstitial edema (B-lines).
