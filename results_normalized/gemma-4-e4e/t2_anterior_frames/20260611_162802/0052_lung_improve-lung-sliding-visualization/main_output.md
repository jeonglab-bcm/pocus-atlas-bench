## B-lines Assessment
**lung_rockets = true**
The ultrasound sequence clearly demonstrates the presence of B-lines (lung rockets).

The pattern progresses throughout the frames:
1.  **Initial Frames (e.g., 1-3):** Discrete vertical artifacts are visible, suggesting an early septal pattern.
2.  **Mid-sequence Frames (e.g., 4-15):** The artifacts become numerous and start to merge, appearing confluent.
3.  **Final Frames (e.g., 21-26):** The vertical artifacts have become fully confluent, creating a diffuse, uniform white appearance that completely obscures the normal deep lung texture (A-lines).

**Classification: ground_glass**
The final, most severe stage shown is a confluent pattern where B-lines merge into a dense sheet, meeting the criteria for ground-glass lung disease.

## Consolidation Assessment
**consolidation = true**
The lung parenchyma demonstrates significant loss of aeration and takes on a solid, echogenic texture.

**consolidation_type = "hepatization"**
The overall appearance of the lung tissue is highly echogenic, dense, and resembles the texture of solid liver tissue (hepatization).

## Conclusion

**B-lines Assessment:** The sequence demonstrates a progression of B-lines, starting from discrete septal patterns and rapidly evolving into a dense, **ground_glass** pattern due to confluent vertical artifacts.

**Consolidation Assessment:** Significant alveolar consolidation is present, characterized by **hepatization** (the lung parenchyma appearing solid and uniformly echogenic).
