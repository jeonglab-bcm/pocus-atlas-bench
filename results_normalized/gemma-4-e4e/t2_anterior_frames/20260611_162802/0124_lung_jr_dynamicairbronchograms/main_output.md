These artifacts are discrete and well-spaced, with dark (anechoic) lung tissue visible between them. This pattern meets the criteria for **septal B-lines**.

**Consolidation Analysis:**
The lung parenchyma shows normal aeration, as evidenced by the dark, air-filled appearance of the lung tissue between the B-lines. There is no evidence of the solid, tissue-like texture (hepatization), irregular borders (shred sign), or air-filled bronchi within dense parenchyma (air bronchograms). Therefore, **consolidation is not present**.

**Conclusion:**
The lung ultrasound reveals the presence of **septal B-lines**. The lung parenchyma is well-aerated, and there are no signs of alveolar consolidation.
