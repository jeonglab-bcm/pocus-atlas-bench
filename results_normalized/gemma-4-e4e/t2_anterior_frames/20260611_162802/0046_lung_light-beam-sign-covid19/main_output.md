## Summary and Conclusion

**B-lines Observation:** The clip demonstrates a progression of lung pathology. Initially (Frames 1 & 2), the pattern is **septal** (discrete, spaced B-lines). This progresses to a **ground_glass** pattern (Frames 3 & 4), characterized by confluent and merging B-lines forming a diffuse white sheet.

**Consolidation Observation:** Throughout the entire clip, classic signs of alveolar consolidation—such as clear air bronchograms or distinct shredded borders indicative of alveolar filling—are **absent**. The echogenicity is consistent with severe interstitial/alveolar disease (ground glass pattern), but not alveolar consolidation.

**Overall Conclusion:** The primary finding is diffuse interstitial/alveolar disease characterized by a **ground_glass** B-line pattern. There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation.
