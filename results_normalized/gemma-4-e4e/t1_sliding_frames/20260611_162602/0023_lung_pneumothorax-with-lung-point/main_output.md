# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

Across all five frames, the following observations are consistently noted:

1.  **Pleural Line Appearance:** The pleural line is consistently bright and appears dynamic rather than smooth and static. It exhibits a bright, textured appearance (shimmer).
2.  **Sub-Pleural Field:** Immediately below the pleural line, there is a prominent, granular, and speckling pattern.
3.  **Dynamic Speckle:** Between consecutive frames, this sub-pleural texture/speckle pattern shows variations and shifts in depth and orientation relative to the pleural line. It does not remain static.

**Signs of Sliding Present/Absent:**

*   **Shimmering or granular pleural line:** Present. The line is bright and dynamic.
*   **Comet-tail artifacts or B-lines:** While not perfectly delineated, the overall texture suggests the movement associated with these findings.
*   **Seashore sign on M-mode:** Likely present. The visible speckled pattern is highly characteristic of the "seashore sign" (which indicates sliding).
*   **Speckle pattern below the pleural line differs between consecutive frames:** Present. The sub-pleural texture changes across the frames.
*   **Static/Barcode signs:** Absent. The pleura and sub-pleural pattern are not static.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the observation of a shimmering/granular pleural line and the dynamic, textured (speckled) pattern below the pleura that changes across frames, **Pleural Sliding is Present.**
