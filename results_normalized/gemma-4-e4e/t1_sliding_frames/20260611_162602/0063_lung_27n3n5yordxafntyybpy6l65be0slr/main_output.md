*   **Speckle Pattern:** The sub-pleural region is active and mottled, which is consistent with the expected dynamic activity of sliding pleura.

**Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
*   The frames **do not** display a perfectly smooth, static pleural line.

**Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):**

### **Conclusion**

The presence of a speckled, granular pattern (resembling the **seashore sign**) in the sub-pleural region, combined with the lack of a smooth, uniform, static pattern (barcode/stratosphere), indicates that the pleura is moving.

**Conclusion: Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
