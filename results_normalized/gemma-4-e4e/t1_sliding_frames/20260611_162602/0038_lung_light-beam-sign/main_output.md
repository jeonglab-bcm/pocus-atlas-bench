# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

**Frame by Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frame 1:** A distinct, bright horizontal line (the pleural line) is visible at the top of the image. Below this pleural line, the ultrasound shows a dynamic, granular, speckled texture. This texture is consistent with the **"seashore sign,"** indicating that the pleural interface is moving and the sub-pleural tissue is in motion.
*   **Frame 2:** The pleural line remains clearly visible. The sub-pleural region continues to display the granular, speckle pattern characteristic of the **"seashore sign."** There is no evidence of a smooth, static, or barcode pattern.
*   **Frame 3:** The pleural line is observed, and the sub-pleural speckle pattern is dynamic and granular, confirming the **"seashore sign."**
*   **Frame 4:** The dynamic sub-pleural texture (speckled/granular) continues across the frame, consistent with the **"seashore sign."**

**Conclusion:**

Based on the observation of the granular and moving speckle pattern (seashore sign) in the sub-pleural region across all frames, the sign of **pleural sliding is present**.
