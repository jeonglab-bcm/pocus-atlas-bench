# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

This is a detailed analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) frames.

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis

*   **Observation:** All five frames (Frame 1 through Frame 5) show a consistent pattern.
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright line at the top of the image) appears smooth, sharp, and perfectly static across all frames.
*   **Sub-Pleural Field:** Below the pleural line, the ultrasound field consists of multiple parallel horizontal lines (A-lines).
*   **Motion/Texture:** There is no visible shimmering, granular movement, or variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between any of the frames. The A-lines do not appear to "move" or change their structure.

### Assessment of Sliding Signs

*   **Shimmering/Granular Line:** Absent. The line is smooth and fixed.
*   **Comet-tail/B-lines:** Not present (or not visible in a dynamic way).
*   **Seashore Sign:** Absent. The pattern is clearly defined by static horizontal lines (A-lines), not a granular "sandy" pattern.
*   **Speckle Variation:** Absent. The sub-pleural speckle and pattern are identical across all frames.
*   **Static/Smooth Pleural Line:** Present. The line remains perfectly still.
*   **Stratosphere/Barcode Sign:** Present. The pattern consists entirely of horizontal A-lines.
*   **Static A-lines:** Present. The A-lines are fixed and unchanging between frames.

### Conclusion

Based on the clinical criteria provided:

**Pleural Sliding is ABSENT.**

The presence of a smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line and a field composed of stable horizontal A-lines (Stratosphere/Barcode sign) indicates that no movement or vibration of the lung surface is being detected during the capture of these frames.
