# 0130_lung_jr_pedsptxx

This sequence of images demonstrates the following sonographic findings:

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis

*   **Frame 1:** The pleural line is visible near the superior border of the lung field. Immediately below the pleural line, the lung parenchyma exhibits a granular, highly textured pattern, rather than a uniform black (anechoic) appearance.
*   **Frame 2:** The granular, speckled pattern below the pleural line persists, appearing dynamic and "busy."
*   **Frame 3:** The sub-pleural field continues to display the textured, non-static pattern, consistent with movement.
*   **Frame 4:** The granular speckling is maintained throughout the observed field, indicating a consistent pattern of movement relative to the transducer.

### Assessment of Pleural Sliding

**Signs Observed (Sliding Present):**
*   **Seashore Sign:** The presence of a speckled, granular pattern immediately below the pleural line across all four frames is highly indicative of the seashore sign, which signifies the movement of the visceral and parietal pleura over the underlying lung tissue.
*   **Speckle Pattern Variation:** The sub-pleural field is not a static, uniform black space. The texture is clearly active and varied across the field.

**Signs Observed (Sliding Absent):**
*   No signs of sliding absence (such as a static, smooth pleural line, or a barcode/stratosphere sign) are present.

### Conclusion

Based on the continuous granular texture and the consistent appearance of the **Seashore sign** throughout the observed sequence, the conclusion is that **pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
