### Evaluation Against Sliding Criteria

Based on the visual evidence, the pleural sliding is **absent**. The persistent, smooth, and static appearance of the pleural line across all observed frames indicates that the lung surfaces are fixed relative to each other, consistent with conditions such as pneumothorax or lung collapse.
