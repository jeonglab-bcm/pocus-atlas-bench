### **Frame-by-Frame Analysis**

The sequence of frames (2/270 through 26/270) all depict the lung interface (the pleural line) and the sub-pleural region.

*   **Pleural Line:** The interface between the pleura and the lung tissue is consistently visible as a bright, horizontal line.

*   **Dynamic Observation:** Across the sequential frames, there is evident variation in the sub-pleural speckle. The pattern does not remain rigidly fixed and unchanging; the "sand" below the pleural line appears to shift or shimmer, which is the key characteristic of lung motion.

### **Assessment of Sliding Signs**

*   *Observation:* **Not present.** The sub-pleural region is not static black with clean, uniform horizontal A-lines.
*   *Conclusion:* The pattern is too "busy" to be a simple barcode sign.

*   *Observation:* **Present.** The sub-pleural area exhibits a granular, dynamic, "sandy" texture, consistent with the **Seashore Sign**. This indicates the presence of lung motion and the interaction of the pleural surfaces.
*   *Conclusion:* The shifting speckle and granular texture confirm dynamic pleural sliding.

**3. Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):**
*   *Observation:* **Not present.** The entire field captured in these frames shows the granular, dynamic pattern, and there is no clear transition point to a static, black barcode region.

### **Final Conclusion**

**Pleural sliding is present.** 

The presence of the **Seashore Sign**—the granular, shimmering, and dynamic sub-pleural pattern—is conclusive evidence that the pleura are sliding against each other, indicating a normally functioning lung interface.
