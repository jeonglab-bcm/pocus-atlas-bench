*   **Frame 1 (Top):** The pleural line is visible as a bright interface. The sub-pleural region displays a speckled, granular pattern, which is characteristic of a "Seashore sign." There are no signs of a perfectly static, smooth line (Stratosphere/Barcode sign).
*   **Frame 2:** The pleural line and the granular sub-pleural pattern remain consistent with the previous frame. The texture suggests movement and the presence of air-filled lung tissue.
*   **Frame 3:** The image maintains the established pattern: a bright, textured pleural line overlying a granular (seashore) pattern. There is no evidence of a static, perfectly horizontal barcode.
*   **Frame 4 (Bottom):** All features from the preceding frames are maintained. The pleural line remains textured/shimmering, and the sub-pleural region displays the characteristic granular speckling (Seashore sign).

**Conclusion:**

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

**Reasoning:**
The frames consistently display the **Seashore Sign** (granular/speckled pattern below the pleural line) and the pleural line itself appears bright and textured (suggestive of shimmering/movement) rather than perfectly smooth and static (Stratosphere/Barcode sign). This indicates the movement of the lung parenchyma across the chest wall.
