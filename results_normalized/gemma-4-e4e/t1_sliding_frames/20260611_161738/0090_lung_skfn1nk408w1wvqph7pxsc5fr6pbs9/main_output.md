**Justification:**
The key finding is the presence of the **Seashore Sign**. The absence of the "Stratosphere" or "Barcode" sign (which would appear as only perfectly horizontal, static lines) confirms that the lung is not consolidated or pneumothorax has not occurred, and movement is occurring across the pleural interface.
