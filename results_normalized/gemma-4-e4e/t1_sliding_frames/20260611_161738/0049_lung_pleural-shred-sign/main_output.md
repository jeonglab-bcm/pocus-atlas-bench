# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the sequential analysis of the provided frames, here is the detailed observation and conclusion:

### Analysis of Frames

1.  **Pleural Line Appearance:** In all four frames, the interface between the air (above) and the lung parenchyma (below) is clearly visible. The pleural line itself appears bright and textured, rather than smooth and perfectly static.
2.  **Sub-Pleural Speckle Pattern:** Below the pleural line, the lung parenchyma exhibits a characteristic granular, shimmering, or "speckled" pattern. This pattern is consistent across the frames, demonstrating movement and variation in the sub-pleural tissue.
3.  **Absence of Barcode/Static Signs:** There is no evidence of a smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line (which would suggest the "barcode" sign). There are no static, unchanging A-lines.

### Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

**Reasoning:** The ultrasound images demonstrate the **"seashore sign"**, characterized by the shimmering/granular appearance of the pleural line and the dynamic, speckled nature of the sub-pleural tissue. This indicates that the pleura is vibrating and sliding across the chest wall during respiration.
