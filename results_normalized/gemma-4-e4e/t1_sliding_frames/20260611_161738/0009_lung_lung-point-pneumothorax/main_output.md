# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

This is an analysis of a sequence of lung ultrasound frames (LUS).

## Frame-by-Frame Analysis:

**Frame 1:**
*   The pleural line is visible.
*   The pattern immediately below the pleural line is not a sharp, static horizontal line (Barcode). Instead, it appears textured, speckled, and granular, consistent with movement. This is indicative of the **Seashore sign**.

**Frame 2:**
*   The pleural line remains visible.
*   The granular, speckled texture below the pleural line persists. The pattern maintains its granular nature across this frame, confirming sub-pleural motion.

**Frame 3:**
*   The pleural line is visible.
*   The pattern beneath the pleural line is clearly shimmering/speckled. The sub-pleural tissue is not static; it exhibits the characteristics of the Seashore sign, signifying active movement of the pleural interface.

**Frame 4:**
*   The pleural line remains visible.
*   The granular, moving pattern is maintained. There are no static horizontal lines present; the speckled quality persists.

**Frame 5:**
*   The pleural line is visible.
*   The speckled, granular pattern is consistent with the previous frames.

---

## Conclusion on Pleural Sliding:

**Assessment:** Pleural sliding is **present**.

**Justification:**
In all frames, the ultrasound displays a granular, speckled pattern (often referred to as the "Seashore sign" or granular pleural line) beneath the pleural interface, rather than a smooth, perfectly static horizontal line (Barcode sign). The lack of a static barcode pattern and the persistent shimmering/speckled quality across the entire sequence indicate that the pleura is moving against the chest wall during the ultrasound capture, which is the sonographic definition of pleural sliding.
