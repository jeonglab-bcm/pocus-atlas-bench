# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
All frames presented in the sequence are virtually identical, showing a single static image. The key features observed are:
1.  **Pleural Line:** A bright, hyperechoic horizontal line is visible at the top of the image.
2.  **Sub-Pleural Pattern:** Below this line, the tissue shows a **textured, grainy, or "sandy" speckle pattern**.
3.  **Artifact Pattern:** This granular pattern strongly resembles the "seashore sign," where the speckle pattern below the pleural line indicates the normal movement and sliding of lung tissue.

**Assessment of Pleural Sliding:**

*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** The presence of a grainy/speckled sub-pleural pattern (consistent with the seashore sign) suggests that the lung is moving and sliding normally.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** There is no evidence of a smooth, sharply defined, static line, nor is there the clear "barcode" or "stratosphere" pattern that would definitively indicate a loss of sliding (pneumothorax).
*   **Signs of BOTH:** No transition points or alternating patterns are evident, as the image is uniform.

**Conclusion:**
The ultrasound evidence, specifically the presence of the textured, granular sub-pleural pattern (seashore sign), indicates that **pleural sliding is PRESENT**.
