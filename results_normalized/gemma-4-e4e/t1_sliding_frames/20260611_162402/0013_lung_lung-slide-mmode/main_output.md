# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

**Detailed Analysis of Sequential Frames:**

**Observation:**

1.  **Pleural Line:** In every frame, the pleural line (the bright horizontal boundary at the top) appears sharp, smooth, and static. It does not show any signs of granular texture or shimmering (which would indicate movement/sliding).
2.  **Sub-Pleural Pattern:** The area immediately below the pleural line is characterized by a series of perfectly regular, equidistant, and static horizontal echoes (known as A-lines).
3.  **Dynamic Assessment:** Across all nine frames, this A-line pattern remains entirely unchanged. There is no dynamic speckle variation, and there are no B-lines (comet-tail artifacts) originating from the pleural interface that would indicate air/lung tissue movement across the pleura.

**Applying the Diagnostic Criteria:**

*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** Absent. (No shimmering, no B-lines, no "seashore" pattern, no variation in speckle.)
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** Present. (The pleural line is static, and the underlying pattern is a "Barcode" or "Stratosphere" sign characterized by perfectly static A-lines.)
*   **Signs of BOTH:** Absent. (There is no transition or alternating pattern.)

**Conclusion:**

Based on the consistent and static nature of the pleural line and the underlying lung pattern, **pleural sliding is absent.** The findings are consistent with the **Barcode/Stratosphere sign**.
