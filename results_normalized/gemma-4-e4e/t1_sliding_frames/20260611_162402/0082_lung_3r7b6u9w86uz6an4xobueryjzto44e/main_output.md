### 2. Assessment of Pleural Sliding

**Signs of Sliding Present:**
*   **Granular Pleural Line/Texture:** The top interface is not a perfectly smooth, static line.
*   **Speckle Pattern Below Pleural Line:** The pattern is complex, grainy, and dynamic (moving and changing between frames).
*   **Seashore Sign:** The overall appearance of the subpleural region (the bright, dynamic, speckled texture) strongly aligns with the "seashore sign." This indicates normal lung air and fluid motion.

**Signs of Sliding Absent:**
*   **Smooth/Static Pleura:** The pleural line is not smooth or static, ruling out complete collapse or severe pathology causing stasis.
*   **A-lines:** The pattern is not composed of perfectly static horizontal lines, ruling out a simple barcode/stratum sign.

**Signs of Both (Lung Point):**
*   There is no visible transition point where the dynamic movement switches abruptly to a completely static, barcode pattern. The entire field observed is consistent with movement.

---

### 3. Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is present.**

The ultrasound images demonstrate the **Seashore Sign**, characterized by a visible, dynamic, and granular pattern (speckle) beneath the pleural interface, which confirms the presence of normal lung air and fluid motion (pleural sliding).
