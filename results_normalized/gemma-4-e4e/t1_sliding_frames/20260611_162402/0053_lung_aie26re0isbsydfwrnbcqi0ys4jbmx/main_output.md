**Observation and Analysis:**

In all four frames, the pleural line is visible as a bright, distinct interface separating the superficial (pleural) layer from the lung tissue (parenchyma) below.

The sub-pleural area (the region immediately beneath the pleural line) exhibits a heterogeneous, granular, and speckled pattern. This granular texture is consistent across all consecutive frames, indicating a dynamic interaction rather than a static, fixed interface.

**Applying the Criteria:**

1.  **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** The presence of a visible granular/speckled texture below the pleural line aligns with the description of the "Shimmering or granular pleural line" or the "Seashore sign" (representing normal lung movement and scatter).
2.  **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** There are no smooth, sharply defined, static pleural lines, nor are there the perfect horizontal lines (A-lines) required to indicate a fixed pleura.
3.  **Signs of BOTH:** There is no clear visual evidence of an alternating boundary or transition between a fixed and moving segment of the lung. The entire visible area displays characteristics of dynamic movement.

**Conclusion:**

Pleural sliding is **present**. The persistent, granular/speckled pattern immediately below the pleural line indicates normal dynamic movement of the lung tissue relative to the chest wall.
