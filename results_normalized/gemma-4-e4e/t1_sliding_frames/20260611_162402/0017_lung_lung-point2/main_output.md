# 0017_lung_lung-point2

This analysis examines four sequential frames from a lung ultrasound (LUS) to determine the presence or absence of pleural sliding.

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

*   **Frame 1:** The pleural line (the bright interface at the top of the image) appears sharp, smooth, and static. The sub-pleural region in M-mode displays a highly organized, parallel pattern (A-lines), which is characteristic of a stable, non-sliding interface.
*   **Frame 2:** The appearance is consistent with Frame 1. The pleural line remains smooth and fixed. There is no visible shimmering or change in the speckle pattern below the line compared to the previous frame.
*   **Frame 3:** The pleural line remains sharp and static. The M-mode pattern shows stable A-lines, indicating no movement of the lung surface relative to the chest wall.
*   **Frame 4:** The pleural line is unchanging and smooth. The pattern below the line is stable, confirming the lack of dynamic change across the sequence.

**Assessment:**

*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** There is no shimmering, granular line, nor are there any comet-tail artifacts or changes in the sub-pleural speckle indicative of movement.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The pleural line is consistently smooth, sharply defined, and appears static across all frames. The M-mode pattern exhibits stable A-lines/static speckle, which is characteristic of the "Stratosphere" or "Barcode" sign.
*   **Signs of BOTH:** There is no visible transition point or alternating pattern.

**Conclusion:**
Based on the static nature of the pleural line and the stable, organized pattern observed in the M-mode across all four frames, **pleural sliding is Absent.**
