*   **Frame 2:** The bright pleural line remains visible. The sub-pleural speckle pattern shows noticeable variation compared to Frame 1, confirming the presence of movement at the pleural interface.

*   **Frame 4:** The pleural line is clearly visualized. The sub-pleural speckle pattern exhibits distinct differences in texture and intensity compared to Frame 3, indicating continuous movement of the pleura.

**Conclusion:**

**Pleural sliding is present.**

The presence of a bright, visible pleural line coupled with the dynamic changes in the sub-pleural speckle pattern (i.e., the texture and appearance vary significantly between consecutive frames) confirms that the pleura is moving against the chest wall during respiration, which is the definition of pleural sliding.
