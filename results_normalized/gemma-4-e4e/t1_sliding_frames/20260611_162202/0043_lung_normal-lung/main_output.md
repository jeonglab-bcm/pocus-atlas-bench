*   **Frame 2:** Comparing Frame 2 to Frame 1, the sub-pleural texture remains dynamic and granular. There is no sign of a smooth, static line or perfectly horizontal A-lines across the entire field.
*   **Frame 3:** The granular/speckled pattern persists, indicating active movement or interaction at the pleural surface.
*   **Frame 4:** The texture remains active and textured, characteristic of dynamic sub-pleural echoes.
*   **Frame 5:** The persistent granular pattern confirms the dynamic nature of the interface.

**Conclusion:**

**Pleural sliding is present.**

**Justification:**
The primary evidence is the presence of a **seashore sign**—the sub-pleural area is characterized by a dynamic, granular, and speckled pattern rather than a smooth, static line. The persistent variation and "snow-like" texture in the sub-pleural tissue across all five frames indicate that the lung is moving against the chest wall, which is the definition of pleural sliding.
