***

### Frame-by-Frame Observation

**Frame 1:**
*   **Pleural Line:** Visible, bright, and well-defined.
*   **Sub-pleural pattern:** The pattern immediately below the pleura is highly regular, repetitive, and organized. This structure is consistent with **A-lines** (the characteristic "clean" lung pattern).
*   **Sliding Sign:** No clear shimmering or dynamic movement is visible.

**Frame 2:**
*   **Pleural Line:** Stable.
*   **Sub-pleural pattern:** The A-line pattern persists and appears identical to the pattern in Frame 1.
*   **Sliding Sign:** There is no variation or change in the sub-pleural speckle across frames.

**Frame 3:**
*   **Pleural Line:** Stable.
*   **Sub-pleural pattern:** The A-line pattern remains highly organized and static.
*   **Sliding Sign:** No dynamic movement or change is observed.

**Frame 4:**
*   **Pleural Line:** Stable.
*   **Sub-pleural pattern:** The A-line pattern maintains its perfect regularity and static appearance.
*   **Sliding Sign:** Consistent absence of change in the sub-pleural pattern.

### Conclusion Regarding Pleural Sliding

**Assessment:** Pleural sliding is **ABSENT**.

**Justification:**
The primary indicator of absent sliding is the persistent and static nature of the lung pattern below the pleural line. In these frames, the sub-pleural pattern is consistently and uniformly **A-lines**. A-lines are static and repetitive, which is the classic ultrasound signature of no pleural friction or movement (i.e., the pleural layers are stuck together).

*   **Signs of Sliding Absent:** The pattern below the pleura (A-lines) remains perfectly static and unchanged across all four frames, and there is no evidence of the sub-pleural speckle changing or shimmering.
