### Conclusion

**Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**

**Reasoning:**
Across all frames, the sub-pleural zone (the area immediately below the bright pleural line) exhibits a persistent **granular or shimmering speckle pattern**. This appearance is known as the **Seashore Sign** on ultrasound. The presence of this texture and the dynamic nature of the interface rule out the "Stratosphere" or "Barcode" sign (which would indicate absent sliding/pneumothorax). The consistent granular pattern confirms the movement of the pleura relative to the chest wall.
