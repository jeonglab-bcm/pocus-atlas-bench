### Conclusion

**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

**Reasoning:** The ultrasound sequence consistently demonstrates a granular, dynamic speckle pattern in the sub-pleural region, which is known as the **Seashore sign**. The absence of a smooth, static pleural line and the presence of this dynamic texture confirm that the pleura is moving in sync with respiratory efforts (sliding).
