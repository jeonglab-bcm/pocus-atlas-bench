**Assessment against the Criteria:**

*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** Absent. There is no shimmering, no granular pleural line, and no B-lines are visible.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** Present. The pleura is static, and the sub-pleural pattern is unchanging, matching the definition of a static, horizontal "barcode" pattern.
*   **Signs of BOTH:** Absent. There is no transition or alternating pattern visible.

**Conclusion:**
Based on the lack of dynamic movement (shimmer, B-lines, or speckle variation) and the presence of a static, horizontal sub-pleural pattern, **Pleural Sliding is ABSENT.**
