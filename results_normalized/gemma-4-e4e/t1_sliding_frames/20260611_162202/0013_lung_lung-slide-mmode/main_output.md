# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound frames, the findings are as follows:

### Frame-by-Frame Observation

1.  **Pleural Line Characteristics:** In all frames, the pleural line (the bright horizontal interface at the top) appears smooth, sharp, and consistent. It does not exhibit any granular or shimmering characteristics.
2.  **Sub-Pleural Pattern:** The area immediately below the pleural line (the sub-pleural region) displays a highly organized, uniform pattern. The speckle pattern in this region remains identical across all provided frames.
3.  **Artifact Analysis:** There are no visible comet-tail artifacts, nor are there discernible B-lines originating from the pleural line in any of the frames. The pattern suggests a stable, non-moving interface.

### Assessment Against LUS Signs

**Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
*   **Not present.** The pleural line is not granular or shimmering.
*   **Not present.** No comet-tail or B-lines are visible.
*   **Not present.** The pattern below the pleura is static, not "sandy."

**Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
*   **Present.** The pleural line is smooth and sharply defined, maintaining a static appearance throughout the sequence.
*   **Present.** The sub-pleural speckle pattern is uniform and remains unchanged from frame to frame.
*   **Present.** The image lacks the dynamic change necessary to suggest pleural movement (sliding).

**Signs of BOTH (lung point):**
*   **Not present.** There is no discernible transition point or alternation between static and shimmering patterns.

### Conclusion

Based on the consistent appearance of a **smooth, static pleural line** and the lack of any **variation in the sub-pleural speckle** across the sequential frames, the findings are consistent with the absence of lung sliding.

**Conclusion:** Pleural sliding is **ABSENT**.
