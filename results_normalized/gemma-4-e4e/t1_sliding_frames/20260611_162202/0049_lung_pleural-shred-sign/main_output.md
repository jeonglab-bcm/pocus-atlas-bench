# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

This analysis focuses on the appearance of the pleural line and the sub-pleural artifacts across the sequential frames.

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frame 1:**
*   **Observation:** The pleural line (indicated by the yellow arrow) is clearly visible. The region immediately below the line exhibits a grainy, textured pattern (sub-pleural speckle). The line itself does not appear perfectly sharp and static.

**Frame 2:**
*   **Observation:** The pleural line remains visible. The texture below the line maintains its granular quality. Compared to Frame 1, there is no clear evidence of a "smooth" or "static" A-line, suggesting movement.

**Frame 3:**
*   **Observation:** The pleural line is bright. The sub-pleural region clearly shows a mottled, granular speckle pattern. This texture is consistent with the appearance of the **seashore sign** (sandy, granular pattern), which is a classic indicator of pleural sliding.

**Frame 4:**
*   **Observation:** The pleural line remains intact. The overall visual impression across all four frames is one of a dynamic interface rather than a fixed, motionless barrier. The sub-pleural speckle is varied and granular, not a static horizontal line.

### Conclusion

**Pleural Sliding: PRESENT**

**Rationale:**
The analysis demonstrates signs of pleural sliding. The pleural line (the interface) is not smooth or perfectly static (ruling out the **Stratosphere/Barcode sign**). Instead, the sub-pleural tissue shows a visible granular and textured pattern, consistent with the **Seashore sign**. This granular, moving interface confirms that the lung surface is oscillating against the chest wall during respiration.
