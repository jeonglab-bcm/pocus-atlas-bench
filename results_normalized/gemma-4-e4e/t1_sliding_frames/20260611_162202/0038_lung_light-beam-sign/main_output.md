### Assessment of Pleural Sliding

**Signs of sliding PRESENT (Absent):**
*   The pleural line does not exhibit shimmering or granular movement across consecutive frames.
*   No definitive "comet-tail" or strong B-lines are seen originating dynamically from the pleural line.
*   The sub-pleural pattern does not demonstrate significant change in speckle between frames, suggesting a fixed interface.

**Signs of sliding ABSENT (Present):**
*   The pleural line is smooth and maintains a static, unmoving appearance across all observed frames.
*   The sub-pleural pattern is consistent, static, and unchanging, which is consistent with a non-moving, fixed interface.

**Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):**
*   There is no evidence of a transition zone or alternating patterns between the observed segments.

### Conclusion

Based on the examination of these sequential static images, the pleural line is smooth, and the underlying sub-pleural texture appears **static** and unchanged across all frames.

Therefore, the signs are consistent with **Sliding ABSENT**. The image represents a fixed, static interface.
