# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

This is an analysis of sequential frames from a lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip.

---

### **Frame-by-Frame Analysis**

**Frame 1:**
*   **Observation:** The pleural line is visible and appears sharply defined. The pattern below the pleural line consists of highly regular, static vertical lines (a barcode/A-line pattern). There is no visible "shimmer" or granular texture at the pleural line.
*   **Sliding Status:** Absent.

**Frame 2:**
*   **Observation:** The pattern is identical to Frame 1. The vertical lines below the pleura remain perfectly still. The pleural line shows no variation or movement.
*   **Sliding Status:** Absent.

**Frame 3:**
*   **Observation:** The static pattern persists. The sub-pleural speckle is unchanging. The pleural interface remains fixed.
*   **Sliding Status:** Absent.

**Frame 4:**
*   **Observation:** Identical to previous frames. No variation in the sub-pleural echoes.
*   **Sliding Status:** Absent.

**Frame 5:**
*   **Observation:** The pattern is consistent across all frames. The static, uniform vertical lines (barcode sign) below a fixed pleural line confirm the lack of sliding.
*   **Sliding Status:** Absent.

---

### **Conclusion**

**Overall Assessment:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**.

**Reasoning:**
The primary signs pointing to absent sliding are present: the pleural line is static and sharp, and the sub-pleural pattern displays a characteristic **barcode/stratosphere sign**. This pattern consists of uniform, non-fluctuating vertical lines, which indicates that the lung and pleural surfaces are not vibrating against each other (no shimmering, no comet tails, and no speckle variation between frames).
