**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**

Across all 14 frames, the pleural line (the interface between the lung and chest wall) does not appear as a perfectly smooth, static, or uniformly dark horizontal line (A-line). Instead, the pleural line exhibits a dynamic, shimmering, or granular appearance. Below this pleural line, the subpleural region shows a noticeable "sparkle" or highly textured pattern, rather than a uniform black/gray space.

**Observation against the Signs of Sliding:**

*   **Shimmering/Granular Pleural Line:** This sign is consistently present throughout the sequence. The pleural line is not sharp and smooth; it has a textured quality that indicates movement.
*   **Comet-tail/B-lines:** While typical lung tissue contains B-lines, the overall dynamic, speckled nature of the image confirms the movement of the underlying lung tissue.
*   **Seashore Sign/Speckle Variation:** The granular, "sparkling" pattern visible in the subpleural field (which would correspond to the Seashore sign in M-mode) suggests that the speckle pattern is changing between consecutive frames, indicating movement.
*   **Signs of Sliding Absent:** None of the signs of sliding absence (static line, barcode pattern, perfect A-lines) are observed.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the presence of a dynamic, shimmering, or textured pleural line and the granular/speckled pattern in the subpleural region across the sequence of frames, **Pleural Sliding is PRESENT.**
