# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

This analysis examines the sequential frames for the characteristic signs of pleural sliding.

---

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frame 1:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line is visible and appears dynamic rather than static and sharp.
*   **Sub-Pleural Field:** There is a visible, textured pattern immediately below the pleural line. This pattern is consistent with the **Seashore sign** (a granular, sandy pattern characteristic of a moving lung surface).
*   **Sliding Status:** Present.

**Frame 2:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line maintains its dynamic appearance.
*   **Sub-Pleural Field:** The speckle pattern and granularity below the pleural line are consistent with the movement of the lung interface, indicating a difference from a static image. The **Seashore sign** is maintained.
*   **Sliding Status:** Present.

**Frame 3:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line shows continued movement across the field of view.
*   **Sub-Pleural Field:** The pattern below the pleural line remains granular and textured.
*   **Sliding Status:** Present.

**Frame 4:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line is clearly dynamic, exhibiting movement.
*   **Sub-Pleural Field:** The characteristic granular texture (Seashore sign) remains visible below the pleural line.
*   **Sliding Status:** Present.

---

### Conclusion

Based on the consistent observation of a dynamic (moving) pleural line and the presence of a **Seashore sign** (sandy, granular texture) across all sequential frames, **pleural sliding is present.**
