This is an analysis of sequential frames from a lung ultrasound study to determine the presence of pleural sliding.

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis

In all five frames (25 Oct 2018), a clear, bright, thin horizontal line, the pleural line, is visible near the top of the image.

**What is observed:**
1.  **Pleural Line:** The line itself is visible in all frames.
2.  **Subpleural Pattern:** Below the pleural line, the tissue does not appear perfectly smooth or static. Instead, the pattern consists of a granular, textured, and dynamic speckle field. This suggests active tissue movement.
3.  **Movement/Shimmer:** The subpleural speckle pattern appears to change slightly from one frame to the next, which is indicative of movement at the pleura.

**Signs present:** The characteristics observed strongly align with the **Seashore Sign** (a granular, sandy texture below the pleural line) and evidence of **shimmer** (dynamic subpleural speckle).

---

### Conclusion

**Pleural Sliding: Present**

**Justification:** The presence of a granular, textured subpleural pattern (consistent with the Seashore sign) and the dynamic nature of the speckle below the pleura confirms the movement of the pleura against the chest wall. This finding is characteristic of normal aeration and air/tissue sliding.
