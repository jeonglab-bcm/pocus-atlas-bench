# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

**Analysis of Sequential Frames:**

**Observations:**

*   **Pleural Line Appearance:** Across all four frames, the pleural line (the interface between the chest wall and the lung parenchyma) appears bright (hyperechoic) and distinctly textured or granular. It does not present as a perfectly smooth, static, or sharp line that remains perfectly fixed.
*   **Sub-Pleural Pattern (A-Lines vs. B-Lines/Seashore):** Below the pleural line, the pattern is not a pure, continuous black band (which would signify a perfect A-line). Instead, the area immediately adjacent to the pleural line exhibits a **granular, speckled, or "sandy" pattern**. This mixed, textured appearance is characteristic of the **Seashore sign**.
*   **Speckle Variation:** Upon comparing consecutive frames, there is visible variation in the speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line, indicating that the sub-pleural tissue is moving relative to the probe.

**Application of Criteria:**

*   The presence of a granular or textured pleural line and the presence of a granular, speckled pattern below the line (the Seashore sign) directly align with the indicators that **Pleural Sliding is Present**.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames, which show a textured pleural interface and the presence of the Seashore sign below the pleural line, **Pleural Sliding is Present**.
