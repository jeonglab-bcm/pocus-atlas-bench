### Conclusion:

The sequential frames demonstrate a clear transition between dynamic and static patterns within the same ultrasound field.

*   **Evidence for Sliding Present:** Observed in Frames 1, 3, and 4 (Granular/Snowstorm pattern).
*   **Evidence for Sliding Absent:** Observed in Frame 2 (Static/Barcode/A-lines pattern).

Since the images display a transition point where the sliding pattern (granular) alternates or coexists with a non-sliding pattern (linear/static) in the sub-pleural region, the diagnosis is:
