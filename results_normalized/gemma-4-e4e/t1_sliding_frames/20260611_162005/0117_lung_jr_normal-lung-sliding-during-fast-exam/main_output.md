# 0117_lung_jr_normal-lung-sliding-during-fast-exam

**Analysis of Ultrasound Frames:**

**Frame-by-Frame Observation:**

*   **Pleural Line:** Across all five frames, the pleural line (the horizontal, bright interface at the top of the lung field) is consistently smooth, thin, and sharply defined. It does not exhibit any shimmering, granular texture, or movement over the sequence.
*   **Sub-pleural Pattern:** The area immediately below the pleural line is uniformly anechoic (black). This area is characterized by highly parallel, unchanged A-lines (lung sliding artifacts are absent). There is no evidence of a granular "sandy" texture (Seashore sign) or the vertical, streaky lines of B-lines.
*   **Comparison:** The visual pattern and characteristics of the pleural line and the sub-pleural speckle remain static and identical across every frame. There is no discernible change or variation between consecutive frames.

**Evaluation Against Sliding Criteria:**

*   **Signs of Sliding Present:** None of the criteria for present sliding are met (no shimmering, no comet-tail artifacts, no difference in speckle).
*   **Signs of Sliding Absent:** All criteria for absent sliding are met: the pleural line is smooth and static, and the pattern below it is a static A-line pattern (the Stratosphere/barcode sign).
*   **Signs of Both (Lung Point):** No transition or mixture of sliding/non-sliding patterns is observed within the field of view.

**Conclusion:**

Based on the observation that the pleural line remains smooth and static throughout the sequence, and the absence of any change or granular texture in the sub-pleural area, **pleural sliding is absent**. This presentation is consistent with the "stratosphere sign."
