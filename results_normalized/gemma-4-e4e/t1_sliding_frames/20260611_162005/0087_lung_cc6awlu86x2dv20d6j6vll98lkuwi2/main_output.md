### Assessment of Pleural Sliding:

*   **Observation:** The primary observation is the presence of **random, scattered speckles** (a "granular" pattern) in the subpleural space below the pleural line.
*   **Analysis against Criteria:**
    *   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** The scattered, granular speckles observed are consistent with the **Seashore sign**, which indicates movement of the pleural surfaces and thus the presence of sliding.
    *   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The pattern is definitely *not* a smooth, uniform line or a "barcode/stratosphere" pattern, which would indicate a static interface.

### Conclusion:

Based on the observation of the scattered, granular speckles (Seashore sign) in the subpleural space across all frames, **pleural sliding is present**.
