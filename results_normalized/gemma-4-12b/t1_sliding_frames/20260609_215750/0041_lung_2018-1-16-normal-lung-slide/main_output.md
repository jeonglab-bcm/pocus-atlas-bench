**Assessment of Signs:**

*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The pleural line is smooth, sharply defined, and appears perfectly static. The A-lines above the pleural line remain completely unchanged across all frames, and there is no variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** Although a B-line is visible, the lack of any shimmering, texture change, or speckle variation across the sequential frames indicates a static pleural surface.
