1.  **Frames 1-4:** The pleural line appears very **smooth, sharply defined, and static**. The A-lines (horizontal reflections) are also very crisp and do not show movement or shimmering. This is a sign of **absent sliding**, often seen in a pneumothorax.

The lung point is the physical location where the pneumothorax meets the aerated lung, where sliding is present on one side and absent on the other.
