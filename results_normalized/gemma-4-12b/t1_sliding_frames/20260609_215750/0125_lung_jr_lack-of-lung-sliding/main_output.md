# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound (LUS) frames provided:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears as a very smooth, sharply defined, and hyperechoic horizontal line. There is no visible "shimmering" or granular texture.
*   **Artifacts:** There are prominent, horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) below the pleural line. These lines remain perfectly static and do not shift or change in position between any of the frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern in the sub-pleural area does not show any variation or movement across the sequence.
*   **Transition:** There is no visible transition point where sliding begins or ends within the field of view.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.**

The images exhibit a "stratosphere" or "barcode" appearance characteristic of a lack of pleural sliding, which can be associated with conditions such as a pneumothorax or pleural effusion.
