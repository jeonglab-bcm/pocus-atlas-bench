# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence, here is the observation and conclusion:

### Frame-by-Frame Observations:
*   **B-mode analysis:** In all frames, there is a clearly visible, smooth, and sharply defined pleural line. Below this line, horizontal A-lines are present. The sub-pleural speckle pattern remains remarkably stable and static across the sequence, with no visible shimmering or granular texture.
*   **M-mode analysis:** The M-mode displays a classic **"barcode sign"** (also known as a **stratosphere sign**). The lines are perfectly horizontal and parallel across the entire display. There is no "seashore sign" (which would show a granular, sand-like pattern below the pleural line). This indicates that the chest wall is moving but the underlying lung is not sliding against it.

### Signs Identified:
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
    *   Smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line.
    *   **Stratosphere / barcode sign** on M-mode (horizontal lines only).
    *   No variation in sub-pleural speckle between frames.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The presence of a consistent barcode sign on M-mode and the lack of shimmering on the pleural line are characteristic findings, often associated with a pneumothorax.
