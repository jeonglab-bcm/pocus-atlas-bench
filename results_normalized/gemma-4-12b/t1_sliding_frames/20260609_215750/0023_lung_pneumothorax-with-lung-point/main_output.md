# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on an analysis of the four frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video, here is the observation:

**Frame-by-frame Analysis:**
*   **Frame 1:** A horizontal, bright hyperechoic pleural line is visible. Beneath it, several vertical B-lines and horizontal A-lines are present.
*   **Frame 2:** The image is nearly identical to Frame 1. The pleural line and the associated artifacts (A-lines and B-lines) do not appear to have moved.
*   **Frame 3:** Again, the pleural line remains static. There is no visible shimmering or change in the position of the B-lines.
*   **Frame 4:** The pleural line remains completely fixed in the same position as in the previous frames.

**Evaluation of Signs:**
*   **Sliding PRESENT:** No evidence is found. The pleural line does not shimmer, and the artifacts do not show movement or variation in speckle pattern.
*   **Sliding ABSENT:** The pleural line is smooth, sharply defined, and appears perfectly static across all frames. The A-lines and B-lines remain unchanged and fixed in position.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** There is no visible transition point between a sliding and non-sliding segment in these frames.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The lack of any movement or shimmering of the pleural line across the frames is a classic sign of absent sliding, which can be associated with conditions like a pneumothorax.
