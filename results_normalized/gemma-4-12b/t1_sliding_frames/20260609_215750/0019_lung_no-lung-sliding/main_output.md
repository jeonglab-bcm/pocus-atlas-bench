# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) images, here is the observation:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Frame 1:** A bright, slightly granular pleural line is clearly visible. There is a distinct speckle pattern in the lung tissue below it.
*   **Frame 2:** There is a subtle shift in the speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line compared to Frame 1.
*   **Frame 3:** The sub-pleural speckle pattern continues to change slightly, indicating movement of the underlying tissue.
*   **Frame 4:** The pleural line remains bright and textured (shimmery), and the speckle pattern continues to shift.
*   **Frame 5:** Significant differences in the noise/speckle pattern are visible compared to the earlier frames.
*   **Frame 6:** The final frame shows a clearly different sub-pleural speckle pattern than the first, confirming movement.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.** This is evidenced by the granular/shimmery appearance of the pleural line and, most importantly, the continuous variation in the speckle pattern below the pleural line between consecutive frames. There is no evidence of a lung point (no clear transition between sliding and non-sliding areas).
