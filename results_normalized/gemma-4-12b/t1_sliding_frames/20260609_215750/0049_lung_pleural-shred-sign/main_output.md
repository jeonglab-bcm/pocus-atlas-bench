# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line is visible as a bright horizontal interface. It has a **granular or textured appearance** rather than being perfectly smooth and sharp. There is some discernible **shimmering** across the frames as the visceral pleura moves against the parietal pleura.
*   **B-lines:** There are numerous, dense **B-lines** (vertical hyperechoic artifacts) originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These appear to fluctuate slightly in their position and intensity between frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** While the overall pattern below the pleural line is consistent (representing the wet lung), there is some subtle variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames, consistent with active movement.
*   **Transition:** There is no visible transition point or "lung point" where the sliding disappears; the sliding appears consistent across the entire visible segment.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** 

The primary indicators are the shimmering and granular texture of the pleural line combined with the presence of active B-lines.
