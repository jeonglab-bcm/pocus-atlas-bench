# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video, here is the assessment:

**Observations:**
*   **Speckle Pattern:** There is a clear and continuous variation in the speckle pattern (the granular, grainy appearance) located immediately below the bright, horizontal pleural line across all frames. It is not static; it moves and shifts as the video progresses.
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line itself appears slightly "shimmering" and textured rather than being a perfectly smooth, fixed, and immobile interface.
*   **A-lines:** While there are horizontal A-lines below the pleural line, they are not perfectly static; they move in tandem with the underlying speckle pattern.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The primary indicator is the dynamic change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames, which confirms that the visceral pleura is sliding over the parietal pleura.
