# 0052_lung_improve-lung-sliding-visualization

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames provided:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears textured and granular rather than perfectly smooth.
*   **B-lines:** There are numerous vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line that extend to the bottom of the screen. 
*   **Dynamic Changes:** Comparing the frames (e.g., comparing frame 22/270 to frame 263/270), the pattern, position, and intensity of the sub-pleural speckle and the B-lines change significantly. They are not static.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The dynamic nature of the B-lines and the textured appearance of the pleural line across the sequence are characteristic signs of lung sliding.
