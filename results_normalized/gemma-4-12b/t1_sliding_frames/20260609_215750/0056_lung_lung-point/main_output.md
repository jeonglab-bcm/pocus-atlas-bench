# 0056_lung_lung-point

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided ultrasound sequence, here are the observations:

### Frame-by-Frame Observations:
*   **Frame 1:** Shows a pleural line with several vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) originating from it. A granular "seashore" pattern is visible below the pleural line.
*   **Frame 2-4:** The image remains virtually identical to Frame 1. The position of the pleural line and the individual B-lines does not shift or "shimmer."

### Sign Analysis:
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** There is a granular pleural line and visible B-lines; however, they do not exhibit the necessary shimmering or movement between frames to confirm sliding.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The pleural line and the sub-pleural speckle pattern (the "sand" of the seashore sign) are remarkably static across all four frames. There is no visible variation in the speckle pattern between consecutive frames.
*   **Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):** There is no clear transition point visible where sliding appears on one side and is absent on the other.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.** 

The lack of movement in the pleural line and the static nature of the B-lines across the video sequence are key indicators of the absence of sliding, which is a classic finding in a pneumothorax.
