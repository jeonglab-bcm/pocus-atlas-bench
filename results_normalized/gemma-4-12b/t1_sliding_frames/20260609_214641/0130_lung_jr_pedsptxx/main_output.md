Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line is clearly visible as a bright, horizontal interface in the upper-middle portion of the image. It exhibits a **granular and textured appearance** rather than being a perfectly smooth, thin line. 
*   **Artifacts:** There are repeating horizontal echoes (A-lines) below the pleural line, and some vertical, comet-tail-like artifacts (B-lines) are visible, particularly on the right side.
*   **Movement:** While the five frames are very similar, the prominent granular texture of the pleural line is a hallmark of active movement between the visceral and parietal pleura.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The primary indicator is the **granular/shimmering texture** of the pleural line. A smooth, sharply defined, and static pleural line would be expected if sliding were absent (as seen in a pneumothorax).
