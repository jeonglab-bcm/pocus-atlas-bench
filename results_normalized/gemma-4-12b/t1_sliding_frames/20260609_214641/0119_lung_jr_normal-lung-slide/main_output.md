**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Frames 1-3:** The pleural line appears as a smooth, bright, and sharply defined horizontal interface. Below it, horizontal artifacts (A-lines) are clearly visible. There is no noticeable "shimmering" or granular texture on the pleural line, and the position of the A-lines remains virtually unchanged between frames.
*   **Frames 4-5:** The appearance remains very consistent with the previous frames. The pleural line stays static, and there is no visible variation in the speckle pattern or the artifacts below the line.

**Assessment of Pleural Sliding:**
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** None. There is no shimmering, no granular texture on the pleural line, no B-lines, and no detectable change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** Present. The pleural line is smooth, sharply defined, and remains static across the entire clip. The A-lines are perfectly static and unchanged.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** None. There is no transition point where sliding is observed.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The static appearance of the pleural line and the lack of movement in the sub-pleural artifacts are characteristic signs of absent sliding.
