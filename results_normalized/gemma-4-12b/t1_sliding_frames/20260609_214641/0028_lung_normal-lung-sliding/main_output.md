# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) video, here is the assessment:

### Frame-by-Frame Observation:
Across all 20 frames, the image remains almost entirely static. The pleural line is a bright, horizontal, and sharply defined interface. The area below the pleural line contains horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines). There is no perceptible "shimmering," movement, or change in the speckle pattern of the pleural line or the tissue beneath it as the video progresses.

### Assessment of Signs:

**Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
*   **Shimmering/Granular pleural line:** Not observed. The line remains smooth and sharp.
*   **Comet-tail artifacts or B-lines:** No significant B-lines are visible; the predominant artifacts are horizontal A-lines.
*   **Speckle pattern variation:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line is virtually identical in every consecutive frame.

**Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
*   **Smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line:** **Present.** The pleural line is very distinct and does not move or shimmer throughout the clip.
*   **A-lines that remain perfectly static:** **Present.** The horizontal reverberation lines below the pleural line are fixed in position across all frames.
*   **No variation in sub-pleural speckle:** **Present.** The texture of the lung parenchyma below the line is constant.

**Signs of BOTH (lung point):**
*   **Visible transition point:** Not observed. The entire visible segment of the pleura is static.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is absent.** 

The ultrasound shows a static pleural line with fixed A-lines and no shimmering or speckle variation, which are characteristic findings of a lack of lung sliding (potentially indicative of a pneumothorax, depending on the clinical context).
