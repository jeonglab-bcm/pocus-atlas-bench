# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence provided, here is the assessment:

### Observation of Frames:
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright, horizontal interface near the top of the image) remains smooth, sharply defined, and static across all six frames. There is no evidence of "shimmering" or a granular texture that would indicate movement.
*   **Sub-pleural Speckle:** The speckle pattern located just below the pleural line shows no variation between consecutive frames; it appears "frozen" rather than dynamic.
*   **B-lines:** The vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) extending from the pleural line are also static in their position and appearance across the sequence.
*   **Uniformity:** There is no visible transition point within this view where the pleural line changes from moving to static, which would indicate a lung point.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.**

The primary evidence for this is the smooth, static appearance of the pleural line and the lack of any change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between frames. These are classic signs of absent pleural sliding, which can be associated with conditions such as a pneumothorax.
