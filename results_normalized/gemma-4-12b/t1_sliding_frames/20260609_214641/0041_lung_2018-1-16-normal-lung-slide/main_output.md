### Frame-by-Frame Analysis:
*   **Frame 1:** Shows a clear pleural line with a textured, slightly granular appearance. Multiple vertical B-lines originate from the pleural line and extend toward the bottom of the image.
*   **Frame 2:** A slight shift in the position and intensity of the B-lines and the underlying sub-pleural speckle pattern is visible compared to Frame 1.
*   **Frame 3:** Further subtle movement of the B-lines and a change in the speckle pattern are observed, indicating dynamic motion.
*   **Frame 4:** The movement continues, with the B-lines and the granular texture of the pleural line persisting as they shift slightly.

### Assessment:
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
    *   **Shimmering/Granular Pleural Line:** The pleural line has a textured, shimmering appearance rather than a static, smooth line.
    *   **B-lines:** Multiple prominent B-lines are present, originating from the pleural line.
    *   **Speckle Pattern Change:** There is a visible variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames, which is a key indicator of movement.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** Not observed. The pleural line is not static or perfectly smooth.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** Not observed. There is no visible transition point between a sliding and non-sliding segment in these frames.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The shimmering texture of the pleural line, the presence of multiple B-lines, and the observable movement of the sub-pleural speckle pattern across the frames confirm the presence of sliding.
