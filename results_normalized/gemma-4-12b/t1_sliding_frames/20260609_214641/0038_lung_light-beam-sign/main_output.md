# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) clip:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears bright and has a granular, "shimmering" texture rather than being a perfectly smooth, static line.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** There is a clear variation in the speckle pattern (the "snowy" appearance) in the lung parenchyma below the pleural line between consecutive frames. This indicates that the lung tissue is moving relative to the chest wall.
*   **B-lines:** There are prominent vertical B-lines originating from the pleural line, which are typical in lung ultrasound and do not preclude sliding.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** This is evidenced by the shimmering/granular appearance of the pleural line and the noticeable change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern across the four frames.
