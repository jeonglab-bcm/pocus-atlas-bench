# 0043_lung_normal-lung

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video, here is the assessment:

### **Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright horizontal interface at the top of the lung tissue) appears smooth, sharply defined, and static throughout the clip. It does not exhibit the "shimmering" or granular texture typical of active sliding.
*   **A-lines:** The horizontal linear artifacts (A-lines) are very regular, parallel, and appear almost perfectly static. There is no noticeable movement or shift in their position across the consecutive frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The sub-pleural speckle pattern remains very consistent between frames, with no significant variation or "grainy" movement.
*   **Lung Point:** There is no visible transition point where sliding is present on one side and absent on the other; the entire field of view appears uniform.

### **Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.**

The findings—specifically the static, sharply defined pleural line and the perfectly stable A-lines—are characteristic of a lack of pleural sliding.
