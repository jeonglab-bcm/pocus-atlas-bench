# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence of the left lung, here is the observation:

**Frame-by-frame observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears as a bright, horizontal interface. It exhibits a "shimmering" or slightly granular texture rather than being a perfectly smooth, static line.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** Looking closely at the sub-pleural area (the speckle pattern immediately beneath the pleural line), there is a noticeable variation in the position and shape of the speckles between consecutive frames.
*   **A-lines:** While horizontal A-line reverberation artifacts are present, they are not perfectly static; they shift slightly in tandem with the underlying pleural movement.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** 

This is primarily supported by the **variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern** between frames and the **shimmering appearance of the pleural line**, both of which are hallmarks of active sliding between the visceral and parietal pleura.
