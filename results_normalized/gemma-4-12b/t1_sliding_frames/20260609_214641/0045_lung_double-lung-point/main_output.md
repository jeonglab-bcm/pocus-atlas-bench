# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the provided frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis
*   **Frames 1 through 6:** Throughout the sequence, a bright, horizontal pleural line is visible. Upon close inspection, the pleural line exhibits a granular, "shimmering" texture rather than a perfectly smooth, static appearance. More importantly, the speckled pattern of the tissue immediately below the pleural line is constantly shifting and changing its texture from one frame to the next.

### Assessment of Signs
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
    *   **Shimmering/granular pleural line:** Yes, the pleural line has a bright, textured appearance characteristic of sliding.
    *   **Speckle pattern below the pleural line:** Yes, there is a distinct and continuous variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern across all frames. This "moving" speckle is a primary indicator of the visceral pleura sliding against the parietal pleura.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
    *   The pleural line is **not** smooth or static; it shows active movement.
    *   The sub-pleural speckle is **not** static; it changes between every frame.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):**
    *   There is no visible transition point where sliding stops in this specific view.

### Conclusion
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The clear shimmering of the pleural line and the dynamic movement of the sub-pleural speckle pattern between frames are definitive signs of sliding.
