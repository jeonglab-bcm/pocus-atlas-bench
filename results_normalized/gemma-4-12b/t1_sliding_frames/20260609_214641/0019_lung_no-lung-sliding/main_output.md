# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) frames:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears as a smooth, horizontally oriented, and sharply defined bright interface. It does not exhibit the characteristic "shimmering" or granular texture associated with active pleural sliding.
*   **A-lines:** There are horizontal artifacts (A-lines) visible below the pleural line. These lines remain almost perfectly static and unchanged across all consecutive frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The sub-pleural speckle pattern (the "lung" area below the pleural line) shows very little variation between frames, lacking the characteristic "shifting" or "grainy" movement of a sliding lung.
*   **M-mode Signs:** While this is a B-mode view, the static appearance of the A-lines and the smooth pleural line are indicative of the "barcode sign" (or "stratosphere sign" in M-mode), where the lack of movement indicates no pleural sliding.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The appearance of a smooth, static pleural line combined with static A-lines is characteristic of a "barcode sign," which is often associated with a pneumothorax.
