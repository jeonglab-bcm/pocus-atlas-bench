# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on an analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) frames, here is the step-by-step assessment:

### Frame-by-Frame Observations:
Across all frames, the **M-mode** (top portion of the image) displays a very consistent pattern of horizontal, parallel lines without any granular texture below the pleural line. This is the classic **"barcode sign"** (also known as the **stratosphere sign**).

In the **B-mode** (bottom portion), there is a visible pleural line with several **B-lines** (vertical comet-tail artifacts) originating from it. However, the lack of dynamic movement or "shimmering" in the M-mode is the primary diagnostic indicator.

### Assessment of Signs:
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** Not observed. There is no "seashore sign" (sandy granular pattern) or noticeable shimmering/granularity of the pleural line on M-mode.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** **Present.** The M-mode shows a clear **stratosphere / barcode sign** (horizontal lines only). This indicates that the visceral pleura is not sliding against the parietal pleura.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** Not observed. There is no visible transition between sliding and non-sliding regions in these views.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is absent.** 

The presence of a barcode sign on M-mode is a highly specific finding for a **pneumothorax**, as it indicates that the lung is not moving against the chest wall.
