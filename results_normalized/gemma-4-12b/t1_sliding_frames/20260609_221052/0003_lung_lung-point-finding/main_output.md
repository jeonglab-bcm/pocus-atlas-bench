Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright, white, curved interface near the top) appears smooth and sharply defined. It does not exhibit any shimmering or granular movement between frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The ultrasound speckle pattern of the lung parenchyma beneath the pleural line remains perfectly static across the entire sequence. There is no discernible "granular" texture or flickering.
*   **A-lines:** The horizontal echoes (A-lines) are present but remain unchanged in position and appearance, further indicating a lack of movement at the pleural interface.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.**

The primary indicators are the smooth, static nature of the pleural line and the lack of any variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames.
