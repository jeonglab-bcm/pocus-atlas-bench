Based on the analysis of the ultrasound images provided:

**Frame-by-frame Observation:**
The sequence shows a consistent M-mode tracing of the pleural line. In the M-mode display (bottom half), the area above the pleural line (the "sea") appears as relatively straight, horizontal lines. Below the pleural line (starting at approximately 1.5 cm depth), there is a distinct, grainy, shimmering texture that resembles sand. This is the classic **seashore sign**.

**Assessment of Signs:**
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
    *   **Seashore sign on M-mode:** There is a clear sandy granular pattern below the pleural line, which is the definitive ultrasound sign of pleural sliding.
    *   **Shimmering pleural line:** The granular texture indicates the movement of the visceral pleura relative to the parietal pleura.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
    *   The tracing is not a "barcode" or "stratosphere" sign (which would show continuous horizontal lines throughout).
    *   The pleural line is not perfectly static.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
