# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence:

**Observations:**
*   **Shimmering/Granular Pleural Line:** The pleural line is not a static, sharp white line; it displays a bright, textured, and shimmering appearance that changes slightly between frames.
*   **B-lines:** A distinct B-line is visible, originating from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the image.
*   **Speckle Pattern Variation:** The "speckle" or grainy pattern of the tissue just below the pleural line changes visibly between consecutive frames (e.g., compare frames 3 and 4, or 7 and 8). These are classic signs of the lung surface moving relative to the chest wall.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The combination of a shimmering pleural line, the presence of B-lines, and the visible variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern across the frames confirms the presence of sliding.
