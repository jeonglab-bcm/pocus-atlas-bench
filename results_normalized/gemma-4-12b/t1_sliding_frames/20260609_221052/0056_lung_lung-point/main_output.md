# 0056_lung_lung-point

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames provided:

### Observations:
*   **B-lines:** There are multiple prominent, vertical, hyperechoic (bright) artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the image. These are characteristic B-lines.
*   **Seashore Sign:** The area below the pleural line exhibits a speckled, granular texture (the "seashore sign"), which is typical when the lung surface is moving against the chest wall.
*   **Pleural Line Texture:** The pleural line appears somewhat granular and textured rather than perfectly smooth and static.
*   **Consistency:** There is no clear transition point visible in these frames where sliding appears to stop or start abruptly, which would characterize a "lung point."

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The presence of prominent B-lines and the seashore sign below the pleural line are strong indicators of active sliding in this lung ultrasound.
