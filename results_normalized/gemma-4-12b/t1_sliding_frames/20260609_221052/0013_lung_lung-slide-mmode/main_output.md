**Signs Observed:**
*   **M-mode (Stratosphere / Barcode Sign):** The M-mode displays very regular, horizontal lines throughout. The lack of "shimmering" or granular texture that changes over time is characteristic of a "barcode" or "stratosphere" sign.

*   **Speckle Pattern:** There is no variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames, which would normally occur if the lung were sliding against the chest wall.
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears sharply defined and does not exhibit the characteristic shimmering or "grainy" texture associated with active sliding.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.** 

The presence of the **barcode (stratosphere) sign** on M-mode combined with the lack of movement in the B-mode speckle pattern are classic indicators of absent pleural sliding, often seen in conditions such as a pneumothorax.
