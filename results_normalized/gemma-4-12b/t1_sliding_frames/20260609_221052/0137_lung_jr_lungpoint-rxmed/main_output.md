# 0137_lung_jr_lungpoint-rxmed

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears as a relatively smooth, sharp, and static horizontal hyperechoic interface. There is no visible "shimmering" or granular texture that changes between frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern of the underlying lung parenchyma remains almost identical across all five frames. There is no detectable variation in the speckle pattern, which is a key indicator of pleural sliding.
*   **A-lines:** The horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) below the pleural line are perfectly static and do not shift or change in any way between the frames.
*   **Lung Point:** There is no visible transition point where sliding is present on one side and absent on the other within the field of view.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**. The static nature of the pleural line and the lack of speckle variation are characteristic signs of absent sliding, which can be seen in conditions such as a pneumothorax.
