# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) frames, here is the observation:

**Frame-by-Frame Observation:**
*   **Pleural Line Appearance:** The pleural line (the bright, hyperechoic horizontal interface) exhibits a **shimmering and granular texture**. It does not appear as a perfectly smooth, static, and sharply defined line.
*   **Sub-pleural Speckle:** There is a subtle but detectable **variation in the speckle pattern** just below the pleural line as the frames progress. The texture of the lung tissue appears to "move" slightly with each breath/pulse.
*   **A-lines:** While horizontal A-lines are visible (reverberation artifacts), they are not perfectly static; they appear to move in tandem with the shimmering pleural line.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

The presence of a shimmering, granular pleural line and the visible changes in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames are hallmark signs of pleural sliding. There is no clear evidence of a "lung point" transition in this specific view.
