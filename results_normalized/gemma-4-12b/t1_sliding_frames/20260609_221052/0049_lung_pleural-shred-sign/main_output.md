# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video provided, here is the assessment:

### **Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Frame 1:** A clear horizontal hyperechoic pleural line is visible. Below it, there is a granular, speckled pattern (speckle pattern) with some vertical B-lines originating from the pleural line.
*   **Frame 2:** The granular speckle pattern beneath the pleural line shows a slight change in its arrangement/intensity compared to Frame 1.
*   **Frame 3:** The speckle pattern continues to fluctuate slightly, maintaining a granular "sand-like" appearance below the stable horizontal "shore" of the pleural line.

### **Assessment of Pleural Sliding:**
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** There is a clear **shimmering/granular speckle pattern** below the pleural line. The speckle pattern noticeably **differs between consecutive frames**, indicating the movement of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura. This is characteristic of the "seashore sign" in B-mode.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The pleural line is not static; there is no "barcode" sign or perfectly unchanged A-lines.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** There is no visible transition point in this specific field of view where sliding stops and begins.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The granular speckle pattern (the "sand" in the seashore sign) fluctuates between frames, confirming the movement of the lung surface.
