# 0043_lung_normal-lung

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames in the video clip, here is the observation:

**Observations:**
*   **Granular/Shimmering Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright horizontal interface near the top) appears textured and has a shimmering, granular quality rather than being a perfectly static, smooth line.
*   **Dynamic Speckle Pattern:** Most importantly, the speckle pattern below the pleural line clearly changes and shifts between consecutive frames. This movement indicates that the visceral and parietal pleura are moving relative to each other.
*   **A-lines:** The horizontal artifacts (A-lines) are also moving in synchronization with the underlying lung tissue.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
