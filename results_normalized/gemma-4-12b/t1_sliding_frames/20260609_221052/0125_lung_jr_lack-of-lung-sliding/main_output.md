# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears smooth, sharply defined, and notably static across all frames. There is no visible shimmering or granular texture changes that would indicate the visceral pleura is moving against the parietal pleura.
*   **A-lines:** The horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) remain in the exact same position relative to the pleural line throughout the sequence, showing no shift or movement.
*   **Sub-pleural Speckle:** The speckle pattern in the region below the pleural line is constant. There is no variation in the texture or position of the speckles between consecutive frames.
*   **B-lines:** There are no prominent B-lines or comet-tail artifacts visible.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**. The static appearance of the pleural line and the unchanging sub-pleural speckle pattern are characteristic signs of a lack of sliding.
