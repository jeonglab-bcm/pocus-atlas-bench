# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Observations:**
*   **M-mode Appearance:** The M-mode images show a series of regular, horizontal lines below the pleural line. This is a classic **barcode (or stratosphere) sign**.
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright horizontal line at the top of the M-mode section) appears smooth and static across all frames without any visible shimmering.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** Comparing the frames consecutively, the speckle pattern below the pleural line remains unchanged and perfectly static. There is no variation in the "sand" or granular pattern that would indicate sliding.
*   **Overall Movement:** There is no visible shimmering or granular movement of the pleural interface.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of the **barcode sign** and the **lack of variation in sub-pleural speckle** across the sequence indicates that **pleural sliding is ABSENT**.
