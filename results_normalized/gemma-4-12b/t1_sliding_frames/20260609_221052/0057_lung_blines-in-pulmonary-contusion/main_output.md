# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip, here is the observation:

*   **Observation:** The pleural line exhibits a shimmering, granular texture. When comparing the frames consecutively, there is a clear and dynamic change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern and the position of the bright granular areas above the pleural line. The horizontal A-lines also appear to shift slightly rather than remaining perfectly static.
*   **Conclusion:** **Pleural sliding is present.** The movement of the speckle pattern between frames is a definitive sign of the visceral and parietal pleura moving against each other.
