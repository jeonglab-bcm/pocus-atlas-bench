# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence, here is the observation:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Frame 1:** The pleural line (located at approximately 2.5–3 cm depth) shows a bright, granular, and textured appearance. Several vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) are originating from it.
*   **Frame 2:** The pleural line maintains its shimmering, granular texture.
*   **Frame 3:** The granular texture remains consistent, with slight variations in the speckle pattern just below the pleural line compared to the previous frame.
*   **Frame 4:** The pleural line continues to show a shimmering rather than a static, sharp appearance.
*   **Frame 5:** The textured pleural line is clearly visible, and B-lines persist, confirming dynamic motion.

**Assessment of Signs:**
*   **Shimmering/Granular Pleural Line:** Present. The pleural line is not a perfectly smooth, sharp, static white line; it has a distinct "shimmering" or granular quality across all frames.
*   **B-lines:** Present. Multiple B-lines originate from the pleural line.
*   **Speckle Pattern Variation:** Present. There is a subtle but noticeable variation in the granular speckle pattern below the pleural line between consecutive frames.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
