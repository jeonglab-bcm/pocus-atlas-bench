# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line is observed as a smooth, sharply defined, and highly echogenic (bright) horizontal interface. It remains perfectly static across all frames.
*   **A-lines:** There are prominent horizontal hyperechoic lines (A-lines) below the pleural line. These lines appear completely static and do not shift or change between consecutive frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern in the sub-pleural region shows no visible shimmer or granular variation. There is no "seashore" texture.
*   **Motion:** There is a lack of any shimmering or "shaking" of the pleural line that would indicate lung movement against the chest wall.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.** 
The visual evidence—specifically a smooth, static pleural line with perfectly stationary A-lines and a lack of sub-pleural speckle variation—is characteristic of a lack of sliding, which can be seen in conditions such as a pneumothorax or pleural effusion.
