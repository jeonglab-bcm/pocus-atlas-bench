# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames, here is the evaluation:

**Observations:**
*   **Speckle Pattern Variation:** By comparing consecutive frames (e.g., comparing frame 1 to frame 6), the granular "speckle" pattern immediately below the pleural line is visibly changing and shimmering.
*   **Pleural Line Texture:** The pleural line (the bright horizontal interface at approximately 1.2 cm depth) appears granular rather than perfectly smooth and static.
*   **Absence of Barcode Sign:** There is no "barcode" or "stratosphere" sign (which would consist of only horizontal, parallel lines without granular texture).
*   **Consistency:** The shimmering and granular texture are consistent across the entire visible pleural line in all frames, with no clear transition point to a static area.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.** The movement of the speckle pattern below the pleural line across the sequence is a definitive sign of lung sliding.
