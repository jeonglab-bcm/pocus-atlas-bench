# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the ultrasound video clip provided:

**Frame Analysis:**
*   **Frame 1:** Shows a well-defined pleural line with multiple vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) originating from it. The area below the pleural line has a consistent speckle pattern.
*   **Frame 2:** The pleural line and the B-lines appear virtually identical to Frame 1. There is no perceptible shifting or shimmering of the pleural surface.
*   **Frame 3:** The image remains static. The speckle pattern below the pleural line shows no variation in position or texture compared to the previous frames.
*   **Frame 4:** Again, the pleural line and the associated artifacts are perfectly static.

**Assessment of Signs:**
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** No shimmering or movement of the pleural line is observed. While B-lines are present, they do not move or "dance" across the screen between frames. The speckle pattern below the pleural line is completely static.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The pleural line is sharply defined and remains perfectly static across all four frames. There is no variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern, which is a hallmark sign of absent sliding (often associated with a "barcode" or "stratosphere" sign on M-mode).

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**. The perfect stability of the pleural line and the underlying speckle pattern across all frames indicates a lack of movement between the visceral and parietal pleura.
