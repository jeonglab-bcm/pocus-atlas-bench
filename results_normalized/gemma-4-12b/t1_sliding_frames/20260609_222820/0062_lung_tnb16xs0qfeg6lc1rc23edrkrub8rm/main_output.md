To the left of this point, the pleural line is shimmering and accompanied by B-lines (sliding present). To the right of this point, the pleural line appears smoother, sharper, and more static, with no B-lines (sliding absent).

**Conclusion:**
**Both (Lung Point)**
The image shows a clear transition point where pleural sliding is present on one side (left) and absent on the other (right). This is a characteristic finding for a "lung point," which is a highly specific sign of pneumothorax.
