# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the five sequential frames provided:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** In all five frames, the pleural line appears as a smooth, sharp, and highly well-defined horizontal bright line. 
*   **Dynamics:** There is no discernible shimmering, granularity, or movement of this line between any of the frames.
*   **Artifacts:** Horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) are visible below the pleural line. These A-lines remain perfectly static and unchanged across all five frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line does not show any variation or "shimmering" between consecutive frames.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.** 

The visual evidence meets the criteria for absent sliding: a smooth, sharply defined, and static pleural line combined with perfectly static A-lines and a lack of sub-pleural speckle variation across the sequence.
