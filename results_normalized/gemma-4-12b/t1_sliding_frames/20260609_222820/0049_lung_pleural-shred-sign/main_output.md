# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line exhibits a noticeable shimmering and granular texture rather than being a perfectly smooth, static horizontal line.
*   **Artifacts:** There are prominent hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line. These B-lines and the underlying speckle pattern show subtle changes and movement between consecutive frames.
*   **Dynamics:** The texture of the pleural interface is not "fixed"; it appears to have the characteristic "shimmer" associated with the movement of the visceral and parietal pleura against each other.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
