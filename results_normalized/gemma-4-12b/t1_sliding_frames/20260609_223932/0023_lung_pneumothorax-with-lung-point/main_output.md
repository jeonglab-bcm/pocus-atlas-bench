# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence:

*   **Frame 1:** Shows a clearly defined, horizontal pleural line with static horizontal artifacts (A-lines) below it.
*   **Frame 2:** The pleural line and the sub-pleural speckle pattern remain virtually unchanged from the first frame.
*   **Frame 3:** There is no visible shimmering or movement of the pleural line; it remains sharp and static.
*   **Frame 4:** The texture of the pleural line and the underlying artifacts continue to show no perceptible motion or variation.
*   **Frame 5:** Similar to the previous frames, the image is static with no change in the speckle pattern or pleural line position.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**. The pleural line is smooth, sharply defined, and perfectly static across all frames. There is no shimmering, granular texture, or variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames, which are all indicative of a lack of lung motion relative to the chest wall.
