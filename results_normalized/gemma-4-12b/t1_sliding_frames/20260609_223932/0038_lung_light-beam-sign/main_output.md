# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip, here are the observations:

**Analysis of Frames:**
- **Frame 1:** A bright, textured (granular) pleural line is visible at the top. Below it, a prominent vertical hyperechoic artifact (B-line) is seen.
- **Frame 2:** The pleural line maintains its granular appearance, but the specific texture and bright spots on the interface have changed slightly compared to Frame 1.
- **Frame 3:** Continued shimmering and granularity are observed on the pleural line. The B-line persists.
- **Frame 4:** The pleural line continues to show a granular, textured appearance with slight variations in its pattern from the previous frames.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**

**Reasoning:**
The pleural line exhibits a **shimmering or granular** appearance (a bright, textured interface) rather than a smooth, static one. Additionally, the presence of dynamic **B-lines** originating from this granular pleural line is a characteristic sign of sliding. There is no visible transition point (lung point) in these frames, as the characteristics of the pleural line remain consistent across the entire visible segment.
