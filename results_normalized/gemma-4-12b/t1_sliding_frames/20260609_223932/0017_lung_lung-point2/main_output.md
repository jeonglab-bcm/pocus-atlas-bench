# 0017_lung_lung-point2

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS), here is the evaluation:

**Observations:**
*   **M-mode Tracing:** The bottom half of each frame shows a clear **seashore sign**. There is a distinct horizontal boundary (the pleural line) at a depth of approximately 1.5 cm.
*   **"Sea" vs. "Sand":** Above the pleural line, the tracing is composed of smooth, horizontal lines (the "sea"). Below the pleural line, the tracing displays a grainy, shimmering, and speckled texture (the "sand").
*   **Consistency:** This granular pattern below the pleural line remains consistent across all six sequential frames, indicating continuous movement of the visceral and parietal pleura against one another.
*   **Absence of Barcode Sign:** There is no "barcode" or "stratosphere" sign (where the entire tracing consists only of horizontal lines), which would indicate a lack of sliding.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **PRESENT**.
