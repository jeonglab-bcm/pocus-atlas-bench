# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip, here are the observations:

*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright white horizontal interface near the top) appears smooth, sharply defined, and remains static across all frames. There is no visible "shimmering" or granular texture moving with respiration.
*   **Sub-pleural Artifacts:** While B-lines (vertical comet-tail artifacts) are present, they do not exhibit significant variation in their speckle pattern or position between consecutive frames. They appear "frozen" in place.
*   **Transitions:** There is no visible transition point where sliding suddenly appears or disappears within this specific view.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The static nature of the pleural line and the unchanging speckle pattern of the artifacts below it are characteristic signs of absent sliding.
