# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence, here is the assessment:

**Frame-by-frame Observation:**
*   **Frames 1, 2, and 3:** These frames show a pleural line with some sub-pleural irregularities and bright artifacts. While there is some granular texture, it is difficult to definitively confirm sliding in these static-looking frames alone.
*   **Frame 4:** This frame shows a significant change. There is a visible transition point across the center-left of the image. On the **right side**, the pleural line has a bright, textured, and "shimmering" granular appearance consistent with **pleural sliding**. On the **left side**, the area is darker and the pleural line appears smooth and fixed, consistent with **absent sliding**.

**Conclusion:**
**Both (Lung Point)**

The sequence, particularly Frame 4, demonstrates a clear transition point where pleural sliding is present on one side and absent on the other. This finding is the hallmark of a "lung point," which is a highly specific sign for a pneumothorax.
