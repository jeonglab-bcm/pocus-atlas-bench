# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on an analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound (LUS) frames provided:

### Observation:
*   **M-mode Analysis:** The M-mode image (top part of each frame) shows a classic **"barcode" or "stratosphere sign."** This is characterized by a series of horizontal, parallel lines without any granular or shimmering texture. This indicates that the pleural surface is not moving relative to the ultrasound probe.
*   **B-mode Analysis:** The pleural line appears as a smooth, sharply defined, and static interface. There is no visible shimmering, granular texture, or significant variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between the consecutive frames.
*   **Signs of Sliding:** There is a lack of a "seashore sign" (no granular/sandy pattern below the pleural line in M-mode) and no evidence of a shimmering pleural line.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.**

The presence of the **stratosphere/barcode sign** on M-mode is a definitive indicator of absent pleural sliding, which can be seen in conditions such as a pneumothorax or a large pleural effusion.
