# 0043_lung_normal-lung

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence, here is the assessment:

### Frame-by-Frame Observations
*   **Pleural Line:** Across all frames, the pleural line (the bright horizontal interface near the top) appears relatively smooth and static. There is a notable lack of the "shimmering" or granular texture that occurs when the visceral and parietal pleura move against each other.
*   **Sub-pleural Artifacts:** The horizontal artifacts (A-lines) below the pleural line remain very consistent. They do not shift or change position significantly from one frame to the next.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern beneath the pleural line is static, showing no variation between consecutive frames that would indicate underlying tissue movement.

### Assessment based on criteria
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** Not observed. There is no shimmering of the pleural line and no dynamic B-lines originating from it.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** Observed. The pleural line is static, and the horizontal artifacts remain perfectly unchanged across all frames. The overall appearance resembles a **barcode or stratosphere sign**, where horizontal lines are prominent and lack the vertical variation seen with normal sliding.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** Not observed. There is no clear transition point between sliding and non-sliding segments in these frames.

### Conclusion
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The static appearance of the pleural line and the persistent "barcode" artifacts are classic signs of an absent sliding sign, often associated with a pneumothorax.
