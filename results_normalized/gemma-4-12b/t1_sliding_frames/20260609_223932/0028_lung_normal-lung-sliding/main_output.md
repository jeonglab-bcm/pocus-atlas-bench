# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames in this lung ultrasound (LUS) clip:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line is a well-defined, smooth, horizontal hyperechoic line.
*   **Movement:** There is no visible "shimmering" or granular movement of the pleural line across the frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line remains static and does not shift or change between consecutive frames.
*   **Artifacts:** While horizontal A-lines are visible, they appear perfectly static.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The static nature of the pleural line and the lack of variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern are characteristic signs of absent sliding.
