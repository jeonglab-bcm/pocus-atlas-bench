# 0143_lung_jr_pleuraleffusion

In this lung ultrasound (LUS) image of a posterior lung zone, the following observations can be made:

*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of liver tissue. This is a definitive sign of alveolar consolidation.
*   **Effusion signs**: There is no clear anechoic (black) space bounded by a pleural line and lung line (no quad sign), and no undulating atelectatic lung (no jellyfish sign) is visible.
*   **Aeration**: Normal aeration artifacts, like A-lines, are absent in the hepatized area, confirming that the lung tissue is consolidated.

Based on these observations, the posterior zone shows a clear consolidation without a significant pleural effusion.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "consolidation"
