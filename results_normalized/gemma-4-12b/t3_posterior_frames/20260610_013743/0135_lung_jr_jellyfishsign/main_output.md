# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

The ultrasound video shows a classic PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) pattern in the posterior lung zone. 

Key observations include:
- **Jellyfish sign**: The most prominent finding is a mass of consolidated lung tissue that is floating and undulating within a surrounding fluid collection, characteristic of a "jellyfish" appearance.
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: The consolidated lung tissue appears solid and echogenic, similar in texture to liver parenchyma.
- **Shred sign**: The border between the consolidated tissue and the surrounding area is irregular and "shredded."
- **Air bronchograms**: Small, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within the consolidated tissue, representing air-filled bronchi.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "both"
