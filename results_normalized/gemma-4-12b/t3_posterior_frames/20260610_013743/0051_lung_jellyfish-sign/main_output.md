# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large anechoic space in the dependent region, characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, irregular, echogenic mass of lung tissue. This is the classic **jellyfish sign**, which represents atelectatic lung tissue floating in an effusion. The mass exhibits **tissue-like hepatization** (solid appearance) and an irregular **shred sign** at its border where it meets the aerated lung. The large anechoic area also displays a **quad sign**, bounded by the pleural line, rib shadows, and the lung line.

Since both significant pleural effusion signs and consolidation (atelectasis) signs are clearly present, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
