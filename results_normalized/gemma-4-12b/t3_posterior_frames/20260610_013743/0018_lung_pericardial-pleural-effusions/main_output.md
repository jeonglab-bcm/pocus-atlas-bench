# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

The image shows a characteristic **jellyfish sign**, which is a hallmark of a **PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome)** pattern.

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent, large anechoic (black) space representing a significant **pleural effusion**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** Within this fluid collection, there is an irregular, echogenic, and somewhat undulating mass of lung tissue. This is the **jellyfish sign**, representing atelectatic (collapsed) lung tissue floating within the pleural effusion.
*   Because both a massive pleural effusion and consolidated/atelectatic lung tissue are clearly visible, this meets the criteria for both.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
