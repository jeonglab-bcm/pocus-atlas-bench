The ultrasound images show a posterior lung zone at the PLAPS point. There is a large, hypoechoic (anechoic) area at the bottom of the pleural space, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid-filled space, there is a floating, echogenic, and irregularly shaped structure that appears to undulate with respiration; this is the **jellyfish sign**. The texture of this floating tissue is solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue, which indicates **tissue-like hepatization** (alveolar consolidation).

Because both significant pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation (jellyfish sign) are present, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
