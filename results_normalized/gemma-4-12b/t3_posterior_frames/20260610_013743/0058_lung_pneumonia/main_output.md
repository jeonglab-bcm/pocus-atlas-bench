# 0058_lung_pneumonia

The ultrasound images show a clear **PLAPS** pattern in the posterior lung zone.

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent **quad sign**, evidenced by an anechoic (black) fluid collection bounded superiorly by the pleural line, inferiorly by the lung line, and laterally by rib shadows. The appearance of some undulating tissue within this fluid is suggestive of a **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** Below the fluid collection, the lung tissue is clearly **hepatized** (solid-appearing, similar to liver texture) rather than aerated. Within this hepatized lung, there are numerous small, bright, punctate foci characteristic of **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
