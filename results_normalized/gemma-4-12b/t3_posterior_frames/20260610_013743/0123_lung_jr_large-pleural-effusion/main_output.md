# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

The provided ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show several hallmark features of a **PLAPS pattern**:

*   **Effusion Signs**: There is a large, predominant anechoic (black) space in the dependent posterior/lateral zone, indicating a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, the lung tissue is irregular and appears to be "floating," which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation Signs**: The lung parenchyma adjacent to and within the fluid collection appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue (**tissue-like hepatization**). There is also an irregular, "shredded" border between the aerated lung and the consolidated/fluid-filled area (**shred sign**).

Because both a large fluid collection and consolidated, hepatized lung tissue are clearly visible, the finding is classified as both effusion and consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
