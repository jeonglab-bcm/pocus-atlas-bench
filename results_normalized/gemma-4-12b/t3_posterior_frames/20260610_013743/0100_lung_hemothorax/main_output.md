# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The video sequence shows a posterior lung zone with clear signs of both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation.

In several frames (notably 4 and 8), a distinct **jellyfish sign** is visible, where undulating, atelectatic lung tissue is seen floating within a dark, anechoic pleural effusion. Additionally, many frames (such as 3, 6, and 7) exhibit a prominent **shred sign**—an irregular, jagged border between the consolidated and aerated lung—along with **tissue-like hepatization** (the lung appearing solid and liver-like). Some punctate hyperechoic foci suggestive of **air bronchograms** are also visible within the consolidated areas.
