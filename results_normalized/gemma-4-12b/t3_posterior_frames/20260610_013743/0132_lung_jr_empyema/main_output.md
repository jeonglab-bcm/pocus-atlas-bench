# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

The ultrasound images show a large hypoechoic (dark) area consistent with a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, there is an echogenic, irregularly shaped structure that appears to be undulating or "floating," which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**. This sign indicates atelectatic (consolidated) lung tissue submerged within a pleural effusion. Additionally, the lung parenchyma within the fluid exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, as it appears solid and lacks normal aeration artifacts.

Conclusion:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
