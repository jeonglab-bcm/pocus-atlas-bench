# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images show a large anechoic (black) space in the posterior dependent lung zone, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a mobile, irregular, hyperechoic structure that appears to "float" and undulate. This is the classic **Jellyfish sign**, which represents consolidated or atelectatic lung tissue suspended within a pleural effusion. Additionally, the irregular border of this consolidated tissue demonstrates a **Shred sign**.
