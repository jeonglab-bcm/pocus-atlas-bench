# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

The ultrasound image shows a large, well-defined anechoic (black) space in the posterior lung zone. This area is bounded by the pleural line superiorly, a lung line inferiorly, and rib shadows laterally, creating a clear quadrilateral shape. This is characteristic of the **quad sign**, which indicates a large pleural effusion in the dependent portion of the lung. While there is some adjacent tissue, the predominant feature is the fluid collection.
