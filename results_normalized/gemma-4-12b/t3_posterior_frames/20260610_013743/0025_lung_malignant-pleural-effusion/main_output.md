# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

Based on the provided ultrasound images of a posterior lung zone, the following observations can be made:

*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of liver tissue (hepatization). This indicates a loss of normal aeration.
*   **Shred sign**: There are irregular, "shredded" borders visible at the deep edges of the consolidated lung, where it meets aerated lung tissue.
*   **Air bronchograms**: Punctate hyperechoic foci can be seen within the hepatized tissue, representing air-filled bronchi.
*   **Effusion signs**: While there is a dark area at the inferior aspect, classic signs like a prominent quad sign or jellyfish sign are not the primary finding compared to the extensive consolidation.

The presence of hepatization and the shred sign confirms the presence of a PLAPS pattern characterized by consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "consolidation"**
