# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

In this lung ultrasound (LUS) image of the posterior lung zone, several key pathological signs are visible:

*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: A large portion of the dependent lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, losing its normal aerated texture and resembling liver tissue. This indicates significant alveolar consolidation.
*   **Effusion signs**: There is a prominent anechoic (black) collection at the bottom left of the image, consistent with a large pleural effusion.
*   **Shred sign**: The border between the consolidated (hepatized) lung and the aerated lung is irregular and jagged, characteristic of the shred sign.

Since both prominent signs of consolidation (hepatization, shred sign) and signs of pleural effusion are present, the PLAPS pattern is confirmed.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
