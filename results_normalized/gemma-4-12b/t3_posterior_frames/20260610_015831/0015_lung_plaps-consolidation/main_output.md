# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

Based on the provided ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone, here is the assessment:

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs**: There is a large, hypoechoic (dark) area below the pleural line, characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, the consolidated lung tissue appears to "float" and has an undulating shape, which is indicative of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs**: The lung tissue within the effusion appears solid and echogenic, similar to liver texture, which is known as **tissue-like hepatization**. The interface between this consolidated tissue and the aerated lung shows an irregular, "shredded" appearance, known as the **shred sign**. Additionally, small, bright punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within the consolidated tissue, which are **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
