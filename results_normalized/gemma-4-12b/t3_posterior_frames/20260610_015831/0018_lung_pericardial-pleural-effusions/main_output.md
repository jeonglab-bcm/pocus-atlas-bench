# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

In this lung ultrasound (LUS) image of the posterior lung zone, there are clear signs of a PLAPS pattern:

*   **Effusion signs**: A large anechoic (black) space is visible at the bottom of the image, indicating a significant **pleural effusion**. The consolidated lung is suspended within this fluid, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs**: The lung parenchyma appears solid and hypoechoic, resembling liver tissue, which is **tissue-like hepatization**. There is an irregular, shredded border between the consolidated and potentially aerated lung, representing a **shred sign**.

Since both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are present, the classification is:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
