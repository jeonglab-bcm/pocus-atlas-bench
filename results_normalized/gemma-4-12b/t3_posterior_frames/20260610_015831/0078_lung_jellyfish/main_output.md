# 0078_lung_jellyfish

In this lung ultrasound (LUS) of the right posterior lung zone (RT POSTERIOR), several classic signs of PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) are visible across the frames:

*   **Effusion signs**: There is a prominent anechoic (black) space at the top of the lung zone, indicating a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, the lung tissue appears to be undulating, characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs**: The underlying lung parenchyma shows **tissue-like hepatization**, where it appears solid and echogenic, similar to the texture of liver tissue. Within this consolidated area, small, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible, which are **air bronchograms**. A **shred sign** is also apparent where the consolidated lung tissue meets the more aerated portion of the lung.

Because both significant pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are clearly demonstrated, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = both**
