# 0073_lung_2017-10-9-dove-in-pleural-fluid

The ultrasound image shows a large, anechoic (black) fluid collection in the posterior/dependent zone, characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, there is a solid-looking, echogenic mass of lung tissue that appears to be floating and undulating. This is the classic **jellyfish sign**. The lung parenchyma itself shows **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and similar in texture to liver tissue. Because both significant pleural effusion and consolidated (hepatized) lung are present, this is a clear example of a PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "both"
