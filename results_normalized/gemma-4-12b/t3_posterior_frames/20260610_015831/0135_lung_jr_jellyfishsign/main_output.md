# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

The lung ultrasound (LUS) video shows a posterior lung zone with clear signs of PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome).

**Observations:**
*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The dependent lung parenchyma has a solid, liver-like appearance with loss of normal aeration artifacts.
*   **Shred sign:** There is an irregular, shredded border between the hepatized (consolidated) lung and the aerated lung.
*   **Pleural effusion:** A large anechoic (black) space is visible in the dependent zone, indicating a significant pleural effusion.
*   **Jellyfish sign:** The hepatized lung tissue appears to be "floating" and undulating within the surrounding fluid collection.

Based on these observations:
**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
