# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show several hallmark signs of **PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome)**:

1.  **Effusion Signs**: There is a large, anechoic (black) area representing a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, there is a hyperechoic, undulating mass of lung tissue that moves with respiration, characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
2.  **Consolidation Signs**: The solid-appearing tissue on the left side of the fluid collection exhibits **tissue-like hepatization** (resembling liver texture). Within this hepatized area, there are punctate hyperechoic foci, which are **air bronchograms**.

Because both a large pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue (with air bronchograms) are clearly visible, the condition is classified as having both components.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "both"
