# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

In these sequential frames of a posterior lung ultrasound (LUS), several characteristic signs of pleural syndrome are visible:

*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma in the upper and central portions of the image appears solid and echogenic, lacking the normal aeration artifacts seen in healthy lung. Its texture resembles that of the liver.
*   **Shred sign**: There is a clearly visible, irregular, and "shredded" deep border between the consolidated (hepatized) lung tissue and the surrounding areas.
*   **Jellyfish sign**: The consolidated lung tissue appears to be "floating" and undulating within a larger hypoechoic/anechoic fluid collection, strongly resembling a jellyfish.
*   **Effusion signs**: The large, dark (anechoic) area surrounding the consolidated tissue is indicative of a pleural effusion.

The presence of both consolidated lung tissue (hepatization, shred sign) and a surrounding fluid collection (jellyfish sign, anechoic space) confirms a PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = both
