In these ultrasound frames, a clear PLAPS pattern is visible in the posterior lung zone. 

**Observations:**
*   **Jellyfish sign:** There is a prominent, undulating, and somewhat irregular hyperechoic mass "floating" within a surrounding hypoechoic fluid collection. This represents consolidated lung tissue moving within a pleural effusion.
*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The consolidated lung tissue itself appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver parenchyma, which is characteristic of severe alveolar consolidation.
*   **Shred sign:** The borders of the consolidated lung mass are irregular and "shredded" as they interface with the surrounding fluid and aerated lung.
*   **Air bronchograms:** Small, punctate hyperechoic foci can be seen within the consolidated lung tissue, representing air-filled bronchi.

These findings collectively indicate both a significant pleural effusion and underlying alveolar consolidation.
