# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large hypoechoic area, indicative of a pleural effusion. Floating within this fluid collection is a mass of lung tissue that is irregular and undulating, which is a classic "jellyfish sign." The lung parenchyma within this floating mass appears solid and echogenic, showing tissue-like hepatization. These findings are characteristic of both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation in the dependent lung zone.
