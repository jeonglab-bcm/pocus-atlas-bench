1.  **Effusion signs**: A prominent **quad sign** is visible, characterized by the anechoic (black) space between the pleural line (top) and the lung line (bottom). Within this fluid collection, the lung tissue appears to float and undulate, which is a clear **jellyfish sign**.

Additionally, small hyperechoic foci are visible within the hepatized lung, which are **air bronchograms**.

Because both significant fluid collection and solid lung consolidation signs are present, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = both**
