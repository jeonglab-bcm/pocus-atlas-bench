# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The lung ultrasound (LUS) images show several characteristic signs of a PLAPS pattern in the posterior lung zone:

*   **Effusion signs**: There is a large, hypoechoic/anechoic area at the bottom of the frame, indicating a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a distinct, undulated, and shredded-looking piece of lung tissue that appears to be "floating," which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs**: The lung parenchyma above the fluid collection appears dense and echogenic, lacking normal aeration artifacts, which is consistent with **tissue-like hepatization**. There is also a clear **shred sign** visible at the irregular, jagged border between the consolidated/atelectatic lung tissue and the surrounding area.

Because both pleural effusion signs (jellyfish sign) and consolidation signs (hepatization, shred sign) are clearly visible, the classification is:

**plaps = true**
**type = both**
