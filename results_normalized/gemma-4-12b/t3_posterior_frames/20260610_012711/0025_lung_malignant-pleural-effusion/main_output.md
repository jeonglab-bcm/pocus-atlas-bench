# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large area of **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue rather than aerated lung. Within this consolidated area, multiple small, punctate anechoic (black) spots are visible, which are characteristic **air bronchograms**. The border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding areas is irregular, exhibiting a **shred sign**. These findings are classic indicators of pulmonary consolidation.
