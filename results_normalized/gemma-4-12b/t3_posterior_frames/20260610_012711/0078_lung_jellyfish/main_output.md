# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The lung ultrasound images of the right posterior zone show several characteristic signs of a PLAPS pattern:

1.  **Effusion signs**: There is a large, well-defined anechoic (black) space in the dependent region, indicating a significant **pleural effusion**. A hyperechoic **pleural line** is visible at the superior margin of this fluid collection. The anechoic space is bounded by the pleural line, the lung line (the boundary of the collapsed lung), and the rib shadows, creating a clear **quad sign**.
2.  **Consolidation signs**: Within the fluid-filled space, the lung tissue appears dense, solid, and echogenic, which is characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**. This indicates that the collapsed lung has become consolidated.
3.  **Jellyfish sign**: In the sequence, the hepatized lung tissue appears to move and undulate within the surrounding pleural fluid, mimicking the appearance of a jellyfish, which is a classic sign of consolidated lung within an effusion.

Based on these observations, the image shows both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
