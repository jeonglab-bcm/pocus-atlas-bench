# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

In the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) images of the right posterior/lateral zone, there is a clear **PLAPS pattern**.

**Observations:**
*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: The posterior/dependent lung zone displays a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue, indicating that the normally air-filled lung is filled with fluid/exudate (hepatized).
*   **Air bronchograms**: Within the consolidated area, there are visible small hyperechoic (bright) punctate and linear foci, which represent air trapped in the bronchi within the consolidated lung.
*   **Shred sign**: The border between the consolidated lung and the aerated lung is irregular and "shredded" in several frames.
*   **Effusion**: There is no clear anechoic (black) space or quad sign, suggesting that the primary finding is consolidation rather than a significant free pleural effusion.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "consolidation"**
