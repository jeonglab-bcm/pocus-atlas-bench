# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

Based on the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) video sequence of a posterior lung zone, here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) space representing a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, the consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and undulating, which is a classic representation of the **jellyfish sign**. The presence of a large anechoic space bounded by the pleural line and the lung mass also suggests a **quad sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** Below the effusion, there is a large area of lung parenchyma that lacks normal aeration. It appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue, which is characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**. The borders of this consolidated area are irregular and "shredded," indicating the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of both a pleural effusion and hepatized consolidated lung tissue clearly identifies a PLAPS pattern.
