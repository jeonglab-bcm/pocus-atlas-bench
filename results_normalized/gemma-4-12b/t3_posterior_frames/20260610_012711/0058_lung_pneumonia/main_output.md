# 0058_lung_pneumonia

The ultrasound images show a posterior lung zone with clear signs of both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation. 

Specifically, there is a large anechoic (black) fluid collection in the dependent region, within which a floating, irregular piece of lung tissue is visible, characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**. The lung tissue itself has lost its normal aerated appearance and instead appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver texture (**tissue-like hepatization**). Within this consolidated area, small bright spots are visible, which represent **air bronchograms**. Additionally, a quadrangular space can be observed between the pleural line and the consolidated lung tissue, forming the **quad sign**. The irregular border of the consolidated lung is also indicative of a **shred sign**.

Based on these observations:
**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
