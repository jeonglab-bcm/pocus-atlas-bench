# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

The ultrasound image shows a large anechoic (black) space bounded by the pleural line, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, undulating mass of consolidated lung tissue, which is a classic **jellyfish sign**. The consolidated lung itself has a solid, liver-like texture, indicating **tissue-like hepatization**. The irregular border between the consolidated tissue and the fluid/aerated lung also suggests a **shred sign**.

Because both clear pleural effusion signs and consolidation signs are present simultaneously in the posterior/lateral lung zone, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
