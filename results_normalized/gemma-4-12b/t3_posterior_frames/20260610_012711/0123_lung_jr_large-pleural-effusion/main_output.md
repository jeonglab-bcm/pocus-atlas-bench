# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) show a clear combination of both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation.

Specifically, there is a large anechoic (black) space representing a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, there is a distinct area of lung tissue that appears solid and grey, exhibiting **tissue-like hepatization**. This consolidated lung tissue floats within the effusion, demonstrating the **jellyfish sign**. The irregular, jagged border where the consolidated lung meets the aerated lung is a classic **shred sign**. Additionally, small hyperechoic (bright) punctate foci can be seen within the hepatized lung, representing **air bronchograms**.

Conclusion:
**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
