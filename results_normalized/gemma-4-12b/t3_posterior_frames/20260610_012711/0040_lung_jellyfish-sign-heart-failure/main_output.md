# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

The lung ultrasound (LUS) images show a clear **PLAPS pattern** in the posterior lung zone.

Specifically, the following features are observed:
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: The superior portion of the image shows lung parenchyma that has lost its normal aeration artifacts and appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue.
- **Pleural effusion signs**: There is a large anechoic (black) area below the consolidated lung. The consolidated lung tissue appears to be "floating" within this fluid, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**. The large fluid collection also contributes to a **quad sign** as it is bounded by the pleural line, the lung line, and rib shadows.

Because both consolidation and effusion are clearly visible, the classification is:
**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
