# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

The images demonstrate a typical PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) pattern in the posterior lung zone.

**Observations:**
- **Effusion signs**: There is a large, hypoechoic (dark) area representing a significant pleural effusion in the dependent zone. The presence of irregular, floating-looking lung tissue within this fluid is suggestive of a jellyfish sign.
- **Consolidation signs**: The lung parenchyma in the posterior region shows clear tissue-like hepatization, appearing solid rather than aerated. A prominent "shred sign" is visible, characterized by the irregular, jagged border between the consolidated lung and the more aerated lung tissue above it.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
