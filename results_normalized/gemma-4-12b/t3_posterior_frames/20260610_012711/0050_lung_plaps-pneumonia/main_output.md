# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

Based on the analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) images:

1.  **Hepatization**: The lung parenchyma in the posterior/lateral zone shows a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue, indicating it has lost its normal aeration.
2.  **Shred Sign**: There is a prominent, irregular, and "shredded" border between the hepatized lung and the deeper, more aerated lung tissue. This is a classic indicator of consolidation.
3.  **Air Bronchograms**: Small, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within the hepatized lung, representing air-filled bronchi.
4.  **Effusion**: There is a large anechoic (black) space adjacent to the consolidated lung, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = both**
