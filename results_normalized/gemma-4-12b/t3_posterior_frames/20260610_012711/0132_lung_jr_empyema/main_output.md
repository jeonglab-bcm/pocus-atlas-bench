# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

The ultrasound images show a posterior lung zone exhibiting a clear **PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome)** pattern.

Specifically, there is prominent **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue, which is a hallmark of alveolar consolidation. Additionally, an irregular, "shredded" border between the consolidated and aerated lung regions can be seen (**shred sign**). While there is a large hypoechoic area, the predominant finding is the consolidated tissue.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = consolidation**
