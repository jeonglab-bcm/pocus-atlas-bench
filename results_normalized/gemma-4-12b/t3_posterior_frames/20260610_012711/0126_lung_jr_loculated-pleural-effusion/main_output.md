# 0126_lung_jr_loculated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images show a posterior lung zone with several characteristic features of the PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) pattern:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) space representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a floating, undulating, and shredded piece of lung tissue is visible, which is a classic **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue itself appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue, which is known as **tissue-like hepatization**. There is an irregular, shredded appearance at its deep border (shred sign).

Given the presence of both a clear pleural effusion with a jellyfish sign and consolidated, hepatized lung tissue, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
