# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large anechoic fluid collection, indicative of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a distinct, hyperechoic, and irregular mass of lung tissue that exhibits the **jellyfish sign**, representing atelectatic lung floating in fluid. The lung tissue itself shows **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic (resembling liver texture), which signifies alveolar consolidation.

Because both a significant fluid collection (effusion) and consolidated/atelectatic lung (hepatization/jellyfish sign) are clearly visible, this is a classic presentation of a PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
