# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

Based on the lung ultrasound (LUS) image provided:

The image shows a classic **PLAPS** pattern in the posterior/lateral lung zone. Specifically, I observe:
*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma has a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue, indicating a lack of normal aeration.
*   **Air bronchograms**: There are multiple small, punctate hypoechoic/anechoic areas within the hepatized tissue, representing air-filled bronchi.
*   **Shred sign**: The border between the consolidated lung and the adjacent aerated lung (visible towards the bottom left) is irregular and "shredded."
*   There is no clear evidence of a large pleural effusion (no distinct quad sign or jellyfish sign is prominent in this view), making it primarily a consolidation.

Conclusion:
**plaps = true**
**type = "consolidation"**
