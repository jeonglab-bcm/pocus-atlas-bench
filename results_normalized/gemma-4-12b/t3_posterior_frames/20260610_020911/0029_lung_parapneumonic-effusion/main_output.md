# 0029_lung_parapneumonic-effusion

The ultrasound images show a large anechoic (dark) area in the posterior, dependent lung zone, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is an irregular, echogenic mass of lung tissue that appears to be "floating" and undulating. This is the classic **jellyfish sign**, which indicates atelectatic lung segments within an effusion. The lung tissue itself shows **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic like liver tissue, with a visible **shred sign** at its irregular deep border. These combined features are definitive for PLAPS.
