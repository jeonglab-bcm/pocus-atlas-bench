# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a prominent anechoic (black) space in the dependent region, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within and adjacent to this fluid collection, there is a region of tissue-like, echogenic lung parenchyma that lacks normal aeration artifacts, representing **hepatization** (consolidation). The presence of these features, including the distinct lung line and the "quad sign" appearance of the fluid collection, confirms a PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = both
