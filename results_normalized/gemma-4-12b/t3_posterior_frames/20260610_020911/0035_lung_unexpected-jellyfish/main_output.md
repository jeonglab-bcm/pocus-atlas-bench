# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound:

1.  **Effusion signs**: There is a large, prominent **anechoic (black) space** bounded by a bright pleural line superiorly and a lung line inferiorly, which is indicative of a pleural effusion (a variation of the **quad sign**). Within this fluid collection, there is a distinct, undulating mass of lung tissue that appears to be floating, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
2.  **Consolidation signs**: The lung tissue within the fluid collection exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic (similar to liver tissue) rather than aerated. There is also an irregular border between the hepatized lung and the surrounding fluid, suggesting a **shred sign**.

Since both significant fluid collection (effusion) and solid, hepatized lung tissue (consolidation) are clearly visible in the posterior lung zone, the pattern is classified as both.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
