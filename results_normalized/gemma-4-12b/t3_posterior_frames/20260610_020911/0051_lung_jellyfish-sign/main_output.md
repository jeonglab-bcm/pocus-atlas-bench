# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The ultrasound sequence clearly demonstrates a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a prominent, hyperechoic, and solid-appearing lung structure that exhibits tissue-like hepatization (it looks like solid organ tissue rather than aerated lung). This structure is seen floating and undulating within the fluid, which is a classic "jellyfish sign." These findings confirm both the presence of pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation.
