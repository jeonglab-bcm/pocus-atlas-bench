# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

In this posterior lung ultrasound (LUS) image, there is a large, hypoechoic (dark) fluid collection in the dependent zone, characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. 

Notably, there is a floating, undulating, hyperechoic structure suspended within this fluid. This is the classic **jellyfish sign**, which indicates atelectatic lung tissue floating in a pleural effusion. Additionally, the consolidated lung tissue itself appears solid and echogenic, demonstrating **tissue-like hepatization**. The clear separation between the pleural line and the consolidated lung within the fluid collection also suggests a **quad sign**.

Because both significant effusion and consolidation (with jellyfish sign) are clearly visible, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
