# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone demonstrate clear signs of alveolar consolidation.

**Observations:**
*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, closely resembling the texture of the liver. This indicates that the alveoli are filled with fluid/cells rather than air.
*   **Shred sign:** An irregular, "shredded" border is visible between the consolidated lung tissue and the adjacent aerated lung.
*   **Air bronchograms:** Small, punctate hyperechoic foci can be seen within the hepatized area, representing air-filled bronchi.
*   There is no clear evidence of a large pleural effusion (no distinct quad sign or jellyfish sign is prominent), making consolidation the primary finding.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "consolidation"
