# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

Based on the sequential lung ultrasound (LUS) images provided, here is the analysis of the posterior lung zone:

**Observations:**
*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma in the posterior/dependent zone appears solid and echogenic, closely resembling the texture of liver tissue. This indicates significant alveolar consolidation.
*   **Air bronchograms**: Within the hepatized area, several bright, punctate, and linear hyperechoic foci are visible, which represent air-filled bronchi within the consolidated lung.
*   **Shred sign**: The interface between the consolidated lung and the adjacent aerated lung shows an irregular, "shredded" border.
*   **Effusion signs**: There is a clear pleural line and a lung line (the border of the consolidated area). The hypoechoic space between them, combined with the way the consolidated lung appears to "float" or undulate, is highly suggestive of a **jellyfish sign** and a **quad sign**, indicating a surrounding pleural effusion.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = both**
