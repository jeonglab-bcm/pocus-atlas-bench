# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

The ultrasound video shows a clear PLAPS pattern in the posterior lung zone.

**Observations:**
- **Consolidation signs**: There is a large, hyperechoic area with a "hepatized" texture, meaning the lung tissue appears solid like liver tissue. A distinct "shred sign" is visible where the consolidated tissue meets the aerated lung, showing an irregular, jagged border. Furthermore, several small, white punctate foci are visible within the consolidated area, which are air bronchograms.
- **Effusion signs**: There is a clear anechoic (black) space above the consolidated tissue and below the pleural line, indicating a pleural effusion. The consolidated tissue also shows a slight, undulating "jellyfish-like" movement within this fluid collection.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "both"
