# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images show a posterior lung zone with clear signs of **consolidation**.

**Observations:**
*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: The posterior/lateral lung zone appears solid and echogenic, mimicking the texture of liver tissue (hepatized) instead of normal aerated lung.
*   **Shred sign**: There is a jagged, irregular border between the consolidated lung tissue and the aerated lung above it.
*   **Air bronchograms**: Small, punctate hyperechoic (white) spots are visible within the hepatized area, representing air trapped in the bronchi.

Because these hallmarks of consolidation are present, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "consolidation"**
