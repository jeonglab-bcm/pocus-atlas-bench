# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound sequence demonstrates a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a distinct, undulating, and irregularly shaped echogenic mass of lung tissue that appears to be "floating." This is a classic **jellyfish sign**, indicating consolidated lung tissue within a surrounding effusion.

Additionally, the lung parenchyma situated above the fluid collection shows a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue, known as **tissue-like hepatization**, which indicates alveolar consolidation. The irregular border between the consolidated lung and the fluid/aerated lung may also suggest a **shred sign**.

Based on these findings, both pleural effusion and consolidation are clearly visible in the posterior lung zone.
