# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip of the right posterior lung zone, the following observations are made:

1.  **Consolidation signs**: The posterior lung zone demonstrates **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue. There is a prominent **shred sign**, characterized by an irregular, "shredded" border between the consolidated lung and the aerated lung. Additionally, small hyperechoic foci can be seen within the hepatized tissue, representing **air bronchograms**.
2.  **Effusion signs**: There is a large anechoic (black) space surrounding the consolidated lung, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. The consolidated lung appears to float within this fluid, which can be described as a **jellyfish sign**.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = both**
