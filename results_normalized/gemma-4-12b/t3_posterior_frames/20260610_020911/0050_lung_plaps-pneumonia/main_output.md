# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

The ultrasound image shows a clear PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) pattern in the posterior/lateral lung zone.

Specifically:
- **Consolidation signs**: There is prominent **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma has a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue. A distinct **shred sign** is visible, representing the irregular, jagged border between the consolidated lung and the aerated lung. Small punctate hyperechoic foci are also visible, consistent with **air bronchograms**.
- **Effusion signs**: There is a large anechoic area surrounding the consolidated lung, characteristic of a pleural effusion. The appearance of the consolidated lung "floating" within this fluid collection is consistent with a **jellyfish sign**. The bounded anechoic space also contributes to a **quad sign**.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = both
