# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The images show a posterior lung zone ultrasound (LUS) with characteristic signs of a Posterior Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome (PLAPS). 

In the dependent (posterior) region, there is a large anechoic (black) area representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, a mass of solid-looking, undulating lung tissue is visible, which is the classic **jellyfish sign**. This tissue exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, as it appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver parenchyma, rather than having normal aerated lung features. The presence of both a clear fluid collection (effusion) and consolidated lung tissue (consolidation) within that fluid confirms the diagnosis.

**plaps = true**
**type = both**
