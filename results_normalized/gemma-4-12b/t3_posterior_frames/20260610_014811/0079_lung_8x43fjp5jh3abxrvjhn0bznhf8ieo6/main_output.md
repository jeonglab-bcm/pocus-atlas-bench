The lung ultrasound image shows a large anechoic (black) space, which is indicative of a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, irregular, hyperechoic structure with a "shredded" border. This is a classic **jellyfish sign**, representing atelectatic lung tissue suspended in the fluid. The appearance of this tissue is solid and echogenic, consistent with **tissue-like hepatization** (consolidation). 

Because both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are clearly visible, the findings meet the criteria for PLAPS.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = both**
