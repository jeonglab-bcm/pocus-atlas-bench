# 0100_lung_hemothorax

In the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) images of the posterior lung zone, the following signs are observed:

*   **Effusion signs**: There is a large, predominantly anechoic (black) space representing a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a floating, undulating mass of consolidated lung tissue is clearly visible, which is a classic **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs**: The lung tissue within the fluid is clearly **tissue-like (hepatized)**, appearing solid and echogenic like liver parenchyma, indicating dense consolidation/atelectasis. Furthermore, there are bright, punctate hyperechoic foci within this hepatized area, consistent with **air bronchograms**.

Because both a large pleural effusion with a jellyfish sign and consolidated, hepatized lung tissue with air bronchograms are present, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
