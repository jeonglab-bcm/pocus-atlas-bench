# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

In the provided ultrasound sequence of the posterior lung zone, several diagnostic signs of **PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome)** are clearly visible:

1.  **Effusion signs**: There is a large, prominent anechoic (black) space representing a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, a floating, undulating, and somewhat irregular echogenic structure is observed, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
2.  **Consolidation signs**: The floating structure within the effusion shows **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic like liver tissue. It also exhibits an irregular **shred sign** at its borders. Some punctate hyperechoic foci can be seen within this consolidated tissue, suggesting **air bronchograms**.

The presence of both a large pleural effusion and a hepatized "jellyfish" of consolidated lung confirms a positive PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = both
