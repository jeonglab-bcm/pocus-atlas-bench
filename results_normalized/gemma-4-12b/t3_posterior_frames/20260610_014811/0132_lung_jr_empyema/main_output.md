# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

Based on the ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone, here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma in the central and lower-left portion of the image appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue. This is a clear sign of alveolar consolidation.
*   **Shred sign**: There is a prominent irregular, "shredded" border between the consolidated (hepatized) lung and the adjacent darker region, which is characteristic of the shred sign.
*   **Effusion signs**: There is a large, predominantly anechoic (black) space surrounding the consolidated lung area, which is highly suggestive of a pleural effusion in this posterior/dependent position.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
