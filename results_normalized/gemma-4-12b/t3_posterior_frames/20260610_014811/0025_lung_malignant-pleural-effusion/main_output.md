# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images show a posterior lung zone with clear signs of a PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) pattern.

**Observations:**
*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma in the central and left portions of the image appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of liver tissue (hepatized lung).
*   **Air bronchograms**: Numerous small, punctate, and linear anechoic (black) foci are visible within the hepatized lung tissue, representing air-filled bronchi.
*   **Shred sign**: The deep border of the consolidated area is irregular and "shredded" where it meets adjacent lung tissue.
*   **Effusion signs**: There is a prominent, large, anechoic (black) hypoechoic area at the base of the lung (bottom of the image), indicating a pleural effusion. Some of the consolidated lung tissue appears to be floating within this fluid, reminiscent of a **jellyfish sign**.

Conclusion:
**plaps = true**
**type = both**
