# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound video shows a posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) with clear signs of both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) area representing pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a floating, undulating, hyperechoic structure is visible, which is the **jellyfish sign** (representing atelectatic lung floating in fluid). The fluid collection is bounded by the pleural line superiorly and the lung line inferiorly, forming a **quad sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma in the lower portion of the frame appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver texture, which is **tissue-like hepatization**. There is a jagged, irregular border between the consolidated lung and the adjacent aerated lung, known as the **shred sign**.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "both"
