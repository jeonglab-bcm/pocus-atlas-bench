# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

The ultrasound images demonstrate a clear PLAPS pattern in the posterior lung zone. 

**Observations:**
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) space in the posterior dependent zone, bounded superiorly by a pleural line and inferiorly by a lung line, which constitutes a clear **quad sign**. 
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma adjacent to the fluid collection exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic like liver tissue. There is a discernible **shred sign** at the irregular border between the consolidated and aerated lung. Furthermore, several hyperechoic punctate foci are visible within the hepatized lung, representing **air bronchograms**.

Because both prominent fluid collection (effusion) and solid lung parenchyma (consolidation) are clearly visible, the classification is as follows:

**plaps = true**
**type = "both"**
