# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

Based on the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) images of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line**: A clear, hyperechoic (bright) linear structure is visible at the top of the lung field.
*   **Tissue-like Hepatization**: Deep to the pleural line, the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, losing the normal aeration artifacts (A-lines) and resembling solid organ tissue like the liver.
*   **Jellyfish Sign**: There is a prominent, vertically oriented, and slightly undulating piece of consolidated lung tissue "floating" in the dependent zone, which is a classic jellyfish sign. This indicates consolidated lung tissue within a surrounding fluid or significant atelectasis.
*   **Shred Sign**: There is an irregular, "shredded" border between the consolidated, hepatized lung and the rest of the aerated lung.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = both** (due to the presence of clear hepatization and the jellyfish sign, which indicates both consolidation and an effusion/significant dependent atelectasis).
