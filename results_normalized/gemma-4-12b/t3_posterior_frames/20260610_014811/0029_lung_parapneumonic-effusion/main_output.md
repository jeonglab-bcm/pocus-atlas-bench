# 0029_lung_parapneumonic-effusion

Based on the ultrasound images provided of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the assessment:

**Observations:**
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma in the dependent (posterior/lateral) region appears solid and echogenic, lacking the normal aeration artifacts. It has a texture similar to liver tissue, which is a classic sign of alveolar consolidation.
- **Shred sign**: There is an irregular, "shredded" deep border between the consolidated (hepatized) lung and the more aerated portion of the lung.
- **Effusion signs**: There is no clear evidence of a large pleural effusion, such as a distinct quad sign or a "jellyfish" floating in a significant anechoic space. The area is dominated by solid-appearing lung tissue.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "consolidation"**
