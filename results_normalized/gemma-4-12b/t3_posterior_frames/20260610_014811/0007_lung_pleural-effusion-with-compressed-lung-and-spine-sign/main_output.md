# 0007_lung_pleural-effusion-with-compressed-lung-and-spine-sign

Based on the sequential lung ultrasound (LUS) images provided, I observe the following:

1.  **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma in the posterior/lateral zone appears solid and echogenic, closely resembling the texture of liver tissue. This is a hallmark of alveolar consolidation.
2.  **Air bronchograms**: Small, punctate hyperechoic (bright) foci are visible within the hepatized lung, representing air-filled bronchi in consolidated tissue.
3.  **Pleural effusion**: There is a large, prominent anechoic (black) space adjacent to and beneath the hepatized lung, indicating a significant pleural fluid collection.
4.  **Jellyfish sign**: The hepatized lung appears as a somewhat rounded, undulating mass floating within the surrounding pleural effusion.

Because both significant consolidation (hepatization) and a large pleural effusion are clearly visible, the PLAPS pattern is present.

plaps = true
