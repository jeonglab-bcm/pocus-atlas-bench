# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

The ultrasound images show a large anechoic (black) area in the posterior/lateral lung zone, indicating a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a distinct, convex, and solid-appearing (hepatized) piece of lung tissue that appears to "float." This is the characteristic **jellyfish sign**, which represents consolidated lung tissue within a surrounding effusion. 

Because both a clear fluid collection and signs of lung consolidation (hepatized tissue floating in fluid) are present, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "both"
