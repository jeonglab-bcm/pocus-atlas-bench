# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

The ultrasound images show a classic PLAPS pattern in the posterior lung zone. 

Key observations include:
*   **Pleural Effusion:** A large anechoic (black) space is visible, indicating a significant pleural effusion.
*   **Jellyfish Sign:** Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, undulating mass of lung tissue that resembles a "jellyfish" in water.
*   **Tissue-like Hepatization:** The lung tissue visible within the effusion appears solid and echogenic, similar in texture to the liver (hepatized), indicating alveolar consolidation.
*   **Shred Sign:** The borders of the consolidated lung segment are irregular and "shredded" as they interact with the surrounding fluid and aerated lung.

Conclusion:
plaps = true
type = both
