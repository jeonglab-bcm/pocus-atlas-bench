# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound sequence shows a large anechoic (black) space in the posterior lung zone, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, there is a floating, undulating, and irregular soft-tissue structure, which is a clear **jellyfish sign**. This represents a piece of collapsed, atelectatic lung floating within the fluid collection. Additionally, the surrounding lung parenchyma exhibits a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue, known as **tissue-like hepatization**, indicating significant alveolar consolidation.

Since both jellyfish sign (effusion) and hepatization (consolidation) are clearly visible, the PLAPS pattern is present.

**Conclusion:**
plaps = true
type = "both"
