# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

In this lung ultrasound (LUS) image of the posterior lung zone, several signs characteristic of PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) are visible:

*   **Effusion signs**: There is a large anechoic (black) space indicating a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, the lung tissue appears to be floating and undulating, which is a classic **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs**: The lung parenchyma adjacent to the fluid has a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue, known as **tissue-like hepatization**. Small, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within this consolidated lung, representing **air bronchograms**.

Because both significant fluid collection and lung consolidation are present, the findings are characteristic of PLAPS.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
