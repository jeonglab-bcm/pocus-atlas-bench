# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on a systematic analysis of the provided M-mode ultrasound frames:

**Frame-by-frame Observation:**
In all six frames, the ultrasound image shows a consistent, uniform pattern of horizontal stripes. There is no shimmering, granular texture, or movement observed below the pleural line. The pattern remains static and identical throughout the sequence.

**Assessment of Signs:**

*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** None. There is no shimmering or granular "beach" pattern (seashore sign) visible.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** Present. The images demonstrate a clear **"barcode" or "stratosphere" sign**, characterized by horizontal lines only. This indicates a lack of movement between the visceral and parietal pleura.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** None. There is no visible transition point where the pattern changes from sliding to non-sliding within the frame.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The presence of the barcode sign is highly indicative of a pneumothorax.
