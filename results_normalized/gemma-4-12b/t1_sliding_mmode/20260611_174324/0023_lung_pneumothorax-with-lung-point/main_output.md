# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video, here is an analysis of the findings:

*   **Pleural Line Analysis:** The pleural line (the bright, horizontal interface) shows a **shimmering and granular texture** rather than a perfectly smooth, static appearance.
*   **B-lines:** There are multiple vertical, hyperechoic **B-lines** originating from the pleural line.
*   **Dynamics between frames:** When comparing consecutive frames, the position and intensity of the B-lines and the underlying **sub-pleural speckle pattern change slightly**. This indicates movement of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura.
*   **Lung Point:** There is no clear visible transition point in this specific view where sliding suddenly stops or starts; the shimmering movement is consistent across the visible pleural line.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
