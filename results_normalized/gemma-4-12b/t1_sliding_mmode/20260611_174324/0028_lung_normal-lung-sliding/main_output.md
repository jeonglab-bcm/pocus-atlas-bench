# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip, here are the observations:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears as a smooth, sharply defined, and very static hyperechoic (bright white) horizontal line. There is no visible shimmering, granular texture, or "grainy" movement characteristic of pleural sliding.
*   **Artifacts:** There are prominent horizontal A-lines visible below the pleural line. These A-lines remain perfectly static and do not shift or change their position between any of the consecutive frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The pattern of the tissue and artifacts below the pleural line shows no significant variation between frames, indicating a lack of underlying pulmonary motion relative to the chest wall.
*   **Absence of Sliding Signs:** There is no evidence of the "seashore sign" (a granular, sandy pattern below the pleural line) or comet-tail/B-line artifacts originating from a moving surface.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The static nature of the pleural line and the perfectly stationary A-lines are classic signs of a lack of sliding, which can occur in conditions such as a pneumothorax.
