# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence:

**Observations:**
*   **M-mode Analysis:** The top portion of the images displays a prominent **barcode sign** (also known as the stratosphere sign). This is characterized by uniform, horizontal parallel lines across the entire M-mode trace. There is a lack of a "sandy beach" (seashore) pattern, which would be seen as a granular, shimmering texture below the pleural line if sliding were occurring.
*   **B-mode Analysis:** The pleural line appears relatively smooth and static across the frames. There is no visible shimmering, granular texture, or significant speckle variation in the sub-pleural region between consecutive frames.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.** The presence of the barcode sign on M-mode is a classic indicator of absent pleural sliding, which can be seen in conditions such as a pneumothorax or a large pleural effusion.
