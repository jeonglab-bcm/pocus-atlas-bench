### Frame-by-Frame Observations:
*   **B-mode (Top Panel):** Throughout all 12 frames, the pleural line appears as a smooth, sharply defined, and static interface. There is no visible shimmering or granular texture.
*   **M-mode (Bottom Panel):** The M-mode trace consistently displays a **barcode (stratosphere) sign**. This is characterized by parallel, horizontal lines throughout the entire recording. There is no "seashore" sign (sandy, granular pattern below the pleural line) that would indicate the movement of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The sub-pleural speckle pattern remains virtually unchanged between consecutive frames, indicating a lack of motion.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is absent.** 

The presence of the **barcode / stratosphere sign** on M-mode, combined with a static, smooth pleural line on B-mode, is a classic indication of a lack of pleural sliding, which can be seen in conditions like pneumothorax or pleural effusion.
