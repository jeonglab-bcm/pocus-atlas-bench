# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Analysis of frames:**
*   **Frame 1-5:** In all frames, there is a prominent horizontal hyperechoic line representing the pleural line. Below this line, there are horizontal reverberation artifacts known as A-lines. There is also a very clear, vertical hyperechoic artifact originating from a specific point on the pleural line, which is a **B-line**.

**Assessment of Pleural Sliding:**
*   **Signs of sliding:** The pleural line does not exhibit significant shimmering or a granular texture. It remains relatively flat and well-defined. While a B-line is present, it does not inherently confirm the presence of sliding in every context, especially when the line itself appears static.
*   **Signs of sliding absent:** The pleural line appears **smooth, sharply defined, and static** across the five frames. There is no visible "shimmering" or granular movement of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura. The A-lines also appear to remain in a stable, static position relative to the pleural line across the sequence.
*   **Signs of both:** There is no visible "lung point" or transition from sliding to non-sliding in this specific view.

**Conclusion:**
Based on the smooth, static appearance of the pleural line and the lack of shimmering across the frames, **pleural sliding is absent**.
