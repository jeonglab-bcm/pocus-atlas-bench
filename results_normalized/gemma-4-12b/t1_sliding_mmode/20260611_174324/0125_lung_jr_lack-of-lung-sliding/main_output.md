# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) frames, here is the observation:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** There is a visible, bright, and slightly granular/shimmering pleural line.
*   **B-lines:** Multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) are originating from the pleural line. These B-lines are clearly moving and shifting their position relative to the surrounding tissue between consecutive frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line is dynamic and shows variation from frame to frame, rather than remaining perfectly static.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The movement of the B-lines and the shimmering texture of the pleural line are clear indicators of active sliding.
