# 0119_lung_jr_normal-lung-slide

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the ultrasound clip:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Frames 1, 2, and 3:** The pleural line is clearly visible as a bright, horizontal interface. While it appears relatively smooth and sharply defined, there is some granular speckle visible below it, which can sometimes indicate subtle shimmering.
*   **Frame 4:** A distinct change is observed. A hypoechoic (darker) zone appears below a segment of the pleural line. This creates a visible transition point where the pleural line appears to "stop" or change character as it meets this darker area.

**Conclusion:**
**Both (lung point)**

The sequence demonstrates a classic **lung point**. You can see a transition point where the pleural line exhibits characteristics of sliding on one side and becomes fixed/static on the other (where the darker, collapsed lung area appears in Frame 4). This is a definitive sign of a pneumothorax.
