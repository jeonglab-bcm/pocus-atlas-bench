**Assessment of Signs:**

*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** There is no "barcode" or "stratosphere" sign; the artifacts are vertical, not horizontal. The line is not perfectly static or sharply smooth.
*   **Signs of BOTH:** There is no visible transition point (lung point) where the pleural line changes from shimmering to static within this view.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **PRESENT**.
