# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided ultrasound images, here is the observation:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line appears as a smooth, sharply defined, and hyperechoic (bright) horizontal line. It does not exhibit the characteristic "shimmering" or granular texture associated with active sliding.
*   **Motion:** Across all six frames, the pleural line and the horizontal A-lines beneath it remain perfectly static. There is no detectable movement or shift in the interface.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern in the sub-pleural space shows no variation or "shimmer" between consecutive frames.
*   **Artifacts:** There are no visible B-lines (comet-tail artifacts) originating from the pleural line in these views.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The findings are consistent with a smooth, static pleural line and stable A-lines, which are characteristic signs of absent sliding.
