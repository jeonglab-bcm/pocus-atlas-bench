# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line Texture:** The pleural line (the bright horizontal interface) is not perfectly smooth or static; it exhibits a shimmering, granular appearance as it moves across the four frames.
*   **B-lines:** A prominent vertical hyperechoic artifact (B-line) originates from the pleural line and extends deep into the lung parenchyma. The presence of this dynamic artifact is a strong indicator of pleural movement.
*   **Sub-pleural Speckle:** There is a subtle but observable variation in the speckle pattern below the pleural line between consecutive frames, further supporting motion.
*   **A-lines:** The horizontal artifacts above the pleural line do not appear perfectly fixed; they show slight shifts consistent with sliding.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The shimmering/granular texture of the pleural line and the presence of dynamic B-lines are the primary indicators of this finding.
