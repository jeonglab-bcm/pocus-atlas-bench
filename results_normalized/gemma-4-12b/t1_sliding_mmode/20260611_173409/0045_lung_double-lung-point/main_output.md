# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip:

### Frame-by-Frame Observations:
*   **Pleural Line Appearance:** In all frames, the pleural line is visible as a bright, horizontal echogenic interface. It remains sharply defined and maintains a very consistent position throughout the sequence.
*   **Motion Analysis:** There is no noticeable "shimmering" or granular movement of the pleural line as it would be expected with normal lung sliding. The interface appears static.

*   **Continuity:** There is no transition point visible where sliding is present in one part of the image and absent in another (no "lung point").

### Assessment of Signs:
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** None. The pleural line is not shimmering or granular.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
    *   **Smooth, sharply defined, static pleural line:** Present.
    *   **A-lines that remain perfectly static:** Present.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** None. There is no transition point or alternating pattern.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The static nature of the pleural line and the presence of stable A-lines are characteristic of a lack of sliding, which can be a clinical sign of conditions such as a pneumothorax.
