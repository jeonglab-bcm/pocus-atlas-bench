Actually, there might be a very subtle shimmer. Let's look at the speckles. They don't seem to change much at all. They look like a frozen frame sequence.
The "shimmering" would be a dynamic change in the grainy texture. Here, the grain seems almost identical in every frame.
This is often seen in pneumothorax or with poor probe contact, but in the context of a test, it's likely meant to show "absent sliding".
The A-lines are very regular and static.
The "barcode sign" or "stratosphere sign" usually refers to M-mode, which this isn't. This is B-mode.
In B-mode, absence of sliding is characterized by a static pleural line and static A-lines.
Presence of sliding is characterized by a shimmering/grainy pleural line.
Since there's no movement/shimmer between frames, it's absent.
