# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the ultrasound sequence, here are the observations:

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis:
*   **Frames 1-3:** The pleural line appears as a bright, hyperechoic horizontal interface. The speckle pattern in the area below the pleural line (representing the lung parenchyma) shows subtle, shimmering changes in texture and position between consecutive frames.
*   **Frames 4-6:** The sliding continues to be evident as the granular pattern below the pleural line shifts slightly. The pleural line itself maintains a somewhat textured, "shimmering" appearance rather than being a perfectly static, smooth line.
*   **Frames 7-8:** The movement persists, indicating continuous interaction between the parietal and visceral pleura.

### Assessment of Signs:
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
    *   **Speckle pattern:** There is a visible variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames. This is a hallmark of pleural sliding, as the lung tissue moves against the chest wall.
    *   **Pleural line texture:** The pleural line has a slightly granular, shimmering appearance rather than a fixed, sharp edge.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** None observed. The speckle pattern is not static (no barcode-like appearance in this B-mode view), and the pleural line is not perfectly fixed.
*   **Signs of BOTH (Lung Point):** Not observed in this specific view; the sliding appears consistent across the visible segment.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is present.** The dynamic changes in the speckle pattern below the pleural line across the frames confirm that the visceral pleura is sliding against the parietal pleura.
