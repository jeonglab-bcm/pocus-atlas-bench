# 0056_lung_lung-point

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright white horizontal interface near the 1cm mark) appears very sharp, well-defined, and static across all four frames. There is no noticeable shimmering or granular texture change between consecutive frames.
*   **Sub-pleural Speckle:** The speckle pattern and the B-line artifacts located below the pleural line remain virtually unchanged from frame 1 through frame 4.
*   **Transition:** While there is a large anechoic (black) area on the left, which may represent a large pleural effusion, there is no clear "lung point" transition where sliding is seen on one side and absent on the other; the sliding appears absent across the visible pleural line.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The static appearance of the pleural line and the lack of change in the underlying speckle pattern across the sequence are characteristic signs of absent sliding.
