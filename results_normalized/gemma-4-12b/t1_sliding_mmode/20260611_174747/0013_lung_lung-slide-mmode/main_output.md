# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on an analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) frames, here is the step-by-step assessment:

### Frame-by-Frame Observation
Across the sequential frames provided, the following features are consistently visible:
*   **M-mode (Top portion):** There is a distinct transition at approximately 2.5 cm depth. Above this line, there are regular horizontal lines. Below this line, the pattern changes to a **sandy, granular texture**. This is the classic **seashore sign**.
*   **B-mode (Bottom portion):** The pleural line is visible at the same depth. It appears slightly textured rather than a perfectly smooth, sharp static line.
*   **Temporal changes:** While the frames provided appear nearly identical, the presence of a clear seashore sign in M-mode is a definitive indicator of motion.

### Assessment of Signs
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
    *   **Seashore sign on M-mode:** The granular "sand" pattern below the pleural line is clearly visible, which is caused by the movement of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura.
    *   **Shimmering pleural line:** The pleural line has a textured, shimmering appearance in the B-mode images.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
    *   No **barcode (stratosphere) sign** is present, as the horizontal lines do not continue uniformly to the bottom of the M-mode display.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):**
    *   There is no visible transition point where the seashore sign turns into a barcode sign or vice versa within the captured field of view.

### Conclusion
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** 
The primary diagnostic evidence is the **seashore sign** on M-mode, characterized by the sandy granular appearance below the pleural line.
