*   **Transition Points:** There is no visible transition point where sliding appears on one side of the screen and is absent on the other, which would indicate a "lung point."

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The static nature of the pleural line and the underlying A-lines, combined with the lack of shimmering or speckle variation, are characteristic signs of absent pleural sliding (which can be seen in conditions like pneumothorax).
