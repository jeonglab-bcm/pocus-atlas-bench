# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames in the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Observations:**
*   **Speckle pattern variation:** There is a clear and significant change in the speckle pattern directly below the pleural line between consecutive frames. This dynamic change is a primary indicator of motion.
*   **Granular pleural line:** The pleural line appears textured and shimmering rather than a perfectly static, smooth line.
*   **B-lines/Comet-tail artifacts:** Vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line are visible and appear to be dynamic.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
