# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames provided in the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip:

**Observations:**
*   **Shimmering Pleural Line:** The pleural line (located approximately at the 3-4 cm depth mark) is not a static, sharp white line; it has a bright, textured, and "shimmering" appearance as it moves across the frames.
*   **Dynamic Speckle Pattern:** There is a clear and continuous variation in the speckle pattern of the tissue below the pleural line between consecutive frames. This indicates that the underlying lung is moving relative to the chest wall.
*   **B-lines:** Multiple comet-tail artifacts (B-lines) are visible originating from the pleural line, which are characteristic of a dynamic lung surface.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
