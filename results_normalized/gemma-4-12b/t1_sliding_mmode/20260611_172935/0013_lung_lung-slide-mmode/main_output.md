# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound (LUS) frames provided:

### Observations:
1.  **M-mode Analysis (Top portion):** The ultrasound shows a clear **seashore sign**. There are horizontal, static lines in the upper portion (representing the chest wall/soft tissue), and a granular, "sandy" pattern below the pleural line (between depths of 2.5 and 4 cm). This sandy pattern indicates the dynamic movement of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura.
2.  **B-mode Analysis (Bottom portion):** The pleural line appears somewhat shimmering and textured rather than perfectly smooth and static.
3.  **Comparison across frames:** While the frames are very similar, the presence of the seashore sign in the M-mode is a definitive diagnostic indicator that pleural sliding is occurring. There is no "barcode" or "stratosphere" sign (which would consist of horizontal lines throughout the entire M-mode image).
4.  **Lung Point:** There is no visible transition point where the sliding stops and sliding is absent on the same image; the entire field of view appears to exhibit sliding.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** This is primarily confirmed by the **seashore sign** on the M-mode scan.
