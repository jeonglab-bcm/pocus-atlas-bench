# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on an analysis of the sequential M-mode ultrasound frames:

**Observations:**
*   **M-mode pattern:** The area below the pleural line (the horizontal interface) consists of horizontal, parallel lines that remain static across all frames.
*   **Texture:** There is a notable lack of granular shimmering or "sand-like" texture below the pleural line.
*   **Sign identified:** These findings are characteristic of the **"barcode" or "stratosphere" sign**, where the lack of motion between the visceral and parietal pleura results in a uniform, horizontal pattern.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.**
