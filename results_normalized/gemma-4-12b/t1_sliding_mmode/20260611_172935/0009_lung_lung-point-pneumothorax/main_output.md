# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence, here are the observations:

*   **Pleural Line Appearance:** The pleural line (the bright horizontal/curved interface at the top of the lung parenchyma) appears smooth and sharply defined. It does not exhibit a "shimmering" or granular texture that changes over time.
*   **Artifact Analysis:** There are prominent horizontal artifacts (A-lines) below the pleural line. These lines remain in a fixed, static position relative to the pleural line across all 12 frames, showing no displacement or movement.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The granular speckle pattern of the tissue below the pleural line is consistent and unchanged between consecutive frames, indicating no movement of the lung surface against the parietal pleura.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.** The presence of a static pleural line with fixed A-lines and no shimmering is characteristic of a lack of sliding, which can be a clinical sign of a pneumothorax.
