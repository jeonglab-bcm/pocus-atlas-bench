# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS), here is the analysis:

**Frame-by-frame Observation:**
Throughout the sequence, the pleural line remains a smooth, sharp, and perfectly static hyperechoic line. There is no shimmering, granular texture, or movement of the pleural interface. Additionally, the horizontal A-line artifacts below the pleural line remain completely stationary and do not change position or shape between any of the frames. The speckle pattern beneath the line also shows no variation.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The static appearance of the pleural line and the unchanging A-lines are classic signs of a lack of sliding, which can be seen in conditions such as a pneumothorax or pleural effusion. There is no visible transition point, indicating no lung point is present in this view.
