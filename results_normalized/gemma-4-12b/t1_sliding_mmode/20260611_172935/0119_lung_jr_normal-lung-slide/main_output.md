# 0119_lung_jr_normal-lung-slide

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observations:**
*   **Frames 1-4:** The pleural line (the bright, horizontal interface at the top) shows subtle shimmering and a granular texture, which are signs of **pleural sliding**.
*   **Frames 5-6:** There is a distinct transition. A large, wedge-shaped hypoechoic/anechoic area appears, and the pleural line becomes more static and less defined on the right side of the image. The left side continues to show some shimmering.

**Conclusion:**
The video demonstrates a **lung point**, as there is a visible transition point where sliding is present on one side and absent on the other.
