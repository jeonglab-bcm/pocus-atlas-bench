# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

Based on an analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video, here is the observation:

**Frame-by-Frame Observations:**
*   **Frames 1-3:** The pleural line shows a textured, somewhat "shimmering" appearance rather than being a smooth, static white line. There are faint vertical artifacts (B-lines) originating from this interface.
*   **Frames 4-6:** The sub-pleural speckle pattern (the grainy texture just below the pleural line) shows subtle dynamic changes between consecutive frames.
*   **Frames 7-10:** The shimmering of the pleural line becomes more evident, and the texture of the underlying lung tissue continues to shift, indicating movement of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura.

**Analysis of Signs:**
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
    *   **Shimmering/granular pleural line:** The pleural line is textured and changes its appearance slightly between frames rather than remaining perfectly static.
    *   **B-lines:** Vertical artifacts are visible originating from the pleural line.
    *   **Speckle pattern variation:** There is a noticeable difference in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames, which is a key indicator of pleural movement.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The pleural line is not a smooth, static, sharply defined line, and there is no "barcode" or "stratosphere" pattern visible.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** There is no clear transition point where sliding is present on one side and absent on the other within the shown view.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.** The dynamic shimmering of the pleural line and the changing speckle pattern beneath it are clear indicators of lung movement during respiration.
