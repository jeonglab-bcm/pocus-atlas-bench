# 0017_lung_lung-point2

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) images, here is the observation:

**Observations:**
*   **M-mode analysis:** The bottom portion of each frame shows a consistent "barcode sign" (also known as the stratosphere sign). This is characterized by horizontal, parallel lines with no granular movement below the pleural line. 
*   **B-mode analysis:** The pleural line appears smooth and static across all frames. There is no visible "shimmering" or granular texture that would indicate lung movement.
*   **Sequential change:** There is no detectable change in the speckle pattern or the pleural line between the frames, which further confirms a lack of movement.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** 
The presence of a clear barcode sign on M-mode and a static pleural line on B-mode are classic indicators of absent lung sliding, often associated with conditions like pneumothorax or pleural effusion.
