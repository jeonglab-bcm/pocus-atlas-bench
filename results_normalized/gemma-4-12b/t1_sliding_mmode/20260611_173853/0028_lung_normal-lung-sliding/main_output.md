# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** There is a bright, sharply defined horizontal line representing the pleural surface. Throughout the sequence, this line appears smooth and remains static.
*   **A-lines:** Multiple horizontal artifacts (A-lines) are visible below the pleural line, parallel to it. These A-lines remain in a fixed position and do not move up or down across the frames.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The granular texture (speckle pattern) of the lung parenchyma below the pleural line shows no significant variation or movement between consecutive frames.
*   **Shimmer:** There is an absence of the typical "shimmering" or granular movement that characterizes the visceral pleura sliding against the parietal pleura during respiration.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The presence of a static, smooth pleural line combined with static A-lines and no sub-pleural speckle variation are characteristic signs of absent sliding, which can be indicative of a pneumothorax.
