# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames provided:

### Observations:
1.  **M-mode Analysis (Top Portion):** The M-mode display shows distinct, parallel horizontal lines across the entire width of the image. There is a notable absence of a "shimmering" effect or the "seashore sign" (which would appear as a sandy, granular texture below the pleural line). Instead, the appearance is consistent with a **"barcode" or "stratosphere sign,"** where the horizontal lines remain uniform and static.
2.  **B-mode Analysis (Bottom Portion):** The pleural line appears relatively static and smoothly defined. There is no visible transition point (lung point) where sliding would be present on one side and absent on the other.
3.  **Frame Consistency:** Throughout the sequence of frames, there is very little to no change in the speckle pattern below the pleural line or the appearance of the pleural interface itself.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is absent.** 

The presence of the **stratosphere / barcode sign** on M-mode, combined with the lack of shimmering on the pleural line in the B-mode frames, are classic indicators of absent lung sliding, which can be associated with conditions like a pneumothorax.
