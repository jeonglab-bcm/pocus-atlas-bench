### Assessment of Pleural Sliding:
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** Not observed. There is no shimmering or granular texture of the pleural line, and no B-lines are seen originating from a moving interface.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** **Observed.** The image demonstrates a classic **"barcode sign"** (also known as the stratosphere sign when seen on M-mode). This is characterized by multiple horizontal lines with a complete absence of vertical movement. The pleural line appears static, and there is no variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between frames.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** Not observed. There is no visible transition point or alternating patterns within the field of view.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.** The appearance of the "barcode sign" is highly suggestive of a pneumothorax or severe pulmonary consolidation.
