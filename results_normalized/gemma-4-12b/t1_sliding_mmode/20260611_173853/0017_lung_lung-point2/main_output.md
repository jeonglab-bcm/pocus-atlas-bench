# 0017_lung_lung-point2

Based on an analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound (LUS) frames, here are the observations:

**Observations:**
*   **B-mode (Top Image):** The pleural line appears as a relatively smooth, sharply defined horizontal interface. There is no noticeable shimmering, granular texture, or significant change in the speckle pattern below the pleural line between consecutive frames.
*   **M-mode (Bottom Image):** The trace shows a series of parallel, horizontal lines with very little vertical displacement. This is a classic **"stratosphere" or "barcode" sign**. In a normal lung with sliding, you would expect a "seashore sign" (a granular pattern below a moving pleural line), but here, the lines remain horizontal and static.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The presence of the barcode sign on M-mode is a key indicator of a lack of pleural movement, which can be associated with conditions such as a pneumothorax.
