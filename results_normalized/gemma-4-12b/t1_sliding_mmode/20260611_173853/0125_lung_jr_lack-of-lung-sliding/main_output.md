# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided ultrasound sequence, here is the assessment:

### Observations
*   **Pleural Line:** A prominent, bright (hyperechoic) horizontal line is visible at the top of the dark space. It appears smooth and sharply defined.
*   **A-lines:** Below the pleural line, there are multiple horizontal, parallel reverberation artifacts known as A-lines.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The texture of the lung parenchyma below the pleural line is visible but remains consistent across the entire sequence.

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis
*   **Frame 1 to 6:** Throughout the sequence, there is no discernible movement of the pleural line. The A-lines remain in perfectly static positions, and the sub-pleural speckle pattern shows no variation between any of the frames. There is no "shimmering" or "granular" texture indicative of motion.

### Conclusion
**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.**

The observation of a smooth, sharply defined, and perfectly static pleural line combined with static A-lines and no variation in sub-pleural speckle across all frames are classic signs of absent pleural sliding.
