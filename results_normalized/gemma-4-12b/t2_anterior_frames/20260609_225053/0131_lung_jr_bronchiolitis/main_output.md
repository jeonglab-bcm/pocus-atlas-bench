# 0131_lung_jr_bronchiolitis

In this lung ultrasound (LUS) video of an anterior lung zone, the following observations can be made:

**B-lines Assessment:**
Throughout the sequence, prominent hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) are visible arising from the pleural line. While some frames show more discrete B-lines, they frequently coalesce into a diffuse, confluent white sheet that completely obscures the horizontal A-lines. This appearance is characteristic of a "ground glass" pattern, indicating significant interstitial or alveolar edema.
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **B-line type = "ground_glass"**

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The lung parenchyma maintains a degree of aeration, as evidenced by the presence of B-lines and the lack of a solid, tissue-like appearance. There is no evidence of "hepatization" (liver-like texture), a "shred sign" (irregular deep border), or "air bronchograms" (hyperechoic foci within consolidated tissue).
