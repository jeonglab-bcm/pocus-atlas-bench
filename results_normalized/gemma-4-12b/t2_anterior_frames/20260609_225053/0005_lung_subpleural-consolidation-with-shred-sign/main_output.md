# 0005_lung_subpleural-consolidation-with-shred-sign

In this sequence of lung ultrasound (LUS) frames, the following observations are made:

**Frame-by-frame Analysis:**
*   **Frame 1:** Multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines) are observed originating from the pleural line. These B-lines are coalescing into a diffuse white "curtain" or "sheet" that obscures the horizontal A-lines.
*   **Frame 2:** The pattern persists, showing confluent B-lines covering a significant portion of the lung field.
*   **Frame 3:** Continued presence of coalescing B-lines with a ground-glass appearance.
*   **Frame 4:** The ground-glass pattern remains consistent throughout the final frame.

**B-lines Assessment:**
*   **lung_rockets = true**: Vertical artifacts are clearly present.
*   **subtype = ground_glass**: The B-lines are confluent and coalescing, creating a diffuse white sheet that obscures the underlying A-lines, rather than being discrete and well-spaced.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
*   **consolidation = false**: There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign, or air bronchograms. The lung parenchyma appears to be primarily occupied by the B-line artifacts rather than solid consolidation.
*   **consolidation_type = null**

**Conclusion:**
The ultrasound shows a **ground-glass pattern** of B-lines (lung_rockets = true) without evidence of consolidation (consolidation = false).
