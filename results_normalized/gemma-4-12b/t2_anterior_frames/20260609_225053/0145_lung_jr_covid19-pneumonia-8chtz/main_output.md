# 0145_lung_jr_covid19-pneumonia-8chtz

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS), here is the analysis:

### **Observations**
*   **B-lines**: There are prominent hyperechoic vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. In the central portion of the image, these B-lines are confluent and merge into a diffuse white sheet, which obscures the underlying A-lines.
*   **Consolidation**: A significant portion of the lung zone (on the right side of the image) shows a solid, echogenic appearance that resembles liver tissue, indicating **hepatization**. Furthermore, there is a clearly visible irregular, jagged border between the consolidated lung and the aerated lung, which is the **shred sign**.

### **Conclusions**
*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**
*   **Subtype**: **ground_glass** (due to the coalescing, diffuse white sheet appearance)
*   **Consolidation present (consolidation = true)**
*   **Consolidation Type**: **hepatization** (predominant tissue-like appearance with a visible shred sign)
