# 0046_lung_light-beam-sign-covid19

In these ultrasound images of the anterior lung zone, several hyperechoic, vertical artifacts are observed originating from the pleural line. These artifacts extend to the bottom of the screen without fading, which are characteristic of B-lines. Specifically, these B-lines are confluent and coalesce into a diffuse white sheet, obscuring the normal A-lines. This pattern is classified as ground-glass B-lines, often indicative of alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial disease. There are no signs of hepatization (solid, liver-like texture), a shred sign, or air bronchograms, indicating that there is no consolidation present.
