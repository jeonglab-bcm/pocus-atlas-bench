# 0147_lung_jr_acute-chest-syndrome

In the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) images of an anterior lung zone, the following features are observed:

*   **B-lines (lung rockets):** There are multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line on the left side of the screen. These artifacts are prominent and appear to coalesce in several areas, merging into a diffuse, white, sheet-like appearance that obscures the underlying A-lines.
*   **Consolidation:** A large area of consolidated lung is visible on the right side of the image. This region exhibits a solid, echogenic texture that resembles liver tissue, characteristic of **hepatization**. At the border between this consolidated area and the more aerated lung, an irregular, "shredded" appearance is visible, known as the **shred sign**.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **B-line subtype = ground_glass**
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = hepatization**
