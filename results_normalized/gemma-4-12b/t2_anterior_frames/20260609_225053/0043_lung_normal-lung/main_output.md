# 0043_lung_normal-lung

The ultrasound images show a "white-out" appearance of the anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
- **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**: There are numerous hyperechoic vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line.
- **Subtype**: These B-lines are confluent, merging into a diffuse, dense white sheet that completely obscures the horizontal A-lines and the underlying pleural line. This represents a **ground_glass** pattern, indicating severe interstitial or alveolar disease.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma has lost its normal aerated appearance and instead shows a solid, echogenic texture that resembles liver tissue. This is a hallmark of dense alveolar consolidation.
- **Consolidation = true**: The presence of tissue-like hepatization confirms consolidation.
- **Consolidation_type**: The predominant pattern is **hepatization**.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **B_lines_subtype**: ground_glass
- **consolidation**: true
- **consolidation_type**: hepatization
