# 0138_lung_jr_clines

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) frames:

**B-lines Assessment:**
The images show prominent, broad, and hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line. These artifacts are not discrete or well-spaced; instead, they are confluent and coalescing, merging into a wide, diffuse white band that extends to the bottom of the screen. This pattern is characteristic of ground glass B-lines.
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **B-line subtype**: ground_glass

**Consolidation Assessment:**
In the center of the image, there is a clear, irregular, and jagged border between a more solid-looking, echogenic area on the left and the aerated lung/B-line area on the right. This irregular border is a classic "shred sign," indicating an area of consolidation where the lung is partially consolidated and partially aerated.
- **consolidation**: true
- **consolidation_type**: shred_sign

**Conclusion:**
The ultrasound shows **ground glass B-lines** and a prominent **shred sign**, indicating a consolidative process in the anterior lung zone.
