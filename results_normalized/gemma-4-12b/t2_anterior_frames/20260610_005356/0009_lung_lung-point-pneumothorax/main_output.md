### Observations

**B-lines:**
Multiple vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) are clearly visible arising from the pleural line. These artifacts are numerous and somewhat confluent, especially in the central part of the ultrasound sector, creating a diffuse, bright, "white" appearance of the lung parenchyma. While some horizontal A-lines are still discernible, the B-lines are the prominent vertical feature.

There is no identifiable shred sign (irregular deep border) or punctate/linear air bronchograms. The presence of horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) further confirms that the lung remains primarily aerated.
