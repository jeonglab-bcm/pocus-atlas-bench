# 0133_lung_jr_subpleuralconsolidation

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip of the anterior lung zone:

### **Observations**

**B-lines Assessment:**
The ultrasound images show numerous vertical hyperechoic artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These artifacts are very dense and frequently coalesce into a diffuse white sheet, obscuring any normal A-line patterns. This indicates a **ground-glass** pattern of B-lines.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is a prominent area of alveolar consolidation in the lower right portion of the screen.
- **Hepatization**: The consolidated lung parenchyma has a solid, tissue-like appearance with echogenicity similar to liver tissue.
- **Shred sign**: The border between the consolidated and aerated lung is notably irregular and "shredded."
- **Air bronchograms**: Within the hepatized region, there are several small, punctate hyperechoic foci representing air-filled bronchi.

### **Conclusions**

- **B-lines present (lung_rockets)**: true
- **B-line type**: ground_glass
- **Consolidation present**: true
- **Consolidation type**: hepatization (with prominent shred sign and air bronchograms)
