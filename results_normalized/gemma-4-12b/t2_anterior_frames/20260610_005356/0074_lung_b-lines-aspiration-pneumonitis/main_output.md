# 0074_lung_b-lines-aspiration-pneumonitis

Based on the sequential analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip:

**B-lines Assessment:**
The images demonstrate prominent, confluent vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line. These artifacts merge into a diffuse white sheet that extends to the bottom of the screen, obscuring the underlying lung parenchyma. This is a characteristic **ground glass** pattern, which often indicates alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial disease.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
In several frames, particularly those in the lower half of the sequence, a large area of lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of the liver. This **tissue-like hepatization** indicates alveolar consolidation. At the border between this consolidated area and the aerated lung, an irregular, "shredded" appearance is visible, confirming the presence of a **shred sign**.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets = true**
- **subtype = "ground_glass"**
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "hepatization"** (with an associated shred sign)
