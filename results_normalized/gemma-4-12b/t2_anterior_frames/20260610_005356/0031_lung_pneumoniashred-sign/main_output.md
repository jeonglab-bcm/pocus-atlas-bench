**B-lines Assessment:**

These artifacts are not discrete; instead, they are coalescing and merging to form a diffuse "white sheet" that obscures the usual horizontal A-lines. This appearance is characteristic of **ground glass** B-lines, which typically indicate significant alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial lung disease.

The predominant finding is the severe B-line pattern.
