# 0147_lung_jr_acute-chest-syndrome

In this sequence of lung ultrasound (LUS) images, all four frames show very similar findings in the anterior lung zone.

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis

**Frame 1:**
- **B-lines:** Confluent, coalescing B-lines are visible, creating a "ground-glass" appearance where they merge into a diffuse white sheet.
- **Consolidation:** There is a large area of consolidation. The lung parenchyma exhibits **tissue-like hepatization** (it looks like solid liver tissue). A **shredded border** is clearly visible between the consolidated area and the surrounding aerated lung. Additionally, several bright, punctate hyperechoic foci can be seen within the hepatized area, representing **air bronchograms**.
- **Conclusion:** `lung_rockets = true`, `ground_glass`, `consolidation = true`, `consolidation_type = "hepatization"`

**Frame 2:**
- **B-lines:** Similar to Frame 1, the B-lines are confluent and merge into a ground-glass pattern.
- **Consolidation:** The same large area of hepatized consolidation persists, with a shredded border and air bronchograms.
- **Conclusion:** `lung_rockets = true`, `ground_glass`, `consolidation = true`, `consolidation_type = "hepatization"`

**Frame 3:**
- **B-lines:** Confluent B-lines are present, appearing as a ground-glass pattern.
- **Consolidation:** The hepatized consolidation, shredded sign, and air bronchograms are consistently present.
- **Conclusion:** `lung_rockets = true`, `ground_glass`, `consolidation = true`, `consolidation_type = "hepatization"`

**Frame 4:**
- **B-lines:** The images show confluent B-lines forming a ground-glass appearance.
- **Consolidation:** The findings are stable, showing extensive hepatized consolidation with an irregular shredded border and internal air bronchograms.
- **Conclusion:** `lung_rockets = true`, `ground_glass`, `consolidation = true`, `consolidation_type = "hepatization"`
