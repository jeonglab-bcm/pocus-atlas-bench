# 0039_lung_hepatization-of-lung

Based on the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip of the anterior lung zone, here is the analysis:

### **B-lines Assessment**
*   **Observation:** Throughout the clip, the lung shows clear **A-lines** (horizontal reverberation artifacts) rather than vertical B-lines. There are no hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line that extend to the bottom of the screen.
*   **Conclusion:** **B-lines absent (lung_rockets = false)**.

### **Consolidation Assessment**
*   **Observation:** A large portion of the lung field appears solid and homogeneous, lacking the normal aeration of the lung. The texture is very similar to the liver, which is characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**. There are no prominent "shred signs" or distinct "air bronchograms" clearly dominating the appearance; it is primarily a solid, liver-like texture.
*   **Conclusion:** **Consolidation present (consolidation = true)**.
*   **Classification:** **consolidation_type = "hepatization"**.
