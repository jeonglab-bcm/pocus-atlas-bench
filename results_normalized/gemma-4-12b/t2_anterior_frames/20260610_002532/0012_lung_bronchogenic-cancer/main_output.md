# 0012_lung_bronchogenic-cancer

Based on the analysis of the ultrasound image provided:

### Observations:
- **B-lines:** No hyperechoic vertical B-lines are observed in this image. The lung lacks normal aeration artifacts in the majority of the field.
- **Consolidation:** There is a large area of consolidated lung tissue. The parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, very similar to liver tissue. This is characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**.
- **Additional Signs:** An irregular, **shredded border** (shred sign) is visible at the edge of the consolidated area. There are also some small, punctate hyperechoic foci within the hepatized area, which may represent **air bronchograms**.

### Conclusions:
- **lung_rockets:** false
- **consolidation:** true
- **consolidation_type:** hepatization
