# 0027_lung_subpleural-consolidation

The lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip of the anterior lung zone (RH / T ZONE 1) shows a prominent, well-defined hypoechoic area with an irregular, jagged border. This appearance is characteristic of a subpleural consolidation.

**Analysis:**
- **B-lines:** There are no clear, vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (lung rockets) arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. The horizontal artifacts present are mostly obscured by the consolidated tissue. Therefore, B-lines are absent.
- **Consolidation:** A large hypoechoic region is visible in several frames (most notably in frames 4, 5, 10, and 11), which represents consolidated lung tissue. The deep border of this region is notably irregular and jagged, which is the "shredded sign," indicating the transition between aerated and consolidated lung.

**Conclusion:**
- **lung_rockets:** false
- **consolidation:** true
- **consolidation_type:** shred_sign
