# 0036_lung_dynamic-air-bronchograms

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip:

**B-lines Assessment:**
The video shows numerous vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line. These B-lines are very dense and frequently coalesce to form a diffuse, white "sheet" that obscures the underlying anatomy. This is characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern, which indicates severe interstitial or alveolar edema.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
There is a clear area of consolidation in the lower portion of the image. The lung parenchyma in this region has lost its normal aerated appearance and instead shows a solid, tissue-like echogenicity resembling liver tissue, known as **hepatization**. Within this hepatized area, there are multiple punctate hyperechoic foci representing **air bronchograms**. An irregular, shredded border (shred sign) is also visible at the edge of the consolidated zone.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **B-line type**: ground_glass
- **consolidation**: true
- **consolidation_type**: hepatization
