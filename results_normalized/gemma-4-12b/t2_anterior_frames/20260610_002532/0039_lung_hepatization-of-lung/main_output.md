### **B-lines Assessment**
Throughout the clip, there are no vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. Instead, the lung parenchyma exhibits a dense, solid appearance.
- **lung_rockets = false**

### **Consolidation Assessment**
The ultrasound shows clear evidence of alveolar consolidation in the anterior lung zone:
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, losing its normal aeration and resembling the texture of the liver (hepatization).
- **Shred sign**: There is an irregular, "shredded" border between the consolidated lung and the adjacent aerated regions.
- **Air bronchograms**: Multiple punctate and linear hyperechoic foci are clearly visible within the hepatized lung, representing air-filled bronchi.

**Conclusions:**
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "hepatization"** (While the shred sign and air bronchograms are also clearly visible, the predominant finding is the solid, tissue-like hepatized appearance of the lung.)
