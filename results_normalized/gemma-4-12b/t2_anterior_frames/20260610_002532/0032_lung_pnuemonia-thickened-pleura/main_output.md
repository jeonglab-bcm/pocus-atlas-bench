# 0032_lung_pnuemonia-thickened-pleura

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video frames:

**Observation:**
The ultrasound images of the right hemithorax (zone 3 low) show a prominent presence of multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line. These artifacts are confluent and coalesce, merging into a diffuse white sheet that obscures the normal horizontal A-lines. This appearance is characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern, which is often associated with alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial disease. Additionally, on the right periphery of the image, there is a localized area where the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of the liver, which indicates a **hepatized** consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets):** true
*   **B-line subtype:** ground_glass
*   **Consolidation:** true
*   **Consolidation type:** hepatization
