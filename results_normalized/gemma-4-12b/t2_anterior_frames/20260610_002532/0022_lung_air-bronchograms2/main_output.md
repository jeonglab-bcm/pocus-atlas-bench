# 0022_lung_air-bronchograms2

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) image provided:

**Observations:**
*   **B-lines:** The image shows confluent, vertical hyperechoic artifacts that merge into a diffuse white sheet, completely obscuring any horizontal A-lines. This is characteristic of a ground-glass pattern.
*   **Consolidation:** The lung parenchyma has a solid, grainy appearance that resembles liver tissue, indicating **tissue-like hepatization**. 
*   **Air Bronchograms:** Within this hepatized region, there are multiple distinct, punctate hyperechoic (bright white) foci, which represent air-filled bronchi.
*   **Shred Sign:** The border of the consolidated area appears irregular and "shredded," especially toward the bottom-center of the main lesion.

**Conclusions:**
*   **lung_rockets = true**
*   **B-line_subtype = ground_glass**
*   **consolidation = true**
*   **consolidation_type = air_bronchogram** (due to the highly prominent punctate hyperechoic foci within the hepatized lung).
