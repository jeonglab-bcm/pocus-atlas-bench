# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

Based on the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip of an anterior lung zone, here is the analysis:

### Analysis of Findings:
*   **B-lines (lung rockets):** The image clearly shows multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These are classic B-lines.
*   **B-line Subtype:** The B-lines are very numerous and, in several regions of the clip, they are clearly coalescing and merging into a diffuse white area that obscures the underlying lung architecture. This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** B-lines, which often indicate alveolar edema or diffuse interstitial disease.
*   **Consolidation:** There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation in this zone. The lung parenchyma does not have a solid, tissue-like appearance (**hepatization**), there is no irregular **shred sign** at the border of aeration, and no **air bronchograms** are visible. The presence of B-lines confirms that the lung is still aerated.

### Conclusions:
*   **lung_rockets**: true
*   **B-line subtype**: ground_glass
*   **consolidation**: false
*   **consolidation_type**: null
