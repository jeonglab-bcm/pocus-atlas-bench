# 0039_lung_hepatization-of-lung

The video shows a lung ultrasound (LUS) of an anterior lung zone.

**B-lines Assessment:**
The image is dominated by a large area of consolidation. I do not see any prominent, well-defined B-lines (lung rockets) that extend from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen. Therefore, B-lines are absent.
- **lung_rockets = false**

**Consolidation Assessment:**
A large area of the lung is visible as solid, echogenic tissue, which is characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**. The border between this consolidated area and the more aerated lung on the right is irregular and jagged, representing a **shred sign**. Additionally, there are several punctate hyperechoic foci within the hepatized area, which are **air bronchograms**.
- **consolidation = true**
- **consolidation_type = "hepatization"** (as it is the predominant characteristic of the large consolidated area, although shred sign and air bronchograms are also clearly visible).
