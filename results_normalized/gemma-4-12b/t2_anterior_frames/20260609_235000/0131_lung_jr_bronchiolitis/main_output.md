*   **lung_rockets = true**: Multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts are clearly visible, originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen.

Instead, they appear confluent and coalesced, particularly on the left side of the image, creating a diffuse white sheet that obscures horizontal A-lines. ### Consolidation Assessment
*   **consolidation = false**: The lung parenchyma does not exhibit signs of consolidation. There is no tissue-like hepatization (the lung does not look like liver tissue), no shredded border (shred sign) is visible between aerated and consolidated lung, and there are no distinct punctate or linear hyperechoic foci representing air bronchograms within a solid-looking area.

**Final Conclusion:**
The ultrasound shows a **ground_glass** pattern of confluent **B-lines** (lung rockets) without evidence of **consolidation**.
