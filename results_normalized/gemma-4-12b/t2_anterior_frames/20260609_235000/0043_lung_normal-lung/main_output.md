# 0043_lung_normal-lung

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip of the anterior lung zone, here is the assessment:

### Analysis of Findings

*   **B-lines:** There are numerous, dense vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line. These artifacts are highly **confluent**, merging together to create a diffuse "white-out" appearance that completely obscures the underlying A-lines (horizontal reverberation artifacts). This pattern is characteristic of **ground-glass** opacities, indicating significant interstitial or alveolar edema.
*   **Consolidation:** The lung parenchyma does not exhibit a solid, liver-like texture (hepatization) with a clear, irregular shredded border (shred sign) or distinct air bronchograms. While the area is very bright, the predominant feature is the coalescing B-line pattern rather than solid tissue replacement.

### Conclusions

*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets):** true
*   **B-line subtype:** ground_glass
*   **Consolidation:** false
*   **Consolidation type:** null
