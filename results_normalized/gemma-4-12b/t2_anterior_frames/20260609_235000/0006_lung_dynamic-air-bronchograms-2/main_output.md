# 0006_lung_dynamic-air-bronchograms-2

In this lung ultrasound (LUS) video of an anterior lung zone, the following features are observed:

### B-lines Assessment
*   **B-lines present (lung_rockets = true)**: There are prominent, vertical hyperechoic artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading.
*   **Subtype**: These B-lines are very dense and confluent, coalescing to form a diffuse "white-out" appearance that obscures the underlying lung parenchyma. This is characteristic of **ground_glass** B-lines.

### Consolidation Assessment
*   **Consolidation present (consolidation = true)**: The lower portion of the lung zone shows a loss of normal aeration. The lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, mimicking the texture of liver tissue.
*   **Predominant type**: The primary finding is **hepatization** (tissue-like appearance). Additionally, there are some punctate and linear hyperechoic foci within the consolidated area, which represent **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusion:**
The video demonstrates **ground_glass** B-lines and **hepatization** with air bronchograms, indicating significant lung pathology such as severe pulmonary edema or pneumonia.
