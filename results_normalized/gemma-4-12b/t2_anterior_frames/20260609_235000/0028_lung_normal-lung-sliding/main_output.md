# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip of the anterior lung zone:

### Analysis
The video shows a clear pleural line with several horizontal, hyperechoic reverberation artifacts beneath it, known as **A-lines**. These artifacts indicate normal aeration of the underlying lung. There are no vertical, comet-tail artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. Furthermore, the lung parenchyma does not exhibit a solid, tissue-like appearance (hepatization), nor is there any evidence of a shredded border (shred sign) or punctate hyperechoic foci (air bronchograms).

### Conclusions
