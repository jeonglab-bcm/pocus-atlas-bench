# 0012_lung_bronchogenic-cancer

Based on the provided ultrasound images of the anterior lung zone, here is the analysis:

### Analysis
The ultrasound images show a large area of consolidated lung parenchyma. 
*   **B-lines:** There are no hyperechoic vertical artifacts (lung rockets) extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen. The lung appears solid rather than aerated in this region.
*   **Consolidation:** There is clear evidence of alveolar consolidation.
    *   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The large, homogeneous, and echogenic area below the pleural line resembles solid organ tissue (liver-like), which is a hallmark of lung hepatization.
    *   **Shred sign:** An irregular, "shredded" border is visible on the right side of the consolidated mass where it meets the adjacent aerated lung.
    *   **Air bronchograms:** Multiple punctate, hyperechoic (bright) foci are visible within the hepatized tissue, representing air-filled bronchi within the consolidated lung.

### Conclusions
*   **lung_rockets:** false
*   **consolidation:** true
*   **consolidation_type:** hepatization (Note: shred sign and air bronchograms are also clearly present)
