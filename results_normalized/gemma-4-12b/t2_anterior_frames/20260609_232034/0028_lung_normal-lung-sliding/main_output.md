# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip, here are the observations:

The ultrasound image primarily shows horizontal reverberation artifacts, which are characteristic **A-lines**. There is no evidence of hyperechoic vertical artifacts (lung rockets) that extend to the bottom of the screen without fading. Therefore, **B-lines are absent**.

Furthermore, the lung parenchyma appears normally aerated. There is no evidence of **tissue-like hepatization**, **shred signs**, or **air bronchograms**, which would indicate alveolar consolidation. The presence of A-lines suggests that the lung is well-aerated.

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets**: false
- **consolidation**: false
- **consolidation_type**: null
