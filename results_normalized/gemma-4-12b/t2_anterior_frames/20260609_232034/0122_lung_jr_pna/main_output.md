# 0122_lung_jr_pna

The sequence of images shows a lung ultrasound (LUS) of an anterior lung zone with prominent consolidation.

**Frame-by-frame analysis:**
- In all frames, the upper part of the image shows the pleural line, but the area below it is dominated by a large, solid-appearing region.
- The lung parenchyma lacks the normal aerated appearance (A-lines or B-lines) and instead exhibits a dense, echogenic texture similar to solid organ tissue.
- Within this consolidated area, there are several small, bright (hyperechoic) punctate foci, which are characteristic of air bronchograms.

**B-lines Assessment:**
- **B-lines absent (lung_rockets = false)**: There are no vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) arising from the pleural line. The lung is replaced by consolidated tissue.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
- **Consolidation present (consolidation = true)**: The lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, having lost its normal aeration.
- **Consolidation type**: The predominant appearance is **hepatization**, as the lung texture strongly resembles liver tissue. The presence of small hyperechoic foci also indicates **air bronchograms**.

**Conclusion:**
- lung_rockets: false
- consolidation: true
- consolidation_type: hepatization
