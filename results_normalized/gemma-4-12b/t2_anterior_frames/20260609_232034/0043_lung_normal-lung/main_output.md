# 0043_lung_normal-lung

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound (LUS) frames:

### Observations
*   **B-lines:** The images show a predominantly white, hyperechoic lung zone. In the initial frames, there is a lack of distinct A-lines, and the B-lines are confluent, merging into a diffuse white sheet. This is characteristic of a **ground-glass** pattern, which indicates severe interstitial or alveolar disease.
*   **Consolidation:** As the sequence progresses, the lung parenchyma becomes increasingly solid and echogenic, losing its typical aeration artifacts. It develops a texture that resembles solid organ tissue (like the liver), which is the hallmark of **tissue-like hepatization**. There are also some irregular borders visible in several frames, suggestive of a shred sign, but hepatization is the predominant feature.

### Conclusions
*   **B-lines Assessment:**
    *   **lung_rockets**: true
    *   **B-line subtype**: ground_glass
*   **Consolidation Assessment:**
    *   **consolidation**: true
    *   **consolidation_type**: hepatization
