# 0148_lung_jr_pediatric-consolidation

The ultrasound images of the anterior lung zone demonstrate the following:

**B-lines Assessment:**
- **Presence:** There are numerous vertical artifacts originating from the pleural line.
- **Pattern:** These B-lines are confluent and coalescing, merging into a diffuse white sheet that obscures the underlying anatomy and A-lines. This creates a "white-out" appearance.
- **Classification:** **lung_rockets = true**, **B-line subtype = ground_glass**.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
- **Presence:** There is a large, hyperechoic area in the upper portion of the lung field (roughly between 1–4 cm depth) that lacks normal aeration.
- **Features:** This area shows **tissue-like hepatization** (resembling liver parenchyma), a prominent **shred sign** (an irregular, shredded border where the consolidated lung meets more aerated tissue), and several small, punctate hyperechoic foci consistent with **air bronchograms**.
- **Classification:** **consolidation = true**, **consolidation_type = "shred_sign"** (due to the prominent irregular border of the consolidated mass).

**Conclusion:**
The scan shows a significant area of **lung consolidation** with a prominent **shred sign**, hepatization, and air bronchograms, accompanied by a background of **ground-glass** B-lines.
