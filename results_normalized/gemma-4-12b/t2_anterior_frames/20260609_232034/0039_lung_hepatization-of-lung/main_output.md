### Summary Assessment

**B-lines Assessment:**
The image shows multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts arising from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen without fading. These artifacts vary in density: some are discrete and well-spaced (septal pattern), while others merge into a more confluent, diffuse white sheet (ground-glass pattern).

**Consolidation Assessment:**
A significant portion of the lung parenchyma appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue, which is characteristic of **hepatization**. A clear, irregular, and "shredded" border is visible between the consolidated and aerated lung segments, known as the **shred sign**. Additionally, small punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within the hepatized lung, identifying **air bronchograms**.
*   **Conclusion:** `consolidation = true`, **predominant type:** `hepatization`
