# 0131_lung_jr_bronchiolitis

In this sequence of lung ultrasound (LUS) frames from an anterior lung zone, we can observe the following:

### Frame-by-Frame Analysis:
- **Frame 1:** The pleural line is visible with a mix of horizontal A-lines and some nascent vertical B-lines.
- **Frames 2-3:** The vertical hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) become increasingly numerous and begin to coalesce.
- **Frames 4-8:** The B-lines become very dense and confluent, merging into a "white lung" or "ground glass" appearance that obscures the background and the A-lines.

### B-lines Assessment:
- **Presence:** **B-lines are present (lung_rockets = true)**. These are the vertical hyperechoic artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen.
- **Subtype:** **ground_glass**. The B-lines are highly confluent and coalescing into a diffuse white sheet, characteristic of significant interstitial or alveolar fluid/edema.

### Consolidation Assessment:
- **Presence:** **Consolidation is absent (consolidation = false)**. There is no evidence of tissue-like hepatization, a shred sign, or air bronchograms. The presence of B-lines indicates that the lung remains partially aerated despite the significant interstitial fluid.
- **Consolidation Type:** **null**.

**Conclusion:** The images demonstrate a **ground glass** pattern of confluent B-lines without signs of alveolar consolidation.
