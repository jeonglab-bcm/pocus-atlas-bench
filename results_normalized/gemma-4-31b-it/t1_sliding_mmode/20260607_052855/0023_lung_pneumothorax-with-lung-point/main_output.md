*   **p01**: Shows dense, continuous parallel horizontal lines throughout, both above and below a very straight pleural line. No granular texture is present. Classification: **Stratosphere**.
*   **p02**: Similar to p01, exhibiting a consistent "barcode" pattern of parallel horizontal lines with no granularity. Classification: **Stratosphere**.
*   **p03**: Exhibits the same pattern of continuous parallel horizontal lines throughout the image. Classification: **Stratosphere**.

*   **p05**: Clearly shows a granular/sandy texture below the pleural line, contrasting with the parallel lines above it. Classification: **Seashore**.
*   **p06**: Strong granular "sandy" appearance below the pleural line. Classification: **Seashore**.
*   **p07**: Distinct granular texture below the pleural line. Classification: **Seashore**.
*   **p08**: Distinct granular texture below the pleural line. Classification: **Seashore**.
*   **p09**: Distinct granular texture below the pleural line. Classification: **Seashore**.

**Overall Decision:**
There is a clear spatial transition across the analyzed positions. Positions **p01 through p03** show a **Stratosphere** pattern (absent pleural sliding), while positions **p04 through p09** show a **Seashore** pattern (present pleural sliding). This coexistence of both patterns across different lateral positions is the defining characteristic of a lung point.

Overall label: **both**
