# 0041_lung_2018-1-16-normal-lung-slide

Based on the sequential analysis of the frames, here is the observation:

*   **Frame-by-frame analysis:** As the frames progress, the bright, horizontal pleural line exhibits a shimmering, granular motion. More importantly, the speckle pattern (the grainy appearance) immediately below the pleural line changes and shifts from one frame to the next. There are also some vertical hyper-echoic artifacts (B-lines/comet-tail artifacts) originating from the pleural line.
*   **Assessment of signs:** 
    *   The **shimmering of the pleural line** is clearly visible.
    *   The **variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern** between consecutive frames is a hallmark of lung sliding.
    *   There is no evidence of a static "barcode" appearance or a completely fixed pleural line.

**Conclusion:** Pleural sliding is **PRESENT**.
