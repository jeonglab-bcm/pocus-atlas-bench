# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS), here are the observations:

*   **Pleural Line:** There is a bright, hyperechoic pleural line. Across the sequential frames, this line exhibits a **shimmering, granular appearance**, which is characteristic of the visceral and parietal pleura sliding against one another during respiration.
*   **Sub-pleural Region:** The speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line is not static; it **changes and shifts between consecutive frames**, indicating active movement.
*   **Artifacts:** Subtle vertical hyper-echoic artifacts (B-lines/comet-tails) are visible originating from the pleural line, further supporting the presence of sliding.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **PRESENT**.
