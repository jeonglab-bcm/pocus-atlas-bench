# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

*   **Frame-by-frame analysis:** Across all frames, the pleural line (the bright, hyperechoic horizontal interface) is not static. It exhibits a characteristic "shimmering" or granular motion. Additionally, a prominent vertical, hyper-echoic artifact (a B-line or comet-tail artifact) is visible, originating from the pleural line and extending toward the bottom of the screen. This B-line shows slight movement consistent with respiratory motion.
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
    *   **Shimmering pleural line:** The bright interface between the chest wall and the lung shows dynamic, granular movement between frames.
    *   **B-lines:** A clear comet-tail artifact is present, which is a strong indicator of pleural apposition and sliding.
    *   **Dynamic speckle pattern:** The echo pattern below the pleural line varies between frames, reflecting the movement of the lung surface.

No signs of absent sliding (such as a perfectly static pleural line or a "barcode" pattern) or a lung point (a transition from sliding to non-sliding) are observed.

**Conclusion: Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
