# 0119_lung_jr_normal-lung-slide

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) sequence, here are the observations:

*   **Pleural Line Appearance:** Across all seven frames, the pleural line (the bright, horizontal interface) appears smooth, sharply defined, and completely static. There is no "shimmering" or granular movement typically seen when the visceral and parietal pleura slide against each other.
*   **Sub-pleural Area:** The speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line remains unchanged between consecutive frames.
*   **Artifacts:** There are clear, horizontal, parallel lines below the pleural line, known as **A-lines**. These reverberation artifacts remain perfectly static and unchanged in position across the entire sequence.
*   **Absence of B-lines:** No vertical comet-tail artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line are observed.

**Conclusion:**
Because the pleural line is static, there is no shimmering movement, and the A-lines remain unchanged across all frames, **pleural sliding is absent**. This appearance is characteristic of a pneumothorax (where air separates the pleural layers).
