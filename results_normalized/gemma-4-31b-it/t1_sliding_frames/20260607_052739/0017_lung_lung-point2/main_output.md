Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound clip:

**Observations:**
*   **B-mode (Top Image):** The pleural line is bright and exhibits a shimmering, granular appearance that changes slightly between frames, indicating movement between the visceral and parietal pleura.
*   **M-mode (Bottom Image):** There is a distinct transition at the pleural line (approximately 1.5 cm depth). Above the pleural line, the image consists of parallel horizontal lines (representing stationary tissue). Below the pleural line, there is a grainy, speckled pattern resembling sand. This classic appearance is known as the **"seashore sign."**

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **PRESENT**. The presence of the seashore sign on M-mode is a definitive indicator that the lung is sliding against the chest wall.
