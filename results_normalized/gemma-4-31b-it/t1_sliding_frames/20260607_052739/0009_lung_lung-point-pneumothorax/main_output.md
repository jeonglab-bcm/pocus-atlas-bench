# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

*   **Frame-by-frame observation:** Comparing the frames shows a distinct "shimmering" or granular movement at the pleural line (the bright horizontal interface near the top). The pattern of speckles and the appearance of the vertical B-lines change subtly from frame to frame, indicating dynamic motion.
*   **Application of signs:**
    *   **Shimmering pleural line:** Present. The pleural interface is not static; it exhibits the characteristic shimmering motion of sliding lungs.
    *   **B-lines:** Numerous vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines) are present, originating from the pleural line.
    *   **Dynamic speckle pattern:** The area immediately below the pleural line changes in appearance between consecutive frames.

Conclusion: **Pleural sliding is present.**
