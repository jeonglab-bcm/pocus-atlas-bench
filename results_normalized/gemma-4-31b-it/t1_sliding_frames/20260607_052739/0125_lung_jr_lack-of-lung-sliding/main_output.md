# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound video clip, here is the observation and conclusion:

**Observation:**
- **Pleural Line:** A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is clearly visible. 
- **Dynamic Movement:** Comparing the frames, the pleural line exhibits a characteristic "shimmering" or granular motion. This is the classic "shimmer sign," where the visceral pleura slides against the parietal pleura during respiration.
- **Sub-pleural Region:** There is a slight variation in the speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line between consecutive frames, further indicating motion.
- **Other Findings:** Normal A-lines (horizontal, parallel hyperechoic lines) are present throughout the frames. There is no evidence of a static, smooth pleural line or a transition point (lung point).

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
