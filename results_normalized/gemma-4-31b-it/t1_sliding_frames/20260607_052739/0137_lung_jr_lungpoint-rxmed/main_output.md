# 0137_lung_jr_lungpoint-rxmed

Based on the sequential analysis of the ultrasound frames, here is the observation:

1.  **Frame-by-Frame Analysis:** Across all frames, the bright, hyperechoic pleural line is not static. Instead, it exhibits a characteristic "shimmering" or granular movement.
2.  **Sub-pleural Observation:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line changes between consecutive frames, which indicates that the visceral pleura is sliding against the parietal pleura during respiration.
3.  **Absence of Static Signs:** There is no evidence of a smooth, fixed pleural line or a completely static sub-pleural region (which would suggest the "barcode" sign in M-mode or absent sliding). There is also no transition point where sliding disappears, which would indicate a lung point.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.** The observed shimmering of the pleural line and the dynamic change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern are classic signs of normal pleural sliding.
