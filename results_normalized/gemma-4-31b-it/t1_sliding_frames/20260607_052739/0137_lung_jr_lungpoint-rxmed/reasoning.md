    *   **Frame 6:** Shimmering effect is apparent.
    *   **Frame 7:** The pleural line continues to move/shimmer.
    *   **Frame 8:** Shimmering is evident.

2.  **Identify signs of sliding:**

    *   **Speckle pattern change:** Yes, the texture below the pleural line changes between consecutive frames, which is characteristic of the sliding of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura.
    *   **Comet-tail artifacts/B-lines:** I don't see obvious, distinct B-lines in these specific static frames, but the overall movement is the key here.

3.  **Identify signs of absent sliding:**
    *   Smooth, static line? No, it's shimmering.
    *   Barcode sign? Not applicable as M-mode is not provided.
    *   Static A-lines? The A-lines might be there, but the pleural line itself is moving.

4.  **Identify signs of a lung point:**
    *   Transition from sliding to no sliding? No, the entire visible segment of the pleural line appears to be shimmering.

5.  **Conclusion:**
    *   The pleural line shows a shimmering, granular appearance that changes from frame to frame.
    *   The sub-pleural speckle pattern is not static.
    *   These findings are indicative of pleural sliding.
