# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound video clip, here is the detailed observation:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
*   **Pleural Line:** Across all frames, there is a bright, hyperechoic, and sharply defined pleural line located at a depth of approximately 1.7 cm. This line remains completely static and does not exhibit the characteristic "shimmering" or granular movement associated with normal lung sliding.
*   **Sub-pleural Region:** Below the pleural line, horizontal reverberation artifacts (A-lines) are clearly visible. These A-lines remain perfectly static and unchanged across all frames, mirroring the lack of movement at the pleural interface.
*   **Artifacts:** There are no comet-tail artifacts or B-lines originating from the pleural line.
*   **Speckle Pattern:** The sub-pleural speckle pattern is unchanging between consecutive frames, further confirming the absence of motion.

**Conclusion:**
The observed signs—a smooth, sharply defined, and static pleural line, combined with static A-lines and a lack of sub-pleural shimmer—are characteristic of absent pleural sliding.

**Pleural sliding is ABSENT.**
