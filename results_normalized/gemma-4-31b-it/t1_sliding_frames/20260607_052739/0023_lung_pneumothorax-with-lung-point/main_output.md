# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound of the left lung, here is the observation:

**Frame-by-Frame Analysis:**
Across all frames, the bright, hyperechoic pleural line is not static. There is a visible, rhythmic "shimmering" motion of the pleural line and the sub-pleural region. The grainy, speckled pattern immediately below the pleural line changes from frame to frame, coinciding with the respiratory cycle.

**Assessment:**
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** The pleural line exhibits a shimmering, granular appearance, and the speckle pattern below the line varies between consecutive frames.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** There is no static pleural line or unchanging A-line pattern.
*   **Signs of BOTH:** No transition point (lung point) is observed; the sliding motion is consistent across the visualized area.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
