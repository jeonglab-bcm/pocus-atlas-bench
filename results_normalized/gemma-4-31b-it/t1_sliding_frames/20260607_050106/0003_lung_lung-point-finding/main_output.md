*   **Sub-pleural Patterns:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line varies significantly between consecutive frames, reflecting the movement of the visceral pleura sliding against the parietal pleura.
*   **Artifacts:** There are subtle vertical, comet-tail-like artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line, which are typically associated with lung sliding.
