1.  **Frame-by-frame observation:** Throughout the clip, the bright, hyperechoic pleural line (located at approximately 1.5 to 2 cm depth) exhibits a consistent "shimmering" effect.
2.  **Sub-pleural analysis:** The grainy, speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line is not static; it changes and shifts with each subsequent frame, indicating movement of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura.
3.  **Additional signs:** There are some faint vertical artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line, which further support the presence of pleural sliding.

**Conclusion:**
The shimmering pleural line and the dynamic change in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between frames are characteristic signs of **pleural sliding present**.
