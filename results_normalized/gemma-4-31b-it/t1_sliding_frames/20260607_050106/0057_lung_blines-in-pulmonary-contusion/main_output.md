*   **Frames 1-2:** A bright, textured pleural line is visible. Below this line, there is an irregular, hyperechoic pattern. There is a subtle change in the texture of the sub-pleural area between these two frames.
*   **Frames 3-5:** These frames show a clear shimmering effect of the pleural line. Vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (B-lines/comet-tails) are originating from the pleural line and extending deeper into the lung. The speckle pattern below the pleural line changes dynamically between these frames.
*   **Frames 6-7:** As the probe moves, the pleural line remains visible, and the sub-pleural texture continues to vary, confirming a dynamic interface.

### Assessment of Signs:
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
    *   **Shimmering pleural line:** The pleural interface is granular and exhibits a shimmering movement across the frames.
    *   **B-lines:** Multiple vertical, hyperechoic artifacts originating from the pleural line are present, which are characteristic of sliding lung.
    *   **Dynamic speckle pattern:** There is clear variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern between consecutive frames, indicating the movement of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura.

*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** None observed. There is no smooth, static pleural line, nor are there static A-lines.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** No transition point between a sliding area and a static area is visible in these frames.

### Conclusion:
**Pleural sliding is present.** The observed shimmering of the pleural line and the presence of dynamic B-lines confirm that the lung is sliding against the chest wall at this location.
