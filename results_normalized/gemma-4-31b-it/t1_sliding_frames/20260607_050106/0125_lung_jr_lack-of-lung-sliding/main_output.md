# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the provided lung ultrasound frames:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** A bright, horizontal pleural line is visible.
*   **Artifacts:** There are prominent vertical, bright, laser-like artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These are known as **B-lines**.
*   **Dynamics:** While these are static frames, the presence of B-lines is a strong indicator that the visceral and parietal pleura are in contact and sliding against each other during respiration.

**Assessment:**
*   The presence of **B-lines** originating from the pleural line is a primary sign that pleural sliding is occurring.
*   There are no signs of a "barcode" pattern (which would be seen on M-mode) or a completely static, smooth pleural line without any dynamic artifacts.
*   There is no evidence of a "lung point" (a transition from sliding to non-sliding).

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **PRESENT**.
