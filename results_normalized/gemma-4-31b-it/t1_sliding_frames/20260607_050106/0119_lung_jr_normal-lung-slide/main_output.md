# 0119_lung_jr_normal-lung-slide

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound frames, here is the observation:

*   **Pleural Line:** There is a bright, sharply defined pleural line. Across all frames, this line remains smooth and static; it does not exhibit the shimmering or granular "sliding" motion typical of normal lung movement against the chest wall.
*   **Artifacts:** Prominent horizontal A-lines (reverberation artifacts) are visible below the pleural line. These lines remain perfectly static and unchanged throughout the sequence.
*   **B-lines:** There are no vertical comet-tail artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line.
*   **Sub-pleural Pattern:** The speckle pattern beneath the pleural line does not vary between frames, indicating a lack of dynamic movement.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**.
