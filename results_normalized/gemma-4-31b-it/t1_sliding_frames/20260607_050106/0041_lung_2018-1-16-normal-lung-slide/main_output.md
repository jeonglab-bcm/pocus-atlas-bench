# 0041_lung_2018-1-16-normal-lung-slide

Based on the sequential analysis of the ultrasound frames:

1.  **Pleural Line Observation:** A distinct, hyperechoic (bright) pleural line is visible at a depth of approximately 3-4 cm.
2.  **Movement Analysis:** Comparing the consecutive frames, the pleural line exhibits a subtle "shimmering" motion. More importantly, the speckle pattern in the sub-pleural region (the area immediately below the bright line) changes and shifts between frames. This indicates that the visceral pleura is sliding against the parietal pleura during respiration.
3.  **Absence of Negative Signs:** There is no evidence of a static, "frozen" pleural line or a barcode-like pattern that would suggest a lack of sliding (which would be seen in a pneumothorax).

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
