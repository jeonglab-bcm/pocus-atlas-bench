# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound video:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line:** The pleural line is bright and demonstrates a characteristic "shimmering" or granular movement between frames.
* **Artifacts:** There are small, vertical, bright comet-tail artifacts (B-lines) originating from the pleural line that change in position and appearance across the frames.
* **Sub-pleural Pattern:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line varies between consecutive frames, which is typical of the sliding motion between the visceral and parietal pleura.
* There is no evidence of a static, smooth pleural line or a barcode sign (stratosphere sign). No transition point between sliding and non-sliding areas is observed.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **PRESENT**.
