# 0017_lung_lung-point2

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video:

**Observations:**
- **B-mode image (top):** The pleural line appears as a bright, horizontal interface that remains static across all frames. There is no evidence of shimmering or granular motion.
- **M-mode image (bottom):** The M-mode display shows a series of parallel horizontal lines both above and below the pleural line. This creates a pattern that looks like a barcode or a series of strata. The characteristic "sandy" or granular appearance (seashore sign) normally seen below the pleural line when the lung is sliding against the chest wall is completely missing.

**Assessment against signs:**
- **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** None. There is no shimmering, no seashore sign, and no dynamic variation in the sub-pleural speckle pattern.
- **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The **stratosphere / barcode sign** is clearly present on M-mode, and the pleural line remains smooth and static.
- **Signs of BOTH:** None. There is no transition point or alternation between patterns.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**. The presence of the stratosphere sign on M-mode is highly suggestive of a pneumothorax.
