# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound images:

1.  **Pleural Line Observation:** Across all frames, the pleural line appears as a smooth, sharply defined, bright horizontal line. It remains static and does not show any "shimmering" or granular movement characteristic of pleural sliding.
2.  **A-line Analysis:** Numerous horizontal A-lines (reverberation artifacts) are visible below the pleural line. These lines remain perfectly static and unchanged in position and appearance from frame to frame.
3.  **Sub-pleural Speckle Pattern:** The speckle pattern beneath the pleural line shows no variation or movement between consecutive frames.
4.  **Absence of B-lines:** There are no comet-tail artifacts or B-lines originating from the pleural line.

**Conclusion:**
The observation of a static pleural line and unchanging A-lines across all frames indicates that **pleural sliding is absent**.
