# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound video:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The bright, horizontal interface (the pleural line) shows a shimmering, granular motion from frame to frame. It is not static or perfectly smooth.
*   **Artifacts:** There are numerous vertical, hyperechoic artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These are **B-lines**.
*   **Dynamic Changes:** Comparing the consecutive frames, the pattern and intensity of the B-lines shift, and the speckle pattern below the pleural line varies. This indicates that the visceral and parietal pleura are moving against each other.

**Conclusion:**
The observation of a shimmering pleural line and the dynamic movement of the sub-pleural B-lines are characteristic signs of **pleural sliding present**.
