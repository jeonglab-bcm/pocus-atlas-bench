*   **Sub-pleural Pattern:** The grainy speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line changes slightly in texture and position between frames. This dynamic movement indicates that the visceral pleura is sliding against the parietal pleura during the respiratory cycle.
*   **Absence of Static Signs:** There are no perfectly static, unchanging horizontal A-lines, nor is there a "barcode" appearance that would suggest an absence of sliding.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
