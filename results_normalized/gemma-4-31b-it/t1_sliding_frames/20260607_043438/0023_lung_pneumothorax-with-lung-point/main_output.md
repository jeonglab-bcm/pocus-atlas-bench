# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the sequential analysis of the ultrasound frames, here is the observation:

1.  **Frame-by-frame analysis:** Comparing the consecutive images, there is a clear and consistent change in the speckle pattern immediately below the bright, hyperechoic pleural line. The texture is not static; it exhibits a "shimmering" or granular movement from frame to frame.
2.  **Assessment of signs:**
    *   **Pleural Sliding Signs:** The "shimmering" appearance and the dynamic variation of the speckle pattern below the pleural line are characteristic signs of pleural sliding.
    *   **Absence of Static Signs:** There is no static "barcode" appearance or a perfectly frozen pleural line that would suggest a lack of sliding (such as in a pneumothorax).
    *   **Lung Point:** There is no visible transition zone where sliding is present in one area and absent in another across the pleural line in these frames.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
