# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on the provided frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS) clip, here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **B-mode (bottom image):** There is a bright, smooth, and static pleural line with horizontal A-lines below it. There is no evidence of shimmering or granular movement at the pleural interface across the sequence of frames.
*   **M-mode (top image):** The M-mode image shows a series of parallel, horizontal lines extending from the pleural line down to the bottom of the screen. This is the classic **"barcode sign"** (also known as the **"stratosphere sign"**). In a normal lung with pleural sliding, we would expect a "seashore sign," where the area below the pleural line appears sandy or granular.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**.
