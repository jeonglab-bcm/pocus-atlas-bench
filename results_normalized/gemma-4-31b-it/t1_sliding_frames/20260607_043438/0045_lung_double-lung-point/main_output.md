# 0045_lung_double-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The bright, hyperechoic pleural line exhibits a "shimmering" motion across the frames, rather than remaining static.
*   **Sub-pleural Area:** There is a dynamic change in the speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line between consecutive frames. This movement is consistent with the sliding of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura during respiration.
*   **Absence of Static Signs:** There are no perfectly static A-lines or a smooth, unchanging pleural line that would suggest the absence of sliding.
*   **Uniformity:** The sliding is present uniformly across the visualized segment of the pleural line, with no transition point observed that would indicate a lung point.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
