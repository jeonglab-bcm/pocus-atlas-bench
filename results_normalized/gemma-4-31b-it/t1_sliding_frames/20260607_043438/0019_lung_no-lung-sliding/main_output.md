# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

1.  **Pleural Line Observation:** The pleural line appears as a smooth, bright, and sharply defined horizontal interface. Across all frames, there is no visible "shimmering" or granular movement typical of lung sliding.
2.  **Sub-pleural Pattern:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line and the horizontal A-lines remain perfectly static and unchanged from frame to frame. There are no dynamic B-lines or comet-tail artifacts originating from the pleural line.
3.  **Dynamic Assessment:** The lack of movement at the pleural interface indicates that the visceral and parietal pleura are not sliding against each other.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**.
