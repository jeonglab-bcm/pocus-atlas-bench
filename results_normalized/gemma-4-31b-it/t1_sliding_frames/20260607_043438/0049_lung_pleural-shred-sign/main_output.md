# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

1.  **Frame-by-frame observation:**
    *   Throughout the sequence, a bright, hyperechoic **pleural line** is clearly visible.
    *   There is a continuous, shimmering granular motion at the pleural interface across all frames.
    *   Prominent vertical, laser-like artifacts (**B-lines**) are seen originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen.
    *   The speckle pattern below the pleural line changes dynamically between consecutive frames, indicating active movement between the visceral and parietal pleura.

2.  **Assessment of signs:**
    *   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** The shimmering pleural line, the presence of B-lines, and the changing sub-pleural speckle pattern are all classic indicators that pleural sliding is occurring.
    *   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** There is no static, sharply defined "barcode" appearance; the pleural line is not fixed.
    *   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** There is no visible transition point where sliding suddenly disappears; the motion is consistent across the imaged area.

**Conclusion: Pleural sliding is present.**
