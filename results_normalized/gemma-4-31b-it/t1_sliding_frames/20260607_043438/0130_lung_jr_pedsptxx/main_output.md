Based on the analysis of the sequential frames provided from the lung ultrasound (LUS) video clip:

**Frame-by-Frame Observation:**
- Throughout all frames, the pleural line (the bright, hyperechoic horizontal interface) remains completely static and smooth.
- There is no evidence of "shimmering" or granular movement at the pleural interface between consecutive frames.
- No comet-tail artifacts or B-lines are visible originating from the pleural line.
- The speckle pattern in the sub-pleural region remains unchanged across all frames, indicating a lack of movement between the visceral and parietal pleura.

**Conclusion:**
The observation of a smooth, static pleural line and the absence of shimmering or B-lines across the frames indicates that **pleural sliding is absent**. This is a characteristic finding often associated with a pneumothorax.
