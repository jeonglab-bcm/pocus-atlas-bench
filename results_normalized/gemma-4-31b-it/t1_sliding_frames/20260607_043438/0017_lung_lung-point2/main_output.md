# 0017_lung_lung-point2

Based on the analysis of the provided lung ultrasound frames, here is the observation:

**Observation:**
*   **B-mode (top image):** The pleural line appears as a bright, horizontal, and static line.
*   **M-mode (bottom image):** The image shows a pattern of parallel horizontal lines both above and below the pleural line. This appearance is known as the **stratosphere sign** or **barcode sign**. In a normal lung with sliding, the area below the pleural line would have a grainy, "sandy" appearance (the seashore sign). The absence of this granular pattern and the presence of continuous horizontal lines indicate that there is no relative motion between the parietal and visceral pleura.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is absent.** The presence of the stratosphere/barcode sign on M-mode is a classic indicator that lung sliding is not occurring, which is highly suggestive of a pneumothorax.
