# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound images, here is the observation:

*   **Pleural Line Appearance:** The pleural line is visible as a bright, hyperechoic horizontal line. Across the sequential frames, this line exhibits a shimmering, granular quality rather than remaining completely static and smooth.
*   **Sub-pleural Texture:** The area immediately below the pleural line shows a grainy, shifting "speckle" pattern. This pattern varies between consecutive frames, which is the characteristic appearance of the visceral pleura sliding against the parietal pleura during respiration.
*   **Comparison:** There is no evidence of a "stratosphere" or barcode-like static appearance, nor is there a transition point where sliding suddenly stops (which would indicate a lung point).

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **present**.
