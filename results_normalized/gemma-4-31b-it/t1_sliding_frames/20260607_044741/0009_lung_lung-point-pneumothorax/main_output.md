# 0009_lung_lung-point-pneumothorax

Based on the sequential analysis of the lung ultrasound frames, here is the observation:

*   **Pleural Line Analysis:** The bright, hyperechoic pleural line (located at approximately 2-3 cm depth) is not static. It exhibits a "shimmering" or granular motion as it moves between consecutive frames.
*   **Sub-pleural Region:** The speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line is dynamic, changing its appearance from frame to frame. This creates the granular "sandy" appearance characteristic of pleural sliding in B-mode.
*   **Artifacts:** While horizontal A-lines (reverberation artifacts) are present, the interface at the pleural line is clearly moving.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is PRESENT.**
