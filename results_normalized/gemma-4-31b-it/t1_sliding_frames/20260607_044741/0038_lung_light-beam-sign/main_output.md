* **Frame 1:** A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is visible. The area below it is relatively dark.
* **Frame 2:** A bright, vertical, comet-tail artifact (B-line) appears, originating from the pleural line and extending downward.
* **Frame 3:** The B-line persists, and there is a slight shimmer/movement observed along the pleural line.
* **Frame 4:** The vertical B-line remains, and the texture of the pleural line continues to change slightly.

* **Frame 6:** The vertical artifact remains visible, and the sub-pleural speckle pattern varies.
* **Frame 7:** The B-line continues to be present, and the pleural line maintains its shimmering appearance.

**Observed Signs:**
* **Shimmering pleural line:** The bright interface of the pleura shows dynamic movement across the frames.
* **B-lines/Comet-tail artifacts:** There is a clear vertical artifact originating from the pleural line that varies slightly in intensity and position, which is a characteristic sign of pleural sliding.
* **Dynamic sub-pleural speckle:** The appearance of the area below the pleural line changes between frames.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
