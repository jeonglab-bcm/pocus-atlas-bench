# 0017_lung_lung-point2

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observations:**
*   **B-mode (top image):** The pleural line appears as a bright, smooth, and static horizontal line. There is no visible "shimmering" or movement of the lung against the chest wall between the frames.
*   **M-mode (bottom image):** The image shows a series of parallel horizontal lines both above and below the pleural line. This pattern is characteristic of the **"barcode sign"** (also known as the **"stratosphere sign"**). In a normal lung with sliding, the area below the pleural line would have a granular, "sandy" appearance (the "seashore sign").

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**. The presence of the barcode sign on M-mode is a key indicator that the visceral and parietal pleura are not sliding against each other, which is frequently associated with a pneumothorax.
