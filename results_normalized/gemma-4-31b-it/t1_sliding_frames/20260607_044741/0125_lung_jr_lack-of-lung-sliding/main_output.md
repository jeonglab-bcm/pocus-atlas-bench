# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential lung ultrasound frames:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** The pleural line (the bright, hyperechoic horizontal line located between the ribs) remains smooth and sharply defined across all frames. There is no observable "shimmering" or granular movement characteristic of pleural sliding.
*   **Artifacts:** Prominent horizontal A-lines are visible below the pleural line. These A-lines remain in identical positions across every frame, showing no change in their pattern or position.
*   **Sub-pleural Region:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line is static and does not vary between consecutive frames. There are no B-lines or comet-tail artifacts present.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **absent**. The static nature of the pleural line and the unchanging A-lines are characteristic signs of an absence of sliding, which can be seen in conditions such as a pneumothorax.
