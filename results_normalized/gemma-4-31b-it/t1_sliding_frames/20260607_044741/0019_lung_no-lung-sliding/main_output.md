# 0019_lung_no-lung-sliding

Based on a frame-by-frame analysis of the provided lung ultrasound images:

*   **Frame-by-frame observation:** As you move through the sequence of frames, the bright, horizontal interface (the pleural line) exhibits a shimmering, dynamic quality. Most importantly, the granular "speckle" pattern located immediately below the pleural line changes its appearance and position between consecutive frames. 
*   **Signs of sliding:** The observed shimmering of the pleural line and the changing sub-pleural speckle pattern are characteristic signs of pleural sliding. This occurs as the visceral pleura of the lung slides against the parietal pleura of the chest wall during respiration.
*   **Signs of absence/lung point:** There is no evidence of a static "barcode" pattern, nor is there a visible transition point where sliding suddenly stops (which would indicate a lung point).

**Conclusion:** Pleural sliding is **present**.
