# 0028_lung_normal-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

1.  **Frame-by-frame observation:**
    *   The images show a longitudinal view of the chest wall with a bright, hyperechoic pleural line.
    *   Comparing the frames, the pleural line is not static; it exhibits a shimmering, granular motion as the patient breathes.
    *   The speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line varies from frame to frame, which is the characteristic "shimmering" effect of the visceral and parietal pleura sliding against each other.
    *   While horizontal A-lines (reverberation artifacts) are present deeper in the image, the movement at the pleural interface is clear.

2.  **Application of signs:**
    *   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** Observed. There is a shimmering/granular pleural line and the sub-pleural speckle pattern differs between consecutive frames.
    *   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** Not observed. The pleural line is not static or smooth.
    *   **Signs of BOTH:** Not observed. There is no transition point from sliding to non-sliding visible.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
