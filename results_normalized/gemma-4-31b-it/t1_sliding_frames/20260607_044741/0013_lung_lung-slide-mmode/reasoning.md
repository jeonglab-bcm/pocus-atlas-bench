# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode — Reasoning

The user wants me to analyze a series of lung ultrasound (LUS) frames to determine if pleural sliding is present, absent, or both (lung point).

1.  **Analyze the images:**
    *   The images show a split screen: an M-mode image on top and a B-mode image on the bottom.
    *   **B-mode (bottom):** I see a bright, horizontal line (pleural line) with A-lines (horizontal reverberation artifacts) below it. In the sequence of frames, the pleural line and the area immediately beneath it seem to remain very static. There's no obvious "shimmering" or granular movement associated with respiration.
    *   **M-mode (top):** This is the key for diagnosing pleural sliding.
        *   In the first few frames, the top part (above the pleural line) is horizontal lines. The bottom part (below the pleural line) is also characterized by horizontal lines.
        *   Specifically, the pattern below the pleural line consists of parallel horizontal lines, mirroring the pattern above. This is the classic "stratosphere sign" or "barcode sign".
        *   A "seashore sign" would have a sandy/granular appearance below the pleural line, which is not present here.
        *   Across all frames, the M-mode image remains consistently "barcode-like". The horizontal lines are persistent and do not change into a granular pattern.

2.  **Evaluate based on the provided signs:**
    *   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:**
        *   Shimmering/granular pleural line: Not observed.
        *   Comet-tail artifacts/B-lines: Not seen.
        *   Seashore sign: Not seen.
        *   Speckle pattern differs: The sub-pleural area looks static.
    *   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:**
        *   Smooth, static pleural line: Yes, it looks static.
        *   Stratosphere / barcode sign: Yes, the M-mode shows clear horizontal lines throughout, both above and below the pleural line.
        *   A-lines remain static: Yes.
        *   No variation in sub-pleural speckle: Correct.
    *   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):**
        *   Transition point: Not observed.
        *   Alternating patterns: Not observed.

3.  **Conclusion:**
    *   The presence of the "barcode sign" or "stratosphere sign" on M-mode is highly indicative of absent pleural sliding. This is a characteristic finding in pneumothorax (though it can occur in other conditions like pleural adhesions or severe consolidation).
    *   Based on the evidence, pleural sliding is absent.
