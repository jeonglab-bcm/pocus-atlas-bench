# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on the provided sequential frames of the lung ultrasound (LUS), here is the analysis:

**Observation:**
*   **B-mode (Bottom Image):** The pleural line appears as a smooth, bright horizontal interface. The A-lines (horizontal reverberation artifacts) beneath it remain static and unchanged across all frames, without the characteristic shimmering or granular motion typically seen with breathing.
*   **M-mode (Top Image):** The M-mode display shows a pattern of parallel horizontal lines extending both above and below the pleural line. This absence of a granular "sandy" appearance below the pleural line is known as the **stratosphere sign** or **barcode sign**. This pattern is consistent across every frame in the sequence.

**Conclusion:**
Signs of pleural sliding are **ABSENT**. The presence of the barcode sign on M-mode is a key indicator that the visceral and parietal pleura are not sliding against each other.
