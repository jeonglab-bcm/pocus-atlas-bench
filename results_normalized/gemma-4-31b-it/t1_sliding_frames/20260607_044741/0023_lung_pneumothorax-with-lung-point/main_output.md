# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound:

1.  **Frame-by-Frame Observation:**
    *   The bright, hyperechoic horizontal line representing the pleural interface is visible.
    *   Across the frames, the pleural line exhibits a **shimmering, granular movement**, which is characteristic of the visceral and parietal pleura sliding against each other during respiration.
    *   There are visible vertical **comet-tail artifacts (B-lines)** originating from the pleural line.
    *   The **speckle pattern below the pleural line is dynamic**, changing between consecutive frames, rather than remaining static.

2.  **Evaluation of Signs:**
    *   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** Observed (shimmering pleural line, presence of B-lines, and changing sub-pleural speckle pattern).
    *   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** Not observed (the pleural line is not static, and there is no "barcode" appearance).
    *   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** Not observed (the sliding is consistent across the viewed area of the pleural line).

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **present**.
