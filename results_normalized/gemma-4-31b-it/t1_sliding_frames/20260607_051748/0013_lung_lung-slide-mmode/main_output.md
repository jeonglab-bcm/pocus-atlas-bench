# 0013_lung_lung-slide-mmode

Based on the provided sequential frames from the lung ultrasound (LUS), here is the analysis:

**B-mode Analysis (Bottom section of each frame):**
*   The images show a bright, horizontal pleural line.
*   Below the pleural line, horizontal A-lines (reverberation artifacts) are visible. 
*   While the B-mode images are static, the overall pattern is consistent with a normally aerated lung.

**M-mode Analysis (Top section of each frame):**
*   The M-mode displays a clear transition at the pleural line.
*   **Above the pleural line:** There are distinct, parallel horizontal lines, representing the static tissues of the chest wall.
*   **Below the pleural line:** There is a grainy, speckled pattern resembling sand. This is the classic **"seashore sign."** 
*   The presence of this granular pattern below the pleural line indicates that the lung is sliding against the chest wall during respiration.

**Conclusion:**
The observation of the **seashore sign** on M-mode confirms that **pleural sliding is present**.
