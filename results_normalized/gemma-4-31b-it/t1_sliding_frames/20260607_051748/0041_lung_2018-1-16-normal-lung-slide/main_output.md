# 0041_lung_2018-1-16-normal-lung-slide

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames:

**Observations:**
* **Pleural Line:** There is a bright, horizontal pleural line that exhibits a shimmering, granular motion as the frames progress.
* **Artifacts:** Numerous vertical, comet-tail-like artifacts (B-lines) are seen originating from the pleural line. These B-lines are dynamic, flickering and changing slightly in appearance between consecutive frames.
* **Sub-pleural Region:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line is not static; it changes dynamically, indicating the movement of the visceral pleura against the parietal pleura.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of a shimmering pleural line, dynamic B-lines, and a changing sub-pleural speckle pattern are all classic signs of **pleural sliding present**.
