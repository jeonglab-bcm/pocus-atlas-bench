# 0038_lung_light-beam-sign

Based on the analysis of the sequential ultrasound frames:

*   **Frame-by-frame observation:** Across the frames, the bright, hyperechoic pleural line exhibits a shimmering or "granular" motion. The speckle pattern in the region immediately below the pleural line is not static; it changes and shifts with each frame, indicating movement between the visceral and parietal pleura.
*   **Assessment of signs:**
    *   **Pleural sliding is PRESENT:** The shimmering movement of the pleural line and the dynamic variation of the sub-pleural speckle pattern are classic signs of pleural sliding.
    *   **Pleural sliding is NOT absent:** The pleural line is not static or smooth, and there is clear movement.
    *   **Lung point is NOT observed:** There is no visible transition from a sliding area to a non-sliding area within the view.

**Conclusion: Pleural sliding is present.**
