# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

Based on the sequential analysis of the lung ultrasound frames:

*   **Pleural Line Observation:** The pleural line (the bright, horizontal interface) exhibits a clear "shimmering" or granular movement between the frames. This indicates that the visceral and parietal pleura are sliding against each other during respiration.
*   **Artifact Analysis:** There are prominent, vertical, laser-like artifacts originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. These are **B-lines**, which are strongly associated with the presence of lung sliding.
*   **Dynamic Pattern:** The speckle pattern below the pleural line changes from frame to frame, reflecting the movement of the lung.

There are no signs of a static "barcode" pattern or a fixed, smooth pleural line that would suggest an absence of sliding, nor is there a transition point characteristic of a lung point.

**Conclusion: Pleural sliding is present.**
