# 0125_lung_jr_lack-of-lung-sliding

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames provided:

**Observations:**
*   **Pleural Line:** A smooth, bright, and sharply defined pleural line is visible.
*   **A-lines:** Multiple horizontal, parallel hyperechoic lines (A-lines) are present below the pleural line.
*   **Dynamics:** When comparing the frames sequentially, there is no shimmering or granular movement at the pleural line. The A-lines remain perfectly static and unchanged in their position and appearance across all frames. There are no B-lines or comet-tail artifacts.

**Conclusion:**
The signs indicate that **pleural sliding is absent**. The static nature of the pleural line and the unchanging A-lines are characteristic of a lack of sliding, which is highly suggestive of a pneumothorax in this clinical context.
