# 0003_lung_lung-point-finding

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound:

*   **Observation:** Across the frames, the pleural line (the bright, horizontal interface) exhibits a shimmering, granular quality. More importantly, the speckle pattern immediately below the pleural line is dynamic, changing in appearance from one frame to the next. This movement is characteristic of the visceral pleura sliding against the parietal pleura during respiration.

*   **Conclusion:** Pleural sliding is **present**.
