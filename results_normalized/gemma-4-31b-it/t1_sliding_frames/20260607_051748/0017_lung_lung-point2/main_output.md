# 0017_lung_lung-point2

Based on the analysis of the sequential frames from the lung ultrasound video:

**Observations:**
*   **B-mode analysis (top image):** There is a bright, thin pleural line that exhibits a shimmering, granular appearance across the frames, indicating motion at the interface between the visceral and parietal pleura.
*   **M-mode analysis (bottom image):** The M-mode image shows a clear distinction at the pleural line (approximately 1.5 cm deep). 
    *   **Above the pleural line:** There are parallel, horizontal lines, representing the static tissues of the chest wall.
    *   **Below the pleural line:** There is a granular, "sandy" pattern.
*   The combination of these two patterns in M-mode is known as the **"seashore sign."**

**Conclusion:**
The presence of the seashore sign on M-mode and the shimmering pleural line on B-mode are definitive indicators that **pleural sliding is present**.
