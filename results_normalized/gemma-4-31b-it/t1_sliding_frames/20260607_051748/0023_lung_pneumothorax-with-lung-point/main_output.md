# 0023_lung_pneumothorax-with-lung-point

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound, here is the analysis:

**Frame-by-frame observation:**
Across all seven frames, the bright, hyperechoic pleural line is visible. As the frames progress, there is a subtle but consistent "shimmering" or granular movement at this interface. Additionally, the speckle pattern in the area immediately below the pleural line changes from frame to frame, reflecting the motion of the visceral pleura sliding against the parietal pleura.

**Assessment:**
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** There is a clear shimmering/granular appearance of the pleural line and a changing speckle pattern below the pleural line between consecutive frames.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** The pleural line is not static, and there is no "barcode" appearance (though M-mode is not provided, the B-mode motion is evident).
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** There is no visible transition point where sliding disappears across the field of view; the motion is uniform.

**Conclusion:**
**Pleural sliding is present.**
