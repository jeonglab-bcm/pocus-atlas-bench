# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

Based on the sequential analysis of the provided lung ultrasound frames:

*   **Frames 1-5:** A bright, hyperechoic pleural line is visible at approximately 2-3 cm depth. This line is not static; it exhibits a characteristic **shimmering or granular motion** between frames, indicating that the visceral and parietal pleura are sliding against each other. Additionally, multiple **B-lines (vertical comet-tail artifacts)** are clearly visible originating from the pleural line and extending downwards, which is a strong indicator of pleural sliding.
*   **Frames 6-7:** As the probe is moved, the pleural line remains visible and continues to show shimmering and associated artifacts, although the angle changes.

**Conclusion:**
Pleural sliding is **PRESENT**. The observed shimmering of the pleural line and the presence of B-lines are classic ultrasound signs that rule out a pneumothorax at the scanned location.
