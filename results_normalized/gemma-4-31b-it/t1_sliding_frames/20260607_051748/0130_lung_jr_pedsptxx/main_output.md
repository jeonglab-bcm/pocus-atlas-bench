# 0130_lung_jr_pedsptxx

Based on the sequential analysis of the ultrasound frames:

1.  **Frame-by-frame observation:** Throughout the sequence, the bright, white pleural line exhibits a characteristic shimmering or granular movement. The texture and position of the pleural line are not static; they fluctuate slightly with each frame.
2.  **Sub-pleural analysis:** The speckle pattern in the region immediately below the pleural line changes and shifts between consecutive frames. This indicates that the visceral pleura is sliding against the parietal pleura during respiration.
3.  **Artifacts:** Subtle, vertical bright artifacts (comet-tail artifacts) can be seen originating from the pleural line, which often accompany normal pleural sliding.

**Conclusion:**
The presence of a shimmering pleural line and the dynamic variation of the sub-pleural speckle pattern confirm that **pleural sliding is present**.
