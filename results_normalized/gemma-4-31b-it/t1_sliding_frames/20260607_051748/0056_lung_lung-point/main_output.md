# 0056_lung_lung-point

Based on the sequential analysis of the lung ultrasound frames:

*   **Frame-by-frame analysis:** As the frames progress from 1 through 7, there is a clear, dynamic movement of the bright, hyperechoic pleural line. This is characterized by a "shimmering" effect. Furthermore, the granular speckle pattern beneath the pleural line changes and shifts from one frame to the next, reflecting the motion of the visceral pleura sliding against the parietal pleura during respiration.
*   **Signs of sliding PRESENT:** The shimmering motion of the pleural line and the varying sub-pleural speckle pattern are classic signs of pleural sliding.
*   **Signs of sliding ABSENT:** None of these are observed; the pleural line is not static, and there is significant variation in the sub-pleural image between frames.
*   **Signs of BOTH (lung point):** There is no transition point visible where sliding disappears; the sliding is consistent across the viewed segment of the pleura.

**Conclusion: Pleural sliding is present.**
