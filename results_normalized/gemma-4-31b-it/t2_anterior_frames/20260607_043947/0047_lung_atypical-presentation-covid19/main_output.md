# 0047_lung_atypical-presentation-covid19

Across the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound, the following observations are made:

- **B-lines Assessment**: Multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts are clearly visible, originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the image. These artifacts move in synchrony with the pleural line during respiration. In several frames (e.g., frames 4 and 7), the B-lines are discrete and well-separated. In other frames (e.g., frames 3 and 5), these lines coalesce and become more confluent, creating a brighter, more diffuse appearance. This combination of discrete and coalescing vertical artifacts indicates a mixed B-line pattern.
- **Consolidation Assessment**: There is no evidence of alveolar consolidation. The lung parenchyma does not show tissue-like hepatization, there is no irregular "shred sign" at the lung border, and no punctate or linear air bronchograms are present. The area below the pleural line is characterized by aeration artifacts rather than solid tissue.

**Conclusion:**
