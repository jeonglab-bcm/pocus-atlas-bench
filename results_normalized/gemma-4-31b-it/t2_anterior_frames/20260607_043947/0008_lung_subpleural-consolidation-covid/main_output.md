# 0008_lung_subpleural-consolidation-covid

Based on the analysis of the lung ultrasound (LUS) video frames:

**Analysis:**
- **Frame-by-frame observation:** Each frame reveals a large, anechoic (dark) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Beneath this fluid collection, the lung parenchyma appears as a solid, hyperechoic mass with a texture resembling the liver (hepatization). Within this consolidated lung tissue, multiple small, bright punctate foci are visible, which are characteristic of air bronchograms.
- **B-lines:** There are no vertical, laser-like artifacts extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen. No B-lines (lung rockets) are observed.
- **Consolidation:** The lung tissue shows clear signs of alveolar consolidation, specifically tissue-like hepatization and the presence of air bronchograms (hyperechoic dots within the solid lung).

**Conclusions:**
- **lung_rockets:** false
- **consolidation:** true
- **consolidation_type:** air_bronchogram
