*   **B-lines Assessment**: Each frame clearly demonstrates multiple hyperechoic vertical artifacts (B-lines or lung rockets) that originate from the bright pleural line and extend to the bottom of the image without fading. These B-lines are discrete and well-separated, with dark lung parenchyma visible between them. They do not coalesce into a diffuse white sheet. This pattern is characteristic of a septal B-line distribution.
