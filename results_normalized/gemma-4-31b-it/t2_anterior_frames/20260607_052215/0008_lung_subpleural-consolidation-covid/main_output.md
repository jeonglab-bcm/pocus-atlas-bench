These artifacts merge into a diffuse white sheet, obscuring any A-lines and creating a "white lung" appearance. This pattern is characteristic of coalescing B-lines.

However, there is no evidence of lung parenchyma exhibiting tissue-like hepatization (liver-like texture), a shred sign (irregular deep border), or air bronchograms (punctate hyperechoic foci within the lung). The lung tissue adjacent to the fluid displays a ground-glass pattern rather than solid consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
