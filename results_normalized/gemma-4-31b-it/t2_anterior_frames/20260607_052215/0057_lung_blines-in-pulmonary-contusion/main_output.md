# 0057_lung_blines-in-pulmonary-contusion

**B-lines Assessment:**
Across multiple frames, there are prominent hyperechoic vertical artifacts that originate from the pleural line and extend to the bottom of the screen. These B-lines are not discrete or well-separated; instead, they are confluent and coalescing, merging into diffuse "white sheets" that obscure normal A-lines. This appearance is characteristic of a ground-glass pattern, which is often associated with alveolar-interstitial syndrome (e.g., pulmonary edema or pneumonia).
