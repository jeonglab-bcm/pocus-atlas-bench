# 0046_lung_light-beam-sign-covid19

Based on the sequential analysis of the lung ultrasound frames:

**B-lines Assessment:**
*   **Observation:** In all frames, there are clear hyperechoic vertical artifacts that originate from the pleural line and extend all the way to the bottom of the image. In some areas and frames (e.g., the left side of frame 1 and frame 7), these B-lines are discrete and separated. In other areas (e.g., the right side of frames 4 and 7), the B-lines coalesce to form wide, confluent "white sheets" of hyperechogenicity.
*   **Conclusion:** B-lines are present. Because both discrete (septal) and coalescing (ground-glass) patterns are visible, the subtype is **mixed**.
