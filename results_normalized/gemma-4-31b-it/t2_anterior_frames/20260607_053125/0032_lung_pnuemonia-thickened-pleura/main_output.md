# 0032_lung_pnuemonia-thickened-pleura

Across the sequential frames of this lung ultrasound clip from the right hemithorax (Zone 3 Low), the following observations are made:

**B-lines Assessment:**
Multiple hyperechoic, vertical artifacts are clearly visible, originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the image. These "lung rockets" are present throughout the sequence. In some regions, the B-lines are discrete and separated by darker lung parenchyma, characteristic of a septal pattern. In other regions, the B-lines coalesce or merge into a more diffuse, bright appearance, characteristic of a ground-glass pattern. Because both discrete and confluent vertical artifacts are observed, the pattern is mixed.

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The lung parenchyma does not exhibit tissue-like hepatization; it remains predominantly aerated (though fluid-filled as evidenced by the B-lines). There is no evidence of a "shred sign" at the lung border, nor are there any visible air bronchograms within the tissue. Therefore, no consolidation is present.
