# 0033_lung_air-bronchograms-in-lll-pneumonia

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound for the left lung zone 2, here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **B-lines:** There are no hyperechoic vertical artifacts extending from the pleural line to the bottom of the screen. Normal A-lines are not clearly visible because the lung is not normally aerated in this region.
*   **Consolidation:** There is a large area of the lung parenchyma that has lost its normal aeration and now exhibits a solid, tissue-like echogenicity resembling the liver, which is characteristic of **hepatization**. Within this consolidated area, multiple bright, hyperechoic punctate and linear foci are clearly visible; these represent **air bronchograms** (air trapped within the bronchi of the consolidated lung). An irregular border between the consolidated and non-consolidated lung is also present, which is consistent with the **shred sign**.

**Conclusions:**
*   **B-lines Assessment:** `lung_rockets = false`
*   **Consolidation Assessment:** `consolidation = true`
*   **Consolidation Type:** `consolidation_type = "air_bronchogram"` (due to the prominent hyperechoic foci within the hepatized lung tissue).
