# 0049_lung_pleural-shred-sign

**B-lines Assessment**
The images consistently show a prominent, broad hyperechoic vertical artifact originating from the pleural line and extending to the bottom of the screen. This artifact is characteristic of a B-line (lung rocket). The artifact is confluent and broad, rather than being a series of discrete, well-spaced lines, which creates a "white lung" appearance in that region. This indicates a ground-glass pattern.
