# 0031_lung_pneumoniashred-sign

Across the sequential frames of the right lung ultrasound, there is a significant loss of normal aeration artifacts.

**B-lines Assessment:**
There are confluent, coalescing hyperechoic vertical artifacts that merge into a diffuse white sheet, obscuring any normal A-lines. This "white lung" appearance is characteristic of ground-glass B-lines, indicating severe alveolar-interstitial syndrome or edema.
- **lung_rockets**: true
- **subtype**: ground_glass

**Consolidation Assessment:**
The lung parenchyma exhibits a solid, echogenic appearance resembling the texture of the liver, which is a hallmark of hepatization. Furthermore, there is an irregular, "shredded" border between the consolidated and partially aerated areas (shred sign), and small punctate hyperechoic foci within the hepatized tissue are visible, representing air bronchograms.
- **consolidation**: true
- **consolidation_type**: hepatization
