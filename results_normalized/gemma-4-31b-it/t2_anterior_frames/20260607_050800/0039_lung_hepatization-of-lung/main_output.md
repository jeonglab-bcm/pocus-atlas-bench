# 0039_lung_hepatization-of-lung

Based on the sequential frames of the lung ultrasound:

### B-lines Assessment
The images do not show any vertical, hyperechoic artifacts (lung rockets) extending from the pleura to the bottom of the screen. The normal aeration artifacts are replaced by a solid tissue-like appearance.
- **lung_rockets**: false

### Consolidation Assessment
The ultrasound images show a large area of lung parenchyma that has lost its normal aerated appearance and instead exhibits a solid, echogenic texture similar to that of the liver (**hepatization**). Within this consolidated area, there are several small, hyperechoic punctate and linear foci, which are characteristic of **air bronchograms**. Additionally, there is an irregular, "shredded" deep border between the consolidated area and the remaining aerated lung (shred sign), particularly visible on the right side of the consolidated mass.
- **consolidation**: true
- **consolidation_type**: "hepatization" (as the predominant tissue-like appearance is liver-like)
