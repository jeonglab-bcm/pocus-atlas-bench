# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The provided ultrasound frames of the right posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) demonstrate the following:

1.  **Pleural Effusion**: There is a large, anechoic (black) fluid collection filling a significant portion of the pleural space.
2.  **Consolidation**: Within this fluid, there is a region of consolidated lung parenchyma that has a tissue-like, hepatized appearance (similar to liver texture).
3.  **Dynamic Signs**: The consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within the pleural fluid, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
4.  **Air Bronchograms**: Small, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within the hepatized lung, representing air bronchograms.

Since both a pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are clearly present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.
