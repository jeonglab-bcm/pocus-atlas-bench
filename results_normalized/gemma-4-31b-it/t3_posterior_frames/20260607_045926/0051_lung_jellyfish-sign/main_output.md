# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The ultrasound images demonstrate a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, a piece of consolidated lung tissue is visible, appearing solid and echogenic (tissue-like hepatization). This consolidated lung fragment is floating and undulating within the fluid, which is the classic **"jellyfish sign."**

Because both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are present in the posterior/lateral dependent zone, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
