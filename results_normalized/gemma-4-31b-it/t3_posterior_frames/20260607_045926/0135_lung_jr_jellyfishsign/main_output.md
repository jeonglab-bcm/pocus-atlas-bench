# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large, anechoic (black) space, which is diagnostic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a hyperechoic, consolidated mass of lung tissue that exhibits a tissue-like appearance (hepatization). This consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within the pleural fluid, which is a classic finding known as the "jellyfish sign."

Because both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are clearly visible, this is a PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
