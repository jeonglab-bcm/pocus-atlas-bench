# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

The ultrasound frames show a large anechoic (black) space, which represents a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, there is a fragment of lung tissue that appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue (**hepatization**). This consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating with respiration, which is a classic **"jellyfish sign"**. The overall appearance is characteristic of a combination of pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation in the posterior lung zone.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
