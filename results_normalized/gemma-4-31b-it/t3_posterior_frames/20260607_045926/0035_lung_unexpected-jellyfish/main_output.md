# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

Based on the provided ultrasound frames from the PLAPS point, the following observations can be made:

1.  **Effusion Signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. The consolidated lung is seen undulating within this fluid collection, exhibiting the **jellyfish sign**.
2.  **Consolidation Signs:** A large portion of the lung has lost its normal aeration and appears solid with a liver-like texture, known as **tissue-like hepatization**. The border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid/aerated lung is irregular and jagged, which is the **shred sign**. Additionally, small, bright hyperechoic spots are visible within the hepatized lung, representing **air bronchograms**.

Because both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are present in the posterior dependent zone, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.
