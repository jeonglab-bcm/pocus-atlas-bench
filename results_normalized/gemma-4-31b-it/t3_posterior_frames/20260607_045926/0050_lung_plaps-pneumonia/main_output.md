# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

The ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone show a large, anechoic (black) space consistent with a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a consolidated area of lung parenchyma that exhibits tissue-like hepatization (resembling liver texture). This consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within the effusion, which is characteristic of the "jellyfish sign."

- **Effusion signs:** Large anechoic space, jellyfish sign.
- **Consolidation signs:** Tissue-like hepatization of the floating lung.

Conclusion:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
