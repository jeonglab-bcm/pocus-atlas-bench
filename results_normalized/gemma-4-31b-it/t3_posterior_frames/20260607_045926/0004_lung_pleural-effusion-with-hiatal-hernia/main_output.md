# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

Based on the provided sequential ultrasound frames from the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), the following observations can be made:

1.  **Effusion signs**: There is a prominent anechoic (black) space surrounding the consolidated lung tissue, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. The way the consolidated lung is suspended within this fluid collection is suggestive of the **jellyfish sign**.
2.  **Consolidation signs**: The lung parenchyma exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic, resembling liver texture. Within this hepatized area, there are small, punctate hyperechoic foci, which represent **air bronchograms**. Additionally, the border between the consolidated and aerated lung is irregular, which is characteristic of the **shred sign**.

Because both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are clearly visible, this represents a PLAPS pattern.
