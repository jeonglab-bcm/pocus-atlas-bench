# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) show the following findings:

1.  **Consolidation:** There is a large area of **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration and appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of the liver.
2.  **Air Bronchograms:** Within the consolidated tissue, multiple small, punctate hyperechoic foci and some small anechoic areas are visible, which are characteristic of air bronchograms (air-filled or fluid-filled bronchi within consolidated lung).
3.  **Effusion:** Adjacent to the consolidated lung, there is a significant **anechoic (black) space**, indicating a pleural effusion.
4.  **Shred Sign:** The border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid is irregular and fragmented, representing the "shred sign."

Since both lung consolidation (hepatization) and pleural effusion are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
