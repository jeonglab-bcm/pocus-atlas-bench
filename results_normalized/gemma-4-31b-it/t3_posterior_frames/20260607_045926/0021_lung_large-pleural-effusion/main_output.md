# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a significant anechoic (black) area, which is diagnostic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a fragment of lung tissue that appears solid and echogenic (hepatized/consolidated) and is seen floating and undulating with the respiratory cycle. This appearance is the classic "jellyfish sign," which represents a combination of pleural effusion and associated lung consolidation/atelectasis.

Conclusion:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
