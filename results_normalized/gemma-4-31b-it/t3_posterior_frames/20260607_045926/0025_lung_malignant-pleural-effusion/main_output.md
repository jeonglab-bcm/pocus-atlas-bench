# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

The provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) show a complete absence of normal lung aeration (no A-lines). 

The images demonstrate:
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma has lost its normal appearance and now resembles solid organ tissue (like the liver), which is characteristic of alveolar consolidation.
- **Air bronchograms**: Small, punctate hyperechoic (bright) foci are visible within the consolidated lung tissue.
- **Effusion**: There are significant anechoic (black) areas, both within the consolidated lung (possibly cavitations) and surrounding it, indicating the presence of pleural fluid.

The combination of a hepatized, solid-appearing lung with associated anechoic fluid collections confirms the presence of both consolidation and pleural effusion.

**Conclusion:**
- plaps = true
- type = "both"
