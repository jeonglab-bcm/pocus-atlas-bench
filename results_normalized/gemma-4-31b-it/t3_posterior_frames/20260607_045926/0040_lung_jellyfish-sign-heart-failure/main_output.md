# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

The ultrasound images from the PLAPS (Posterolateral Alveolar and/or Pleural Syndrome) point demonstrate the following findings:

*   **Pleural Effusion:** There is a large, anechoic (black) space consistent with fluid collection. The presence of this fluid surrounding the lung, with the lung appearing to float and undulate within it, is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Alveolar Consolidation:** The lung tissue seen on the left side of the images exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, meaning it has lost its normal aerated appearance and now resembles solid organ tissue (like the liver). There is an irregular, shredded-looking border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid, characteristic of the **shred sign**.

Because both a significant pleural effusion and consolidated lung parenchyma are present, this is a mixed PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
