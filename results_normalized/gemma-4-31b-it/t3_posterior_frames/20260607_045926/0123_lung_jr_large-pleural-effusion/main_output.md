# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

Based on the provided lung ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), the following observations are made:

1.  **Effusion Signs**: There is a large, predominantly anechoic (black) space occupying most of the image, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, irregularly shaped mass of lung tissue, which is the classic **"jellyfish sign"**.
2.  **Consolidation Signs**: The floating lung tissue exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic similar to liver texture. The irregular, "torn" border between this consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid is consistent with the **"shred sign"**.

Because both a significant fluid collection and consolidated lung tissue are present, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
