# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone demonstrate a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, irregularly shaped echogenic mass representing consolidated/atelectatic lung tissue. The dynamic movement of this consolidated lung within the surrounding fluid is typical of the "jellyfish sign." Because both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation (atelectasis) are clearly visible, this represents a PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
