These sequential ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone demonstrate the following:

*   **Pleural Effusion:** There is a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural fluid collection.
*   **Consolidation/Atelectasis:** Within the fluid, there is a visible fragment of lung tissue that is hyperechoic and exhibits a "tissue-like hepatization," resembling liver texture. This floating, undulating piece of lung within the effusion is the classic **"jellyfish sign."**

Because both a significant fluid collection and hepatized lung tissue are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.
