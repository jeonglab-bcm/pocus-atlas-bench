# 0007_lung_pleural-effusion-with-compressed-lung-and-spine-sign

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a combination of pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation:

1.  **Consolidation signs**: The lung parenchyma exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and homogeneous, similar to the texture of the liver. Within this consolidated area, there are multiple anechoic and hypoechoic spaces representing **air bronchograms**. The irregular, jagged border between the consolidated and aerated/fluid-filled areas is characteristic of the **shred sign**.
2.  **Effusion signs**: There is a prominent anechoic (black) space surrounding the consolidated lung, indicating a **pleural effusion**. The appearance of the consolidated lung "floating" within this fluid is suggestive of a **jellyfish sign**.

Since both pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
