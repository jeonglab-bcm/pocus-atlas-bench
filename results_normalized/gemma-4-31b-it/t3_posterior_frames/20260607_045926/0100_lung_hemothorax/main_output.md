# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large anechoic (black) area, which is diagnostic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a consolidated piece of lung tissue with a "tissue-like" or "hepatized" appearance. This consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within the fluid, which is a classic finding known as the "jellyfish sign."

Because both a significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present, this fits the criteria for a PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
