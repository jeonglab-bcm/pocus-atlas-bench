# 0096_lung_ecrc29rbuigumwcs1dakjt6c9osenx

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large anechoic (black) space, which is diagnostic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, undulating, and irregular piece of lung tissue that exhibits a tissue-like, echogenic appearance resembling liver texture (**hepatization**). This specific finding—consolidated lung floating within a pleural effusion—is known as the **jellyfish sign**.

Because both a pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation (atelectasis) are clearly visible, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
