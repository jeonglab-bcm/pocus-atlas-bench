# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

The ultrasound images of the right posterior/lateral lung zone (PLAPS point) show the following:

*   **Consolidation signs:** There is a large area of lung parenchyma that has lost its normal aeration and now exhibits a tissue-like, liver-like appearance (**hepatization**). Within this consolidated area, several small, hyperechoic linear and punctate foci are visible, which are characteristic of **air bronchograms**.
*   **Effusion signs:** An anechoic (black) space is clearly visible, representing a **pleural effusion**. The consolidated lung is seen undulating within this fluid collection, which is the **jellyfish sign**.

Since both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

- **type = "both"**
