# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The ultrasound images demonstrate a large anechoic (black) area, which is indicative of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, undulating piece of consolidated lung parenchyma that moves during respiration, a finding known as the "jellyfish sign." The floating lung tissue exhibits hepatization (a solid, tissue-like appearance).

Because both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, this represents a PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
