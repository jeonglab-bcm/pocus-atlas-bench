# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

Based on the provided lung ultrasound (LUS) images of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the analysis:

**Observations:**
*   **Absence of normal aeration:** There is a complete absence of normal lung artifacts such as A-lines or typical lung sliding.
*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The lung parenchyma exhibits a solid, echogenic appearance resembling the texture of an organ like the liver. This "hepatization" is a hallmark sign of lung consolidation.
*   **Internal architecture:** There are numerous internal hypoechoic to anechoic spaces. While traditional air bronchograms are often hyperechoic, these internal voids within the consolidated tissue represent air or fluid-filled bronchi/cysts.
*   **Lack of effusion signs:** There is no clear anechoic (black) fluid collection bounded by the pleural line (quad sign) or undulating lung tissue within fluid (jellyfish sign) visible in these frames.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "consolidation"**
