# 0143_lung_jr_pleuraleffusion

The ultrasound frames from the posterior lung zone show a significant anechoic (black) space between the chest wall and the lung, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is fragmented, hyperechoic lung tissue that moves and undulates during respiration, creating a classic "jellyfish sign." The texture of this fragmented lung is solid and resembles liver tissue, indicating alveolar consolidation (hepatization).

Conclusion:
- plaps = true
- type = "both"
