# 0058_lung_pneumonia

The provided ultrasound frames from the posterior lung zone demonstrate a combination of findings:

1.  **Pleural Effusion:** There is a prominent, large anechoic (black) space in the superficial/superior aspect of the image, characteristic of a pleural effusion.
2.  **Alveolar Consolidation:** Below the fluid collection, the lung parenchyma exhibits "tissue-like hepatization," appearing solid and echogenic, similar to liver texture.
3.  **Air Bronchograms:** Within the hepatized lung tissue, there are numerous punctate and linear hyperechoic foci, which represent air bronchograms (air trapped in the bronchi of consolidated lung).

Because both a pleural effusion and pulmonary consolidation are present in this posterior dependent zone, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.
