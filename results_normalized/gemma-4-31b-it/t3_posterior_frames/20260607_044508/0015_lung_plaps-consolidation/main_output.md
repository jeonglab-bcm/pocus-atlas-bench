# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

The provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) demonstrate the following:

- **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration and appears solid and echogenic, with a texture very similar to that of the liver.
- **Air bronchograms**: Within the consolidated lung tissue, there are several hyperechoic (bright) punctate and linear foci, which represent air trapped within the bronchi.
- **Absence of effusion**: There is no anechoic (black) fluid collection, and signs like the quad sign or jellyfish sign are not present.

These findings are characteristic of alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "consolidation"**
