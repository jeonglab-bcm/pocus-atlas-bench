# 0029_lung_parapneumonic-effusion

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show the following findings:

1.  **Effusion signs**: There is a prominent anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, the consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating, which is the "jellyfish sign."
2.  **Consolidation signs**: The compressed lung parenchyma has a solid, echogenic, tissue-like appearance, resembling liver texture, which is indicative of "tissue-like hepatization." 

Because both signs of pleural fluid and lung consolidation are clearly visible in the dependent lung zone, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
