# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

The ultrasound frames demonstrate a large anechoic (black) area consistent with a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, echogenic mass of lung tissue that exhibits **tissue-like hepatization** (appearing similar to liver texture) and contains small hyperechoic foci indicative of **air bronchograms**. The dynamic movement of this consolidated lung tissue within the effusion is a characteristic **"jellyfish sign"**.

Conclusion:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
