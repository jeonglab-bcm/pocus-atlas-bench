# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

The provided ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) demonstrate the following findings:

1.  **Pleural Effusion**: There is a large, anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion.
2.  **Consolidation**: Within the pleural effusion, there is a large, irregularly shaped, echogenic mass. This represents a consolidated (hepatized) segment of the lung, which has lost its normal aeration.
3.  **Jellyfish Sign**: The consolidated lung segment is seen floating and undulating within the pleural fluid, a pathognomonic finding known as the "jellyfish sign."

Because both a significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
