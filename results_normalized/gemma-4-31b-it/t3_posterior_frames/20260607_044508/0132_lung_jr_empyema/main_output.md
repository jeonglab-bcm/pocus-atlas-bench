# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

The ultrasound images from the PLAPS point demonstrate a large area of **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration and appears solid, resembling liver tissue. Within this consolidated area, there are small hyperechoic foci consistent with **air bronchograms**. Additionally, an irregular interface is visible between the consolidated lung and the adjacent aerated lung, which is characteristic of the **shred sign**. There is no evidence of a significant pleural effusion (no quad sign or jellyfish sign).

* **plaps** = true
* **type** = "consolidation"
