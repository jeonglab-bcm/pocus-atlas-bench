# 0126_lung_jr_loculated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images from the PLAPS point show a large anechoic (black) region, which is diagnostic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is an irregularly shaped, echogenic mass of lung tissue that undulates and floats, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**. This floating lung tissue has a tissue-like, hepatized appearance, indicating **consolidation** or atelectasis.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
