# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is an irregularly shaped, echogenic mass that exhibits a tissue-like, hepatized appearance. This floating, undulating piece of consolidated/atelectatic lung is representative of the "jellyfish sign."

Because both a significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.
