# 0073_lung_2017-10-9-dove-in-pleural-fluid

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, irregular, and echogenic mass of lung tissue that undulates with respiration. This is a classic "jellyfish sign," where atelectatic, consolidated lung floats within an effusion. The floating lung parenchyma exhibits a tissue-like, hepatized appearance, which is typical for consolidation.

Because both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are present, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- plaps = true
- type = "both"
