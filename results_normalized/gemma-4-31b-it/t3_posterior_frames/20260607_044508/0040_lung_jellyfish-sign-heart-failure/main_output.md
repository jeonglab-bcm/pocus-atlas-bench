# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

The ultrasound frames demonstrate a large anechoic (black) space, which is diagnostic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating piece of consolidated lung tissue that undulates with respiration, a classic finding known as the "jellyfish sign." The consolidated lung tissue itself shows a hepatized, tissue-like appearance.

Because both a pleural effusion (anechoic space, jellyfish sign) and alveolar consolidation (hepatized lung) are present, this is a positive PLAPS finding.
