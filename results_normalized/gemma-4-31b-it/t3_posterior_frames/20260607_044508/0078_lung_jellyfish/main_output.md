# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The ultrasound images of the right posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) show the following findings:

*   **Pleural Effusion:** There is a large anechoic (black) area representing a pleural fluid collection. Within this fluid, the consolidated lung is seen floating and moving, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation:** The lung parenchyma within the fluid is hyperechoic and has a texture similar to liver tissue, indicating **tissue-like hepatization**. The deep border of this consolidated area is irregular and fragmented, which is known as the **shred sign**.

Since both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, this represents a positive PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
