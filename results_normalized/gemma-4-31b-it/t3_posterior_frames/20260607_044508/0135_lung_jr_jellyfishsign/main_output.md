# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a combination of findings:

1.  **Pleural Effusion**: There is a large, anechoic (black) space in the dependent area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion.
2.  **Consolidation**: Adjacent to and within the fluid collection, there is a large, echogenic area with a solid, tissue-like appearance resembling the liver (hepatization). This indicates alveolar consolidation.
3.  **Shred Sign**: An irregular, "shredded" interface is visible between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid, a classic sign of consolidation.

Because both pleural fluid and tissue-like lung consolidation are present in this posterior zone, this represents a PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
