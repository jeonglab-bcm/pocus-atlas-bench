# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show the following findings:
- **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration and appears solid and echogenic, mimicking the texture of liver tissue.
- **Air bronchograms**: There are multiple punctate and linear hyperechoic (bright) foci within the consolidated lung, representing air trapped in the bronchi.
- **Shred sign**: An irregular, jagged border is visible between the consolidated area and the surrounding tissues.

There is no evidence of a significant anechoic fluid collection that would suggest a pleural effusion (no quad, sinusoid, or jellyfish signs).

Conclusion:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "consolidation"**
