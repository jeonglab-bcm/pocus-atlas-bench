# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

The ultrasound frames demonstrate the following findings:
- There is a prominent anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**.
- Within this fluid collection, there is a large, echogenic area with a "tissue-like" or "hepatized" appearance, indicating **alveolar consolidation**.
- The appearance of this consolidated lung floating and undulating within the surrounding effusion is highly suggestive of the **jellyfish sign**.

Given the presence of both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue in the posterior zone, this meets the criteria for a PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- plaps = true
- type = "both"
