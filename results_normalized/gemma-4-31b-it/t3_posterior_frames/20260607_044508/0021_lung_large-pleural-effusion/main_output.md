# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound frames from the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) show a large anechoic (black) space, which is diagnostic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, undulating piece of consolidated lung tissue with a liver-like (hepatized) appearance, characteristic of the "jellyfish sign." The anechoic area bounded by the pleural line and the consolidated lung creates a "quad sign." The Presence of both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue is observed.

- plaps = true
- type = "both"
