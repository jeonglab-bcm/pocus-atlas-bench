# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

The ultrasound images demonstrate a large anechoic (black) space in the posterior/lateral lung zone, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a fragment of consolidated lung tissue that is floating and moving with respiration, which is the "jellyfish sign." This consolidated lung tissue has a tissue-like, hepatized appearance, and contains small, hyperechoic foci consistent with air bronchograms.

Because both a significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.
