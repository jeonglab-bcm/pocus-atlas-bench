# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) show a large anechoic (black) space, which is indicative of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a fragment of lung tissue that appears consolidated and hepatized (tissue-like appearance). This consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within the fluid in synchronization with respiration, which is the characteristic "jellyfish sign." 

Because there is evidence of both a pleural effusion and consolidated/atelectatic lung tissue, this pattern is classified as PLAPS.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
