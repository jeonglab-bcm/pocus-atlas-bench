# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

Based on the provided sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone:

1.  **Effusion signs**: There is a large, prominent anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a hyperechoic, undulating piece of consolidated lung that appears to be floating and moving slowly, which is the classic **"jellyfish sign."**
2.  **Consolidation signs**: The floating lung tissue exhibits a **tissue-like hepatization** pattern, appearing solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of liver rather than aerated lung.

Because both a significant pleural effusion and consolidated/atelectatic lung are present, this is a clear PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
