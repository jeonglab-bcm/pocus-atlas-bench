# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show:

1.  **Pleural Effusion**: There is a prominent anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion.
2.  **Consolidation/Atelectasis**: A hyperechoic area with a tissue-like appearance (hepatization) is visible, representing consolidated or collapsed lung parenchyma.
3.  **Jellyfish Sign**: The consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within the surrounding pleural fluid, which is a classic "jellyfish sign."

Since both a fluid collection (effusion) and hepatized lung tissue (consolidation) are present, this is a PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
