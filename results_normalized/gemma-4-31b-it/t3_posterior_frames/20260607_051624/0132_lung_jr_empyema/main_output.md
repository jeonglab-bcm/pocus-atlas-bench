# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) demonstrate the following:

*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration artifacts and instead appears as a solid, echogenic mass resembling liver tissue.
*   **Shred sign:** There is a visible irregular, "shredded" border between the consolidated lung area and the surrounding tissues.
*   **Air bronchograms:** Within the hepatized lung tissue, there are several small hyperechoic (bright) punctate foci, which are characteristic air bronchograms.
*   **Absence of effusion:** There is no clear anechoic space representing a pleural effusion (no quad sign, sinusoid sign, or jellyfish sign).

Based on these findings, there is a clear pattern of alveolar consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "consolidation"**
