The ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone show the following:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) area representing a pleural effusion. The consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within this fluid collection, which is characteristic of the "jellyfish sign."
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma displays "tissue-like hepatization," appearing solid and echogenic (similar to liver tissue). Additionally, an irregular border (shred sign) is present between the consolidated and non-consolidated regions.

Because both a significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present, this is a PLAPS pattern.
