# 0143_lung_jr_pleuraleffusion

The provided ultrasound frames from the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) show the following:

1.  **Effusion signs**: There is a prominent anechoic (black) space between the chest wall and the lung, characteristic of a pleural effusion. This space forms a quadrilateral shape bounded by the pleural line and the lung, which is consistent with the **quad sign**.
2.  **Consolidation signs**: Within the fluid collection, there is an area of lung parenchyma that appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue (**tissue-like hepatization**). The border between this consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid is irregular and jagged, exhibiting the **shred sign**. Additionally, some hyperechoic linear echoes are visible within the hepatized lung, representing **air bronchograms**.

Because both pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present, the pattern is classified as PLAPS.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
