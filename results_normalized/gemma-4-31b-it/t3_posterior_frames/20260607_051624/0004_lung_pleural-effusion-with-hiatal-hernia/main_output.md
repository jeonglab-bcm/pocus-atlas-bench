# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

Based on the provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone:

*   **Consolidation Signs**: There is a large area of **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration and appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue. Within this consolidated area, several punctate hyperechoic foci are visible, which are **air bronchograms**. The border of this consolidation is irregular, demonstrating the **shred sign**.
*   **Effusion Signs**: Adjacent to the consolidated lung, there is a clear anechoic (black) space, which is diagnostic of a **pleural effusion**.

Because both signs of lung consolidation and pleural effusion are present in the dependent zone, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
