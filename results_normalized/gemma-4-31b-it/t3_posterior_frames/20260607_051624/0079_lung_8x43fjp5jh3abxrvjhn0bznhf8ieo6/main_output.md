The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, undulating piece of consolidated lung tissue that resembles a "jellyfish," indicating the **jellyfish sign**.

The consolidated lung tissue itself exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic. Inside this hepatized area, several punctate and linear hyperechoic foci are visible, which are **air bronchograms**, representing air trapped within the bronchi of the consolidated lung. The irregular border between the consolidated lung and the fluid further supports a **shred sign**.

Because both an effusion and consolidated lung tissue are present in the dependent posterior zone, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
