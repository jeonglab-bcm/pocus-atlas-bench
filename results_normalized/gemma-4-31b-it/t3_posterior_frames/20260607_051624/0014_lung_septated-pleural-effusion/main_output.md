# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images show a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, hyperechoic, undulating mass of lung tissue that moves with respiration, which is a classic "jellyfish sign." The lung tissue itself appears hepatized (tissue-like), indicating alveolar consolidation or atelectasis.

- **Effusion signs:** A large anechoic area is present, and the moving lung within it demonstrates the **jellyfish sign**.
- **Consolidation signs:** The floating lung mass shows **tissue-like hepatization**.

Based on these findings, there is evidence of both pleural effusion and lung consolidation.
