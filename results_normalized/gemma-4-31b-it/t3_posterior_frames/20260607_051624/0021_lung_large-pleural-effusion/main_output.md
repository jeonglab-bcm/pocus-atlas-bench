# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images show a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, undulating piece of lung tissue that moves with respiration, which is known as the "jellyfish sign." This floating lung tissue also displays tissue-like hepatization, indicating alveolar consolidation (atelectasis) resulting from the compression by the surrounding fluid.
