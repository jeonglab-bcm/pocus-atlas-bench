# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The ultrasound sequence of the right posterior lung zone shows the following findings:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) space representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a consolidated piece of lung is floating and undulating with respiration, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue floating in the fluid exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid with a texture similar to liver. Additionally, small, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within this tissue, representing **air bronchograms**. The border between the consolidated and non-consolidated lung is irregular, showing a **shred sign**.

Because both a pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are clearly visible, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
