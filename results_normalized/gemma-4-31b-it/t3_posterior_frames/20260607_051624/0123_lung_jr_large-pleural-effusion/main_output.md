# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

The provided ultrasound frames from a posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) demonstrate the following:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, prominent anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion.
*   **Consolidation signs:** Within this fluid collection, there is a region of lung parenchyma that has lost its normal aeration and appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of liver tissue (**tissue-like hepatization**). The border between this consolidated lung and the surrounding area is irregular and jagged, which is known as the **shred sign**.

Because both a significant fluid collection and hepatized lung tissue are present, this represents a combined pattern of pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation.
