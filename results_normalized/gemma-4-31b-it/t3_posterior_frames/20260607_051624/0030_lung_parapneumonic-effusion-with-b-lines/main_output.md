# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

The ultrasound frames from the right posterior lung zone (RT H/T ZONE 2 LOW) demonstrate several key findings:

1.  **Pleural Effusion**: There is a large anechoic (black) region, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion.
2.  **Lung Consolidation**: A solid, echogenic area with a tissue-like appearance (hepatization) is visible, representing consolidated or atelectatic lung.
3.  **Jellyfish Sign**: The consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and undulating within the pleural fluid, creating the characteristic "jellyfish sign."
4.  **Shred Sign**: The border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid/aerated lung is irregular and "shredded."

Because both significant pleural fluid and hepatized lung tissue are present, this represents a combined PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
