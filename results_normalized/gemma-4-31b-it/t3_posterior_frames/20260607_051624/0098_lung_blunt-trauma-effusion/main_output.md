# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

These sequential ultrasound frames of a posterior lung zone demonstrate a classic PLAPS pattern.

Observation:
- There is a large, anechoic (black) region consistent with a **pleural effusion**.
- Within this fluid collection, a consolidated piece of lung parenchyma is visible, appearing "hepatized" (tissue-like texture).
- This consolidated lung is floating and undulating within the effusion, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
- Small, hyperechoic (bright) punctate foci are visible within the consolidated lung, representing **air bronchograms**.
- The anechoic space bounded by the pleural line and the consolidated lung creates a **quad sign**.

Since both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are present, the classification is as follows:

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
