The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large, anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is an undulating, echogenic tissue-like mass, which represents hepatized/consolidated lung floating in the fluid—this is a classic "jellyfish sign." The presence of both a significant fluid collection and consolidated lung parenchyma indicates a PLAPS pattern.
