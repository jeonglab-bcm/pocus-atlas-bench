# 0143_lung_jr_pleuraleffusion

The ultrasound frames from the PLAPS point demonstrate the following findings:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) space between the pleural line and the underlying lung, characteristic of a pleural effusion. The appearance is consistent with a **quad sign**, where fluid is bounded by the chest wall and rib shadows.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung parenchyma below the fluid collection exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic, resembling liver texture, rather than showing normal aeration artifacts (like A-lines).

Because both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present in this posterior dependent zone, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "both"**
