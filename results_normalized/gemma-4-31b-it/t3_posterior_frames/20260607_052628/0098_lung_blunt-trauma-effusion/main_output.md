# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

These ultrasound frames from the PLAPS point demonstrate a clear PLAPS pattern.

Observations:
*   **Effusion signs:** There is a large, anechoic (black) space in the dependent area of the lung, characteristic of a pleural effusion. The floating, irregular mass of lung tissue within this fluid collection is consistent with the "jellyfish sign."
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue floating in the effusion exhibits "hepatization," meaning it has a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue. The irregular, jagged border between this consolidated lung and the surrounding anechoic fluid is typical of the "shred sign."

Conclusion:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
