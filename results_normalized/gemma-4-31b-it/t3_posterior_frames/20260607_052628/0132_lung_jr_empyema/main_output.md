# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) demonstrate the following findings:

*   **Consolidation signs**: The lung parenchyma exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing as a solid, echogenic mass resembling liver texture. Within this consolidated area, there are multiple hyperechoic punctate foci, which are characteristic **air bronchograms**. Additionally, the border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding area is irregular, representing the **shred sign**.
*   **Effusion signs**: There is a large, anechoic (black) space surrounding the consolidated lung, indicating a **pleural effusion**. The consolidated lung appears to be floating or partially submerged within this fluid.

Since both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are clearly visible, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- plaps = true
- type = "both"
