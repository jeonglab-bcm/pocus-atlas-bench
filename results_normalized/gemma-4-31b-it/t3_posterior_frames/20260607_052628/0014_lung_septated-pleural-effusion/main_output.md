# 0014_lung_septated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images demonstrate a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, undulating piece of lung tissue that resembles a jellyfish, which is the pathognomonic **"jellyfish sign"**. The floating lung tissue itself exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, indicating alveolar consolidation (likely compressive atelectasis due to the effusion).

Because both a pleural effusion and consolidation are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
