# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The ultrasound images of the right posterior lung zone show a large anechoic (black) space, which is diagnostic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is an undulating, irregular mass of hyperechoic lung tissue that exhibits tissue-like hepatization, resembling liver texture. This appearance of consolidated lung floating in fluid is known as the "jellyfish sign," indicating a combination of pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation or atelectasis.
