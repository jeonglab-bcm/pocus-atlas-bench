# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone:

1.  **Effusion Signs:** There is a large anechoic (black) space, indicating a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, a consolidated piece of lung is seen floating and undulating with respiration, which is the characteristic **jellyfish sign**.
2.  **Consolidation Signs:** The floating lung tissue exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic, similar to liver texture, rather than normal aerated lung.

Because both pleural fluid and consolidated lung tissue are clearly visible, this is a positive PLAPS finding.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
