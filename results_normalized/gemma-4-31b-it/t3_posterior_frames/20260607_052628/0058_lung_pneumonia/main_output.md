# 0058_lung_pneumonia

The ultrasound images show a posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) with the following findings:

1.  **Effusion Signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) area representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a floating, undulating piece of lung tissue is visible, which is characteristic of the **"jellyfish sign."**
2.  **Consolidation Signs:** The deeper lung tissue exhibits **hepatization**, appearing solid and tissue-like rather than aerated. Within this consolidated area, there are multiple punctate hyperechoic foci, which are typical **air bronchograms**. Additionally, the irregular border between the consolidated lung and the effusion represents a **"shred sign."**

Since both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

- **type = "both"**
