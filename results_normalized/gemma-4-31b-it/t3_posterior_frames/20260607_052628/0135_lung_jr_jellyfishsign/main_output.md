# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

The ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) demonstrate the following:

1.  **Pleural Effusion**: There is a large, anechoic (black) space located superiorly and laterally, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion.
2.  **Alveolar Consolidation**: The dependent lung parenchyma appears solid and hyperechoic, resembling liver tissue (hepatization) instead of normal aerated lung.
3.  **Mixed Findings**: The consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within the pleural fluid, creating a "jellyfish sign." Additionally, small hyperechoic foci are visible within the consolidated lung, representing air bronchograms.

Because both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation (hepatization) are clearly present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
