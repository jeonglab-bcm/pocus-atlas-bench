# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The ultrasound frames show a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Floating within this fluid is a consolidated, tissue-like mass of lung that moves dynamically with respiration, creating a classic "jellyfish sign." The lung tissue itself exhibits hepatization, appearing solid and echogenic, which indicates alveolar consolidation or compressive atelectasis.

Since both signs of pleural effusion (anechoic space, jellyfish sign) and lung consolidation (tissue-like hepatization) are present, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
