# 0025_lung_malignant-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone demonstrate a clear pattern of lung consolidation.

*   **Observations:**
    *   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration (no A-lines or B-lines) and instead exhibits a solid, echogenic appearance resembling liver tissue (hepatization).
    *   **Air bronchograms:** There are numerous punctate and linear hyperechoic (bright) foci within the consolidated area, which are characteristic of air bronchograms (air trapped within the bronchi of consolidated lung).
    *   **Lack of Effusion:** There is no evidence of a classic "quad sign," "sinusoid sign," or "jellyfish sign" that would indicate a significant pleural effusion. The anechoic spaces seen are within the consolidated tissue rather than being free-flowing pleural fluid.

Based on these findings, there is a definitive PLAPS pattern characterized by consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "consolidation"**
