# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

The ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone show a loss of normal aeration artifacts. The lung parenchyma has a solid, echogenic appearance resembling liver tissue, which is characteristic of **tissue-like hepatization**. Within this consolidated area, there are small, bright, punctate and linear hyperechoic foci, representing **air bronchograms**. Furthermore, the border between the consolidated and aerated lung is irregular and jagged, demonstrating the **shred sign**. No anechoic fluid collections indicative of a pleural effusion are seen.

Conclusion:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "consolidation"**
