# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

The provided ultrasound frames of a PLAPS point show a large anechoic (black) region, which is indicative of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid, there is a consolidated area of lung tissue that appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue (**tissue-like hepatization**). This consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within the surrounding fluid, which is a characteristic finding known as the **jellyfish sign**.

Since both signs of fluid collection (effusion) and lung consolidation are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

- **type = "both"**
