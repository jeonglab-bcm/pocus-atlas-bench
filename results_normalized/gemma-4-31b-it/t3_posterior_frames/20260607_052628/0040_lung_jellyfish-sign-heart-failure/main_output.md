# 0040_lung_jellyfish-sign-heart-failure

The ultrasound frames show a posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) with the following findings:

*   **Pleural Effusion:** A large, anechoic (black) space is visible, which is characteristic of fluid collection in the pleural space. The arrangement of the pleural line superiorly and the collapsed lung inferiorly forms a **quad sign**.
*   **Consolidation:** There are areas of echogenic, tissue-like parenchyma that resemble the liver (**hepatization**), particularly on the left side of the image. The irregular border between this consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid is indicative of a **shred sign**. Additionally, the lung tissue appears to float within the effusion, suggesting a **jellyfish sign**.

Because both an anechoic fluid collection and hepatized lung tissue are present, this is a combined pattern of pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
