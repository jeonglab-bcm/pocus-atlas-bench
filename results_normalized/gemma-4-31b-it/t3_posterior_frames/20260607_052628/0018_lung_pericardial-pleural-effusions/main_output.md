# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), the following observations are made:

1.  **Effusion signs**: There is a prominent anechoic (black) area representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, undulating, irregular fragments of lung tissue are visible, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
2.  **Consolidation signs**: The lung parenchyma exhibits a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue (**tissue-like hepatization**). The interface between the consolidated lung and the aerated/fluid-filled areas is irregular, demonstrating the **shred sign**. Additionally, several small, hyperechoic punctate foci are visible within the consolidated lung, which are **air bronchograms**.

Because both an effusion and lung consolidation are clearly present, the findings are consistent with a PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
