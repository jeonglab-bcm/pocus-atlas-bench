# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

The provided ultrasound sequence from the PLAPS point shows a large, anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a piece of consolidated lung tissue that is floating and undulating with respiration, creating a classic "jellyfish sign." The consolidated lung itself exhibits tissue-like hepatization.

Because both signs of pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are present, this is a positive PLAPS finding.
