# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) demonstrate a large anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, irregularly shaped, hyperechoic mass of lung tissue. This undulating, consolidated lung floating in fluid is known as the "jellyfish sign." The appearance of the lung tissue is tissue-like (hepatization), indicating consolidation or atelectasis.

Because both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
