# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The ultrasound frames demonstrate a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a consolidated piece of lung that has a tissue-like, hepatized appearance. This consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within the surrounding fluid, which is the classic **"jellyfish sign."**

Because both a significant pleural effusion and pulmonary consolidation (the floating lung fragment) are present, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
