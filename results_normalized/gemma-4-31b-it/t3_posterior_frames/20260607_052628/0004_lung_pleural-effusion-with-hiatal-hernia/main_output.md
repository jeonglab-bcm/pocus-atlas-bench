# 0004_lung_pleural-effusion-with-hiatal-hernia

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames provided of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), here is the analysis:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) space representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, a large, echogenic, irregular mass of consolidated lung is floating, which is characteristic of the **"jellyfish sign."** The space is bounded superiorly by the chest wall and inferiorly by the lung, creating a quadrilateral-like appearance consistent with the **"quad sign."**
*   **Consolidation signs:** The floating lung mass shows **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic, similar to liver texture. Within this hepatized area, small hyperechoic punctate foci are visible, which are **air bronchograms**. The irregular border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid is consistent with the **"shred sign."**

Because both a pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are clearly visible, the findings are diagnostic of a PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
