1.  **Effusion:** There is a significant anechoic (black) space, representing a pleural effusion, which surrounds the lung tissue.
2.  **Consolidation:** Within the pleural fluid, the lung appears solid and echogenic with a texture similar to liver tissue (**hepatization**). Small, bright, punctate hyperechoic foci are visible within this consolidated lung, characteristic of **air bronchograms**.
