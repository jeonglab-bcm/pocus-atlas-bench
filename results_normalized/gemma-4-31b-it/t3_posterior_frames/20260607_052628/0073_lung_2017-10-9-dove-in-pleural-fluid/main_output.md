# 0073_lung_2017-10-9-dove-in-pleural-fluid

The ultrasound frames from the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) demonstrate a large anechoic (black) space, which is diagnostic of a **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a fragment of consolidated lung tissue that appears echogenic and tissue-like (hepatization). This consolidated fragment is seen floating and undulating within the effusion, which is the classic **"jellyfish sign."**

Because there is both a significant pleural effusion and evidence of lung consolidation (atelectasis), both patterns are present.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
