The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone demonstrate the following findings:

*   **Pleural Effusion:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) space, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion.
*   **Consolidation:** Floating within the fluid collection is a mass of consolidated lung tissue with a "tissue-like" or "hepatized" appearance. This combination of consolidated lung undulating within an effusion is known as the **jellyfish sign**.
*   **Air Bronchograms:** Within the consolidated area, there are several small, punctate hyperechoic (bright) foci, which represent air bronchograms.
*   **Shred Sign:** The irregular, shredded appearance of the border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid/aerated lung is also evident.

Because both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, this represents a PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- plaps = true
- type = "both"
