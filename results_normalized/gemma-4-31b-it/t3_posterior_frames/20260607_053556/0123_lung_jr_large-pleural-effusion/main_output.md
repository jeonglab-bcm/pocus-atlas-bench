# 0123_lung_jr_large-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone demonstrate a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a fragment of consolidated lung tissue that is floating and undulating, which is known as the "jellyfish sign." The consolidated lung exhibits a tissue-like, hepatized appearance. The presence of this large fluid collection and the associated floating consolidated lung indicates both pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation.
