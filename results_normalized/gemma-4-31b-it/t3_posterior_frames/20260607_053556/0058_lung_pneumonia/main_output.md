# 0058_lung_pneumonia

The provided ultrasound sequence of the posterior lung zone shows several key findings:

1.  **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) space, characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a small piece of atelectatic lung is seen floating and undulating with respiration, which is the **jellyfish sign**.
2.  **Consolidation signs:** Below the effusion, the lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration and appears solid and echogenic, resembling liver tissue (**tissue-like hepatization**). Within this consolidated area, there are multiple punctate hyperechoic foci, representing **air bronchograms**.

Because both pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

Conclusion:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
