# 0021_lung_large-pleural-effusion

These sequential ultrasound frames from a posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) show a large anechoic (black) space, which is diagnostic of a pleural effusion. Floating within this fluid is a fragmented, echogenic piece of lung tissue with a tissue-like, hepatized appearance. This undulating, fragmented lung within the effusion is characteristic of the "jellyfish sign," indicating associated lung consolidation.

Conclusion:
- plaps = true
- type = "both"
