# 0132_lung_jr_empyema

The ultrasound frames show the following findings:

*   **Consolidation**: There is a large area of lung parenchyma that has lost its normal aeration and appears solid and echogenic, resembling the texture of liver tissue (tissue-like hepatization).
*   **Air Bronchograms**: Within this consolidated area, multiple punctate and linear anechoic/hypoechoic foci are visible, which are characteristic of air bronchograms.
*   **Pleural Effusion**: An anechoic (black) space is visible adjacent to the consolidated lung, representing a pleural effusion.

Since both signs of alveolar consolidation and pleural effusion are present in the posterior lung zone, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
