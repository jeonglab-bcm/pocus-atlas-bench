# 0098_lung_blunt-trauma-effusion

The ultrasound frames from the PLAPS point show a combination of pleural effusion and lung consolidation:

- **Effusion signs**: There is a clear anechoic (black) area representing a pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a piece of consolidated lung is floating and undulating, which is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.
- **Consolidation signs**: The floating lung tissue exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic, similar to liver parenchyma, rather than the normal aerated lung.

Since both fluid and hepatized lung tissue are present, this represents a PLAPS pattern.

Conclusion:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
