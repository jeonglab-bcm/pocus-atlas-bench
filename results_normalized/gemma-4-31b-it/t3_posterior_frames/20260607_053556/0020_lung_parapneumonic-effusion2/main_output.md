# 0020_lung_parapneumonic-effusion2

The ultrasound frames show a large, anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a piece of consolidated lung tissue that appears hepatized (tissue-like texture) and is floating and undulating with respiration. This dynamic finding is the classic "jellyfish sign," which indicates atelectatic lung within a pleural effusion.

Because both a significant fluid collection and consolidated lung tissue are present, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
