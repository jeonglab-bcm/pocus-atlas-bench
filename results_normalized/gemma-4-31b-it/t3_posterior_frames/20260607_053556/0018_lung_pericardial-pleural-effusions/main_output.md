# 0018_lung_pericardial-pleural-effusions

Based on the provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone (PLAPS point), the following observations are made:

1.  **Consolidation Signs**: There is a prominent area of **tissue-like hepatization**, where the lung parenchyma has lost its normal aeration and appears solid and echogenic, similar to liver tissue. Within this consolidated region, multiple hyperechoic (bright) punctate and linear foci are visible, which are characteristic **air bronchograms**.
2.  **Effusion Signs**: Adjacent to and surrounding the consolidated lung, there are anechoic (black) spaces, indicating the presence of a **pleural effusion**.

The combination of consolidated (hepatized) lung with air bronchograms and an associated pleural effusion in the posterior dependent zone is diagnostic of a PLAPS pattern.

*   **type = "both"**
