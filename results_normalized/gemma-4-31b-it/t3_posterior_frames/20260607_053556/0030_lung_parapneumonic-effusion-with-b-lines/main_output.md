# 0030_lung_parapneumonic-effusion-with-b-lines

The ultrasound images of the right posterior lung zone (Zone 2 Low) show a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a consolidated, echogenic mass of lung tissue that appears to be floating and undulating, which is the classic **"jellyfish sign."** The lung tissue itself shows **hepatization**, resembling liver texture, indicating consolidation or atelectasis.

Because both signs of pleural effusion and lung consolidation/atelectasis are present, this is a positive PLAPS finding.

**Conclusion:**
- plaps = true
- type = "both"
