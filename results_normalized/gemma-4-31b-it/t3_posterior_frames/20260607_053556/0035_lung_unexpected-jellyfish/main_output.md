# 0035_lung_unexpected-jellyfish

The ultrasound images show a large, anechoic (black) area consistent with a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a large, echogenic, tissue-like mass that exhibits **hepatization**, representing consolidated lung. The undulating movement of this consolidated lung within the fluid is characteristic of the **jellyfish sign**.

Since both a pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are present in the posterior/lateral dependent zone, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
