# 0050_lung_plaps-pneumonia

Based on the provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone:

1.  **Effusion signs**: There is a large anechoic (black) area consistent with a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a consolidated piece of lung is floating and undulating, which is a classic **jellyfish sign**.
2.  **Consolidation signs**: The floating lung tissue exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, appearing solid and echogenic. Within this hepatized area, there are punctate hyperechoic foci representing **air bronchograms**. The irregular border between the consolidated lung and the surrounding fluid/aerated areas is indicative of the **shred sign**.

Since both signs of pleural effusion and alveolar consolidation are clearly present in the dependent posterior zone, this is a positive PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
