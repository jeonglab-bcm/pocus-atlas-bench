# 0029_lung_parapneumonic-effusion

Based on the sequential ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone, the following observations are made:

*   **Tissue-like hepatization:** The lung parenchyma exhibits a solid, echogenic appearance similar to liver tissue, indicating a loss of normal aeration.
*   **Air bronchograms:** There are multiple bright, hyperechoic punctate and linear foci within the consolidated lung, representing air trapped in the bronchi.
*   **Shred sign:** An irregular, shredded deep border is visible between the consolidated lung and the adjacent areas.

There is no evidence of a significant pleural effusion (no clear quad sign or jellyfish sign). The dominant finding is lung consolidation.

**Conclusion:**
*   **plaps = true**
*   **type = "consolidation"**
