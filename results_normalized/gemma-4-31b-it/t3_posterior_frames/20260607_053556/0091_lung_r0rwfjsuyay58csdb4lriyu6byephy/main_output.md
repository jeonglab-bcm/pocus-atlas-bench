The sequential frames of the lung ultrasound in the posterior zone (PLAPS point) show the following findings:

*   **Tissue-like hepatization**: The lung parenchyma has lost its normal air-filled appearance and appears as a solid, echogenic mass with a texture similar to the liver.
*   **Air bronchograms**: Within the hepatized lung, there are multiple small, punctate hyperechoic (bright) foci, which represent air trapped in the bronchi of the consolidated lung.

Based on these observations, the patterns are consistent with alveolar consolidation.
