# 0143_lung_jr_pleuraleffusion

The provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone demonstrate the following:

*   **Effusion signs:** There is a prominent anechoic (black) space between the chest wall and the lung, representing a **pleural effusion**. The lung surface oscillates with respiration, creating a **sinusoid sign**.
*   **Consolidation signs:** The lung tissue below the fluid has a solid, liver-like echo texture (**tissue-like hepatization**). The consolidated lung is seen floating and undulating within the effusion, which is the characteristic **jellyfish sign**.

Because both a pleural effusion and lung consolidation/atelectasis are present in this dependent zone, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
