# 0126_lung_jr_loculated-pleural-effusion

The ultrasound images from the PLAPS point show the following findings:

1.  **Pleural Effusion**: There is a large anechoic (black) area, representing a significant pleural effusion. Within this fluid, a fragmented piece of lung tissue is seen floating and undulating with respiration, which is characteristic of the **"jellyfish sign"**.
2.  **Alveolar Consolidation**: The floating lung tissue exhibits **tissue-like hepatization**, meaning it has a solid appearance similar to liver parenchyma rather than the normal air-filled lung. The irregular, jagged border of this consolidated lung fragment against the fluid is known as the **"shred sign"**. Additionally, small hyperechoic foci within the hepatized tissue are indicative of **air bronchograms**.

Since both pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present in this posterior dependent zone, the findings are diagnostic of PLAPS.
