# 0073_lung_2017-10-9-dove-in-pleural-fluid

The ultrasound images show a large anechoic (black) space, characteristic of a pleural effusion, filling a significant portion of the posterior lung zone. Within this fluid, there is a floating, irregularly shaped, echogenic mass that resembles liver tissue (hepatization), representing consolidated/atelectatic lung. This classic appearance of consolidated lung floating in pleural fluid is known as the "jellyfish sign."

Because both pleural effusion and lung consolidation are present:

- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
