# 0100_lung_hemothorax

The provided ultrasound frames of the posterior lung zone show a large anechoic (black) area, which is characteristic of a pleural effusion. Within this fluid collection, there is a consolidated piece of lung that moves and undulates with respiration, creating a "jellyfish sign." The consolidated lung tissue itself exhibits hepatization (tissue-like appearance).

Since both a significant pleural effusion and lung consolidation are clearly visible, this is a positive PLAPS finding.
