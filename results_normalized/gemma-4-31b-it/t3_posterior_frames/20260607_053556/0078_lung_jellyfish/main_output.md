# 0078_lung_jellyfish

The ultrasound images of the right posterior lung zone (PLAPS point) demonstrate the following:

*   **Pleural Effusion:** There is a large anechoic (black) space, indicating a significant pleural effusion. 
*   **Consolidation:** Within this fluid collection, there is a fragment of lung tissue that appears solid and echogenic, similar to liver texture (tissue-like hepatization).
*   **Jellyfish Sign:** This consolidated lung tissue is seen floating and moving within the anechoic fluid, which is a characteristic "jellyfish sign."

Since both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung are present, the PLAPS pattern is positive for both.

**Conclusion:**
- plaps = true
- type = "both"
