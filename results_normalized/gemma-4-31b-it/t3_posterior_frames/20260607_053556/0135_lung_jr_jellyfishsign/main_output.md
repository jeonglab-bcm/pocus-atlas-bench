# 0135_lung_jr_jellyfishsign

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show a large, hyperechoic area with a texture resembling solid organ tissue, such as the liver. This "tissue-like hepatization" is characteristic of lung consolidation. Additionally, there is an irregular, "shredded" border between this consolidated area and the aerated lung, which is the "shred sign." There is no evident anechoic space to suggest a significant pleural effusion (no quad, sinusoid, or jellyfish signs).

Conclusion:
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "consolidation"**
