# 0015_lung_plaps-consolidation

The ultrasound images of the posterior lung zone show the following:

1.  **Tissue-like Hepatization**: The lung parenchyma has lost its normal aerated appearance and now exhibits a solid, echogenic texture similar to that of the liver.
2.  **Air Bronchograms**: Within the consolidated, hepatized lung, there are multiple punctate and linear hyperechoic (bright) foci, which are characteristic of air trapped in the bronchi.
3.  **Pleural Effusion**: There are significant anechoic (black) areas, particularly prominent in the first frame and along the periphery of the consolidated lung in subsequent frames, indicating the presence of a pleural effusion.

The presence of both solid, tissue-like lung and anechoic fluid collections confirms a PLAPS pattern involving both consolidation and effusion.

**Conclusion:**
- **plaps = true**
- **type = "both"**
