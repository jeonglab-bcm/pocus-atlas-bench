# 0051_lung_jellyfish-sign

The ultrasound images from the posterior lung zone show a large, anechoic (black) area, which is diagnostic of a significant **pleural effusion**. Within this fluid collection, there is a floating, undulating piece of lung tissue that has a tissue-like, hepatized appearance, characteristic of consolidation or atelectasis. This floating lung within the effusion is a classic representation of the **"jellyfish sign."** The fluid collection is bounded by the chest wall and the consolidated lung, forming a **"quad sign."**

Since both a pleural effusion and consolidated lung tissue are present, this is a classic PLAPS pattern.

- **plaps**: true
- **type**: "both"
